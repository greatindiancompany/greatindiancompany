# Two Clocks: Bulk-Drug Parks Through FY 2027, and BioE3

Mid-term, for Indian pharma and biotech, is two official clocks that should not be averaged into one forecast. One clock is chemical and fermentation infrastructure for critical drug inputs, with a published end year. The other is a biotechnology policy for biomanufacturing, approved later, with thematic sectors and no park-construction table.

## Clock one ends in FY 2026–27

The Lok Sabha reply of 6 February 2026 says the Cabinet approved the Scheme for Promotion of Bulk Drug Parks on 20 March 2020. The same reply states the tenure as FY 2020–21 to FY 2026–27, with ₹3,000 crore allocated and a ceiling of ₹1,000 crore per park. Three parks are approved: Jambusar in Gujarat, Haroli in Himachal Pradesh, and Nakkapalli in Andhra Pradesh. On that February date, common infrastructure was still a works programme. Gujarat’s civil packages were at an advanced stage and utility execution was underway. Andhra Pradesh was installing an external water line, seeking power-design approval, and acquiring more land after an environmental clearance in January 2026. Himachal Pradesh had floated the zero-liquid-discharge and steam tender.

A mid-term trajectory that respects the text says this: the central grant is available inside a scheme that, as stated in February 2026, runs through FY 2026–27, and the common facilities were not described as commissioned. Whether a tenant can take steam and effluent treatment inside that window depends on the state implementing agency’s contracts after that reply. The reply is a status, not a promise that every facility will be ready on the last day of the tenure.

Beside the parks, the bulk-drug PLI continues as a sales incentive for greenfield projects on a notified product list. The same reply puts that list at 41 products and the approvals at 48 projects covering 33 bulk drugs, inside a ₹6,940 crore outlay. The original cabinet design paid incentive for six years on incremental sales over 2019–20. Anyone projecting “the rest of the decade” should tie the remaining incentive years to the firm’s own approval and the current guidelines, including corrigenda posted through 2026 on the Department’s schemes page.

The pharmaceuticals PLI, ₹15,000 crore and 55 approved applicants in that reply, is the parallel track for high-value drugs and for intermediates outside the bulk-drug list, in greenfield and brownfield plants. Its mid-term shape is disbursement against eligible sales by those applicants, not the construction of the three parks.

## Clock two starts in August 2024

On 24 August 2024 the Cabinet approved the BioE3 policy of the Department of Biotechnology: Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment, for high-performance biomanufacturing. The Prime Minister’s Office note says the policy supports research and entrepreneurship across thematic sectors, and that it will accelerate technology development by establishing biomanufacturing and bio-AI hubs and biofoundries. The sectors named are high-value bio-based chemicals, biopolymers and enzymes; smart proteins and functional foods; precision biotherapeutics; climate-resilient agriculture; carbon capture and its utilisation; and marine and space research.

A Press Information Bureau note on 27 November 2024 restates the objective as a framework for biomanufacturing and lists the same six themes, with slightly tighter labels: bio-based chemicals and enzymes, functional foods and smart proteins, precision biotherapeutics, climate-resilient agriculture, carbon capture and utilisation, and futuristic marine and space research. It records that sectoral expert committees had identified gaps and strengths. It does not publish a rupee outlay in the passage that sets those themes, and this brief will not supply one.

BioE3 can touch pharma where the sector is precision biotherapeutics or bio-based chemicals. It is still a Department of Biotechnology policy. The bulk-drug parks remain a Department of Pharmaceuticals infrastructure scheme. A company can sit in both conversations. A forecast that adds park acres to biofoundries and calls the sum one pipeline has mixed the clocks.

## What a five-year note can say in 2026

It can say the park grant window, as last stated to Parliament, runs through FY 2026–27, with construction incomplete at the February 2026 snapshot. It can say PLI cash flows follow audited eligible sales for approved products. It can say BioE3 names the biological manufacturing themes the Cabinet adopted in 2024. It cannot, from these documents, publish a combined market size for “Indian pharma and biotech in 2030.”
