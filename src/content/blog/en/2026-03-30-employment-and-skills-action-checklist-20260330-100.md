An operator, founder, or investor needs a short path through India's employment releases, with the date and the denominator still visible. This is that path as of 24 September 2026. Each line points at the document that carries the figure.

## Quote the week and the year as different facts

Open the August 2026 monthly bulletin of the Periodic Labour Force Survey, released on 15 September 2026. For persons aged 15 and above, on the current weekly status, write: participation 55.6%, employment 52.8%, unemployment 5.0%. If the decision is rural or urban, write rural unemployment 4.1% and urban unemployment 6.8%, rural participation 58.2% and urban participation 50.4%. The month-to-month reading is in the [August bulletin brief](/blog/employment-and-skills-what-changed-20260330-010).

Keep the July 2023–June 2024 annual press note as the structural benchmark. Usual status, persons aged 15 and above: participation 60.1%, employment 58.2%, unemployment 3.2%. The same note's weekly-status unemployment for that year is 4.9%. The definitions, and the arithmetic that keeps unemployment inside the labour force, are in the [key-numbers brief](/blog/employment-and-skills-key-numbers-20260330-020).

Before a percentage goes into a board note, the sentence should already contain three words: the age group, the status (usual or weekly), and the month or the July–June year. The survey methodology that produces the monthly bulletin was modified in January 2025. The 3.2% line ends with the 2023–24 annual round. The seven-year table is in the [long-term brief](/blog/employment-and-skills-long-term-trend-20260330-050).

## Treat the wage ceiling as a decision still being implemented

On 16 September 2026 the Cabinet approved an EPFO wage ceiling of ₹25,000 a month, up from ₹15,000. The note expects more than 51 lakh additional employees to enter mandatory coverage. It also says the ministry and EPFO still have to complete the statutory steps. Until those steps are notified, payroll software and offer letters stay on the rules in force. The scale cited alongside the decision is about 7.98 crore contributing members and about 7.68 lakh contributing establishments. The employer reading is in the [EPFO brief](/blog/employment-and-skills-impact-on-smes-20260330-030).

## Split a payroll month into new subscribers and net addition

The latest monthly payroll narrative published by the Ministry of Labour and Employment in the sources used here is the provisional July 2025 release: net addition 21.04 lakh members, new subscribers about 9.79 lakh, rejoins about 16.43 lakh. The release says later filings revise the month. EPFO posts the provisional tables at its payroll page. Use a net figure as movement in covered membership. Use the new-subscriber figure when the question is first enrolments. The July 2025 age split is specific: 61.06% of new subscribers were 18–25.

## Keep training counts in the training column

From the 4 February 2026 Rajya Sabha reply, up to 31 December 2025: PMKVY had trained and certified over 1.64 crore candidates; Jan Shikshan Sansthan had trained 34.14 lakh persons since 2018–19; apprentices engaged under the national apprenticeship scheme totalled 51.30 lakh since 2016–17; ITIs stood at 14,688, against 9,776 in 2014. The reply says PMKVY 4.0 does not track placements the way the short-term training of the first three versions did. Quote these as training and engagement. The scheme map is in the [Skill India brief](/blog/employment-and-skills-policy-explainer-20260330-060).

If the question is women's work, add the calendar-year caution from the 9 April 2025 note: the percentage of helpers in household enterprises fell from 19.9% to 18.1% between 2023 and 2024, and the office says that decline seems to have contributed to the drop in rural female participation and employment. That reading is in the [household brief](/blog/employment-and-skills-impact-on-households-20260330-040).

## Where the next document will appear

The monthly survey note is published by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation on [mospi.gov.in](https://www.mospi.gov.in/). Labour and EPFO decisions appear through the Press Information Bureau and on [epfindia.gov.in](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_en/Estimate_of_Payroll.php). Skill India counts in this brief come from the ministry's parliamentary replies on [pib.gov.in](https://www.pib.gov.in/). When a newer bulletin replaces August 2026, replace the weekly-status triple and leave the 2023–24 annual benchmark in place until the office publishes the next annual-style year on the new design.

The ways these figures get joined badly are listed in the [misreading brief](/blog/employment-and-skills-risk-watch-20260330-080).
