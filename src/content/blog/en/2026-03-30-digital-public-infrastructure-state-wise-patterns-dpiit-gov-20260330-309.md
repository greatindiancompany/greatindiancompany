# India DPI Across States: Published Patterns, Not a Ranking

## The national note is not a state league table

The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 gives national totals for Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, UMANG, and Direct Benefit Transfer. It does not rank states or union territories on those series. This page does not supply a ranking either. A rank that is not in the note would be an invented one.

The update date on this brief is 23 September 2026. That date does not fill the missing state table.

## Patterns the note does publish

Four geographic cuts are in the note. They are not interchangeable.

- Fifth-generation mobile service is stated as available in 99.9 percent of districts, covering 85 percent of the population. As of December 2025, 5.18 lakh 5G base stations had been installed. Wireless subscribers were 125.87 crore at the end of December 2025. Household smartphone ownership is given as a national 85.5 percent, not by state.
- The National Non-Communicable Diseases platform is stated as operational across 31 states and union territories. As of December 2025, 74.97 crore beneficiaries had been enrolled, and over 8.64 crore patients were under treatment for hypertension and diabetes.
- DIKSHA is described as adopted by almost all states and union territories, and by central boards including CBSE. The note's counts are national: 2.11 crore registered users and 566 crore learning sessions as of 5 March 2026.
- ONDC retail sellers, 1.16 lakh-plus as of December 2025, are placed in 630-plus cities and towns. The note does not list the cities.

UMANG is described as a single window for central, state, and local services. As of 5 March 2026 it had more than 2,400 services and 10.25 crore registrations. That is an institutional span. It is not a state score.

## What a state pattern would still need

A usable state pattern would repeat one definition — Aadhaar authentications, UPI volume, DigiLocker users, or common-service-centre transactions — for each state, with one as-of date. The March note does not do that.

Digital India's pillars include broadband highways, universal mobile access, and public internet access through common service centres. Those are programme headings on the pillars page. They are not a current state table.

District 5G coverage and a health platform in 31 states and union territories are the closest published spreads. They answer different questions. They should not be merged into one digital-readiness rank.
