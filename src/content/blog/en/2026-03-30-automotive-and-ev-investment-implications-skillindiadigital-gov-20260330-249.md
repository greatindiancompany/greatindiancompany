# How PLI Auto Turns Local Content into a Payable Incentive

PLI Auto pays for determined sales of advanced automotive technology after a domestic-value-addition test. It does not pay at the showroom, and it does not pay an applicant who has only been approved. That distinction is the investment implication. Capital committed under the scheme and cash received under the scheme are different lines in the ministry's own review.

## What the scheme was built to pay

The [ministry page](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/en/pli-scheme-automobile-and-auto-component-industry) says the Union Cabinet approved PLI Auto on 15 September 2021 with a budgetary outlay of ₹25,938 crore for five years, FY 2022-23 to FY 2026-27. Incentives apply on that window and are disbursed in the following year, FY 2023-24 through FY 2027-28. The focus is zero-emission vehicles: battery electric and hydrogen fuel cell. A [PIB review dated 13 January 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2214039) describes the covered period as FY 2023-24 to FY 2027-28, with disbursement from FY 2024-25 to FY 2028-29, and says FY 2023-24 was the first performance year. Both statements are official. The practical reading is the one the review then documents: claims are paid a year after the performance year.

The same review states the rates: 13 to 18 percent for electric-vehicle and hydrogen fuel-cell components, and 8 to 13 percent for other advanced automotive technology components. There are two tracks, set out in the [23 September 2021 notification](https://pliauto.in/docs/press_release/2021-09-24%20PIB%20PR%20Govt%20notifies%20PLI%20for%20Auto_PressRelease.pdf). Champion OEM incentive is sales-value linked and applies to battery-electric and hydrogen fuel-cell vehicles. Component Champion incentive is sales-value linked and applies to advanced components, CKD and SKD kits, and aggregates across two-wheelers, three-wheelers, passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, and tractors. On 9 November 2021 the ministry notified 19 advanced-technology vehicle categories and 103 component categories, as recorded in a [later PIB note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2115609).

The gate is domestic value addition of at least 50 percent. As of 31 December 2025, eight Champion OEM applicants had DVA certificates for 94 variants, and ten Component Champion applicants had certificates for 37 variants. The review counts 82 approved applicants in all. Approval is the entry list. The certificate is what makes a variant payable.

## Cash so far, against the five-year estimate

The January 2026 review gives the five-year estimate attached to those 82 applicants: investment of ₹42,500 crore, incremental sales of ₹2,31,500 crore, and 1.48 lakh jobs. The original [September 2021 notification](https://pliauto.in/docs/press_release/2021-09-24%20PIB%20PR%20Govt%20notifies%20PLI%20for%20Auto_PressRelease.pdf) had estimated fresh investment of over ₹42,500 crore, incremental production of over ₹2.3 lakh crore, and additional employment of over 7.5 lakh. The investment estimate is stable across the two texts. The jobs estimate in the 2026 review is lower. This brief uses the later figure when it talks about the current official estimate, and keeps the 2021 figure as the launch estimate.

Achieved, as the review states them: cumulative investment of ₹35,657 crore and cumulative determined sales of ₹32,879 crore till 30 September 2025, with employment of 48,974. Claims of ₹322 crore for performance year FY 2023-24 were disbursed in FY 2024-25. Claims of ₹1,999.94 crore were disbursed for performance year FY 2024-25. Till 31 December 2025, incentives had been provided for 13,61,488 units: 10,42,172 electric two-wheelers, 2,38,385 electric three-wheelers, 79,540 electric four-wheelers, and 1,391 electric buses.

Investment reported is much closer to the five-year estimate than determined sales are. That is what a sales-linked incentive looks like in the early performance years. The cheque follows certified sales, not the capex announcement.

## Two neighbouring cheques

The same review covers the Advanced Chemistry Cell programme: ₹18,100 crore to build 50 GWh, approved in May 2021, seven years with two years of gestation. Investment attracted was ₹2,878 crore till 30 October 2025. The Scheme to Promote Manufacturing of Electric Passenger Cars, notified on 15 March 2024, requires an approved applicant to invest at least ₹4,150 crore within three years, reach 25 percent domestic value addition in that period and 50 percent within five years, and limits reduced-duty imports to 8,000 electric cars a year. Duty foregone per applicant is limited to ₹6,484 crore or the committed investment.

A portfolio that adds these three outlays into one "auto EV incentive" will double-count policy and miss the different clocks. PLI Auto pays on certified component and vehicle sales. The cell scheme pays on cell capacity. The car scheme trades a minimum investment and a rising local-content ratio for a limited import concession.
