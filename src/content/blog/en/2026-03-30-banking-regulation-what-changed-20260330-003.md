# What changed in India's digital lending and KYC rules

A founder taking a loan through an app, or a bank onboarding a customer by video, is now dealing with two dated Reserve Bank texts. One consolidates digital lending and adds a public app directory. The other changes when a KYC application may be refused.

## Digital lending, restated and extended

On 8 May 2025 the Reserve Bank issued the [Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848). They apply to digital lending by commercial banks, primary urban co-operative banks, state and central co-operative banks, non-banking financial companies including housing finance companies, and all-India financial institutions.

The Directions say they consolidate earlier instructions and add two measures. The [press release of the same day](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60403) names those additions: transparency when a lending service provider aggregates loan offers from more than one regulated entity, and a public directory of digital lending apps.

Most of the Directions took effect immediately. Paragraph 6, the multi-lender aggregation rule, took effect on 1 November 2025. Paragraph 17, the app directory, took effect on 15 June 2025.

Rules carried forward from the earlier guidelines still bind the lender:

- The loan is disbursed into the borrower's bank account. It is not sent to a lending service provider, except for cases the Directions list, such as a statutory mandate, a co-lending flow between regulated entities, or a specific end-use paid to the end beneficiary.
- Repayment goes to the regulated entity's bank account. A pass-through or pool account of a third party is not the repayment path. Cash recovery of a delinquent loan is a narrow exception, and that cash must be reflected in the borrower's account the same day.
- The borrower gets an explicit exit during a cooling-off period set by the lender's board. That period cannot be shorter than one day. Exit means paying the principal and the proportionate annual percentage rate, without a penalty. A one-time processing fee may be kept only if the Key Fact Statement disclosed it.
- A default-loss guarantee on a specified portfolio cannot exceed five percent of the amount disbursed from that portfolio.

The new comparison screen is practical. Where a service provider matches a borrower to more than one lender, the digital view of each offer must show the regulated entity's name, the amount and tenor, the annual percentage rate, the monthly repayment, and penal charges if they apply, plus a link to that lender's Key Fact Statement. The matching method has to be consistent for similarly placed borrowers and products, and it has to be documented.

## The app directory is a claim check, not a licence

Each regulated entity reports the digital lending apps it deploys or joins, including those of its service providers, on the Reserve Bank's Centralised Information Management System. The chief compliance officer, or another board-designated official, certifies that the data are correct and that the apps comply with the Directions. The Reserve Bank publishes what is submitted and does not verify or validate it. The press release states the same limit: the list helps a customer check an app's claimed association with a regulated entity. A customer complaint about an app is for the regulated entity to handle.

## KYC: a refusal now needs a reason

The [Master Direction on Know Your Customer, 2016](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=11566), updated as on 14 August 2025, still defines video-based customer identification. A video process that meets the Direction's standards is treated on par with face-to-face identification.

The [Know Your Customer (2nd Amendment) Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12893&Mode=0), also dated 14 August 2025, change the operating rule in five places:

- Persons with disabilities are named inside the class of disadvantaged customers the Direction already addresses.
- No application for onboarding, or for periodic updation of KYC, shall be rejected without application of mind. The officer records the reasons.
- Customer due diligence also covers an occasional transaction of ₹50,000 or more, whether one transaction or several that appear connected, and any international money-transfer operation.
- Aadhaar face authentication is included among the authentication methods in the digital-KYC explanation.
- A liveness check in video identification shall not result in exclusion of a person with special needs.

## What this does not decide

The Directions do not cap every interest rate, and they do not make the Reserve Bank a licensor of each app. A name on the public directory means a regulated entity reported it. Credit approval, pricing, and a decision to exit during the cooling-off window remain facts of the particular contract, which the Key Fact Statement is meant to show before the borrower signs.
