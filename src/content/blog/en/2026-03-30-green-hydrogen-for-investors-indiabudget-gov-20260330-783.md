# How to Read India’s Green Hydrogen Awards Without Mixing the Columns

Underwriting Indian green hydrogen means refusing to let the largest number on a page become the base case. The documents support four columns. A memo with one column is not diligence.

## Column one: aims

On 17 June 2026, Minister Pralhad Joshi said the Mission aims at 5 million tonnes a year of green hydrogen capacity, about 125 GW of renewables, investment exceeding ₹8 lakh crore, over 6 lakh jobs, and about 50 million tonnes a year of carbon emissions avoided. The Press Information Bureau note of 12 November 2025 states the same production, renewable, investment, jobs, and emissions language, and adds an expectation that fossil-fuel imports fall by more than ₹1 lakh crore. An aim can justify strategic interest. It cannot be the volume in a cash-flow model unless the contract in front of you adds up to it. The [mission page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/national-green-hydrogen-mission/) is the design document behind the aim. Its own outlay is ₹19,744 crore, which is the public scheme envelope, not the ₹8 lakh crore expectation.

## Column two: awards and who pays

Incentives for 8,62,000 tonnes a year of production, and for 3,000 MW a year of electrolyser manufacturing at 15 companies, are eligibility for support. Read the guideline for the tranche before you treat the award as revenue. The [schemes list](https://mnre.gov.in/en/hydrogen-schemes-guidelines/) dates manufacturing guidelines to 28 June 2023 and 16 March 2024, and Mode-2A and Mode-2B guidelines to 16 January 2024. Mode-2B refinery projects, 30 kilo-tonnes a year at Panipat, Bina, Vizag, and Numaligarh, are build-own-operate, with project-specific investment by the developers, according to the 11 August 2026 reply. The developer’s equity is real. The incentive is a term in the model. On 24 July 2026 MNRE added a model electrolyser-as-a-service agreement. If the stack is contracted as a service, the capex column moves. Read the agreement before you assume the owner is the offtaker.

Fertiliser ammonia is a price-and-quantity column of its own. The November 2025 note prints 7.24 lakh tonnes a year at ₹55.75 per kg. The June 2026 speech prints agreements for 6.7 lakh tonnes a year to 11 plants. Use the document that matches the contract you are shown. Do not average them.

## Column three: a place

Pudimadaka’s line in the 5 August 2026 annex is investment committed of ₹85,000 crore excluding renewable projects, with daily tonnages for ammonia, methanol, and aviation fuel, and hydrogen planned at 0.21 million tonnes a year by 2030. That is one proponent’s picture, as told to Parliament. Kandla’s 3,400 acres on a 30-year lease is a land position. Paradip’s ₹797.17 crore jetty is handling infrastructure on a public-private path, at 4.0 million tonnes a year of cargo capacity, which is not hydrogen production. Tuticorin’s 0.1, 0.22, and 0.5 million-tonne figures are expected capacities on allotments. Expected is the annex’s word. Hub recognition under Component B2, described by PIB on 10 October 2025, can arrive without direct financial assistance. Status is not a grant.

## Column four: commissioned, contracted, sanctioned

The minister cited JSW’s 3,600 tonnes a year as commissioned in November 2025. The August annex reports Kandla’s 1 MW demonstration plant complete at ₹14.1 crore, and Kandla’s 5 MW unit as a contract with an end date of 31 December 2026. Mobility has ₹41.582 crore released against about ₹410.77 crore allocated, as of 29 July 2026. Commissioned, contracted, and sanctioned are three words. A portfolio slide that picks one of them for every asset is not a portfolio.

Certification is a condition for many of these cash flows. If the project takes the incentive or sells in India, the November 2025 note says the final certificate is mandatory. Price the verifier and the 12-month average. A kilogram that misses 2 kg of CO2 equivalent is not the product the column assumed.

Great Indian Company publishes this reading order for investors who would rather mark each document to a column than inherit a national investment headline.
