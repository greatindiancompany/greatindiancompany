The Reserve Bank of India's release titled Payment System Indicators - August 2026 puts four volume columns and four value columns on one page. The pair that answers "what moved" is the August 2025 column and the August 2026 column. RBI marks the data provisional.

## UPI grew in count a little faster than in rupees

UPI volume was 2,00,083.10 lakh transactions in August 2025 and 2,45,089.58 lakh in August 2026. That is a rise of 22.5 percent. Value moved from ₹24,85,473 crore to ₹29,82,356 crore, a rise of 20.0 percent.

The average transaction is value divided by volume, after putting both in rupees. August 2025 works out to about ₹1,242. August 2026 works out to about ₹1,217. More payments went through, and the typical payment was slightly smaller.

Inside UPI, the USSD sub-line was 0.69 lakh transactions in August 2025 and 0.74 lakh in August 2026. USSD is already inside the UPI total.

For the full year labelled FY 2025-26, the same table shows UPI at 24,16,168.86 lakh transactions and ₹3,14,23,250 crore. That column is the financial year, not the month of August.

## IMPS handled fewer payments and more rupees

IMPS volume fell from 4,772.62 lakh to 3,599.42 lakh, a drop of 24.6 percent. IMPS value rose from ₹5,97,549 crore to ₹7,03,724 crore, a rise of 17.8 percent. The average IMPS payment therefore rose, from about ₹12,520 to about ₹19,551.

NEFT moved the other way on both measures. Volume went from 8,288.60 lakh to 8,825.02 lakh. Value went from ₹37,85,260 crore to ₹44,08,552 crore.

RTGS, the large-value system, also expanded. Volume went from 259.68 lakh to 315.19 lakh. Value went from ₹1,63,71,216 crore to ₹2,05,83,738 crore. UPI's lead in transaction count sits beside a larger RTGS value total, not in place of it.

## Cards split

Credit-card payments rose from 4,935.77 lakh to 6,324.47 lakh, up 28.1 percent. Value rose from ₹1,91,170 crore to ₹2,02,449 crore, up 5.9 percent. The average credit-card payment fell from about ₹3,873 to about ₹3,201.

Debit-card payments eased from 1,120.80 lakh to 1,038.65 lakh, down 7.3 percent. Value eased from ₹37,406 crore to ₹36,697 crore.

Cheque clearing on the CTS line managed by NPCI fell in count, from 446.03 lakh to 413.81 lakh, and rose in value, from ₹5,40,025 crore to ₹5,79,857 crore.

## Acceptance stocks in the same release

The infrastructure block is a stock, "as on" a date, and the unit is lakh.

Static UPI QR codes were 6,977.47 lakh as on August 2025 and 8,130.31 lakh as on August 2026, up 16.5 percent. RBI's note says this line includes only static UPI QR codes, and that the line was added from September 2020.

Bharat QR codes were 65.73 lakh and then 53.30 lakh. Reported PoS terminals were 119.65 lakh and then 102.08 lakh. The release warns that card and prepaid-instrument figures may not be comparable across periods because definitions have been revised. A lower PoS stock in this table is a reported stock, and it needs that note beside it before anyone treats it as a count of shops that closed.

## Where the rest of the movement lives

Domestic payment frauds in this release are a separate block, with their own coverage. August 2026 shows 3.43 lakh frauds and a value of ₹460 crore, against 2.40 lakh and ₹411 crore in August 2025. The [fraud brief](/blog/digital-payments-risk-watch-20260330-076) walks through the rate and the reporting boundary.

NPCI's UPI product statistics are the product series for the same system. The comparisons above use the RBI columns, which keep UPI, IMPS, NEFT, cards and RTGS in one unit system.

The [August 2026 number set](/blog/digital-payments-key-numbers-20260330-016) lists the level of each rail. The [August path since 2022](/blog/digital-payments-long-term-trend-20260330-046) shows which of these moves continue a longer run. Both sit on the [Great Indian Company brief index](/blog).

Figures are the August 2025 and August 2026 columns of Payment System Indicators - August 2026, except the fraud comparison, which uses Part V of that release. Great Indian Company did not re-collect the bank returns behind the table.
