# Future of Work in India: Notes for Policy Teams

## Three columns

A future-of-work note goes wrong when it adds three different objects into one jobs sentence. The environment ministry is the wrong file for this topic. The columns below use the labour ministry, the statistics ministry, and the skill ministry.

## Coverage

Column one is statutory coverage. Since 21 November 2025 the four labour codes have been in force, and a Lok Sabha reply on 20 July 2026 says Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026. The coverage rules include mandatory appointment letters, a floor wage, fixed-term benefit parity with gratuity after one year, ESIC described as voluntary under 10 employees and mandatory for hazardous work even with one employee, and a definition of gig and platform workers. Coverage is a statement about who the code reaches.

## Registration

Column two is administrative registration. e-Shram, launched by the Ministry of Labour and Employment on 26 August 2021, registers unorganised workers, including gig and platform workers. EPFO and ESIC administer organised-sector social security, and ministry materials say gig and platform schemes may be implemented through them. A registration stock measures how many records exist. It can rise after a registration drive whether or not the household survey moves.

## The survey

Column three is the household survey. The Periodic Labour Force Survey, from the National Statistics Office, estimates participation, employment, and unemployment. The annual report for January to December 2025 was issued on 27 March 2026. Language about what changed in the labour market belongs in this column, with the rate taken from the release and left there until the note quotes the table in full.

## Concurrent rules

The July reply states that states and Union Territories frame rules, that most had finalised or published them, and that a few had not finished. A central dashboard that implies uniform shop-floor practice overclaims. The appropriate government belongs in the note.

## Skilling as its own file

Guidelines for PMKVY 4.0 from the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship govern a training scheme for FY 2022-2026, with the National Council for Vocational Education and Training, the National Skills Qualification Framework, the National Skill Development Corporation, and Skill India Digital. Report training as training. NITI Aayog's June 2022 gig report recommended platform-linked skilling and recommended that PLFS identify gig workers. A policy team can follow that measurement advice and still publish gig headcount only from the survey table that contains it.

## A sentence that matches the record

The codes are in force, central rules were notified on 8 May 2026, state rules remain the concurrent variable, and employment levels are reported in PLFS. A jobs-created or jobs-lost figure that the 20 July 2026 answer did not contain, after Parliament asked for that figure, stays out of the note.
