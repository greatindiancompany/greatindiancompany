# Food Processing In India: A 90-Day Operating Checklist

## What ninety days can finish

Ninety days is long enough to confirm a licence, a supervisor, a cold-chain break, and an export recognition. It is not long enough to build a new cluster. This checklist is an operating pass. It does not predict a policy event inside the window.

## Week by week, in plain tasks

**Licence status.** On FoSCoS, confirm whether the unit holds a licence or a registration, whether the product categories on the certificate match what the plant actually makes, and whether a renewal or modification is due. Section 31 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 is the reason this is first. A sanction under a MoFPI scheme does not stand in for it.

**Supervisor and handlers.** Identify the food safety supervisor and the FoSTaC certificate. Check that onsite handler training has been done at least quarterly, which is the interval FoSTaC states, and that the record exists. If the certificate is missing, book the training. Do not wait for a visit.

**Cold chain on the route you use.** Write the steps from intake to dispatch: pre-cooling or the lack of it, the store, the vehicle. MoFPI's cold-chain design is an integrated path, not a room in isolation. Mark the step where temperature is nobody's job. If you depend on a hired chamber, name the operator and the product it is meant to hold.

**FPO or other supply.** If raw material comes from an FPO, write the product, the season, and who grades it. If you are the FPO, open the PMFME portal and read the district's One District One Product before you draft a common facility. The common-infrastructure page expects a project report, a State Nodal Agency, and capacity that others can hire. Ninety days can assemble that file. It should not assume a grant is approved inside the same window.

**Export, only if you export.** If the product is on APEDA's schedule, confirm exporter registration and, for fresh horticulture, whether the pack house you use is on the recognized list for that product and is inside its validity. If the product is marine, do not stop at APEDA. That schedule does not list marine products.

**Scheme window.** If a project still quotes PMKSY, re-read the page that dates continuation through 31 March 2026. That date has passed. Do not file as if this checklist extends it.
