# A 90-day checklist for food processing operators

This checklist starts on 24 September 2026 and runs about ninety days, into late December. It is built from windows and rules already published. It does not assume a new expression of interest will open, and it does not treat World Food India 2025, which was held in September 2025, as an event still ahead.

## Confirm which scheme year you are actually in

Demand No. 45 has voted 2026-27 budget estimates of Rs 915 crore for PMKSY, Rs 1,200 crore for the food-processing production-linked incentive, and Rs 1,700 crore for PMFME. The ministry's Year End Review of 9 January 2026 still describes the extra PMKSY outlay and the PMFME extension as running through 2025-26, while PLI runs through 2026-27.

Before spending money on a detailed project report, open the ministry site and the relevant scheme page and read the guideline dated for the current year. A budget line means the Centre has an appropriation. It does not, by itself, mean a fresh application round is open.

## If you already hold a PMKSY sanction

1. Log the approval date against the 24-month schedule for general areas or the 30-month schedule for difficult areas, as the cold-chain guidelines in the annual report state them.
2. Ask which instalment is pending on the scheme management system. The ministry built that system to show status from application through grant release, with email alerts when a proposal's status changes.
3. If the project is past its schedule, quantify the delay rule before you renegotiate with the bank: the annual report provides for a cut in the approved grant of up to 5 percent of the instalment for each month of delay.
4. Check that the claim matches what the grant covers. For cold chain, that is technical civil works and eligible plant and machinery, within a ceiling of Rs 10 crore.

## If you are in the PLI set

The scheme's last published implementation year is 2026-27. Selected companies file quarterly review reports on the PLI portal. In this quarter, match the sales you will report to the category on the approval: ready-to-cook or ready-to-eat including millets, processed fruits and vegetables, marine, mozzarella, the SME innovative or organic track, the millet component, or the branding-abroad track. Incentive already disbursed, Rs 2,162.553 crore across 161 cases as of the January 2026 review, is paid against eligible performance. A missed quarterly file is an operator problem, not a budget problem.

## If you are a micro enterprise

Use the PMFME portal and the district resource person list posted there. The September 2025 FAQ is the operating summary:

- The individual grant is 35 percent of eligible cost, capped at Rs 10 lakh, and it does not cover land or working capital.
- You contribute at least 10 percent. The rest is a bank loan. Only one person in a family (self, spouse, children) can take the assistance.
- A project report is mandatory. Machinery quotations need GST details. The premises can be owned, rented, or leased, and the lease should cover the loan period.
- An ODOP product is not required for the subsidy. It matters if you want branding and marketing priority.
- Self-help group members can seek seed capital up to Rs 40,000, with a cap of Rs 4 lakh for the group, released through the federation.

Track status on the portal after filing. A sanctioned loan is not the same as a released subsidy.

## Food safety in the same ninety days

If you manufacture or pack, confirm whether you need registration, a state licence, or a central licence under the Licensing and Registration Regulations, 2011. If you hold a state or central licence, check the FoSTaC cover: at least one certified food safety supervisor for every 25 food handlers on each premises. If you sell online, the Labelling and Display Regulations require the mandatory label information to be available to the buyer before sale, aside from batch and date fields. Do this before a festival-season listing, not after a failed inspection.

## Exports, if that is the channel

Pull the product line from APEDA rather than from a blended "processed food" headline. APEDA's 2024-25 tables, sourced to DGCIS, are specific: processed vegetables, cereal preparations, miscellaneous preparations, and the rest each have their own value. A buyer meeting is useful after the product's export number is known, not before.
