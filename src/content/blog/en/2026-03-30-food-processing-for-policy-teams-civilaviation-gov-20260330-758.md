# Food Processing In India: What State Teams Actually Align

## Alignment is a handoff, not a joint press note

State and central teams touch the same plant at different gates. PMFME puts a State Nodal Agency and a State Level Approval Committee in the path of a group facility, then MoFPI. FSSAI licensing is a food-safety function, filed on FoSCoS, not a scheme appraisal. APEDA registration and pack-house recognition are export functions. A state food-processing cell that tries to issue all three will duplicate bodies that already exist. This brief is about the handoff. It does not assign a new scheme.

## What the state nodal path owns

PMFME is a centrally sponsored scheme. For common infrastructure, the ministry page tells FPOs, self-help groups, and cooperatives to send a detailed project report to the State Nodal Agency. After the State Level Approval Committee approves, the nodal agency recommends it to MoFPI. The page prints a grant threshold above which the ministry must see the file, and it says training support can be written into the same report. Policy teams help by making that path legible: who the nodal agency is, when the committee sits, and which district product the file must serve.

One District One Product is the state's public pattern. The common processing facility is for that product. A state list that quietly substitutes a different crop, because a particular investor prefers it, fights the scheme text. Farm-gate sorting, grading, warehousing, and cold storage are the other asset the page names. Both are supposed to be open for others to hire for a substantial part of capacity. A policy review can ask whether approved facilities are in fact hireable. That question does not need a tonnage table.

## What the state should not reopen

PMKSY components still described as under implementation include cold chain, processing capacity, agro-processing clusters, food-safety infrastructure, research-side human resources, and Operation Greens. The page says Mega Food Parks and backward-and-forward linkages were discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with committed liability. State pitches built around a new Mega Food Park are asking for a component the ministry page has set down. The same page dates continuation through 31 March 2026. Policy notes should say that the printed window has ended, and should wait for a later MoFPI decision before telling applicants the umbrella is open.

Operation Greens gives states a perishable list to plan around, starting from tomato, onion, and potato and described as 22 products, with examples on the page. Use the ministry's list. Do not extend it by local preference and still call it Operation Greens.

## What stays with FSSAI and APEDA

State industry departments do not grant the section 31 licence. They can tell an applicant to use FoSCoS and to appoint a FoSTaC-trained supervisor. They do not register an exporter of a scheduled product. APEDA does, and it publishes regional offices, including in state capitals and trade cities, for the export community. Sending a horticulture exporter to the recognized-packhouse list is the useful handoff. Sending a marine exporter there, as if the APEDA fruit schedule covered fish, is not.
