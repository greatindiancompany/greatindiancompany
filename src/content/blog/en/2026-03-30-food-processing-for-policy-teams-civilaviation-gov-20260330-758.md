# How a policy team should read MoFPI's three schemes

A state or central policy team that treats "food processing" as one scheme will mis-assign both credit and delay. The ministry runs a central-sector infrastructure umbrella, a centrally sponsored micro-enterprise scheme, and a central-sector incentive for larger manufacturers. They share a ministry and a budget demand. They do not share an implementing agency or a success metric.

## Sort the schemes by who implements them

PMKSY and PLISFPI are central sector. The ministry sanctions, and for PMKSY it releases grant against a project. Applications are invited nationally. Parliamentary replies on the ministry site say the schemes are not reserved for a state. A state government can sponsor a project or clear a local permission. It does not receive a PMKSY block grant to reallocate.

PMFME is centrally sponsored. The FAQ of 9 September 2025 sets the share at 60:40 for most states, 90:10 for North Eastern and Himalayan states, and 100 percent central funding for Union territories without a legislature. The state nodal agency, the district resource person, and the lending bank are all on the critical path. A review meeting that asks only the ministry why a district has few sanctions will be looking at one of three counters.

One District One Product is the piece a state actually chooses. Products for 726 districts in 35 states and Union territories were picked from the local ecosystem and then approved. The state can recommend a change. The ministry approves it. Policy teams should keep that product list current, because branding and marketing priority follows it, while the capital subsidy does not require it.

## Sort components by whether new projects exist

Inside PMKSY, the Annual Report for 2024-25 is explicit. New sanctions continue for cold chain, processing and preservation capacity, agro-processing clusters, food testing laboratories, research and development, and Operation Greens. Mega food parks, backward and forward linkages, and the skill and HACCP activities continue only for liabilities already committed. A state proposal for a new park is a proposal against a closed component, however strong the local crop is.

The 31 July 2025 Cabinet decision, reported on 9 January 2026, added Rs 1,920 crore and took the 15th Finance Commission outlay to Rs 6,520 crore, with Rs 1,000 crore of that aimed at 50 irradiation units and 100 NABL-accredited labs. A policy team writing a state pipeline should tag irradiation and labs as the named new capacity, and should not assume every cold-chain idea fits that tag. Fourteen irradiation proposals were approved in 2025-26.

## Sort the numbers by what they commit the government to

Demand No. 45 for 2026-27 is the commitment of cash for the year: Rs 4,064.16 crore for the ministry, of which Rs 915 crore is PMKSY, Rs 1,200 crore is the food-processing PLI, and Rs 1,700 crore is PMFME. The 2025-26 actuals on the ministry's expenditure statement are the precedent for how an opening estimate can shrink. PMKSY spent Rs 570.02 crore against a budget estimate of Rs 903.38 crore. PMFME spent Rs 1,296.53 crore against Rs 2,000 crore. PLI spent essentially its full Rs 1,200 crore. A state brief that announces the budget estimate as "funds allocated to the state" is wrong on both the amount and the geography.

Physical targets need the same care. PMFME's target is 2,00,000 micro enterprises, and 1,72,707 loans had been sanctioned by the January 2026 review. Sanctioned is not commissioned. Incubation centres make that gap obvious: 76 approved, 23 commissioned. PMKSY's 1,618 approvals and 1,185 operational projects are the right pair to quote together. Investment of Rs 21,917 crore and more than 7.22 lakh jobs are described as expected on operationalisation. NABCON's waste-reduction findings are an evaluation of cold-chain projects, summarised by the ministry, not a national post-harvest loss rate.

PLI's 3.4 lakh employment figure is reported from the scheme. ASI's 22.96 lakh persons in registered food processing, for 2022-23, is a survey. Publishing them in one sentence without the labels will double-count people who may already be in the survey.

## What a useful state note contains

Name the nodal agency for PMFME and the sharing ratio that applies to that state. List ODOP products that are due for a change request, separately from units that only need the capital subsidy. For infrastructure, list projects by component and by status on the scheme management system, and mark mega food parks as legacy. Quote 2026-27 budget estimates as national appropriations. Attach the survey year to every employment and GVA figure. That note will be shorter than a scheme slogan, and it will survive contact with the budget.
