# Where Firms Already Sit on India's Digital Public Rails

## Opportunity means a counted on-ramp

The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 does not estimate a market size for private vendors on India's digital public infrastructure. It does count institutions and firms already on public rails. Those counts are the landscape. No total addressable market is added in this 23 September 2026 edit.

## Payments acceptance

The note says 691 banks were live on UPI. January 2026 volume was 21.70 billion transactions worth over ₹28.33 lakh crore. The same passage says the rail carried 81 percent by volume of domestic retail payments. The opportunity the note supports is acceptance, reconciliation, and merchant tools on a rail that already carries most retail digital volume. It does not name which apps hold that volume. The statistics are footnoted to NPCI.

## Open commerce and public buyers

ONDC, launched in 2022, had 1.16 lakh-plus retail sellers in 630-plus cities and towns as of December 2025. The note describes an interoperable network rather than a single marketplace. Participation is the counted fact. Share of national e-commerce is not.

Government eMarketplace, as of November 2025, had over 24 lakh sellers and service providers, including more than 11 lakh micro and small enterprises, against more than 1.67 lakh buyer organisations. Cumulative gross merchandise value exceeded ₹16.41 lakh crore. The note says micro and small enterprises account for 44.8 percent of cumulative order value and have received orders worth over ₹7.35 lakh crore. Selling to government through this marketplace is a documented channel. It is not a guaranteed order.

## Government APIs and consented financial data

API Setu, as of March 2026, had 2,559 publishers, 6,592 consumers, and 10,530 organisations around a catalogue of 8,036 APIs. That is a catalogue a firm can publish to or consume from. MeitY initiated the platform in March 2020.

The Department of Financial Services page, as of 31 March 2026, shows 179 financial information providers and 989 financial information users live on the account-aggregator framework, with 17 licensed aggregators. The page says consumer registration is voluntary and that data moves only with explicit consent. The progress figures are credited to Sahamati. The opportunity on record is consented data movement between regulated entities.

## Health, learning, and documents sit beside the catalogue

DigiLocker had 67.63 crore users as of 5 March 2026. eSanjeevani had served 45.42 crore patients. DIKSHA had 2.11 crore registered users. These are public systems with stated reach. A private product can sit beside them only where the operator allows an integration. The note does not list open commercial APIs for each one. API Setu is the catalogue that does publish a count. India Stack Global names the platforms. It does not grant access.

## What is not an opportunity figure

No vendor revenue and no unused-market percentage appear in the note. A landscape claim that needs those numbers is outside this brief.
