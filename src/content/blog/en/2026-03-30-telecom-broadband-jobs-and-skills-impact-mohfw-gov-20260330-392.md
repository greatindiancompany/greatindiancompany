# The Broadband Skills the Official Programmes Actually Specify

A jobs brief that prints a headcount the Department of Telecommunications has not published is worse than no brief. The Press Information Bureau notes and the TRAI subscription book used here describe tasks, service levels, and household targets. They do not publish a national telecom employment total, a wage, or a training-seat count. This page stops at the tasks.

## Keep the network up for ten years

The Amended BharatNet design, in PIB's 21 December 2024 note, includes operation and maintenance for ten years. Payment to the project implementation agency is tied to a service-level agreement. Uptime is watched from a centralised network operating centre. Fibre faults are watched with a remote fibre monitoring system at the block. Power backup of an adequate level is specified at gram panchayats and blocks. Those four clauses are a skills list: fibre splicing and restoration, network-operating-centre monitoring, power systems in places with weak grid supply, and contract management against an uptime number.

The 12 February 2026 mission update shows why the skills are scarce relative to the map. Optical fibre with 95 percent uptime had reached 42,000 villages by December 2025. The 2030 target is 2.7 lakh villages. Gram panchayats counted as online under Phases I and II, nearly 2.14 lakh of 2.56 lakh, are a much larger set than the uptime set. The distance between a lit panchayat and a network that stays up is an operating job, and it outlasts launch day.

## Install the last connection

Tamil Nadu's written numbers in that February 2026 reply make the last-drop skill concrete. The state had 10,869 service-ready gram panchayats and 808 fibre-to-the-home connections delivered through BharatNet. The amended programme there plans fibre-to-the-home for 5,27,506 households, implemented by the state over ten years. Household installers, drop-cable crews, and customer-support staff are the capacity between those two figures. Nationally, fixed wired broadband was 48.14 million lines at end-July 2026, beside 1,027.50 million mobile broadband lines. The install base for fibre into a home is still the smaller trade.

5G fixed wireless is the other install trade. TRAI counted 13.21 million such subscribers at end-July 2026, split 6.56 million urban and 6.64 million rural. A rural majority means the installer is often outside a metro. Unlicensed-band radio fixed wireless, 5.14 million lines and 71.07 percent urban, is a different crew mix. A roster built only for macro towers will be short of customer-premises radio skills.

## Run the permission and the tower power

Right-of-way disposal has fallen, on the national average, from 455 days to 30.4 days. Thirty-three of 36 states and union territories had implemented the 2024 rules by the February 2026 reply, and Tamil Nadu's average was still about 85 days. State portal staff, surveyors, and utility coordinators are part of broadband delivery. The mission also sets a 2030 target of powering 30 percent of mobile towers with sustainable energy, from a stated base of 12.38 percent. Tower-energy technicians sit in that gap. PIB does not say how many towers that percentage covers, so this brief does not convert it into posts.

Machine-to-machine connections, 137.70 million at end-July and led by Airtel with 61.33 percent, add a SIM-and-device skill that is separate from a consumer fibre install.
