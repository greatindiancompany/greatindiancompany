# Food Processing In India: Breaks In Cold Chain, Compliance, And Export

## Scenarios without odds

This map does not assign probabilities. It names failures that follow from how the rules are written. A cold chain that breaks, a licence that does not match the product, an export plant that is not recognized for the crop, and an FPO supply that was never graded are different events. They are often funded as if they were one project.

## Cold chain breaks

MoFPI's integrated cold-chain component is there because the loss happens in the gap: at the farm gate, in a store that is the wrong temperature, or in a truck that is not refrigerated. A scenario to write down is a pack house or pre-cooling step that was never built, with only a distant chamber on the drawing. Another is a hired vehicle that is insulated on the invoice and ambient on the day. Operation Greens names perishables, from tomato, onion, and potato outward to a wider list of 22. Those crops punish a gap faster than a shelf-stable grain. The risk is the missing link, not a national loss percentage this page refuses to invent.

## Compliance breaks

Section 31 means operating without a licence is not a paperwork delay. It is outside the statute, except where the petty-business registration route applies. A second scenario is a valid licence for a product the plant no longer makes. A third is a missing food safety supervisor, or a supervisor who has not trained handlers on site at the quarterly interval FoSTaC describes, and who has no record. Schedule 4 then fails in the inspection even though the certificate number exists somewhere in a folder.

## Export breaks

APEDA recognition for a horticulture pack house is product-scoped and time-bound. A scenario is a consignment of a fruit the certificate does not list, or a certificate read after its validity. Another is an organic claim that was not produced and packed under the National Programme for Organic Production, for which APEDA is the secretariat. A third is a marine shipment documented as if APEDA's fruit-and-vegetable schedule covered it. It does not. Meat has its own inspection function in plants and storage. Using a horticulture pack house as if it were a meat plant mixes two APEDA procedures.

## FPO and cluster breaks

PMFME common infrastructure is supposed to be hireable for a substantial part of capacity, and the processing line is for the district ODOP product. A scenario is a facility built for a crop the district list does not name, or a line reserved for the promoters so other units cannot hire it. Another is a project report that stops at the State Nodal Agency because the State Level Approval Committee has not moved it, while the plant has already ordered machines. Mega Food Park assumptions are a further scenario: the PMKSY page says that component was discontinued for the 15th Finance Commission cycle aside from committed liability.
