# Food Processing In India: Where Execution Usually Stalls

## Bottlenecks are gates that are already written

This review does not rank states by delay, and it does not invent a clearance time. The stalls that show up in the published procedure are enough to plan around.

## The group-facility sequence

On the PMFME common-infrastructure page, an FPO, self-help group, or cooperative prepares a detailed project report and sends it to the State Nodal Agency. The State Level Approval Committee approves, and the nodal agency recommends the proposal to MoFPI. The page says a proposal for a group grant above a threshold it prints must go to the ministry. Only after that approval does the page say the file moves to a financial institution for the loan. The grant is disbursed into the applicant's bank account after the loan is sanctioned.

Execution stalls when any one of those hands is treated as optional: a report without raw-material sources and a marketing channel, a file that never reaches the state committee, or machinery ordered before the loan exists. The page also says a substantial part of the capacity must be available for others to hire. A design that keeps the line captive fails that condition even if the civil work is finished.

The two facility types are narrow. Farm-gate premises for assaying, sorting, grading, warehousing, and cold storage, or a common processing facility for the district ODOP product. A project that is really a retail shop, or a crop the district has not listed, stalls because it is the wrong asset.

## The cold-chain shape

Integrated cold chain stalls when the proposal is a standalone chamber. Published cold-chain guidelines required farm-level infrastructure plus a distribution hub or refrigerated transport, and they said standalone facilities other than irradiation would not be considered. If the current PMKSY guideline still holds that shape, a single-room proposal will not move. Confirm the current text. Do not assume the 2016 wording is the live one, and do not assume the integrated idea has been dropped just because this brief will not pretend to be the guideline.

A second stall is the discontinued list. Mega Food Parks and backward-and-forward linkages are described as discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with committed liability. Teams still preparing those formats are in a queue the page says is not the live one. The PMKSY continuation date printed on the page, 31 March 2026, is itself a stall risk: the date has passed, and this brief will not invent a new window.

## Licence and pack house, after the concrete

Civil work can finish while FoSCoS still has no licence or registration for the products on the line. Export stalls one step later, when the horticulture pack house is not on APEDA's recognized list for that product, or the recognition has lapsed. Those are not MoFPI bottlenecks. They are the next gate, and they do not move because a scheme file moved.
