# What Policy Teams Can Track From Releases Already Out

Policy teams lose months asking for a new dashboard when the Department of Telecommunications, TRAI, and the Press Information Bureau already publish the series. The work is to track the series that answer different questions, and to stop substituting one for another in a cabinet note.

## Keep four BharatNet numbers in separate columns

From the 12 February 2026 reply and the 21 December 2024 design note, a state brief needs at least:

- Gram panchayats online under Phases I and II: nearly 2.14 lakh of 2.56 lakh, approximate historic cost Rs 42,000 crore.
- Villages with optical fibre at 95 percent uptime: 42,000 in December 2025, against a 2030 target of 2.7 lakh.
- Household fibre actually delivered. The national reply does not give one national fibre-to-the-home total. Tamil Nadu's written statement does: 808 connections provided, and 5,27,506 households planned under the amended programme.
- The amended programme's design envelope: Rs 1,39,579 crore, ring fibre to 2.64 lakh gram panchayats, on-demand fibre to about 3.8 lakh other villages, and ten years of maintenance.

A review that reports only the first bullet will describe a near-complete project. The mission scores the second bullet. Households live or fail on the third.

## Track the mission pairs, including the ones marked done

The 12 February 2026 pairs are the mid-term scoreboard: anchor institutions 68.8 percent toward 90 percent; fixed download speed 61.55 Mbps toward 100 Mbps; rural internet subscribers 47.16 per 100 toward 60; towers on sustainable energy 12.38 percent toward 30 percent; fibre mapping 94 percent toward 100 percent by March; right-of-way disposal 30.4 days, described as the 2030 marker already met, down from 455 days. TRAI's March quarter rural internet density, 48.31 per 100, is a second series with its own date. Publish both with dates. Do not replace the mission baseline with the TRAI quarter inside a target table, or the target loses its pair.

## Track permissions as a state list

The Telecommunications (Right of Way) Rules, 2024 have applied since 1 January 2025. Implementation was 33 of 36 states and union territories in the February 2026 reply. Tamil Nadu and West Bengal were named as pending, and Tamil Nadu's disposal time was about 85 days against the national 30.4. A central note that stops at "uniform charges" will miss the state notification. Digital Bharat Nidhi, created by renaming the Universal Service Obligation Fund under the Telecommunications Act, 2023, and administered under the rules notified in August 2024, is the funding column beside the permission column. Schemes such as BharatNet, uncovered-village mobile sites, and the island submarine cables sit in that fund. They should not be mixed into TRAI's adjusted gross revenue, which was Rs 86,716 crore for industry in the March 2026 quarter, with public-sector undertakings at 3.12 percent of access adjusted gross revenue.

## Use TRAI for the monthly operating picture

Press Release No. 116/2026 is the template for a monthly annex: total broadband, the wired and wireless split, 5G fixed wireless with its rural share, tele-density by licensed service area, and portability. Delhi at 368.33 percent and Bihar at 64.19 percent, with nine areas under the national 94.68 percent, are the inequality lines. TRAI's footnotes on which cities sit inside which area belong in the annex, or the inequality line will be challenged and should be.

The statute, the fund rules, and the permission rules are unpacked in the [policy explainer](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/telecom-broadband-policy-shift-explainer-mohfw-gov-20260330-152).
