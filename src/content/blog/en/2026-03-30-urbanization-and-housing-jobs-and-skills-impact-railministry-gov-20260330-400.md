# Jobs in Urban Housing: The One Official Work Figure, and Its Date

## What the housing ministry has counted

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs has not, in the releases used here, published an employment survey of urban construction. The National Statistical Office’s Periodic Labour Force Survey is the official household survey for work and unemployment, with an urban and rural split. This brief does not lift a rate out of that survey. A rate needs the specific PLFS quarter or annual report in hand, and a housing note that borrows one without the table is how bad figures travel.

The figure the housing record does contain is in the December 2024 PIB background note on PMAY-Urban. Beside 1.18 crore houses sanctioned and 88.32 lakh completed or delivered, the note says an estimated 917 crore person-days of work had been generated in construction, along with an estimated 570 lakh metric tonnes of cement and 130 lakh metric tonnes of steel. The note frames all of this as the experience of the preceding eight to nine years. It is an estimate attached to that stock of houses. It is not a monthly payroll, and it is not a forecast for PMAY-Urban 2.0.

The same note’s vertical split shows where the work sat: about 75 lakh houses under Beneficiary Led Construction, about 18 lakh under partnership and in-situ slum redevelopment, and about 25 lakh under the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme. Beneficiary-led houses are built by or for the household. Partnership houses are built as projects. Subsidy houses are purchased with a loan. The labour content is not the same, and the note does not split the 917 crore person-days across the three.

## Who the successor scheme names, without hiring them

PMAY-Urban 2.0, in that note, gives special attention to safai karamcharis, street vendors identified under PM SVANidhi, artisans under PM Vishwakarma, anganwadi workers, building and other construction workers, and residents of slums and chawls. Naming a group as a beneficiary focus is an eligibility decision. It does not publish a training seat or a wage. About 89 lakh houses under the first scheme were in a woman’s name or in joint ownership. That is a title outcome, which matters for who holds the asset, and it is still not a jobs count.

The July 2025 PIB reply describes the institutions that handled the closed subsidy: the National Housing Bank, the State Bank of India, the Housing and Urban Development Corporation, and then banks and housing finance companies. The Interest Subsidy Scheme continues to run through lenders. The employment effect inside those firms is not reported.

## Skills the other missions actually describe

The AMRUT 2.0 guidelines release of 27 October 2021 includes a capacity-building component whose audience is listed: elected representatives, municipal functionaries, contractors, managers, plumbers, plant operators, workmen, consultants, students, women, and citizens. The mission also says it will co-opt women and youth for concurrent feedback under a gig-economy model. Both lines describe a training and feedback design. Neither line states how many people were trained.

Smart Cities work, on MoHUA’s mission page, includes command centres in all 100 cities, SCADA monitoring of water networks in 31 cities, and RFID-enabled waste vehicles in 48 cities. Operating those systems is a municipal skill. The page reports assets and coverage. It does not report a headcount of operators. After financial closure on 31 March 2025, Advisory No. 27 of 10 June 2025 asks states to decide what the special purpose vehicles still do. Staffing follows that state decision.

Census 2027 will employ a large temporary enumeration workforce, and PIB has published the calendar and the ₹11,718.24 crore outlay. The housing content of Phase I is the condition of the house and its amenities. The enumerator count is a census operation, not a MoHUA housing-scheme job, and this brief does not guess it.

## A usable reading

Use 917 crore person-days as a dated ministry estimate of construction work on the first PMAY-Urban, and stop there. Use the AMRUT audience list as a hiring map for water and sewerage operations. Use the command-centre asset list as a map of systems that need operators after the mission budget has closed. For a wage, a vacancy, or an urban unemployment rate, go to the PLFS table or to a state recruitment notice, and cite that document by period.
