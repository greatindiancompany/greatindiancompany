# Food Processing In India: What A Founder Runs Day To Day

## The plant comes before the scheme

A founder in food processing runs a product, a licence, a floor, and a route to the buyer. MoFPI schemes can fund a piece of that. They do not choose the product for you, except where PMFME's district list gives a preference you should read before you lock a line. This note is the operating set. It leaves grant arithmetic on the ministry pages.

## Name the product against two lists

Write the product in the words the buyer uses, then check two public lists. FSSAI's standards and the categories on your FoSCoS application have to match what you manufacture. If you will export, check APEDA's scheduled products. Fruits and vegetables and their products, meat, poultry, dairy, bakery, honey, cereals, and the rest of that published list are APEDA's mandate. Basmati rice is in the Second Schedule. If your product is not on the list, APEDA registration is the wrong next step. Marine products are a PLISFPI segment and are not on the APEDA schedule. Do not file the wrong authority because a scheme page mentioned the sea.

If you are small and tied to one district, open the PMFME portal and read the One District One Product. A common processing facility under that scheme is for the ODOP produce. Building a different line and hoping the district label will stretch is how group projects stall.

## Hold the licence and the supervisor as weekly work

Section 31 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 is the founder's non-negotiable: no food business without a licence, unless you are in the petty category the regulations put on registration. FoSCoS is where you apply, renew, and modify. On the floor, appoint a food safety supervisor with a valid FoSTaC certificate. That person trains every handler on site at least quarterly and keeps the record, against Schedule 4 of the 2011 licensing regulations. Put the date of the last training where you can see it. A founder who cannot find that date does not have the system, even if the licence PDF is in email.

## Decide which asset you are actually building

If the failure is spoilage between harvest and dispatch, the asset is a cold chain: pre-cooling or a pack house near the crop, storage at the right temperature, and a vehicle that holds it. MoFPI lists that integrated chain as a PMKSY component. If the failure is that an FPO has produce and no shared grader or kettle, the asset is the common facility on the PMFME page, and it has to be hireable by others for a substantial part of capacity. If the failure is an export rejection risk on fresh produce, the asset is an APEDA-recognized pack house for that product, inside its validity.

Do not spend the next year on a Mega Food Park proposal. The PMKSY page says that component was discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with committed liability only. And do not treat the PMKSY window dated through 31 March 2026 as still open. That date has passed. Ask the ministry page what it says on the day you file.
