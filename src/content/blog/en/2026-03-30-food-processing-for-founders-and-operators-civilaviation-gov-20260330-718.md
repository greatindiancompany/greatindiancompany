# A founder's map of food processing support

The useful question for a founder is not whether food processing is a priority. It is which file matches the size of the unit, and which costs will still be yours after the ministry says yes.

## Start with the size test

The PMFME FAQ of 9 September 2025 draws a bright line. A micro enterprise, for this scheme, has investment in plant and machinery or equipment of not more than Rs 2.5 crore and annual turnover of not more than Rs 10 crore. Inside that line, the individual offer is a credit-linked grant of 35 percent of eligible project cost, capped at Rs 10 lakh. Eligible cost is plant and machinery plus technical civil work, and civil work has to stay under 30 percent. Land, rent, a leased workshed, and working capital are outside the grant. You put in at least 10 percent. A bank lends the rest. One person in the family can take it. You must be over 18. There is no minimum education requirement. The premises can be owned, rented, or leased if the term covers repayment.

That is a machine-and-shed subsidy tied to a loan, not a cheque for recipes, inventory, or a shop deposit. New units and expansions are both eligible. The project report is mandatory, and a district resource person is the ministry's designated help for that report and for the bank. Machinery quotes have to show GST.

If several producers need a packhouse, a cold room, or a common line, the group track is the same 35 percent with a ceiling of Rs 3 crore. Farmer producer organisations, cooperatives, self-help groups and their federations, and government agencies can carry it. Self-help group members who are processing food can also ask for seed capital up to Rs 40,000 each, and not more than Rs 4 lakh for the group, released through the federation.

## Use the district product only for what it actually unlocks

One District One Product is approved for 726 districts. You do not have to make that product to get the capital subsidy or the training. You do get priority in branding and marketing if you do. Switching the district's product needs a state recommendation and ministry approval, so do not hold a machine loan while that discussion runs. Twenty-seven marketing and branding proposals had been approved by the January 2026 year-end review, two of them national proposals for NAFED, which is a signal that branding files are few and formal, not an automatic add-on to every subsidy.

## If you are building a chain, you are in a different scheme

A cold store linked to the farm, a processing line above the micro cap, or a cluster of units looks to PMKSY, not to the Rs 10 lakh ceiling. The components still taking new projects are cold chain, creation or expansion of processing capacity, agro-processing clusters, food testing labs, research, and Operation Greens. The cold-chain grant stops at Rs 10 crore and pays only technical civil works and eligible machinery. In a general area you need equity of at least 20 percent and a term loan of at least 20 percent. Finish in 24 months, or 30 in a difficult area, or the instalment can be cut by up to 5 percent for each month you are late.

Do not apply for a new mega food park. That component is closed to new sanctions and open only for commitments already made. Irradiation can be a standalone cold-chain project. The 31 July 2025 Cabinet decision, as the January 2026 review describes it, includes support for 50 multi-product irradiation units and 100 NABL-accredited testing labs. Fourteen irradiation proposals were approved in 2025-26. Read the current expression of interest before assuming a slot remains.

## If you already have scale and a brand

The production-linked incentive runs through 2026-27 for firms that meet a minimum sales base and a minimum investment. The named segments are ready-to-cook and ready-to-eat foods, including millets, processed fruits and vegetables, marine products, and mozzarella, plus an innovative or organic track for smaller firms in those segments, and a separate grant for branding and shelf space abroad. Quarterly review reports are part of staying in the scheme. A founder under the PMFME turnover cap should not spend a season rewriting the business as a PLI applicant.

## Sell only what you are allowed to sell

Whatever the grant, the Food Safety and Standards Act still applies. Existing PMFME units are asked for their FSSAI licence in the file. New units still need one before sale. A state or central licence brings a training duty: at least one FoSTaC-certified food safety supervisor for every 25 food handlers on the premises. Pre-packaged food needs a label that meets the Labelling and Display Regulations, including the information a buyer must see before an online purchase.

The ministry's investor portal, Nivesh Bandhu, is the place it says it gathers state policies, raw-material clusters, and cold-chain locations. Use it to see what is already built near your crop. Use the scheme FAQ to see what you can ask the Centre to co-fund. Those are different pages, and a founder needs both.
