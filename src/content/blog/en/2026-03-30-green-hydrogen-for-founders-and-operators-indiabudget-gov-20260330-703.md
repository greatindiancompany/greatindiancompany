# What a Green Hydrogen Operator in India Has to Clear

An operator does not need the whole Mission on day one. The sequence in the official record is short, and skipping a step is how projects collect adjectives instead of permits.

## Call the product by the rule that governs the word

If you will call the output green in India, the 19 August 2023 standard applies. The [PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1950421) sets a well-to-gate ceiling of not more than 2 kg of CO2 equivalent per kg of hydrogen, stated as a 12-month average, counting water treatment, electrolysis, gas purification, drying, and compression. Electrolysis and biomass are both inside the definition. A plant that runs and misses the average has a failed label. It does not have a paperwork delay that a later letter will waive. The [mission page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/national-green-hydrogen-mission/) adds the power conditions the Mission actually wrote down: a waiver of interstate transmission charges for renewable energy used to make green hydrogen, renewable-energy banking, and time-bound open access and connectivity. Those are how electrons are supposed to arrive. They are not a promise of a rupee price per kilogram. No such price is stated on that page.

## Certify when the note says you must

The PIB note of 12 November 2025 says a final certificate is mandatory if you take a central or state incentive, or if you sell or use the hydrogen in India. MNRE’s [scheme notice](https://mnre.gov.in/en/notice/green-hydrogen-certification-scheme-of-india/) is dated 29 April 2025. The portal was launched on 17 June 2026. The Bureau of Energy Efficiency accredits the agencies that check you. Appointing that agency is an operating step, in the same family as appointing an auditor. The August 2023 release does not say the certificate replaces factory, environmental, or explosives permissions. Budget time for those as well.

## Name the buyer before you name the capacity

Fertiliser plants are the ammonia customers in the November 2025 note, at 7.24 lakh tonnes a year and ₹55.75 per kg, and in the June 2026 speech, at 6.7 lakh tonnes a year across 11 plants. If you cannot make ammonia, you are not in that contract. Four refineries have Mode-2B awards totalling 30 kilo-tonnes a year, at Panipat, Bina, Vizag, and Numaligarh, and the developer builds, owns, and operates. Steel, for now, is a pilot: ₹84 crore sanctioned for 100 percent hydrogen-injection trials, on the June speech. Road transport is 12 awarded pilots in the 29 July 2026 Lok Sabha annex, with reporting duties on efficiency and safety, not a national fleet tender. If you cannot name the plant, the port lease, or the consortium, you do not have a customer. You have a slide.

## Decide who owns the electrolyser

SIGHT pays eligible manufacturers. Fifteen companies hold incentives for 3,000 MW a year of factory capacity, on the June speech. If you would rather not own the stack, MNRE published a model electrolyser-as-a-service agreement on 24 July 2026. Read the allocation of uptime, power, and replacement in that file before you treat the phrase as a saving. The mission page says competitive bids are to use equipment the government has approved. A service contract that supplies an unapproved machine can lose the tender that justified the site.

## If you are at a hub, read the land rule for that hub

Kandla’s 3,400 acres are on a 30-year lease awarded by tender, in the 5 August 2026 annex. Paradip’s jetty is common infrastructure for hinterland plants, approved for public-private partnership at ₹797.17 crore, and it is not your private berth. Tuticorin’s expected tonnes sit on named allotments. Being “in a green hydrogen state” is looser than being on that land. In June 2026 the minister counted six dedicated state policies, seven integrated frameworks, and four still being finalised, and the release did not name the states.

Great Indian Company writes this sequence for founders who would rather clear a certificate and a buyer than collect mission adjectives.
