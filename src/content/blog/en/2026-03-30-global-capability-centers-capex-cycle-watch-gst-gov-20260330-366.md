# What Official Series Say About Captive-Hub Capital Spending

A capital-expenditure cycle is a sequence of buildings, fit-outs, equipment, and power connections. India's official publications around captive technology work measure three neighbouring things: foreign equity, economy-wide fixed capital formation, and the floor space STPI itself operates. A reader can watch those. A reader cannot yet watch a government series of GCC campuses.

## Equity is not a campus invoice

DPIIT's computer-software-and-hardware equity line, as placed in the Lok Sabha on 20 July 2026 from the March 2026 factsheet, was **USD 13,946 million** in 2025-26 after **USD 7,814 million** in 2024-25. The earlier factsheet, updated to March 2025, shows **USD 9,394 million** in 2022-23, then **USD 7,973 million**, then **USD 7,814 million**. Fresh equity into the sector slowed for two years and then jumped.

That jump can fund an Indian company. It can also be a share purchase, a holding structure, or hardware. It is compiled from remittances the Reserve Bank reports to DPIIT. It is not a survey of fit-out contractors. Reinvested earnings, which are how an established captive often pays for the next floor, sit outside the equity column. On the March 2025 factsheet, total FDI inflow in 2024-25 on the wider definition was **USD 81,043 million**, against equity of **USD 50,018 million**.

Gross FDI of **USD 94.84 billion** in 2025-26, cited by the Ministry of Finance in that Lok Sabha answer, is larger again, and net FDI was **USD 6.95 billion**. A capex story that uses only the gross number will overstate the capital that stayed.

## Economy-wide capital formation is a wide bucket

MoSPI's provisional estimates put real GDP growth at **7.7 percent** in 2025-26 and real GVA growth at **7.9 percent**. On the expenditure side, both private final consumption and gross fixed capital formation grew more than **7.5 percent** in the year. In the January–March 2026 quarter, real GFCF grew **10.8 percent**. The same note says manufacturing, trade and transport, and financial, real estate, and professional services reached double-digit growth at constant and current prices.

Information technology is inside "Financial, Real Estate, IT, Professional Services & Ownership of Dwelling". At constant 2022-23 prices that group is **₹77,09,678 crore** in the 2025-26 provisional estimate, up **10.4 percent**, after **10.0 percent** and **5.5 percent** in the two prior years. The base-year value, identical in constant and current prices, is **₹60,18,587 crore**. Ownership of dwellings and financial services are in the same cell. A 10.4 percent print is not a technology-campus cycle.

## The floor space the government does publish

STPI's annual report for 2024-25 is concrete because the society is describing its own estate:

- **17 lakh sq ft** of IT-grade infrastructure, including **5.60 lakh sq ft** of raw incubation space and more than **4,200** plug-and-play seats.
- **411** incubatees as on 31 March 2025.
- New centre space at Koraput (**10,000 sq ft**) and Jajpur (**41,200 sq ft**), plus a list of centres still being built, from Agra and Gorakhpur to Jamshedpur and Balasore.
- Five data centres, at Chennai, Bengaluru, Mohali, Bhubaneswar, and Vijayawada, of about **55,000 sq ft** and about **700** racks, serving **99** customers as on 31 March 2025.

This is public digital infrastructure and incubation. Private captives lease and build a much larger stock that this report does not inventory. STPI's export total of **₹10,69,270.59 crore** (tentative) tells you where registered software exporters book exports. It does not tell you the square feet they occupy.

## Why the cycle can look quiet in the accounts

The RBI software survey for 2025-26 shows **91.7 percent** of software services exports delivered off-site, **US$ 203.0 billion** of **US$ 221.4 billion**. Off-site delivery scales with people, networks, and software more than with a new shell. A captive can raise export-like activity, in the survey's sense, with limited balance-sheet capex, and a year of strong computer-software equity can equally be something other than towers. Watching one series as "the GCC capex cycle" will call a cycle that the other series does not confirm.
