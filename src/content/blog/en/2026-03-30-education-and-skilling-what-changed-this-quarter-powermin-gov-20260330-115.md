## The quarter in three replies

From 23 September 2026, the quarter that had just closed is July–September 2026. Three written parliamentary replies in the first half of August are the dated education and skilling statements this brief uses. They are not a new Cabinet policy. Each one answers a specific question.

On 3 August 2026, the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship told the Lok Sabha that Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) 4.0 is demand-driven, that no state-wise training targets have been set, and that the scheme is extended for two years beyond 31 March 2026. The same reply did not describe a PMKVY 5.0 guideline.

On 10 August 2026, the Ministry of Education told the Lok Sabha that the All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE) 2023-24 puts the higher-education gross enrolment ratio at 30, up from 23.7 in 2014-15, and total enrolment at 4.50 crore, up from 3.42 crore. It also said more than 700 universities and over 7,500 affiliated colleges had adopted the National Credit Framework (NCrF).

On 12 August 2026, the skill ministry told the Rajya Sabha that, as on 30 June 2026, more than 5.44 lakh candidates had been trained under PMKVY 4.0 in listed new-age sectors, and that Rs 414.86 crore had been utilized for future and new-age job roles.

## What did not change in these replies

The National Education Policy 2020 remains the standing school and higher-education policy. Its targets, including a 50% higher-education gross enrolment ratio by 2035, were not replaced. The February 2025 Cabinet decision on the Skill India Programme, with an outlay of Rs 8,800 crore for 2022-23 to 2025-26, was not restated as a new outlay in August.

These replies also do not publish a placement rate, a state ranking, or a 2026 figure for public education spending as a share of GDP.

## How to read the extension line

The 3 August reply says PMKVY 4.0 “is extended for two years beyond 31.3.2026.” That sentence sits next to the earlier Cabinet window, which ran the composite Skill India Programme through 2025-26. Readers should keep the two statements apart: one is the February 2025 outlay period, the other is the August 2026 extension of PMKVY 4.0. This brief does not convert the extension sentence into a different end date.
