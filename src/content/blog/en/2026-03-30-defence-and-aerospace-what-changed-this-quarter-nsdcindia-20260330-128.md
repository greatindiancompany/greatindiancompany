# The sixth positive indigenisation list is the defence change on the record

## The notice

On 18 August 2026 the Department of Defence Production notified a sixth Positive Indigenisation List of 405 items. The Press Information Bureau release puts the estimated business potential at Rs 3,070 crore. Sixteen items belong to the Indian Coast Guard. The other 389 belong to defence public sector undertakings.

The list is made of line replaceable units, sub-systems, sub-assemblies, spares, components, and raw materials. The release names the platforms those parts sit on: the Advanced Light Helicopter, the Light Utility Helicopter, the Su-30MKI, the Light Combat Aircraft, the AL-31FP engine, the T-72, the T-90, the BMP-II, warships, the Konkurs-M, Invar, and MRSAM missiles, plus radars, sonars, fire-control systems, satellite communication, and high-explosive anti-tank ammunition. Those names are categories of work. They are not a performance claim, and they are not an order.

## What the list does

Each item carries an indicative indigenisation timeline. The release says that after indigenous development succeeds, the item will be procured from Indian industry. Until that happens, a line on the list is an offer, not a closed import ban and not a contract.

The route named in the notice is practical. Defence public sector undertakings and the Coast Guard indigenise through their Make procedure and through in-house development, with industry participating, and with MSMEs named in particular. The item-level list is on the SRIJAN portal at srijandefence.gov.in.

## The longer count in the same release

SRIJAN opened in August 2020 so that defence public sector undertakings and service headquarters could offer items to industry. The 18 August 2026 release says that, up to June 2026, the portal had offered more than 33,000 defence items. That pool includes 5,012 items notified under the first five positive indigenisation lists.

The same release says more than 15,700 defence items have been indigenised, with an estimated import-substitution value of about Rs 9,000 crore over five years. It also says defence public sector undertakings had placed procurement orders, including in-house production, of about Rs 10,000 crore on domestic vendors up to March 2026.

## Do not add a second official count

A PIB factsheet covering the position till May 2026 describes 10 positive indigenisation lists, five from the Department of Military Affairs and five from the Department of Defence Production, covering 5,521 items. It also says that, as of May 2026, SRIJAN listed over 41,000 vendors and 2.7 lakh products.

Those sentences use different units from the August release: lists and vendors on one page, items offered and items indigenised on the other. Adding 405 to 5,521, or adding vendors to items, produces a number neither page published. Open both pages and keep their dates.

## What did not change in this notice

The August release does not publish a jobs total, a foreign-investment inflow, an export figure, or a capital-budget outturn. Those sit in other Ministry notices. It also does not describe classified programmes.
