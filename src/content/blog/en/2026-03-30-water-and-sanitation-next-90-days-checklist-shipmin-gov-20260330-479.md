# What to read on the water dashboards over the next 90 days

From 24 September 2026, the next 90 days run to late December 2026. Nothing in the official record used here schedules a new Cabinet decision inside that window. The useful work is to re-read four public systems against the last dated print, and to notice which question each system is even able to answer.

Do not expect these pages to confirm a rumour. If a figure is not on them, it is not yet on this record.

## Rural taps

Open the [JJM India dashboard](https://ejalshakti.gov.in/jjmreport/jjmindia.aspx) and write down the as-on date in the header before you write down a percentage. The last two national prints in this brief are around 15.80 crore households (81.61 percent) of 19.36 crore on 10 March 2026, and more than 15.90 crore (82.19 percent) of the same denominator on 4 August 2026. A later screen is comparable only if the denominator and the status word are the same.

On that visit, separate three village marks: entered on the information system, reported by the state department, and certified by the Gram Sabha. JJM 2.0 says certification now waits on in-village maintenance. If the dashboard has not yet added a column for Jal Arpan, do not treat a 100 percent connection mark as a handover.

Then open the [state-wise financial report](https://ejalshakti.gov.in/JJM/JJMReports/Financial/JJMRep_StatewiseAllocationReleaseExpenditure.aspx). Ask whether FY 2026-27 releases have moved beyond the 6 August 2026 print: ₹5,289.09 crore reimbursed to ten states, and ₹865.87 crore through SNA SPARSH across 13 states, inside a reported total of ₹6,154.96 crore. The narrative of that print is the [6 August PIB note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295509).

## Village waste

Open the [SBM-G dashboard](https://sbm.gov.in/sbmgdashboard/) and record its as-on date. The 11 June 2026 cut on that system was 5,86,944 SBM villages, 5,69,020 ODF Plus, of which 5,07,634 Model, 3,463 Rising, and 57,923 Aspiring. The question for this quarter is whether Aspiring villages are moving into Rising or Model, not whether the ODF Plus total has crept up by a few thousand. A PIB feature of 17 September 2026 already stated a later round number, 5.25 lakh Model villages inside 5.69 lakh ODF Plus villages. Check whether the dashboard's integer for Model has caught up with that feature, and whether the feature and the dashboard are using the same day.

## Urban projects

Open [amrut.mohua.gov.in](https://amrut.mohua.gov.in/) and the state approved-project view the ministry cited in Parliament. The 9 July 2026 balances to beat, or to see unchanged, are 7,687 projects awarded with capex of ₹1,37,130.13 crore, and physically completed works of ₹66,472 crore, against 8,777 approved projects costing ₹1,97,191.85 crore. Track water-supply projects and sewerage projects as separate rows. A rise in park projects is not a rise in sewage treatment.

## What not to do with the printout

Do not average the March household count with the August household count and call it a trend. Do not convert the IIM Bangalore and ILO person-year estimate into jobs created this quarter. Do not treat the ₹8.69 lakh crore JJM outlay as cash that must appear on the financial report by December 2026. The outlay runs to December 2028.

If a page has no as-on date, do not cite it next to a page that does.
