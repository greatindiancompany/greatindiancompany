# A 90-Day Reading List Before an API or Biotech Decision

Ninety days is enough to build a decision file. It is not enough to invent a window that the Department of Pharmaceuticals has not opened. The checklist below is a reading order. Where a scheme is invite-based, the schemes index states the invitation date. If that date has passed and no later invitation is posted, the honest status is closed until a new notice appears.

## Days 1 to 30: name the instrument

Open the Department of Pharmaceuticals schemes page and write down which row you are actually in.

If the product is a critical key starting material, intermediate, or API, open the bulk-drug PLI row: the 21 July 2020 gazette, the 29 October 2020 guidelines, later corrigenda, and the approved-applicant list. The 6 February 2026 Lok Sabha reply describes this scheme as greenfield production of 41 identified products. Confirm the molecule is on the current eligible list in the latest invitation or guidelines, not only on the 53-product cabinet list from March 2020.

If the product is a biopharmaceutical, a complex generic, a patented or near-expiry drug, or an API outside that notified bulk-drug list, open the pharmaceuticals PLI row instead. The gazette is 3 March 2021. The guidelines are 1 June 2021. The 2021 application window announced with SIDBI ran from 2 June to 31 July 2021. A 2026 decision uses whatever corrigendum is current. It does not reuse a closed window.

If the need is a plot with shared effluent treatment and solvent recovery, open the bulk-drug park row and the February 2026 reply. Only Gujarat (Jambusar, Bharuch), Himachal Pradesh (Haroli, Una), and Andhra Pradesh (Nakkapalli, Anakapalli) are approved. Write to the state implementing agency named in that reply — GIDC, Himachal Pradesh Bulk Drug Park Infrastructure Ltd., or Andhra Pradesh Bulk Drug Infrastructure Corporation — and ask whether plots are being allotted and when the common facility you need is contracted. The reply itself shows those facilities at different stages of tender and construction.

If the need is a lab bench, a pilot plant, or a bio-incubator, leave the Department of Pharmaceuticals page. Read the Department of Biotechnology’s National Biotechnology Parks Scheme note and BIRAC’s incubator map. Those are scaling and incubation addresses. They are not bulk-drug park allotments.

If the need is research funding rather than a sales incentive, open the PRIP row. The schemes page lists a gazette notification dated 1 October 2025 and scheme guidelines. Read them before describing PRIP as a production subsidy.

## Days 31 to 60: open the licence file

Create or recover the SUGAM account and match the product to a permission type. CDSCO states that SUGAM is the route for manufacture, import, and clinical-trial permissions under the clinical-trial rules, and for biologicals including vaccines and recombinant products. New-drug and clinical-trial applications follow the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, which CDSCO hosts with checklists and FAQs.

In parallel, identify the state drug controller for the manufacturing site. State authorities licence a large share of manufacturing and sale. SUGAM can hold licence details issued by state FDAs; that does not mean the state stopped being the licensing authority. Download G.S.R. 922(E) of 28 December 2023 and mark which parts of revised Schedule M the site does not yet meet. That gap is a capex and a timeline item.

For a clinical trial or a bioavailability study, confirm the ethics committee and the six-monthly SUGAM status report the 2019 rules require. Put the report dates on a calendar now.

## Days 61 to 90: write what you still do not know

Record four blanks rather than filling them from memory. One: the date of the approval letter, if any, and the product name on it. Two: whether any Department invitation on the schemes page is still open on the day you check. Three: the state agency’s written stage for effluent treatment, steam, and power at the park, if a park plot is in the plan. Four: which licence is central and which is state. A blank with a document still to request is a better board paper than a borrowed export rank.

Price control is a separate check if the molecule is scheduled. The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority administers that regime. A PLI award does not set a ceiling price.

The regulatory split behind steps 31 to 60 is explained in [the CDSCO brief](/blog/pharma-and-biotech-regulatory-outlook-mnre-gov-20260330-434). Great Indian Company publishes the reading order so a quarter’s work starts from the Department’s index and the regulator’s portal: [greatindiancompany.com](https://greatindiancompany.com).
