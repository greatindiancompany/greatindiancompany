# India DPI for Policy Teams: Who Holds Which System

## Assign the system before the target

Policy teams inherit a stack that already has owners. The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 and the Digital India pages split those owners. A single mission dashboard is not what these sources describe. This 23 September 2026 edit does not create one.

## MeitY and the programme frame

Digital India, launched on 1 July 2015, is a MeitY programme. The about page states three vision areas: digital infrastructure as a utility to every citizen, governance and services on demand, and digital empowerment of citizens. The pillars page sets out nine original pillars, from broadband highways and public internet access through e-governance, e-Kranti, and IT for jobs.

DigiLocker and API Setu are MeitY systems in the March note. DigiLocker launched in 2015 and had 67.63 crore users as of 5 March 2026. API Setu, initiated in March 2020, showed 8,036 APIs and 10,530 organisations in the note's March 2026 catalogue. That catalogue is the published measure of data exchange through the government API platform. India Stack Global, on the Digital India site, is the outward showcase of 15 platforms, not the operating ministry for each one.

## Identity, payments, and transfers

Aadhaar counts in the note are footnoted to UIDAI: more than 144 crore numbers as of March 2026, and over 2,707 crore authentications in 2024-25. UPI counts are footnoted to NPCI: 21.70 billion transactions in January 2026 and 691 banks live.

Direct Benefit Transfer and the Public Financial Management System are the welfare-payment machinery. The note says PFMS was mandated in December 2014 for payment, accounting, and reporting under Direct Benefit Transfer. The stated results are cumulative transfers crossing ₹49.09 lakh crore as of January 2026, and reported savings of more than ₹4.31 lakh crore from 2015 to March 2024. Jan Dhan accounts, 57.71 crore as of March 2026, are the account end of that pipe.

## Sector ministries keep their systems

eSanjeevani and the national non-communicable disease platform sit with health in the note. The disease platform is operational in 31 states and union territories, with 74.97 crore beneficiaries enrolled as of December 2025. DIKSHA sits with NCERT and the Ministry of Education and is described as adopted by almost all states and union territories. POSHAN Tracker sits with the Ministry of Women and Child Development, delivered through the National e-Governance Division, with 14.03 lakh anganwadi centres onboard as of January 2026. e-Courts sits with the Department of Justice. Phase III has a stated outlay of ₹7,210 crore for 2023 to 2027.

## What a policy team still does not have

The national note has no state table for Aadhaar, UPI, or DigiLocker. Comparable execution data would use one definition, one as-of date, and one row per state. The 24 country cooperation agreements, dated February 2026 in the note, are an external workstream. They do not substitute for that missing state table. The nine Digital India pillars name access and jobs as programme aims. They are not current state scores.
