# Jobs and skills inside India's manufacturing trade diversification

Diversifying a supply chain hires people only where a task moves. Official notes describe that mechanism. They do not, in the documents this brief uses, publish a single national jobs total for "supply chain diversification." Repeating a round number from a slide would be a guess. The useful reading is which tasks the policy expects to create, and which skills a plant has to budget for itself.

## What the PLI note actually says about work

The 2 August 2023 PIB note, from the Minister of State for Commerce and Industry, says the schemes have the potential to boost production, employment, and growth over roughly the next five years. It also says the 14 sectors were chosen in part because they could multiply employment, exports, and wider gains. "Potential" and "could" are the verbs in that reply. There is no headcount next to them.

The concrete labour structure in the same note is the vendor cascade. Anchor units are expected to build a supplier base along the value chain, with most ancillary units in the MSME sector. Of 733 applications selected at that date, 176 MSMEs were beneficiaries in bulk drugs, medical devices, pharma, telecom, white goods, food processing, textiles, and drones. That is a map of where shop-floor work and tool-room work are supposed to sit: not only inside the anchor, but in the firms that feed it. An anchor that imports the same kit it used to import has not created that second layer of jobs.

Sectors differ. Technical textiles, food processing, and white-goods assembly pull different skills from specialty steel or advanced chemistry cells. A jobs claim that adds them into one "manufacturing employment" line hides the training problem.

## Skills the logistics and mineral notes name

The National Logistics Policy, launched on 17 September 2022, treats people as part of the logistics system rather than as a footnote to roads. A PIB progress note on that policy says implementation runs through the Comprehensive Logistics Action Plan, which includes human resource development alongside digital logistics systems, asset standardization, state engagement, and export-import logistics. The same note says the policy emphasizes training and capacity-building with the Capacity Building Commission. It does not count drivers, warehouse staff, or customs clerks hired.

Critical minerals add a thinner, more specialized layer. The PIB note of 24 March 2025 on the National Critical Mineral Mission says the mission includes provisions for skill development and research support, next to Rs 500 crore for processing parks and Rs 1,500 crore for recycling. Processing and recycling are the jobs that turn a mined or recovered material into an input a cell plant or electronics line can use. Exploration jobs and processing jobs are not the same occupation.

Trade diversification adds a clerical and quality skill that factory tours skip. A preference under an agreement in force is claimed with a certificate of origin. Someone in the plant or the customs desk has to know the origin rule. DGFT's job is to run that system. A firm that hires only assemblers and nobody who can document origin will miss the trade half of the strategy.

## What a founder should budget

Budget for three benches, and do not convert them into a national statistic. First, the anchor's own technicians and quality staff for the PLI product. Second, the MSME vendors' tooling and process skills, because that is the cascade the 2023 note describes. Third, logistics and origin documentation, because a part that cannot move or cannot claim a preference is not a diversified source. If a later PIB note publishes a jobs figure for a named scheme, cite that note and its date. Until then, leave the cell blank.
