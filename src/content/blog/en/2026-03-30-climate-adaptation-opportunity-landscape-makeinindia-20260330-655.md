# The Climate Adaptation Opportunity Landscape in India

An opportunity, on these pages, is a defined public task. It is not a market-size claim. No document cited here prices an Indian adaptation industry.

## A district that has to act on a hot day

The National Disaster Management Authority's October 2019 guidelines are about preparing a heat action plan: warnings, health response, and public advice. Its heat page points readers to cool-roof material for house owners. The India Meteorological Department supplies the declaration rule. The 6 April 2021 colour table, carried on the Press Information Bureau, steps a day from no action, to be updated, to be prepared, to take action. A useful product is one a district can run on those days. A dashboard that cannot say which colour it serves is not yet that product.

## Water, soil, and animals

The National Water Mission and the National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture, as the environment ministry's FAQ of 1 December 2021 describes them, are the demand side: efficiency, over-exploited areas, soil and moisture, and location-specific farming. A PIB note of 5 February 2018 adds animal husbandry, forestry, and tourism to the adaptation fund's eligible list. Where public project money is the buyer, the path is a state executing entity under a NABARD-implemented file. Check the ministry's [approval list](https://moef.gov.in/index.php/list-of-approvals-projects-under-nafcc-concept-note-and-project-documents-details-dpr) before assuming a tender. The fund was made a non-scheme in November 2022.

## Coastal work is already named

The 3 February 2022 PIB note does not invite a generic coastal platform. It names tasks: integrated farming in the Kaipad wetlands of north Kerala, habitat and livelihood work in the Gulf of Mannar, and heat and cyclone practice for dairy animals on the Andhra coast. Adjacent work has to fit the Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019, and the mainland hazard line. If a proposal cannot say which of those constraints it meets, it is not yet a project.

## Knowledge is a mission, not a private index

The National Mission on Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change, in the December 2021 FAQ, is the slot for climate science, regional modelling, and adaptation strategy in agriculture, water, and health. That is a research task. It does not authorise a private index to stand in for IMD or NDMA.

The test before a pitch: name the mission or the guideline, name the state file if you need public money, and say which hazard's rule you are implementing. The public signal underneath that test is the [market-signal brief](/blog/climate-adaptation-market-signal-brief-makeinindia-20260330-215). Great Indian Company leaves the landscape as tasks a reader can open, not as a funnel. See [greatindiancompany.com](https://greatindiancompany.com).
