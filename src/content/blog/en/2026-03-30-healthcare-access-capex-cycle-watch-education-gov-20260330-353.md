## A mission with a published window

The Pradhan Mantri Ayushman Bharat Health Infrastructure Mission is the capital programme that sits under the access story. The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare describes it as a centrally sponsored scheme with some central-sector components, and states an outlay of ₹64,180 crore for 2021–22 to 2025–26. The purpose, in that update, is capacity across primary, secondary, and tertiary care so the system can answer outbreaks and disasters as well as ordinary illness. Public health and hospitals remain a state subject. The ministry’s role is technical and financial support. A sanction in Delhi is not a building in the district.

The same update says administrative approvals for those five financial years total ₹33,081.82 crore. The physical lines attached to that sum are specific: 10,609 building-less Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, 5,456 urban mandirs, 2,151 block public health units, 744 integrated public health laboratories at district level, and 621 critical care hospital blocks. That is the capital cycle in one paragraph. It is an approval list. It is not a completion list.

## What each line is for

Building-less mandirs are the blunt constraint. The primary-care design asks sub-centres and primary health centres to deliver twelve groups of services, including non-communicable-disease screening and basic emergency care. A centre reported as operational on the ministry portal can still be a building the PM-ABHIM list treats as missing. The ministry’s 25 March 2025 reply counted 1,76,573 mandirs operationalised till February 2025. That count and the 10,609 building-less approvals answer different questions. One is a functioning-status report from states. The other is a construction sanction. Both can be true.

Urban mandirs matter because the original Health and Wellness Centre design covers rural and urban primary health centres, and because city outpatient care is where private clinics already sit. Block public health units are the layer between the clinic and the district. District laboratories are how a sample stops travelling for want of a local bench. Critical care blocks are how a district hospital holds a patient who would otherwise be referred to a medical college or an empanelled private ICU.

Indian Public Health Standards 2022 are the quality benchmark for district and sub-district hospitals. A new block that does not meet those standards is square footage. The standards portal says a facility needs a score of 80 percent or higher to be counted compliant, and that states committed to compliance in 50 percent of facilities by 2025–26, with a Central Council goal of all institutions by 2026. This page does not report the score.

## The window has a date

FY 2025–26 ended on 31 March 2026. This brief was updated on 24 September 2026. The outlay cited above is the outlay the ministry published for that scheme period. It is not an automatic appropriation for the year now underway. Anyone watching the capital cycle should look for a successor provision in budget papers and for a restatement of the approval figures, and should keep sanctions, releases, and commissioned beds in separate columns. Inventing the next outlay because the last one was ₹64,180 crore is how infrastructure memos go wrong.

Releases to states follow proposals and the year’s resource envelope. The ministry has said implementation uses the National Health Mission’s institutions for the centrally sponsored components. A state that does not put the work into its programme plan does not get the building by national announcement alone.

## Why private hospitals should read a public capex list

PM-JAY pays empanelled public and private hospitals at par for packages both are allowed to perform. A critical care block or a district laboratory changes what the public hospital can do before a patient reaches a private provider. Primary-care buildings change how many people are screened before anyone is admitted. Capital at the bottom and the middle of the system is an access fact and a competitive fact. The [investment note](/blog/healthcare-access-investment-implications-education-gov-20260330-233) uses that point. Staffing the buildings is the [jobs note](/blog/healthcare-access-jobs-and-skills-impact-education-gov-20260330-393).
