Regulation of an Indian flight, and of the trip after it, is a stack of safety rules, security rules, airport charges, traffic rights, and entry conditions. An outlook that names only the tourism ministry will miss the layer that stops the passenger. This page does not forecast a new rule.

## The stack, in the order a trip meets it

1. **Directorate General of Civil Aviation.** Civil Aviation Requirements, the air operator certificate, airworthiness, and crew licensing. No certificate, no scheduled flight. The National Civil Aviation Policy of 2016 does not stand in for a current requirement.
2. **Bureau of Civil Aviation Security.** Screening and the airport security programme. A building that has not met this layer does not open the lane.
3. **The airport operator and air navigation.** The Airports Authority of India operates many airports and provides air navigation services. Some major airports are run under concession instead. The conditions of use are what the airline accepts. They are not a destination brochure.
4. **Airports Economic Regulatory Authority.** Aero tariffs at major airports. The order is the charge. A note about passenger experience is not.
5. **Traffic rights and slots.** Air services arrangements for international flights. Seasonal slots where the field is constrained. UDAN awards for supported regional routes, with conditions and an end date.
6. **Entry of the guest.** Visas, including the e-Tourist visa category, are a home-affairs and immigration function. Some hill, border, island, and wildlife areas add a state or forest permit. A Ministry of Tourism circular waives neither.
7. **The stay.** Hotel star classification by the Ministry of Tourism is voluntary. Fire, local, and food-safety permissions are not. The monument gate is controlled by the Archaeological Survey of India or by the temple or state authority that actually holds it. Goods and services tax on the ticket and the room depends on that supply. This page states no rate.

Aircraft leasing, including structures used at GIFT City, sits beside this stack. A lease does not substitute for an operator certificate, and deregistration of an aircraft is its own procedure.

## A review without a prediction

Each quarter, check whether any document you already use has a new requirement, a new charge, or a new permission. Record that no change was observed when that is true.
