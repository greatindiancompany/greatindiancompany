# A sourced snapshot of India's food processing

This page is a ledger. Each figure is only as good as the document and the year next to it. Where two official texts disagree, both are shown. Nothing here is a forecast.

## The sector

From the Ministry of Food Processing Industries Annual Report 2024-25, unless another source is named:

- Average annual growth of food processing over the nine years ending 2023-24 was about 6.55 percent, against about 4.43 percent for agriculture and allied activities, at 2011-12 prices.
- In 2022-23, food processing was 7.93 percent of manufacturing GVA and 8.45 percent of GVA in agriculture, at 2011-12 prices.
- The Year End Review of 9 January 2026 says GVA rose from Rs 1.34 lakh crore in 2014-15 to Rs 2.24 lakh crore in 2023-24, on first revised estimates.
- Registered factories in 2022-23: 42,801. Persons engaged: 22.96 lakh, or 12.41 percent of registered manufacturing employment. The January 2026 review cites ASI 2023-24 for a later organised-employment share of 12.83 percent, without a new headcount.
- Unincorporated food processing in 2022-23: 46.57 lakh workers, 15.21 percent of unregistered manufacturing employment.
- Women workers in 2022-23: 2.17 lakh in registered units, and 11.25 lakh in unincorporated food processing.
- FDI equity inflow from April 2014 to March 2025: US$7.33 billion, as stated in the Year End Review. The annual report states the policy: 100 percent FDI on the automatic route for food processing, subject to sectoral rules, and 100 percent on the government route for trading, including e-commerce, in food manufactured in India.
- More than 72.28 percent of food products are in the 0 percent or 5 percent GST slabs, as the annual report puts it.

## Exports, two baskets

- Agri-food exports including processed food were US$46.43 billion in 2023-24, about 11.7 percent of merchandise exports of US$395.39 billion (annual report).
- Processed food's share of agri-food exports rose from 13.7 percent in 2014-15 to 20.4 percent in 2024-25 (Year End Review).
- APEDA's processed value-added products were US$8,222 million in 2024-25, 28.67 percent of APEDA product exports of US$28,682 million (APEDA administrative report 2024-25, DGCIS-based). Largest lines: miscellaneous preparations US$1,477 million, cereal preparations US$934 million, processed vegetables US$897 million, pulses US$855 million.

## The three schemes

PMKSY. Launched 3 May 2017. Original outlay Rs 6,000 crore for 2016-20, extended to 2020-21. Annual report figure for the restructured 15th Finance Commission cycle: Rs 5,520 crore. Cabinet decision of 31 July 2025, as reported on 9 January 2026: Rs 6,520 crore for 2021-22 to 2025-26, including an additional Rs 1,920 crore, of which Rs 1,000 crore is for 50 irradiation units and 100 NABL-accredited testing labs. Projects approved since component launches: 1,618, of which 1,185 operational, with processing and preservation capacity of 270.51 lakh metric tonnes.

Cold chain, as on 31 December 2024: 394 projects taken up, 286 completed, 108 under implementation. Combined project cost Rs 11,466.38 crore, private investment Rs 8,495.53 crore, grant-in-aid Rs 2,970.85 crore. Completed capacity included 8.46 lakh metric tonnes of cold storage and related stores, and 1,477 reefer vehicles. Grant ceiling Rs 10 crore a project.

PMFME. Outlay Rs 10,000 crore. Target 2,00,000 micro enterprises. Extended up to 2025-26. Loans sanctioned: 1,72,707, including 56,543 since January 2025. Individual support: 35 percent credit-linked grant, ceiling Rs 10 lakh. Seed capital released since inception: Rs 1,282.98 crore to 3.76 lakh self-help group members. ODOP approved for 726 districts in 35 states and Union territories.

PLISFPI. Approved 31 March 2021. Outlay Rs 10,900 crore. Period 2021-22 to 2026-27. Approved proposals: 170. Reported investment: Rs 9,702 crore. Reported employment: 3.4 lakh. Incentives disbursed: Rs 2,162.553 crore in 161 cases.

## Money voted for 2026-27

Demand No. 45: ministry budget estimate Rs 4,064.16 crore, about 13.79 percent above the 2025-26 revised estimate of Rs 3,571.57 crore. Scheme budget estimates: PMKSY Rs 915 crore, PLI Rs 1,200 crore, PMFME Rs 1,700 crore.

The ministry's own 2025-26 statement shows actual expenditure of Rs 570.02 crore for PMKSY, Rs 1,199.96 crore for PLI, and Rs 1,296.53 crore for PMFME, against a ministry actual of Rs 3,275.32 crore.

## Production ranks, 2021

From Agriculture Statistics at a Glance, 2023, as printed in the annual report: first in milk, pulses, and dry onion; second in vegetables, fruit, rice, and wheat; third in total cereals; fifth in meat.
