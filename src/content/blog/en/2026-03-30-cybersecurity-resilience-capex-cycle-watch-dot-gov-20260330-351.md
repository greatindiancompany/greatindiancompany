Capex in India's cybersecurity resilience is equipment, a lab, a log platform, or a staffed operations room that is commissioned and then used. A cycle is that use across more than one incident or audit. This page does not say a cycle is underway, and it quotes no project cost.

## Assets tied to a control

- **Security testing before connection.** Spend on a product that must clear a security test under Indian Telecom Security Assurance Requirements before it joins a telecom network. The National Centre for Communication Security is the Department of Telecommunications office for that test. The shelf is [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/). The asset earns its keep when a named product passes and is connected. A lab photograph is not the pass.
- **The trusted-source path.** Procurement limited to trusted products from trusted sources under the National Security Directive on the Telecommunication Sector. The portal workflow is an operating cost. It is capex only when it changes which equipment you buy and install.
- **Detection and logs.** A platform that keeps the logs the applicable direction asks you to keep, and that can produce them when an incident is reported. Storage that nobody can retrieve is a cost.
- **The staffed watch.** Seats on the shift when the incident happens. A building called a security operations centre, with no roster, is not the asset. The people are on the [jobs page](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-jobs-and-skills-impact-dot-gov-20260330-391).
- **Audit closure.** Tools bought to close a licence security-audit finding. The asset is the closed finding, dated. The purchase order is not.

Nomura research pages were paired with an earlier draft. They are not a capex register. The link is omitted.

## What would show a cycle

Three observations together, across more than one incident or audit: spend you can place on a system, commissioning, and a record that the control ran. A foundation stone for a "cyber hub" without the third observation is an announcement. Support from a ministry can change who funds the asset. It does not create a passed test.

For each asset write the owner, the system, the instrument that required it, the design capacity, and the last date it was used. Unknown stays unknown.
