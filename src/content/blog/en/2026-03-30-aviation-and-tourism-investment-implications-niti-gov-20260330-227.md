An investment in Indian aviation or tourism underwrites one clock. An aircraft lease, an airport concession, a supported regional route, and a hotel do not share a payback. This page draws that distinction. It offers no valuation and no return.

## Four clocks

- **Scheduled airline.** Cash goes out on leases or owned aircraft, crew, fuel, and airport charges before the ticket is flown. The air operator certificate and the slots are the permission. A busy quarter can sit next to a lessor who has not been paid. Working capital is the operation.
- **Major-airport concession.** The asset is the concession term and the traffic that uses the runway. Aero charges at a major airport follow an Airports Economic Regulatory Authority order. Retail and parking are a different revenue. An airline that cuts the city is a volume risk you do not control. A new terminal that flights do not use does not earn the order's assumed throughput.
- **UDAN or another thin route.** Support under the Regional Connectivity Scheme is attached to a route, conditions, and a capped fare on part of the seats. The support has an end. When the award stops, the aircraft can still have a lease. Treat the route as a contract with a date, not as a permanent annuity.
- **Hotel or tour product.** Keys, a houseboat, or a camp earn money inside a season. Domestic guests and foreign guests are different pipes, and the foreign guest also needs a visa the hotel does not issue. A commission to an online agent, or a permit the guest fails, can take the rate you modeled. A star classification from the Ministry of Tourism is not occupancy.

Leasing an aircraft through an Indian structure, including arrangements at GIFT City, is a financing choice. The financing does not create a slot or a guest. If a lessee fails, deregistration and repossession are a Directorate General of Civil Aviation and contract question. This page does not describe any lessor's position.

## What the model has to show

Name the payer, the season the cash goes out, and the permission that can stop the asset: the certificate, the slot, the tariff order, the visa, or the local licence. A scheme sanction that is not in the bank account is not revenue. The [capex page](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-capex-cycle-watch-niti-gov-20260330-347) is the asset list.
