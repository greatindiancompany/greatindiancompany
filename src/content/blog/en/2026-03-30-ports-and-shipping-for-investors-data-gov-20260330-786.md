# Ports and shipping for investors: how the assets differ

An investor who buys "Indian maritime" as one theme is buying unlike cash flows. A landlord port, a private terminal, a listed shipping company, a yard subsidy, and a recycling plot do not share a cycle. The ministry pages are enough to separate them. They are not enough to value them. Nothing below is a return estimate.

## The port asset is a board, a concession, or a company

Eleven of the operating major ports are port authorities under the Major Port Authorities Act, 2021. They set their own tariffs for services and for access to land. Their accounts follow the rules notified under the Act. You do not buy the authority. You buy a concession inside it, or you lend against one. Since 13 August 2025, a dispute with the authority, or a stressed concession the Centre or the board refers, goes to the Adjudicatory Board for Major Ports in Mumbai. The old tariff authority, which used to sit between the concession and the price, has ceased. Tariff risk now sits with the port's own rate decision and with the contract. That is cleaner, and it is also less cushioned by a national regulator.

Kamarajar Port Limited is the exception the Indian Ports Association labels as a company. It is grouped with the major ports for operations and it is not the same legal form as Deendayal or Paradip. Read the company, not the sector average.

Volume evidence for these ports is the association's traffic series, including the April-to-July 2026 note posted on 6 August 2026. Use the file. A Sagarmala capacity sentence aimed at 2025 is the wrong denominator for a 2026 valuation. So is a single all-India tonne that mixes containers at Jawaharlal Nehru with iron ore at Paradip and petroleum at Deendayal.

Evacuation is part of the asset even when it is not on the port's balance sheet. If the rail exit is a project of Indian Port Rail and Ropeway Corporation, the concession's dwell time depends on a joint venture formed in 2015. Model the siding.

## The ship asset and the yard asset

The Shipping Corporation of India is a listed central enterprise, incorporated in 1961, headquarters in Mumbai, Navratna from 1 August 2008, with the shipping division stating a government holding of 63.75 percent. Around it sit different vehicles: a GIFT City subsidiary incorporated on 12 August 2024, a land-and-assets company listed from 19 March 2024, and Inland and Coastal Shipping Limited, which operates river services and the Jal Vahak incentive. The fleet paragraph on the same ministry page is dated 30 April 2019. Do not use that vintage as the corporation's current deadweight. Infrastructure status, notified on 19 September 2025, matters to anyone financing an Indian-flag ship of 10,000 gross tonnage or more, or a ship built in India of 1,500 gross tonnage or more. It is a classification for lenders. It is not a freight rate.

Yard investment is subsidy math plus a dock. The July 2026 reply sets out ₹24,736 crore of vessel assistance, a ₹25,000 crore maritime fund with a manager appointed on 26 May 2026, and ₹19,989 crore for clusters and brownfield capacity. Three clusters are in-principle, in Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu. Four brownfield proposals are in-principle, at an estimated cost the reply puts above ₹2,500 crore. In-principle approval is not an asset in service. The 51 private yards among the ministry's count of 61 are where most equity would actually sit. Assistance of 15 to 25 percent changes the bid. It does not remove the cost gap the scheme itself is written to offset.

Alang is a recycling asset with a compliance screen. The shipping division locates nearly 98 percent of Indian ship recycling there and says 115 of 131 allotted plots are Hong Kong Convention compliant. A credit note equal to 40 percent of scrap value, in the December 2025 scheme design, is relevant only if the scrap happens in India and a new build follows. Plot compliance is the underwriting question.

Sagarmala Finance Corporation, a Reserve Bank-registered maritime non-banking company since 19 June 2025, is a financier in this landscape. It is not a diversified port stock.
