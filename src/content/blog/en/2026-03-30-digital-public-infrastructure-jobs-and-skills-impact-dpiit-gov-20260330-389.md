# Jobs and Skills on India's Digital Public Infrastructure

## Two platforms, one pillar

Digital India's pillars page lists IT for Jobs among the nine pillars of the programme launched on 1 July 2015. The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 describes two operating systems under education and skilling. It does not publish a jobs-created total. This brief does not add one. The 23 September 2026 update does not invent a placement rate.

## DIKSHA is the school-learning rail

DIKSHA, launched in 2017, is an initiative of NCERT under the Ministry of Education. The note says almost all states and union territories use it, along with central boards including CBSE. As of 5 March 2026 the note records 566 crore learning sessions, 2.11 crore registered users, 18.52 crore course enrolments, 14.71 crore completions, and 12.69 crore certificates issued.

Those are learning counts. A certificate on DIKSHA is not, in the note, a placement into a job.

## Skill India Digital Hub joins existing labour portals

The note says the Skill India Digital Hub launched in 2023 to combine online training, skill credentials through APIs, payments, and job discovery. It says the upgraded platform integrates Udyam, e-Shram, the National Career Service, and ASEEM. It describes government-to-citizen, business-to-citizen, and business-to-business services, and says institutions can adapt curricula to workforce needs.

No enrolment, completion, or placement figure for the hub is printed in this note. The honest reading is that a national skills window now points at portals that already exist. It is not a measured employment effect.

## Adjacent systems that are not job statistics

UMANG carries exam results and scholarship services among more than 2,400 services. The note's 723.36 crore UMANG transactions, as of 5 March 2026, are all-service transactions, not hires. Government eMarketplace's more than 11 lakh micro and small enterprise sellers are firms on a procurement rail, not a count of employees. UPI's January 2026 volume of 21.70 billion transactions is commerce, not employment.

India Stack Global lists DIKSHA among the platforms showcased for other countries. A place on that list does not add a jobs number.

## The labour reading that stays inside the note

The published skills fact is the reach of a national learning platform, plus a hub that joins enterprise, worker, and career portals. A wage, vacancy, or placement effect is outside the note. Digital India's about page says the programme works with agencies, institutions, and industry on digital skills. That sentence is a programme aim. It is not a headcount.
