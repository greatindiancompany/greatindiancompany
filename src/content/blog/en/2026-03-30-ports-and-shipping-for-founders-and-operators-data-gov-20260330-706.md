# Ports and shipping for founders and operators on the coast

An operator on the Indian coast is usually selling one of four things: a berth service, a coastal or river movement, a ship repair or a new hull, or software that sits between them. The ministry pages will not write your customer list. They will tell you who can say yes.

## If you want a berth or waterfront

Decide first whether the water is a major port or a state port. The twelve operating major ports are under port authorities, except Kamarajar, which the Indian Ports Association lists as a limited company. The Ports Wing names them. Everyone else, among about 200 non-major ports, is a state maritime board or state government matter, and only around 65 of those handle cargo. A pitch deck that says "minor ports are easier" has skipped the landlord.

At a major port, the board sets the tariff for services and for access to land. The old central tariff authority closed on 13 August 2025. Your concession dispute, if you are a public-private operator or a captive berth user, goes to the Adjudicatory Board in Mumbai. Read the model concession path and the published policies before you price a bid: land policy, berthing policy, and the policy for waterfront and associated land for port-dependent industry. The master-plan and scale-of-rates rules are how the port is supposed to apply money and publish prices.

Ask where the cargo goes after the hook. Sagarmala's connectivity pages exist because a modern berth with a weak rail exit does not earn. Indian Port Rail and Ropeway Corporation is the last-mile rail company. Your model needs that siding, or a road that is not theoretical. For performance, the port will be judged on turnaround, berth output, detention, and dwell. Those are the numbers a concessionaire lives with after the ribbon-cutting.

## If you move coastal or river cargo

Coastal shipping and inland waterways are a Sagarmala pillar, and they are operationally a ship problem. Certificates and manning sit with the Directorate General of Shipping in Mumbai, an attached office since 1949. The cargo the programme talks about is coal, cement, steel, foodgrain, fertiliser, and petroleum, in parcels large enough to beat a truck. You need a berth that will take a coastal ship, bunkers, and a receiver.

River cargo has a named public operator and a named incentive. Inland and Coastal Shipping Limited, owned by the Shipping Corporation, implements the Jal Vahak scheme announced on 15 December 2024, with a 35 percent incentive for cargo owners on inland waterways. If your customer can claim that incentive, say so. If you are only moving coastwise between two major ports, do not quote it.

The shipping division prints a per-tonne cost comparison that does not clearly separate water, rail, and road. Do not put that sentence in a customer proposal. Use a real pair of ports and a real parcel.

## If you build, repair, or connect systems

There are 61 shipyards on the ministry's count, 51 of them private. Assistance of 15 to 25 percent per eligible vessel is milestone-based under guidelines issued in December 2025, with domestic-content conditions in the schedules. Read the schedules. A series order is treated more generously than a one-off, according to the 27 December 2025 note. Public buyers are being asked to pool demand through the ministry's technical committee. That pool is not a guarantee you will be in it.

Repair is the nearer service if you cannot finance a newbuilding dock. The development scheme can support brownfield docks and shiplifts at 25 percent. In-principle is not a purchase order. The July 2026 reply's four brownfield proposals and three clusters tell you the Centre has started a list. They do not tell you that your yard is on it.

If you sell systems, the Indian Ports Association's Port Community System and National Logistics Portal Marine are the installed base at major ports. Integrate with those systems.
