# Future of Work in India: Execution Bottlenecks in the Labour Codes

## Unfinished steps

The four labour codes came into force on 21 November 2025. A Lok Sabha reply on 20 July 2026 says Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026. The bottlenecks below are unfinished steps described in that reply and in the ministry's own account of schemes and records. This page grades no ministry and no state with a score.

## State rules

Labour is concurrent. The 20 July 2026 reply says most states and Union Territories had finalised or published rules, including pre-published drafts, and a few were still pre-publishing or finalising them. For a worker or a firm still on the unfinished side of that sentence, the operative detail is the hold. The ministry said on 21 November 2025 that existing rules continue during transition. That hold is a reason to read the local text, and the code still applies.

## A defined gig worker and a payable scheme

The Code on Social Security defines gig and platform workers. The ministry's note structures an aggregator contribution as 1 to 2 percent of annual turnover, capped at 5 percent of the amount paid or payable to those workers. The July reply says the definition exists so that schemes for listed benefits can be formulated. The distance between a definition, a contribution clause, and a scheme a worker can draw is the bottleneck. e-Shram, launched on 26 August 2021, can register unorganised workers, including gig and platform workers. A registration record completes a benefit payment only when the scheme says so. The current scheme list on the labour ministry's site is the check.

## Letters that stop at the contractor's gate

The ministry says appointment letters are mandatory and that fixed-term employees receive benefit parity and gratuity after one year. In many workplaces the person at the site is on a vendor's roll, the principal employer holds a purchase order, and no letter states wages and social security. The ministry's contract-worker note places health and social-security duties on the principal employer. Execution stalls when that duty is treated as the vendor's private file.

## A certificate and a hire

PMKVY 4.0, under the Skill India Programme for FY 2022-2026, runs training on National Skills Qualification Framework courses, with the National Council for Vocational Education and Training, support from the National Skill Development Corporation, and Skill India Digital as the record. For IT, business-process, and platform hiring, the stall is the match between that certificate and the work an employer will appoint someone to do. The labour codes do not perform the match. The Periodic Labour Force Survey can show how people with vocational training sit in the workforce. Those tables stay in the survey. A training-management system is not a substitute for it.

## One return, many old back offices

The ministry describes single registration, one pan-India licence, and one return. Payroll, HR, and licence workflows built for multiple filings are the practical stall. The simplification reaches the establishment when those systems follow the appropriate government's notified rule.
