## Use the next planning cycle to re-read primary pages

A ninety-day corridor checklist is a set of documents to open, not a forecast of announcements. The pages below are the ones that moved the public record through 2025 and 2026. If a newer reply appears on the same site, replace the figure. If it does not, keep the dated figure.

## Rail

Open the 7 August 2026 Rajya Sabha reply and write down four facts before any vendor pitch: Eastern corridor 1,337 km commissioned, Western corridor 1,506 km commissioned, about 443 trains a day, and Dankuni–Surat still in preparation and updation of its detailed project report. Then open the 19 March 2025 reply and mark the three reports that were under examination and not sanctioned: Kharagpur–Vijayawada, the Palghar–Dankuni east-west alignment, and Vijayawada–Nagpur–Itarsi. Ask whether any later Railways release has sanctioned one of those three. Silence means the March 2025 status still stands.

Check DFCCIL's own milestone page for the 31 March 2026 JNPT–Vaitarna trial, so the operator's account and the minister's account are both in the file. Check the cargo-terminal line in the August reply: 142 terminals, about ₹10,000 crore private investment, 224 million tonnes a year of estimated capacity, 146 million tonnes handled in 2025-26. If a shipper names a terminal, ask which of those 142 it is, and whether it sits on dedicated track.

## Ports, waterways, and roads

Open the 27 March 2025 Sagarmala note and record 839 identified projects, about ₹5.79 lakh crore, and 272 projects completed as of 19 March 2025 at about ₹1.41 lakh crore. Note the five pillars, and that port-connectivity enhancement is one of them. Open the 1 February 2026 Budget summary and record the proposal to operationalise 20 national waterways over five years, starting with National Waterway 5 in Odisha from the Talcher and Angul mineral belt and Kalinga Nagar to Paradeep and Dhamra, plus the proposed Coastal Cargo Promotion Scheme and the 6 percent to 12 percent modal-share aim by 2047.

Open the 18 December 2024 Bharatmala reply for port and coastal roads: 18 projects, 424 km awarded, 189 km constructed, in the named coastal states. Open the 15 February 2022 park note only as history: 35 parks identified, Jogighopa then under construction, bids invited at Nagpur, Chennai, and Bengaluru. Then check whether a later MoRTH or port release has a completion date for the park you care about. The September 2025 DPIIT note does not supply that date.

## Commerce layer

Open the 16 September 2025 DPIIT note for the services layer: Unified Logistics Interface Platform above 160 crore transactions as of August 2025, Logistics Data Bank above 75 million containers and 101 inland container depots, E-Logs at 100 of 140 issues resolved, and the NCAER cost study still in its final stages. Do not fill the cost blank with an older private estimate. Open the September 2023 IMEC memorandum if the conversation is westward, and the 16 April 2025 roundtable if someone quotes 30 percent and 40 percent. Those percentages belong to the minister's statement, filed as an expectation.

## A pass-fail for any pitch

A credible corridor pitch in this cycle can point to a commissioned asset, a sanctioned kilometre, a completed Sagarmala project, or a numbered PIB reply. A pitch that needs the Dankuni–Surat sanction, a finished logistics-cost percentage, or an operating IMEC sailing is ahead of the documents on this list.
