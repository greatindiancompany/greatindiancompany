# How a founder should read the water missions before building

The programmes will not meet you as one customer called "India water". They meet you as a state engineering department, a Gram Panchayat, a Village Water and Sanitation Committee, or an urban local body. The official pages tell you which of those is in front of you, and which claim you are not yet allowed to make.

## Start with the stage, not the percentage

On the [JJM dashboard](https://ejalshakti.gov.in/jjmreport/jjmindia.aspx), a household connection is entered after the head of household's name and Aadhaar are verified. A state department then reports that water is being supplied to households, schools, and anganwadi centres. A Gram Sabha certifies the village after that. JJM 2.0, approved on 10 March 2026, says the panchayat's own Har Ghar Jal declaration waits until in-village maintenance exists. The handover of that moment is Jal Arpan.

If your product marks a village "done" when the connection percentage hits 100, you are shipping the first stage and calling it the last. As on 4 August 2026 the ministry's reported national stock was already more than 15.90 crore of 19.36 crore households. A company whose only offer is a new household connection is competing for the remaining minority, in the states where that minority still lives. A company whose offer is uptime, water-quality surveillance, or a record the panchayat can use at Jal Utsav is aimed at the rule the Cabinet actually added.

## Know which computer the state already has

Four systems are named in the documents a founder will be asked about.

The JJM information system and its public dashboard are where a connection becomes official. Sujalam Bharat is the identity layer still being instituted: one Sujal Gaon or Service Area ID per village, source to tap, with geo-tagging on PM Gati Shakti. SNA SPARSH is the payment rail that releases money against an individual scheme. The rural sanitation dashboard is a different system, with Aspiring, Rising, and Model as the classes that matter.

A tool that does not map to one of those objects will be a side spreadsheet. A tool that claims to replace the Gram Sabha's resolution, or the third-party verification required within 90 days of an ODF Plus Model declaration, is claiming a legal step it does not have.

## Pick the buyer the mission actually uses

For a rural scheme still under construction, the counterparty is the state or Union Territory department implementing JJM, inside a memorandum the Cabinet note says each state will have. After Jal Arpan, the people who have to keep the scheme in use are the Gram Panchayat and the Village Water and Sanitation Committee. The state does not disappear. It is the body that had to establish maintenance before the declaration.

For village waste, the classes on [sbm.gov.in](https://sbm.gov.in/sbmgdashboard/) tell you the job. An Aspiring village, of which there were 57,923 on 11 June 2026, still needs the waste stream it does not have. A Model village needs the status to survive verification, not a new toilet slogan.

For a city, leave the rural dashboard. AMRUT 2.0 projects are in State Water Action Plans on [amrut.mohua.gov.in](https://amrut.mohua.gov.in/). Sewerage is a few hundred large projects, not a twin of the rural connection drive. The ministry told Parliament that 605 such projects had been approved, at ₹71,133.01 crore.

## A claim you can stand behind

Say which stage you serve, which as-on date you measured, and which institution signs. Do not say you serve "82 percent of India", and do not quote the IIM Bangalore and ILO person-year estimate as customers waiting. The Cabinet note that carries that estimate is [here](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2237548). The payment rail is described [here](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295509).
