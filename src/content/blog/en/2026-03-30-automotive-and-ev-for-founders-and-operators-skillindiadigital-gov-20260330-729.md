# A Founder's Reading Order for PM E-DRIVE, PLI Auto, and Charger Rules

An operator in this market is collecting certificates, not collecting announcements. Three instruments pay on three different proofs. Reading them in the order below keeps a two-wheeler price, a component plant, and a charging site from being planned as if they were one scheme.

## Start with the gazette that sets the cheque

Open [S.O. 4424(E) of 10 August 2026](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/Gazette%20Notification%2010%2008%202026%20on%20enhancement%20of%20Outlay%2011900%20cr.pdf) before the portal's "About" paragraph. The gazette is the later text. The outlay is ₹11,900 crore. The scheme runs to 31 March 2028. Claims must be in by 31 December 2027. If a sub-component's money runs out earlier, that sub-component closes.

For a two-wheeler OEM the commercial terms from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028 are ₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹5,000 per vehicle and at 15 percent of ex-factory price. The vehicle must be registered and fitted with an advanced battery. Private and corporate buyers qualify, as the [portal](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/) states, not only commercial fleets. The portal generates an Aadhaar-authenticated e-voucher at purchase. The cap on supported two-wheelers is 45,79,120, with ₹2,767 crore behind that line. As on 22 July 2026, 20,56,831 two-wheelers had already been reimbursed under the earlier ceiling. Build the remaining volume against the lower rate, and re-read the gazette if the ministry reviews the per-kWh figure, which the notification says it may do as costs fall.

Leave L5 off the product plan. It closed on 26 December 2025. The portal's claim deadline for those vehicles was 25 April 2026.

For any other vehicle, match the category to its own cap on the portal: 5,643 trucks and ₹500 crore, 3,811 ambulances and ₹500 crore, 14,028 buses and ₹4,391 crore, 39,034 e-rickshaws and e-carts and ₹50 crore. Then open the rate card in the [28 July 2026 reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2290696) and the category notification on the ministry list. Trucks and buses also have phased-manufacturing amendments dated 3 September 2026. Eligibility follows the schedule in force on the relevant date, not the schedule in a 2025 slide.

## Then separate the manufacturing cheque from the demand cheque

PLI Auto does not pay because PM E-DRIVE paid. The [January 2026 review](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2214039) requires domestic value addition of at least 50 percent. Eight Champion OEM applicants had that certificate for 94 variants, and ten component applicants had it for 37 variants, as of 31 December 2025, out of 82 approved applicants. Rates are 13 to 18 percent for electric-vehicle and hydrogen fuel-cell components and 8 to 13 percent for other advanced components. Disbursement follows the performance year: ₹322 crore paid in FY 2024-25 for FY 2023-24, and ₹1,999.94 crore paid for FY 2024-25. A founder who books the incentive in the year of the sale is a year early, and only if the variant is certified.

The phased-manufacturing certificate that the July reply says OEMs have taken from ministry testing agencies is the PM E-DRIVE localisation proof. FAME II's portal had required AIS 156 for that older subsidy. The testing-agency upgrade inside PM E-DRIVE is ₹780 crore. Plan certification lead time as part of the launch, because the agency is the gate.

If the product is a cell, it is a third scheme: ₹18,100 crore, 50 GWh, two years of gestation and five of performance. The review's installed fact is 1 GWh in pilot at one beneficiary since March 2024. A pack assembler and a cell maker are not in the same incentive.

## If the product is a charger, start from the power ministry

Public charging support of ₹2,000 crore requires the Ministry of Power guidelines of 17 September 2024. Swapping and battery-charging stations are funded for upstream infrastructure when they meet the 10 January 2025 guidelines. Chargers also follow a phased-manufacturing schedule under S.O. 3318(E), including IS 17017 and a ban on CKD import of those components from a single supplier. As on 1 July 2026 the approved book was ₹689 crore and 6,562 chargers. A site that is eligible on paper still has to be inside a sanction.

The e-voucher, the DVA certificate, and the charger standard are three folders. The operator who keeps them in one folder will claim the wrong scheme.
