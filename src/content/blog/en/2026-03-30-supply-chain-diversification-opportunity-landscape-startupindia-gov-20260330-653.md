# Where manufacturing localization creates a trade opportunity

Opportunity, in this stack, is a place the government has already named. It is not every product a global buyer might someday move. The named places are the 14 production-linked sectors, the vendor layer under them, a mineral-processing chain, and the tariff lines of agreements that are in force.

## The 14 sectors are the manufacturing map

The 2 August 2023 PIB note lists them in full: mobile manufacturing and specified electronic components; critical key starting materials, drug intermediates and active pharmaceutical ingredients; medical devices; automobiles and auto components; pharmaceutical drugs; specialty steel; telecom and networking products; electronic and technology products; white goods covering air conditioners and LEDs; food products; man-made fibre and technical textiles; high-efficiency solar PV modules; advanced chemistry cell batteries; and drones and drone components. The outlay attached to that list was Rs 1.97 lakh crore. The reply said the Cabinet had not approved further sectors as of that day. A later note may add one. Until it does, an opportunity memo for an unlisted product should not borrow this incentive.

Inside the list, the opportunity the note itself describes is the cascade. Anchor units are expected to create a vendor base, mostly MSMEs. At that date, 176 MSMEs were among 733 selected applicants, in bulk drugs, medical devices, pharma, telecom, white goods, food processing, textiles, and drones. A component maker that can qualify against one of those anchors is closer to a real order than a firm pitching "diversification" in the abstract. Bulk drugs and key starting materials are the import-substitution version of the same idea: the opportunity is the intermediate, not only the finished medicine.

## Processing is an opportunity even when mining is not

The National Critical Mineral Mission, approved on 29 January 2025, puts processing and recycling on the official map. The 24 March 2025 PIB note records Rs 500 crore for critical-mineral processing parks and Rs 1,500 crore for recycling, plus KABIL's lithium work in Argentina and an Australia MoU on lithium and cobalt. For a manufacturer, the opportunity is not the brine block. It is the park, the recycler, and the intermediate that a battery or electronics line can buy without a single overseas refiner. That opportunity is funded as a provision. It is not a plant you can book this week unless a later notice says the park is open.

## Trade opportunity is a tariff line

Commerce's note on the India-UAE CEPA, in force from 1 May 2022, names where Indian goods received immediate duty elimination on the UAE side: oil seeds and oils, beverages, cotton, fish and fish products, textiles, clothing, gems and jewellery, leather, footwear, pharmaceuticals, and many engineering products. The UAE eliminated duties on 97.4 percent of tariff lines, corresponding to 99 percent of imports from India. That is a sourcing and selling opportunity for firms already able to meet origin rules. It is a poor opportunity for a firm that cannot get a certificate of origin.

On 13 May 2026 the Commerce and Industry Minister described a wider set: nine agreements with 38 economies over three and a half years, four operational, five expected within 12 months, plus earlier agreements with Japan, Korea, and ASEAN. He also stated exports of USD 863 billion in the year ending 31 March 2026 and an aspirational USD 1 trillion target. The opportunity is market access where the agreement is operational. The trillion figure is a government aim, not a purchase order.

NICDC plug-and-play parks, noted in PIB's 27 November 2025 logistics piece, are the location version of the same landscape: a shed on a corridor, not a pin on a national average.
