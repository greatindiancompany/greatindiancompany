# A 90-Day Checklist for State Capacity and State Finance

This checklist covers the ninety days from 24 September 2026, roughly through 23 December 2026. It lists dates that already exist in official instructions. It does not invent a meeting that has not been notified.

## Before 15 December: the public-finance IT file

Part IV of the Department of Expenditure's SASCI guidelines, dated 27 March 2026, asks states and UTs to submit a report to the Comptroller and Auditor General by 15 December 2026 once they finish reforms under each component. The components are worth opening before that date, because a December submission cannot invent a system that is not live:

- At least six of seven IFMIS modules: e-sanction, e-budget, e-payment including e-Kuber, e-receipt, e-voucher, e-HRMS, and 100 percent Aadhaar-based direct benefit transfer for beneficiary schemes, including state schemes. Incentive: Rs 50 crore.
- A works-management system through the life of the work, and an e-procurement system through to award. Another Rs 50 crore.
- Live integration of the financial system with works, and with e-procurement. Another Rs 50 crore.
- Full digitisation of drawing and disbursing officers, treasuries and works or forest divisions. Another Rs 50 crore.

The CAG examines the submissions and recommends releases to the Department of Expenditure by 31 December 2026. That second date falls just outside a strict ninety-day window from 24 September, and it is the date the file is aiming at. The Rs 4,000 crore part pays only for reforms undertaken in 2026-27. A state that wants the incentive has to be able to show the system, not a tender.

## Through the quarter: the second installment

Untied Part I releases 66 percent first and 34 percent after at least 75 percent of the first installment is used, every Centrally Sponsored Scheme Single Nodal Agency account is closed, unspent central share and interest are deposited in the Consolidated Fund of India, and project details for 2020-21 to 2024-25 are on PFMS. There is no public calendar that says "second installment on this Monday." There is a condition. In this quarter a finance department, a contractor waiting on a state bill, or a scheme officer should ask whether those three proofs are in, not whether a new guideline has been printed. The guideline already exists.

Utilization certificates for 2025-26 reconstruction funds related to natural disasters of that year may be filed up to 31 December 2026. That is the one disaster-related date inside or just beside this window. Other unspent 2025-26 SASCI amounts are adjusted against the first 2026-27 installment, with the exceptions named in the guideline.

Part III's second half, the incentive for more than 10 percent growth in own-resource capital expenditure in the first six months of 2026-27 over the same months of 2025-26, will be knowable only once those months are closed and the accounts or the CAG's monthly key indicators are used. Ninety days from late September is when that half-year comparison becomes something a state can document. SASCI releases themselves are excluded from the growth sum.

## What will update, and what will not

NeSDA 2025 opened its portal on 25 May 2026. PIB said DARPG would run consultations, capacity-building sessions and review meetings with nodal officers during the cycle, and asked for timely data. This checklist does not state a submission deadline the 28 May note did not print. The action is to watch DARPG for the nodal-officer timetable and to keep using the monthly Way Forward series as the stocktake. The latest edition cited here is December 2025, released 22 January 2026: 24,090 e-services, 8,656 of them in local governance and utilities, as of 31 December 2025. A newer monthly, if DARPG posts one, replaces that stock. The status page is the index.

CPGRAMS is monthly. The June 2026 central report came out on 29 July 2026: 14-day average disposal in central ministries for 2026, 1,99,968 cases redressed in June, 78,793 pending. The next reports after June are the ones this quarter should read. They are a series, the fiftieth of which was June, so the habit is the point.

The Reserve Bank's State Finances study is annual. The 2025-26 edition was released on 23 January 2026. A new edition is not part of this ninety-day list. The January figures, a 3.3 percent gross fiscal deficit and liabilities budgeted at 29.2 percent of GDP at end-March 2026, remain the fiscal frame until RBI replaces them.
