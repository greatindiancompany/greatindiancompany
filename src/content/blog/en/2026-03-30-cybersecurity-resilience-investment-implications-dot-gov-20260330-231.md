# What cybersecurity rules change in a spending plan

An investment memo can treat India's cybersecurity rules as a set of required capabilities. It cannot treat CERT-In's incident series as a loss series. The Lok Sabha reply of 24 March 2026 says CERT-In does not maintain estimated financial loss from cyber incidents, and the National Crime Records Bureau does not maintain the amount recovered in cyber-crime cases.

## Capabilities the texts require

**Retention and logging.** The April 2022 CERT-In directions require logs of ICT systems for a rolling 180 days inside India. Once the relevant DPDP rule is in force, Rule 6 requires logs and personal data to be retained for one year to detect and investigate unauthorised access, unless another law requires otherwise, and Rule 8 requires a further one-year retention of specified traffic data and processing logs. A spending plan that funds 90 days of log storage is short of the direction already in force.

**A named security office, separated from delivery targets.** For Reserve Bank regulated entities covered by the 2023 IT directions, the Chief Information Security Officer is a senior executive who does not report directly to the Head of IT and is not given business targets. The CISO's office manages and monitors the security operations centre. The CISO places a cyber-preparedness review before the board, the board risk committee or the IT strategy committee at least quarterly. That is a staffing and governance cost, not a software line.

**Monitoring, scaled by regulator.** SEBI requires continuous monitoring through an own, group, third-party or Market SOC, and it requires BSE and NSE to provide the Market SOC so that smaller entities are not left to build one alone. Telecom entities must establish a SOC, keep specified logs for a period the Central Government sets on its portal, and have audits by a certified agency at intervals set on that portal. These are recurring operating costs with a capital component for the first build.

**Assurance.** Government and critical-sector organisations are directed, in CERT-In's own description of its assurance work, toward empanelled auditors. The MSME baseline recommends a baseline audit at least yearly. SEBI requires cyber audit and vulnerability assessment on the timelines in the framework, after the glide path dates of 1 January 2025 and 1 April 2025. IRDAI's guidelines put annual review of the information and cyber security policy on the CISO, with approval by the information-security risk committee.

**Recovery.** The Reserve Bank directions require business-continuity and disaster-recovery capabilities that can restore critical operations, including security controls, after a cyber incident. The MSME controls ask for a backup schedule, a restoration test, and a minimum business-continuity plan for critical applications. The DPDP security-safeguard rule, when commenced, includes backups so processing can continue if confidentiality, integrity or availability is compromised.

## What the public rupee figures are, and are not

MeitY's Demand No. 27 separates CERT-In's establishment budget from the Cyber Security Projects scheme. For 2026-27 the CERT-In line is ₹269 crore, of which the capital column is ₹152 crore and the revenue column is ₹117 crore. Cyber Security Projects are ₹790 crore in that year's budget estimate, after a 2025-26 revised estimate of ₹600 crore against a ₹782 crore budget estimate. The Data Protection Board line, which the demand note ties to salary and establishment, is ₹10 crore in the 2026-27 budget estimate, against a ₹2 crore revised estimate the year before.

The Home Ministry's reply separately records ₹132.93 crore released to states and Union territories under the Cyber Crime Prevention against Women and Children scheme. That purse is not part of Demand No. 27. Adding it to the MeitY lines produces a number no budget document prints.

The Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System figure in the same reply — more than ₹8,690 crore saved across more than 24.65 lakh complaints up to 31 January 2026 — is funds stopped after a citizen report. It is not an estimate of total fraud, and it is not a TAM.

A spending plan that follows the rules will show logs, a separated security office, monitoring, audit and recovery. A plan that cites a national loss number is citing something these institutions say they do not keep.
