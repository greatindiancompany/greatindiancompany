# Future of Work in India: Evidence Map for Labour and Skills

## Match the question to the record

Four institutions publish four kinds of future-of-work record. This snapshot names the record and leaves the figures inside it.

## Household employment

The Periodic Labour Force Survey, from the National Statistics Office in the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, estimates labour force participation, the worker population ratio, and the unemployment rate, in rural and urban areas, in usual status and in current weekly status. The press note for the Annual Report 2025, covering January to December 2025, is dated 27 March 2026. Questions about whether more people are working, or whether unemployment moved, belong to that series and its later bulletins.

## Law and administration

The Ministry of Labour and Employment is the source for the four codes in force from 21 November 2025 and for the Central Rules which a Lok Sabha reply on 20 July 2026 says were notified on 8 May 2026. It is the source for mandatory appointment letters, fixed-term gratuity after one year rather than five, the IT and ITES salary-by-the-7th note, and the aggregator contribution of 1 to 2 percent of turnover capped at 5 percent of amounts paid or payable to gig and platform workers. It is the source for e-Shram, launched on 26 August 2021 as a database of unorganised workers, including gig and platform workers.

e-Shram answers a registration question. EPFO and ESIC administer organised-sector social security, and the ministry's materials say gig and platform schemes can be implemented through them. Adding a registration stock to a PLFS rate counts the same idea twice, under two definitions.

## A count Parliament's answer left out

Lok Sabha Unstarred Question No. 9, answered on 20 July 2026, asked for formal jobs created versus jobs lost in manufacturing and MSMEs over five years. The minister's answer explains enabling provisions of the codes. An evidence note that fills in a count the tabled answer withheld would be writing its own statistic.

## Skills

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship's PMKVY 4.0 guidelines describe a training design: the National Council for Vocational Education and Training, the National Skills Qualification Framework, implementation support from the National Skill Development Corporation, and Skill India Digital, for a phase set in FY 2022-2026. That file answers how a public short-term training scheme was built. Employment of those trainees is a PLFS question, in tables this page leaves closed.

## Gig size

NITI Aayog published *India's Booming Gig and Platform Economy* in June 2022. The note issued with that report says it recommended a separate enumeration and the collection of gig-worker information in PLFS. Until a PLFS table for that purpose is read in full, the 2022 report remains a recommendations document. Its estimates stay there.
