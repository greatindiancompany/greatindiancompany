# Future of Work in India: Investor Diligence on Labour Rules

## Questions the record can answer

Investors underwriting Indian platforms, IT and business-process services, staffing firms, and multi-state employers can ask labour questions the Ministry of Labour and Employment has already put in writing. Workforce size comes from a cited survey table or from the company's own rolls. On 20 July 2026 a Lok Sabha question asked for formal jobs created versus jobs lost in manufacturing and MSMEs. The answer described legal provisions and gave no number. A pitch that offers a precise national bridge from the codes to jobs is ahead of that parliamentary record.

## Six checks

1. Which establishments have the Centre as the appropriate government, and which follow a state? Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026. The same Lok Sabha reply says state and Union Territory rules were mostly finalised or pre-published, with a few still in process as of that date. One India-wide labour-cost assumption skips that split.

2. Are appointment letters on file for the people the company counts, including fixed-term staff? The ministry's 21 November 2025 note makes letters mandatory and says fixed-term employees receive permanent-worker benefits and gratuity after one year rather than five. Ask for the policy and a sample.

3. If the company calls itself a gig platform, is it an aggregator under the Code on Social Security? The ministry's note states a contribution of 1 to 2 percent of annual turnover, capped at 5 percent of the amount paid or payable to gig and platform workers. Ask which scheme is notified, and which benefit is only described as capable of being formulated. The July reply's list is life and disability cover, accident insurance, health and maternity benefits, and old-age protection.

4. For IT or ITES revenue, does payroll release salary by the 7th, as the ministry's sector note requires? A miss is a compliance fact and a working-capital fact. It says nothing, by itself, about demand for digital exports.

5. Does the deck cite a gig-worker market size? NITI Aayog's June 2022 report, *India's Booming Gig and Platform Economy*, recommended that the Periodic Labour Force Survey identify gig workers. Prefer that survey identification, once published, to a reused estimate. PLFS annual results for January to December 2025 were issued on 27 March 2026. Read them for employment conditions. A total addressable market is a separate build.

6. Is public skilling part of the hiring story? PMKVY 4.0 was a Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship scheme window for FY 2022-2026. A certificate pipeline is a training fact. Wages still follow the Code on Wages and the appointment letter.

## What holds up

Diligence that separates statute, scheme notification, state rule, and survey can proceed without a national headcount. Where an investment depends on a particular employment figure, that figure needs a cited PLFS table or the company's audited workforce.
