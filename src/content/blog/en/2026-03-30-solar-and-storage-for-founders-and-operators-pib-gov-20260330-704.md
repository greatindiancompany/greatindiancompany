# How a Rooftop Operator Should Read PM Surya Ghar

A rooftop founder does not need the whole electricity plan. The operating documents are the February 2024 cabinet design, the August 2026 progress note, the national portal, and the module list that decides what may be installed on a net-metered roof.

## What the household is offered

PIB release 2010133, 29 February 2024, is the design. PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana has an outlay of Rs 75,021 crore. It aims at one crore households and free electricity up to 300 units a month. Central assistance is 60 percent of system cost for 2 kW systems and 40 percent of the additional system cost between 2 and 3 kW. Assistance stops rising after 3 kW. At the benchmark prices stated then, that was Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW, and Rs 78,000 for 3 kW or higher.

The household registers on the national portal, chooses the state and the distribution company, and chooses the vendor and the equipment. The portal is meant to show system size, a benefits calculation, and vendor rating. Surplus power can be sold to the distribution company. The note says a 3 kW system generates more than 300 units a month on average. Collateral-free loans were described then as around 7 percent for systems up to 3 kW.

Quote those rupee caps as the cabinet's benchmark at that date. Re-check the portal before you print a customer quotation. A press note is not the subsidy calculator.

## What the work looks like now

PIB release 2294208, 4 August 2026, describes the machine an operator actually runs. The process is online from application to subsidy. Jan Samarth handles paperless loans. PFMS is described as releasing subsidy within 15 days. More than 80 distribution companies are on APIs. There is a digital net-metering agreement. Vendor star ratings are visible to households. The DCR portal checks domestic content. Deemed approval and a feasibility waiver apply up to 10 kW. About 23,000 third-party inspections had been carried out.

The same note's commercial facts, as of that date: 34,219 registered vendors and 29,469 active; more than 2.32 lakh people trained; 14.8 GW commissioned; more than 50.06 lakh households benefiting; Rs 28,024 crore of subsidy released by direct benefit transfer. Loans are stated at 5.75 percent, repo plus 50 basis points, sanctioned for 21.87 lakh applicants, with 17.5 lakh installations completed through loans. That 5.75 percent line replaces "around 7 percent" as the later official description. The sanction still belongs to the lender.

Thirty-two states and Union Territories had removed application and net-metering charges. If you work in a state that is not in that count, do not promise a waiver the note does not give you. The note does not name the remaining states.

For customers who cannot pay upfront, the note describes utility-led aggregation: 1.6 lakh installations done for PMAY, BPL, and SC/ST households in four states, and the model sanctioned in twelve. That is a different sale from a household that picks you on the portal.

## The hardware rule that can void the job

MNRE's ALMM page, updated 16 September 2026, says net-metering projects must use List-I modules. The first cell list was issued on 31 July 2025. PIB release 2082901 says that from 1 June 2026, modules in net-metering and other covered projects must use List-II cells, with an exemption tied to the bid date for projects already bid before that December 2024 order. A rooftop firm buying modules in 2026 should match both the module and the cell to the current lists, and should keep the DCR check in the job file. A PLI award held by a factory does not replace enlistment.

## A small operating checklist

Register and stay in the active vendor set, because the August note distinguishes registered from active. Keep the model on List-I and the cell path compliant. Price assistance from the portal, using the cabinet formula only as the design. Track the distribution company's API and charges in the specific state. Expect a public rating and third-party inspection.
