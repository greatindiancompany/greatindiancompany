# Which payments and credit rules bind before a product launches

The regulatory outlook for Indian financial digitalization is not a prediction of the next press conference. It is a map of which activity already has a direction, and which popular label does not. A product launch that picks the wrong map will be supervised under the right one anyway.

## Match the activity to the direction

Domestic digital payments sit under the [Authentication mechanisms for digital payment transactions Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898), issued on 25 September 2025, with compliance due by 1 April 2026. At least two distinct factors are required unless an exemption applies. The directions do not cover cross-border digital payments as a class. They do require card issuers, by 1 October 2026, to validate non-recurring cross-border card-not-present transactions when an overseas merchant or acquirer requests authentication, and to put a risk-based mechanism around cross-border card-not-present transactions. That October date is the nearest item still ahead of this note, which is written on 24 September 2026.

Holding customer funds for merchants is a payment-aggregator question. The [Regulation of Payment Aggregators Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896) of 15 September 2025 split the business into physical, online, and cross-border. A payment gateway that only provides technology and does not handle funds is not an aggregator. Non-banks need authorisation and a net-worth path from ₹15 crore to ₹25 crore. Banks do not need that authorisation. Cross-border aggregation is limited to current-account transactions that FEMA does not prohibit. An only-physical aggregator that missed the 31 December 2025 application date was required to wind up by 28 February 2026.

Lending through an app is the [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848). A payment aggregator that only aggregates payments stays outside those directions. The moment it also acts as a lending service provider, it is inside them. Multi-lender screens have had to show a fair comparison of offers since 1 November 2025. Default loss guarantee is capped at five per cent of the amount disbursed from a fixed loan set, and it is barred for revolving credit offered digitally and for credit cards.

Sharing financial information with consent is the [NBFC-Account Aggregator Directions, 2016](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10598), updated as on 6 September 2024. The aggregator is always a base-layer NBFC. It does not move money. A financial information user must be regulated by a financial-sector regulator, a list that includes the Reserve Bank, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, and the Department of Revenue.

## Labels that are not directions

UPI is a payment system of the National Payments Corporation of India, described as public infrastructure by MeitY on the [Digital India page](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/). Membership, product circulars, and limits come from NPCI and from Reserve Bank permissions such as the credit-line-on-UPI circular. They are not a general fintech licence.

The Open Credit Enablement Network is an open protocol for connecting loan agents and lenders. It is not a Reserve Bank direction and it does not authorise lending. Any loan that results is still digital lending if it meets that definition, and the regulated entity remains responsible.

The Digital Rupee is still best read from the [concept note](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationReportDetails.aspx?ID=1218) and the 2022 pilot releases. Wholesale e₹ was for government-security settlement. Retail e₹ was a token with no interest, distributed by banks. Those papers do not announce a date on which e₹ replaces deposits or UPI. An outlook note should not invent one.

The practical outlook for a board is therefore a checklist of identities. Are you an issuer, an aggregator, a lender, a lending service provider, an account aggregator, or a protocol that sits beside one of those? The direction follows the identity.
