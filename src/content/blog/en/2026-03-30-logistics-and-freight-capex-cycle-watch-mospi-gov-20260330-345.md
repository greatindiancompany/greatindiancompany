Watch India’s freight capex cycle in the budget of the ministry that builds the asset. PM Gati Shakti is the planning overlay. The [written Lok Sabha reply of 11 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2297818) says it sets no quantified targets and no expenditure requirement of its own. If a cycle note starts with “Gati Shakti outlay,” it has already left the official record.

## Three books, one planning table

The Network Planning Group reviews central projects for multimodal fit, last-mile links, and clashes with other networks. As on 11 August 2026 it had evaluated 396 projects, estimated at about ₹18.66 lakh crore. Sanctioned: 256. Under implementation: 198. Capex that is only evaluated is a drawing. Capex that is sanctioned and not under implementation is a political and administrative fact, not a pour of concrete. The roads row is where that distinction bites: 171 projects evaluated, 108 sanctioned, 53 under implementation, estimated cost ₹9,01,668 crore. Railways: 162 evaluated, 102 sanctioned, 102 under implementation, ₹5,43,315 crore.

The [starred reply the same day](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf) adds the three-year flow the pipeline table does not. In that window the Centre approved 1,115 national highway projects of about 18,493 km, costing about ₹4.75 lakh crore, and constructed 32,370 km of national highways. It sanctioned 237 railway projects of 9,703 km at about ₹1,90,333 crore: 40 new lines, 17 gauge conversions, 180 doublings. Construction kilometres and sanctioned kilometres answer different questions. A capex cycle that quotes only one of them is half a cycle.

The roads ministry’s own programme page for the corridor build is [Bharatmala](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala). Multi-modal logistics parks developed under that programme have model concession documents on the same ministry site. Those parks are capex where road, and often rail or warehouse, are supposed to meet. The concession paper is the commercial document. The Lok Sabha count is not a substitute for reading it.

## Ports are a different cheque book

Sagarmala sits with the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. The programme page is [here](https://shipmin.gov.in/en/division/sagarmala). The August replies name Sagarmala, with expressways, dedicated freight corridors, and industrial corridors, among the connectivity efforts they associate with logistics performance. They do not fold port capex into the roads figure of ₹4.75 lakh crore. Port projects that passed through the Network Planning Group are inside the 396. The public annex does not give a clean project count for the ports ministry without a broken cell. Do not invent one. Ask the ports ministry for the project, the sanction, and whether it is in the 198.

Dedicated freight corridors are named in the August reply as part of the connectivity story, and they were already in the Prime Minister’s 17 September 2022 launch speech as work that had been sped up before the logistics policy existed. A corridor that is “expedited” in a speech still needs an operating schedule from the company that runs it. This brief does not guess how many kilometres are commissioned.

## How to watch the next turn of the cycle

Four dates matter more than a sentiment index. The Union Budget, for the scheme head of roads, rail, and ports. The next Network Planning Group stocktake, for the movement between evaluated, sanctioned, and under implementation. The roads ministry’s project circulars, for a specific highway or park. The ports ministry’s project and cargo notes, for whether new capacity is meeting ships. Gati Shakti’s public query platform, which the written reply says opened in September 2025, is a way to see layers. It is not an appropriation bill.

A cycle that looks “up” because 396 projects have been evaluated can still be slow if the roads gap — sanctioned, not yet under implementation — stays wide. That gap, not the slogan, is the watch item.
