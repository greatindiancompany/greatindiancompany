# A Dated Snapshot of India's Telecom and Broadband Figures

Every figure below is tied to the publication that printed it. Where two official series differ, both rows are kept. This page does not average them and does not extend them past their date.

## Subscribers at 31 July 2026

Source: TRAI Press Release No. 116/2026, 28 August 2026.

| Item | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Broadband subscribers | 1,094.48 million (1,087.50 million at end-June; monthly growth 0.64 percent) |
| Broadband excluding machine-to-machine cellular connections | 1,005.72 million |
| Fixed wired broadband | 48.14 million (monthly growth 0.90 percent) |
| Fixed wireless segment, including 5G fixed wireless, Wi-Fi, and satellite | 18.84 million (monthly growth 2.60 percent) |
| Mobile wireless broadband | 1,027.50 million (monthly growth 0.59 percent) |
| 5G fixed wireless subscribers | 13.21 million (urban 6.56 million, rural 6.64 million) |
| Unlicensed-band radio fixed wireless | 5.14 million (urban 3.65 million, rural 1.49 million) |
| Telephone subscribers | 1,354.28 million |
| Tele-density | 94.68 percent (urban 154.12 percent, rural 61.05 percent) |
| Wireline subscribers | 48.01 million |
| Wireline tele-density | 3.36 percent (urban 8.28 percent, rural 0.57 percent) |
| Top five broadband providers' share | 98.59 percent |
| Active wireless subscribers on the peak visitor-location day | 1,204.01 million (about 93.48 percent of the wireless mobile base) |
| Mobile number portability requests in July | 15.98 million |
| Machine-to-machine cellular connections | 137.70 million |
| Delhi licensed service area tele-density | 368.33 percent |
| Bihar licensed service area tele-density | 64.19 percent |

Jio 535.12 million, Airtel 384.23 million, Vodafone Idea 129.96 million, BSNL 27.28 million, and Atria Convergence Technologies 2.47 million are the top five broadband providers in that note. Nine licensed service areas were below the all-India tele-density. TRAI includes machine-to-machine connections in the headline tele-density and uses population projections from the July 2020 technical-group report.
