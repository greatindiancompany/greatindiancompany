# Risk map for India's ports, coastal shipping, and shipyards

These are not imagined stress cases. Each one is a weakness the ministry's own pages already describe, plus the way a reader can tell whether it is getting better. No probability is attached. The pages do not publish one.

## Evacuation fails after the berth is built

The Sagarmala page says port capacity can match demand and still fail if cargo cannot leave the gate. India's cargo is generated inland, so the lead distance is long, and road and rail dominate. Coastal shipping and inland waterways are the modes the programme wants to thicken. The page's own comparison is stark: about 2 percent of freight on inland waterways as of 2025, against more than 30 percent in developed economies. If the Indian Ports Association's monthly notes show rising dwell and pre-berthing detention while tonnes also rise, the evacuation risk is the one that is printing. Last-mile rail sits with Indian Port Rail and Ropeway Corporation, not only with the port board. A scenario in which berth capex arrives and the siding does not is the scenario that page is warning about.

## The Centre-state split drops cargo out of the series you are watching

Twelve major ports operate under the ministry. About 200 non-major ports sit with state maritime boards, and around 65 of them handle cargo. A risk model that uses only the association's major-port file will miss a state jetty that takes the incremental coal or container. The reverse mistake is to treat a state maritime board's concession as if the Adjudicatory Board for Major Ports could hear it. That board, constituted on 13 August 2025, deals with major-port concessionaires, captive berth users, stressed major-port projects, and major-port user complaints. State-port disputes stay in the state framework. The Ports Wing lists an Indian Ports Act, 2025, but the page still describes non-major ports as a state subject.

## The yard is subsidised because it is expensive

The assistance scheme exists, in the 24 July 2026 reply's words, to address the cost disadvantage of Indian yards against global yards. The December 2025 note prices that policy at 15 to 25 percent per vessel. If the subsidy is the business case, the risk is execution of the manual: milestone certification, domestic content, and independent valuation. A cluster that stays at in-principle stage does not change a yard's cost. The July reply's live status is in-principle approval for three greenfield clusters, in Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu, and for four brownfield proposals. The ₹25,000 crore Maritime Development Fund has a manager, named on 26 May 2026, and guidelines dated 20 February 2026. Until investments are listed, financing risk and announcement risk are the same fact.

Output risk is visible in the units. The reply cites recent national shipbuilding output in the tens of thousands of gross tonnes and a capacity aim in the millions of gross tonnes a year. The path between them runs through docks, design skill at the India Ship Technology Centre, and orders. The shipping division's demand-aggregation plan, and the reply's mention of a fleet plan of more than 400 vessels, are how public buyers are supposed to create that order book. A plan is not a keel.

## Statistics go stale, and recycling compliance is uneven

The shipping division's fleet ranking is dated 30 April 2019. Using it as a current risk indicator, or as comfort, is a data risk. Sagarmala's capacity sentences still point at 2025. Prefer the association's dated traffic notes for major ports, and say so when the month is missing.

At Alang-Sosiya, the shipping division says 115 of 131 allotted plots are Hong Kong Convention compliant, out of 153 plots, and that nearly 98 percent of Indian recycling happens there. The gap between allotted and compliant plots is the environmental and market-access risk for recycling. The shipbreaking credit note, 40 percent of scrap value in the December 2025 scheme note, only helps an owner who scraps in India. A non-compliant plot does not become compliant because the credit exists.
