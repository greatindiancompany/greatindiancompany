# Awarded, Released, and Running: Where India’s Hydrogen Projects Actually Stand

The bottleneck in India’s green hydrogen record is the distance between a sanction and a running asset. The 2026 papers are rich in sanctions. They are sparing with assets described as complete.

## Mobility: a large sanction, a small release

On 29 July 2026, the Lok Sabha was told that 12 pilot projects will deploy 70 hydrogen-powered vehicles, 27 buses and 43 trucks, with 16 refuelling stations on 21 routes. Financial assistance allocated is about ₹410.77 crore. The amount released is ₹41.582 crore. The [annex](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1648_XD1qA0.pdf) is full of named corridors, from Greater Noida–Delhi–Agra and Ahmedabad–Vadodara–Surat to Kochi–Kozhikode, Kandla–Gandhidham, and routes around Bina in Madhya Pradesh. Consortia include NTPC, Tata Motors with Indian Oil, NHPC, and others written as abbreviations in the annex.

A route list is not a photograph of buses in service. The answer requires executing agencies to submit performance reports on efficiency, fuel consumption, safety, and utilisation. That is the language of a trial that still has to produce data. Until those reports are public, the bottleneck is evidence of operation. Another announcement of the same 12 projects would not remove it. The mission page’s mobility ceiling, ₹496 crore through 2025-26, is an older programme line. The July answer is the execution snapshot, and the snapshot says most of the allocated assistance had not been released.

## Ports: approval, allotment, and one small plant that is finished

Paradip’s jetty is approved at 4.0 million tonnes a year of handling capacity and ₹797.17 crore, for a public-private partnership, in the 5 August 2026 annex. Approval for a partnership is a procurement stage. The berth is described as common infrastructure for manufacturers in the hinterland. It is not described as commissioned.

Tuticorin’s large numbers are expected capacities on allotted land: 0.1, 0.22, and 0.5 million tonnes a year for three firms. Beside them, the annex describes a 10 Nm³ pilot commissioned in April 2025 with port funding of ₹3.88 crore. Expected tonnes and a commissioned pilot are different objects.

Kandla has the clearest split. A 1 MW demonstration plant is reported complete at ₹14.1 crore. A 5 MW plant, scalable to 10 MW, is a contract at ₹62.80 crore with completion dated 31 December 2026. Adding the completed megawatt to the contracted megawatts and calling the sum “operating” erases the only distinction the annex bothers to draw. The [annex](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU2987_27NWXF.pdf) is the source for both sentences.

## Factories and molecules that are still letters

Fifteen companies have incentives for 3,000 MW a year of electrolyser manufacturing, on the minister’s statement of 17 June 2026. Manufacturing capacity awarded is the right to build a line and claim support under guidelines on MNRE’s [schemes list](https://mnre.gov.in/en/hydrogen-schemes-guidelines/). It becomes supply when stacks are delivered. Production incentives awarded for 8,62,000 tonnes a year, in the same speech, are the downstream letters. Mode-2B’s 30 kilo-tonnes at four refineries are build-own-operate awards. None of those sentences is a census of operating electrolysers.

The short commissioned list in these pages is JSW’s 3,600 tonnes a year, which the minister said started in November 2025, plus the small port pilots the annex marks as complete. Certification can become the next bottleneck after construction: the November 2025 note says a final certificate is mandatory for incented hydrogen or hydrogen sold or used in India, and the portal opened on 17 June 2026. A finished plant that cannot obtain an accredited check cannot deliver the legal product the offtaker thought it bought.

The Mission Secretariat at MNRE, the Empowered Group, and the port authorities are the institutions that can shorten these gaps. A new national target would not. Great Indian Company watches released rupees, contractual completion dates, and plants described as commissioned, because those are the execution facts the 2026 answers contain.
