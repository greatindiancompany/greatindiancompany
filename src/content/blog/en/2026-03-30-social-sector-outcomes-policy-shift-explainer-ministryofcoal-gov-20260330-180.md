# How India’s Rural Wage Guarantee Changed on 1 July 2026

India’s main rural social-protection programme for wage work changed its statute this year. From 1 July 2026, the Viksit Bharat–Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 applies in every rural area, and the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 is repealed from the same day. Parliament passed the new Act in December 2025. The outcome that the law itself promises is still a household entitlement to unskilled wage work. The design around that entitlement is different.

## What the household is now owed

The Press Information Bureau’s note of 11 May 2026 states a statutory guarantee of 125 days of wage employment in a financial year for every rural household whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work. Under the repealed Act the figure was 100 days. The guarantee is not a budget line that a ministry may exhaust and then close. If work is not provided in the prescribed time, the worker remains entitled to an unemployment allowance: not less than one-fourth of the notified wage for the first thirty days of the financial year, and not less than one-half for the remaining period.

Wages are to be paid weekly or, in any case, within fifteen days from the closure of the muster roll, into a bank or post office account. If payment is late, compensation is 0.05 percent of the unpaid wages for every day of delay beyond the sixteenth day after the muster roll closes.

Notified wages changed with the commencement. A 1 July 2026 release says no notified daily wage in the country is below ₹300, and the national average notified wage rose from ₹298.8 to ₹327.4. Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, and Himachal Pradesh are named as states where the increase was in the range of 15 to 25 percent. Until a new rate is notified, the May note said existing MGNREGA rates would continue. The July notification is the rate card to use now.

## What did not reset to zero

The transition rules are the part operators miss when they treat 1 July as a greenfield scheme.

Existing job cards for workers whose e-KYC is complete stay valid until a Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Card is issued. Workers are not to be refused work only because e-KYC is pending. Demand can still be made orally, in writing on the existing Form-6, or on a digital platform. Works already under way on the commencement date may continue. Where those works cannot absorb demand, new works from Schedule I of the new Act may be opened.

Attendance is to be captured on the National Mobile Monitoring System with face authentication, and the May note says exception handling is built in so that a connectivity failure does not by itself strike off a genuine worker.

A Lok Sabha reply from the Ministry of Rural Development on 11 August 2026 said every state and Union territory had notified the scheme and started works, and that more than 9.69 crore person-days had been generated in roughly the first month. That is an early output, not a full-year outcome, and not a measure of whether households are reaching 125 days.

## Where the work itself is supposed to point

Permissible works are grouped into four themes: water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood infrastructure, and mitigation of extreme weather. Core rural infrastructure explicitly includes school buildings and Anganwadi centres, which is the direct join to nutrition and education outcomes. Administrative expenditure is raised from 6 percent to 9 percent, which is the ministry’s stated room for field staff, training, and remuneration.

States may notify a pause of up to sixty days in a financial year so that sowing and harvesting are not starved of labour. The 125-day guarantee is meant to be met in the rest of the year. A pause that is notified and a pause that quietly suppresses demand are different facts. Only the notified pause is what the Act describes.

Worksites have a legal list: drinking water, shade, a first-aid kit, and, where five or more children under five are present, a woman worker paid to look after them. Accident care and an ex-gratia link to the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana are also written into the worksite rules.

## Money, and what it does not prove

For 2026-27 the central share is ₹95,692.31 crore, described by the ministry as the largest budget-estimate provision yet for a rural employment programme. Including the estimated state share, the same note says the total outlay is expected to exceed ₹1.51 lakh crore. The Lok Sabha reply of 11 August said sanctions of ₹25,844.97 crore had gone out as a first instalment for the 1 July 2026 to 31 March 2027 window. Allocation is not person-days, and person-days are not durable assets. The outcome test is whether households that ask for work receive it, are paid on time, and leave behind the assets the four themes name.
