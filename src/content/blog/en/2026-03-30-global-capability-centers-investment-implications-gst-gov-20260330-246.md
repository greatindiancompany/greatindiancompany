# Investment Implications of Computer-Software FDI

Investors often meet India's captive hubs through a foreign-investment headline. The headline is real, and it is easy to misread. Equity into "computer software and hardware" is the closest official sector bucket. It is not a count of capability-centre deals, and it is not the same number as gross or net FDI.

## Three balances, one year

The Ministry of Finance, answering Lok Sabha Unstarred Question No. 10 on 20 July 2026, said India registered gross FDI inflow of **USD 94.84 billion** in 2025-26, against **USD 80.61 billion** in 2024-25. Net FDI recovered to **USD 6.95 billion** from **USD 0.96 billion**. The answer associates the net pattern with higher repatriation and with overseas direct investment by Indian companies under the outward-investment rules notified in 2022.

The same answer, citing the DPIIT FDI factsheet for March 2026, gives equity inflow only:

| Sector | 2023-24 | 2024-25 | 2025-26 | Cumulative, April 2000–March 2026 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Services sector | 6,640 | 9,347 | 10,010 | 128,853 |
| Computer software and hardware | 7,973 | 7,814 | 13,946 | 124,644 |

Figures are USD million. DPIIT's services sector includes financial services, banking, insurance, non-financial business services, outsourcing, R&D, courier, and technical testing. Outsourcing and R&D sit there, not in the computer-software line. A captive that books its inflow as software, and a captive that books it as R&D or business services, land in different rows.

Computer software and hardware was the largest of the ten sectors in that table in 2025-26. The jump from **USD 7,814 million** to **USD 13,946 million** is about **78 percent**. It followed two softer years. DPIIT's factsheet updated to March 2025 shows the same sector at **USD 9,394 million** in 2022-23, **USD 7,973 million** in 2023-24, and **USD 7,814 million** in 2024-25, with a cumulative **USD 110,698 million** from April 2000 to March 2025, equal to **15 percent** of FDI equity on that older sheet. The March 2026 cumulative of **USD 124,644 million** continues that stock. This brief does not recompute the percentage share, because the July 2026 answer does not print the new all-sector equity total beside it.

## What the equity line will not underwrite

Fresh equity is a remittance into share capital, reported through the Reserve Bank and compiled by DPIIT. A mature captive can fund payroll and fit-out from retained earnings. Those earnings show up, if at all, in the reinvested-earnings component of the wider FDI concept, which the factsheet keeps apart from equity. The March 2025 factsheet put total FDI inflow in 2024-25, on the international-practice definition, at **USD 81,043 million**, of which equity was **USD 50,018 million**. That total is a different concept from the "gross FDI" of **USD 80.61 billion** the Finance Ministry later cited for the same year. Quote the label with the figure.

State tables do not rescue the gap. On the March 2025 factsheet, FDI equity in 2024-25 was **USD 19,589 million** into Maharashtra, **USD 6,619 million** into Karnataka, **USD 6,091 million** into Delhi, **USD 5,711 million** into Gujarat, **USD 3,681 million** into Tamil Nadu, **USD 3,147 million** into Haryana, and **USD 2,994 million** into Telangana. Those are all-sector inflows. DPIIT says state-wise data is maintained from October 2019. They are a poor proxy for where a software captive spent capital.

The route is liberal for this activity. Make in India lists IT and BPM at 100 percent on the automatic route. The July 2026 Lok Sabha answer says more than 90 percent of FDI inflow arrives on that route. Liberal entry explains why equity can arrive quickly. It does not explain whether the equity built a captive, a product company, a data-centre holding structure, or a stake purchase.

## A usable investment reading

Pair the equity line with the RBI software-export survey, and keep them labeled. Exports excluding commercial presence were **US$ 221.4 billion** in 2025-26, up **8.2 percent**. That is a flow of services, mostly off-site, mostly to the United States. Equity is a flow of capital into a sector bucket that also contains hardware. A year can show a jump in one and a steady print in the other, which is what 2025-26 shows relative to the prior software-equity path.

For a single asset, the decision file is still the Indian company's own filings, the parent's segment note, and the current FDI policy text. The national buckets tell you the tide. They do not price the centre. The export-side reading is in the [market-signal brief](/blog/global-capability-centers-market-signal-brief-gst-gov-20260330-206).
