## Risks the documents already name

This map stays inside risks that a policy or a parliamentary reply states. It does not assign probabilities, and it does not forecast a crisis.

**Secondary school dropout.** NEP 2020 says near-universal elementary enrolment has not carried through. In the figures it prints, the gross enrolment ratio falls from 90.9% in Grades 6–8 to 79.3% in Grades 9–10 and 56.5% in Grades 11–12. It cites 3.22 crore out-of-school children aged 6 to 17 in the NSSO survey of 2017-18. The risk to the 2030 goal of full pre-school-to-secondary enrolment is that pattern, unless later school data, which this brief does not have, show it has closed.

**A 2035 enrolment aim that is still far from 50%.** The 10 August 2026 reply puts higher-education gross enrolment at 30 in 2023-24, against the policy’s 50% aim for 2035, including vocational education. The risk is slow progress, not a cancelled target. The reply does not say the target has changed.

**Training that clusters where proposals are filed.** PMKVY 4.0 has no state-wise targets. If project implementing agencies do not propose work in a district, the scheme design does not fill that district with a quota. The 7 February 2025 Cabinet note says PM-NAPS is meant to encourage apprenticeships in MSMEs, aspirational districts, and the North-East. That is an intention. It is not evidence that the gap has closed.

**Attendance and assessment integrity.** The 12 August 2026 reply makes Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance mismatches with inspection findings a high-severity breach. Listed responses include suspension of training, recovery of payments, loss of accreditation, blacklisting, and police complaints. The risk for a learner is a batch that stops. The risk for a funder is money recovered after it was treated as spent.

**Credit that exists on paper and not on a campus.** More than 700 universities and over 7,500 colleges have adopted the National Credit Framework, as reported from AISHE 2023-24. Institutions outside that count do not, on this reply, offer the same mobility. A learner who assumes every college will accept a skill credit is ahead of the text.

## Scenarios that stay conditional

If school retention after Grade 8 stays at the ratios printed in 2020, the 100% secondary goal for 2030 remains distant. If higher-education enrolment stays near the 2023-24 level of 4.50 crore, the 50% ratio is not met by arithmetic already in hand. If PMKVY centres treat on-the-job training as optional where NCVET has prescribed it, the 12 August rule blocks assessment. If centres match biometric attendance to inspection records, that particular penalty path is not triggered.

These are conditional readings of published rules. They are not predictions that a named institution will pass or fail.
