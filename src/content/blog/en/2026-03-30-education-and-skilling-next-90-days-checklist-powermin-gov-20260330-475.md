## What the next 90 days can actually be checked against

From 23 September 2026, ninety days run to 22 December 2026. No education or skilling document used for this brief schedules a new Cabinet meeting, a budget line, or a survey release inside that window. The useful work is to apply rules that are already in writing, and to avoid filling the gaps with guesses.

## Checks for anyone running or funding training

1. Keep two dates apart. The 7 February 2025 Cabinet note funds the Skill India Programme for 2022-23 to 2025-26, at Rs 8,800 crore. The 3 August 2026 Lok Sabha reply says PMKVY 4.0 is extended for two years beyond 31 March 2026. Do not treat those as one sentence.
2. Do not build a state-target tracker for PMKVY 4.0. The 3 August reply says there are no state-wise targets, including for Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra. Targets follow proposals from project implementing agencies.
3. Before a batch is assessed, confirm whether NCVET prescribes on-the-job training for that job role. If it does, assessment comes only after the trainee finishes it. Duration comes from the qualification pack.
4. Match classroom attendance to the Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System. The 12 August 2026 reply says a mismatch with a Kaushal Samiksha Kendra inspection is a high-severity non-compliance. Consequences listed there include suspension, stopping and recovering payments, cancelling accreditation or affiliation, blacklisting, and, in serious cases, a police complaint.
5. Use Rs 49 per hour only where it belongs. The 12 August reply ties that highest Common Norms rate to new-age courses, so providers can fund infrastructure, tools, and trainers. It is not a rate for every course.
6. Record training on the Skill India Digital Hub, which the same reply names as the place for self-paced digital learning alongside mandatory employability-skills modules. Do not keep a private spreadsheet as the scheme record.

## Checks for colleges and school systems

7. If a university or affiliated college claims NCrF adoption, the reference count is AISHE 2023-24 as stated on 10 August 2026: more than 700 universities and over 7,500 affiliated colleges. Adoption is not universal in that sentence.
8. If a degree programme counts skill courses toward the degree, the UGC procedure cited the same day allows up to 50% of credits from skill-based courses. It does not require every programme to reach 50%.
9. For diploma entry, use the AICTE Approval Process Handbook 2023-27 rule cited that day: Class 10 plus a two-year ITI can support second-year diploma entry in engineering and technology, and a two-year undergraduate engineering diploma at NCrF level 5.0 can support third-year lateral entry.

## Figures this checklist will not invent

Do not add a placement rate, a 2026 education-to-GDP ratio, a state ranking, or a PMKVY 5.0 guideline. The 3 August reply, asked about PMKVY 5.0 guidelines, answered by stating the PMKVY 4.0 extension and did not describe a 5.0 text.
