# Investment Implications of India's Public Digital Rails

## Rails change distribution, not the discount rate

An investment implication is a change in who can reach a customer or a government buyer. The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 supports three of those changes with counts. It does not support a valuation, a hurdle rate, or a sector return. This page, updated on 23 September 2026, does not add one.

## Payments: many banks, one rail

UPI in the note is an interoperable real-time rail with 691 banks live and, in January 2026, 21.70 billion transactions worth over ₹28.33 lakh crore. The note says that rail carried 81 percent by volume of India's retail payments. For a payments or merchant-acceptance business, the published fact is distribution on a shared system that already dominates retail volume.

The note does not state what share of that volume any private app keeps, or what it earns per transaction. Those are the inputs a valuation would need. They are not here.

## Commerce and procurement: participation is counted

ONDC's published fact is the seller count. The note says the network launched in 2022 and, as of December 2025, had 1.16 lakh-plus retail sellers across 630-plus cities and towns. The design point in the note is interoperability across platforms, not a single store.

Government eMarketplace's published fact is public-buyer demand. As of November 2025, cumulative gross merchandise value exceeded ₹16.41 lakh crore, with more than 1.67 lakh buyer organisations. Micro and small enterprises are stated at 44.8 percent of cumulative order value.

A company that sells through either channel is using a public on-ramp the note has sized in participants and, for the marketplace, in order value. Neither figure is revenue guidance for a private firm.

## Identity and documents lower a repeated cost

The note says Aadhaar supported over 2,707 crore authentication transactions in 2024-25. DigiLocker had 67.63 crore users as of 5 March 2026, with over 950 crore documents issued by March 2026. The implication is that identity checks and issued documents can sit on public systems. The note does not price that saving for a lender, an insurer, or an employer.

## What not to underwrite from this note

Do not underwrite a total addressable market. Do not treat government transfer volume as private revenue. Cumulative Direct Benefit Transfer had crossed ₹49.09 lakh crore as of January 2026. That is benefits paid, not a sales pool. Do not turn the eight countries where the note says UPI is live into a cross-border revenue line. Presence is not a published revenue pool.
