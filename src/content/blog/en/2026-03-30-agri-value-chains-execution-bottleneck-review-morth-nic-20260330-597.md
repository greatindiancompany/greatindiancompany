# Where FPO and mandi execution still stalls

Registration is no longer the bottleneck in the 10,000 Farmer Producer Organisation scheme. Money after registration is.

The scheme was launched in 2020 with an outlay of ₹6,865 crore. It offers up to ₹18 lakh per FPO over three years for management, a matching equity grant of up to ₹2,000 per member capped at ₹15 lakh per FPO, and a credit guarantee on a project loan of up to ₹2 crore. As on 31 December 2025, 10,000 FPOs were registered. Matching equity of ₹430.77 crore had gone to 6,557 of them. Credit-guarantee cover of ₹662.71 crore had gone to 2,671. A 1 March 2026 reply confirmed the same registration total, including 1,175 all-women FPOs and 23.55 lakh women members. The equity and guarantee counts were not updated in that later reply. On the December figures, equity had reached about two-thirds of registered FPOs and the guarantee about a quarter.

That gap is the operating fact. An FPO can be on the national list, eligible in principle for ₹18 lakh of management support, and still be outside the equity grant and outside the guarantee. Lenders were the reason the guarantee exists. The cover’s reach, 2,671 organisations, shows it has not become automatic.

## The mandi gate is a second list

e-NAM, as on 28 February 2026, had 4,724 FPOs on the platform, beside 1.80 crore farmers and 2.72 lakh traders, across 1,656 mandis. The 4,724 and the scheme’s 10,000 are different registers. Subtracting them would pretend every scheme FPO is supposed to be an e-NAM user, and that every e-NAM FPO came from this scheme. The useful statement is narrower. A national electronic market that has onboarded 4,724 FPOs has not, on that count, become the sales counter for every registered producer company.

Assaying is the practical stall inside the mandis that are integrated. The Centre’s grant is up to ₹75 lakh per mandi for labs, weighing, grading, and related kit. The reply singles out 134 Rajasthan mandis using AI and machine-learning machines to cut test time. It does not say the other mandis in the 1,656 have the same machines. Trade value already shows the uneven result: Rajasthan and Haryana account for about ₹2.54 lakh crore of the ₹4.82 lakh crore traded since inception, while states with many holdings have much thinner e-NAM value. Integration without a working assay, and without a buyer on the screen, is a login.

Cold chain has the same shape, in projects rather than FPOs. As on 30 June 2026, 311 of 409 approved projects were complete and 98 were ongoing. Tamil Nadu had 15 of 26 projects complete. A sanctioned grant that is still an ongoing project is not storage a farmer can book this season. Cumulative releases of ₹2,540.89 crore against sanctions of ₹3,089.08 crore leave a gap of about ₹548 crore that follows milestones, not the harvest calendar.

The execution order, on the official counts, is registration first, equity next, borrowed working capital last, and a mandi or a cold room only where the kit is actually commissioned. A pitch that stops at “10,000 FPOs” or “1,656 mandis” is describing the completed step.

Great Indian Company puts the stall here so the scheme rules stay usable. Those rules, with the rupee caps, are in [the operator’s instruments](/blog/agri-value-chains-for-founders-and-operators-morth-nic-20260330-717). Where the mandis and holdings sit is in [the state pattern](/blog/agri-value-chains-state-wise-patterns-morth-nic-20260330-317).
