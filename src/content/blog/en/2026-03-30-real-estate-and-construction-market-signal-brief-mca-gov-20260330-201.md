# What the RBI House Price Index Showed for Q1 2026-27

The market signal worth reading in late September 2026 is a slow all-India registration-price gain, concentrated in a handful of the eighteen cities the Reserve Bank of India actually measures. It is not a national boom, and it is not the same object as a listed asking price or a lender's valuation.

## The print

On 24 August 2026 the RBI released the House Price Index for Q1 2026-27, press release 2026-2027/958. The index is quarterly. It is built from transaction-level data received from registration authorities. The base year is 2022-23. Coverage is eighteen cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru (listed by the release as Bangalore), Lucknow, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kanpur, Kochi, Hyderabad, Thiruvananthapuram, Pune, Ghaziabad, Thane, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Chandigarh, and Nagpur.

The all-India index increased by 1.1 percent from the previous quarter and stood at 117.5. The release says that quarterly move was primarily driven by Chandigarh, Lucknow, and Thiruvananthapuram. Annual growth was 3.6 percent, the same pace as in Q1 of the previous year, mainly contributed by Chandigarh, Jaipur, Kanpur, Lucknow, and Thiruvananthapuram.

A 3.6 percent annual rise on a registration-based index is the fact. It does not, by itself, say what a new launch in a micro-market outside these eighteen cities did, or what an under-construction quoted rate did.

## What this index refuses to be

The series was rebased in 2025. An RBI release on 9 October 2025 moved the base from 2010-11 to 2022-23 and added eight cities to the earlier ten: Hyderabad, Thiruvananthapuram, Pune, Ghaziabad, Thane, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Chandigarh, and Nagpur. Comparisons that splice the old 2010-11 level onto 117.5 are not comparisons the Bank has published in this release.

City time series are on the Database on Indian Economy under Statistics, Real Sector, Prices and Wages: [data.rbi.org.in](https://data.rbi.org.in/DBIE/#/dbie/home). The press note's city names are contributors, not a ranking of all eighteen. A city absent from the sentence is not a city the release has declared flat.

## The other official price system

National Housing Bank RESIDEX is a second official system, not a footnote to the RBI index. NHB's [about page](https://residex.nhbonline.org.in/Dashboard/About) describes three housing price indices because three prices can exist for the same city: registered prices from sub-registrar offices, assessment prices from primary lending institutions, and market prices for under-construction stock. Under-construction prices, on that page, are developer inventory at different stages of construction. They are primary-market stock and do not include resale.

From the quarter ended March 2026, NHB publishes a series with FY 2024-25 as the base year, after a FY 2017-18 series that had been updated through the December 2025 quarter. Coverage on the about page is 50 cities, including 18 state and Union Territory capitals and 37 smart cities, with an intention to go past 100. The [live dashboard](https://residex.nhbonline.org.in/) labels the assessment-price view with base year FY 2024-25 and says the calculation uses a four-quarter moving average. This brief does not copy a city year-on-year figure from that dashboard. The table is the place to read the current quarter, and it moves.

RBI's 117.5 and an NHB assessment index are not convertible without a method note. One is a central-bank index of registered transactions in eighteen cities on a 2022-23 base. The other is a housing-bank family of indices, now on a 2024-25 base, across a wider city set, and split by the stage of the price.

## How an operator should use the signal

Use the RBI print to answer a narrow question: in the cities and the quarter the Bank measures, did registered prices accelerate? In Q1 2026-27 the all-India answer was no acceleration in the annual rate. The 3.6 percent pace was unchanged from the year-earlier quarter, on a small quarterly step of 1.1 percent. Use NHB when the question is assessment value, under-construction primary stock, or a city outside the RBI eighteen. Use neither as a substitute for the price in a sale agreement, which under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act is a project-level disclosure on the state regulator's site.
