## Work follows a public function

An opportunity in healthcare access is a job the official design already assigns to someone, and that a state or a hospital cannot finish alone. It is not a market-size slide. None of the documents used here publish a total addressable market for vendors. Where a rupee figure appears below, it is a scheme outlay or a benefit ceiling, not a vendor revenue pool.

## Claims support where the state holds the risk

In trust mode, the National Health Authority says the State Health Agency pays hospitals and may hire an implementation support agency for claims and related work. The agency still owns empanelment, beneficiary identification, audits, and payment. A company can sell claims operations, medical audit, or hospital helpdesks into that gap. The buyer is the agency or its support contractor. The rules are the package master, the reserved public-only list, and the grievance system reached at 14555. Insurance-mode states buy a different service: the insurer pays, under premium and margin rules published on the Authority’s about-page. Selling a trust-mode product into an insurance-mode state without reading the tender is a category error.

## Drugs, diagnostics, and buildings at the clinic

Ayushman Arogya Mandirs are supposed to provide free essential drugs and diagnostics across twelve service groups, led by a mid-level provider with multi-purpose workers and ASHAs. The ministry has approved, under PM-ABHIM, 10,609 building-less mandirs and 5,456 urban mandirs, inside a ₹64,180 crore mission for 2021–22 to 2025–26. The work around that list is construction quality, laboratory fit-out, medicine supply, and the recording tools a small team will actually use. The mandir page also says states may partner with non-profits and private actors to fill primary-care gaps. That sentence is the opening. It still sits inside public protocols and public reporting.

Urban mandirs and urban polyclinics matter because the standards recognise multi-specialty urban primary health centres for ambulatory specialist care. City operators who only know corporate hospitals are looking at the wrong door for that work.

## District hospitals and public labs

Approvals also include 744 district integrated public health laboratories and 621 critical care hospital blocks, plus 2,151 block public health units. Indian Public Health Standards 2022 define the secondary-care facility those blocks are meant to strengthen, and they prefer in-house clinical staff over outsourced clinical work. The opportunity is equipment, lab operations, and critical-care support that helps a public hospital meet a standard and keep a PM-JAY package in the district. Public hospitals are paid at par with private ones for scheme work. A tool that assumes a private-only counterparty misses half the rate card.

## Digital rails, kept in their lane

The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, launched in September 2021, offers a 14-digit health account, a facility registry, a professionals registry, and a sandbox for software that uses them. Scan-and-share, described from October 2022, is an outpatient registration flow. That is a real integration market for hospital information systems. It does not authorize a PM-JAY package and it does not check SECC eligibility. Products that blur the two will fail a procurement question from any serious state agency.

## Work that is smaller than it looks

Empanelment and de-empanelment mean a private hospital’s scheme revenue can be switched off. Quality systems and claim discipline are therefore operating work, not a branding exercise. Beneficiary identification is a lookup in the Authority’s own system. A 2021 Authority notice arranged free PVC PM-JAY cards with Common Service Centres. That notice is not the September 2024 rule covering people aged 70 and above without an income test. Charging people to discover their own eligibility cuts against the public citizen portal the about-page already describes.
