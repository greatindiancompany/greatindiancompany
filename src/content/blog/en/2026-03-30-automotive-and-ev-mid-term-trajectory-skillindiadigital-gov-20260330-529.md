# The Official Timelines Still Running on India's EV Schemes

The mid-term path is a set of end dates the government has already printed. It is not a demand forecast. Four clocks are running at once: a demand-incentive scheme that now ends on 31 March 2028, a sales-linked manufacturing scheme that pays a year late, a cell programme with a two-year gestation, and a charger stock the power ministry has described out to 2030.

## Demand incentive: one terminal date, with a closed segment inside it

[S.O. 4424(E) of 10 August 2026](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/Gazette%20Notification%2010%2008%202026%20on%20enhancement%20of%20Outlay%2011900%20cr.pdf) says PM E-DRIVE, outlay ₹11,900 crore, runs from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2028. EMPS-2024, from 1 April to 30 September 2024, sits inside that window. The gazette says 31 March 2028 is the terminal date for all segments, and that no payments will be made after it. The last date to file any claim is 31 December 2027.

Two limits can end a segment sooner. The scheme is fund-limited, a point the ministry already made when it first extended the scheme on 7 August 2025 inside the old ₹10,900 crore: if funds for a sub-component run out, claims stop. And L5 electric three-wheelers were closed on 26 December 2025 because the target sales were achieved. A timeline that draws a single bar to March 2028 for every vehicle type will mis-state L5.

The two-wheeler path inside the open window is a step-down that has already happened. FY 2024-25 was ₹5,000 per kWh, capped at ₹10,000. From 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028 the gazette sets ₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹5,000, and at 15 percent of ex-factory price. The trajectory of the rate is flat at the lower level through the remaining scheme, unless a later notification reviews it. The gazette itself says the per-kWh amount is subject to review as vehicle costs fall, and would be notified if it changes.

Penetration is the only adoption path the ministry has put in a sentence. The 28 July 2026 reply says the electric share rose from 0.7 percent in FY 2019-20 to 8.2 percent in FY 2025-26, and says no impact assessment of fuel or emissions has been done. The 8.2 percent is a reported level. It is not a projection to 2028.

## Manufacturing incentive: pay in the year after the sale

The [PLI Auto page](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/en/pli-scheme-automobile-and-auto-component-industry) sets incentives from FY 2022-23 to FY 2026-27 and disbursement from FY 2023-24 to FY 2027-28. The [January 2026 review](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2214039) says the first performance year was FY 2023-24, with ₹322 crore disbursed in FY 2024-25, and ₹1,999.94 crore disbursed for performance year FY 2024-25. The mid-term fact is the lag. A plant that qualifies in a given year shows up in the Union government's cash outflow the next year, and only for variants with at least 50 percent domestic value addition.

The cell clock started earlier. Approved in May 2021, the ₹18,100 crore programme runs seven years: two of gestation, five of performance, aimed at 50 GWh. By the January 2026 review, 40 GWh had been awarded and one firm had 1 GWh installed, in pilot production since March 2024. The performance years are the part of that seven-year design still being used to judge output. The review does not restate the calendar end date in a single line; it states the structure. Count forward from May 2021 only as a reading of "seven years," and prefer a later gazette if the ministry reprints the end date.

## The 2030 line is a charger aim

A [Ministry of Power note](https://www.powermin.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/fab513bc9acc0ad7a794454fe1e329f4.pdf) says the 2024 guidelines should help take chargers from 34,000, as the note puts it, to about 1 lakh by 2030. That is the furthest official year in this set. It sits in a July 2025 upload. PM E-DRIVE's ₹2,000 crore charging line ends with the scheme in March 2028, so the path from the July 2026 sanction of 6,562 scheme chargers to a national stock near 1 lakh is not funded entirely inside PM E-DRIVE.
