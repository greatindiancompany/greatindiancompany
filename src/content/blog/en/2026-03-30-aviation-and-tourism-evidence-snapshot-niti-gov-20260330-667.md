# The aviation and tourism figures that have a date on them

This page is the check-list of figures used across the aviation and tourism briefs. Each line names the series, the date, and the document. A figure without those three is not ready to quote.

## Regional connectivity

- 679 RCS routes operationalised since the scheme began; 348 still operational. Viability-gap funding ₹807.02 crore in 2023-24, ₹628.42 crore in 2024-25, ₹738.13 crore in 2025-26 (provisional). Lok Sabha, 23 July 2026.
- Of the routes discontinued before the support tenure ended, the state annexure sums to 165. Uttar Pradesh 34, Uttarakhand 17, Assam 15. Same reply.
- As on 10 July 2026: 679 routes, 95 airports including 17 heliports and 2 water aerodromes, 168 lakh passengers, 3.58 lakh flights. Lok Sabha, 30 July 2026.
- 188 RCS routes made operational in the last three financial years. Cumulative viability-gap funding as on 30 June 2026 includes Karnataka ₹823.73 crore, Maharashtra ₹632.29 crore, and Uttar Pradesh ₹523.87 crore. Lok Sabha, 6 August 2026.
- Modified UDAN launched 4 July 2026. Window 2026-27 to 2035-36. Outlay ₹28,840 crore. Target: 120 new destinations and 4 crore passengers over ten years. Inside the outlay: ₹12,159 crore for 100 airports and ₹3,661 crore for 200 heliports. Lok Sabha, 6 August 2026. The seat-cap, permit, and taper rules are in the PIB note of 16 July 2026.

## Airline traffic and fleet statements

- Domestic airlines, passengers carried: January–July 2026, 984.03 lakh, against 977.79 lakh, annual growth 0.64 percent. DGCA monthly notes. Single months from successive cumulatives: May 153.91 lakh, June 134.64 lakh, July 119.99 lakh.
- June carriers: IndiGo 89.20 lakh, Air India group 32.22 lakh, Akasa 8.61 lakh, SpiceJet 2.63 lakh. July: 80.82, 28.75, 6.65, and 1.87 lakh. Same DGCA releases.
- On 16 July 2026 the civil aviation minister said daily domestic traffic was about five lakh passengers and that May had served over 1.5 crore.
- On 8 May 2026 he said the commercial fleet is expected to reach 1,100 aircraft by 2027 and over 2,250 by 2035, with outstanding deliveries of 1,640 aircraft. That is his statement at the leasing summit, not a DGCA register extract.
- On 30 July 2026 the ministry said it does not choose airline fleets or networks.

## Airports

- Capacity over 580 million passengers a year. Traffic handled in 2025-26 about 420 million. AAI and private capex about ₹19,260 crore, ₹19,317 crore, and ₹17,840 crore in 2023-24, 2024-25, and 2025-26. Lok Sabha, 6 August 2026.
- Greenfield: 25 in-principle approvals, 14 operationalised, 6 site clearances. Airports expected to handle more than 3,000 million passengers by 2046-47. The reply does not adopt a “1 billion by 2047” figure that was put in the question. Hub-and-spoke operations launched 25 June 2026. Same day.
- Minister’s airport history on 16 July 2026: 65 airports in 2001, 74 in 2014, 90 added in the following twelve years, 55 strips revived.

## Tourism

- 2024 international tourist arrivals 20.57 million (1.40 percent of world arrivals, rank 20). Foreign tourist arrivals 9.95 million. Non-resident Indians 10.62 million. Earnings US$35.016 billion (2.02 percent of world receipts). India Tourism Data Compendium 2025.
- Domestic tourist visits 2024: 2,948.19 million, up 17.51 percent. Top five states, 60.38 percent: Uttar Pradesh 646.81 million, Tamil Nadu 306.84 million, Karnataka 304.56 million, Andhra Pradesh 290.27 million, Rajasthan 230.08 million.
- Foreign-exchange earnings, January–December 2025: ₹2,73,638 crore, down 6.62 percent from ₹2,93,033 crore in 2024, up 26.4 percent from 2019. Ministry of Tourism monthly table.
- Travel and tourism, 2023-24: about 84.63 million jobs, direct and indirect, and 5.22 percent of GDP. Compendium. PIB on 31 July 2026 pairs 5.22 percent with ₹15.73 lakh crore.
- Delhi and Mumbai airports handled 57.2 percent of foreign air arrivals in 2024. Air was 83.41 percent of foreign arrivals.
