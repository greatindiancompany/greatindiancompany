Evidence about Indian aviation or tourism is a record with a date, an airport or a property, a flight or a guest, and a unit. This page names records that can meet that test. It reprints none of them, and it carries no passenger count, fare, or occupancy.

## Records that can change a decision

Use a row only when you can open the order, the table, or the licence.

- **The operator.** A Directorate General of Civil Aviation requirement, direction, or air operator certificate. A news recap of a circular is not the circular.
- **Traffic that flew.** The Directorate's traffic statistics for a carrier and a sector, or the airline's own record of departures operated. Filed intentions are a different column.
- **Airport charges.** An Airports Economic Regulatory Authority order for a named major airport. Where that regime does not apply, the Airports Authority of India schedule or the operator's published charges. A concession speech is not a tariff.
- **Rights and regional routes.** The air services arrangement or the designation you are relying on, and the UDAN award or discontinuation for that route. A scheme homepage is not the route's status.
- **Security.** A Bureau of Civil Aviation Security requirement the airport programme must meet.
- **The guest.** The visa category as immigration publishes it, and the permit rule of the state, forest, or border authority for that district. Foreign-arrival counts in the Ministry of Tourism's India Tourism Statistics series are a national series. They are not one property's occupancy, and they are not a visa decision.
- **The stay and the gate.** The hotel's own sold-room record. Star classification, if you claim it, from the Ministry of Tourism process. The ticket rule of the Archaeological Survey of India, or of the authority that actually holds the monument gate.

A NITI Aayog homepage, a bank newsroom, and a consulting insights page are not these records. They were paired with earlier drafts of this topic by rotation. They are omitted here rather than replaced with a link this text does not use.

## What a usable row contains

Date, airport or property, flight or room, unit, and who wrote it down. "India" is not a place for an operating decision. A direction word with no unit is not a row. The series worth keeping week to week are on the [signal brief](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-market-signal-brief-niti-gov-20260330-187). When those rows may be called a quarterly change is the [what-changed test](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-what-changed-this-quarter-niti-gov-20260330-107).

If the cell is empty, write the gap: which figure, which airport, which dates. Do not fill it with another city's average.
