# Checks before you borrow, deposit, or book a small-firm loan

These checks follow current Reserve Bank instructions. They are not a credit approval, a coverage calculator, or a list of banks under supervision.

## 1. Before a loan through an app

Use the [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848).

- Confirm which regulated entity is lending. If a service provider shows several offers, each offer should name the regulated entity, the amount, the tenor, the annual percentage rate, the monthly repayment, penal charges if any, and a link to the Key Fact Statement. That comparison rule has applied since 1 November 2025.
- Read the Key Fact Statement before you agree. Under the [circular of 15 April 2024](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12663&Mode=0), a retail or small-firm term loan sanctioned on or after 1 October 2024 carries a statement with a unique proposal number, an annual percentage rate that includes the lender's charges, and a validity of at least three working days when the tenor is seven days or longer, or one working day when it is shorter. A charge that is not on the statement needs your explicit consent later. Credit-card receivables are outside that circular.
- Know the cooling-off exit. The board sets the period, and it cannot be shorter than one day. You may leave by paying principal and the proportionate annual percentage rate, without a penalty. A processing fee can be retained only if the statement disclosed it.
- Check where the money moves. Disbursement should reach your bank account, not the service provider's, aside from the exceptions the Directions list. Repayment should go to the regulated entity, not through a third-party pool account.
- Treat the public app directory as the regulated entity's certified list. The Reserve Bank publishes the submission and does not validate it. A complaint about the app goes to that regulated entity.

## 2. Before you accept a KYC refusal

The [KYC amendment of 14 August 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12893&Mode=0) says no application for onboarding, or for periodic updation, shall be rejected without application of mind, and the officer records the reasons. Persons with disabilities are expressly covered. A video liveness check shall not exclude a person with special needs. If you already have a Central KYC identifier, the master direction allows the institution to use it with your explicit consent, which can avoid submitting the same documents again. An occasional transaction of ₹50,000 or more, or an international money transfer, can itself require customer due diligence.

## 3. Before you leave a large balance at one bank

The [Press Information Bureau note](https://pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1849973) on deposit insurance records a cover of ₹5 lakh per depositor, raised from ₹1 lakh on 4 February 2020. The ceiling aggregates balances in the same right and same capacity across all branches of one insured bank, and it includes interest. A second bank is a second ceiling. From 1 September 2021, if all-inclusive directions restrict withdrawals, the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation is liable for an interim payment up to that cover within 90 days. The note does not insure money above ₹5 lakh at that bank.

## 4. Before a small-firm credit conversation

Under the [priority-sector directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12799&Mode=0), bank loans to micro, small, and medium enterprises qualify as priority sector, and loans up to ₹50 crore to start-ups that meet the enterprise definition can qualify too. Receivables discounted on the Trade Receivables Discounting System for those enterprises can count. Domestic commercial banks, other than regional rural banks and small finance banks, have a micro-enterprise sub-target of 7.5 percent of the relevant credit base. A shortfall can be placed in official funds at a rate linked to Bank Rate, which is the bank's incentive, not a promise that your application will be sanctioned.

## 5. Before you treat a weak bank as an ordinary counterparty

The [prompt corrective action circular of 2 November 2021](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12186&Mode=0) applies to scheduled commercial banks other than small finance banks, payment banks, and regional rural banks. Entry can follow a breach of capital, of net non-performing advances at 6 percent and then 9 and 12 percent, or of the leverage minimum. Mandatory effects start with a stop on dividend or profit remittance. The Reserve Bank's supervisory assessment is part of invocation, so a ratio in a disclosure is not, by itself, a public label that the framework has been applied. Exit looks for four clean quarters, one of them audited, plus supervisory comfort.
