# What to Watch in Indian Rail and Metro Over the Next 90 Days

From 24 September 2026, the next 90 days run to late December 2026. This list is what the ministries have already left open. It is not a calendar of announcements they have promised.

## Freight corridor: watch the project report, not a groundbreaking

The 7 August 2026 Rajya Sabha reply said both the Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor (Ludhiana–Sonnagar, 1,337 km) and the Western corridor (Dadri–JNPT, 1,506 km) are commissioned, with about 443 trains a day. The same reply said a new corridor from Dankuni to Surat was announced in Union Budget 2026 and that preparation of the detailed project report has been taken up. Over the next 90 days the useful question is whether a later PIB or Railway Board note moves that report past “taken up.” Until it does, the operating network is the two commissioned corridors.

## Terminals: 142 open, hundreds still at approval

Commissioned Gati Shakti cargo terminals stood at 142 on 7 August 2026, with about ₹10,000 crore of private investment and 146 million tonnes handled in 2025-26 against 224 million tonnes a year of estimated capacity. The 23 July 2026 reply recorded in-principle approvals at 310 further locations. The policy expects construction within 24 months of approval. The watch item is commissioning notices against that approval stock, not an assumption that 310 locations open this quarter.

## Hiring notices that are already live

The 22 July 2026 reply listed three 2026 employment notifications: 11,127 assistant loco pilots (March 2026), 6,557 technicians (May 2026), and 119 section controllers (June 2026). Panels for more than 3,300 candidates had been finalised in the fiscal year up to 30 June 2026. The next useful prints are exam stages and further panels under those notices. The annual calendar is the document. A social-media vacancy claim that is not a centralised employment notification is not this list.

## Station spend versus the year’s allocation

Plan Head 53 for 2026-27 was allocated ₹11,972 crore, with ₹4,633 crore spent up to June 2026, according to the 23 July reply. The next official expenditure print shows whether customer-amenities work is tracking the allocation. The April release had identified 1,338 stations under the Amrit Bharat Station Scheme and reported modernisation completed at 208. A completion count above 208 is a fact only when the ministry publishes it.

## What not to fill in

FY 2026-27 freight loading for the months after March 2026 is not in the releases used here. The 1,670 million tonne figure is the 2025-26 result. The metro length to cite remains about 1,095 km across 26 cities from the 15 March 2026 PIB note, until MoHUA or PIB issues a later total. Vande Bharat stands at 162 chair-car services and 2 sleeper services as of 23 July 2026. Further sleeper services depend on path, stock, and maintenance infrastructure. Those conditions are the checklist, not a launch date.
