The National Logistics Policy treats people as a logistics input, not as a footnote to highways. It does not, in the papers that define it, tell you how many jobs the sector will add.

The [cabinet note of 21 September 2022](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/policy-introduces-unified-logistics-interface-platform-standardization-monitoring-framework-and-skill-development-for-greater-efficiency-in-logistics-services/) splits the work. Gati Shakti builds the integrated network. The logistics policy is supposed to raise the quality of services and of human resources: streamlined rules, skill development, logistics taught in higher education, and technology on the job. The launch bundle included training courses on Gati Shakti and logistics on the iGOT platform. The [launch speech the same week](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-launches-national-logistics-policy/) said the policy had “immense potential” for employment and that stronger logistics would raise the standing of workers. Potential is not a headcount.

## The only skills numbers in the August 2026 reply

The [11 August 2026 reply](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf) to Starred Question 327 makes two concrete claims.

Logistics-related courses have been introduced in more than 100 universities and institutes. That is a supply of classrooms. It does not say how many seats are filled, which campuses run an operations lab rather than an elective, or whether a graduate can run a yard.

Sixty-eight qualification packs for logistics skills have been made operational, to standardise training and narrow the skill gap. Digital literacy is embedded in those packs so that a trainee is expected to handle the systems the job now uses, not only the physical task. A qualification pack is a standard against which a training provider and an employer can hire. It becomes a labour-market fact only when firms ask for it and candidates hold it. The reply does not publish placement rates.

Those two lines are the official skills impact. A brief that adds “millions of jobs” has left the source.

## What operators should do with a pack, and what they should not

Hire against the task. A multi-modal park, a port yard, a cold chain, and a city distribution floor do not share one pack. The Sectoral Plan for Efficient Logistics, which the same reply says has been started for coal, steel, fertilizers, cement, pharmaceuticals, and food, is a hint that the Centre itself does not think one logistics worker serves every chain. Pharmaceuticals and food pull quality, temperature, and trace. Coal and steel pull heavy haul and rail interface. If your chain is on that list, ask which packs match the nodes you actually run. If it is not, do not assume the 68 cover you.

Digital literacy inside the packs matches the rest of the policy. E-way bills, FASTag, the Unified Logistics Interface Platform, and the Logistics Data Bank only help a firm whose people can use them. A fleet that has bought the software and not the skill will look compliant on a slide and slow on the road.

Gati Shakti University was mentioned by the Prime Minister at the 2022 launch as a future source of talent. The August 2026 reply’s skills lines are the courses and the packs, not a university census. Do not fold an unnamed graduating class into a workforce plan.

## What this does not say about wages or headcount

Nothing in these documents reports logistics employment, driver shortage, or warehouse wages. Those series, when they exist, come from labour surveys and from company rosters. A statistics-ministry homepage does not become a skills source because a template once linked it. The skills source for this policy is the August reply’s 68 packs and 100-plus institutes, plus the 2022 decision to put logistics into higher education and onto iGOT.

Road building still creates construction work. The kilometres in the August reply — 32,370 km of national highways constructed in the three-year window it describes — are a civil-works fact on the [roads ministry’s](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala) side of the house. They are not logistics-operations jobs, and they end when the package ends.

For a founder, the practical reading is short. Ask a training partner which of the 68 packs they deliver. Ask a campus which of the 100-plus courses is a logistics operations course rather than a generic management paper. Keep hiring plans tied to your nodes. Leave the national jobs boom out of the model until someone counts it.

[Great Indian Company](https://greatindiancompany.com/) would rather cite 68 packs than a round jobs number nobody tabled.
