## Separate the revenue lines

A hospital in India can earn from cash patients, from a state scheme wider than PM-JAY, and from PM-JAY packages. The National Health Authority’s design pays the third line only: listed secondary and tertiary admissions for eligible families, at a package rate, cashless to the patient, at an empanelled hospital. The about-page does not abolish cash practice for other patients or other services. An underwriting model that dumps all occupancy into “Ayushman” will overstate scheme revenue and understate cash-price risk.

Ask for a trailing split of billed value: PM-JAY packages, state top-up above the national ₹5 lakh or beyond the SECC list, other insurance, and cash. The top-up is a state liability. If the state has not budgeted it, the line is weaker than the national brand suggests.

## Name the counterparty

In trust mode the State Health Agency pays the hospital and holds the risk. It may use an implementation support agency for claims. In insurance mode an insurer selected by tender pays, and holds the risk, inside margin rules the Authority publishes: administrative-cost bands that move with the claim ratio, surplus returned to the agency, and a sharing rule when claims exceed a threshold. Category A and Category B on that page do not use the same bands. In mixed mode both structures are present. The diligence question is which contract pays this hospital, in this state, this year. A mode cited from a 2022–23 annual report is a starting clue and a stale one until the current tender is read.

Public hospitals are paid at par for the packages they are allowed to do. They are not a footnote. They are an alternative provider on the same rate. States may reserve procedures for them and may deduct a percentage of their claims. Your private asset can be locked out of a procedure the public hospital down the road is paid to perform.

## Case mix the 2024 decision actually changes

The Cabinet decision of 12 September 2024 covers every person aged 70 and above irrespective of income. The Authority’s FAQ makes the ₹5 lakh a family amount for those members. Two older people in one household share it. Younger members of an already covered family do not each gain another ₹5 lakh. Households that were outside the SECC net can now appear in scheme data when someone turns 70. That is a case-mix shift toward older admitted patients, which means more of the procedures older patients need, inside the package list. It is not a doubling of the eligible population. Do not model it as one.

The about-page’s “over 12 crore” families and the 2021 guideline’s “about 10.74 crore households” should both be in the memo, with sources. Utilization belongs in the hospital’s own claim file.

## Capacity the state may build beside you

The ministry’s PM-ABHIM update states a ₹64,180 crore outlay for 2021–22 to 2025–26 and approvals of ₹33,081.82 crore, including 621 critical care hospital blocks and 744 district laboratories. If a block is sanctioned in your catchment, high-acuity volume can move to the public hospital as it is commissioned. Ask for commissioning status, not the national approval total. The published scheme period ended with FY 2025–26. A model that extends the outlay into later years without a new budget line is guessing.

National Health Accounts 2015 figures still printed on the PM-JAY page, including 62 percent out-of-pocket spending, are labelled 2015. They are not the residual cash market for this hospital in 2026.

## A short diligence pack

Current empanelment letter and any de-empanelment history. The state package master and the reserved-public list. The payment mode and recent settlement lags. The share of revenue from the 70-plus cover versus the SECC base, if the hospital can split it. Nearby public critical-care and laboratory projects. A quality certificate only if you have seen it. The November 2021 empanelment guideline is the national frame; the state adds the live criteria.
