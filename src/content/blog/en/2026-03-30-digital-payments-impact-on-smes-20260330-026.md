A merchant, an acquirer, or an investor in acceptance wants two things from official data: how people pay at a counter, and how wide the acceptance kit is. The Reserve Bank's Payment System Indicators - August 2026 answers part of that, and it leaves a clean gap where person-to-merchant UPI would be. The figures are provisional.

## The acceptance stocks RBI prints

The infrastructure section is in lakh, "as on" the date.

| Stock | As on August 2025 | As on August 2026 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Static UPI QR codes | 6,977.47 | 8,130.31 |
| Bharat QR codes | 65.73 | 53.30 |
| PoS terminals | 119.65 | 102.08 |

Static UPI QR codes at 8,130.31 lakh are 813.03 million codes. RBI says the line counts static UPI QR codes and that it has been published since September 2020. One shop can hold many codes, and a dynamic QR shown on a till is outside this line. The number is a code stock.

Bharat QR, at 53.30 lakh, is a much smaller reported stock than a year earlier in this table. PoS terminals are reported at 102.08 lakh, against 119.65 lakh as on August 2025. The release says card and prepaid figures may not be comparable across periods after definition changes. Quote the stocks. Keep the comparability note tied to any story about shops opening or closing.

## What passed over card terminals

In August 2026 the card block splits point-of-sale payments from the rest.

Credit cards at PoS: 3,088.37 lakh transactions, ₹79,355 crore. Other credit-card payments: 3,236.11 lakh, ₹1,23,094 crore. On credit cards, the "other" line, which includes e-commerce, carried more transactions and more value than the PoS line.

Debit cards at PoS: 761.34 lakh, ₹22,829 crore. Other debit-card payments: 277.31 lakh, ₹13,868 crore. Debit-card spending in this table still leans to the physical terminal.

Prepaid cards at PoS: 862.41 lakh, ₹1,258 crore. Other prepaid-card payments: 1,382.69 lakh, ₹3,717 crore.

These are card transactions at terminals and in the residual category. They are not a census of small shops.

## The merchant line that carries the BHIM name

BHIM Aadhaar Pay is a debit-transfer line, separate from UPI. In August 2026 it recorded 26.75 lakh transactions and ₹720 crore. The average is about ₹2,692. In the August 2025 column of the same table the line was 25.38 lakh and ₹718 crore.

That is the BHIM-named series inside this RBI release. Volume for the BHIM application, as a UPI payer app, is published by NPCI on its BHIM product statistics. This brief does not copy an app total from anywhere else.

## The UPI gap that matters for a merchant

UPI in August 2026 was 2,45,089.58 lakh transactions and ₹29,82,356 crore. The RBI table does not split that into person-to-person and person-to-merchant. A merchant-discount or P2M share has to come from an NPCI or RBI note that actually publishes the split. Until it does, the acceptance facts a reader can stand on here are the static QR stock, the Bharat QR stock, the PoS stock, the card PoS flows, and BHIM Aadhaar Pay.
