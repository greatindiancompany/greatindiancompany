# The Diligence File for an Indian Pharma or Biotech Claim

An investment memo about Indian pharma or biotech is checkable. The government has published the schemes, the park locations, the regulator’s lane, and the dates on which counts were true. Diligence is the act of putting the company’s claim next to that record. What follows is the file to ask for, and the mismatches that should slow a commitment.

## The scheme letter

Ask which scheme, by its gazette name. “PLI” is not a name. The bulk-drug scheme and the pharmaceuticals scheme have different product boundaries, stated again in the 6 February 2026 Lok Sabha reply: the second covers inputs other than those notified under the first, plus high-value drug categories, and it allows brownfield projects. The first, in that reply, is greenfield and tied to 41 identified products. If the company claims both incentives for one notified bulk drug, the reply’s boundary sentence is the question to put in the next meeting.

Ask for the approval letter or, if the company is only intending to apply, the invitation it believes is open. The Department’s schemes page lists invitations and corrigenda with dates, including a bulk-drug corrigendum dated 4 May 2026. A closed 2021 application window for the pharmaceuticals PLI, which the Press Information Bureau described as 2 June to 31 July 2021, is not a current offer. The approved-applicant lists dated 6 November 2023 for bulk drugs and 3 May 2023 for pharmaceuticals are name lists as of those dates. A company absent from the relevant list, and holding no later approval, should not be modelled as an approved beneficiary.

Ask how the incentive is calculated. For the bulk-drug scheme, the March 2020 cabinet decision uses incremental sales over 2019–20 for six years, at 20 percent for fermentation products and 10 percent for chemical synthesis, inside a ₹6,940 crore envelope. Later guidelines can adjust claim mechanics. The model should cite the guideline version the company will actually file under. The cabinet’s expected incremental sales of ₹46,400 crore is a scheme-level expectation. It is not the company’s revenue.

## The site letter

If the memo says the plant will sit in a bulk-drug park, the February 2026 reply allows three answers: Jambusar in Bharuch through the Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation, Haroli in Una through Himachal Pradesh Bulk Drug Park Infrastructure Ltd., or Nakkapalli in Anakapalli through Andhra Pradesh Bulk Drug Infrastructure Corporation. Any other district is not an approved central bulk-drug park under that reply. Tamil Nadu is specifically absent.

Ask which common facilities the tenant’s process needs, and match them to the reply’s stage. A floated steam tender in Himachal Pradesh, power-design approval still in process in Andhra Pradesh, and utility execution underway in Gujarat are different availability dates. The central grant ceiling is ₹1,000 crore per park. The tenant’s own reactor cost is not inside the published common-infrastructure figure. Get the company’s capex from the company.

A biotech-park or incubator claim belongs on a different letter. DBT’s scheme note names specific centres, including Lucknow, Hyderabad, TICEL, the Golden Jubilee park for women in Chennai, Guwahati, Cochin, and Bengaluru. BIRAC’s map names incubators. BioE3 is a 24 August 2024 policy with thematic sectors, not an allotment. A sentence that says “we are in BioE3” needs a grant, a hub membership, or a partnership document. The policy approval alone does not confer one.

## The licence letter

Ask for the manufacturing licence and who issued it. CDSCO’s portal places new drugs, clinical trials, standards, and import quality with the centre, and records that state FDAs issue licences SUGAM can store. A new-drug or trial-stage company should show the SUGAM filing and, for trials, the ethics-committee and six-monthly report duties under the 2019 rules. Every manufacturing site should show its position against Schedule M as substituted by G.S.R. 922(E) on 28 December 2023. An incentive approval with no licence path is a financing story waiting on a health-ministry outcome.

## Mismatches that matter

A volume rank with no year, an export figure with no year, or a jobs total the cabinet note never published are presentation risks. FY 2021–22 exports in the 1 September 2022 PIB note are real for that year. A headcount is not in the 2020 employment sentence. A link to the renewable-energy ministry does not evidence any line in this file.

The underwriting logic for the incentive itself is in [how to read a PLI award](/blog/pharma-and-biotech-investment-implications-mnre-gov-20260330-234).
