# Climate Adaptation for Policy Teams in India

A policy note that helps is one that assigns each sentence to the right instrument. A note that stacks every climate adjective into one paragraph cannot be implemented by a state, a city, or a project unit.

## Four lines, each with a date

The National Action Plan on Climate Change was released on 30 June 2008. On 21 July 2025, the Press Information Bureau reported it as nine missions, including human health, revised to sit with India's Nationally Determined Contribution, and at various stages of implementation. That is the frame. It is not a clearance.

State Action Plans on Climate Change exist, on that same reply, in 34 States and Union Territories, for state-specific actions. The files are on the environment ministry's [state plan page](https://moef.gov.in/state-action-plan-on-climate-change). A district note that never names the state's plan is still a preamble.

The National Adaptation Fund for Climate Change was set up in 2015-16. NABARD is the National Implementing Entity, and the State Steering Committee is the gate, as PIB described on 5 February 2018. The fund was made a non-scheme in November 2022. As of the 5 August 2024 reply, the stock was 30 projects in 27 States and Union Territories, at ₹847.48 crore of sanctioned project cost. Put those sentences in the fund paragraph. Do not put them in the mission paragraph.

Hazard rules that missions do not replace: the National Disaster Management Authority's [guidelines](https://ndma.gov.in/Governance/Guidelines), the India Meteorological Department's warning tests, and the Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019, with the mainland hazard line. A heat action plan does not demarcate a coast. A coastal clearance does not declare a heat wave.

## One spending sentence, labelled

PIB on 29 January 2026 reports adaptation and resilience-related domestic spending of 3.7 percent of GDP in FY16 and 5.6 percent in FY22. Place it next to development budgets. Do not place it next to the fund's ₹847.48 crore, or the reader will think they are the same money.

## Keep mitigation in its own paragraph

The July 2025 reply's solar capacity and non-fossil share answer a different question from a heat plan or a hazard line. Merging them produces a note that looks busy and cannot be assigned to an office. How the missions themselves split adaptation from mitigation is the [policy explainer](/blog/climate-adaptation-policy-shift-explainer-makeinindia-20260330-175).

## The review question

For a named district: which state-plan action applies, which disaster guideline applies, whether an adaptation-fund file exists, and whether today's IMD warning changes anything this week. If the note cannot answer those four, it is not finished.

The ministry's FAQ of 1 December 2021 still offers a design test worth keeping: protect the poor, hold growth and ecological sustainability together, and implement through local governments and public-private partnerships. That is a test for a scheme. It is not a result.
