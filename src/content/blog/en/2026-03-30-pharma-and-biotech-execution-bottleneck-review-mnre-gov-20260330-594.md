# Park Execution Is a Utility Schedule, Not a Launch Line

The bottleneck in India’s bulk-drug park scheme is visible in the verbs of the 6 February 2026 Lok Sabha reply. Awarded, floated, under installation, in process, underway. Those verbs are the execution review. A ground-breaking photograph does not outrank them.

## What the parks were supposed to share

The cabinet decision of 21 March 2020 funded common infrastructure so that manufacturers would not each build solvent recovery, distillation, power and steam, and effluent treatment. The February 2026 reply expands that list: central effluent treatment, solid waste, storm-water drains, common solvent storage and recovery, a warehouse, a power substation, raw and demineralised water, steam, cooling, logistics, an advanced testing laboratory with microbiology and stability chambers, an emergency response centre, a hazardous-operations audit centre, and a centre of excellence. Central money is for those facilities, capped at ₹1,000 crore per park or at 70 percent of their cost, 90 percent in Himachal Pradesh, whichever is less. The scheme runs through FY 2026–27.

A tenant’s bottleneck is the subset of that list the process cannot start without. A chemical-synthesis API plant waits on solvent recovery, steam, and effluent treatment. A fermentation plant waits on those plus reliable utilities and a way to handle biological waste. The laboratory in the list matters when the park, rather than a private QC block, is supposed to cover complex testing. None of that runs because the grant was approved in principle in September 2022.

## Three sites, three stages

Gujarat, through the Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation at Jambusar, Bharuch, is the furthest of the three in the reply’s language. Civil tenders for roads, drainage, water, effluent collection, and racks have been awarded, and those works are at an advanced stage of completion. Tenders for the effluent treatment plant, solvent recovery, and the treatment, storage, and disposal facility have also been awarded, and execution is underway. Advanced and underway are still unfinished. They are the strongest official stage words in the reply, and they are not “commissioned.”

Andhra Pradesh, through Andhra Pradesh Bulk Drug Infrastructure Corporation at Nakkapalli, Anakapalli, has awarded tenders for internal roads, power, water, drainage, and utility buildings. Fencing is complete. The external water pipeline is under installation. Approval of the power-infrastructure design is in process. Additional land is under acquisition, with environmental clearance for that land granted in January 2026. Power design and extra land are classic critical-path items. A plant schedule that assumes grid power and the full site boundary should carry those two items as open.

Himachal Pradesh, through Himachal Pradesh Bulk Drug Park Infrastructure Ltd. at Haroli, Una, has awarded tenders for internal roads, drainage, bridges, and water, and those works are in progress. The tender for zero-liquid-discharge facilities, including the effluent plant and steam generation and distribution, has been floated. Site demarcation, grading, and levelling are in progress. A floated tender is a document on a procurement portal. It is earlier than an awarded contract, which is earlier than execution. For a fermentation or synthesis tenant, the floated steam and effluent package is the binding constraint in this reply.

## The other bottleneck is commissioning of PLI plants

Parks are shared capex. The bulk-drug PLI is private greenfield production. On 1 September 2022 an official note said 14 of 51 approved projects had been commissioned. The February 2026 reply does not repeat a commissioned count. It says 48 projects are approved for 33 bulk drugs. The execution question for a PLI project is commercial manufacture of the approved molecule and a valid claim under the current guidelines. Approval lists dated 6 November 2023 on the Department’s site name firms. Commissioning is a later fact and has to be dated when it is claimed.

State drug licences and revised Schedule M sit on this path as well. A common effluent plant can be ready while a tenant still lacks a manufacturing licence. CDSCO and the state licensing authority are the execution gate for sale. The Department of Pharmaceuticals is the execution gate for the grant and the incentive.
