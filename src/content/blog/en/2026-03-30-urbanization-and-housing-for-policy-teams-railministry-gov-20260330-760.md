# How the Centre, the States, and the Cities Split Urban Housing

## One word, three governments

“Urban housing” in an Indian file is often a central scheme, a state rule, and a city project described as if they were one decision. They are not. A policy team that publishes a single owner for all three will be wrong in a way a legislator can check.

## What MoHUA holds

The ministry launched the Smart Cities Mission, AMRUT, and Housing for All in urban areas on 25 June 2015. It holds the scheme guidelines, the central share, and the national progress statements. For Smart Cities it chaired the apex committee, and its mission page now records financial closure on 31 March 2025, claims of ₹47,458 crore against a ₹48,000 crore outlay, and a decision that no new city will be taken up. For AMRUT 2.0, the 27 October 2021 PIB release records Cabinet approval on 12 October 2021 and an indicative outlay of ₹2,99,000 crore, with releases in instalments of 20:40:40. For PMAY-Urban 2.0, the Cabinet decision of 9 August 2024 and the assistance of ₹2.30 lakh crore are central. The Interest Subsidy Scheme’s caps were stated to Parliament through PIB on 24 July 2025.

MoHUA also holds the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act as its administering ministry on India Code, the 2016 commencement notification, the 2017 commencement document, and the public list of state authority websites. It does not hold the project register. The 31 July 2026 force majeure advisory is a central document about how state authorities may extend registration. The extension order, if any, is still the authority’s.

The Registrar General, not MoHUA, holds the population count. MoHUA’s urban-growth page republishes Census 2011. Census 2027’s calendar and the ₹11,718.24 crore operational outlay are PIB releases of the census exercise. A housing ministry dashboard should not be asked to invent the urban share in between.

## What the state holds

PMAY-Urban 2.0 requires a memorandum, a demand survey, and an Affordable Housing Policy. The December 2024 note could count in-principle houses only for States and Union Territories that had signed. Central assistance per unit then differs: ₹2.25 lakh in the North-East and the hill and specified territories named in that note, ₹2.50 lakh in other Union Territories, ₹1.50 lakh in other states. A state briefing that quotes ₹2.50 lakh as the central cheque in a general-category state is overstating the Centre’s share by a lakh a unit.

AMRUT asks the state to aggregate city plans into a State Water Action Plan and to meet reform conditions, including property tax and user charges, for uninterrupted funds from the third year. Reuse targets are written at state level: at least 20 percent of city water demand and 40 percent of industrial water demand from treated used water.

Smart Cities put a High Powered Steering Committee under the chief secretary. Advisory No. 27 of 10 June 2025 asks that committee to decide the future role of the special purpose vehicles. After central closure, the residual decision is a state decision.

RERA rules, the authority, and the appellate tribunal are established by the appropriate government. Two states can apply one Act and present a buyer with different agreement forms and different register designs. Policy comparison has to cite the rule, not the Act’s short title alone.

## What the city holds

The urban local body identifies PMAY beneficiaries and, with the state, grounds houses. The first scheme’s December 2024 note tied sanctions to 4,618 such bodies. AMRUT’s tap and sewer connection is a city service, described in a City Water Action Plan, with a City Water Balance Plan underneath it. The Smart Cities special purpose vehicle implemented the city proposal; the advisory forum was the collaboration body. Integrated command centres now sit in all 100 selected cities. Keeping them useful is a city operating problem under state direction.

Census houselisting windows were chosen by each State and Union Territory inside April to September 2026. A city’s housing-condition numbers will arrive through that census channel, not through the municipal budget speech, unless the Registrar General has published them.

## A reporting rule that survives an audit

Publish four lines and refuse a fifth. Central share sanctioned and released, with the scheme and the date. State memorandum and reform status, with the document. City project or beneficiary progress, with the annex date, such as 31 March 2026 for Smart Cities. Legal status on the state RERA register, with the registration number. The fifth line, a blended “urban transformation score,” has no official definition. Great Indian Company does not publish one.
