# The 31 March 2026 PLI snapshot, with the caveats

This page collects the PLI figures that official documents state for **31 March 2026**, and the older figures a careful reader will otherwise mix in. It does not update them past the replies cited. The update date of this brief is 24 September 2026.

## The parliamentary stock

Source: written reply of the Minister of State for Commerce and Industry, Jitin Prasada, to [Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1423](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1423_Rd4xWf.pdf), answered on 28 July 2026. Position as on 31 March 2026:

- 14 sectors
- Approved outlay: ₹1.91 lakh crore
- Applications approved: 892
- Actual investment: over ₹2.40 lakh crore
- Production and sales: over ₹22.66 lakh crore
- Exports: over ₹15.2 lakh crore
- Employment: over 14.15 lakh, direct and indirect, of which 8.4 lakh is described as direct
- Cumulative incentive disbursement: ₹35,354 crore

DPIIT is described as the nodal department for coordination and monitoring. Administrative ministries implement their schemes.

DPIIT's Annual Report 2024-25 states the incentive outlay as Rs. 1.97 lakh crore. That is an earlier official figure. It is not a second outlay on top of ₹1.91 lakh crore. The report is the [DPIIT Annual Report 2024-25](https://www.dpiit.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/06/3d9c9c2daeefb97bb9ce964370938b71.pdf).

## The previous official stock

Source: Rajya Sabha reply of 27 March 2026, [PIB PRID 2246089](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246089). Position as on 31 December 2025:

- Investment over ₹2.16 lakh crore
- Production and sales over ₹20.41 lakh crore
- Exports over ₹8.3 lakh crore
- Employment more than 14.39 lakh, direct and indirect
- Electronics incentives disbursed about ₹15,554 crore
- Automobile and auto-component incentives disbursed about ₹2,377.56 crore

Employment in this earlier reply is higher than in the March 2026 stock. Investment, sales, and exports are lower. Do not "correct" either reply.

The 21 July note also says cumulative exports reported under the schemes were ₹4 lakh crore as on 31 March 2024 and ₹15.2 lakh crore as on 31 March 2026, with a column for FY 2024–25 at ₹6.5 lakh crore. The December 2025 export stock of over ₹8.3 lakh crore sits between the FY 2024–25 column and the 31 March 2026 stock. That is consistent with a cumulative series. It is not a reason to relabel the columns as annual increments.

Product claims in the July replies — mobile phone production about 2.4 times since scheme launch, imports down about 77 percent, about 99.2 percent of phones used in India made domestically, pharmaceutical sales over ₹3.64 lakh crore, 191 bulk drugs made in India for the first time, about 55,000 metric tonnes of capacity across 26 APIs — belong to those replies. They are not rows in the investment table.
