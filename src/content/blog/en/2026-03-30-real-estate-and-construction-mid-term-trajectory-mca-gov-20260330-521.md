# What PMAY-U 2.0 Fixes Through 2029, and What It Leaves Open

The mid-term path of urban housing policy is a dated mission, not a trend line. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban has been with the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs since June 2015. PMAY-U 2.0 starts from 1 September 2024 and, on the official FAQ, runs for five years from 2024 to 2029. The ministry page states the new envelope as an investment of ₹10 lakh crore, central assistance of ₹1.6 lakh crore, and one crore additional households. Alongside that, the first mission has been extended to finish houses already sanctioned. Those are two tracks. Completing an old sanction is not the same decision as admitting a new family.

## Who the five years are for

The [FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/PMAYMIS2_2024/faq.html) defines a beneficiary family as husband, wife, and unmarried children, in an urban area, with no pucca house anywhere in India in anyone's name in that family. Income bands are up to ₹3 lakh for EWS, from ₹3 lakh up to ₹6 lakh for LIG, and from ₹6 lakh up to ₹9 lakh for MIG. A pucca house is an all-weather unit whose roof and walls are of durable materials, whether or not the floor is kutcha. A new assisted house has at least two rooms, a kitchen, and a toilet, plus electricity and water.

People who already took a housing benefit from the Centre, a state, or a local body in the last twenty years are out. So are beneficiaries whose PMAY-U houses were curtailed by the central sanctioning committee after 31 December 2023. A family on the rural-urban edge chooses PMAY-Gramin or PMAY-U 2.0 according to jurisdiction, not both. The trajectory therefore cannot be "every household that was ever surveyed." The exclusions are the path.

States participate after a memorandum of agreement. Towns notified during the five years enter through a state gazette and ministry approval. The scheme page's coverage figure, 5,494 towns and urban development authorities, is the inclusion list it states, not a forecast of houses.

## What is supposed to be built

Beneficiaries choose one of four verticals. Beneficiary-led construction is an EWS house of 30 to 45 square metres on land the family holds, or on a heritable, non-transferable patta the state provides at its own cost. Partnership projects can be public or private; states set an upper sale price per square metre of carpet area through the state committee. Affordable rental housing is for people who need a house for a period rather than a title, including migrants, industrial workers, and working women, through conversion of vacant government housing or new construction. The interest subsidy, up to ₹1.80 lakh, applies to loans sanctioned and disbursed on or after 1 September 2024, and it is paid in five yearly instalments only while the loan is active and more than half the principal is still outstanding.

Carpet-area caps differ: up to 45 square metres for the construction verticals, 60 for rental, 120 for the subsidy, and not below 30. Support is not a blank cheque for a large unit.

Ownership conditions bind the path. The house is in the woman's name or in joint name, with stated exceptions where there is no adult woman, or the applicant is a widower, unmarried, separated, or transgender. For five years after completion the house is for residence and is not to be sold or rented. That lock means the mission does not, on its own terms, restock the open sale market inside the decade.

In-situ slum redevelopment is no longer a separate vertical. Tenable slums can be proposed as beneficiary-led lists, or as partnership redevelopment where the slum sits on public land. That is a design change from the first mission, recorded in the FAQ, not a new target invented here.

## What the mission does not decide

It does not repeal the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act. A partnership project offered for sale still meets the state regulator if it is large enough to be registered.

It does not, by itself, enact a rent law. The Model Tenancy Act was circulated in June 2021. The July 2022 parliamentary reply named four states. Rental stock under the affordable-rental vertical can grow while state tenancy statutes stay unreformed. Those are different levers.

It does not publish, on the pages used here, a year-by-year schedule that sums to one crore. The one crore figure is the mission size. Annual city demand is supposed to sit in city-wise plans, then in projects cleared by the state committee and the central committee. A mid-term review that cannot see those sanctions cannot see the trajectory. The dashboard and the sanction orders linked from the [ministry page](https://www.mohua.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/pradhan-mantri-awas-yojana-urban-MjNzYjMtQWa) are the record. This note does not invent the gap between one crore and the houses occupied so far.
