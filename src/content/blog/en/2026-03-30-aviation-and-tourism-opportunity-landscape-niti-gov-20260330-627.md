An opportunity is a handoff an Indian airline or traveler already pays to have done better. A gap with no payer is a research topic. This page lists types. It estimates no market.

## Types you can inspect on one route

- **A turn that protects a slot.** At a constrained airport, a handler or an airline process that returns the aircraft inside the time already allocated. The payer is the airline that loses the next rotation when the turn slips. A lounge is not that service.
- **Maintenance the carrier already buys elsewhere.** A Directorate General of Civil Aviation-approved organisation that can perform a check an airline and its lessor already purchase abroad, on a type you can staff. The opportunity is the signed work. A hangar drawing is not.
- **A thin route that is still there after support.** UDAN and other regional flying open city pairs. The durable version is a schedule that can be crewed and filled without treating viability funding as permanent. If the town cannot fill the aircraft in the lean month, there is no route business yet.
- **The transfer the guest already improvises.** From the airport to the circuit: a car that meets the flight, a permit step described accurately, a room that matches the arrival time. The payer is the person who already landed. A new hotel that ignores the last flight of the day is a different bet, and it belongs on the [capex watch](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-capex-cycle-watch-niti-gov-20260330-347).
- **A stay for a guest who already comes.** A pilgrimage week, a winter heritage circuit, a medical stay beside a hospital that already receives patients, a domestic weekend at a beach that already has seats. The work is a cleaner handoff, not a claim that the place has been discovered.
- **Distribution that sells what exists.** A desk that sells the room and the flight that are on sale, and that does not promise a visa or a park permit the firm cannot issue.

## Filters before you build

Name the payer and the current cost of the bad handoff, using their invoice or their cancellation note. Try the fix for one season before a new terminal or a new hotel. Check the lean weeks and the weather window, not only the festival fortnight. A no on the payer ends the idea.
