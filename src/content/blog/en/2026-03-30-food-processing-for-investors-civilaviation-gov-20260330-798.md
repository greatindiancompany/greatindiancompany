# How an investor should read food processing disclosures

Food processing disclosures in India mix sanctioned project cost, reported beneficiary investment, budget estimates, and memorandums signed at a trade fair. An investor can underwrite the sector only after those words are put back into their documents. This note is a reading guide. It is not a valuation.

## Separate four stocks of capital

The cold-chain book in the Annual Report for 2024-25 is the clearest stock, and it is dated 31 December 2024. Three hundred and ninety-four projects had a total cost of Rs 11,466.38 crore. Private investment was Rs 8,495.53 crore. Grant-in-aid was Rs 2,970.85 crore. The grant is capped at Rs 10 crore a project and is limited to technical civil works and eligible plant and machinery. In general areas the promoter must bring equity of at least 20 percent and a term loan of at least 20 percent. The public money is a minority slice of a project that still has to be built inside 24 months, or 30 in a difficult area.

PLISFPI is a second stock. The Year End Review of 9 January 2026 says beneficiaries have reported investment of Rs 9,702 crore, and that Rs 2,162.553 crore of incentive has been disbursed in 161 cases, against 170 approved proposals. Ask which of those cases is the company in front of you, which category it sits in, and whether its quarterly review is current. The incentive is not equity. The scheme runs through 2026-27, and Demand No. 45 provides Rs 1,200 crore for that year, the same figure as the 2025-26 budget and revised estimates.

The third stock is event intention. World Food India, 25 to 28 September 2025, produced MoUs the ministry valued at more than Rs 1,02,000 crore. A memorandum is a disclosure of interest. It does not appear in the cold-chain cost table. Price it at zero in a base case until it becomes a sanctioned project with a term-loan document.

The fourth is foreign equity. The annual report records 100 percent FDI on the automatic route for food processing, subject to sectoral regulation, and 100 percent on the government route for trading, including e-commerce, in food products manufactured in India. The cumulative equity inflow the Year End Review cites for April 2014 to March 2025 is US$7.33 billion. A single deal's approval route depends on whether the investor is entering manufacturing or trading Indian-made food.

## Ask what happened to budgets in a weak year

PLI's payout year is not automatically a full budget year. The ministry's 2024-25 expenditure statement shows the scheme's budget estimate at Rs 1,444.02 crore, the revised estimate at Rs 700 crore, and actual expenditure at Rs 450.49 crore. In 2025-26 the same line held near Rs 1,200 crore from estimate through actual. An investment case that needs the incentive to arrive on the original budget calendar should show the 2024-25 path as a worked example of delay, and the 2025-26 path as a worked example of a year that held.

PMFME is a smaller cheque and a sharper condition. The grant is 35 percent up to Rs 10 lakh, only with a bank loan, and not for working capital. For a portfolio of micro units, the investor's risk is the bank's sanction and the state's share of a centrally sponsored scheme, not the Rs 10,000 crore headline. The national sanction count, 1,72,707 loans, is evidence that banks have been willing in aggregate. It is not evidence about the next applicant.

## Questions that belong in a diligence list

- Is the grant sanctioned, released, or still an application? The scheme management system is the ministry's status record for PMKSY components.
- What is the drop-dead month for the 24- or 30-month schedule, and has any instalment already been reduced for delay?
- Which PLI category was approved, and what sales base year does the claim use?
- Does the company hold a state licence or a central licence, and is the FoSTaC supervisor ratio covered for every 25 food handlers?
- If the story is export-led, which APEDA line is it: processed vegetables, cereal preparations, miscellaneous preparations, or something else? Those lines do not move together.
- If the story quotes jobs, is the number from ASI 2022-23 (22.96 lakh persons in registered units), from a scheme expectation, or from the company's own payroll?

Priority sector classification of food and agro-processing loans, and the place of cold chain and food parks on the Harmonised Master List of Infrastructure, can change how a lender books the asset. They do not change whether the plant is finished. The finished share, on the ministry's own cold-chain count at end-2024, was 286 projects out of 394.
