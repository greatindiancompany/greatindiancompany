# Food Processing In India: Diligence Questions Before A Term Sheet

## Ask which rule the revenue depends on

A food-processing term sheet fails when the model assumes a scheme, a licence, or an export registration the company does not have. The questions below are answerable from documents. This brief does not supply a valuation, a tonnage, or a rupee outlay. Scheme pages print those. Using them as the company's numbers is the mistake.

## Scheme questions

Which instrument is the project actually under?

- If the answer is PMKSY, which component: cold chain, processing and preservation capacity, an agro-processing cluster, food-safety infrastructure, research institutions, or Operation Greens? If the answer is a new Mega Food Park, or a backward-and-forward linkage project, the ministry page says those were discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle except for committed liability. Ask for the commitment letter, not a slide.
- The same page dates PMKSY continuation through 31 March 2026. That date has passed. Ask what later MoFPI document, if any, the company is relying on. If there is none, the model should not assume a new sanction.
- If the answer is PMFME, is the applicant an individual micro unit or an FPO, self-help group, or cooperative? Is the product the district's One District One Product where the scheme requires or prefers it? For a common facility, is a substantial part of capacity open to hire, and has the State Nodal Agency and the State Level Approval Committee actually moved the file?
- If the answer is PLISFPI, is the product inside the four segments: ready-to-cook or ready-to-eat including millets, processed fruits and vegetables, marine products, or mozzarella cheese? Was the company selected? The page required mandated plant-and-machinery investment in 2021-22 and 2022-23, with 2020-21 spend able to count, and it waived those minimums for entities selected on the innovative or organic path. The incentive period runs through 2026-27 and is fund-limited, with a ceiling fixed at approval. Ask for the approval letter and the ceiling. Do not gross up the scheme's national outlay.

## Licence and export questions

Does the FoSCoS record show a licence or registration whose product list matches the plant? Who is the food safety supervisor, and where is the quarterly handler-training record FoSTaC describes?

If exports are in the model, is the product on APEDA's schedule? If yes, is the exporter registered, and for fresh horticulture is the pack house recognized for that product inside its validity? If the product is organic, was it produced and packed under the National Programme for Organic Production? If the product is marine, which authority registered the exporter? APEDA's schedule is the wrong answer to that last question.

## What not to underwrite from this brief

Do not underwrite a processing-rate gap versus other countries, a state ranking, or an employment total. Those are not in the documents this note relies on. Underwrite the asset you can see: the line, the cold chain, the common facility, or the recognized pack house, plus the licence that lets it run.
