# What Counts as a Market Signal in Urban Housing

## A signal is a rule a counterparty can price

MoHUA does not publish a national house-price index in the releases this brief uses. A sentence about “prices firming” or “demand returning” cannot be anchored there. The signals that are on the page are eligibility caps, a closed subsidy, and a registration statute.

## The interest subsidy tells a lender the boundary

The Press Information Bureau text of 24 July 2025, reporting a Lok Sabha reply, sets the Interest Subsidy Scheme under PMAY-Urban 2.0 as follows. Income up to ₹9 lakh. Home loan up to ₹25 lakh. Property value up to ₹35 lakh. Carpet area up to 120 square metres. Subsidy of 4 percent on the first ₹8 lakh, for up to 12 years, paid in five yearly instalments, with a maximum of ₹1.80 lakh. The loan has to be active, and more than half the principal outstanding, at each release.

That is a market boundary. A loan above ₹25 lakh, a property above ₹35 lakh, or a carpet above 120 square metres is outside the subsidy even if the household’s income qualifies. A lender who books the subsidy on day one, and then watches the principal fall below the halfway mark, is outside the release rule as written. The earlier Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme, which paid up to ₹2.67 lakh and closed on 31 March 2021 for middle-income borrowers and on 31 March 2022 for economically weaker and low-income borrowers, is not a pipeline. PIB records ₹58,885 crore already released to 25,04,220 beneficiaries under that closed scheme.

The per-unit assistance of ₹2.50 lakh under Beneficiary Led Construction and Affordable Housing in Partnership, from the December 2024 PIB note, is a different instrument. It is not added on top of the ₹1.80 lakh in this brief, because the ministry has not published a worked example that stacks them for one household.

## RERA is the disclosure signal, not a price signal

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, as India Code records it, is an Act to regulate and promote the sector, to make sale of a plot, apartment, or building transparent, to protect allottees, and to create an authority, an adjudicating officer, and an appellate tribunal. MoHUA records that Parliament passed it in March 2016 and that the President assented on 25 March 2016.

Section 4 requires seventy percent of the amounts realised from allottees, from time to time, to be deposited in a separate account in a scheduled bank and used only for the cost of construction and the land. That rule changes how a developer may use buyer collections. It does not tell a buyer what the carpet-area price should be. Carpet area is the statutory measure the Act standardises; a sale that is still negotiated on super built-up area is a disclosure problem, which is the point of registration under sections 3 to 19. Those registration sections were outside the 1 May 2016 commencement notification and are covered by the 2017 commencement document MoHUA hosts.

State authorities, not a single national exchange, hold the project register. MoHUA publishes the list of state and Union Territory authority sites. A “national unsold inventory” figure is not in that list.

## What is not a signal

Launch-day language from 25 June 2015, city rankings, and a Smart Cities completion percentage are programme accounts. They describe public projects and civic systems. They are not a transaction price for a private apartment. The Smart Cities Mission’s financial closure on 31 March 2025, with no further budgetary provision for a new city, is a supply signal for municipal technology contracts. It is handled in the investment note, not here.

Read the caps against a live term sheet. If the term sheet sits outside income, loan, property value, or carpet area, the subsidy is not part of the yield. If the project is not on the relevant state RERA register, the separate-account rule has not yet attached in the way a buyer can check.
