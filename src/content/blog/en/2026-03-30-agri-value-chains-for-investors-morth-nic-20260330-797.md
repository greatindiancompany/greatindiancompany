Diligence on an agri value chain walks the crop from field to invoice. The walk replaces a sector paragraph. This page is a question list for that walk. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and it is not a view on returns.

## Questions that should have a document behind them

Ask for the document, not the narrative.

- **Catchment.** Which markets or villages supplied last season, and what share of intake did the top ten supply points represent?
- **Buyer concentration.** Which buyers took volume, on what written specification, and what happens to a lot that misses it?
- **Price position.** Are purchase and sale prices fixed, formula-linked, or open? For how many days is the book uncovered?
- **Loss.** What shrink and reject rates were recorded, by crop, and where were the rejected lots sold?
- **Utilization.** For each cold room or line, what share of design capacity ran in peak weeks and in lean weeks?
- **Working capital.** What did the company pay farmers relative to pickup, and what did buyers pay relative to delivery, from bank dates?
- **Licenses and failures.** Which permits does the step require, and which lots failed a residue, label, or yard check last season?
- **Second season.** Does the model assume the same volume in the weeks the crop is not harvested? If yes, where is that volume contracted?

If the answer is a total addressable market, ask again for last season's lots. ## Risks to put in the memo as scenarios

Weather, an open price, a logistics stop, a buyer who does not pay, and a compliance miss hit different lines. Write them as scenarios with a trigger and a weekly action. The blank map is on the [risk page](/blog/agri-value-chains-risk-and-scenario-map-morth-nic-20260330-557). Do not convert the map into a single risk score.

## Scope

No company is assessed. No multiple is justified. An investor who cannot obtain the documents listed above does not yet have diligence on the chain. They have a pitch.
