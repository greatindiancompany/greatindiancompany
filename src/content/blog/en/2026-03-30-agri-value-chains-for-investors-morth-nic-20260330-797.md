Diligence on an Indian agri value chain walks one path from the farmer to the invoice. The path is mandi trade, minimum-support-price procurement, dairy collection, or export. This page is a question list. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and it states no return.

## Documents, not a sector paragraph

- **Which path.** Is the offtake a commission agent, a state purchase centre, a dairy union or private dairy, or an export contract? A pitch that says "agri" without one of those buyers has not chosen a chain. The cash clocks are on the [investment page](/blog/agri-value-chains-investment-implications-morth-nic-20260330-237).
- **Season.** Which weeks of kharif, rabi, potato loading, or milk flush did cash actually leave last year? A monthly average hides the peak.
- **State law.** Which APMC Act applies, and does the company buy inside a notified yard? The 2020 national trade-area statutes are repealed. Ask for the state position in force.
- **Procurement dependence.** If the model needs paddy or wheat bought at the support price, ask for centre-wise acceptance, not for the Cabinet decision. If the crop is horticulture, do not accept a procurement analogue.
- **Quality failures.** Moisture at the mill, fat and solids-not-fat disputes at the dock, or residue failures on export lots. Ask where rejected lots were sold.
- **Intermediary credit.** How many days did the company pay farmers, and how many days did agents, unions, or importers pay the company, from bank dates?
- **Financeable stock.** For stored commodities, is the warehouse registered with the Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority, and is any electronic negotiable warehouse receipt actually pledged? A godown photograph is not a receipt.
- **Trade and stock-limit exposure.** Any Essential Commodities Act limit or Directorate General of Foreign Trade status change that hit this commodity in the last two seasons, with the notification attached.
- **Lean weeks.** Where is volume contracted when the harvest or the flush is over?

What makes a supporting table usable is on the [evidence page](/blog/agri-value-chains-evidence-snapshot-morth-nic-20260330-677). Weather, an open price, a logistics or power stop, a buyer who does not pay, and a compliance miss should be written as scenarios with a trigger. The blank map is the [risk page](/blog/agri-value-chains-risk-and-scenario-map-morth-nic-20260330-557).

An investor who cannot obtain these documents does not yet have diligence on the chain.
