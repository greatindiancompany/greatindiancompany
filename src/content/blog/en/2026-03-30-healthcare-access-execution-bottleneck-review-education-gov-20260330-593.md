## Two empanelments, one word

The Ayushman Arogya Mandir about-page uses “empanelment” for something a hospital contract does not mean. Frontline workers make a household list and register every person living in the catchment. The page calls that registration a right of residence. It is how the clinic knows who it is responsible for. PM-JAY empanelment is a hospital’s approval to bill listed packages. A family can be on the clinic register and off the hospital-cover list. A hospital can be empanelled for packages and invisible to the clinic’s Monday roster. Execution fails when staff treat these as one event.

## What the clinic is built to finish

The design converts sub-centres for about 3,000 to 5,000 people so that time to care is within 30 minutes, and it converts primary health centres as well. The sub-centre team is multi-purpose workers and ASHAs, led by a mid-level health provider. The primary health centre is the first referral for many conditions, and its medical officer is responsible for the cluster. Twelve service groups run from childbirth to basic mental health. For non-communicable disease, the intended path is early identification, basic management, counselling, adherence, and referral along a written pathway.

The ministry’s 25 March 2025 reply shows how large the front of that path has become on paper: 1,76,573 centres operationalised till February 2025, and crores of hypertension and diabetes screenings. A screening is the start of the path. The bottleneck is everything after the test: the drug, the repeat visit, the referral that a receiving facility accepts, and the transport.

PM-ABHIM approvals include 10,609 building-less mandirs. A centre can be reported operational and still be on a construction list. That is a physical bottleneck sitting under a rising national total.

## Where PM-JAY actually starts

PM-JAY pays for secondary and tertiary hospitalization, cashless, at an empanelled hospital, for an eligible family, up to ₹5 lakh a year. It includes a short pre-admission window and 15 days after discharge. It does not turn a positive screening into an authorized package. The Authority’s information system separates the steps: a beneficiary identification system checks the person, a transaction system records admission through discharge and the claim, and portability depends on that exchange working across states. A patient who was screened at a mandir still has to be eligible, admitted for a listed procedure, and treated at a hospital allowed to perform it.

Some procedures are reserved for public facilities. The referral that makes clinical sense can be a referral the private hospital is not allowed to bill, or a referral the district hospital cannot staff. Indian Public Health Standards 2022 put secondary care at district and sub-district hospitals and tell states to hire clinical staff in-house. An empty specialist post is the bottleneck the standard is pointing at.

## The public capacity meant to catch the referral

The ministry’s PM-ABHIM update pairs primary-care buildings with 2,151 block public health units, 744 district integrated public health laboratories, and 621 critical care hospital blocks, inside approvals of ₹33,081.82 crore over 2021–22 to 2025–26. A block public health unit is the layer that should make a referral shorter than a trip to the state capital. A district laboratory is what makes a sample local. A critical care block is what makes “stabilize here” possible. Until those are commissioned, the handoff jumps from a mandir to whatever empanelled hospital will take the case under portability.

State mode decides the last mile of payment. In trust mode the state agency pays the hospital. In insurance mode the insurer does. A clinically complete handoff that stalls at settlement teaches hospitals to hesitate. Hesitation at the admission desk is an execution failure patients experience as refusal.

## What to inspect in one district

Write five names: the mandir, the referring primary health centre, the block public health unit if it exists, the district hospital, and one empanelled private hospital. For one condition, diabetes that now needs admission, mark which of those five can screen, which can stabilize, which can bill PM-JAY, and which packages are reserved. The gaps on that sheet are the bottlenecks. National totals will not show them.
