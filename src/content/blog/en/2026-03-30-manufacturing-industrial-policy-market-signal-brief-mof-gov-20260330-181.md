# Where India's PLI investment is concentrating

The useful market signal in India's PLI record is not the headline outlay. It is where reported investment and reported employment actually sit. Both come from the sector annexure in the Press Information Bureau note of 21 July 2026, [PRID 2287008](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287008), for cumulative figures till March 2026.

## Three lines hold most of the investment

The annexure total for cumulative investment is ₹2,40,138 crore. Three lines inside it are:

- High-efficiency solar PV modules, Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: ₹64,873 crore
- Pharmaceutical drugs, Department of Pharmaceuticals: ₹45,158 crore
- Automobiles and auto components, Ministry of Heavy Industries: ₹44,326 crore

Added together, those three published lines are ₹1,54,357 crore. That is this page's addition, not a ratio published by the government. It is about two-thirds of the annexure total. A general claim that "PLI is spread evenly across 14 sectors" does not survive the table.

The next large investment lines are specialty steel at ₹23,896 crore and large-scale electronics manufacturing at ₹20,580 crore. IT hardware 2.0 is ₹908 crore. Drones and drone components are ₹595 crore. Small lines are real schemes. They are not where the reported capital has gone.

## Employment does not follow the same map

The annexure employment column sums to 8,49,069. The footnote says indirect employment of 5.66 lakh has been reported under three sectors: large-scale electronics, IT hardware, and solar PV modules. The 28 July 2026 reply to [Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1423](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1423_Rd4xWf.pdf) describes 8.4 lakh as direct employment inside a wider 14.15 lakh figure. Use the annexure when you need a sector, and the parliamentary sentence when you need the all-sector headline. They are not the same cut.

Inside the annexure column, food products (Ministry of Food Processing Industries) are listed at 3,29,200. Large-scale electronics are 1,69,249. Pharmaceutical drugs are 1,14,880. Advanced chemistry cell batteries show ₹4,570 crore of investment and 1,245 employment. Capital intensity and headcount are different signals. A jobs story built only on the solar or battery investment lines will overstate employment. A capital story built on the food line will understate investment.

## Product signals the replies do state

The 28 July reply and the 21 July note give a few product facts, not a full sector scorecard:

- Mobile phone production is described as about 2.4 times the level at the launch of the large-scale electronics scheme. Imports of mobile phones are described as down about 77 percent, with about 99.2 percent of mobile phones used in India manufactured domestically.
- Pharmaceutical sales under that scheme are over ₹3.64 lakh crore, with 1,931 products manufactured domestically under the scheme, including 191 bulk drugs made in India for the first time.
- Bulk-drug capacity of about 55,000 metric tonnes is described across 26 critical APIs.
- Twenty-two medical-device applicants have commenced operations, and 55 unique devices have been commissioned.
- Air-conditioner compressor capacity is described as moving from 1 million units in 2021 to 10 million units in 2025-26 under the white-goods scheme, which the annexure assigns to DPIIT.

These are scheme claims in a ministerial reply. They are not an audit of every factory in the sector, and they do not say what share of the wider industry sits outside PLI.
