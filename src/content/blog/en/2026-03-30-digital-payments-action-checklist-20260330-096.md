The Reserve Bank posts a new Payment System Indicators file most months. The index is the stable door: Statistics, Data Releases, Payment System Indicators. The edition behind the other briefs on this desk is the August 2026 file. The checks below are how to read the next one without inventing a total.

## Before any percentage

Open the HTML or the workbook from the index, and read the title. Confirm the month. Then read the header before the first figure. In the August 2026 file the volume header is FY 2025-26, August 2025, July 2026, August 2026, and the value header repeats that pattern. The financial-year column is a year. The last column is the month in the title.

Volume is in lakh. Value is in ₹ crore. Infrastructure counts are in lakh as well. A figure copied into a deck without the unit will be wrong by a factor of a thousand or a lakh.

The note on the file says the data are provisional. A later release can reprint a month. If a chart splices two editions, keep the edition name on the point.

## Lines that already contain other lines

UPI includes the USSD sub-line. Adding USSD again double-counts a small number. In August 2026 that sub-line was 0.74 lakh.

Total digital payments are labelled (1+2+3+4+5). UPI is inside item 2, retail credit transfers. Adding UPI on top of the digital total double-counts 2,45,089.58 lakh transactions in the August 2026 file. Paper is outside the digital total and inside the grand total.

Part II, mobile apps and internet or netbanking and ATMs, is a channel view. It is not a new system sitting above Part I.

## Names that point at a different book

BHIM Aadhaar Pay is the debit-transfer line in this file. The BHIM app, as a UPI payer app, is NPCI's BHIM product statistics.

Credit cards and debit cards in this file are scheme-wide. RuPay usage is NPCI's RuPay product statistics.

UPI's person-to-merchant split is not a row here. If the briefing needs that split, it needs an NPCI or RBI table that prints it.

The UPI QR stock is static codes only, on the footnote's terms, from September 2020.

There is no state column. A regional claim needs another release. The entity-wise UPI, IMPS, NETC, NFS, AePS, CTS and BBPS file is organised by entity.

## Comparisons that need the footnote in the sentence

Card payments and prepaid instruments may not be comparable with earlier periods, because categories were refined and definitions revised. Quote a stock. If you also quote an older stock, quote the warning.

Fraud rows have a narrower reporter set: scheduled commercial banks excluding regional rural banks, non-bank prepaid issuers, and non-bank credit-card issuers, through the Central Payments Fraud Information Registry. Attempts are out. The August 2026 row is 3.43 lakh frauds and ₹460 crore. That sentence is incomplete without the coverage.

## A short close for a founder or a policy note

Write the month, the column, the unit, and the release name. Leave a blank where the file has no row. The [policy explainer](/blog/digital-payments-policy-explainer-20260330-056) is the map of those rows. The [August 2026 levels](/blog/digital-payments-key-numbers-20260330-016) are a worked example. Both are on the [Great Indian Company brief index](/blog).

The worked examples use Payment System Indicators - August 2026. The index URL is the place to collect the following month.
