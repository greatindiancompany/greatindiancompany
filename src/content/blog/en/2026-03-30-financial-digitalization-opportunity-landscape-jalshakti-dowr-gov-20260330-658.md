# Where a founder can still build on India's credit and payments rails

The opportunity in Indian financial digitalization is real, and it is narrower than a pitch that says "we are the UPI for credit". UPI is already built. The National Payments Corporation of India runs it, and MeitY describes it as shared infrastructure on the [Digital India page](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/). A new company joins a rail, it does not found one. The open roles are the ones the directions name.

## Four roles that have a rulebook

A non-bank can become a payment aggregator if it is an Indian company, applies through the Reserve Bank's portal, and meets the net-worth path in the [15 September 2025 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896): ₹15 crore at application, ₹25 crore by the end of the third financial year, maintained thereafter. The business is to collect customer payments through the merchant's interface and settle them to the merchant. Physical, online, and cross-border are different categories. A gateway that never handles funds does not need this licence, and it also does not have the aggregator's float. Escrow is the constraint and the point. Merchant due diligence and a dispute officer are part of the product.

A company can be a lending service provider without becoming the lender. The [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848) define that role as an agent for one or more of acquisition, work incidental to underwriting and pricing, servicing, monitoring, or recovery. The opportunity is distribution and servicing for a bank or NBFC that stays on the hook. The lost opportunity, which was never a lawful one, is keeping the principal, setting an undisclosed preference among lenders, or holding the borrower's repayments. If you work with several lenders, the fair-offer screen has been mandatory since 1 November 2025. A default loss guarantee is available only inside the five per cent cap, only in cash, a lien-marked fixed deposit, or a bank guarantee, and only from a company. It is not available on revolving digital credit or credit cards.

A company can be an NBFC-account aggregator. The [2016 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10598) require a certificate, net owned funds of at least ₹2 crore, and a leverage ratio no higher than seven. The customer pays, or the user pays, for a consent service. The data does not become the company's asset. No other business rides along, except deploying surplus that is not held for trading. This is a narrow franchise. It is still a franchise, because unlicensed scraping is not the alternative the directions allow.

A bank, including a small finance bank after 12 February 2025, can attach a pre-sanctioned credit line to UPI under the [September 2023 circular](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12532). The founder's opportunity next to that product is the experience around a limit the bank has already approved: consent, statements, and repayment. It is not the unilateral creation of a limit inside a handle. Payment banks and regional rural banks are outside that particular permission.

## The protocol is a tool, not a fifth licence

The Open Credit Enablement Network publishes an open set of interactions among loan agents, lenders, and data providers. A founder can implement it to talk to more than one lender without a private bilateral format. The messages do not replace the regulated entity, the key fact statement, the in-India data rule, or the ban on pass-through accounts. A protocol demo that disburses to the agent's account is a failed demo under the lending directions, however clean the API.

What is not on the map: an unlicensed lending app, a data store that calls itself an aggregator, a payments firm that settles merchants without authorisation, and a Digital Rupee wallet that promises interest. Retail e₹, in the Reserve Bank's November 2022 pilot design, does not earn interest.

NPCI's [statistics page](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics) tells you the rail is used. It does not tell you which of the four roles above has room in a given segment. That judgement is local: merchant category, lender appetite, and whether you can staff the nodal officer the directions require.
