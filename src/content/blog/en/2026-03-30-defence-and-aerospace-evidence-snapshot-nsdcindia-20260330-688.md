# Which Indian defence figures are safe to repeat

## How to use this page

Every figure below is tied to one government page and one date. If a pitch, a note, or a chart cannot name the page, drop the figure. This snapshot does not fill gaps with estimates. Where two official pages disagree, both wordings are shown.

## Aims, which are not results

The public aims, repeated in the November 2025 note and earlier by the Raksha Mantri, are Rs 3 lakh crore of annual defence production and Rs 50,000 crore of exports by 2029. The November note also stated a nearer production aim of Rs 1.75 lakh crore for the fiscal year then underway. An aim stays in the aim column until a year-end release moves it.

## What is not in this snapshot

These pages do not give India's share of global defence spending, a classified order book, unit prices for the platforms named on the sixth list, or a national defence-employment census. Country totals are worded differently across PIB texts ("around 80" in one, "over 100" in another) and should be quoted with the page, not merged.

Great Indian Company keeps this as a checking table. The argument built on a row belongs in the brief for that row, not in a second unverified total.
