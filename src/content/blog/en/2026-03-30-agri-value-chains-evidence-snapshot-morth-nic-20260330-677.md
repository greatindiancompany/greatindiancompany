# Eight official prints, each with a date

This page is a snapshot, which means every figure keeps the date and the document it came from. Adding them into an index would invent a unit none of the publishers used. The Directorate of Economics and Statistics stands behind the production estimates the agriculture ministry released. The sowing report is a Crop Weather Watch Group progress note. The rest are scheme stocks.

## Production, for the year that has an estimate

On 27 May 2026 the agriculture minister released the Third Advance Estimates for 2025-26. Foodgrain production is estimated at 376.563 million tonnes, 18.8 million tonnes or 5.3 per cent above 357.732 million tonnes in 2024-25. Rice is 154.024 million tonnes, against 150.184. Wheat is 120.657 million tonnes, against 117.945. Maize is 55.093 million tonnes, against 43.409. Pulses in the release include tur at 3.592 million tonnes, gram at 12.514, and lentil at 1.762. Oilseeds are 43.059 million tonnes. Sugarcane is 500.063 million tonnes. Cotton is 29.024 million bales of 170 kilograms.

These are estimates of a crop already taken. They are the supply that storage, mandis, and processing are clearing through 2026. They are not the kharif crop sown in mid-2026.

## Area, for the crop that does not yet have a tonne figure

As on 4 September 2026, published on 8 September, kharif area was 1,086.31 lakh hectares, against 1,104.00 a year earlier and a normal of 1,104.46. Rice was 421.82 lakh hectares, down 16.37 and above a normal of 412.00. Pulses were 117.13, below a normal of 123.63. Cotton was 109.17, against a normal of 125.51. Maize was 91.09 lakh hectares, above a normal of 80.77.

## The price the public buyer is authorised to pay

On 13 May 2026 the Cabinet set kharif MSPs for marketing season 2026-27. Common paddy is Rs 2,441 a quintal, up Rs 72, a 50 per cent margin over a cost of Rs 1,627. The release says margins are higher for moong (61 per cent), bajra (56 per cent), maize (56 per cent), and tur (54 per cent). From 2014-15 to 2025-26, paddy procurement was 8,418 lakh metric tonnes and payments to paddy growers were Rs 16.08 lakh crore.

## The mandi tape

The National Agriculture Market, in the Lok Sabha reply of 17 March 2026, had 1,656 mandis in 23 states and 4 Union territories on the portal. As on 28 February 2026: 1.80 crore farmers, 2.72 lakh traders, 4,724 FPOs. Trade from inception through February 2026: 13.22 crore metric tonnes and Rs 4,82,350 crore. The grant for integrating a mandi is up to Rs 75 lakh. The rupee total is cumulative since 2016.

## The farmer company

The 10,000-FPO scheme, outlay Rs 6,865 crore, had 10,000 FPOs registered as on 1 March 2026, of which 1,175 had only women members. Women farmers registered under the scheme: 23.55 lakh. As on 31 December 2025, equity grant of Rs 430.77 crore had reached 6,557 FPOs, and guarantee cover of Rs 662.71 crore had reached 2,671. Ceilings: Rs 18 lakh over three years for management, Rs 15 lakh of matching equity, Rs 2 crore of guaranteed project loan.

## The loan and the grant

AIF, as on 26 January 2026: loans of Rs 80,224.15 crore sanctioned for 1,50,431 projects, investment mobilised Rs 1,27,508 crore. As on 15 July 2026, a later reply's label: capital investment leveraged Rs 1,61,537.22 crore, including Rs 50,332.66 crore for farm-gate storage and logistics. Subvention is 3 per cent, the interest cap in the August 2025 note is 9 per cent, and guarantee support runs through CGTMSE and NABSanrakshan up to Rs 2 crore.

Cold chain under the food-processing ministry, as of 30 June 2026: 409 projects approved, 311 complete, grant sanctioned Rs 3,089.08 crore, released Rs 2,540.89 crore, cold storage created 9.236 lakh metric tonnes. National cold storage cited for 31 January 2026: 402.87 lakh metric tonnes. Food-processing PLI as on 31 December 2025: 168 applicants, investment about Rs 9,207 crore, capacity around 35 lakh metric tonnes a year, incentive disbursed Rs 2,714.79 crore.

## The transfer and the export

PM-KISAN, Cabinet note of 31 July 2026: continued from 2026-27 to 2030-31 with an outlay of Rs 3.15 lakh crore. Stock then: more than Rs 4.47 lakh crore through 23 instalments. The 23rd instalment was over Rs 18,984 crore to more than 9.49 crore farmers. The annual amount remains Rs 6,000.

APEDA's 2024-25 annual report, from DGCIS: agri exports USD 51,913 million, up from USD 48,757 million. The summary table puts APEDA products at USD 28,590 million and 55 per cent of agri exports. The prose of the same report prints USD 28,601 million for APEDA products. Both cells are in the document.
