# Public Workflows Where Social Outcomes Still Need Operators

An opportunity in this sector is a workflow a ministry has already described and not yet closed with an outcome. It is not a market size. None of the releases below prints a contract value, a private revenue pool, or a tender calendar. An operator who adds those from outside this page should say so.

## The Anganwadi worker’s day is the product surface

The user on the nutrition side is the Anganwadi worker: 13.30 lakh workers across nearly 13.99 lakh centres, as of 31 July 2026, for almost 8.94 crore beneficiaries. The Poshan Tracker is the job aid for opening the centre, attendance, early childhood activities, and growth monitoring. Facial recognition is the ministry’s check that take-home ration reaches the person registered on that app. A tool that cannot issue against a registered beneficiary, or that assumes a smartphone in the parent’s hand as the system of record, is beside the workflow.

The ninth Poshan Maah, from 9 September 2026, adds tasks the ministry has written down: weekly rotation of hot cooked meals, a menu board at the centre, at least one meeting of the Anganwadi Management Committee during the month (guidelines dated 23 June 2026), food testing with the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India, and the revised home-visit scheduler with 34 developmental milestones for children aged 0-3. Navchetna and Aadharshila are the named early-childhood curricula. The gap those tasks sit on is visible in NFHS-6: an adequate diet at 6-23 months is 15.3%, and exclusive breastfeeding under six months is 55.8%, down from 63.7%. Counselling content that only celebrates current breastfeeding (95.6%) misses the line that fell.

## Maternity cash is a pendency workflow

Poshan Maah also marks ten years of the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana and asks states to find eligible women, clear pending direct-benefit transfers, and use the scheme dashboard. That is an enrolment and exception queue, not a new benefit design. Household health-insurance or financing coverage at 60.2% in NFHS-6, up from 41.0%, is the parallel coverage fact on the health-financing side. Claims operations are not described in that fact sheet.

Immunisation’s digital rail is named in the 29 May 2026 note as U-WIN, next to a card-based full-vaccination rate of 87.1% and a second measles-containing dose at 71.8%. The unfinished work is the child who has “any vaccine” (the ministry says that share stays above 96%) and does not have the full card schedule.

## Schools: build for the residual, not the average ratio

UDISE+ 2025-26 puts a computer in 69.9% of schools and internet in 67.4%. Electricity is already in 95.0% and drinking water in 99.5%. Ramps with handrails are in 58.2%. Pupil-teacher ratios of 10, 12, 17, and 21 are inside the policy reference of 30:1. The workflow that is still open is the rest: schools without a computer, 1,00,843 single-teacher schools, and a secondary retention rate of 51.9%. A district product that assumes every school is connected and every cohort reaches Class 10 will fail the ministry’s own census.

## The rural job-card change is a live migration

From 1 July 2026, workers with completed e-KYC keep their old job card until a Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Card is issued. Attendance is face authentication on the National Mobile Monitoring System, with an exception when connectivity fails. Wages move by direct benefit transfer within fifteen days, or delay compensation applies. Works are supposed to fall into water, core rural infrastructure (including schools and Anganwadis), livelihoods, and weather protection. An operator supporting gram panchayat planning, muster exceptions, or asset geo-tagging is supporting a workflow the Act already requires. Person-days in the first month do not mean the card migration is finished.

Across these workflows the buyer is usually a state department and the user is a frontline worker.
