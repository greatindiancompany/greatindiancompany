# A Dated Record of India's Solar and Storage Figures

This page is a record, not a forecast. Every figure below is copied from a named official page, with the date that page gives. If a later note revises a number, the later note wins. Nothing here fills a blank with an estimate of our own.

## Module manufacturing

MNRE's PLI page, last updated 8 August 2023, states an outlay of Rs 24,000 crore for high-efficiency solar PV modules. The incentive is for five years after commissioning, on manufacture and sale.

Tranche I: Cabinet approval 7 April 2021, guidelines 28 April 2021, outlay Rs 4,500 crore, implementing agency IREDA, letters of award in November and December 2021 to three bidders for 8,737 MW of fully integrated capacity.

Tranche II: Cabinet approval 21 September 2022, guidelines 30 September 2022, outlay Rs 19,500 crore, implementing agency SECI, letters of award in April 2023 to 11 bidders for 39,600 MW of fully or partially integrated capacity.

SECI's solar page states that around Rs 13,937.535 crore of PLI was allocated to those 11 manufacturers, payable over five years after commissioning. That allocation is not the Rs 19,500 crore outlay, and neither figure is reported on these pages as cash already disbursed.

## Household rooftop

PIB release 2010133, 29 February 2024: outlay Rs 75,021 crore; one crore households; up to 300 units a month; assistance capped at 3 kW; scheme outcome of 30 GW residential rooftop, 1,000 BUs, and 720 million tonnes of carbon-dioxide equivalent over 25 years; an estimate of around 17 lakh direct jobs.

PIB release 2294208, 4 August 2026: more than 50.06 lakh households benefiting; 14.8 GW commissioned under the scheme; Rs 28,024 crore subsidy released through direct benefit transfer; nearly 19 lakh households on zero electricity bills; 34,219 registered vendors and 29,469 active; more than 2.32 lakh people trained. The 30 GW line and the 14.8 GW line are not the same kind of number.

## Utility solar at SECI

SECI what-we-do page, as of 1 May 2025: over 73.8 GW awarded, comprising 45.9 GW solar, 16.4 GW wind, and 11.5 GW hybrid. SECI solar page, as of March 2025: 21.67 GW of solar commissioned. Awarded and commissioned stay in separate rows.

## Storage plan and storage grant

MNRE storage overview, last updated 21 November 2023, quoting CEA's National Electricity Plan 2023: 82.37 GWh required in 2026-27 (47.65 GWh pumped storage and 34.72 GWh batteries); 411.4 GWh in 2031-32 (175.18 GWh pumped storage and 236.22 GWh batteries). Energy storage obligation: 1 percent in FY 2023-24 to 4 percent in FY 2029-30, met only if at least 85 percent of stored energy in the year is renewable.

PIB release 2118325, 3 April 2025: battery viability-gap scheme approved 6 September 2023; capacity raised from 4,000 MWh to 13,200 MWh inside Rs 3,760 crore; grant cut to Rs 46 lakh per MWh or 30 percent of capital cost, whichever is lower; no financial closure and no expenditure in 2024-25. The plan's 236.22 GWh and the scheme's 13,200 MWh are not substitutes.

## Farm solar, one dated count

PIB release 2042069, 6 August 2024: PM-KUSUM central assistance of 30 percent, or 50 percent in the northeast, hills, and islands, for standalone pumps and for solarising grid pumps; 4,11,222 farmers benefited as on 30 June 2024.
