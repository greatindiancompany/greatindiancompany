# Which cyber duties are in force, and which dates are still ahead

Regulatory outlook, as of 24 September 2026, is a calendar. Several major instruments are already in force. The personal-data rulebook is only partly commenced. Reading PIB's phrase "full operationalisation" without the commencement clauses will put a team a year early on some duties and late on others.

## Already governing

**CERT-In directions of 28 April 2022.** The direction says it becomes effective 60 days after issue. Six-hour reporting of specified incidents, and 180-day log retention inside India, are current duties for the entities the direction names. The CERT-In page also lists a 27 June 2022 extension affecting MSMEs and subscriber validation by data centres, VPS, cloud and VPN providers. Open that extension before assuming every clause of the April direction bit on the sixtieth day for every entity.

**Reserve Bank IT directions.** Issued 7 November 2023, in effect from 1 April 2024, for the banks, NBFCs, credit information companies and All India Financial Institutions listed in the direction. Local area banks and NBFC core investment companies are outside it. Foreign banks operating through branches are on a comply-or-explain approach accepted by the Reserve Bank.

**IRDAI guidelines of 24 April 2023.** In force for insurers, foreign reinsurance branches, the listed intermediaries, and the Insurance Information Bureau. Entities that had already completed the 2022-23 security audit were told to ensure compliance from the next financial year. The circular superseded the 2017, December 2020, September 2022 and October 2022 instruments it names.

**SEBI CSCRF of 20 August 2024.** The glide path has passed: 1 January 2025 for six categories that already had a cyber circular, and 1 April 2025 for entities receiving the framework for the first time. Category is fixed at the start of a financial year from the previous year's data and stays for that year. SEBI also issued a clarification circular on 31 December 2024, SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/184. The operative changes are in that circular; this brief does not paraphrase them.

**Telecom cyber security rules.** G.S.R. 720(E) of 21 November 2024 came into force on publication. G.S.R. 771(E) of 22 October 2025 amended definitions and identifier provisions, including telecommunication identifier user entities and the mobile-number validation platform. Incident reporting under rule 7 — six hours, then twenty-four — is a rule directed at the telecommunication entity.

## Commenced only in part

The DPDP Rules, 2025, G.S.R. 846(E) of 13 November 2025, commence in three steps.

Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21 took effect on publication. Those rules cover the Board's appointment, pay, meetings, digital functioning, and officers. PIB also says appeals from the Board lie with the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal. The appeal procedure itself is Rule 22, which is in the eighteen-month group, so a note that treats the appeal rule as already commenced is ahead of the gazette. The demand for grants treats the Board as an establishment to be funded.

Rule 4, Consent Manager registration and obligations, takes effect one year after publication. From September 2026 that date is close. PIB says Consent Managers must be Indian companies.

Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23 take effect eighteen months after publication. That set includes the standalone consent notice, processing for subsidies and benefits by the state, reasonable security safeguards, personal-data-breach intimation, erasure timelines, the public contact point, verifiable consent for children and for persons with disabilities who have a lawful guardian, and additional obligations of a significant data fiduciary, including a yearly data-protection impact assessment and audit and a possible restriction on transferring specified personal data and related traffic data outside India.

PIB's 14 November 2025 release summarises those duties in plain language and says organisations have an 18-month phased period. The gazette clauses are the binding calendar. A board paper in September 2026 should say: Board and Consent Manager machinery are the near dates; safeguard and breach-notice duties are the later date; CERT-In, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and telecom duties do not wait for either.
