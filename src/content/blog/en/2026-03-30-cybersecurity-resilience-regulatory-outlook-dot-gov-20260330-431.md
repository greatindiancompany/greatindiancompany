The regulatory outlook for India's cybersecurity resilience is the list of instruments you will open again, and the date you will open them. It is not a forecast. This page does not predict a notification, a gazette date, or a new national strategy.

## The set worth re-reading

Each line is a shelf. The next text on that shelf, if any, is what you re-read. This page does not claim a text is pending.

- **Telecom statute and rules.** The Telecommunications Act, 2023, and the Telecommunications (Telecom Cyber Security) Rules, 2024, made under it. Amendments and directions under those rules belong on the Department of Telecommunications site, [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/). Take the date from the notification.
- **Licence security conditions.** The security schedule of the licence you hold. A sector overview on a homepage is not your schedule.
- **Testing and trusted sources.** Indian Telecom Security Assurance Requirements, mandatory testing and certification of telecommunication equipment, and trusted-source determinations under the National Security Directive on the Telecommunication Sector. A procedure can change without a new Act.
- **CERT-In.** Directions to entities on incident reporting and related practices, under the Information Technology Act. Re-read the direction that names your entity. Do not treat a Department of Telecommunications update as a rewrite of that direction.
- **Critical information infrastructure.** Duties aimed at protected systems sit with that framework, not in a telecom licence file, unless the file says so.
- **Sector regulators.** Reserve Bank directions for banks, SEBI directions for market infrastructure, IRDAI directions for insurers. Each is its own outlook.
- **Data protection.** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, is a different statute from a telecom security rule. Do not merge the two into one "cyber outlook." This page does not state which rules under that Act are in force.

McKinsey's India insights index was paired with an earlier draft. Commentary is not an instrument. The link is omitted.

## How to keep the outlook honest

For each shelf write: last document you opened, its printed date, the duty it sets, and the next date you will look. If nothing new is there, write "no new instrument seen" and the date you looked. That sentence is a result. A prediction is not.
