Part V of Payment System Indicators - August 2026 is the Reserve Bank's domestic payment-fraud series. It is not a count of every attempted scam in the country. The notes say the figures come from the Central Payments Fraud Information Registry, as reported by scheduled commercial banks excluding regional rural banks, non-bank prepaid issuers, and non-bank credit-card issuers. Attempts are excluded. The rest of the file is marked provisional, and this block should be read with its own coverage.

## August 2026 against August 2025

| Month | Frauds (lakh) | Value (₹ crore) | One fraudulent payment in every | FTS |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| August 2025 | 2.40 | 411 | 1,00,735.94 transactions | 0.166 bps |
| August 2026 | 3.43 | 460 | 83,538.64 transactions | 0.152 bps |

The count rose 42.9 percent. The rupee value rose from ₹411 crore to ₹460 crore. "One in every X" fell from about 1,00,736 to about 83,539, which means a reported fraud turned up more often per payment than it did a year earlier. The FTS column is fraud value divided by payment value, times 10,000, and RBI prints it in basis points. That ratio eased from 0.166 to 0.152. More fraud reports, a higher incidence per transaction, and a slightly smaller share of payment value.

Inside the printed months of 2026, June is the high count: 3.53 lakh frauds, ₹489 crore, one in every 75,719.54 transactions, FTS 0.150 bps. March 2026 is the low value ratio in that stretch: 2.59 lakh frauds, ₹337 crore, one in every 1,03,465.72 transactions, FTS 0.097 bps.

## The longer reported series

The same block starts in September 2022, at 1.71 lakh frauds, ₹249 crore, one in every 58,177.41 transactions, FTS 0.127 bps. By August 2026 the count was higher and the "one in every X" number was also higher. A higher X means a reported fraud was attached to fewer payments than in September 2022. Payment volumes grew faster than the fraud count over that span. The last twelve months, August 2025 to August 2026, went the other way on incidence.

The series is worth plotting as printed. It is not a complete crime survey, and a month's jump is not, by itself, the story of one named fraud.

## Why the volume mix matters to a risk team

In August 2026, UPI was 2,45,089.58 lakh transactions, which was 87.1 percent of digital payment volume in this file. A failure or a fraud pattern on UPI is a system event because of that share. The fraud block does not split the 3.43 lakh frauds by rail, so this brief stops at the share of payments and does not assign the frauds to UPI.

USSD, the feature-phone sub-line inside UPI, was 0.74 lakh transactions in August 2026, worth ₹8 crore. The channel is present and small. People who depend on it are a thin slice of the official UPI count.
