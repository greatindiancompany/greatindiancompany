# Urban Housing Checklist for the Next 90 Days

## The window

This checklist runs from the update date, 24 September 2026, for about ninety days, into late December 2026. It lists events and documents the official record has already dated. It does not predict a Cabinet decision.

## Open these because the date is published

1. Census reference date for snow-bound areas. PIB’s note of 25 April 2026 fixes 00:00 hours on 1 October 2026 as the reference date for Ladakh and the snow-bound non-synchronous areas of Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, and Uttarakhand. Population Enumeration in those areas is scheduled in September 2026. By the end of this ninety-day window that reference date will have passed. What will not automatically appear is a published table. Wait for the Registrar General. Do not fill the gap with the 2011 urban share.

2. Houselisting elsewhere. Phase I was scheduled in state-chosen 30-day blocks from April through September 2026, with 15 days of self-enumeration before each block. September 2026 is the last month of that published span. A state that has finished fieldwork has still not, by that fact alone, released housing-condition tables. Ask for the state notification of its 30-day window if a local claim depends on it. PIB’s release on the calendar is the national source.

3. The rest of the country. Population Enumeration remains scheduled for February 2027, reference date 1 March 2027. Nothing in the next ninety days is that enumeration. Budget and training notices may appear. They are not results.

## Open these because money or permission depends on them

4. PMAY-Urban 2.0 applications. The July 2025 PIB reply points beneficiaries to pmay-urban.gov.in. If a loan is meant to carry the Interest Subsidy Scheme, check the published tests before sanction: income up to ₹9 lakh, loan up to ₹25 lakh, property up to ₹35 lakh, carpet area up to 120 square metres, and the rule that each yearly instalment needs an active loan with more than half the principal outstanding. The December 2024 note’s demand-survey date of 31 March 2025 is already past. Do not assume the survey closed everywhere. Ask the state mission for its own status.

5. State RERA register and MoHUA’s advisory of 31 July 2026. That PDF advises a four-month extension of registration and completion for registered projects whose completion date falls on or after 28 February 2026, and it leaves the order to the state authority. In this quarter, check whether the relevant authority has issued that order, and whether the project is on the register. The advisory is not the register.

6. Affordable Housing Policy. The December 2024 PIB note required states to prepare one by March 2025. Ninety days from now is a good time to read the policy a state actually notified, if it notified one. Absence of a document on the state site is itself the finding. This checklist does not score states.

## Stop waiting for these

7. A new Smart Cities city list. MoHUA’s mission page says financial closure was 31 March 2025 and that no new city will be taken up, because there is no further budgetary provision. The 6 August 2026 Lok Sabha reply is the progress account as on 31 March 2026. Use the annex to check a city. Do not budget a central tender for a 101st city in this quarter.

8. A new official urbanisation rate. MoHUA’s urban page still carries Census 2011. The next ninety days can contain enumeration activity. They have not been scheduled, in the PIB notes used here, as the release date of a new national percentage.

9. An extension of AMRUT 2.0 past the guideline horizon. The 27 October 2021 PIB release runs the indicative outlay from 2021-22 through 2025-26. A continuation would be a new release. Until that release exists, do not put it in a cash-flow forecast.
