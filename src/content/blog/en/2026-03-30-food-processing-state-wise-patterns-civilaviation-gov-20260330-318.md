# Where India's food factories actually sit

Food processing looks national in a scheme guideline and local on the ground. The only state factory census the ministry reprints in its latest annual report is the Annual Survey of Industries for 2022-23. Scheme money, by contrast, follows applications. A state that ranks high in factories is not guaranteed the next cold-chain sanction, and a state with few registered factories may still have a deep unorganised base.

## The registered-factory map, ASI 2022-23

The Annual Report for 2024-25 says India had 42,801 registered food-processing factories. The five largest shares are Andhra Pradesh (12.68 percent), Tamil Nadu (11.67 percent), Telangana (8.65 percent), Punjab (8.08 percent), and Maharashtra (6.59 percent). Table 2.4 gives the counts behind those shares: Andhra Pradesh 5,427, Tamil Nadu 4,996, Telangana 3,702, Punjab 3,457, and Maharashtra 2,820.

Those five account for just under half of registered factories. The rest of the country is not empty. Uttar Pradesh has 2,407 registered factories, West Bengal 2,230, Chhattisgarh 2,225, Assam 1,750, and Odisha 1,356. The pattern is a southern and north-western concentration of registered plants, with large Hindi-belt and eastern states present but not leading the factory count.

Registered means a factory under the Factories Act, as the survey defines it. A household that makes papad or pickle for a local market will not appear in this ranking.

## Do not treat the state unorganised column as current

The same table prints a second column of unincorporated enterprises. The footnote says state-wise unit counts are not available in the Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises for 2022-23, so the column falls back to the NSS 73rd round, July 2015 to June 2016. On that old round, Uttar Pradesh shows 3,50,883 enterprises and West Bengal 3,22,590, against a few thousand registered factories each. Andhra Pradesh's unincorporated count in that round is 1,54,330 beside 5,427 factories.

The national unincorporated employment figure the ministry uses elsewhere, 46.57 lakh workers, is from 2022-23. The state enterprise counts are not. Anyone ranking states by "unorganised food units" from Table 2.4 is ranking a survey that ended in June 2016.

## Schemes are demand-driven, with one district layer

Parliamentary replies published by the ministry say PMKSY, PLISFPI, and PMFME are not reserved by state. Applications are invited across the country. A state's share of the next approval list is a result of who applies and who meets the conditions, not a quota printed next to the factory census.

PMFME adds a district layer. One District One Product has been approved for 726 districts in 35 states and Union territories. Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 525, answered on 6 February 2025, says states and Union territories picked products from the local ecosystem, and that they may propose a change with reasons. The subsidy itself is available for non-ODOP products as well. What the district product changes is priority in branding, marketing, and common infrastructure.

The geography of already-built mega food parks is a committed-liability map, not a pipeline. The annual report lists 24 operational parks, including sites at Chittoor, Tumkur, Fazilka, Nalbari, Khargone, Murshidabad, Rayagada, Satara, Ajmer, Surat, Una, West Godavari, West Tripura, Nizamabad, Dewas, Kolasib, Palakkad, Kapurthala, Raipur, Sonipat, and Alappuzha, among others. About 114 units function in those parks. New mega food parks are not being sanctioned in the 15th Finance Commission cycle.

Cold-chain projects are spread by sanction rather than by a state entitlement. As on 31 December 2024, 394 had been taken up and 286 completed. The state annex in the annual report is the place to see that spread; a national brief should not flatten it into "cold chain is a western-India story" without that annex.

## How to use the map

For a plant location, start with the ASI factory counts and the raw-material geography the investor portal was built to show, then check whether the district's ODOP matches the product only if branding money is part of the plan. For a policy comparison across states, pair the 2022-23 factory table with current sanction lists from the scheme portals. Leave the 2015-16 unincorporated column in its own sentence, with the survey dates attached.
