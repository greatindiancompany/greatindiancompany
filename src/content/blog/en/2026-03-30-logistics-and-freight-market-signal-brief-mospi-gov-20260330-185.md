A freight “signal” is useful only if you can name the office that publishes it and the question it can answer. The 11 August 2026 Lok Sabha record names several. It does not name a national spot rate for a truck.

## Signals that exist because a ministry said so

Start with the [reply to Starred Question 327](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf).

The cost signal is a study, not a ticker. DPIIT and NCAER, in an assessment dated 2025 in that reply, estimate logistics cost at about 7.9 percent of GDP. Use it as the government’s current macro claim. Do not use it to price a lane from Nhava Sheva to an inland depot.

The rank signal is the World Bank Logistics Performance Index. The reply says the latest ranking it is citing is 38th, an advance of six places. Ranks move when the Bank publishes, not when a quarter ends. A quiet quarter in Indian trucking can coincide with an unchanged rank.

The project signal is the Network Planning Group count in the [same-day written reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2297818): 396 projects evaluated, about ₹18.66 lakh crore of estimated cost, 256 sanctioned, 198 under implementation. That is a pipeline signal. Estimated cost is not capital deployed, and “evaluated” is not “open to traffic.”

The state signal is institutional, not a league table in this reply. Logistics Ease Across Different States is described as the framework for judging states and Union Territories. The reply does not print this year’s ranks. Twenty-nine states and Union Territories have formulated logistics policies, and 32 have given the sector industry status. Policy on the books is a signal of intent. It is a weak signal of dwell time.

The trade-visibility signal is the Logistics Data Bank, which the reply says tracks export-import containers across ports, container freight stations, and inland container depots. That is a container-trail for trade, not a census of domestic full-truckload.

The commodity signal is the Sectoral Plan for Efficient Logistics, opened for coal, steel, fertilizers, cement, pharmaceuticals, and food. If your cargo is one of those, the relevant plan is a better lead than a national slogan. If your cargo is none of those, do not borrow their constraints.

## Signals that live on ministry sites, not in the cost ratio

Road corridors are a [Bharatmala](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala) question. The August replies credit national highways and expressways, including links to ports, airports, and industrial nodes, as the roads ministry’s contribution to logistics. They do not turn Bharatmala into a freight-rate series.

Port-side programmes sit with the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. [Sagarmala](https://shipmin.gov.in/en/division/sagarmala) is the programme page. The August replies list Sagarmala among the connectivity efforts they associate with lower logistics friction. A programme page is not a monthly tonne table. When you need tonnes, use that ministry’s port statistics release for the month in question, and read the advance-estimate label before you put the number in a model.

Digital access is its own signal. The written reply says a public Gati Shakti query platform was launched in September 2025, with about 1,800 data layers. Asking whether your site, spur, or warehouse shows up on that layer is a market check. A minister’s count of layers is not.

## What still is not a signal

No official series in these pages quotes a pan-India freight rate per tonne-kilometre for this quarter. Empty-return ratios, driver wages, and warehouse rents are commercial facts. They belong in contracts and in company filings, not in a rotated statistics-ministry link.
