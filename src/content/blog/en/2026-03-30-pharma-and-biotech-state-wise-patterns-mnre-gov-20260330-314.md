# Three States Have a Central Bulk-Drug Park

State-wise pattern, for this scheme, means three approvals and a long list of states that applied and were not selected. The Department of Pharmaceuticals received proposals from 13 states. The 6 February 2026 Lok Sabha reply says Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, and Andhra Pradesh were approved, each with a grant-in-aid of ₹1,000 crore. The reply also answers a Tamil Nadu question directly: there is no proposal of the Government of India to establish a bulk-drug park in Tamil Nadu under this scheme.

## The three project sheets

Figures below are from that 6 February 2026 reply.

Gujarat’s park is at Jambusar in Bharuch district, on 2,015.02 acres. The state implementing agency is the Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation. Total project cost is ₹2,507.02 crore. Common-infrastructure cost is ₹1,457.01 crore. Civil tenders for roads, drainage, water, effluent collection, and rack systems are described as an advanced stage of completion. Utility tenders for the common effluent treatment plant, solvent recovery, and treatment, storage, and disposal facilities have been awarded and are being executed.

Himachal Pradesh’s park is at Haroli in Una district, on 1,405.01 acres. The agency is Himachal Pradesh Bulk Drug Park Infrastructure Ltd. Total project cost is ₹1,923 crore. Common-infrastructure cost is ₹1,118.46 crore. Tenders for internal roads, drainage, bridges, and water infrastructure have been awarded and are in progress. The tender for zero-liquid-discharge facilities, including the effluent plant and the steam system, has been floated. Site demarcation, grading, and levelling are in progress. The assistance formula in the reply is 90 percent of common-infrastructure cost for Himachal Pradesh as a hilly state, still subject to the ₹1,000 crore ceiling. Gujarat and Andhra Pradesh are on the 70 percent limb of the same formula, with the same ceiling.

Andhra Pradesh’s park is at Nakkapalli in Anakapalli district, on 2,001.80 acres. The agency is Andhra Pradesh Bulk Drug Infrastructure Corporation. Total project cost is ₹1,876.66 crore. Common-infrastructure cost is ₹1,438.89 crore. Tenders for internal roads, power, water, drainage, and utility buildings have been awarded. Boundary fencing is complete. An external water pipeline is under installation. Approval of the power-infrastructure design is in process. Acquisition of additional land is underway, and environmental clearance for that land was granted in January 2026.

The scheme tenure in the reply runs from FY 2020–21 to FY 2026–27. The national allocation is ₹3,000 crore. An in-principle note on 1 September 2022 had placed the Andhra Pradesh proposal at K.P. Puram and Kodhada in Thondagi mandal of the then East Godavari district, on 2,000.45 acres, and the Una site at 1,402.44 acres. District names and acres moved between that note and the 2026 reply. Use the 2026 sheet for a current description, and cite 2022 only when the point is the earlier proposal.

## What other states show up for

A state with a large formulation industry can have no central bulk-drug park and still have firms inside the PLI. The February 2026 reply, written in answer to a Tamil Nadu question, says two of the approved bulk-drug projects are from Tamil Nadu, and 16 manufacturing units under the pharmaceuticals PLI are situated there. It points infrastructure questions for that state toward the medical-devices park scheme, whose three parks are in Greater Noida, Ujjain, and Kanchipuram. A medical-device park is a different common-infrastructure programme. It does not add a fourth bulk-drug park.

Biotechnology parks are a third map, held by the Department of Biotechnology rather than the Department of Pharmaceuticals. DBT’s National Biotechnology Parks Scheme note lists support, at the time of that note, for parks or incubation centres in Lucknow, Hyderabad, Chennai (TICEL and the Golden Jubilee Biotech Park for Women), Guwahati, Cochin, and Bengaluru. The note does not date the phrase “so far.” A state strategy that treats the Chennai biotech park and the Kanchipuram device park and a hypothetical bulk-drug park as one campus has merged three instruments. BIRAC’s incubator map is a fourth layer: bio-incubators are not the three bulk-drug parks.

## How a state team should brief a site visit

Ask the state implementing agency for allotment status, the date steam and effluent treatment are contracted to be available, and which central instalment has actually been released. The Lok Sabha reply is the national snapshot of scope and stage. It is not a plot-allotment letter. Thirteen states asked. Three were approved. Everyone else is in a different scheme, or in no central park scheme at all.

Construction stage, park by park, is unpacked in the [execution brief](/blog/pharma-and-biotech-execution-bottleneck-review-mnre-gov-20260330-594).
