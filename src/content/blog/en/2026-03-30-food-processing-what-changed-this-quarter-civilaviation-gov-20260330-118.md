# Food Processing In India: What A Quarter Check Can Show

## What this quarter note is

This page does not report a new food-processing circular for the quarter ending 23 September 2026. A useful quarter check is a re-read of the pages that actually govern plants, cold chain, and exports. Those pages sit with the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI), the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), and the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA).

## Dates already printed on the scheme pages

MoFPI's page on Pradhan Mantri Kisan SAMPADA Yojana (PMKSY) states a continuation of that central sector scheme through 31 March 2026. That calendar date is now past. The same page still describes the scheme in those terms. This brief does not announce an extension, and it does not announce that the scheme has been replaced. Anyone treating a cold-chain or cluster file as open should read the page again before filing.

The Production Linked Incentive Scheme for Food Processing Industry (PLISFPI) page states a six-year run from 2021-22 to 2026-27. It also says the mandated investment in plant and machinery was for 2021-22 and 2022-23, with investment made in 2020-21 counted toward that mandate. A reader in this quarter should not assume a fresh investment window on those same terms.

## What would actually count as a change

A real quarterly change would show up as one of these, in the institution's own words:

- A MoFPI update to the PMKSY component list, or a later decision on the window that the page still dates to 31 March 2026.
- A change in PLISFPI segments or in the incentive years the page already sets through 2026-27.
- An FSSAI advisory or a change in how licence and registration are filed on FoSCoS.
- An APEDA change to recognized horticulture pack houses, export standards, or the scheduled-product list.

None of those is asserted here as having occurred this quarter.

## Where to look, and where not to look

Cold chain, agro-processing clusters, processing and preservation capacity, food-safety infrastructure, human resources and institutions, and Operation Greens are the components the PMKSY page says are being implemented. Mega Food Parks and the Creation of Backward and Forward Linkages are among the items that page says were discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with committed liability still provided for. A quarter check that still starts from a new Mega Food Park is reading an older track.

FSSAI's FoSCoS portal is where a food business applies for a new licence or registration, a renewal, or a modification. APEDA's recognized-packhouse list is the live export-plant record for horticulture, filterable by state and product scope.
