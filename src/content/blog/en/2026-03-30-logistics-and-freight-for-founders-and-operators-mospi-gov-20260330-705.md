An operator does not need a theory of India’s logistics policy. An operator needs to know which public tools touch the vehicle, the document, and the customer promise. The stack below is the one the government has already described. The national cost ratio is not part of the stack you price a lane with.

## The tools that already sit on the trip

When the National Logistics Policy was launched on [17 September 2022](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-launches-national-logistics-policy/), the Prime Minister’s list of working tools was e-way bills, FASTag, e-Sanchit, faceless customs assessment, and GST as the tax system behind interstate movement. Those are still the daily layer. The GST system’s public face is [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in/). File the movement document your current notification requires. A blog that quotes a rupee cutoff is not the notification. FASTag is the toll layer on the highway network the roads ministry describes under [Bharatmala](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala). A tag that fails is a controllable dwell. Call it that in the control tower, not “infrastructure.”

If you run export or import boxes, the Logistics Data Bank is the visibility tool the [11 August 2026 reply](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf) says tracks containers across ports, container freight stations, and inland container depots. Ask operations whether a customer can be told where the box is without a call. If the answer is no, the gap is adoption, not the absence of a programme.

If you are building software, [ULIP](https://goulip.in/) is the API door the same reply calls operational. Write the use before you write the pitch. A transporter’s proof-of-movement product and a shipper’s exception product are different uses. Both are more serious than “logistics super-app on government data.”

Associations, not individual drivers, were given E-Logs at launch: a channel to put operational problems in front of the agencies that can fix them. The August reply says a Service Improvement Group now exists for grievances and reform ideas. Use the association if you have one. A lone fleet still lives in the e-way and the tag.

## The paper that changes a site, not a trip

State logistics policy and industry status change the conversation about land, power, and incentives. The August reply says 29 states and Union Territories have formulated policies and 32 have granted industry status. Pull the notification for the state you are in. Do not apply a neighbour’s incentive because the national count includes both of you.

If you are siting a shed or a park, the public Gati Shakti query platform — the written reply of 11 August 2026 says it opened in September 2025 — is the place to test whether the approach road, the rail layer, or the port link is actually mapped. About 1,800 layers is the national claim. Your plot is a yes or a no. Multi-modal parks under Bharatmala have model concession documents on the roads ministry site. Read the link roads in the same sitting as the concession. A park whose highway is sanctioned and not under implementation is a different business from a park whose highway is being built. The planning-group split on that date was 256 sanctioned and 198 under implementation.

Commodity matters. If you haul coal, steel, fertilizers, cement, pharmaceuticals, or food, ask for the sectoral plan the reply says has been initiated. Temperature, trace, and rail interface are not one product. If you haul something else, design for your shipper’s clock and do not wait for a plan with your name on it.

## What to keep out of the operating review

Do not let “7.9 percent of GDP” into a lane quote. That figure, from the DPIIT–NCAER assessment the August reply cites, is a national claim. Your cost is fuel, tolls, empty kilometres, detention, and the wage you pay. Do not let “13–14 percent” in either. That was the 2022 speech’s framing of the problem, not your P&L. Do not hire against a jobs boom. The skills fact in the reply is 68 qualification packs and courses in more than 100 institutes. Ask a training partner which pack matches the role. Leave the rest.

The operator’s stack is short on purpose: document, toll, container trail, a data API with a use, the state notification, and the column a connecting project is actually in. [Great Indian Company](https://greatindiancompany.com/) writes for founders who run that stack, not for the slide that replaces it.
