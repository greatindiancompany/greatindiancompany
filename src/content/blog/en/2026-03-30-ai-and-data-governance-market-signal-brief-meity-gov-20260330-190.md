A market signal is a price or a quantity a buyer can check. For India AI, the checkable signal is not a dollar size of "the AI market." MeitY and PIB have not published that. They have published the terms on which shared public compute is offered, and the size of the public dataset shelf.

## The price

On 6 March 2025, at the Mission's anniversary, MeitY launched the IndiaAI Compute Portal. PIB reported that eligible users would receive up to 40 percent subsidy on AI compute services on the cloud. The Minister said the portal would initially offer 10,000 GPUs, with 8,693 more to be added. The Secretary said this compute pillar was nearly 45 percent of the Mission's funding. That "nearly 45 percent" is the Secretary's statement that day. It is not a later budget re-estimate.

The rate that later notes repeat is about Rs 65 per GPU-hour. The Lok Sabha reply placed on PIB for 25 March 2026 says the average is approximately Rs 65 per GPU per hour, except for select high-end GPUs, against 38,231 GPUs onboarded from 14 empaneled providers. A PIB note of 10 February 2026 describes more than 38,000 GPUs at ₹65 per hour as nearly one third of the global average cost. That comparison is the note's comparison. These briefs do not recompute a world price.

By the PIB backgrounder of 13 August 2026, shared capacity was 45,000+ GPUs as of June 2026, and 237 projects had drawn 93.18 lakh GPU hours by August 2026. Usage is the signal that the shelf is being used. Empanelment is not the same number as hours consumed.

The portal itself is [compute.indiaai.gov.in](https://compute.indiaai.gov.in). Eligibility in the March 2025 note covers startups, MSMEs, academia, researchers, students, and government. A private lab that does not fit those classes should not assume the Rs 65 rate is its invoice.

## The catalogue

AIKosh is the dataset and model shelf. The counts move, so the date is part of the signal.

- 6 March 2025: over 300 datasets and over 80 models, at launch.
- 10 February 2026: 7,500+ datasets and 273 models.
- July 2026, in the 13 August backgrounder: over 14,000 datasets and 331 models.

The shelf got larger. A founder quoting "300 datasets" in a September 2026 deck is quoting the launch day. The home page is [aikosh.indiaai.gov.in](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home). PIB's note of 13 March 2026 says access can be through a secure API and a sandbox, and that some sovereign models, including Sarvam, sit on the shelf. "Anyone can use" in that note still means use on the platform's terms, not a waiver of the DPDP Act once the Act's processing duties begin.

## What is not a signal

The India AI Governance Guidelines, released on 5 November 2025, are a framework. They are not a price. The DPDP penalty ceilings are not a price of compute. The Reserve Bank's April 2018 payment-data rule is a storage instruction for payment systems, not a GPU tariff.

Foundation-model selection is a quantity of public awards, not a revenue line. The 13 August 2026 backgrounder says 20 proposals were selected from 506 applications. An earlier note, on 10 February 2026, said 12 startups had been selected across the first two phases. The later count is the one to use for "how many now," and the earlier count should stay attached to February.

For the rupee outlay behind the subsidy, use the [investment note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-investment-implications-meity-gov-20260330-230). For the sequence of GPU stocks without adding them together, use the [capex note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-capex-cycle-watch-meity-gov-20260330-350).
