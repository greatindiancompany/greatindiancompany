# The 2029 defence targets, set beside the last recorded year

## The aims, in the Minister's words

On 30 August 2024, PIB release PRID 2050090 reported the Raksha Mantri setting two aims: defence production of Rs 3 lakh crore by 2029, and defence exports of Rs 50,000 crore by 2029. He said annual production had crossed Rs 1.27 lakh crore, and that the nearer aim was Rs 1.75 lakh crore in that fiscal year, 2024-25. He also said exports in FY 2023-24 had crossed Rs 21,000 crore.

A target spoken by the Minister and carried by PIB is a public aim. It is not a forecast from Great Indian Company, and it is not a contractual commitment to any one firm.

## What was recorded after the speech

Two later releases fix the base that 2029 has to be read against.

On 9 August 2025 the Ministry reported defence production of Rs 1,50,590 crore in FY 2024-25. That was 18 percent above the previous year's Rs 1.27 lakh crore, and 90 percent above Rs 79,071 crore in FY 2019-20. It is also below the Rs 1.75 lakh crore aim stated for FY 2024-25. The page does not call that nearer aim met.

The export release reports Rs 23,622 crore in FY 2024-25, against Rs 21,083 crore in FY 2023-24. That is above the "crossed Rs 21,000 crore" mark he cited for the previous year, and still far from Rs 50,000 crore.

A PIB note of 20 November 2025 repeats the 2029 pair. It also says India was on track for Rs 1.75 lakh crore of production in the fiscal year then current, which was 2025-26. This note, written in September 2026, does not have a Ministry outturn for FY 2025-26 production. Until that release is opened, the last production figure to use is Rs 1,50,590 crore, with the caveat that the November note describes the same FY 2024-25 as Rs 1.54 lakh crore.

## The gap, without a made-up growth rate

From the August 2025 production figure to the Rs 3 lakh crore aim, and from Rs 23,622 crore of exports to the Rs 50,000 crore aim, the distance is visible. This page does not turn that distance into a compound annual rate, a probability, or a year-by-year path. Doing so would add an assumption the notices do not contain.

The composition of the export gain matters for the path. In FY 2024-25 the private export figure was almost unchanged, at Rs 15,233 crore against Rs 15,209 crore, while DPSU exports rose from Rs 5,874 crore to Rs 8,389 crore. A mid-term story that needs the private line to carry the next jump should say so. The latest year did not show that jump.

## The item-level version of the same horizon

Positive-list timelines are the mid-term in miniature. The 18 August 2026 list gives each of 405 items an indicative date, after which successful Indian development is supposed to become Indian procurement. Those dates will arrive before and after 2029. They are not summed, in the notice, into the Rs 3 lakh crore aim. A planner who wants a bridge between item timelines and the national target will not find it in one table. Build it only from the items the firm actually has.

## What to revisit when a new year closes

Replace Rs 1,50,590 crore only when a Ministry release states the next production year. Replace Rs 23,622 crore only when the next export release states it. Keep the 2029 aims labelled as aims. Great Indian Company will not move the target to make the gap look smaller.
