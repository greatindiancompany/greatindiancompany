# The path from UPI payments to consented credit

The mid-term story of Indian financial digitalization is already visible in the order of the documents. First a real-time payment system that any bank account can join. Then a consent pipe for financial information. Then a conduct code that pulls digital loans back to regulated lenders. Alongside that, a central-bank token that is meant to work like cash, not like a deposit. The trajectory is this sequence. It is not a promise that credit will automatically become as cheap as a UPI transfer.

## Payments became the default interface

The Press Information Bureau [factsheet of 24 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=150962&lang=1) dates UPI's pilot to April 2016, with 21 member banks, and the public rollout to August 2016. MeitY's [Digital India page](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/) describes the result: one app can reach multiple bank accounts for transfers and merchant payments. By July 2026, the same factsheet counts 741 live banks and 2,365.8 crore transactions worth ₹29.87 lakh crore in that month. Annual volume, on that factsheet, moved from 2 crore transactions in FY 2016-17 to over 24,162 crore in FY 2025-26.

The product path on the factsheet shows what "payments" came to include. Mandates and AutoPay. Feature-phone access through UPI 123PAY. Small-value UPI Lite. Credit-card linking. In 2023, a pre-sanctioned bank credit line as a funding account. The Reserve Bank wrote that last permission on [4 September 2023](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12532) and extended it to small finance banks on 12 February 2025. The bank still sets the limit, the tenor, and the interest. UPI is the instruction rail. The credit decision stays at the bank.

## Data and conduct rules arrived because payments were not enough

Account aggregation was on the books early. The [NBFC-Account Aggregator Directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10598) are dated 2 September 2016 and took effect after the Gazette notification of 26 November 2016. They are updated as on 6 September 2024. The aggregator fetches specified information, from deposits to GST returns, and presents it to the customer or to a regulated user. It does not keep the information, and it does not execute the payment. The mid-term use of that pipe is underwriting and personal finance for entities that are already regulated. It is not an open web of scrapers.

Digital lending's consolidation came later, because the conduct problems showed up after apps scaled. The [2025 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848) repealed the 2022 guidelines and the 2023 guarantee circular and restated the rule: the regulated entity lends, disburses to the borrower, and remains liable for the service provider. From November 2025 the borrower can see competing offers on one screen. That is the mid-term shape of "embedded credit". Embedded means the lender is identifiable, not that the marketplace has become the balance sheet.

The Open Credit Enablement Network is a protocol aimed at the same seam: a loan agent, a lender, and a data pipe. It can standardise messages. It cannot change the directions. A protocol flow that hides the lender, parks money with the agent, or treats a five per cent default loss guarantee as the full risk transfer is outside the trajectory the Reserve Bank has written down.

## Cash has a separate track

The [Digital Rupee concept note](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationReportDetails.aspx?ID=1218) and the late-2022 pilots put e₹ beside deposits and UPI, not on top of them as a replacement schedule. Retail e₹ does not pay interest. Wholesale e₹ was tried for government-security settlement so that central-bank money could do a job that otherwise needs settlement guarantees. A mid-term plan that assumes e₹ volume will track UPI volume is guessing. The published design says they solve different problems: a payment instruction against a bank account, versus a token that is itself the money.

The durable trajectory is therefore three layers that stay distinct. NPCI's rail for instructions. The aggregator's consent artefact for data. The regulated lender's balance sheet for credit, with e₹ as public cash in digital form.
