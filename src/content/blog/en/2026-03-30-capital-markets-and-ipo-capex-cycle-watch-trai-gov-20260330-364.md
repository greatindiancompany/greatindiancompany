# Fresh issue or offer for sale: what the IPO rupee funds

Capex-cycle commentary often treats an IPO as money for plants and equipment. In India, a large share of the IPO rupee is paid to shareholders who are selling. SEBI's Annual Report 2025-26, Chapter 3, separates those two flows. The objects of the issue, which sit in the offer document under ICDR, are what remains after that split.

## The year, in the regulator's table

Table 3.1 covers public issues and rights issues. Amounts are crore rupees. SME platform money is included.

| | 2024-25 issues | 2024-25 amount | 2025-26 issues | 2025-26 amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IPOs | 320 | 1,72,328 | 366 | 1,88,616 |
| Of which, offer for sale only | 15 | 58,994 | 19 | 50,849 |
| Of which, fresh issue only | 215 | 22,108 | 214 | 21,866 |
| Of which, both | 90 | 91,226 | 133 | 1,15,901 |
| Fresh piece of mixed deals |  | 44,596 |  | 58,166 |
| Offer-for-sale piece of mixed deals |  | 46,630 |  | 57,735 |
| Follow-on public offers | 2 | 18,150 | 1 | 88 |
| Rights issues | 142 | 19,712 | 139 | 46,168 |

Pure fresh IPOs are the capex-capable pipe, and they are the small one: ₹21,866 crore across 214 issues. Mixed deals are where fresh capital actually scaled, ₹58,166 crore, sitting next to ₹57,735 crore of exits in the same offerings. Add the pure offer-for-sale IPOs and selling shareholders took ₹50,849 crore more, outside the company.

Rights issues are closer to a capex or balance-sheet tool, because the company receives the money. They raised ₹46,168 crore. One issue was about 54 per cent of that. A single large rights deal can look like a capex cycle when it is one issuer's funding plan.

## The longer record says the exit share was the norm

Box 3.2 looks at listings from April 2011 to December 2025. Across 543 main-board and 1,391 SME IPOs, ₹7.9 lakh crore was mobilised. Fresh proceeds were ₹2.9 lakh crore, 37 per cent. Offer-for-sale proceeds were ₹5 lakh crore, 63 per cent. On the main board the offer-for-sale share was under 50 per cent in 2011-2015, above 60 per cent from 2016, and 87 per cent at the 2020 peak. It has eased since. Deals above ₹1,000 crore stayed in a 60 to 90 per cent offer-for-sale range from 2016. The share of main-board IPOs that were more than 70 per cent offer for sale fell from over half in 2017-2021 to about a quarter recently. SME deals stayed in a 0 to 27 per cent offer-for-sale range.

The chapter says the same review covered intended use of net IPO proceeds, as disclosed in offer documents, for 1,620 IPOs. The published narrative that is fully stated in the extract is the fresh-versus-sale split above. It does not, in that narrative, print a single national percentage for plant capex versus debt repayment versus general corporate purposes. Those shares live in each offer document. Treating the ₹1.8 lakh crore of main-board IPO money as a capex print would double-count exits.

## What still has to be financed inside the company

For the fresh piece, ICDR, last amended on 21 March 2026, requires the objects of the issue in the offer document. Chapter 3 also records a process change that makes those objects easier to see before the price band: an abridged prospectus, in a standard form, is filed at the draft stage and hosted online. After listing, use of proceeds does not disappear into a press note. LODR, last amended on 14 July 2026, is the ongoing disclosure regime, including the monitoring and deviation disclosures the regulations require of listed entities. A reader who wants the capex number asks three questions of the document, in order.

1. How much of this issue is fresh, and how much is a sale by existing holders?
2. Of the fresh net proceeds, what do the objects assign to capital expenditure, repayment, or other uses?
3. After listing, what has the monitoring report said against those objects?

Listed companies that are already public have other fresh-capital pipes, and they did not move together in 2025-26. Qualified institutional placements raised ₹67,853 crore, half the previous year's ₹1,35,597 crore. Preferential allotments raised ₹1,48,219 crore, up 76.3 per cent. Debt issues raised ₹9,11,078 crore, down 8.4 per cent, and 98.8 per cent of that was private placement. A company funding a plant may not show up in the IPO table at all.

## A usable capex watch

Watch the fresh column of Table 3.1, the rights total, and the QIP and preferential lines. Then open the objects section of the specific draft. The national IPO headline is a mix of factory finance and shareholder exit. SEBI's own decade share is 37 per cent fresh and 63 per cent exit. The latest year moved the fresh share up inside mixed deals. It did not turn the IPO market into a pure capex market.
