Payment System Indicators - August 2026 is the Reserve Bank of India's monthly scoreboard for the systems it compiles. Volume is in lakh. Value is in ₹ crore. One lakh is 1,00,000, so a volume of 1,000 lakh is 100 million transactions, and 10,000 lakh is one billion. The figures below are the August 2026 column. RBI marks them provisional.

UPI at 2,45,089.58 lakh is 24.51 billion transactions. The value, ₹29,82,356 crore, is ₹29.82 lakh crore. Dividing those two gives an average of about ₹1,217 per UPI payment.

The completed year in the FY 2025-26 column, which is not the August column, shows UPI at 24,16,168.86 lakh transactions and ₹3,14,23,250 crore. That is 241.62 billion transactions and ₹314.23 lakh crore for the financial year printed in the header.

## What the totals already contain

The row label for total digital payments is (1+2+3+4+5): RTGS, retail credit transfers, debit transfers and direct debits, card payments, and prepaid instruments. UPI sits inside retail credit transfers, so it is already inside the digital total. USSD sits inside UPI.

UPI was 87.1 percent of digital payment volume that month and 10.1 percent of digital payment value. RTGS was 68.5 percent of the value of all payments, paper included. The count leader and the value leader are different systems.

Credit cards, debit cards and prepaid instruments in this table are instrument classes. The release does not split them into RuPay and other card schemes. Scheme totals are the subject of NPCI's RuPay product statistics.

## Stocks beside the flows

As on August 2026, again in lakh: credit cards outstanding 1,240.54, debit cards outstanding 10,368.29, prepaid instruments 23,320.35, of which wallets 18,389.26, static UPI QR codes 8,130.31, Bharat QR codes 53.30, and PoS terminals 102.08.

The static QR line is defined that way in the footnote: static UPI QR codes only. A QR count and a PoS count answer different questions, and neither is a count of merchant shops.

The same footnote says card and prepaid figures may not be comparable with earlier periods because RBI has published finer categories and revised definitions. The August 2026 level can be quoted on its own. A jump against an older stock needs that warning in the same sentence.

## A second book for product detail

NPCI publishes UPI product statistics, including the count of banks live on UPI, in its own table. Use that page for the product series. Use this RBI page when the question is how UPI sat next to NEFT, IMPS, cards, wallets and RTGS in one month.
