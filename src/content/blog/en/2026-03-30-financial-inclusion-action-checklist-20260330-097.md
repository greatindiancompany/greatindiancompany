Use this order when a pitch, a board note, or a policy memo cites “financial inclusion” in India. Each step points at the release that can support the sentence, and at the Great Indian Company brief that stays inside that release.

1. **Start with the latest FI-Index note, and read the clause about why it moved.** The [16 July 2026 release](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63164) puts the index at 70.0 for March 2026, against 67.0, and says the gain was mainly Usage. If a later July note names Access instead, the story has changed. The weights, fixed when RBI introduced the index, are Access 35 percent, Usage 45 percent, Quality 20 percent. The short note does not reprint sub-index levels. Follow [what changed](/blog/financial-inclusion-what-changed-20260330-007) and the [year-by-year path](/blog/financial-inclusion-long-term-trend-20260330-047).

2. **Cite Jan Dhan from a dated progress report.** As on 25 March 2026 the [country table](https://www.pmjdy.gov.in/account-statistics-country.aspx) shows 57.93 crore beneficiaries, 32.29 crore of them women, deposits of ₹3,05,244.71 crore, and 40.27 crore RuPay cards. The homepage counter moves. The state report as on 11 March 2026 will not match the 25 March national total, because it is a different day. The [scoreboard](/blog/financial-inclusion-key-numbers-20260330-017) keeps those stocks, and the [state page](/blog/financial-inclusion-regional-view-20260330-067) explains what the map is counting.

3. **Keep the household product and the firm’s credit apart.** A PMJDY account is a personal basic savings account with a RuPay card and, for those who qualify, an overdraft of up to ₹10,000. The scheme page also lists MUDRA eligibility. NSFI 2025–30, released on 1 December 2025, treats new-to-credit micro enterprises as further work. Read the [household page](/blog/financial-inclusion-impact-on-households-20260330-037) before the [small-firm page](/blog/financial-inclusion-impact-on-smes-20260330-027).

4. **If the comparison is international, open [Global Findex 2025](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/042c3272-9a73-43f3-b117-985482acd7f8/content) and stay inside it.** Table B.1 reports 89 percent account ownership in India for 2024, 48 percent making or receiving a digital payment, 27 percent saving formally, and 15 percent borrowing formally. The report says women and men in India are equally likely to have an account, and that 16 percent of account owners had no active account. Do not place the 89 next to the FI-Index of 70 as if they measured the same thing. The [global brief](/blog/financial-inclusion-global-context-20260330-087) is the comparison.

5. **Name the rail before you describe the risk.** NPCI’s AePS is the bank-led, Aadhaar-authenticated service at a touchpoint, used for cash and a short list of other transactions. From 1 January 2026, acquiring banks must complete RBI’s due diligence on AePS touchpoint operators. A Findex “inactive” account (no payment or cash movement for a year) is not the same status as an RBI “inoperative” account (no customer-induced transaction for more than two years). The [policy explainer](/blog/financial-inclusion-policy-explainer-20260330-057) splits the stack. The [risk page](/blog/financial-inclusion-risk-watch-20260330-077) splits the definitions.

6. **Match the reader to the decision.** A founder placing cash-in and cash-out needs the AePS and Bank Mitra rules. An investor sizing a credit book needs the micro-enterprise language in NSFI, not the beneficiary count. A policy reader tracking gender can use two published facts side by side: women are 32.29 crore of 57.93 crore Jan Dhan beneficiaries, and the Findex ownership gap in India is not statistically significant, while the gap in active use is smaller than it was in 2021 and still present. A state official placing agents can use the 11 March 2026 table and the NSFI aim of at least 30 percent women business correspondents by December 2028, from about 15 percent.

Great Indian Company keeps a brief to one of these questions. The credible next step is the official page named in that brief, then the neighbouring brief that answers the question this one refused to blur.
