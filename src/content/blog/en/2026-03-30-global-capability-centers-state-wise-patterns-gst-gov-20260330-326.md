# State Patterns in India's Software Export Footprint

Captive hubs are discussed as a national story and experienced as a city story. The official state table that actually lists software-export rupees is STPI's account of units registered with it. It is not a census of global capability centres, and STPI marks the 2024-25 figures tentative.

## The export stock

Exports by STPI-registered IT/ITeS units were **₹10,69,270.59 crore** in 2024-25, up **13.35 percent** from **₹9,43,312.66 crore** in 2023-24. Of the latest total, **₹6,88,194.12 crore** came from units under the STP scheme and **₹3,81,076.50 crore** from units using only Softex attestation.

The largest lines in the annual report's state table, in ₹ crore:

| State or UT | FY 2024-25 |
| --- | ---: |
| Karnataka | 4,58,669.50 |
| Maharashtra | 2,09,857.71 |
| Telangana | 1,39,571.50 |
| Tamil Nadu | 87,146.59 |
| Haryana | 61,670.66 |
| Uttar Pradesh | 47,917.77 |
| West Bengal | 14,339.95 |
| Delhi | 12,259.59 |
| Gujarat | 9,645.79 |
| Kerala | 7,164.05 |
| Andhra Pradesh | 5,431.26 |
| Odisha | 3,844.45 |

Karnataka's line is a little under **43 percent** of the table total. Maharashtra is about **20 percent** and Telangana about **13 percent**. Adding Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh gives **₹10,04,833.73 crore**, about **94 percent** of the reported total. Those shares are arithmetic on STPI's own table, not a separate official percentage.

The thin end of the same table is the useful check on "pan-India" language. Bihar is **₹5.61 crore**, Himachal Pradesh **₹8.08 crore**, Sikkim **₹7.13 crore**, Jharkhand **₹47.76 crore**, and Assam **₹55.42 crore**. Odisha, at **₹3,844.45 crore**, is a real export base and still a small fraction of Karnataka's line.

## Centres and rupees are different maps

STPI reports **67** centres operational, **59** of them in Tier II and Tier III cities. In 2024-25 it opened centres at Koraput (10,000 sq ft) and Jajpur (41,200 sq ft). Centres at Agra, Gorakhpur, Bareilly, Itanagar, Amritsar, Kangra, Sambalpur, Jamshedpur, Darbhanga, Dhanbad, and Balasore were at various stages of implementation. The society also reports **17 lakh sq ft** of IT-grade space and incubation for **411** incubatees as on 31 March 2025.

The centre map is dispersed. The export map is not. A state policy that counts STPI centres will show a wide footprint. A state policy that counts export rupees will show six states. Both descriptions can be true because they count different objects. Captive hubs named in the PIB note of 25 February 2025 — Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Mumbai, and the National Capital Region — sit inside the heavy export states, which is consistent with the rupee table and is still only a list of hubs offered in a ministerial remark.

## FDI by state is a third map

DPIIT's factsheet to March 2025 reports all-sector FDI equity, not software and not captives. In 2024-25 the leading recipients included Maharashtra (**USD 19,589 million**), Karnataka (**USD 6,619 million**), Delhi (**USD 6,091 million**), Gujarat (**USD 5,711 million**), Tamil Nadu (**USD 3,681 million**), Haryana (**USD 3,147 million**), and Telangana (**USD 2,994 million**). State data on that sheet run from October 2019. Gujarat ranks high in all-sector equity and much lower in the STPI software-export table. Using the FDI ranking as a software-hub ranking will mis-order the states.
