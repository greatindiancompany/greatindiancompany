# A 90-day checklist for operators diversifying a manufacturing trade supply base

Ninety days is enough to find out whether a second source is real. It is not enough to build a cell plant. Use the window to clear identity, the input rule, the preference, the capital screen, and the location. Leave construction for the capex plan.

## Days 1 to 30: identity and the input

Get the Importer-Exporter Code on dgft.gov.in if the firm does not have one. FTP 2023, in force since 1 April 2023, makes that code mandatory for goods trade. Then classify the input and the finished good. Note whether each line is free, restricted, or prohibited, and whether a Quality Control Order applies.

Decide the sales lane before you place the trial order. Under paragraph 2.03(A) of FTP 2023, inputs for Advance Authorisation, EOUs, and SEZs can enter without meeting a mandatory QCO, on conditions that keep those goods out of the domestic market. If you sell in India, that exemption is the wrong plan. If you export, write the pre-import and no-diversion conditions into the purchase order so the trial lot is not stuck.

Pull the last two years of the line from the Commerce databank at tradestat.commerce.gov.in. You want to see whether India already exports or imports that heading, not whether a sector slogan is popular.

## Days 31 to 60: preference, capital, and the scheme

If the customer wants a tariff preference, open DGFT Appendix 2A and confirm the agreement is in force, not merely announced. The India-UAE CEPA has a known start: 1 May 2022. On 13 May 2026 the Commerce and Industry Minister said four of nine recent agreements were operational and five were expected within about 12 months. Do not assume your lane is one of the four. Ask DGFT's certificate-of-origin route, and check Appendix 2B for who may issue the preferential certificate. Run the bill of materials against the origin rule before you promise the duty saving.

If a new investor is funding the Indian entity, read paragraph 3.1.1(a) of DPIIT's Consolidated FDI Policy Circular of 2020. Manufacturing itself is on the automatic route, including contract manufacturing under a legally tenable contract. An investor from a land-border country, or a beneficial owner there, is on the government route. Start that file in this window if it applies. Waiting until the shed is up is how a "China-plus-one" project slips a year.

If the product sits in one of the 14 PLI sectors listed in the 2 August 2023 PIB note, download that ministry's guidelines in this same month. The Rs 1.97 lakh crore outlay is not an application. If the product is outside the list, stop chasing the incentive and cost the project without it.

## Days 61 to 90: place and a review date

Shortlist locations on a DPIIT corridor spine rather than on a state advertisement alone. Read the state's logistics policy if it has notified one, and read LEADS 2025, released on 13 May 2026, for the state's logistics position. The DPIIT-NCAER cost study, reported by PIB on 27 November 2025, puts 2023-24 logistics cost at 7.97 percent of GDP and warns that smaller firms pay more, especially when the first and last 50 kilometres of a medium haul are weak. Visit that last mile. Do not underwrite the national ratio.

End the ninety days with a one-page decision: proceed, proceed only for export, or stop. Attach the IEC, the QCO path, the agreement status, the FDI screen, and the site note. Book a date to reread DGFT notifications, because the appendix on quality orders moves.
