# A dated record of India's water and sanitation figures

Every figure below is tied to the document that states it. Where two documents disagree, both are shown. They usually disagree because the as-on date differs, or because one number is a central share and the other is a total outlay. This page does not pick a favourite.

## Rural tap connections

| What was stated | Figure | As on | Where it is written |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rural households identified by states and Union Territories | 19.36 crore | 10 March 2026 and 4 August 2026 | Cabinet note; Lok Sabha answer |
| Households with tap connections at the start of the mission | 3.23 crore | August 2019 | Both documents. The Cabinet note rounds the share to 17 percent. The Lok Sabha answer says 16.72 percent. |
| Additional households provided connections | more than 12.56 crore | 10 March 2026 | Cabinet note |
| Households reported with tap connections | around 15.80 crore (81.61 percent) | 10 March 2026 | Cabinet note |
| Households reported with tap connections | more than 15.90 crore (82.19 percent) | 4 August 2026 | Lok Sabha answer |

These are reported connections. They are not Gram Sabha certifications, and they are not a count of households with round-the-clock potable supply.

## AMRUT 2.0

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs told the Lok Sabha, using portal data as on 9 July 2026:

- 8,777 approved projects, total cost ₹1,97,191.85 crore including operation and maintenance, central share ₹66,638.59 crore.
- Water supply: 3,572 projects, ₹1,18,968.38 crore.
- Sewerage and septage: 605 projects, ₹71,133.01 crore.
- Water-body rejuvenation: 2,951 projects, ₹6,003.34 crore.
- Parks: 1,649 projects, ₹1,087.12 crore.
- Awarded: 7,687 projects, capex ₹1,37,130.13 crore.
- Physically completed works: ₹66,472 crore.

The 2021 Cabinet approval had set an indicative outlay of ₹2,77,000 crore, including central share of ₹76,760 crore, for FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26. That indicative ceiling is not the approved-project total above.

## Namami Gange sewage treatment

Till June 2026, the ministry told Parliament that 219 sewerage projects costing ₹35,970 crore had been taken up, for 6,610 million litres a day, and that 146 projects with 4,263 million litres a day had been completed. An earlier PIB note, as of December 2025, had put created capacity at 3,977 million litres a day, with 138 of 218 projects operational. In FY 2025-26, a 21 April 2026 note said 538.03 million litres a day was added through 18 projects and 28 sewage treatment plants were completed, against 22 the previous year.
