# Maternity leave, creches and workplace committees

The rules that govern a woman at a workplace in India are headcount rules and procedure rules. They are not a participation target. The Ministry of Labour and Employment administers maternity benefit. The Ministry of Women and Child Development is the nodal ministry for the sexual harassment law. Both have stated the operating thresholds in Parliament and through PIB.

## Paid maternity leave under the 2017 amendment

MoLE has told Parliament that it is implementing the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, as amended by the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017. Section 5, as amended, sets the period at 26 weeks, of which not more than eight weeks fall before the expected date of delivery. The Act’s proviso limits that longer period: a woman with two or more surviving children is entitled to 12 weeks, of which not more than six weeks precede the expected delivery.

A woman who legally adopts a child below the age of three months, or a commissioning mother, is entitled to 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over. Where the nature of the work allows it, section 5(5) allows the employer and the woman to agree that she will work from home after the maternity benefit, for a period and on conditions they agree. The amendment also requires every establishment to tell a woman, in writing and electronically at the time of appointment, about every benefit available under the Act.

The creche rule is section 11A. Every establishment with 50 or more employees shall have a creche within the prescribed distance, either separately or with common facilities. The employer shall allow the woman four visits a day to the creche, including the rest interval.

MoLE’s reply, carried by PIB, adds that the Code on Social Security, 2020, contains provisions for the move from 12 weeks to 26 weeks, a mandatory creche at 50 or more employees, and permission for women workers in night shifts with adequate safety measures. The reply presents those as provisions of the Code. The statute the Ministry says it is implementing is the amended 1961 Act. Night-shift practice still depends on the safety conditions the applicable law and the establishment actually put in place. This brief does not treat a Code provision as a substitute for checking commencement.

## Committees under the sexual harassment law

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, is a different threshold. PIB, quoting the framework MoWCD administers, states that an Internal Committee is mandatory where the workplace has 10 or more employees. A Local Committee, set up by the District Officer, hears cases from workplaces with fewer than 10 employees and complaints against the employer. MoWCD is the nodal ministry. It issues advisories to ministries, States, Union Territories and private bodies on constituting committees and on awareness.

SHe-Box, launched by MoWCD on 29 August 2024, is the single-window portal for a woman to file a complaint online. PIB says the portal forwards the complaint to the relevant Internal or Local Committee and allows the complainant to track it. Workplaces can upload committee details, annual reports, and training information. Separately, specified companies must state in the Board’s report, filed with the financial statements, that they have complied with the requirement to constitute an internal committee. That disclosure is a corporate-law obligation pointed at the same committee, not a second definition of harassment.

The Supreme Court has directed authorities to survey establishments and upload Internal Committee and Local Committee details to SHe-Box. MoWCD’s advisories follow that direction. For an employer, the practical sequence is the committee at 10 employees, the written policy, the orientation the advisories call for, and the SHe-Box record. For a woman in a workplace below that headcount, the path PIB describes is the Local Committee, not an Internal Committee the establishment was never required to form.

## Who these rules do not automatically cover

Usual-status PLFS for 2025 finds 64.2 percent of women workers self-employed and 9.3 percent of rural women workers in regular wage jobs. An own-account worker does not acquire a creche obligation by being counted in the labour force. She may still use Palna, the Mission Shakti creche component, which MoWCD says is open to mothers irrespective of employment status. The establishment rules and the scheme are complements. Quoting the 26-week leave as if it attached to every woman in the 40.0 percent participation rate overstates the statute.
