The recognition rule from 4 February 2026 is G.S.R. 108(E): turnover below ₹200 crore, or ₹300 crore for deep tech; age up to 10 years, or 20 for deep tech; companies, partnerships, LLPs, and cooperative societies; not a split or reconstruction. The Startup India page states that test.

## Procurement, patents, and the valuation sentence

As on the 17 April 2026 release: more than 38,600 startups onboarded on GeM; 2025-26 orders 1,40,260+ worth 19,190+ crore rupees, after 1,19,620+ orders worth 14,103+ crore rupees in 2024-25. Patent applications by startups: more than 19,400 in the cumulative line, 2,850+ in 2024-25 and 4,480+ in 2025-26.

PIB’s 15 January 2026 note says over 2 lakh DPIIT-recognised startups as of December 2025, around 50 percent emerging from Tier-II and Tier-III cities, and a high-value landscape that had grown from four privately held companies above $1 billion in 2014 to over 120, with a combined valuation above $350 billion. The city share has no published city table in that note. The dollar sentence is not a DPIIT field.
