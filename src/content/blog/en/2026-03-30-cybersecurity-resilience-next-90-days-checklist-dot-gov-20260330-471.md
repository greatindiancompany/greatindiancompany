# A 90-day cyber checklist from rules already written

The ninety days from 24 September 2026 run to 23 December 2026. One DPDP commencement falls inside that window. Most other cyber duties are already in force, so the checklist is about evidence you can produce, not about waiting for a new circular.

## Inside the window: Consent Managers

Rule 4 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 comes into force one year after the 13 November 2025 gazette publication. That date falls in this window. The rule is about persons who apply to the Data Protection Board to be registered as Consent Managers, the conditions in the First Schedule, and the Board's power to suspend or cancel a registration.

If the organisation might act as a Consent Manager, this is the quarter to read Part A and Part B of that schedule and the citizenship and corporate conditions PIB summarised — Consent Managers are Indian companies. If it will not, say so in writing so the date is not treated as a firm-wide deadline.

## Still ahead of the window, so prepare without pretending the duty has started

Rules 3 and 5 to 16 commence eighteen months after 13 November 2025, which is after this ninety-day window. They include the independent consent notice, reasonable security safeguards, and breach intimation. Use the window to draft, and label the draft as not yet the commenced duty:

- A notice that can stand alone, with an itemised description of personal data and the specific purpose, plus a link for withdrawal, rights, and a complaint to the Board.
- A safeguard note covering encryption or an equivalent measure named in Rule 6, access control, logs, backups, and the clause you will put in a data-processor contract.
- A breach sheet that can tell an affected person, without delay, what happened, what it means for them, what you are doing, what they can do, and who will answer. Keep a second sheet for the Board's immediate description and the seventy-two-hour detailed report.
- A public contact, on the website or app, of the Data Protection Officer or the person who can answer questions. PIB says requests to access, correct, update, erase or nominate are answered within a maximum of 90 days. Rule 14, in the same later group, requires a published grievance system that responds within a reasonable period not exceeding ninety days. Build that clock into the draft, and keep the draft labelled with the later commencement date.

## Already in force: produce the evidence this month

**CERT-In.** Confirm the reporting path still reaches incident@cert-in.org.in and the phone and fax numbers in the 28 April 2022 direction. Time a tabletop from "we noticed" to "the report went" and see whether six hours is real. Confirm ICT logs are enabled and retained for a rolling 180 days inside India, and that someone can export them. Name the point of contact.

**If you are an MSME under the 1 June 2020 classification notification cited in CERT-In's baseline.** Walk the fifteen control names: asset management, network and email security, endpoint and mobile security, secure configurations, patch management, incident management, logging and monitoring, awareness and training, third-party risk, data protection and backup, governance, password policy, access control, physical security, and vulnerability assessment. The document suggests a baseline audit by a CERT-In empanelled organisation at least once a year. If the last one is older than that, commission it or record why you have not. Awareness training is specified at least twice a year. If the second session of the year has not happened, put it on the calendar inside these ninety days.

**If SEBI regulates you.** The glide path dates have passed. Check which category you are in for this financial year, whether you are on your own SOC or the Market SOC, and whether the half-yearly or yearly efficacy review the framework assigns to your category is on file. Cyber audit and VAPT follow the timelines in the framework, including the 31 December 2024 clarification circular.

**If you are a telecommunication entity.** Rule 7's six-hour and twenty-four-hour reports, the Chief Telecommunication Security Officer's details on the portal, and the SOC log list are current. If you are a telecommunication identifier user entity under the October 2025 amendment, read rule 7A on the mobile-number validation platform before you design a customer-identification flow around it.

**If the Reserve Bank or IRDAI regulates you.** Confirm the CISO's reporting line still matches the direction or guideline, and that the quarterly board review (Reserve Bank) or the information-security risk committee rhythm (IRDAI) has a date in this quarter.

Leave the ninety days with four artefacts: a Consent Manager decision, a labelled DPDP draft, a timed CERT-In report path, and the sector review your regulator already requires.
