# What Urban Housing Readers Can Check in Late 2026

## The quarter in one page

The quarter ending 24 September 2026 does not contain a new national housing mission. The documents a founder, operator, or policy reader can open are a census schedule that is still in force, a parliamentary progress reply on the Smart Cities Mission, and a Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act advisory that the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs lists by date.

Census 2011 remains the last published count of how many people live in urban areas. MoHUA’s urban-growth note still uses that count. Anyone quoting a 2026 urbanisation rate is ahead of the Registrar General.

## Census houselisting is the housing event on the calendar

The Press Information Bureau note of 25 April 2026 records that the intent to conduct Population Census 2027 was notified in the Gazette on 16 June 2025, with a Cabinet outlay of ₹11,718.24 crore. Phase I, the Houselisting and Housing Census, runs in a 30-day window chosen by each State or Union Territory between April and September 2026, after an optional 15-day self-enumeration. That phase collects the condition of the house, the amenities available, and the assets the household possesses. Phase I questions were notified in January 2026.

Phase II, Population Enumeration, is scheduled for February 2027. For Ladakh and the snow-bound non-synchronous areas of Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, and Uttarakhand, Phase II is scheduled in September 2026. The reference date is 00:00 hours on 1 March 2027, and 00:00 hours on 1 October 2026 for those snow-bound areas.

As of this brief’s update, that schedule has not been replaced by a published urban-population total. The housing result of Phase I is the next official picture of housing conditions. It is not yet a table of urban shares.

## Smart Cities: a reply in this quarter, a data date of 31 March 2026

On 6 August 2026 the Minister of State for Housing and Urban Affairs answered Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 3148. The reply states that the Smart Cities Mission was originally approved up to 31 March 2020 and was extended four times, to 31 March 2025. As reported by States and Union Territories as on 31 March 2026, 8,064 projects worth ₹1,64,811 crore had been taken up, of which 7,790 projects worth ₹1,56,257 crore were completed. Of the Central allocation of ₹48,000 crore, cities had claimed ₹47,458 crore. Utilisation reported as on 31 March 2026 was ₹46,377 crore. All 100 cities had established Integrated Command and Control Centres.

The same reply records MoHUA Advisory No. 27 of 10 June 2025, on the future role of the special purpose vehicles and on keeping the command centres in use after financial closure. MoHUA’s own mission page, which gives physical progress as on 1 December 2025, shows an earlier completion count: 7,741 of the same 8,064 projects. The two dates should be kept apart. The August reply is the later official snapshot. It is still a 31 March 2026 position, not a September count.

## What MoHUA listed on the regulatory side

On 31 July 2026 MoHUA’s Housing Division advised every state Real Estate Regulatory Authority that it may extend registration, and the matching completion date, by four months. The projects named in that PDF are registered projects whose completion date, revised date, or already extended date falls on or after 28 February 2026. The ministry tied the advice to Section 6 of the Act and to a Department of Expenditure memorandum of 29 April 2026 that treats the West Asia situation as war for force majeure. The advisory suggests a common order so promoters do not each file an application. It does not itself amend a state register. Whether a given project moved depends on the authority’s order after 31 July.

PMAY-Urban 2.0 did not start in this quarter. The Press Information Bureau record of 24 July 2025 places its start at 1 September 2024. Progress stock from the December 2024 PIB background note is older than this quarter and is handled in the evidence snapshot, with its date attached.

## What a reader should do with this

Treat 1 October 2026 as the published reference date for the snow-bound enumeration, and February 2027 as the published window for the rest of the country. Read the August 2026 Lok Sabha reply when a city claims Smart Cities completion, and match the claim to the annex rather than to a national average. Open the 31 July 2026 advisory before treating a project extension as a local favour.
