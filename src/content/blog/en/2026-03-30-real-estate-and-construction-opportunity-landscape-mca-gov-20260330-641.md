# Affordable Housing, Rental Stock, and REIT Markets

The opportunity in Indian housing splits by who is being served. A family without a pucca house is inside a mission with a fixed assistance cheque. A migrant who does not want to own is inside a rental vertical. A unit-holder in a listed trust is inside a securities regulation. These can all be called "real estate." They are not one pipeline, and a firm that raises capital for one should not describe it as the others.

## Four ways a household is helped

[PMAY-U 2.0](https://pmaymis.gov.in/PMAYMIS2_2024/faq.html) lets an eligible family choose one vertical, with the state, the local body, or a primary lending institution, or through the unified portal, a common service centre, or the municipality. Eligibility is an urban EWS, LIG, or MIG family with no pucca house anywhere in the country. Income stops at ₹9 lakh for MIG. A benefit taken from a government housing scheme in the last twenty years bars entry.

Beneficiary-led construction is the EWS family that already has land, or that receives a heritable, non-transferable patta from the state. The house is 30 to 45 square metres. Central assistance is ₹1.50 lakh in plain states, ₹2.25 lakh in the North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and the Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir, Puducherry, and Delhi, and ₹2.50 lakh in other Union Territories. Plain states must add at least ₹1 lakh, and the special-category list at least ₹0.25 lakh. The FAQ does not set a state share for the other Union Territories. Money moves 40:40:20 by direct benefit transfer as the house progresses. The family may borrow. It may not also take the interest subsidy.

Affordable housing in partnership is public or private construction for eligible buyers, with the same central amounts, and with the state fixing a ceiling sale price per square metre of carpet area. Tenable slums on public land can be redeveloped here. In-situ slum redevelopment is no longer its own vertical.

Affordable rental housing is the non-ownership opportunity. It covers urban migrants, industrial workers, working women, and others who need a house for a period. The [scheme page](https://www.mohua.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/pradhan-mantri-awas-yojana-urban-MjNzYjMtQWa) describes two models inherited from the July 2020 rental-complex sub-scheme: convert vacant government-funded houses, or let a public or private entity build on its own vacant land. On that page, Model 1 had converted 5,783 vacant houses. For Model 2, the page says the ministry has approved 13 for construction of 83,298 units in seven states, with a technology innovation grant of ₹175.95 crore. Work had started on 52,293 units at named sites in Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh, and 36,454 units at Sriperumbudur, Chennai, and Hosur were reported complete. Those are the page's progress lines for that sub-scheme. They are not a fresh census dated September 2026, and they are not the whole of the new rental vertical.

The interest subsidy is the credit opportunity. Loans sanctioned and disbursed on or after 1 September 2024 can receive up to ₹1.80 lakh, released in five yearly instalments while the loan stays active and more than half the principal is outstanding. The lender credits it by reducing principal. Carpet area supported under the subsidy runs up to 120 square metres, against 45 for the construction verticals and 60 for rental.

## What is not a household vertical

A real estate investment trust is an opportunity for completed, income-producing property held for investors. SEBI's regulations of 2014, in the text [last amended on 18 April 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/apr-2026/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-real-estate-investment-trusts-regulations-2014-last-amended-on-april-18-2026-_101013.html), are the rulebook. The [2014 board note](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/docfiles/28905_t.html) described a trust with a sponsor, a manager, and a trustee, and a design that kept not less than 80 percent of asset value in completed and revenue-generating property. The amended text governs today. A REIT is not a PMAY implementation agency, and a PMAY beneficiary is not a REIT allottee. The mission's five-year bar on selling or renting an assisted house is the opposite of a trust whose purpose is income from completed buildings.

Preference groups inside the mission, including building and construction workers, street vendors, and women owners, shape who gets the assisted house. They do not create a separate equity product.

How a lender should keep the subsidy, the home loan, and the trust apart is in the [investment brief](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/real-estate-and-construction-investment-implications-mca-gov-20260330-241). Great Indian Company publishes the landscape so a pitch deck picks one customer and stays with that customer's rulebook.
