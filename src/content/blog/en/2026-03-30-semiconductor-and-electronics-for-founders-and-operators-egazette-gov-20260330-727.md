An operator needs the scheme that matches the thing the company actually makes, the milestone the money is tied to, and the test of who may apply. India's semiconductor and electronics stack has four live doors and one announced door whose claim form is still the document to watch. This brief is the operating version. It is current to 24 September 2026.

## If you design the chip

Use the Design Linked Incentive, notification CG-DL-E-21122021-232049 of 21 December 2021, implemented by C-DAC. The public page is the [ISM design scheme](https://ism.gov.in/design-linked-incentive).

Confirm three things before you spend against an expected reimbursement. First, ownership. A domestic company, in the notification, is one whose beneficial ownership is more than 50 percent with resident Indian citizens, or with Indian companies that are themselves so owned, and that status has to hold for three years after a claim. Second, the category. The [guidelines](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Guidelines-for-Design-Linked-Incentive-DLI-Scheme-30.12.2022.pdf) allow product-design applications from startups and MSMEs, and deployment applications from domestic companies, startups, and MSMEs. Third, the cap. Product design reimburses up to 50 percent of eligible expenditure, at most ₹15 crore per application. Deployment pays 6 percent down to 4 percent of net sales over five years, at most ₹30 crore per application, after the sales threshold in the approval letter.

Tool access is a separate benefit from the reimbursement. The Cabinet note of 15 July 2026 says 105 startups and MSMEs have industry-standard tools, and 24 projects have financial support. Getting onto the tool grid does not start the ₹15 crore clock by itself. Claims follow the milestones in the approval letter: design, tape-out, deployment. The July note says deployment comes after prototyping. Budget the foundry prototype as your cost until that milestone is accepted.

The same July note's design pillar under Semicon 2.0 aims at Indian intellectual property and full chip and system designs. Quote that as the direction. File against the 2021 gazette until a later notification says otherwise. The original application window was three years from 1 January 2022, extendable by the Minister. Ask C-DAC whether a new application is being received this quarter. Do not infer it from the fact that 24 older projects exist.

The [7 February 2026 budget note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?lang=1&PRID=2224839) names a processor path that is public R&D rather than a scheme you apply to as a startup: DHRUV64, a 64-bit microprocessor from C-DAC, and earlier processors under the Digital India RISC-V programme, including SHAKTI, AJIT, VIKRAM, and THEJAS, with DHANUSH and DHANUSH+ described as in development. That note also records academic chips at 180 nm from the Semiconductor Laboratory in Mohali. Those are capability markers. A commercial foundry quote for your own tape-out is still a contract you have to sign, and the note's startup tape-outs at advanced nodes, including 12 nm, were not described as wafers from the Dholera fab.

## If you make a component, a module, or the machine that builds them

Use the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme, gazette CG-DL-E-08042025-262341 of 8 April 2025, portal on the [MeitY scheme page](https://www.meity.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/electronics-component-manufacturing-scheme-UTM1IjMtQWa). Match your product to the segment before you model the rate. Display and camera modules, passives, boards, cells for digital uses, and enclosures are turnover-linked, with cumulative investment thresholds from ₹50 crore to ₹500 crore. High-density and flexible boards and surface-mount passives are hybrid, with a 25 percent capex incentive as well. Supply-chain parts and capital goods are capex-linked at 25 percent from a ₹10 crore minimum. One percentage point of the incentive depends on hitting the employment threshold. The base year is 2024-25. Tenure runs through 2031-32.

Read the application-window amendment on that MeitY page before you assume the original three-month window for sub-assemblies and bare components is the rule. The supply-chain and capital-equipment window in the original text was two years from 1 May 2025.

If an older SPECS acknowledgement is still inside its five-year investment period, keep that file, and keep it out of any PLI incremental-investment claim. The [PLI 2.0 guidelines](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/03/Guidelines_PLI-Scheme-ITHW-min.pdf) refuse to count SPECS-incentivised investment a second time. SPECS itself, 25 percent of capex under gazette CG-DL-E-01042020-218992 of 1 April 2020, had an original application close of 31 March 2023. Later addenda exist. Read them before a new SPECS application is treated as available.

## If you sell the laptop, tablet, or server

PLI 2.0 for IT Hardware is the sales incentive: around 5 percent of net incremental sales for six years, on laptops, tablets, all-in-one PCs, servers, and ultra-small-form-factor devices, outlay ₹17,000 crore. Localising design, integrated-circuit manufacturing, or packaging can count inside that scheme as an incentivised component. The denominator is still the sale of the target-segment product. A packaging house with no laptop or server revenue is in the mission's OSAT category, not in this percentage.

## If you package, test, or propose a fab

The manufacturing book is 12 approved projects, three of them — Micron, Kaynes, and CG Semi — already in commercial production as of 22 July 2026. Location has been the investor's choice, and the results are six states, with Gujarat holding the silicon fab, the gallium-nitride display plant, and the three plants already producing. Fiscal support of up to 50 percent is the framework the February 2026 note describes for the first programme. Your number is the appraisal of your project, not half of someone else's ₹91,000 crore.

Semicon 2.0, ₹1,27,500 crore, approved 15 July 2026, is the door for the next fabs, for more advanced packaging, and for equipment, chemicals, and gases. The [Cabinet release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2284784) is the authority for the pillars. File a manufacturing proposal only when the implementing notification tells you the eligible expenditure. Building a financial model on the headline outlay, before that notification, will not survive diligence.

On quality, the Minister's 26 April 2025 remarks are an operating expectation he stated in public: companies should have design teams, and plants should be able to meet a Six Sigma quality bar. That was a speech at the component-scheme launch, not a new statutory test. It is still the standard the ministry's leadership has said it will use to tell serious applicants from the rest.
