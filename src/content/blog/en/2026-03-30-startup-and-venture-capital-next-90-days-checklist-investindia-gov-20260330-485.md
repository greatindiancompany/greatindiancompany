From 24 September 2026, the next ninety days run to late December. The useful work is to notice which official series have a date, and which decisions are still described as future steps in documents that are already public. Silence after a guideline is not evidence that a sanction has, or has not, been made.

## Confirm the last printed stock before quoting a newer one

The latest exact national recognition figure in the documents used here is 2,40,092 entities as on 30 June 2026, from Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1446, answered on 28 July 2026. A companion reply states that 16,334 of them were recognised from 1 April to 30 June, and that more than 1.15 lakh recognised startups had at least one woman director or partner. No 31 July or 31 August national stock turned up in PIB or Lok Sabha material reviewed for this brief. If a September or December figure appears, replace 2,40,092 only when the new document names its own as-on date.

Pair any new stock with the definition that produced it. G.S.R. 108(E) has applied since 4 February 2026. A comparison with a 2024 stock uses a ₹100 crore turnover ceiling the February notification replaced. Say so in the footnote.

## Read Fund of Funds 2.0 for process, not for a imagined pipeline

DPIIT’s guidelines require a half-yearly review of operations, an annual utilisation report to 31 March covering funds, companies, and net asset value, and an Empowered Committee chaired by the Secretary, DPIIT. They also say another domestic implementation agency “shall be selected” in due course, beside SIDBI. By late September 2026 the public text still describes that second agency as a selection ahead, unless a later DPIIT order has named one. Look for the order. Do not fill the vacancy with a shortlist.

The same guidelines fix what a selected fund must do: Category I or II only, segment caps, and a private placement memorandum that matches the deep-tech definition in G.S.R. 108(E) if the fund wants the deep-tech segment. A manager raising in this window should mark the memorandum against the 4 February 2026 notification, not against the 2019 ₹100 crore text.

Distributions, net of up to 5 percent of returns kept for ecosystem capacity building, go back to the Consolidated Fund of India. That clause matters for anyone modelling the government as a permanent limited partner. The guidelines allow DPIIT to revise operations on the Empowered Committee’s recommendation, inside the Cabinet contours. A revision is a document to wait for, not a rumour to trade.

## Use the consolidated SEBI text

The Alternative Investment Funds Regulations, 2012, show a consolidation last amended on 18 April 2026. SEBI has also issued a master circular that folds in AIF circulars through 31 May 2026 and supersedes the master circular of 7 May 2024. A checklist item for counsel is mechanical: cite the April 2026 regulations page, and check whether a circular you rely on survived into the May 2026 master circular. Pre-2024 FAQ booklets are a poor substitute.

## Keep three scheme clocks separate

The credit guarantee’s per-borrower cover moved from ₹10 crore to ₹20 crore during 2025-26. The Startup India scheme page is where a lender confirms the cover fraction and the fee. The seed-fund corpus of ₹945 crore was fully committed by the end of 2025-26, with the scheme extended for disbursement of approved activity. The question for an incubator in this quarter is drawdown of approvals already made, not a new corpus announcement. The first Fund of Funds had disbursed more than ₹7,000 crore to funds by the end of 2025-26, against a ₹10,000 crore corpus. Further drawdown of that older programme can continue while Fund of Funds 2.0 is only at the guideline stage in the public record used here.

Jobs remain on the 31 March 2026 national print of more than 23.36 lakh direct jobs, self-reported in the earlier annexure. A new jobs claim needs a new as-on date.
