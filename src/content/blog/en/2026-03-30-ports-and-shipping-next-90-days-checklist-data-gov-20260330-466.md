# A 90-day ports and shipping checklist from September 2026

From 24 September 2026, the next ninety days run to late December. Nothing in the ministry's current pages says a new ports act will be notified in that window. The work is to watch documents that already have a home, and to ignore pages that still carry an old date. This is a reading list, not a prediction that each item will be updated.

## Major-port cargo

Open the Indian Ports Association report list, not a recycled dashboard. As of this writing the list includes major-port traffic for June 2026, posted 6 July 2026, and traffic from April to July 2026, posted 6 August 2026, plus daily vessel profiles. In this window, look for the August, September, and October notes if the association keeps that monthly habit. Compare commodity lines. A flat total can hide a shift between petroleum, coal, iron ore, containers, and coastal cargo.

Do not fill a missing month with the Sagarmala page. That page still talks about capacity against a 2025 horizon from the 2016 National Perspective Plan. It will not tell you what Paradip or Jawaharlal Nehru did in October 2026.

If a ranking appears under the Sagar Aankalan guidelines, read turnaround time, output per ship berth day, pre-berthing detention, and dwell time next to the tonnes. The Ports Wing says those rankings are periodic. It does not promise one inside these ninety days.

## Shipyards and the fund

Use the 24 July 2026 Lok Sabha reply as the baseline, not as the latest possible word. On that date the public record showed: assistance-scheme guidelines issued on 26 December 2025; Maritime Development Fund guidelines issued on 20 February 2026; SBI Ventures Limited named fund manager of the Maritime Investment Fund on 26 May 2026; in-principle approval for greenfield clusters in Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu; in-principle brownfield proposals at four yards; and a stated contract value of ₹15,234 crore for assistance approvals over two years.

In this window, check the ministry site and the Press Information Bureau for any approval that adds a yard name, a disbursement, or a fund investment to that baseline. If nothing new is posted, the honest status is unchanged in-principle approval. Do not convert the ₹69,725 crore package into a quarterly order intake.

The assistance scheme and the development scheme run to 31 March 2036. There is no cliff in December 2026. Contract date and domestic-content conditions sit in the guidelines on the ministry site. A bid due this quarter should be checked against those schedules, not against this checklist.

## Disputes, coastal cargo, and the fleet page

If a concession at a major port is in trouble, the forum is the Adjudicatory Board for Major Ports, constituted on 13 August 2025, when the old tariff authority ceased. New filings would surface through that board, not through a revived central tariff order. The port still sets its own rates under the 2021 Act.

Keep coastal and inland cargo in their own file. Major-port coastal tonnes, if the association splits them out, are not the same as cargo moving under the Jal Vahak incentive on national waterways, which the shipping division dates to 15 December 2024 and assigns to a Shipping Corporation subsidiary.

Skip the merchant-fleet paragraph on the shipping division page for any decision this quarter. It is dated 30 April 2019.
