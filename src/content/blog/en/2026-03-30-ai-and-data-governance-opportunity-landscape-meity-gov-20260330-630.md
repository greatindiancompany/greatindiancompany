An opportunity is a door that is open, with the conditions written down. In this field the doors that are open are mission programmes. The door that looks like a new regulated business, the Consent Manager, is dated and narrow.

## Open now: shared compute

The IndiaAI Compute Portal has been open since the 6 March 2025 launch. PIB said eligible users, including startups, MSMEs, researchers, students, and government, would receive up to 40 percent subsidy on cloud AI compute. Later notes put the average near Rs 65 per GPU-hour, with an exception for some high-end GPUs. By August 2026, 237 projects had used 93.18 lakh GPU hours, against a shared pool the 13 August backgrounder puts at 45,000+ GPUs as of June 2026.

The opportunity is access, not ownership of the chip. Providers are empaneled. A founder who needs the hardware classes named at launch, from H100s to Gaudi and Trainium, should treat that March 2025 list as the launch list and check the portal for what is bookable now. The portal is [compute.indiaai.gov.in](https://compute.indiaai.gov.in).

## Open now: the dataset shelf and the application calls

AIKosh, at [aikosh.indiaai.gov.in](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home), held over 14,000 datasets and 331 models as of July 2026, according to the 13 August backgrounder. At launch in March 2025 the same shelf was described as over 300 datasets and over 80 models. The growth is the opportunity. The licence on each dataset is the constraint. The platform does not, by existing, authorise training on personal data outside the DPDP Act once that Act's processing sections are in force.

The 13 August backgrounder says 20 indigenous foundation-model proposals were selected from 506 applications, 12 of them large multimodal models and 8 small language models. That round is a selection already made, not an open call this brief can re-open. The 13 March 2026 note says 12 teams had been shortlisted in phase one, and that models from Sarvam AI, BharatGen, Gnani, and Socket were launched at the India AI Impact Summit in February 2026. A later selection of 20 is not the same sentence as the phase-one 12. Quote the date with the count.

What the March 2026 note still describes as a path, rather than a closed list, is application work: 30 applications aimed at agriculture, health, climate, and disaster, and hackathons with public agencies from which 10 startups were identified to scale. The 13 August note says 62 prototypes had been developed and 20 solutions deployed in public institutions, with 11 national hackathons and challenges in the pipeline. Those are public-problem builds. They are not a grant size this brief can state, because the notes used here do not print a rupee per prototype.

Separate from that, 10 Indian AI startups were selected for the IndiaAI Startups Global programme with Station F and HEC Paris, described on 6 March 2025 as a four-month programme in Paris and confirmed as selected in the 13 March 2026 note. That cohort is a named programme with a count of 10. It is not a standing visa.

## Not open yet: selling consent as a registered manager

The First Schedule is specific. A Consent Manager applicant must be a company incorporated in India, with net worth not less than two crore rupees, and the Board must be able to register it under Rule 4. Rule 4 starts on 13 November 2026. The business cannot subcontract its obligations. It must not be able to read the personal data it routes. Control cannot change by sale or merger without the Board's prior approval.

Building the software early is a product choice. Calling the company a registered Consent Manager before Rule 4 and before a Board that can register it is not. The [ninety-day checklist](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-next-90-days-checklist-meity-gov-20260330-470) is the date. The [jobs note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-jobs-and-skills-impact-meity-gov-20260330-390) is the training path, which is open and is not a hiring promise.

Great Indian Company is interested in the open door, which is compute and datasets a builder can use, and in not dressing a future registration as a market. The library is at [greatindiancompany.com/blog](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog).
