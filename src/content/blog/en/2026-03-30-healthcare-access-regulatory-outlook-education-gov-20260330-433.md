## Empanelment is a contract with the purchaser

A private hospital treats PM-JAY patients because a State Health Agency, or in some cases the National Health Authority, has empanelled it. The Authority’s revised empanelment and de-empanelment guideline of November 2021 says the Authority supports states with the rules and may empanel providers directly in defined situations, including where a state is not implementing the scheme. In the trust-mode description, day-to-day empanelment sits with the state agency. Insurance-mode states still sit under the Authority’s empanelment rules. The about-page does not hand that rulebook to the insurer.

The commercial terms that matter for access are on the Authority’s about-page. Care for covered packages is cashless. Public hospitals are paid at par with private hospitals. Some procedures are reserved for public facilities, and states can edit that list. Package prices are fixed nationally and may be revised by a state within a limit. De-empanelment is part of the same rulebook: access for patients at that hospital ends when the contract ends. Grievances run through the Authority’s central system. The about-page gives beneficiaries the national toll-free number 14555.

This rulebook governs the scheme. It does not, by itself, licence a hospital to operate.

## Public standards are a second book

Indian Public Health Standards apply to public facilities: district and sub-district hospitals, community health centres, primary health centres, and sub-centre level Ayushman Arogya Mandirs. The ministry’s standards portal dates the standards to 2007, with revisions in 2012 and 2022. It says the Central Council of Health and Family Welfare endorsed compliance for all public institutions by 2026, that states committed to compliance in 50 percent of facilities by 2025–26, and that compliance means a score of 80 percent or higher. Funding support is described as moving through the National Health Mission’s conditionality framework.

A district hospital can be IPHS-compliant and still a weak PM-JAY performer, or the reverse. One score measures a public facility against a ministry standard. The other measures whether an empanelled provider is inside a package contract. Private hospitals are outside IPHS. Reading a private chain’s quality claim as if it were an IPHS score mixes the books.

State clinical-establishment law is a third layer again. The Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010, is a central law that applies where states adopt it. Adoption is not universal, and this page does not publish an adoption list it has not re-checked. An operator needs the state act or the state rules, plus the empanelment letter. Scheme empanelment does not replace that state law.

## Digital identity is not hospital cover

The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, launched in September 2021, gives a person a 14-digit Ayushman Bharat Health Account. The mission’s citizen page describes that number as a way to be identified in the digital health ecosystem and to link and share records. It also maintains a health facility registry and a healthcare professionals registry. Those registries are about finding a facility and a professional. They are not the PM-JAY beneficiary identification system, which checks SECC-linked eligibility and state additions.

A product that collects an ABHA number has not established that the person has ₹5 lakh of hospital cover. PM-JAY eligibility is a separate lookup. Scan-and-share outpatient registration, which the mission describes as an October 2022 flow, moves a profile into a hospital’s registration desk. It does not approve a package.

Consent rules on record sharing matter for any company holding clinical data. They do not change the payment rule: covered PM-JAY care is billed to the scheme, under the state mode, at the package rate.

## What a serious outlook watches

Watch four documents, not a rumour of a new act. The current state empanelment criteria and reserved-package list. The IPHS score definition and the state’s own compliance report. The state clinical-establishment position. And the boundary the digital mission draws around ABHA.
