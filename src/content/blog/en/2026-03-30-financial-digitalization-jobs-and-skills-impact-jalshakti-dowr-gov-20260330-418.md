# The operating jobs India's digital credit and payments rules create

Indian financial digitalization does create work. The Reserve Bank documents do not say how many jobs, in which city, or at what wage. What they do is name duties that a firm cannot leave unstaffed if it wants the activity to be lawful. Hiring against those duties is a better plan than hiring against a viral claim about "fintech employment".

## A named officer, not a shared inbox

The [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848) require the regulated entity, and any lending service provider that faces the borrower, to designate nodal grievance officers. The contact details go on the website, on the app, and in the key fact statement. The borrower can lodge a complaint on the app. If the lender rejects the complaint, delays past 30 days, or leaves the borrower unsatisfied, the path is the Reserve Bank's Complaint Management System under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, or a physical complaint to the Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre in Chandigarh. Someone has to own that clock.

The same directions put a certification duty on the chief compliance officer, or another official the board designates. That person certifies that every digital lending app filed on the Centralised Information Management System is accurate and that the app meets the directions, including data rules and a link back to the lender's site. This is a compliance craft: inventory, version control, and a refusal to let marketing call a filing an RBI endorsement. The directions say the Reserve Bank does not verify the filing.

Recovery is a separate skill with a notice rule. When a recovery agent is assigned or changed, the borrower is told by email or SMS before the agent makes contact. Cash recovery on a delinquent loan has to hit the borrower's account the same day. Staff who still treat a phone contact list as a collection tool are working against the text. The app may not access contacts, call logs, files, or media.

## Credit judgement stays a human-accountable function

Default loss guarantee does not replace underwriting. The directions say a robust credit assessment is required regardless of the guarantee. The minimum file includes age, occupation, and income. A guarantee provider that is a lending service provider must be a company, and the arrangement needs a board-approved policy. Every renewal requires a statutory-auditor-certified declaration of the aggregate guarantee outstanding, the number of lenders, the portfolios covered, and past default rates on similar portfolios. That is work for credit, finance, and audit together. It is not a model that a growth team "sets and forgets".

Account aggregators add a different profession. Under the [2016 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10598), the firm writes and manages a consent artefact, identifies the customer, and does not keep the financial information. Staff who come from a data-brokerage background have to unlearn retention. The citizen's charter and the revocation flow are product jobs as much as legal jobs. Because the aggregator cannot use a third party to carry out the aggregation business, the technology team is in-house by rule, not only by preference.

## Payments operations

The [authentication directions of 25 September 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898) make the issuer responsible for the integrity of the two-factor check on domestic digital payments, with full compensation if a loss follows a breach of the directions. Running that control is an operations role: factor design, exemption handling, and incident response. Payment aggregators, under the [2025 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896), must name an officer for merchant issues and publish an escalation matrix, and they must run disputes and refunds inside the Reserve Bank's timelines for failed transactions.

UPI itself, as described on MeitY's [Digital India page](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/), puts grievance and fraud response at the bank and the app, because the account still sits at the bank. NPCI's [UPI statistics section](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics) is where volume, uptime, and incident disclosures sit, alongside the corporation's fraud-awareness material for users. The skill on the firm side is reconciliation and customer support at real-time speed, not a new category of "UPI jobs" with an official headcount.

No document cited here reports a national employment total for these roles. A workforce plan that quotes one is using a source this note does not have. The practical plan is a roster: nodal officer, compliance certifier, credit underwriter, consent operator, merchant-dispute lead, and an issuer-side authentication owner.
