# Which Official Page Holds the Pharma Number You Want to Quote

Indian pharma numbers contradict each other when they are lifted from different years and treated as one table. They sit cleanly when each figure keeps the document that printed it. This page is that index. It does not add a figure the documents omit.

## Cabinet design, 21 March 2020

The Prime Minister’s Office record of the cabinet decision is the source for the original architecture. Three bulk-drug parks. ₹3,000 crore. A maximum of ₹1,000 crore per park. Common facilities named at that time: solvent recovery, distillation, power and steam, common effluent treatment. A bulk-drug incentive of ₹6,940 crore over eight years. Fifty-three identified products, 26 fermentation and 27 chemical synthesis. Incentive rates of 20 percent and 10 percent on incremental sales over 2019–20 for six years. An expectation of ₹46,400 crore of incremental sales and significant additional employment. The third-largest-by-volume description. Import dependence of 80 to 100 percent in some specific bulk drugs.

Quote this page for what was approved in March 2020. Do not quote it for how many projects are approved now, or for the acreage of a park.

## Parliamentary status, 6 February 2026

The Press Information Bureau text of the Lok Sabha reply is the source for the current park sheet and the later PLI counts. Tenure FY 2020–21 to FY 2026–27. Assistance equal to the lower of ₹1,000 crore or 70 percent of common-infrastructure cost, 90 percent for Himachal Pradesh and the other hilly and North-Eastern jurisdictions named there. Grant-in-aid of ₹1,000 crore to each of three parks. Gujarat: Jambusar, Bharuch, 2,015.02 acres, Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation, total project cost ₹2,507.02 crore, common infrastructure ₹1,457.01 crore. Himachal Pradesh: Haroli, Una, 1,405.01 acres, Himachal Pradesh Bulk Drug Park Infrastructure Ltd., ₹1,923 crore and ₹1,118.46 crore. Andhra Pradesh: Nakkapalli, Anakapalli, 2,001.80 acres, Andhra Pradesh Bulk Drug Infrastructure Corporation, ₹1,876.66 crore and ₹1,438.89 crore. Construction verbs for each site. Bulk-drug PLI: 41 identified products, 48 projects, 33 bulk drugs, greenfield. Pharmaceuticals PLI: ₹15,000 crore, 55 applicants, greenfield and brownfield, product boundary against the bulk-drug list.

The 1 September 2022 PIB note is the source for the earlier site sketch, for FY 2021–22 exports of ₹1,75,040 crore of pharmaceuticals and ₹33,320 crore of bulk drugs and intermediates, and for the then-stated 51 approved bulk-drug projects of which 14 had been commissioned. When a 2022 number and a 2026 number differ, report both with their dates.

## Department index for names and versions

The Department of Pharmaceuticals schemes page is the source for document titles and dates: gazettes, guidelines, corrigenda, and approved-applicant lists. Use it to show that a bulk-drug applicant list dated 6 November 2023 exists, that a pharmaceuticals applicant list dated 3 May 2023 exists, and that a bulk-drug corrigendum dated 4 May 2026 is posted. The page is the pointer. The PDF is the text. This brief does not summarise a corrigendum it has not quoted line by line.

The pharmaceuticals PLI sub-page holds the 3 March 2021 gazette. That gazette is the source for Group A, B, and C revenue bands and for the ₹11,000 crore, ₹2,250 crore, and ₹1,750 crore pools. The Press Information Bureau announcement is the source for SIDBI as project management agency and for the application window of 2 June 2021 to 31 July 2021.

## Regulator and biotechnology pages

CDSCO’s site is the source for the organisation’s mandate and for the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019. The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare gazette G.S.R. 922(E) of 28 December 2023 is the source for the substitution of Schedule M. The Department of Biotechnology’s National Biotechnology Parks Scheme note is the source for the seven park and incubator names it lists. The Prime Minister’s Office note of 24 August 2024 is the source for BioE3’s sectors. BIRAC’s incubator map is the source for incubator locations, as a list that can change when BIRAC updates the page.

## Pages that do not hold these numbers

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy does not publish bulk-drug project costs, CDSCO permissions, or PLI applicant lists. A link to that ministry in a pharma note is a routing error. Private outlook pages are not a substitute for the gazette date.

If the task is to see what moved between the cabinet text and the 2026 reply, use [what changed by February 2026](/blog/pharma-and-biotech-what-changed-this-quarter-mnre-gov-20260330-114). Great Indian Company keeps the citation next to the number so the next reader can open the same page: [greatindiancompany.com](https://greatindiancompany.com).
