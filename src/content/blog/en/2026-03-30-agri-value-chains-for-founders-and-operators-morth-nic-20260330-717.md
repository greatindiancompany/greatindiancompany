# How a founder uses the official agri instruments

The instruments are specific, capped, and sequential. A founder who picks the cap that matches the asset will get further than one who quotes the scheme’s full outlay.

For a producer company under the 10,000 FPO scheme, the Ministry’s 3 February 2026 reply sets three numbers. Management support is up to ₹18 lakh per FPO for three years. The matching equity grant is up to ₹2,000 per farmer member, with a ceiling of ₹15 lakh per FPO. The credit guarantee covers a project loan of up to ₹2 crore from an eligible lender. The scheme outlay is ₹6,865 crore, launched in 2020. As on 31 December 2025 the equity grant had actually been paid to 6,557 FPOs (₹430.77 crore) and the guarantee had been issued to 2,671 (₹662.71 crore), out of 10,000 registered. File the equity and the guarantee as separate applications. Registration does not disburse either one.

For a mandi lot, e-NAM’s published path is operational, not promotional. The Ministry’s description of the portal runs from digital gate entry, to a lot with a unique identity, to quality assaying, to online bidding and bid tracking, to price discovery, to an electronic credit in the farmer’s bank account. The Centre will fund up to ₹75 lakh per mandi for the lab, the weighbridge, computers, grading, and packaging that make those steps possible. The mandi has to be proposed by the state. If it is not among the 1,656 integrated as on 30 June 2026, the portal is not your auction. In Rajasthan, 134 mandis were reported on AI-based assaying machines. Elsewhere, ask whether the lab in the ₹75 lakh design is actually switching samples.

For a micro processing unit, PMFME is a 35 percent credit-linked capital subsidy capped at ₹10 lakh per unit. The eligible list includes an individual, a proprietorship, a partnership, an FPO, an NGO, a cooperative, a self-help group, and a private limited company. Shared plant for an FPO, a self-help group, a cooperative, or a government agency can take the same 35 percent up to ₹3 crore, and that common facility is supposed to be hireable by others for a substantial part of its capacity. As on 30 June 2026 the Ministry had approved 2,00,421 micro enterprises. The cap, not that national count, is what a single unit can claim.

For a cold store or a larger processing line, the cold-chain component of PMKSY pays 35 percent of eligible cost in general areas and 50 percent in difficult areas, including the North-East, and for SC/ST, FPO, and self-help group projects. The cap is ₹10 crore. Sanctioned grants on 409 projects stood at ₹3,089.08 crore as on 30 June 2026, with ₹2,540.89 crore released and 98 projects still ongoing. A term sheet should distinguish a sanction from a release.

Price still has to match the crop and the season. Common paddy in Kharif Marketing Season 2026-27 is ₹2,441 a quintal. Wheat in Rabi Marketing Season 2026-27 is ₹2,585. Pulse and oilseed sales to NAFED or NCCF happen when the state has asked and the market is below MSP, through e-Samriddhi or e-Samyukti. Cotton uses Kapas Kisan. An FPO that owns a micro unit, sells on e-NAM, and also hopes for an MSP centre is using three different rulebooks in one week. That is normal. Blending their caps into one grant application is not.
