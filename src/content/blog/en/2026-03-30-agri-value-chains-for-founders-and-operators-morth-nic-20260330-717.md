# A founder's map of the agri chain

A founder in this chain is usually trying to do one of four things: collect crop from many small farms, sell it through a mandi or past the mandi, hold it in a store, or change its form and send it further, sometimes abroad. Each job already has an official instrument. The map is which instrument, which ceiling, and which published stock tells you the instrument is real.

## If you aggregate, start from a farmer company that already exists

The central scheme launched in 2020, with an outlay of Rs 6,865 crore, had registered 10,000 farmer producer organisations as on 1 March 2026. The useful design for an operator is the support attached to one of them, not the headline count. Management assistance is up to Rs 18 lakh over three years. Matching equity is up to Rs 2,000 a member and Rs 15 lakh an organisation. A project loan can be guaranteed up to Rs 2 crore. As on 31 December 2025 the equity grant had reached 6,557 organisations (Rs 430.77 crore) and the guarantee 2,671 (Rs 662.71 crore). A founder who becomes the professional team inside an FPO is paid from that management line. A founder who raises venture equity against the FPO's matching grant is using a public ceiling of Rs 15 lakh as if it were a round. It is not.

As on 1 March 2026, 1,175 of the organisations had only women members, and 23.55 lakh women farmers were registered. That is a defined set a founder can look up, not a segment size to estimate.

## If you sell, learn which screen the lot is allowed on

The National Agriculture Market has run since 2016. On 17 March 2026 the ministry said 1,656 mandis in 23 states and 4 Union territories were integrated. As on 28 February 2026 the registers held 1.80 crore farmers, 2.72 lakh traders, and 4,724 FPOs. Cumulative trade through February was 13.22 crore metric tonnes and Rs 4,82,350 crore. Quote the cumulative as a cumulative. It is not last year's turnover, and it is not your addressable market.

Two operating details matter more than the total. Sale proceeds are to be credited to the farmer's bank account. A State Unified Licence, described in the 13 April 2026 account, is what lets a trader bid across a state line. Without it, the national portal still ends at the state. The same account describes the electronic negotiable warehouse receipt, under the Warehousing Act of 2007, for stock in a WDRA-accredited warehouse, which can be sold on e-NAM and used as collateral. A collection centre that is not an accredited warehouse does not get that receipt because the founder calls it storage.

The Centre will fund the mandi's own kit, up to Rs 75 lakh, for assaying, weighing, grading, and packaging. That applicant is the state. Rajasthan had 134 mandis using AI and machine-learning assaying. A founder selling into a mandi that lacks an assay lab is still selling into a local auction.

## If you build, match the asset to the right cheque

The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund is a loan. The August 2025 note sets a 9 per cent interest cap, a 3 per cent subvention for up to seven years on the first Rs 2 crore, and a guarantee through CGTMSE and NABSanrakshan on that band. After 28 August 2024 the loan covers community farming assets and primary processing only when secondary processing is in the same unit. A standalone second-stage plant is outside. As on 26 January 2026, Rs 80,224.15 crore had been sanctioned for 1,50,431 projects. As on 15 July 2026 a later reply reported Rs 1,61,537.22 crore of capital investment leveraged, including Rs 50,332.66 crore for farm-gate storage and logistics. The August 2025 note said new disbursements were to finish at the end of 2025-26. Confirm a fresh sanction before you model the subvention.

A cold chain that is integrated, not a lone cold store, can instead seek a grant from the food-processing ministry: 35 per cent in general areas, 50 per cent in difficult areas and for FPOs and self-help groups, capped at Rs 10 crore on the 6 August 2026 rate card. As of 30 June 2026, 311 of 409 approved projects were complete. The production-linked incentive, window to 2026-27, had 168 approved applicants and about Rs 9,207 crore of their investment by 31 December 2025, with Rs 2,714.79 crore of incentive paid. It requires the whole manufacturing chain, including primary processing, to be in India.

## If you export, use the scheme that names the gap

APEDA's 2024-25 report puts India's agri exports at USD 51,913 million and, in the summary table, APEDA scheduled products at 55 per cent of that. The tender of 1 September 2026 for Bharat Krishi Niryat Yojana, covering 2026-27 to 2030-31, lists the gaps it will pay toward: packhouses, pre-cooling, refrigerated transport, testing, and market development. FPOs are named stakeholders. The bid date of 15 September 2026 was for the project-management unit, not the last day an exporter can seek assistance.

Under all of this, the crop the chain is clearing is the 2025-26 estimate released on 27 May: 376.563 million tonnes of foodgrain. The crop now in the ground is still an area figure, 1,086.31 lakh hectares of kharif as on 4 September 2026. A founder who puts those two numbers in one sentence has mixed two seasons.
