# How founders should operate an agri value chain in India

Founders get paid for a handoff on a crop, not for the sector. The operating job is to know which lots you take, what the buyer will still accept, where quality is lost, and when cash returns.

## Map the chain you are actually in

Write one page before you add a district or a machine.

- **Crop and grade.** The name the buyer uses, including what fails the grade.
- **Catchment.** The villages or yards that can reach you inside the time the crop allows. A district on a map is not a catchment if the lot spoils on the way.
- **First payment.** Who you pay, on what day relative to pickup, and in what instrument.
- **Your step.** Aggregate, grade, cool, mill, pack, or move. One primary step. A second step needs its own cost line.
- **Buyer.** The name on the purchase order, the specification, and the payment days from your bank, not from the term sheet.
- **Loss.** A measured shrink or reject share from your last season. If you have no season yet, the cell stays empty until you do.

State marketing practice and the buyer's specification both apply. Read them as operating steps, using the [regulatory stack](/blog/agri-value-chains-regulatory-outlook-morth-nic-20260330-437), not as background.

## A weekly cadence

Once a week, on the same day, review five numbers: volume in, volume shipped, reject share, longest wait, and cash collected. The definitions live on the [signal brief](/blog/agri-value-chains-market-signal-brief-morth-nic-20260330-197). If the longest wait repeats, treat that stage as the constraint and stop improving other stages until it moves. The test is the [bottleneck review](/blog/agri-value-chains-execution-bottleneck-review-morth-nic-20260330-597).

Hire against that stage. A grader or a cold-room technician is a throughput decision. The role map is on the [jobs page](/blog/agri-value-chains-jobs-and-skills-impact-morth-nic-20260330-397).

Once a quarter, run the [90-day checklist](/blog/agri-value-chains-next-90-days-checklist-morth-nic-20260330-477) and retire one practice that did not change shipments or cash.

## Decisions that stay small on purpose

Do not add a plant because the crop is large somewhere in India. Do not add a state because the slide has a blank. Do not describe a policy speech as a change in your license. Scale a step you have already run through a full season, including the weeks when volume is thin.

## Scope

This is an operating guide. It reports no company's results and recommends no vendor.
