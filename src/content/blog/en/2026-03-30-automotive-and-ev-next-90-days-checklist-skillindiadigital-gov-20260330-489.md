# A Ninety-Day Reading List for PM E-DRIVE and PLI Auto

The next ninety days from 24 September 2026 run to about 23 December 2026. Nothing in the gazettes makes 23 December a scheme deadline. The work in this window is to stop using superseded dates, and to read three documents that already exist before the next claim cycle.

## Replace the July dates with the August gazette

The [28 July 2026 parliamentary reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2290696) is still the latest disbursement table: ₹2,281.94 crore reimbursed for 23,22,878 vehicles as on 22 July 2026, and a two-wheeler terminal date, as that reply stated it, of 31 July 2026. Thirteen days later the ministry published [S.O. 4424(E)](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/Gazette%20Notification%2010%2008%202026%20on%20enhancement%20of%20Outlay%2011900%20cr.pdf).

Check these lines in the gazette before any model, term sheet, or board note that still says ₹10,900 crore or 31 July 2026:

- Scheme outlay is ₹11,900 crore, from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2028.
- Registered two-wheelers: up to 45,79,120 vehicles and ₹2,767 crore.
- Rate from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028: ₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹5,000 per vehicle and at 15 percent of ex-factory price.
- The scheme remains fund-limited. A sub-component closes if its money is exhausted early.
- The last date to submit any claim to the ministry or the project management agency is 31 December 2027. That date is outside this ninety-day window. It is the date to put on the claim calendar now.
- L5 electric three-wheelers stay closed from 26 December 2025.

The [portal banner](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/) says the two-wheeler terminal date is extended to 31 March 2028 and points at the 10 August 2026 amendment. An older "About" block on the same page still describes a two-year scheme. If the two texts on the homepage disagree, follow the gazette.

## Close the items that have already closed

The portal says the last date to submit L5 claims was 25 April 2026, for L5 vehicles sold or registered on 26 December 2025, and that no claims would be accepted after that deadline. That date has passed. A ninety-day plan that still includes an L5 claim is late on the ministry's own banner.

The July reply's two-wheeler count of 20,56,831 reimbursed vehicles, as on 22 July 2026, sits under the old cap. The new cap is 45,79,120. Do not subtract the July count from the new cap and call the remainder a forecast. The ministry has not published a post-amendment reimbursement table.

## Read the September localisation notices

On 3 September 2026 the [notifications page](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/policy_document) listed a fourth phased-manufacturing amendment for N2 and N3 electric trucks and a fourth for M2 and M3 electric buses. Open both PDFs before placing a purchase order or a localisation plan that uses the March or April 2026 versions. This page does not summarise amendments it has not quoted.

For chargers, confirm compliance with the Ministry of Power's 17 September 2024 guidelines and with S.O. 3318(E) on charger localisation. As on 1 July 2026, ₹689 crore had been approved for 6,562 chargers to three oil-marketing companies and nine states. The reply does not name the nine. If a state government announces a sanction, match it to a later ministry list before treating the July figure as updated.

For buses, the July reply had quantity letters for 13,800 of 14,000 allocated buses, and signed concessions for 600 in Surat and 915 in Hyderabad. The next observable step in that reply is prototype approval and deployment. Ask for that document, not for a fresh policy announcement.

## Keep PLI and the demand cycle on separate checklists

PLI Auto's latest published performance in the 13 January 2026 review is determined sales of ₹32,879 crore till 30 September 2025 and disbursement of ₹1,999.94 crore for the FY 2024-25 performance year. A DVA certificate at 50 percent is the gate. Ninety days will not close the five-year scheme. They are enough time to list which variants have the certificate and which sales are only "approved applicant" sales.

SIAM's [14 April 2026 outlook](https://www.siam.in/pressrelease-details.aspx?pid=605) for FY 2026-27 expects growth to continue and names the West Asia conflict, crude, commodities, the exchange rate, and shipping as the risks it is watching. That is an industry caution sitting next to the scheme calendar. It is not a trigger in the gazette.
