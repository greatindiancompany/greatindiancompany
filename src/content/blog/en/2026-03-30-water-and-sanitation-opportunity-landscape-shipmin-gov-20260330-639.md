# The operating gaps still open in taps, village waste, and sewage

An opportunity, in this record, is a task the government has already defined and not yet closed. It is not a market size. No revenue pool is estimated below. A founder who needs a rupee figure for a pitch will not find one here, because the ministries have not published one for private sales.

## Keep a rural scheme running after the pipe is in

JJM 2.0 requires the state to establish in-village operation and maintenance before a Gram Panchayat declares Har Ghar Jal. The handover is Jal Arpan, to the panchayat and the Village Water and Sanitation Committee. The annual community review is Jal Utsav. The assessment is Jal Seva Aankalan. Water-quality surveillance is named in the same reform.

That is a service job sitting on top of a connection job that, as on 4 August 2026, already covered more than 15.90 crore of 19.36 crore rural households on a reported basis. The residual unconnected households are still a works job. The much wider job, if certification follows the new rule, is making maintenance real in villages that already have a tap on the register. Sujalam Bharat, the source-to-tap identity, is the information layer that job needs. Someone has to create and keep those identities. The Cabinet note says the government will institute the framework. It does not tender it on that page.

## Finish the waste stream a village still lacks

On 11 June 2026 the rural sanitation dashboard showed 57,923 Aspiring villages, which have arranged either solid or liquid waste, and 3,463 Rising villages, which have arranged both and are not yet Model. Model already accounted for 5,07,634 villages. The open operating work is concentrated: the missing waste stream, then visual cleanliness, then the third-party verification the rules require within 90 days of a first Model declaration.

A PIB feature of 17 September 2026 adds two concrete plant counts, not a market: 1,314 GOBARdhan biogas plants operational in 582 districts, and 98,273 household biogas plants constructed. Plants that exist need feedstock, uptime, and slurry use. The feature records that they exist. It does not record a private concession around them.

## Complete sewage that is already approved

Two public balances describe urban and river sewage work that is in the book and not finished.

Under AMRUT 2.0, 605 sewerage and septage projects were approved at ₹71,133.01 crore, inside a wider approved list of 8,777 projects. As on 9 July 2026, physically completed works across the mission stood at ₹66,472 crore, against awarded capex of ₹1,37,130.13 crore. The unfinished awarded work is an execution backlog with a state and a city attached, visible on the ministry's project pages.

Under Namami Gange, till June 2026, 4,263 million litres a day of treatment capacity had been completed out of 6,610 million litres a day taken up. The Department of Water Resources is the home department ([jalshakti-dowr.gov.in](https://jalshakti-dowr.gov.in/)). The gap of sanctioned-but-not-completed capacity is a defined construction and commissioning task in the Ganga basin. A 21 April 2026 note said some projects in FY 2025-26 were being done under the hybrid annuity model. That is a contracting form already in use on part of the book, not a template this brief extends to every city.

## What a serious operator does with this

Pick one gap, name the buyer the rule actually names, and use the as-on date of the official page. For a village scheme the buyer after Jal Arpan is the panchayat and the committee, with the state still responsible for having built the maintenance arrangement. For an Aspiring village the buyer is the state sanitation system and the Gram Sabha that must declare. For an AMRUT sewage project the buyer is the state and the urban local body inside an approved plan. Inventing a fourth buyer, a national water utility that places one order, does not match these programmes.

The definitions are in the [Cabinet note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2237548), the [SBM-G dashboard](https://sbm.gov.in/sbmgdashboard/), the [17 September 2026 feature](https://www.pib.gov.in/FeaturesDeatils.aspx?id=160045&NoteId=160045&ModuleId=2&reg=48&lang=2), and the [AMRUT answer](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU3022_tT0QXq.pdf).
