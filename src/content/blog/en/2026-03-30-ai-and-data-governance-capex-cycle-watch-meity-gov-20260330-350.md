## What this watch covers

Capital spending tied to AI and data governance in India shows up when a public page talks about compute, data infrastructure, standards work, or government systems that need budget. This brief tells you what to mark on the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology site, [meity.gov.in](https://www.meity.gov.in/). Record a number only when the page prints the number. This page supplies no spending total of its own.

## Lines to mark on a MeitY page

Keep a capex note when the page includes one of these:

- A budget, outlay, or scheme size printed in figures.
- A facility type: data center, compute, cloud, network, or a shared digital system.
- A buyer: a ministry, a state, a public enterprise, or a program office.
- A phase or year the page attaches to that spending.
- A governance condition tied to the spend, such as a standard, an audit, or a data rule.

Copy the figure and the unit as printed. If the page describes a program without a figure, write "no figure on page" and keep the qualitative line. That is still a watch item.

## Deutsche Bank insights

The second source is the [Deutsche Bank insights](https://www.deutsche-bank.com/insights) index. Use an individual insight only after you open it and it discusses capital spending, technology, or India. Put any figure from that insight in a commentary column with that page's title. Do not merge it into the MeitY column, and do not treat the index URL as a capex series.

## Cycle check, without a forecast

A cycle watch compares your last note with the current pages:

- New figures, or figures the page has revised.
- Projects that moved from announcement to a named phase, if the page says so.
- Governance conditions that now sit on top of a spend.

Stop there. The next quarter's spending is outside this source set until a page states it.
