# A 90-day MSME productivity checklist from official tools

Ninety days is long enough to repair the firm's place in the official system and short enough to avoid a fantasy transformation programme. Every item below is something a micro, small, or medium enterprise can check against a Ministry, SIDBI, Reserve Bank, or MoSPI page.

## Days 1 to 30: identity and the ceiling

Confirm the Udyam Registration Certificate, or the Udyam Assist Certificate if the firm is an informal micro enterprise. Read the investment and turnover figures on it against the ceilings in force since 1 April 2025: ₹2.5 crore and ₹10 crore for micro, ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore for small, ₹125 crore and ₹500 crore for medium. If a machine on order would cross the investment ceiling, write down the category the firm will enter. Upward moves continue non-tax benefits of the old category for three years, according to the Press Information Bureau note of 14 May 2026. Tax treatment is a separate question for the firm's tax adviser.

If the firm supplies buyers and is not yet on Udyam, register before the next invoice. Samadhaan requires the enterprise to have been registered before the date of the disputed invoice.

## Days 31 to 60: credit and cash

Ask the lending bank two questions in writing. Is the facility eligible for CGTMSE, which covers micro and small enterprises and, for guarantees approved on or after 1 April 2025, credit support up to ₹10 crore? Which category does the bank have on file for priority sector, given that the Reserve Bank tells banks to follow the Udyam Registration Certificate or the Udyam Assist Certificate?

List buyers that are central public sector enterprises. The 14 May 2026 PIB note says Budget 2026-27 mandates the Trade Receivables Discounting System for those enterprises. For other buyers, ask whether they are onboarded on a TReDS platform. Factoring on TReDS is eligible for priority-sector classification. A guarantee and an undiscounted invoice solve different problems.

Pull every invoice older than the written credit period, and any invoice with no written period that is older than fifteen days from acceptance. Section 15 of the MSMED Act caps a written period at 45 days for micro and small suppliers. If a real dispute exists, the Samadhaan route is a filing to the state facilitation council, not a social-media complaint.

## Days 61 to 90: the shop floor you can actually enter

If the firm manufactures and holds Udyam registration, open the ZED portal and decide between the pledge, a Bronze certification, or a later Silver or Gold audit. The Development Commissioner publishes certification costs of ₹10,000, ₹40,000, and ₹90,000, with subsidies of 80, 60, and 50 percent for micro, small, and medium. Lean certification is the parallel process track. The Ministry's 26 June 2026 note, as on May 2026, shows how wide the funnel is: more than 93.61 lakh ZED registrations and more than 6.68 lakh certifications; more than 65,647 Lean registrations and nearly 18,961 certifications. Enter the track whose output you can finish.

If the firm sits in a cluster, ask the implementing agency whether a common facility under the Micro and Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme is approved or already completed. As on June 2026 the Ministry counted 612 approved projects and 364 completed. A nearby completed centre is usable capacity. An unapproved idea is not.

Read one ASUSE table before setting an internal productivity target. The national current-price rise in gross value added per worker between ASUSE 2023-24 and ASUSE 2025 was 4.54 percent, to ₹1,56,539. That is a benchmark for the unincorporated sector, not a target for one factory.
