# How Policy Teams Should Read India’s Social Outcome Releases

A policy brief fails when it puts a beneficiary count and a body measurement in the same sentence and calls both an outcome. The ministries already separate them. The job of a policy team is to keep that separation, name the level of government that can act, and ask for the next document that would change the conclusion.

## Put output and outcome on different lines

Output, from administrative systems: almost 8.94 crore Poshan Tracker beneficiaries as of 31 July 2026; growth monitoring for over 6.3 crore children aged 0-5 as of May 2026; more than 9.69 crore rural person-days in roughly the first month of the new wage Act, as told to the Lok Sabha on 11 August 2026; 1,02,73,020 school teachers in UDISE+ 2025-26.

Outcome, from surveys and participation ratios: stunting 29.3%, wasting 19.0%, underweight 31.8%, exclusive breastfeeding 55.8%, adequate diet at 6-23 months 15.3%, institutional births 90.6%, full vaccination on cards 87.1%, secondary retention 51.9%, labour force participation 55.6% in August 2026 on the current weekly status. A sentence that says “8.94 crore beneficiaries show nutrition has improved” uses the output to impersonate the outcome.

## Say whether a change is in points or relative

The 29 May 2026 headline says stunting fell by 17% and severe wasting by 32%. The levels are 35.5% to 29.3%, and 7.7% to 5.2%. Brief the levels. If you repeat the headline, say it is relative to the NFHS-5 base. The same discipline applies to “any antenatal care is 95.9%” next to “180 days of iron and folic acid is 37.8%.” Both can be true. Only one is a completed course.

## Name who implements

Mission Poshan 2.0 leaves policy and planning with the Centre and day-to-day implementation with the states. Immunisation, health check-up, and referral inside the Anganwadi package are delivered through the National Health Mission. A review that summons only the Department of Women and Child Development will not cover the health third of the package. The National Social Assistance Programme is the mirror case on pensions: the Centre last revised its assistance in 2012, said on 21 July 2026 that no revision is under consideration, and recorded state top-ups from ₹50 to ₹5,700. A brief that quotes one national pension is describing a rate the states have already varied.

The rural wage guarantee is now the VB-G RAM G Act from 1 July 2026. States notify the scheme, may notify up to sixty days of agricultural pause, and owe unemployment allowance and delay compensation when the Act’s conditions are met. Questions for a state review are factual: Has the pause been notified, and for which dates? Are old job cards still being honoured where e-KYC is done? Were delay-compensation cases paid? Person-days alone do not answer those.

## Use the district sheet, and respect its boundary

India and state NFHS-6 fact sheets came out on 29 May 2026. District compendiums came out on 20 and 21 August 2026. Manipur is not in the compendium. IIPS points readers to its publication section and a guest login. A state average that hides a rural-urban gap — Gujarat 39.5% rural stunting against 28.2% urban — is a weak unit for a district plan. Parentheses in a fact sheet mark a thin sample. Do not brief those cells as if they were stable.

Do not brief an NFHS-6 anaemia number. This round’s press note and nutrition lines do not publish one. Do not brief a learning outcome from UDISE+. Do brief the participation exception the school release itself contains: preparatory and secondary dropout fell, and the middle series in the same table moved from 3.5% to 3.6%.

## A short review agenda

Ask each state for four artefacts it already owes: the Anganwadi Management Committee meeting from Poshan Maah, the displayed meal menu, the maternity-benefit pendency on the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana dashboard, and the notified wage-pause calendar if any. Pair those with the NFHS-6 state sheet for stunting, wasting, and underweight, read as three indicators. That is a review. A single national percentage is a preface.
