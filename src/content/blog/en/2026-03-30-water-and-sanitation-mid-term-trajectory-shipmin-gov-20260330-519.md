# Three clocks run on India's water and sanitation missions

A mid-term view of Indian water and sanitation is three programmes with three end dates, plus one longer service vision that is easy to paste onto the wrong date. This brief keeps them apart. It does not forecast how many villages will be certified in a future year.

## Clock one: rural taps, December 2028, and a 2047 service line

JJM 2.0, approved on 10 March 2026, runs to December 2028. The Cabinet note says it is to support certification of all Gram Panchayats as Har Ghar Jal and a tap connection for all 19.36 crore rural households, through memoranda with the states and Union Territories. The same paragraph shifts the method from building infrastructure to citizen-facing service: maintenance, source sustainability, and a digital map from source to tap.

The 24×7 sentence is a different clock. The note says the mission fosters the vision of Viksit Bharat @2047 with assured round-the-clock rural drinking water, by making that structural shift. A plan that promises 24×7 service by December 2028 is tighter than the text. A plan that treats December 2028 as optional because 2047 exists is looser than the text. Certification and universal connection are the 2028 objects. Continuous supply is the 2047 object named beside them.

The stock already reported, more than 15.90 crore households on 4 August 2026, means the remaining connection job is the minority of the 19.36 crore. The certification job, which now includes proof of maintenance, can still be large after the connection percentage looks high. The trajectory is increasingly a functionality trajectory.

## Clock two: rural sanitation through 2026-27

Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) Phase II is, in the ministry's 12 February 2026 statement, being implemented from 2020-21 to 2026-27. The original [operational guidelines](https://swachhbharatmission.ddws.gov.in/sites/default/files/Guidelines/SBMG%20Phase-II%20Operational%20Guidelines.pdf) had described a mission mode from 2020-21 to 2024-25, with an estimated outlay of ₹1,40,881 crore. The February 2026 statement is the later official window. This brief does not assume the rupee outlay was rewritten just because the year span was.

The work inside that window is the climb from open-defecation-free to ODF Plus Model: both waste streams, and visual cleanliness. As on 11 June 2026, 57,923 villages were still Aspiring, against 5,07,634 Model. A 17 September 2026 feature put Model status at 5.25 lakh villages. The mid-term question is whether the Aspiring remainder is closed while the mission window is still the one the ministry has stated, through 2026-27.

## Clock three: urban projects, and a one-year extension on the record

The 2021 Cabinet approval for AMRUT 2.0, published by the Prime Minister's Office, ran the mission from FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26. That original horizon has since been addressed in writing. Minutes of the 13th Apex Committee, issued as a Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs office memorandum dated 17 March 2026, record that the Department of Expenditure granted an extension for FY 2026-27 only, till 31 March 2027, within the existing approved financial outlay, and only to complete projects already considered or taken up. Any further extension, those minutes say, depends on spending at least half of the central share, about ₹38,400 crore, by that date.

The copy of the memorandum used here is the file circulated through the Kerala AMRUT site. It is a ministry document. A reader who wants the ministry's own hosting should still treat the date and the condition as the substance: completion of the existing book by 31 March 2027, inside the outlay already approved, with a spending test attached to anything beyond that.

## The basin programme does not share these dates

Namami Gange's sewerage book, under the Department of Water Resources ([jalshakti-dowr.gov.in](https://jalshakti-dowr.gov.in/)), had 4,263 million litres a day completed till June 2026, out of 6,610 million litres a day taken up. That programme's clock is the commissioning of the remaining plants, not the JJM certification date and not the AMRUT financial year.

Hold December 2028, 2026-27, 31 March 2027, and 2047 as four different sentences.
