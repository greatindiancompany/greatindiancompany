# What the Green Hydrogen Mission Asks of States, Ports, and the Centre

A policy team implementing green hydrogen in India is not asked to do one thing. The mission page splits the work. Later documents show which piece has actually moved.

## The Centre’s split

The [MNRE mission page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/national-green-hydrogen-mission/) gives an Empowered Group, chaired by the Cabinet Secretary, the job of guiding the Mission. It also gives that group the year-wise minimum share of green hydrogen or derivatives for designated consumers. Until a trajectory from that group is the document a state cites, a state renewable policy cannot honestly say the Centre has already imposed a quota. Competitive bidding and export facilitation are the other demand tools on the page. An Advisory Group chaired by the Principal Scientific Adviser is there for science and technology, not for offtake volumes.

MNRE’s secretariat runs the schemes. SIGHT incentives, pilot sanctions, the certification scheme, and the electrolyser service agreement of 24 July 2026 are secretariat products. They are listed, as guidelines, on the [schemes page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/hydrogen-schemes-guidelines/). The Bureau of Energy Efficiency, in the Ministry of Power, accredits the agencies that verify the 2 kg standard notified on 19 August 2023. A state energy department that starts a parallel “green” label will confuse the only threshold the Centre has notified. The useful state role next to that rule is land, the permissions the state actually holds, follow-through on open access, and industrial policy that points at the central certificate.

## Ports implement hubs under a rule that withholds direct money

Revised guidelines for Hydrogen Valley Innovation Clusters and Green Hydrogen Hubs are dated 27 June 2025. The Press Information Bureau’s release of 10 October 2025 says Component B2 lets MNRE recognise a location without direct financial assistance, so projects can seek other central or state benefits. Deendayal, V.O. Chidambaranar, and Paradip were recognised on that basis. The 5 August 2026 Lok Sabha answer adds MNRE’s endorsement of NTPC’s proposal at Pudimadaka, and it is the annex that carries jetty costs, leases, and expected capacities. A state that wants another hub should read Component B2 before it budgets as if the ₹400 crore hub line on the 2023 mission page were an entitlement of every coastline. Project approvals, such as Paradip’s jetty for public-private partnership, are separate decisions. They are not automatic consequences of the word “hub.”

## States were asked to move, and the count omitted the names

On 17 June 2026, Minister Pralhad Joshi said six states had notified dedicated green hydrogen policies, seven had integrated hydrogen into existing industrial or renewable frameworks, and four were still finalising. He asked states to participate. The release does not name the seventeen. A policy team that is unsure which bucket it is in should match its own gazette to that count and correct the record with MNRE, rather than rely on a private list. The workshop that day also launched the certification portal. A state that subsidises a plant should assume the November 2025 rule: a final certificate is mandatory when central or state incentives are taken, or when the hydrogen is sold or used in India. State money triggers the central label. It does not create a local substitute.

## Skills and jobs are a coordination duty

The mission page names the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship as the partner for the skill programme. MNRE issued skill guidelines on 16 March 2024 and a call for proposals on 23 March 2026. A state skill mission can answer that call. The “over 6 lakh jobs” line, repeated by the minister in June 2026, is an aim attached to the 2030 production story. It is not, in that speech, a placement guarantee a state can print on a scheme poster.

Great Indian Company offers this split so a state note does not promise a quota, a hub grant, and a jobs total that the Centre’s own pages have not issued as one package.
