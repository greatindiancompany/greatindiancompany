# What Counts as Evidence on Climate Adaptation in India

Use a document for the claim it actually makes. Adaptation pages go wrong when a true sentence is asked to prove a different sentence.

## Match the claim to the page

The mission list has two dates. The environment ministry's FAQ of 1 December 2021 describes eight missions under the plan released on 30 June 2008. A Press Information Bureau release of 21 July 2025 describes nine, including human health, revised toward the Nationally Determined Contribution. A solar-capacity statistic cannot update that list. The same July 2025 reply is also the page that says 34 States and Union Territories have prepared state plans. The adaptation-fund project count cannot substitute for it.

The fund has its own page. On 5 August 2024, PIB reported 30 projects in 27 States and Union Territories, a total sanctioned project cost of ₹847.48 crore, installment releases to NABARD, and a change to non-scheme status in November 2022. The GDP spending share cannot substitute for that stock. The spending share has its own page too: PIB on 29 January 2026, reporting the Economic Survey, gives 3.7 percent of GDP in FY16 and 5.6 percent in FY22 for adaptation and resilience-related domestic spending. The ₹847.48 crore figure cannot substitute for that share.

Heat has a test, printed on IMD bulletins and in the 6 April 2021 annexure. A city note that says "heatwave" without the test is not the test. The coast rests on the 3 February 2022 PIB note and the Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019. A mitigation paragraph in a national contribution does not demarcate a hazard line. The pace of one project rests on the Andhra Pradesh dairy amounts in the 5 August 2024 reply. Those amounts are not a national delay rate.

## Figures this set will not print

A national jobs total. A private market size. A ranking of states or of India against other countries on adaptation readiness. An adaptation spending share for a year after FY22. An adaptation-fund project count later than 5 August 2024. Any of those may exist in another document. They are not established by the pages this set uses, so they are not stated as facts here.

## How to refresh the snapshot

Open the ministry's [national plan page](https://moef.gov.in/national-action-plan-on-climate-change), the [state plan page](https://moef.gov.in/state-action-plan-on-climate-change), and the adaptation-fund approval list. Search PIB for a reply newer than 29 January 2026. Then open IMD for the warning in force, which expires on a weather clock. If the newer page disagrees with this brief, the newer page wins.
