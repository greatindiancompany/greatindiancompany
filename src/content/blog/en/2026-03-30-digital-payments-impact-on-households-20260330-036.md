The Reserve Bank does not publish a household survey inside Payment System Indicators - August 2026. It does publish volume and value for each rail, and their ratio is the average payment on that rail. For August 2026 those averages run from a few hundred rupees to several lakh. The underlying counts are provisional.

## Average size by rail

An average here is the August 2026 value in rupees divided by the August 2026 number of transactions.

| Rail | Volume (lakh) | Value (₹ crore) | Average |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| UPI | 2,45,089.58 | 29,82,356 | about ₹1,217 |
| PPI wallets | 7,449.52 | 22,492 | about ₹302 |
| Credit cards | 6,324.47 | 2,02,449 | about ₹3,201 |
| Debit cards | 1,038.65 | 36,697 | about ₹3,533 |
| ATM cash withdrawal | 4,144.62 | 2,25,178 | about ₹5,433 |
| AePS at micro-ATMs | 999.48 | 24,747 | about ₹2,476 |
| NEFT | 8,825.02 | 44,08,552 | about ₹49,955 |
| Internet / netbanking channel | 3,262.27 | 1,20,76,198 | about ₹3,70,178 |
| RTGS | 315.19 | 2,05,83,738 | about ₹65.3 lakh |

UPI is the mass-market payment in this table: 24.51 billion transactions at a little over ₹1,200 each. Wallets are smaller still, at about ₹302, on 7,449.52 lakh transactions. NEFT, the internet channel and RTGS are where the large individual transfers sit. RTGS customer and interbank traffic together averaged about ₹65.3 lakh.

## Cash withdrawal is still a large monthly activity

People took cash at ATMs 4,144.62 lakh times in August 2026, using credit cards, debit cards and prepaid cards combined. Almost all of that count is debit cards: 4,120.45 lakh withdrawals, worth ₹2,23,907 crore.

AePS cash withdrawal at micro-ATMs was another 999.48 lakh transactions, worth ₹24,747 crore. That line is in the channels section. It is separate from AePS fund transfers in the retail credit-transfer block, which were 0.23 lakh transactions and ₹13 crore.

Set beside UPI, ATM withdrawals were fewer by a factor of about 59 (2,45,089.58 divided by 4,144.62). The two lines do different jobs. One moves a payment. The other dispenses cash. Both were large in August.

## Cards in the wallet, and cards at the till

Outstanding cards as on August 2026: 1,240.54 lakh credit cards and 10,368.29 lakh debit cards. As on August 2025, in the same infrastructure block, those stocks were 1,124.96 lakh and 10,180.42 lakh.

Debit-card payment volume still fell, from 1,120.80 lakh in August 2025 to 1,038.65 lakh in August 2026, while the stock of debit cards was a little higher. Credit-card payments rose from 4,935.77 lakh to 6,324.47 lakh, and the average credit-card payment fell from about ₹3,873 to about ₹3,201. More credit-card swipes and taps, at a smaller average amount.

## Wallet balances of count, with a warning

Prepaid wallets outstanding were 18,389.26 lakh as on August 2026, against 9,692.18 lakh as on August 2025 in this table. RBI says prepaid-instrument data may not be comparable with earlier periods because the definitions were revised and the categories became finer. The August 2026 wallet-payment flow is usable on its own: 7,449.52 lakh payments, ₹22,492 crore, about ₹302 each. The change in the stock of wallets is not, by itself, a measure of new users.

Merchant-side stocks, including static QR codes, are in the [acceptance brief](/blog/digital-payments-impact-on-smes-20260330-026). The full August column is in the [key-number brief](/blog/digital-payments-key-numbers-20260330-016). Both are on the [Great Indian Company brief index](/blog).
