The execution bottleneck is not a missing GPU. It is a sentence. On 14 November 2025 PIB said the government had notified the DPDP Rules, "marking the full operationalisation of the DPDP Act, 2023." The same note then describes an eighteen-month phased timeline. Both sentences are in the note. Only the gazette says which provisions are in which phase.

## What "full" cannot mean

G.S.R. 843(E), dated 13 November 2025, is the commencement notification. It does not bring the Act in as a block.

In force from publication: section 1(2), section 2, sections 18 to 26, sections 35 and 38 to 43, and sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 44. In the Rules, that matches Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21: title, definitions, and the machinery for appointing and running the Board.

One year from publication, 13 November 2026: section 6(9) and section 27(1)(d), with Rule 4. That is Consent Manager registration and the Board's power to inquire into a breach of registration conditions.

Eighteen months from publication, 13 May 2027: sections 3 to 5, section 6 except sub-section (9), sections 7 to 17, the rest of section 27, sections 28 to 34, 36 and 37, and section 44(2). In the Rules, that is Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23: notice, security, breach intimation, erasure, children's consent, Significant Data Fiduciary duties, the person's rights, cross-border transfer, research standards, appeals, and government calls for information.

If "full operationalisation" is read as "a company must already give a DPDP notice, secure personal data under Rule 6, and face the Schedule," the gazette contradicts the reading. The phased sentence inside the same PIB note is the one that matches the notification.

## The Board is a second bottleneck, not a footnote

Section 18 says the Board is established from a date the Central Government notifies. Bringing section 18 into force lets the government issue that date. It is not itself the date. Rule 17 constitutes search-cum-selection committees, chaired for the Chairperson by the Cabinet Secretary, with the Law Secretary and the MeitY Secretary. This series has not cited a later gazette that names the Chairperson, fixes the headquarters, or says the Board is receiving complaints.

PIB says the Board "will function as a fully digital institution" and that citizens will file complaints through a platform and a mobile app, with appeals to TDSAT. "Will" is the note's tense. TDSAT's role is already written into law: section 2 defines the Appellate Tribunal as TDSAT, and section 44(1), already in force, amends the TRAI Act accordingly. The appeal procedure in Rule 22 starts with the eighteen-month set. A digital office in Rule 20 is in force as a rule about how the Board functions. It still needs a Board.

## Classification that cannot yet be executed

A Significant Data Fiduciary exists only when the Central Government notifies one, or a class, under section 10. Section 10 is on the May 2027 list. Factors in the Act include volume, sensitivity, risk to the person, sovereignty, electoral democracy, security of the State, and public order. No firm becomes one by crossing a user count that the Act does not set. The Third Schedule's user thresholds, two crore for a large e-commerce or social-media intermediary and fifty lakh for an online gaming intermediary, are erasure triggers under Rule 8. They are not the Significant Data Fiduciary test, and Rule 8 is itself on the May 2027 list.

Rule 13(4)'s restriction on transferring specified personal data and its traffic data outside India also waits for that date, and then for a government specification on the advice of a committee. There is nothing to "comply with" in that sub-rule until the specification exists.

## What is not stuck

The IndiaAI compute pool, AIKosh, and the Safe and Trusted AI projects are not waiting on the Data Protection Board. Their bottleneck, if any, is empanelment and access, and the notes through August 2026 show the pool in use: 237 projects and 93.18 lakh GPU hours. CERT-In reporting is not waiting either.

The practical failure mode is a team that spends 2026 building only for a Board that is not yet shown to be sitting, and that ignores the six-hour CERT-In clock that is already running.
