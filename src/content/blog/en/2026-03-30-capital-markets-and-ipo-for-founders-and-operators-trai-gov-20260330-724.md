# What founders should settle before the draft prospectus

The founder's version of an IPO is a valuation and a date. The filing version is a cap table that can survive ICDR. The text to use is the Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2018, last amended on 21 March 2026, plus the amendment notification of that date and the lock-in circular of 8 April 2026. SEBI's Annual Report chapter on primary markets is the plain-language account of what those changes were for.

## Six items that are cheaper to fix before the draft

Dematerialise the people, not only the company. The chapter says the precondition now covers the promoter group, selling shareholders, key managerial personnel, senior management, qualified institutional buyers, and directors. Their holdings have to be in demat before the draft red herring prospectus is filed. A relative in the promoter group with a paper certificate can hold the file. The special window for old physical transfers, open until 4 February 2027, is for a purchase made before 1 April 2019 with the original certificate and deed. It is not a general amnesty for a messy cap table.

If any of those shares are pledged, read the April circular before the lender conversation. Depositories mark the pledged shares non-transferable for the lock-in when a conventional lock-in cannot be created. The company needs matching articles, a written intimation to the pledgee, and a disclosure in the offer document. If the lender enforces or releases the pledge, the remaining lock-in stays. Founders who have financed themselves against pre-IPO stock need this agreed in the loan documents, not discovered in a SEBI query.

Employee stock options survive a founder becoming a promoter, on the chapter's account, if the options were granted at least one year before the draft is filed. The old expectation that they be liquidated is what the reform removed. Options granted inside that last year are a different conversation. Do not assume the one-year line covers a grant made to close the round.

Companies moving back to India get a narrower version of the same relief. The one-year pre-IPO holding exemption for an offer for sale was extended to equity that came out of compulsorily convertible securities acquired under an approved restructuring. Alternative investment funds, foreign venture capital investors, scheduled commercial banks, and insurance companies may count converted shares toward minimum promoter contribution. This is a reverse-flip accommodation. It is not a waiver of disclosure, demat, or lock-in.

Hire a Category 1 merchant banker if the issue is main board. The net-worth floor the chapter states is ₹50 crore. A Category 2 firm, at ₹10 crore, cannot manage that issue. The compliance officer needs a professional legal or company-secretary qualification. The banker also has to be willing to put its net worth's liquid slice, 25 per cent, and its revenue test, on the record.

Write the objects so they match the fresh money only. In 2025-26, fresh-only IPOs raised ₹21,866 crore across 214 issues, while offer-for-sale-only IPOs raised ₹50,849 crore across 19. Mixed deals raised ₹1,15,901 crore, almost evenly split. A founder who tells employees that "the IPO funds the plant" while the draft is mostly a sale by existing holders will be contradicted by the document. The abridged prospectus is filed with the draft and posted online. Assume readers see the split early.

## What the book-building rules change in the room

Anchor allocations up to ₹250 crore now sit in one bucket: 2 to 15 allottees, at least ₹5 crore each. Above ₹250 crore the floor is 5 allottees and the cap is 15, plus 15 more for every additional ₹250 crore or part of it, still with a ₹5 crore minimum. Life insurers registered with IRDAI and pension funds registered with PFRDA join domestic mutual funds in the reserved slice. The reservation is 40 per cent of the anchor portion: 33.33 per cent mutual funds, 6.67 per cent insurers and pension funds. A founder who has spent the pre-file year only with a foreign sponsor has not met the buyer the reservation assumes.

Large issuers should read the six-tier minimum public offer themselves. The public-shareholding destination is still 25 per cent. The opening sale can be smaller, with a longer path, scaled to post-issue capital. That is a dilution decision. It is also a later-supply decision for anyone who buys the first slice.

## The day after listing is a different job

LODR, last amended on 14 July 2026, replaces the project plan with a calendar: financial results, material events, related-party approvals on the scaled threshold, and demat-only corporate actions. Exchanges freeze promoter demat accounts when the standard operating procedure says so. In the 2025-26 exchange-wise table that freeze was applied to 486 companies at BSE, 99 at NSE, and 41 at MSEI. The median SEBI processing time, once a file is in, was 25 working days excluding the issuer's own query time and 84 including it. Budget the longer number, and budget a compliance officer who is still there after the bell.
