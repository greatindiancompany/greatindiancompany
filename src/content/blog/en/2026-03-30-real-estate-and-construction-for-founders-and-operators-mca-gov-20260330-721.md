# What a Promoter Must Open Before Selling or Building in India

A founder in Indian housing is usually one of three people: a promoter selling plots or apartments, an operator delivering a PMAY file, or a broker, including a website, that sits between them. The July 2025 [MoHUA FAQ](https://www.mohua.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/542144159b61c980253a694f478d3a5d.pdf) on the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act is written for the first and the third. It says it is guidance, not a ruling on your project. The Act is on [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158?locale=en). State rules still decide the form, the fee, and the interest rate.

## Before the first advertisement

Register the project with that state's Real Estate Regulatory Authority before you advertise, market, book, or invite anyone to purchase. The authority has 30 days. Silence on a complete application is deemed registration. An incomplete file can be sent back and, after a hearing, rejected.

You are inside the Act if you develop a building, convert a building into apartments, or cut land into plots, for sale, inside a planning area. The threshold in Section 3(2), which the FAQ repeats with "or", leaves out land not exceeding five hundred square metres, or a project not exceeding eight apartments counting every phase, plus projects that already had a completion certificate before the Act commenced, plus renovation that allots nothing new. Do not treat that sentence as a lawyer's opinion on a phased layout. Ask the state authority, and keep the FAQ's disclaimer in the file.

If the person who builds and the person who sells are different, both are promoters and both are liable. A development authority that sells to the public is a promoter too. A joint-development partner does not step out of the definition by holding only the land.

Agents, including web portals that sell registered projects, register in every state where they operate. One national website is not one registration.

## Money, plans, and the five-year tail

Open a separate bank account for each project. Deposit seventy percent of what allottees pay. Use it for land cost and construction cost of that project. Draw in proportion to completion, against certificates from an engineer, an architect, and a chartered accountant in practice. This is not an escrow that waits for the regulator to sign the cheque. The discipline is the certificate and the audit. The audit is due within six months after the financial year, and it must show that project collections stayed with the project.

Do not take more than 10 percent before the agreement for sale. The state prescribes the agreement. Its extra clauses cannot contradict the Act. You are responsible for what the advertisement says. A loss caused by a false particular is a promoter liability under Section 12, as the FAQ states it.

Do not sell open parking. It is common area. A garage with a roof and three walls can be sold separately. Community and commercial facilities in the project are common area and go to the association.

Major plan changes need the competent authority and two-thirds of allottees, excluding your own apartments, one vote per allottee. The same consent, plus the authority, is required before you transfer a majority of your rights in the project. Registration lasts for the completion period you declared. Extension beyond the force majeure list, which is natural calamity and war, is capped at one year and requires an absence of fault.

From handover, structural and specified defects sit with you for five years, and the FAQ says you rectify within 30 days. If you miss the possession date, the allottee can exit for a refund with interest or stay and take monthly interest. The rate matches the rate the allottee would pay you if they were late.

Title and construction insurance under Section 16 applies only after your state notifies it. Do not assume the policy is already compulsory because the section exists.

## If the work is a PMAY house rather than a private sale

The customer is an eligible family, not a free-market allottee. [PMAY-U 2.0](https://pmaymis.gov.in/PMAYMIS2_2024/faq.html) pays one vertical. Beneficiary-led central assistance arrives 40:40:20 against geo-tagged progress. You cannot sell the family both that grant and the interest subsidy. The house should be in the woman's name or in joint name, unless the FAQ's exceptions apply. The family is not supposed to sell or rent it for five years. Quality is watched by a third-party monitor, and complaints route through the state, CPGRAMS, and the mission portal.

If you are pricing a private launch against "the market," the registration-price print is the [RBI index](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63436), not a portal listing. The ninety-day clocks are in the [checklist](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/real-estate-and-construction-next-90-days-checklist-mca-gov-20260330-481). Great Indian Company publishes this desk note so the first document a promoter opens is the state register, not a pitch.
