# The ten-year path of India’s agri chain

Over the decade that official notes now summarise, three things moved together and one moved the other way. Public buying of grain expanded. The rupees paid at MSP expanded faster than the tonnes. Agricultural exports expanded. The average operational holding got smaller.

Foodgrain procurement, measured July to June, went from 761.40 lakh tonnes in 2014-15 to 1,175 lakh tonnes in 2024-25. The MSP bill on that book went from ₹1.06 lakh crore to ₹3.33 lakh crore. Farmers reached went from 1.63 crore in 2021-22 to 1.84 crore in 2024-25, and the value paid in that shorter window went from ₹2.25 lakh crore to ₹3.33 lakh crore. The tonne increase from 2014-15 to 2024-25 is about 54 percent. The rupee increase on the same endpoints is about threefold, which is what a rising MSP does to a larger book. Quote the tonnes and the rupees separately.

Paddy’s longer comparison, 2014-15 to 2024-25 against 2004-05 to 2013-14, is 7,608 lakh tonnes against 4,590 lakh tonnes. Fourteen kharif crops together are 7,871 against 4,679. Payments to paddy growers are ₹14.16 lakh crore against ₹4.44 lakh crore. This is a two-decade contrast printed by the Cabinet in May 2025. It is not the same series as the July-to-June foodgrain total of 1,175 lakh tonnes. One is a single year’s foodgrains. The other is eleven years of paddy added up.

Wheat’s recent path is shorter and more precise. RMS 2022-23 was 188 lakh tonnes, the following season 262 lakh tonnes, and RMS 2024-25 was 266 lakh tonnes, with about ₹0.61 lakh crore paid to more than 22 lakh farmers. The recovery after the thin 2022-23 season is the fact. A smooth upward line from 188 to 266 would misread two jumps and then a flat year.

## Exports rose on a different axis

The Department of Commerce, on 10 June 2026, put agricultural exports at USD 32.08 billion in FY 2014-15 and USD 52.55 billion in FY 2025-26. The latest step was 2.8 percent, from USD 51.12 billion in FY 2024-25 to USD 52.55 billion. Rice at USD 11.5 billion, marine products at USD 8.4 billion, and spices at USD 4.3 billion are the FY 2025-26 lines in that briefing. APEDA’s annual report, using DGCIS, prints FY 2024-25 agri exports at USD 51.91 billion and its own scheduled products at USD 28.59 billion. The decade direction is the same in the Commerce speech. The 2024-25 level is not identical across the two official prints. Keep the source attached to the number.

## The farm that feeds this chain shrank

Between Agriculture Census 2010-11 and 2015-16, operational holdings rose from 138.35 million to 146.45 million, a 5.86 percent increase, while operated area fell from 159.59 million hectares to 157.82 million, a 1.11 percent decline. Average size fell from 1.15 hectares to 1.08 hectares. The chain’s public buyer and export invoice got larger while the typical holding got smaller. That is why aggregation schemes exist. It is also why a national export total cannot be read as income per holding. The income survey the Ministry still cites is older than the export print: ₹10,218 a month per agricultural household in 2018-19, against ₹6,426 in 2012-13.

The mid-term picture is a larger procurement state, a larger export invoice, and a more fragmented production base. The next season’s price is a separate decision, set out in [what changed in kharif MSP](/blog/agri-value-chains-what-changed-this-quarter-morth-nic-20260330-117). The size-class detail inside the 1.08 hectares is in [the census snapshot](/blog/agri-value-chains-evidence-snapshot-morth-nic-20260330-677).
