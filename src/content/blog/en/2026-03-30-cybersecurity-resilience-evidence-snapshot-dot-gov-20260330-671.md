Evidence of cybersecurity resilience in India is a record with a date, an entity or a product, a control, and a unit. This page names records that can meet that test. It reprints none of them, and it carries no incident count, spend figure, or score.

## Records that can change a decision

Use a row only when you can open the document.

- **The telecom instrument.** A Department of Telecommunications notification, rule, or licence amendment. The site that holds that shelf is [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/). The homepage is the shelf. The notification is the row. Telecommunications (Telecom Cyber Security) Rules, 2024, are one such instrument. Quote the gazette or the notification, not a recap.
- **The certificate.** An Indian Telecom Security Assurance Requirements result, or a mandatory testing and certification record, for a named product.
- **The determination.** A trusted-product or trusted-source decision under the National Security Directive on the Telecommunication Sector for the vendor you intend to connect.
- **The incident file.** The CERT-In direction that applies to your entity, next to the report you filed and the times on it. The direction without your file is a duty. Your file without the direction is an internal note.
- **The sector circular.** The Reserve Bank, SEBI, or IRDAI text that binds the licence you hold. A news summary is not the circular.
- **The audit.** The security-audit report the entity holds, with findings and closure dates. A claim that an audit happened is not the report.
- **The designation.** If you rely on critical-information-infrastructure status, the designation of the protected system.

## What a usable row contains

Date, entity or product, control, unit, and who wrote it down. "India" is not a place for an operating decision. A direction word with no unit is not a row. The series worth logging every week are on the [signal brief](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-market-signal-brief-dot-gov-20260330-191). When a row may be called a quarterly change is the [quarter test](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-what-changed-this-quarter-dot-gov-20260330-111). How not to turn a row into a country rank is on the [benchmark page](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-india-vs-global-benchmark-dot-gov-20260330-271).

If the cell is empty, write the gap: which figure, which product, which dates. Do not fill it with another entity's average.
