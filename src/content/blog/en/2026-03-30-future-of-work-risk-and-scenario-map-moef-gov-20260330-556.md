# Future of Work in India: Risk Map for Employers and Platforms

## What this map contains

Each item is a way to misread a document that exists. The map assigns no probability and ties no item to a jobs gained or jobs lost result. On 20 July 2026 a Lok Sabha question asked the Ministry of Labour and Employment for that created-versus-lost count in manufacturing and MSMEs. The answer did not provide one.

## The wrong government

The codes have been in force since 21 November 2025, and Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026. Labour is concurrent. Applying the central handbook to an establishment whose appropriate government is a state, or the reverse, is the first operational risk. The July reply described most states and Union Territories as having finalised or pre-published rules, and a few as still finishing. Some establishments remain closer to the transition position the ministry stated on 21 November 2025, under which existing Act provisions continue until replaced.

## A definition booked as a paid benefit

Gig work, platform work, and aggregators are defined. The ministry's note states a contribution of 1 to 2 percent of annual turnover, capped at 5 percent of amounts paid or payable to gig and platform workers. The July reply describes schemes as something those definitions allow the government to formulate. A platform that books a levy or a benefit at a detail the notified scheme does not yet contain is ahead of the text. A public claim that every defined worker already draws a uniform cash benefit has the same problem from the other side.

## Substituting a survey

NITI Aayog's June 2022 report, *India's Booming Gig and Platform Economy*, recommended that the Periodic Labour Force Survey identify gig workers. Treating that report's estimates, or a later private slide, as the current PLFS is a measurement error. PLFS remains the household survey. The annual report covering January to December 2025 was issued on 27 March 2026. Its rates stay in the release.

## Contract form

The ministry describes fixed-term employees as receiving permanent-worker benefits and gratuity after one year rather than five, and it says appointment letters are mandatory. A contracting chain in which the person on site has no letter stating wages and social security is a documentary risk. The ministry's contract-worker note places health and social-security duties on the principal employer as well as on the contractor.

## The payroll date in IT and ITES

The ministry's IT and ITES note says salary is released by the 7th. Missing that date is a wage-compliance risk for software and business-process employers. Client demand for those services sits in the order book. It is a separate risk, and this map does not express it as a headcount.
