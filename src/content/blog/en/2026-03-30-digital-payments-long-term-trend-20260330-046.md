Five August releases from the Reserve Bank, read at the current-month column of each file, give a clean UPI path. The releases are Payment System Indicators for August 2022, August 2023, August 2024, August 2025 and August 2026. Each file says the data are provisional. For UPI volume, the August figure first printed in 2022, 2023 and 2024 matches the later release that reprints that month, so this path is not an artefact of one revision.

## The August UPI column

| August | Volume (lakh) | Value (₹ crore) | Average |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2022 | 65,819.06 | 10,73,162 | about ₹1,630 |
| 2023 | 1,05,860.16 | 15,76,537 | about ₹1,489 |
| 2024 | 1,49,630.47 | 20,60,736 | about ₹1,377 |
| 2025 | 2,00,083.10 | 24,85,473 | about ₹1,242 |
| 2026 | 2,45,089.58 | 29,82,356 | about ₹1,217 |

From August 2022 to August 2026, UPI volume rose 3.72 times and UPI value rose 2.78 times. The average payment fell from about ₹1,630 to about ₹1,217. The system added transactions faster than it added rupees.

In billions of transactions, the same volumes are 6.58, 10.59, 14.96, 20.01 and 24.51.

## Share of digital payment volume

Total digital payments in the same release, same August column:

| August | Digital volume (lakh) | UPI share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2022 | 90,852.16 | 72.4% |
| 2023 | 1,32,913.16 | 79.6% |
| 2024 | 1,79,267.83 | 83.5% |
| 2025 | 2,35,026.26 | 85.1% |
| 2026 | 2,81,373.84 | 87.1% |

Digital volume itself was 3.10 times its August 2022 level by August 2026. UPI's share of that volume rose by 14.7 percentage points. In August 2026 UPI was still about 10.1 percent of digital payment value, because RTGS and other large-value rails carry the rupees. That value split is calculated in the [August 2026 number brief](/blog/digital-payments-key-numbers-20260330-016).

## Cards moved in opposite directions

Debit-card payment volume in these August columns: 3,033.47 lakh (2022), 2,076.18 lakh (2023), 1,422.45 lakh (2024), 1,120.80 lakh (2025) and 1,038.65 lakh (2026). August 2026 was about 34 percent of the August 2022 count.

Credit-card payment volume: 2,447.83 lakh, 2,905.78 lakh, 3,900.68 lakh, 4,934.43 lakh and 6,324.47 lakh. The 2025 figure is the one printed in the August 2025 release. The August 2026 release reprints August 2025 credit-card volume as 4,935.77 lakh. The revision is 1.34 lakh on a base of about 4,935 lakh. August 2026 credit-card volume was 2.58 times August 2022.

## What this path leaves out

Static UPI QR counts are a poor cousin for a five-year chart. The August 2024 release printed 3,412.10 lakh static codes for that month. The August 2025 release reprints August 2024 as 5,912.93 lakh. A series that jumps inside the revision belongs in a footnote, not in a growth rate. Year-on-year QR movement inside the August 2026 file is in the [what-changed brief](/blog/digital-payments-what-changed-20260330-006).

Fraud rates over the same years are a different block, with a narrower set of reporters. They are in the [risk brief](/blog/digital-payments-risk-watch-20260330-076).

The five releases are linked from this page and from the [Great Indian Company brief index](/blog). Averages and shares are arithmetic on the printed August columns.
