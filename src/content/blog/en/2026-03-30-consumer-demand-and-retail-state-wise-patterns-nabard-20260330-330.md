# Rural and state patterns in household consumption

National PFCE is one household. The Household Consumption Expenditure Survey is the survey that splits that household by place. The factsheet for HCES 2023-24, fielded from August 2023 to July 2024, is the latest detailed all-India and state picture from the National Statistics Office.

Without imputing the value of items received free through social-welfare programmes, average monthly per-capita consumption expenditure was ₹4,122 in rural India and ₹6,996 in urban India. Food was 47.04% of rural MPCE and 39.68% of urban MPCE. Urban MPCE of ₹6,996 is about 70% above the rural figure of ₹4,122. Report 592 of the same survey says that gap is nominal and ignores rural-urban price differences.

Among states, the same unimputed estimates are highest in Sikkim, at ₹9,377 rural and ₹13,927 urban, and lowest in Chhattisgarh, at ₹2,739 rural and ₹4,927 urban. A national average sits far closer to Chhattisgarh’s rural figure than to Sikkim’s. A chain that plans one “India basket” is planning past the survey.

## A second rural signal, from prices rather than budgets

HCES is a level of spending in a survey year. The CPI is a later price change. In the provisional August 2026 index (base 2024=100), rural inflation was 5.23% and urban inflation was 4.31%. Rural food inflation was 6.13% and urban food inflation was 5.64%. The state and Union-territory indices are Statement III of that release. This brief does not pull individual state inflation rates out of a table it has not reproduced line by line; the statement is the source.

The two series answer different rural questions. HCES says the rural budget is smaller and more food-heavy. The August CPI says rural prices were rising faster than urban prices in that month. Neither series is a state GDP of retail.

A second HCES set imputes free welfare items and lifts the all-India averages to ₹4,247 rural and ₹7,078 urban. Quoting one set as if it were the other moves the level. The factsheet presents the unimputed estimates first.
