# What to Watch in India’s Social Programmes Over the Next 90 Days

The next ninety days from 24 September 2026 run to about 23 December 2026. They cover the rest of Poshan Maah, the rabi season in which states may pause rural wage works, and at least two more monthly labour-force bulletins if the National Statistics Office keeps the monthly rhythm it has used since 2025. This list is documents and decisions already described by ministries. It is not a forecast of indicator values.

## Close Poshan Maah against its own checklist

The ninth Rashtriya Poshan Maah was launched on 9 September 2026. The 8 September press note already names the checks a state can be held to during the month:

- Anganwadi Management Committees, under the 23 June 2026 guidelines, meet at least once.
- Supplementary-nutrition menu boards are displayed.
- Hot cooked meals are tested with the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India.
- The revised home-visit scheduler, with 34 milestones for children aged 0-3, is being rolled out.
- Eligible women are enrolled under the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana, and pending direct-benefit transfers are reviewed on the scheme dashboard. The note marks ten years of that maternity-benefit programme.

After 30 September, the useful question is whether those process items were reported, not whether a new stunting rate appeared. NFHS-6 stunting (29.3% in the 2023-24 survey) will not update inside this window unless the ministry releases a further survey product. None is announced in the notes cited here.

## Read the rural wage programme as a live statute

VB-G RAM G has been in force since 1 July 2026. The Lok Sabha was told on 11 August that more than 9.69 crore person-days had been generated in roughly the first month, and that ₹25,844.97 crore had been sanctioned as a first instalment against a central share of ₹95,692.31 crore for 1 July 2026 to 31 March 2027. Over the next ninety days, watch three published instruments rather than a rumour of “fund shortage”:

- Any state notification of the sixty-day agricultural pause. The Act allows an aggregated pause of sixty days. It does not allow the 125-day guarantee to shrink.
- Whether unemployment allowance and the 0.05 percent daily delay compensation show up in state reporting when work or wages are late. The May 2026 note makes both duties.
- Issuance of Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards against job cards that remain valid where e-KYC is already done.

Do not add these person-days to last year’s MGNREGS total. The old Act is repealed.

## Use the district health sheets that are already out

District NFHS-6 compendiums were released on 20 August 2026 for six states and Union territories and on 21 August 2026 for the rest of the covered set. Manipur is excluded. IIPS says the files are in its publication section and that a guest login is used to download them. The next ninety days are for reading those sheets, not for waiting on a national report that these pages have not scheduled. Where a cell is in parentheses, the survey is flagging a small base. Skip it.

## Take the labour-market print as scheduled statistics

The August 2026 PLFS monthly bulletin came out on 15 September 2026. The ministry has been releasing a monthly bulletin and a quarterly bulletin under the current weekly status. The next monthly note, if the pattern holds, will describe September. Record the level, the rural-urban split, and the female split. The August note’s urban female participation rate fell over the year while the rural female rate rose. A one-line “participation improved” summary will miss the same split if it happens again.

## Leave these items off the list

Do not expect a revised central NSAP pension rate. On 21 July 2026 the ministry said no such proposal is under consideration. Do not treat Poshan Tracker beneficiary totals as a substitute for height and weight outcomes. Do not treat UDISE+ facility gains as learning gains. The 2025-26 school report is already published; another census will not land inside ninety days.
