# A founder’s reading order for Indian demand

A founder does not need every table MoSPI publishes. The costly mistakes come from using the right table for the wrong decision. This is a reading order, built only from official series and notified rules.

## 1. Set the price conversation from CPI, not from a growth headline

The August 2026 CPI, base 2024=100, was 4.82% provisional for the combined index, 5.23% rural, 4.31% urban, and 5.95% for food. If the product is food, or the buyer is rural, the all-India 4.82% is the wrong anchor. August is provisional, and the September release is dated 12 October 2026 or the next working day. A price list written in late September should say which month it used.

Real PFCE growth of 7.1% in April–June 2026-27 is the volume backdrop from the 31 August national-accounts note. It is not a licence to assume every category grew 7.1%.

## 2. Size the household from the survey, then split rural and urban

HCES 2023-24, without imputing free welfare items, put average monthly per-capita expenditure at ₹4,122 rural and ₹6,996 urban. Food was 47.04% of the rural figure and 39.68% of the urban figure. Sikkim and Chhattisgarh are not the same market: ₹9,377 versus ₹2,739 in rural MPCE on that unimputed set. A national TAM that multiplies one urban basket by India’s population is not this survey.

## 3. Pick the legal model before the pitch deck names it

If foreign capital is in the round, the Consolidated FDI Policy Circular of 2020 chooses the model.

- Selling your own Indian-manufactured goods, including online, is the manufacturing permission in paragraph 5.2.5.1.
- Running a marketplace is 100% automatic FDI, if you do not own or control inventory. The 25% vendor-purchase test is the line that reclassifies you.
- Holding inventory and selling it online to consumers is the model for which FDI is not permitted.
- A single brand can go online, and must open stores within two years of starting online retail.
- Multi-brand FDI cannot be used for e-commerce retail.

Whatever the cap table, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 require an e-commerce entity to be a company (or the foreign-company and branch forms the rules name), with a nodal officer resident in India, seller details on the listing, and a grievance path. GST section 52 still requires an operator that collects the consideration to collect tax at source and file the monthly statement.

## 4. Hire against the enterprise that actually exists

ASUSE 2025 found other retail trade to be 26.49% of unincorporated establishments, and more than half of trading establishments using the internet. PLFS 2025 puts trade, hotels, and restaurants at 12.9% of workers, and at 23.8% of urban workers. The talent market for a new chain is not the same as the count of kirana proprietors. The survey that includes companies is not ASUSE.
