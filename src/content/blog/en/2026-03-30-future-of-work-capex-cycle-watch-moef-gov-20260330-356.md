# Future of Work in India: Spending on Compliance and Skilling Systems

## Systems, and plant and machinery

A capex watch usually follows factories, machinery, and order books. The future-of-work items in India's labour and skilling record are systems: appointment letters, wage registers, a single return, aggregator payment records, and the public skilling platform. This brief states no rupee cost for that work.

## What employers now have to run

The Ministry of Labour and Employment's note of 21 November 2025 says workers are to receive appointment letters. The compliance handbook for the central sphere says employers keep attendance, wage, overtime, and fines-and-deductions registers, on paper or electronically, and issue wage slips. The ministry's note also describes single registration, a single pan-India licence, and a single return in place of overlapping filings. Configuring that stack is the private spending analogue. It is the operating system for formal employment, including fixed-term employees, who the ministry says become eligible for gratuity after one year rather than five.

For a platform that is an aggregator, the same note sets a contribution of 1 to 2 percent of annual turnover, capped at 5 percent of amounts paid or payable to gig and platform workers. The build is to know who the workers are, what was paid, and how the cap is computed. The ministry launched e-Shram on 26 August 2021 as a database of unorganised workers, including gig and platform workers, and has described a platform-aggregator module there. Connecting payouts to that registration logic is a product and finance build.

For IT and ITES, salary by the 7th, as the ministry's sector note states, is a payroll-calendar constraint. Meeting it is a treasury and HR-system task.

## Public skilling infrastructure

Guidelines from the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship place Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) 4.0 under the Skill India Programme for FY 2022-2026. They name the National Council for Vocational Education and Training as the body that recognises awarding bodies and assessment agencies for National Skills Qualification Framework courses, the National Skill Development Corporation as implementation support, and Skill India Digital as the registry from registration through post-certification tracking. That is public digital infrastructure for skills. The financial window in those guidelines runs through March 2026. A successor scheme, if one is in force, is the text MSDE has published since. This page does not cost the programme.

## What to track

Track notified central and state rules. The Lok Sabha reply of 20 July 2026 says Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026. Track whether single-return filing is live for the establishment's appropriate government, and what MSDE publishes after the PMKVY 4.0 window. A jobs-per-crore claim is not part of this watch.
