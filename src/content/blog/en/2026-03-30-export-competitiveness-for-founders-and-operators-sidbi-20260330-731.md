# What founders and operators need from India’s export trade rules

An exporter does not need a tour of every India scheme. The operating set is an IEC, the right rebate, a credit rule with a cap and a date, and a market whose preference is actually in force.

## Pick the rebate before you pick the portal

RoDTEP refunds duties and taxes embedded in the exported product that are not otherwise credited or remitted. DGFT publishes the schedules, including the rates restored on 23 March 2026 after a one-month 50 percent cut. Open the [RoDTEP page](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=rodtep) and read both the percentage and the value cap for the eight-digit line you ship. The March restoration returned the 22 February 2026 rates. It did not pay your freight.

RoSCTL is the rebate for apparel and garments under chapters 61 and 62 and made-ups under chapter 63. The 22 April 2026 textiles note says both RoSCTL and RoDTEP continue beyond 31 March 2026. If you make shirts, confirm you are on RoSCTL. If you make engineering parts, RoSCTL is the wrong window. Filing the wrong scheme is a common way to discover, months later, that the scrip was never going to issue.

FTP 2023 also cut fee structures and moved authorisations toward an electronic route so that MSMEs, not only large status holders, could use Advance Authorisation and EPCG. The courier cap for e-commerce exports was raised from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh a consignment. Those are filing changes. They still require the IEC and the shipping bill to match.

## The credit rule that is actually dated

The export-finance design published for MSMEs is Niryat Protsahan, not a bank homepage. On 2 January 2026 the government set a 2.75 percent interest subvention on pre- and post-shipment rupee export credit from eligible lenders, on a positive list of about 75 percent of six-digit tariff lines, with a cap of ₹50 lakh per IEC for 2025–26. Extra support for under-represented markets was conditional on operational readiness. The Reserve Bank was to issue the guidelines. A CGTMSE guarantee of up to 85 percent for micro and small exporters, and up to 65 percent for medium exporters, was capped at ₹10 crore of outstanding guaranteed exposure per exporter in a year. Both pieces were pilots.

The parties named in the 2 January 2026 note are the Department of Commerce, the Ministry of MSME, the Ministry of Finance, lenders, CGTMSE, and the RBI for the credit guideline. If your working-capital sanction quotes a different rate, ask which notification it implements.

The ₹50 lakh cap is written for 2025–26, and the rates were to be reviewed in March and September. On 24 September 2026, do not paste the cap into a new financial year until the review or the RBI guideline says it still applies.

## A district plan is a lead, not an order

If you manufacture in a district with a notified export action plan, use the product list as a clue to what the local committee will recognise. It does not create a buyer. Preferential duty into the United Kingdom is the clearer near-term market fact: the India–UK agreement entered into force on 15 July 2026, with zero-duty access stated for nearly 99 percent of India’s exports, if origin rules are met.
