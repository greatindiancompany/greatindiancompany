# Where Urban Housing Execution Actually Stalls

## The bottleneck is a named handoff

MoHUA designs the mission, sanctions or releases against milestones, and publishes the rule. The step that slows a house, a tap, or a registered project is usually the step the same document gives to a state, a city, a lender, or a promoter. Calling the result “implementation lag” without naming that step is how every mission gets the same paragraph.

## PMAY stalls at the state file and at the loan

The December 2024 PIB note on PMAY-Urban 2.0 says states had to sign a memorandum, finish a demand survey by 31 March 2025, and prepare an Affordable Housing Policy by March 2025. At the time of the note, in-principle approval covered more than 6 lakh houses from 29 States and Union Territories that had signed. The bottleneck is visible in that sentence. A state outside the 29 was not in the approval count. A state inside it still had to turn in-principle approval into a grounded house. This brief does not have a later national completion table, so it does not declare the survey finished.

The verticals stall in different offices. Beneficiary Led Construction depends on the household and the urban local body. Affordable Housing in Partnership depends on a public or private partner and on land. Affordable Rental Housing depends on an entity willing to hold rental stock for the groups the note names. The Interest Subsidy Scheme, on the July 2025 PIB terms, stalls at the lender: income, loan, property value, carpet area, and then, each year, an active loan with more than half the principal outstanding. A complete state survey does not cure a loan that fails the principal test.

The first scheme’s own ledger, in that December 2024 note, shows the gap between sanction and delivery: 1.18 crore sanctioned, 1.14 crore grounded, 88.32 lakh completed or delivered. The unfinished share was already the execution fact, before 2.0 began.

## Smart Cities stalls are now a residual list

The mission’s design put a special purpose vehicle in the city and a chief secretary’s committee in the state, with an apex committee at MoHUA. Financial closure on 31 March 2025 removed the central budget as the accelerator. The 6 August 2026 Lok Sabha annex, as on 31 March 2026, shows where work remained. Silvassa had completed 14 of 29 projects. Port Blair had completed 9 of 18. Kavaratti had completed 8 of 12. Faridabad had completed 35 of 44. Amaravati had completed 14 of 19. Cities such as Indore, Surat, and Pune are printed at 100 percent. The bottleneck is no longer selection. Selection of the 100 cities ended years ago, through the two-stage competition announced at launch in June 2015. The bottleneck is the unfinished project in a city whose central assistance has already been claimed or closed.

Advisory No. 27 of 10 June 2025 adds an institutional bottleneck after the works: who keeps the command centre running once the mission directorate is no longer funding new projects. That decision sits with the state committee.

## AMRUT stalls at the plan and at the reform gate

The 27 October 2021 guidelines tell a city to file a water balance plan and a water action plan, and a state to fold those into a State Water Action Plan, before projects are the state’s proposal. Money moves 20:40:40. From the third year, property tax and user charges are mandatory for uninterrupted funding. A city can have a sanctioned tap target, 2.68 crore new connections were proposed nationally, and still lose the later instalment if the reform is not in place. Non-revenue water below 20 percent is a performance condition in the same text, not a slogan. Used water in cities outside the 500 AMRUT cities was placed, in that PIB release, under Swachh Bharat Mission–Urban 2.0. A sanitation delay in a small town may be the wrong mission’s bottleneck.

## RERA stalls at the state register

Sections 3 to 19, the registration duties, commenced through the 2017 notification, not the May 2016 notification that stood up the authorities. A promoter who has not registered, an agent who has not registered, or an authority that has not put the project on a public site is the bottleneck a buyer can see. MoHUA can list the authority’s website. MoHUA does not maintain the project file. The seventy-percent separate account is enforceable only where the Act’s registration duty has attached.
