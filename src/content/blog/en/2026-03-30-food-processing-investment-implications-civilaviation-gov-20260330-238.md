# Food Processing In India: Separate The Scheme From The Plant

## Do not paste a scheme outlay into a model

MoFPI pages state allocations, grant patterns, and projected output for PMKSY, PMFME, and PLISFPI. This brief does not repeat those rupee figures. An outlay is not revenue for a plant, and a projection year on a scheme page is not a forecast for one company. Investment work starts by naming which instrument the project actually sits under.

## Three instruments, three uses of capital

PMKSY is infrastructure. The components still listed for implementation are cold chain, processing and preservation capacity, agro-processing clusters, food-safety and quality infrastructure, human resources and institutions for research and development, and Operation Greens. Capital here is civil work and plant that keep produce intact or add a processing step. The ministry page says Mega Food Parks and backward-and-forward linkage projects were discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, apart from committed liability. A memo that still underwrites a new Mega Food Park is using a closed track.

PMFME is the micro and group track. It is centrally sponsored. Common infrastructure for FPOs and other groups is credit-linked, has to be hireable by others for a substantial share of capacity, and runs through a detailed project report to the State Nodal Agency and the State Level Approval Committee before MoFPI and the bank. The page states grant limits and a figure for help with the project report. Read those on the page. Do not carry them from memory into a term sheet.

PLISFPI is a production incentive for manufacturers that clear a selection, plus support for branding and marketing abroad. The page limits the manufacturing component to four segments: ready-to-cook and ready-to-eat foods, including millet products; processed fruits and vegetables; marine products; and mozzarella cheese. Innovative or organic products of small and medium enterprises in those segments are covered, and the page says the usual minimum-sales and minimum-investment conditions do not apply to entities selected for that innovative or organic path. The mandated plant-and-machinery investment for other selected applicants was tied to 2021-22 and 2022-23, with 2020-21 investment allowed to count. The incentive period on the page runs through 2026-27, paid in the following year, and the scheme is described as fund-limited, with a ceiling fixed at approval.

## What the plant still has to clear

Scheme eligibility does not replace an FSSAI licence, and an export story does not replace APEDA registration when the product is on APEDA's schedule. A domestic processing line and an export pack house are different assets. Marine products appear in PLISFPI. They are not on the scheduled-product list APEDA publishes for its own export mandate.

## A clean implication

The investable question is which component, which year's rules, and which licence the plant already holds. The rupee arithmetic stays in the guideline and in the company's own filings.
