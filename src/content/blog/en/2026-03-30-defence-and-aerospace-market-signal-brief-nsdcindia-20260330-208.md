# Defence exports rose in 2024-25 because DPSUs grew, not because private exports jumped

## The year-end figure

The Ministry of Defence reported defence exports of Rs 23,622 crore in FY 2024-25, about US$ 2.76 billion on the Ministry's own approximation. That is Rs 2,539 crore, or 12.04 percent, above the FY 2023-24 figure of Rs 21,083 crore. The release is PIB PRID 2117348.

The split inside that total is the signal. Private industry contributed Rs 15,233 crore. Defence public sector undertakings contributed Rs 8,389 crore. A year earlier the same release records private exports of Rs 15,209 crore and DPSU exports of Rs 5,874 crore.

Private exports were already the larger share in both years, and they were almost flat: Rs 15,209 crore, then Rs 15,233 crore. DPSU exports rose 42.85 percent. A sentence that says only "exports grew 12 percent" hides which side of the industry moved.

## Authorisations moved with the DPSU line

The Department of Defence Production runs a portal for export-authorisation requests. It issued 1,762 authorisations in FY 2024-25, against 1,507 the year before, a rise of 16.92 percent. The number of exporters rose 17.4 percent in the same period.

An authorisation count is not a rupee count. More permissions can accompany a small change in private export value, which is what the two private figures show. A founder comparing a single firm's order book with the national 12 percent will be comparing unlike things.

## Production share is a different signal

Exports and production do not split the same way. The export release shows private firms carrying most of the export rupees. A PIB note of 20 November 2025 says defence public sector undertakings and other public-sector units accounted for about 77 percent of production, and the private sector 23 percent. It says the private production share moved from 21 percent in FY 2023-24 to 23 percent in FY 2024-25.

Read together, the public record says private firms are the larger exporters and the smaller slice of recorded production. That is a useful market fact. It is not a claim that one side is ahead in technology, quality, or strategic weight. Those words are not in the releases.

## Country counts do not agree across pages

A later PIB text (PRID 2191937) says India supplied defence products to around 80 countries in 2024-25. The 20 November 2025 note says India exports to over 100 nations, and it names the United States, France, and Armenia. Another factsheet has named those three as the leading buyers in 2023-24.

Use the rupee table from PRID 2117348 when the question is value. Use a country sentence only with its date and its wording. "Around 80" and "over 100" are not a range to average.

## What a buyer or lender should do with this

Ask whether the exposure is private export work, a DPSU export programme, or domestic production for the services. The FY 2024-25 growth story is mostly the DPSU export line. The level story is that private firms already supplied about Rs 15,200 crore of exports in each of the two years. Great Indian Company separates those two readings so a pitch deck does not borrow the national growth rate for a flat line.
