# What Climate Adaptation Means for Investors in India

Investors mix three different public trails and then treat the blend as a fund. The documents separate them.

## Trail A is development spending

On 29 January 2026, the Press Information Bureau reported the Economic Survey 2025-26. The release says India's adaptation and resilience-related domestic spending rose from 3.7 percent of GDP in FY16 to 5.6 percent of GDP in FY22. The Survey, as reported that day, calls this a development-led strategy: adaptation advanced through ordinary public investment in core sectors. The series in that note ends at FY22. Extending it to a later year is not a reading of the release.

## Trail B is a closed project list

The National Adaptation Fund for Climate Change is much smaller, and it is a different object. A PIB release of 5 February 2018 says the fund was set up in 2015-16 as a Central Sector Scheme for concrete adaptation projects. Eligible sectors named there include agriculture, animal husbandry, water, forestry, and tourism. NABARD is the National Implementing Entity. A state prepares the proposal with NABARD, and the State Steering Committee on Climate Change must approve it.

On 5 August 2024, Minister of State Kirti Vardhan Singh told the Lok Sabha that 30 projects had been sanctioned in 27 States and Union Territories, including Andhra Pradesh, at a total project cost of ₹847.48 crore. Funds are released to NABARD in installments based on performance and the guidelines. The reply says the fund was made a non-scheme in November 2022. The ₹847.48 crore figure is sanctioned project cost as stated that day. It is not an annual budget, not a disbursement total, and not a pipeline of private returns.

## Trail C is one file with a visible gap

The same reply itemises an Andhra Pradesh dairy project, sanctioned on 16 August 2016 for ₹12,71,36,316, covering Anantapuramu, Nellore, and Vizianagaram, with a component on heat stress and cyclones for dairy animals. NABARD received ₹6,35,68,108 on 26 October 2016. It released ₹5,12,78,000 to the executing entity on 11 August 2017. Utilisation at that entity is printed as 228,49,000. The ministry lists delays in land identification and alienation, in choosing the civil and technical agencies, and in finalising a climate-resilient animal hostel. That is one project's public history. It is not a portfolio internal rate of return.

## What can be underwritten from these pages

A reader can underwrite a public grant path that is no longer described as a scheme, released on performance, in sectors a state has put into a project file. The ministry posts approved project documents on its [NAFCC list](https://moef.gov.in/index.php/list-of-approvals-projects-under-nafcc-concept-note-and-project-documents-details-dpr). These pages do not publish a private adaptation return. Site exposure to heat, flood, or the coast is an operating question for IMD warnings and NDMA guidelines, not a factsheet.

How the wide GDP share and the narrow project cost sit in a capex model is the [capex brief](/blog/climate-adaptation-capex-cycle-watch-makeinindia-20260330-375).
