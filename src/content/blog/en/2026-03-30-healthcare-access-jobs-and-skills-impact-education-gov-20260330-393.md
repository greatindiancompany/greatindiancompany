## The job is a team, not a single new cadre

India’s primary-care expansion is a change in what a sub-centre is supposed to do. The Ayushman Arogya Mandir about-page, under the health ministry, describes the sub-centre team as multi-purpose workers, male and female, and ASHAs, led by a mid-level health provider. That team is meant to deliver comprehensive primary care for a catchment of roughly 3,000 to 5,000 people, with time to care no more than 30 minutes. The page says some states had already upgraded sub-centres to additional primary health centres, and that those too convert.

A primary health centre linked to a cluster of mandirs is the first referral point. The medical officer there is responsible for comprehensive primary care across the cluster and at the centre itself. Staff numbers and qualifications at that centre stay, on this page, as Indian Public Health Standards define them. States may modify staffing for local need. Training named for the conversion covers medical officers, staff nurses, pharmacists, and lab technicians, plus a wellness room, information systems, and a stronger lab.

This is a skill shift inside posts that already existed, plus a mid-level provider at the sub-centre. It is not a claim about how many posts are filled. Vacancy is a state report. This brief does not invent a vacancy rate.

## Twelve service groups change the skill list

The mandir page lists twelve groups: pregnancy and childbirth; newborn and infant care; child and adolescent health; family planning and reproductive health; national programmes for communicable disease; common illnesses and minor ailments; non-communicable diseases; common eye and ENT problems; basic oral care; elderly and palliative care; emergency care; and screening and basic management of mental-health conditions. A worker hired for immunization and antenatal checks is now inside a wider protocol. Screening for hypertension and diabetes, which the ministry counted in crores of tests in its 25 March 2025 reply, is only the visible edge. Follow-up, adherence, and referral are the rest of the job.

The ministry reply of that date also records 5.06 crore wellness sessions, including yoga, as on 28 February 2025. Wellness sessions are a published activity. They are not a headcount of instructors, and they are not proof that the clinical team is in place on the day a patient arrives.

## District hospitals hold the specialist question

The National Health Policy, 2017, wants most secondary care at district level. Indian Public Health Standards 2022 set norms for district and sub-district hospitals as secondary-care facilities, and for community health centres below them. The standards text says implementation is the state’s, with technical support from the ministry, and it advises states to hire clinical and critical staff in-house rather than outsource that work the way security or laundry can be outsourced. The standards portal says compliance means a score of at least 80 percent, that states committed to that status in 50 percent of facilities by 2025–26, and that the Central Council endorsed compliance for all institutions by 2026.

If the district hospital lacks the specialist the standard names, the referral from a mandir has nowhere public to land. PM-JAY then becomes the path into an empanelled private hospital for a listed admission. Workforce at the district hospital is an access fact for patients who never see a private outpatient department. The [execution note](/blog/healthcare-access-execution-bottleneck-review-education-gov-20260330-593) follows that handoff.

## What an employer can and cannot assume

Performance-linked payments and flexible financing are principles on the mandir page. They are not a national wage schedule reproduced here. Community platforms and ASHAs remain part of the team, which means a large share of the work is outside a hospital roster. Private and non-profit partners are explicitly allowed on that page for gap-filling in primary care. A company selling staffing or training is inside a public design that still names public cadres first.

Digital records and ABHA, under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission launched in September 2021, add a recording skill. They do not replace the mid-level provider, the medical officer, or the district specialist. Buildings approved under PM-ABHIM, including critical care blocks, create posts only when a state recruits into them. The [opportunity note](/blog/healthcare-access-opportunity-landscape-education-gov-20260330-633) maps work that sits next to these roles.
