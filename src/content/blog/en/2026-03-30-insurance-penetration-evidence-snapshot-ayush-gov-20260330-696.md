# The Official Figures for India's Insurance Penetration

This page only repeats figures that [IRDAI's Annual Report 2024-25](https://irdai.gov.in/en/document-detail?documentId=8375620) or the [PIB note of 23 April 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2254950&lang=1&reg=3) prints. Where the two notes use different totals, both are shown. Nothing here is a derived ratio.

## Penetration and density

IRDAI's definitions, which PIB repeats: penetration is premium as a percentage of GDP. Density is premium per person.

| Measure | 2023-24 | 2024-25 | Source sentence |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Penetration, total | 3.7% | 3.7% | IRDAI annual report |
| Penetration, life | 2.8% | 2.7% | IRDAI annual report |
| Penetration, non-life | 1% | 1% | IRDAI annual report |
| Density, total | USD 95 | USD 97 | IRDAI annual report |
| Density, life | USD 70 | USD 72 | IRDAI annual report |
| Density, non-life | USD 25 | USD 25 | IRDAI annual report |

IRDAI says density has risen since 2016-17. GDP at current prices, from provisional National Statistical Office estimates cited by IRDAI, was ₹330.68 lakh crore in 2024-25 against ₹301.23 lakh crore in 2023-24, growth of 9.8 percent.

World 2024, as IRDAI cites Swiss Re: penetration 7.3 percent and density USD 943. Life 3 percent and USD 388. Non-life 4.3 percent and USD 555. India was the 10th largest market by nominal premium, with a 1.8 percent global share.

## Rupee scale

PIB's narrative for FY 2024-25: 41.84 crore policies, premium ₹11.93 lakh crore, claims paid ₹8.36 lakh crore, assets under management ₹74.44 lakh crore on 31 March 2025. Life is about 74 percent of premium and 91 percent of assets. Health is 41 percent of non-life gross domestic premium, ahead of motor. Seventy-four insurers are operating.

PIB's growth table, which ends at ₹11.90 lakh crore rather than ₹11.93 lakh crore:

| Premium income, ₹ lakh crore | FY 2020-21 | FY 2024-25 | Growth printed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Total | 8.30 | 11.90 | 43.37% |
| Life | 6.30 | 8.86 | 40.63% |
| Non-life | 2.02 | 3.10 | 53.46% |

Insurance and pension funds, which PIB attributes to the Economic Survey 2025-26, were 29.6 percent of household financial assets in FY 2024-25, against 28.6 percent in FY 2018-19.

## Distribution

PIB: offices 21,338 in March 2024 and 22,076 in March 2025. Distribution network about 48 lakh in FY 2020-21 and nearly 83 lakh in FY 2024-25, counting agents, point-of-sale persons, and institutional partners.

IRDAI agent stocks on 31 March 2025, which are not the same as that 83 lakh network: life individual agents 31.23 lakh (appointed 11.15 lakh and terminated 8.87 lakh during the year); general-insurer agents 7,73,107; stand-alone health agents 15.49 lakh.

IRDAI offices on 31 March 2025: life 12,210; general insurers 8,078; stand-alone health 1,788. The general, health, and specialised table total is 9,866. Added to life offices, that is the 22,076 PIB prints.

## Capital rules, dated

The Department of Financial Services [insurance overview](https://financialservices.gov.in/beta/en/page/insurance-overview) records FDI in Indian insurers at 49 percent from 2015 and 74 percent from the 2021 amendment, and 100 percent for intermediaries from 2019. PIB records the 2025 amendment raising the insurer cap from 74 percent to 100 percent. Insurer counts on the DFS page are as on 31 March 2021: 67 insurers. Do not merge that census with PIB's 74.
