# EPFO payroll and e-Shram describe different women’s labour markets

A single phrase such as “women entering formal work” usually mixes two official registers that the Government itself keeps apart. The Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation records subscribers in covered establishments. The e-Shram portal records unorganised workers who are not those subscribers. The Periodic Labour Force Survey records activity, including self-employment and casual labour, whether or not either register has the person.

## What EPFO is actually counting

PIB, drawing on EPFO payroll, states that during 2024–25, 26.9 lakh net female subscribers were added. In July 2025, about 2.80 lakh new female subscribers joined and the net female payroll addition was about 4.42 lakh. Over the same year, EPFO added over 1.29 crore net subscribers in total. Net payroll is new members, exits, and rejoins. It is a flow into a social-security account. It is not a headcount of all women who worked that year, and it is not a measure of labour force participation.

The establishment covered by the EPF Act is a narrower set than the Indian workforce. A rise in women subscribers is evidence about covered, contributory employment. It supports a statement that more women are being enrolled in that system. It does not, by itself, support a statement about rural self-employment or about unpaid work.

## What e-Shram is actually counting

The Ministry of Labour and Employment launched e-Shram in 2021 as an Aadhaar-seeded database of unorganised workers, each with a Universal Account Number. PIB’s five-year note reports 31.89 crore registrations. Women account for 54.28 percent and men for 45.72 percent. The age profile, given as on 20 August 2026, shows 55.21 percent of registered workers aged 18–40.

Eligibility is the part that keeps the two signals from being added together. A worker must be 16 or older and in the unorganised sector, and must not be an income-tax payer or a member of EPFO or ESIC. A woman who appears in the 26.9 lakh EPFO net addition is outside e-Shram by rule. A woman who is 54 percent of e-Shram is outside the EPFO stock by the same rule. The female majority on e-Shram is a fact about the unorganised register. The EPFO flow is a fact about covered payroll. Summing them double-counts nothing only because the eligibility wall is there; using one as a proxy for the other crosses that wall.

## Where PLFS puts both signals in proportion

On usual status for calendar 2025, MoSPI estimates 20.0 crore women aged 15 and above were employed, out of 61.6 crore workers. Among women workers, 64.2 percent were self-employed and 18.2 percent were in regular wage or salaried employment. In rural areas, 9.3 percent of women workers were in regular wage employment and 70.7 percent were self-employed. In urban areas, 50.9 percent of women workers were in regular wage employment.

EPFO can only draw from the slice of work that covered establishments employ. That slice is closer to urban regular-wage employment than to rural self-employment. e-Shram is built for the complementary slice, and it refuses EPFO members at the door. A market note that quotes 54 percent women on e-Shram and 26.9 lakh women on EPFO in the same sentence needs both definitions in the sentence.

Self-employment inside PLFS also includes helpers in household enterprises. That category is paid in the survey’s economic-activity sense and still sits far from a provident-fund enrolment. The 2025 press note does not give the all-India helper share in the summary. It does show that self-employment remains the majority status for women workers, especially in rural areas.

## How to read a monthly payroll print

A July net addition of about 4.42 lakh women is a month inside a flow. The 2024–25 total of 26.9 lakh is the year. Neither number is a participation rate. The participation rate MoSPI publishes for 2025 is 40.0 percent of women aged 15 and above on usual status, and 34.3 percent on the current weekly status. Payroll can rise while participation is described as stable, because payroll is enrolment in one scheme and participation is a population rate.
