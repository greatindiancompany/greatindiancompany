# How Founders Should Read State Capacity Before a State Bid

A state tender, a municipal pilot or a scheme integration fails in ways a pitch deck does not show. The public record lets a founder check five things before the bid: whether the service sits on one portal, whether the city's layer is real, whether the state's capital cash is between installments, whether the finance stack is being rebuilt this year, and whether the officers who must adopt the product are on a learning system that can see them.

## Start with the portal the citizen already has

DARPG's December 2025 NeSDA Way Forward report, published by PIB on 22 January 2026, is the list to open. It is state-uploaded data as of 31 December 2025. If the product replaces a department website, ask whether that state is among the unified portals named at 100 percent of services: Assam's Sewa Setu, Jammu and Kashmir's e-UNNAT, Karnataka's Seva Sindhu, Odisha One, Madhya Pradesh's MP e-Service, Uttarakhand's Apuni Sarkar, Kerala's e-Sevanam. Four more are named above 90 percent: Chandigarh and Delhi's e-District portals, Saral Haryana, and Rajasthan's e-Mitra. Integrating beside a portal that already carries the catalogue is a different sale from becoming the catalogue.

If the product is a local licence, a utility or a municipal payment, use the other number in the same release: 8,656 of 24,090 e-services are in local governance and utilities, and the release says citizen contact is largely municipal. It points at city portals in Puducherry and Guwahati as examples, which is a hint to ask for the city system by name. The NeSDA 2025 round, launched 25 May 2026, will score city portals on tracking, integration, security and open data. A demo that cannot show request status will look thinner after that assessment than it does in a conference room.

## Ask which SASCI gate the payment sits behind

The 27 March 2026 guidelines allocate Rs 2,00,000 crore as 50-year interest-free loans, above the borrowing ceiling, to be spent in 2026-27. The first untied installment is 66 percent. The second is 34 percent, and it waits on 75 percent use of the first, closure of Centrally Sponsored Scheme nodal accounts, and a PFMS utilization certificate signed by the finance secretary. States are told to pick at most five sectors. If the bid is outside those five, ask what happens to it. If the bid is inside them, ask whether the first installment has been utilized far enough to unlock the second. The Reserve Bank's 23 January 2026 release is the reason this is not a minor cash-management detail: the state deficit printed at 3.3 percent of GDP largely because of these loans. Budgets can show the project. Cash moves when the certificate moves.

Part IV is the question for anyone selling treasury, works or procurement software. The state can earn Rs 50 crore a component for IFMIS modules, a works system, e-procurement, live integration and full digitisation of drawing officers and treasuries, after the Comptroller and Auditor General looks at a report due by 15 December 2026. Price the implementation. Do not price the incentive. The incentive is the state's, and it may be spent on any capital project.

## Grievance volume is a product signal

CPGRAMS is open 24 hours, connected to ministries and states, and it lets a citizen appeal a poor closure. If users of the product already complain there, the June 2026 central report is the baseline: a 14-day average disposal time in central ministries for 2026, and 78,793 cases still pending at the end of June. A state right-to-service deadline may also apply, and as of the 10 December 2025 parliamentary reply those state systems were not API-linked to CPGRAMS. Build for both clocks, or be explicit about which one you serve.

## The officer is part of the deployment

iGOT had more than 1.51 crore users as on 6 March 2026, and MoUs with 29 states and UTs. The same reply says district-level learning is not monitored on the platform. A rollout that depends on block or municipal staff cannot cite a state enrolment total as proof those staff are trained. Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh are the two states the reply quantifies, at 7,46,135 and 9,36,951 registered users. Everywhere else, ask the department for its own completion record.
