# The Frontline Workforce Behind India’s Social Outcomes

India’s social outcomes are delivered by named cadres, not by a headline employment rate. Three official counts matter more than a generic “jobs in the social sector” line: Anganwadi workers on the Poshan Tracker, teachers in UDISE+, and households drawing a rural wage under the Act that replaced MGNREGA. The Periodic Labour Force Survey then says what the wider labour market is doing. It does not say how many of those workers are inside these schemes.

## The nutrition cadre is counted, and it is being retrained

As of 31 July 2026 the Press Information Bureau reported nearly 13.99 lakh Anganwadi centres and 13.30 lakh Anganwadi workers, reaching almost 8.94 crore beneficiaries. That worker is the person who opens the centre, marks attendance, serves the hot cooked meal or issues take-home ration, and records growth. A nutrition outcome that assumes a different user — a parent app with no worker in the loop — is designed against the system the ministry actually runs.

The skill demand on that cadre is specific. A March 2026 ministry reply on the Poshan Tracker said facial recognition is used to confirm take-home ration reaches the registered beneficiary, and that National e-Governance Division coordinators train workers on it. Under Poshan Bhi Padhai Bhi, the Savitribai Phule National Institute of Women and Child Development had trained 3,901 state-level master trainers as of 18 March 2026, who then train workers. The September 2026 Poshan Maah note adds a revised home-visit scheduler with 34 developmental milestones for children aged 0-3, and the Navchetna and Aadharshila curricula for early childhood care. The job is nutrition plus early stimulation plus a biometric distribution step. Vacancy and training coverage beyond the master-trainer figure are not stated in these notes.

Where five or more children under five are present at a VB-G RAM G worksite, the rural employment Act requires a woman worker, paid at the wage rate, to look after them. That is a small, explicit join between the wage programme and childcare.

## Teachers are more numerous, and more of them are women

UDISE+ 2025-26 counts 1,02,73,020 teachers, up 8.3% from 2022-23. Women are 54.9% of teachers. Girls are 48.4% of enrolment. Pupil-teacher ratios are 10 at foundational, 12 at preparatory, 17 at middle, and 21 at secondary, which the ministry sets against the National Education Policy reference of 30:1. Single-teacher schools still number 1,00,843. The staffing outcome that remains is concentration: the average ratio can look comfortable while a lakh schools still have one teacher.

UDISE+ does not report a learning score. A skills claim about students has to wait for an assessment the Ministry of Education publishes as such. What this release supports is a statement about adult employment in schools and about whether children stay: secondary retention is 51.9%.

## Rural wage work changed statute in the middle of the year

From 1 July 2026 the household entitlement is 125 days under VB-G RAM G, at a notified wage that is nowhere below ₹300 and averages ₹327.4. Existing e-KYC job cards stay valid until the new Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Card is issued. The ministry told the Lok Sabha on 11 August 2026 that more than 9.69 crore person-days had been generated in about the first month. That is demand absorbed under the new Act. It is not a full-year household average, and it should not be added to FY 2025-26 MGNREGS totals as if one programme ran uninterrupted on one definition.

Field roles the May 2026 note names — programme officers, mates, and Gram Rozgar Sevaks — are why administrative expenditure rose from 6 percent to 9 percent. The skill at that layer is muster rolls, face authentication with an offline exception, and works chosen in a Gram Sabha under four asset themes.

## The wider labour market is a different series

The August 2026 PLFS monthly bulletin, current weekly status, persons aged 15 and above, was released on 15 September 2026 from a sample of 3,70,160 persons. Labour force participation was 55.6%. The worker population ratio was 52.8%. Unemployment was 5.0% overall, 4.1% rural, and 6.8% urban. Female participation was 34.8% overall, 39.4% rural, and 25.4% urban. The urban female rate is lower than in August 2025 (26.1%). Rural female participation is higher than a year earlier (37.4%).

Scheme employment can rise while the all-India rate barely moves, because PLFS covers the whole economy and the schemes cover registered cadres and households that ask for work. Use PLFS for the market. Use the Tracker, UDISE+, and the rural development replies for the cadres.
