A job in Indian aviation or tourism is attached to a licence and a shift. Hiring for the sector without that licence leaves the cancelled flight or the monument queue where it was. This page states no employment total and no wage.

## Work that already has a name

- **Pilots.** A commercial pilot licence or an airline transport pilot licence, and the type rating for that aircraft, under Directorate General of Civil Aviation rules. A training advertisement is not a signature in the logbook. Without a type-rated captain the aircraft stays.
- **Cabin crew.** Approved under the operator's civil aviation requirements. They are not a hotel front desk with a different uniform.
- **Aircraft maintenance.** A maintenance licence under the civil aviation licensing rules, inside an organisation approved for that aircraft. The person who can sign the release is the constraint. A general engineering degree is not that signature.
- **Air traffic control.** Controllers are trained for the Airports Authority of India's air navigation service. A new terminal does not staff the night shift.
- **Airside work.** Ground handling, load control, and security screening under Bureau of Civil Aviation Security standards. These permissions stop at the fence. The driver at the kerb is a different job.
- **Hotels.** The arrival shift, the kitchen, and housekeeping. The kitchen is a food business with its own licence. The skill that moves a sold night is coverage on the day the flight bank arrives.
- **Guides and naturalists.** A guide a protected monument will accept, and a naturalist a forest department will admit to a park, hold different permissions. A language certificate is not either document.

## Where the skill gates the trip

Ask which departure or which check-in failed for lack of a named role. On a thin regional base the missing person is often a type-rated pilot or a licensed engineer in that town. At a metro it can be a controller, a security lane, or a handler who can turn the aircraft inside the slot. At a destination it can be a guide the monument will take on the festival day. A course aimed at a different licence will not add the departure.
