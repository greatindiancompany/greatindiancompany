## Education and the external definition

DIKSHA, on its about page: launched in 2017, used by almost all states and by CBSE, 36 languages, built on Sunbird. The National Digital Education Architecture was launched on 29 July 2021. The page does not give a current learner count. This ledger does not invent one.

The G20 New Delhi Leaders' Declaration of 9 September 2023 describes digital public infrastructure as shared digital systems, used by public and private actors, that can be built on open standards and can deliver services at societal scale. The ministers' framework of 19 August 2023 names three functions: identification, payments, and data sharing with consent where applicable. The framework is voluntary.

## Data-protection calendar

G.S.R. 843(E), 13 November 2025: part of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 from that date; section 6(9) and section 27(1)(d) one year later; the main operative sections, as listed in the notification, eighteen months later. G.S.R. 844(E) establishes the Data Protection Board from the publication date, with its head office in the National Capital Region. The Rules, 2025 were published on 14 November 2025.

If a later official print replaces any cell above, the replacement should carry its own date. The cell should not be edited into a timeless "current" number.
