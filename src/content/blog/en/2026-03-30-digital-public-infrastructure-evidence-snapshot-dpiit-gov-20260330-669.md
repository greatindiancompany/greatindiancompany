# India DPI Figures in the 6 March 2026 Public Note

## How to read the tables

Every figure below is taken from the Press Information Bureau research note "India's Digital Public Infrastructure," dated 6 March 2026, unless a row says otherwise. The as-of date is often earlier than 6 March. This page was updated on 23 September 2026. That edit does not replace any cell.

Digital India, launched on 1 July 2015, is the MeitY programme frame. India Stack Global is the showcase of named platforms. Neither page is the source of the counts in the tables.

## A second source, not in the PIB note

The Department of Financial Services account-aggregator page gives progress as of 31 March 2026 and credits Sahamati: 17 licensed aggregators, 179 providers live, 989 users live, more than 2.88 billion accounts enabled to share, and 284.6 million accounts linked. Those cells are not from the 6 March note.

## What the tables refuse to do

They do not rank states, name a private app's share of UPI, or state a capital budget for the whole stack. The only programme outlay in the note, among the rows above, is the e-Courts figure.
