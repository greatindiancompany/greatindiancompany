# India inflation scoreboard for August 2026

Three official numbers describe Indian prices in August 2026, and they answer different questions. Retail inflation is a household index. Wholesale inflation is a producer and trader index. The policy projection is the path the Monetary Policy Committee published before the August prints existed.

## Retail: CPI, base 2024=100

Source: MoSPI press release dated 14 September 2026. August is provisional. July is final. [CPI release](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1789381904344_6c792dcf-8a9f-4fca-93d3-99d833bdb358_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_August_2026.pdf).

| Measure | July 2026 (final) | August 2026 (provisional) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPI combined | 4.45% | 4.82% |
| CPI rural | 4.84% | 5.23% |
| CPI urban | 3.96% | 4.31% |
| Consumer Food Price Index, combined | 5.52% | 5.95% |
| Food, rural | 5.79% | 6.13% |
| Food, urban | 5.05% | 5.64% |
| Combined CPI index | 107.95 | 108.74 |

Combined index a year earlier, August 2025, was 103.74. The August 2026 index is 108.74.

Price collection that month covered 1,407 urban markets, including online markets, and 1,465 villages. MoSPI reported a 100% response rate in both rural and urban markets. The September CPI is scheduled for 12 October 2026.

## Inside the retail basket

Combined division inflation in August, from the same release:

| Division | Inflation |
| --- | --- |
| Food and beverages | 5.66% |
| Transport | 4.60% |
| Clothing and footwear | 3.56% |
| Education services | 3.73% |
| Housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels | 2.61% |
| Health | 1.34% |
| Restaurants and accommodation services | 8.38% |
| Personal care, social protection and miscellaneous goods and services | 15.17% |

Two groups inside those divisions are easy to misread if only the division rate is used. “Electricity, gas and other fuels” was 3.09%. “Operation of personal transport equipment,” the group that carries petrol and diesel in household use, was 7.40%. “Other personal effects,” where jewellery sits, was 44.56%.

High item rates in August included silver jewellery at 107.11% (weight 0.3127), ginger at 73.82%, onion at 48.27%, garlic at 43.60%, and gold, diamond, and platinum jewellery at 35.53%. Low rates included tomato at −31.09%, potato at −13.14%, and motor car and jeep at −6.72%.

## Wholesale: WPI, base 2022–23

Source: provisional estimates released 14 September 2026 and carried by the Press Information Bureau. [WPI press note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2309977). | Group | Weight | July 2026 | August 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All commodities | 100 | 9.78% | 9.92% |
| Primary articles | 22.76 | 8.52% | 7.76% |
| Fuel and power | 14.11 | 20.05% | 22.93% |
| Manufactured products | 63.13 | 8.29% | 8.37% |
| WPI food index | 24.99 | 6.65% | 7.05% |

The all-commodities index was 110.8 in August and 110.0 in July. Mineral oils were 38.48%. The August provisional estimate used a weighted response rate of 84.4%. The June index was later finalised at 110.3, and June inflation was revised from 9.87% provisional to 9.97% final.

## Policy anchor and the latest projection

The Reserve Bank’s Annual Report for the year, dated 29 May 2026, records that the Gazette notification of 25 March 2026 kept the target as **headline CPI at 4%**, with an upper tolerance of 6% and a lower tolerance of 2%, from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031. [Annual Report, monetary policy chapter](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx?Id=1463).

The MPC resolution of 5 August 2026, issued before the August CPI and WPI, projects CPI inflation for 2026–27 at **5.0%**: Q2 at 4.7%, Q3 at 5.9%, Q4 at 5.5%, and Q1 of 2027–28 at 5.3%, with risks evenly balanced. Core inflation, defined there as CPI excluding food and fuel, is projected at 4.3% for 2026–27. The repo rate was left at 5.25%. [MPC resolution](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63287).

MoSPI’s August release does not publish that core rate. The 3.9% core figure in the resolution refers to May–June, not to August.

## How to use the card

Quote 4.82% as provisional combined CPI. Quote 9.92% as provisional WPI. Quote 5.0% as the committee’s year projection from 5 August, which the August data had not yet entered. The month-by-month retail path is in the companion note on [what changed through August](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/inflation-trends-what-changed-20260330-004).
