State logistics in India has crossed a paperwork threshold. It has not, in the latest parliamentary stocktake, been reduced to a map of who moves freight well.

On 11 August 2026 the Ministry of Commerce and Industry told the Lok Sabha that 29 states and Union Territories have formulated their own logistics policies, and that 32 have granted industry status to the logistics sector. Both sentences are in the [reply to Starred Question 327](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf). The reply does not name the 29, the states that have a policy without industry status, or the ones that granted industry status without a full policy. Those lists are the next document a state-facing investor should demand. They are not in the answer.

## What changed since the launch note

The [cabinet note of 21 September 2022](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/policy-introduces-unified-logistics-interface-platform-standardization-monitoring-framework-and-skill-development-for-greater-efficiency-in-logistics-services/) said all states and Union Territories had been onboarded, fourteen states had developed logistics policies on the lines of the national policy, and thirteen had drafts. The August 2026 reply says 29 have formulated policies.

That is a real widening of the policy book. It is not a clean subtraction. “Developed” in 2022 and “formulated” in 2026 may not be the same test, and the 2022 note counted states while the 2026 reply counts states and Union Territories. Do not announce “fifteen new policies” from those two sentences. Do say that the Centre’s own papers moved from a launch-era minority of notified state policies to a claimed majority.

Industry status is separate. It can change how a warehouse or a fleet is treated in a state industrial policy: land, power, incentives, and sometimes the department that answers the phone. The August reply asserts that 32 jurisdictions have granted it. It does not say the fiscal terms are alike. A founder comparing two states still has to read the state notification, not the Lok Sabha count.

## The comparison tool, and why this page will not rank anyone

The same reply says Logistics Ease Across Different States has been institutionalized to assess states and Union Territories across logistics domains. LEADS is the official pattern tool. This brief does not reprint an old edition’s ranks and call them current. An edition from 2021 or 2023 answers the year on its cover. If you are choosing a park site in 2026, ask for the latest LEADS report by name and year, then read the pillar that matches your problem: infrastructure, services, the regulatory interface, or the operating enablers. A composite crown is a poor reason to site a cold store.

Gati Shakti’s state layer is wider than logistics policy. The [written reply of 11 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2297818) says all 36 states and Union Territories are on the master plan, and that states have been encouraged to use it for district planning. About 1,800 data layers were integrated nationally. A state can be fully onboarded and still have a missing approach road. Onboarding is a data-sharing fact. District planning is a use fact. The reply does not score either one state by state.

## What actually varies on the ground

The pattern that matters to freight is local and physical. National highway and port-link projects are still sanctioned by the owning ministry. The roads ministry’s [Bharatmala](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala) programme is a national corridor scheme that happens to cross states; it is not a state logistics policy. A state policy can name a logistics park, a truck terminal, or a single-window cell. It cannot, by itself, put a project into the “under implementation” column of the Network Planning Group.

Use the 29 and the 32 as coverage facts. Use LEADS as the named comparison, once you have the edition. Use the state notification for industry status before you model an incentive. Leave the colour-coded championship map alone until the government prints one you can date.
