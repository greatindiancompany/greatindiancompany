# The Railway Capex Cycle: Track, Doubling, and the Metro Budget

The capex cycle in Indian rail is visible in kilometres commissioned, not in a single budget speech. The Ministry of Railways has published the pace, the sanctioned book, and the spend against that book. Metro spending is a different line.

## Commissioning sped up, and the book is still larger than the work done

In the Rajya Sabha reply of 7 August 2026, new track commissioned in 2009-14 was 7,599 km, an average of 4.2 km a day. From 2014 to 2026 the figure was 36,429 km, an average of 8.32 km a day. That is the pace claim.

The stock of work still open was stated as on 1 April 2026. Across Indian Railways, 514 projects were sanctioned, covering 40,000 km at about ₹8.31 lakh crore. Commissioned length to March 2026 was 12,583 km. Expenditure to March 2026 was ₹3,05,253 crore. The category split in that reply:

| Category | Projects | Length (km) | Commissioned to March 2026 (km) | Spend to March 2026 (₹ crore) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New lines | 169 | 16,634 | 3,361 | 1,57,572 |
| Gauge conversion | 29 | 3,987 | 3,045 | 24,227 |
| Doubling | 316 | 19,379 | 6,177 | 1,23,454 |
| Total | 514 | 40,000 | 12,583 | 3,05,253 |

Gauge conversion is the furthest along. New lines and doubling still have most of their sanctioned length ahead. A cycle watcher who quotes only 8.32 km a day will miss that the open book is dominated by doubling and new lines.

The 1 April 2026 reply adds track renewal of about 54,600 km during 2014-26, up to February 2026. Renewal is maintenance of the existing railway. It is not the same statistic as new track.

## Budget support, and what this year has already spent

Gross budgetary support in Budget Estimates 2026-27 is ₹2,78,030 crore, of which ₹79,072 crore is for construction of new tracks. Estimated safety-related expenditure for 2026-27 is ₹1,20,389 crore, including ₹46,189 crore for track safety. These are parallel disclosures, not a sum.

Station works sit under Plan Head 53, Customer Amenities. For 2025-26 the revised estimate was ₹12,120 crore, with expenditure of ₹11,892 crore up to February 2026. For 2026-27 the 23 July 2026 reply gives an allocation of ₹11,972 crore and expenditure of ₹4,633 crore up to June 2026. The scheme covers 1,338 identified stations, of which 157 are in aspirational districts. Modernisation work had been completed at 208 stations as of the April release.

## The metro budget is not inside this book

The 15 March 2026 PIB note places the annual metro budget at ₹29,550 crore in 2025-26, against ₹5,798 crore in 2013-14, and the post-2014 sanction book at 38 projects, 1,051 km, and ₹3.44 lakh crore. That money is reported with the urban affairs programme. It does not appear in the 514-project railway table.
