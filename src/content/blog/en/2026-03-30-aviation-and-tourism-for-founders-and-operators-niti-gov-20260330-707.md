Operate the licence you hold. A scheduled airline, a supported regional route, an airside service, and a hotel or circuit desk do not belong in the same weekly meeting. This page is a way to run that week. It is not a case study, and it names no company as a model.

## Pick one operation

- **Scheduled flying.** The weekly facts are departures completed, the reason for each cancellation, crew legal to fly, aircraft released to service, and cash paid to the lessor and the airport. The air operator certificate is the boundary. Do not add a leisure package to this meeting.
- **A UDAN or other thin route.** Add the award's end date, how the capped-fare seats sold, and the load in the lean week. If the town works in one season only, the plan has to say where the aircraft goes for the rest of the year.
- **Airside service.** Handling, a security programme, or a maintenance release. The customer is an airline protecting a slot. A passenger application is not the product unless that airline is paying you for the turn.
- **A stay or a circuit.** Rooms or vehicles sold against flights that exist, permits you do not issue, and domestic guests kept separate from foreign guests. A star classification or a state listing is optional. The fire licence and the refund are not.

## The weekly page

One page is enough: what flew or what was occupied, what was cancelled and why, which permission expires before the season turns, and which bill fails if this week repeats. Use the [90-day checklist](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-next-90-days-checklist-niti-gov-20260330-467) once a month so the week does not hide an expiring slot or a festival you have not staffed.

Hire the licence that is failing, not a general tourism title. Those licences are mapped on the [jobs page](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-jobs-and-skills-impact-niti-gov-20260330-387). If the same stall repeats for a month, stop adding upstream capacity and read the [bottleneck review](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-execution-bottleneck-review-niti-gov-20260330-587).

Leave national passenger totals out of the operating review. They cannot tell you whether your Friday flight left.
