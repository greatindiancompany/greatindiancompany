# What the water missions actually say about jobs and skills

The Jal Jeevan Mission has one widely repeated employment figure, and it is not a payroll. The Cabinet press note of 10 March 2026 says that a study by IIM Bangalore and the International Labour Organization estimated 59.9 lakh direct and 2.2 crore indirect person-years of potential employment from the mission. Person-years are a volume of work. They are not filled posts, they are not annual jobs in any one year, and the note presents them as an estimate from those institutions.

The same paragraph cites other estimates: SBI Research on women no longer fetching water, a World Health Organization estimate on hours and diarrhoeal deaths, and an estimate associated with Michael Kremer on under-five mortality. Those are impact estimates published inside a press note. This brief uses the person-year sentence only as what it is, an estimate the Cabinet note chose to quote. It does not add a headcount the ministries have not published.

## The institutions that have to know how to run a scheme

JJM 2.0 names the bodies that hold the skill requirement, even though it does not count their members.

The Village Water and Sanitation Committee, with the Gram Panchayat, receives a completed scheme through Jal Arpan. The 6 August 2026 note lists that handover as a reform. A committee that cannot read a scheme, keep a pump running, or record a complaint cannot perform the role the reform assigns.

The Gram Panchayat certifies Har Ghar Jal only after the state has established in-village operation and maintenance. The skill is split. The state has to build the maintenance arrangement. The panchayat has to recognise that the arrangement exists before it declares the village finished. That is a different skill from laying pipe.

Jal Utsav is the annual community review the Cabinet note asks villages to hold. Jal Seva Aankalan is the community-led assessment named in August. Both assume local people can look at a working system and say whether it is still working. Water-quality surveillance is named as part of the JJM 2.0 shift. The Cabinet note does not publish a number of laboratory technicians or a training target attached to that surveillance.

The 13 March 2026 meeting with state ministers put the same point in programme language: the next phase is quality of infrastructure, operation and maintenance, and Jan Bhagidari. The mission directorate was explaining fund-release rules to the state departments that employ the engineers. The skilled cadre the text actually points at is inside those departments and inside the village committees, not in a national jobs dashboard.

## Sanitation skills are implied by the waste task

Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) Phase II asks villages to keep open-defecation-free status and to arrange solid and liquid waste. A PIB feature of 17 September 2026 says 1,314 GOBARdhan biogas plants were operational in 582 districts, and that 98,273 household biogas plants had been constructed, alongside 12.22 crore individual household toilets. Plants of that kind need operators. The feature does not say how many people are employed to run them.

A training market that quotes the IIM-ILO person-years as "60 lakh jobs created" is changing the unit. A training market that starts from Jal Arpan, village waste arrangements, and sewage-plant operation is at least aimed at tasks the programmes name.
