# What the PLI employment count actually includes

PLI employment figures are real official numbers and a poor substitute for a workforce plan. The count, the sector table, and the skills programmes named alongside them answer different questions.

## The headline and the table

The 28 July 2026 reply to [Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1423](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1423_Rd4xWf.pdf) says that as on 31 March 2026 the schemes had generated employment of over 14.15 lakh people, direct and indirect, of which 8.4 lakh is direct.

The 21 July 2026 annexure, [PIB PRID 2287008](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287008), lists "cumulative employment till March 2026" by sector and sums to 8,49,069. A footnote says indirect employment of 5.66 lakh has been reported under three sectors only: large-scale electronics, IT hardware, and solar PV modules. Adding 8,49,069 and 5.66 lakh lands near the 14.15 lakh headline. That addition is a way to read the footnote. It is not labelled as such in the note. The parliamentary phrase "8.4 lakh" direct and the annexure total of 8,49,069 are close and not identical. Cite the sentence you are using.

An earlier official sentence points the other way. On 27 March 2026 the Rajya Sabha was told that as on 31 December 2025 employment was more than 14.39 lakh, direct and indirect ([PIB PRID 2246089](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246089)). The later stock is lower. These pages do not explain the revision. A jobs chart that shows only the March number hides that.

## Where the annexure column sits

The largest lines in that 8,49,069 column are food products at 3,29,200, large-scale electronics at 1,69,249, and pharmaceutical drugs at 1,14,880. Automobiles and auto components are 67,820. White goods, the scheme the annexure assigns to DPIIT, are 52,703. Solar PV modules, the largest investment line at ₹64,873 crore, are 14,794 in this column, before the indirect footnote. ACC batteries are 1,245. Drones are 2,650.

Food processing is the employment story inside the table. Solar and batteries are not. A state that announces "PLI jobs" without the sector is not saying which of these it means.

The corridor programme has a separate estimate. The 30 August 2024 note, [PIB PRID 2050136](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2050136), says the National Industrial Corridor Development Programme is expected to create an estimated 1 million direct jobs and up to 3 million indirect jobs. Expected is not the same word as generated. Do not add those estimates to the 14.15 lakh.

## Skills are named, not measured here

The 28 July reply, answering a question on MSMEs, lists Ministry of MSME programmes that include skilling: the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme, tool rooms and technology centres, PM Vishwakarma, and the Micro and Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme, among others. It does not publish a count of people trained against PLI sectors, a vacancy rate, or a wage. DPIIT's Annual Report 2024-25 discusses Make in India and employability as a reason for choosing 24 sub-sectors. It does not, in the passages used here, publish a skill-gap survey for those sub-sectors.

A founder hiring technicians for a compressor line, a solar module line, or a food plant will not find the wage or the training seat in the PLI employment table. The table can tell you which schemes have reported headcount. It cannot tell you whether your city has the people.
