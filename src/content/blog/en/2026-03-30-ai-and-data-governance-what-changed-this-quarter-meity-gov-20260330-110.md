The quarter that ended for this reading on 24 September 2026 did not produce a new Digital Personal Data Protection commencement. The dates fixed on 13 November 2025 are still the dates. What the government did publish, in July and August, is a fresher stocktake of the IndiaAI Mission.

## The statute did not get a new start date

G.S.R. 843(E), in the Gazette of 13 November 2025, brought some provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in on publication, a smaller set one year later, and the main duties of a Data Fiduciary eighteen months later. Counting from that gazette, those later dates fall on 13 November 2026 and 13 May 2027. Nothing located for 1 July through 24 September 2026 replaces that notification.

On 20 July 2026 the National e-Governance Division held an awareness session for MeitY officials on the Act and the Rules. That is a training session at Electronics Niketan. It is not a gazette.

MeitY's document list still shows a corrigendum to the Rules dated 16 December 2025. That corrigendum is outside this quarter. Open it before treating the 13 November wording as the last edit. This brief does not restate what the corrigendum changed.

## What the July and August notes do change

Three official notes in the quarter update the IndiaAI picture. They do not all use the same rupee figure, and they should not be averaged.

On 24 July 2026 the Minister for Electronics and Information Technology told the Rajya Sabha, in a reply PIB carried on 27 July, that the Mission was approved with an outlay of ₹10,371 crore over five years. The same reply records 13 Responsible AI projects, 58 AI Centres of Excellence, and 27 India Data and AI Labs under the Safe and Trusted AI work.

On 5 August 2026 the Minister of State told the Lok Sabha, published by PIB the next day, that the Mission was approved on 7 March 2024 with a total outlay of Rs. 10,371.92 crore over five years. That is the figure to cite when the rupee needs a paisa. The July reply had rounded it. The same Lok Sabha reply also records Semicon 2.0, approved by the Cabinet on 15 July 2026 with an outlay of Rs. 1,27,500 crore. That is a semiconductor programme. It is not a data-protection amendment.

PIB's backgrounder of 13 August 2026, "Powering the Future," then gives the operating stock:

- Shared compute of 45,000+ GPUs, as of June 2026.
- By August 2026, 237 projects had used subsidised compute, covering 93.18 lakh GPU hours.
- As of July 2026, AIKosh held over 14,000 datasets and 331 AI models.
- From 506 applications, 20 indigenous foundation-model proposals had been selected, of which 12 are large multimodal models and 8 are small language models.
- As of August 2026, IndiaAI initiatives had developed 62 prototypes and deployed 20 solutions in public institutions.
- As of August 2026, 58 AI Centres of Excellence had been approved across States and Union Territories, and 22 had been initiated across 13 States and Union Territories. The note does not name the 13.
- As of July 2026, 13 Responsible AI projects, covering bias, machine unlearning, deepfake detection, privacy-preserving tools, explainability, and risk assessment.

Earlier GPU prints are smaller: 38,231 GPUs from 14 providers in the Lok Sabha reply placed on PIB as on 25 March 2026, and "more than 38,000" in the 13 March 2026 note, which also said a further 20,000 were under process. The June figure of 45,000+ is a later snapshot. Do not add 38,231 and 20,000 and call the sum the June stock.

## What a reader should do with the quarter

If the question is "did my DPDP duty start this quarter?", the answer in the gazette is no. Consent, notice, security safeguards, children's data, cross-border transfer, Significant Data Fiduciary obligations, and the penalty chapters are in the eighteen-month set. They are still ahead of 24 September 2026.

If the question is "what public AI capacity did the government report?", use the 13 August backgrounder for the June and July and August counts, and the 6 August Lok Sabha note for the Cabinet outlay of Rs. 10,371.92 crore. The [execution note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-execution-bottleneck-review-meity-gov-20260330-590) separates the press line "full operationalisation" from the gazette.
