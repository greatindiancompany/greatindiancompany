The next 90 days, counted from 24 September 2026, will not reveal a secret freight plan. They are enough time to check your operation against documents that are already public. Nothing below is a predicted circular. If a ministry issues one, read that circular. Until then, this is the list.

## Week one: put the August record in the file

Download the [11 August 2026 reply](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf) to Lok Sabha Starred Question 327 and the [written reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2297818) of the same day. Mark three lines so the team stops arguing from memory.

Gati Shakti has no spending target of its own. Logistics cost is cited at about 7.9 percent of GDP from a 2025 DPIIT–NCAER assessment, which is not the same sentence as the Prime Minister’s 13–14 percent line in September 2022. The Network Planning Group count is 396 evaluated, 256 sanctioned, 198 under implementation.

If a project you depend on is “on Gati Shakti,” find out which of those three columns it is in. A sales deck that says “sanctioned” when the annex would say “evaluated” is the kind of error this week is for.

## The operating documents, not a new policy

For every lane you run, confirm the GST movement document. The system lives with [GST](https://www.gst.gov.in/). Do not take a rupee threshold from a blog, including this one. Take it from the current rule your compliance lead files under, and note the date of that rule.

Confirm FASTag behaviour on the national highway pieces of those lanes, especially where a failed tag creates a queue you have been calling “driver delay.” Corridor context, if you need the programme rather than the plaza, is the roads ministry’s [Bharatmala](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala) page.

If you touch export or import boxes, ask whether your team can see them on the Logistics Data Bank, which the August reply says covers ports, container freight stations, and inland container depots. Domestic full-truckload will not appear there. Knowing that saves a wasted integration.

If you want government logistics data inside a product, open [ULIP](https://goulip.in/) and write the use-case in one page. The August reply calls the platform operational and API-based. A use-case that you cannot describe in a page is not ready for a ministry review.

## State paper, then the physical gap

Ask counsel or the state cell two questions. Has this state or Union Territory formulated a logistics policy, inside the 29 the August reply claims? Has it granted industry status, inside the 32? Get the notification. A national count does not award you the incentive.

Then walk or call the last mile. The written reply says all 36 states and Union Territories are on the master plan and that a public query platform opened in September 2025. Use it, or ask the state cell to, for the approach road, the rail siding, or the port link your site assumes. About 1,800 layers integrated is a national claim. Your plot is one layer or it is absent.

If your commodity is coal, steel, fertilizers, cement, pharmaceuticals, or food, ask whether a Sectoral Plan for Efficient Logistics constraint — siding, coastal movement, warehouse standard — is already written for it. The reply says those plans have been initiated. It does not attach them. Request the plan from the node you use.

## What not to spend the 90 days on

Do not commission a fresh estimate of India’s logistics cost as a percent of GDP unless you are prepared to read the NCAER method. Another 7.9, or another 14, will not change your lane. Do not rank states from an old LEADS edition and call it this quarter. Do not treat a statistics-ministry homepage as the freight file. The file is the August replies, the GST document, the toll, and the state notification.
