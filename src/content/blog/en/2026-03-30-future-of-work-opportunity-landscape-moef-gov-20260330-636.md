# Future of Work in India: Operating Opportunities under the Codes

## A permission in the text

The openings below are designs in the Ministry of Labour and Employment's 21 November 2025 implementation note and in the codes in force since that date. Headcount, investment totals, and IT or BPM demand forecasts are commercial questions. They are outside this landscape.

## Fixed-term hiring on the company's own roll

The ministry says fixed-term employees receive benefits equal to permanent workers, including leave, medical cover, and social security; equal wages; and gratuity after one year rather than five. It presents that package as support for direct hiring. The operating opening is to hire on a fixed term, with an appointment letter, which the ministry requires for workers generally. The opening depends on the parity being real in the contract.

## Night work with conditions

Women may work at night and in all types of work, with consent and required safety measures. The IT and ITES section repeats night-shift access, equal pay, and salary by the 7th, and ties social security to fixed-term employment and appointment letters. For a firm that runs follow-the-sun support or business-process shifts, the opening is to staff those hours inside the conditions the ministry lists for night work, including written consent, overtime at double the wage, and safe transport. That is an employment-practice change.

## Short engagements with a record

The ministry's export-sector note says fixed-term workers in that sector receive gratuity, provident fund, and other social security, with timely wages. The audio-visual and digital-media note extends appointment letters and wage rules to journalists in electronic media, dubbing artists, and stunt persons. Firms in those chains can put short engagements on a formal letter. Project work then has a record.

## One filing pattern, bounded by the state

Single registration, a pan-India licence, and a single return are described as the replacement for overlapping filings. For an employer in several states, that is a process opening, bounded by the concurrent list. Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026, and states and Union Territories still frame their own rules, as the 20 July 2026 Lok Sabha reply records. The simplification lands where the appropriate government has notified the matching rule.

## Platforms and skill partnerships

An aggregator has a defined place in the Code on Social Security and a stated contribution of 1 to 2 percent of turnover, capped at 5 percent of worker payments. The opening is to keep one worker record. The ministry points to e-Shram, launched on 26 August 2021, and to an Aadhaar-linked Universal Account Number, and scheme rules apply as they are notified. NITI Aayog's 2022 report, *India's Booming Gig and Platform Economy*, urged platform-linked skilling with the skill ministry and the National Skill Development Corporation. That is a partnership opening. Worker totals stay in the documents that estimate them.
