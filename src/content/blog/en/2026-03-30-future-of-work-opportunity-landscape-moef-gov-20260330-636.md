# Where the labour frame creates room to hire, train, and cover workers

An opportunity, in this labour market, is a thing an employer or a worker can do now that the law or a live government system makes easier than it was, with the condition written next to it. It is not a forecast that a sector will be large. The openings below are taken from the Ministry's account of the labour codes, from the Code on Social Security, from e-Shram's published linkages, and from the Economic Survey's description of a pension product. Where the condition is "once notified," the opportunity is real as design and not yet real as cash.

## Hiring for a defined period without pretending the worker is outside the law

The Ministry's note of 21 November 2025 says fixed-term employees receive the same leave, medical, and social-security benefits as permanent workers, at equal wages, and qualify for gratuity after one year of continuous service rather than five. The stated purpose is direct hiring and less contractual layering. For an exporter, a project contractor, or a plant that staffs to an order, that is permission to put people on the company's own rolls for the life of the work, instead of routing them through a chain of contractors to avoid gratuity and benefits.

The condition is administrative. Equal benefits and a one-year gratuity rule raise the cost of the contract relative to an informal arrangement. They lower the legal risk of being found to have used contracting to withhold those benefits. The same note says that during the transition, existing rules continue. A company should match the fixed-term practice to the provision that has actually commenced, not only to the summary table. Appointment letters, which the note makes mandatory, are the document that makes the fixed term enforceable for the worker and legible for the employer.

IT and IT-enabled services get a related, narrower opening. The note says salary in that sector is to be released by the 7th of the month, and that women may work night shifts with consent and safety measures. Firms that already run night support can staff those shifts with women if consent and transport are real. The opportunity disappears if "consent" is a line in a handbook and the shift ends with an unsafe commute. The note lists written consent, double wages for overtime, safe transport, and security arrangements among the conditions.

## A wage floor that covers people the old schedule left out

Under the Code on Wages, as the Ministry describes it, minimum wages are a statutory right for all workers, not only for scheduled employments, and a national floor wage is meant to stop pay below the living standard the Centre sets. For small firms and for MSME workers, the note says minimum wages, timely payment, standard working hours, overtime at double the rate, and paid leave apply, with social-security coverage under the Social Security Code based on employee count. The opportunity for a compliant firm is a competitor who can no longer price a contract on uncovered wages. The opportunity for a worker is a claim that does not depend on whether the employment was on the old schedule. Both depend on the floor and the minimum rates actually notified, and on a forum that will hear the claim.

## Registration as a distribution rail, not as the benefit

e-Shram held over 31.78 crore registrations on 14 July 2026 and 31.89 crore on 20 August 2026. Women were 54.28 percent of the August figure, and 55.21 percent of registrants were aged 18 to 40. The 20 July 2026 Lok Sabha reply says the portal is linked to the National Career Service for jobs and to the Skill India Digital Hub for skilling, and that the "One-Stop-Solution" launched on 21 October 2024 lets a registered worker see scheme benefits in one place. Fifteen central schemes were already mapped.

That is an opening for any public or private programme that needs to reach unorganised workers and is willing to go through systems the worker already has. The Economic Survey lists the Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana, the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme, and the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme among the skill instruments already in the government's toolkit. This page does not invent enrolment totals for them. The opening is the link, not a claim that every registrant has been trained.

On pensions, the Survey records that the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority launched the NPS e-Shramik (Platform Service Partner) model on 29 October 2025 so platform workers can join the National Pension System. Combined with the Ministry's description of an Aadhaar-linked Universal Account Number for gig welfare, the design is portability: a worker who changes city or platform does not have to restart identity. Portability is an opportunity for the worker and a lower onboarding cost for the next platform. It is not, yet, evidence of balances accumulated. The Survey itself says pension coverage remains modest relative to the size of the workforce.

## A defined market, with the skill mix the estimate actually has

NITI Aayog's 2022 report is the opening for anyone building tools or training for gig work, if they use it as a map and not as a promise. It estimated 77 lakh gig workers in 2020–21 and a rise toward 2.35 crore by 2029–30, with about 47 percent of the work medium-skilled, 22 percent high-skilled, and 31 percent low-skilled. Retail and sales and transportation were the large industrial blocks in the estimate. The commercial opening is in services for that medium-skilled majority — payments, safety, training, insurance administration — and in the Seventh Schedule categories the code already lists: ride sharing, delivery, logistics, e-marketplaces, professional services, healthcare, travel and hospitality, and content. The condition is the contribution date under section 114(5). A product that helps an aggregator calculate and remit 1 to 2 percent of turnover, capped at 5 percent of payouts, becomes necessary when that date is notified. It is premature as a compliance product and reasonable as a system built in advance.

The landscape is therefore practical. Hire on a fixed term and price the one-year gratuity. Extend night and higher-paid work to women where safety and consent exist. Use e-Shram's links to skills, jobs, and the pension product instead of building a parallel identity. Build for the medium-skilled gig majority the 2022 estimate described. Leave the headcount scenario as a scenario.
