This page is a ledger. If two official notes disagree, both rows stay. A blank means the document used here does not state the figure. It does not mean the figure is zero.

## Mission money

| Figure | What it is | Where it is printed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rs. 10,371.92 crore over five years | Cabinet outlay of the IndiaAI Mission, approved 7 March 2024 | Lok Sabha reply of 5 August 2026, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295477) |
| ₹10,371 crore | The same outlay, rounded | Rajya Sabha reply of 24 July 2026, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289946) |
| Nearly 45 percent of mission funding | Secretary's statement that day on the compute pillar | 6 March 2025, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2108961) |
| Up to 40 percent | Subsidy on cloud AI compute for eligible users, as announced at launch | Same 6 March 2025 note |
| About Rs 65 per GPU-hour | Average rate, except some high-end GPUs | Lok Sabha material of 25 March 2026, [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2245063) |

The Rs 65 rate is "approximately." The 10 February 2026 note also calls ₹65 an hour nearly one third of a global average. That comparison is the note's, not a recalculation.
