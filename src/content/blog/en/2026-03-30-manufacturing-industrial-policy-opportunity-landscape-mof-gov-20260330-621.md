# Manufacturing lanes India's incentive map actually opens

"Manufacturing opportunity in India" is too wide to act on. The official map has four lanes. They overlap in the real economy and they do not share an application form.

## Lane one: a PLI scheme, with a named ministry

The 21 July 2026 annexure, [PIB PRID 2287008](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287008), is the list that matters for incentive claims. Cumulative investment till March 2026 is in crore of rupees next to each line:

- Large-scale electronics manufacturing, MeitY, 20,580
- IT hardware 2.0, MeitY, 908
- Bulk drugs, Department of Pharmaceuticals, 5,070
- Medical devices, Department of Pharmaceuticals, 1,151
- Pharmaceutical drugs, Department of Pharmaceuticals, 45,158
- Telecom and networking products, Department of Telecom, 5,278
- Food products, Ministry of Food Processing Industries, 9,207
- Drones and drone components, Ministry of Civil Aviation, 595
- White goods, DPIIT, 6,409
- Automobiles and auto components, Ministry of Heavy Industries, 44,326
- Advanced chemistry cell batteries, Ministry of Heavy Industries, 4,570
- Textile products, Ministry of Textiles, 8,117
- Specialty steel, Ministry of Steel, 23,896
- High-efficiency solar PV modules, MNRE, 64,873

The 28 July reply says 892 applications had been approved across these 14 sectors as on 31 March 2026 ([Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1423](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1423_Rd4xWf.pdf)). It does not say the windows are still open, and it does not reprint eligibility. A product that is merely "electronics" may sit in large-scale electronics, IT hardware, telecom, or none of them. White goods, in this table, are the DPIIT scheme, and the reply's narrative ties that scheme to air-conditioner components, including compressor capacity moving from 1 million units in 2021 to 10 million in 2025–26.

Reported investment is a sign of where approved applicants have already put capital. It is not a ranking of which scheme a new firm should enter.

## Lane two: Make in India facilitation, without a PLI claim

DPIIT's Annual Report 2024-25 lists 24 sub-sectors under Make in India 2.0, including furniture, leather and footwear, fisheries, agri-produce, aluminium, agrochemicals, ethanol, ceramics, toys, and sporting goods, alongside several that also appear in PLI. Facilitation through Invest India, state governments, and Project Development Cells is the offer. It is not the ₹1.91 lakh crore incentive outlay. A toy or furniture project should not be written up as a PLI project unless a scheme text says it is one. The report is the [DPIIT Annual Report 2024-25](https://www.dpiit.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/06/3d9c9c2daeefb97bb9ce964370938b71.pdf).

## Lane three: a corridor plot

The 30 August 2024 note, [PIB PRID 2050136](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2050136), offers serviced industrial nodes, with Dholera, AURIC, Greater Noida, and Vikram Udyogpuri described as existing SPV projects, and a further set of approved locations including Khurpia, Dighi, Palakkad, Gaya, and Jodhpur-Pali. The lane is land and infrastructure. Sector targeting at Dholera is described as defence, aviation, high-tech, and pharmaceuticals. That is the release's targeting language, not a reservation that excludes every other industry, and not a substitute for the SPV's allotment rules.

## Lane four: a smaller firm in a cluster

The same 28 July reply, on MSMEs, names the Micro and Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme, the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs, tool rooms, and the Self Reliant India Fund, among other Ministry of MSME instruments. Those are the cluster and small-firm lane. They are not administered as one of the 14 PLI lines in the annexure. A supplier into a PLI anchor can use this lane without being a PLI applicant.

Related electronics programmes sit beside PLI and are easy to merge into it by mistake. The 27 March 2026 reply names the Semicon India Programme, the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme, SPECS, EMC, and M-SIPS as separate initiatives ([PIB PRID 2246089](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246089)). Open the scheme that names your component.
