An opportunity in an agri value chain is a handoff that a buyer already pays to have done better. A gap with no buyer is a research topic. This page lists types of handoff. It does not estimate a market.

## Categories a founder can inspect

Stay inside a crop and a catchment you can visit.

- **Cleaner aggregation.** Lots that arrive mixed, under-weight, or unpaid on time. The buyer already discounts that mess. A tighter collection practice can keep part of the discount.
- **Grading the buyer will accept.** The specification exists in the purchase order and is applied inconsistently. The opportunity is a grade that survives the buyer's re-check.
- **Holding quality.** Time and temperature between intake and dispatch destroy value the buyer would have paid. The opportunity is condition, not a new brand.
- **A processing step the buyer now does themselves.** If the buyer mills, sorts, or packs because suppliers cannot, that step has a price. Confirm the price before you buy the machine.
- **Documents that clear.** Residue tests, traceability, and yard papers that currently fail lots. The opportunity is a lot that passes, sold into a channel that already exists.
- **A second outlet for rejected lots.** Rejects are a product with a lower specification. An outlet you have not named is not yet an opportunity.

Each category fails the same way: the founder prices the national crop instead of the lots in front of them. The [execution page](/blog/agri-value-chains-execution-bottleneck-review-morth-nic-20260330-597) is how to see which category is actually the wait. ## Filters before you build

Three checks, in order. Can you name the buyer and the current cost of the bad handoff? Can you run the fix on one season's volume without a new plant? Does the fix still work in the lean weeks, not only at peak? A no on the first check ends the idea. A no on the second means you are proposing capex, which belongs on the [capex watch](/blog/agri-value-chains-capex-cycle-watch-morth-nic-20260330-357), not in a landscape slide.

## Scope

No market size, no growth rate, and no list of funded companies appears here. An opportunity you cannot tie to a buyer's current invoice is outside this page.
