# The doors an agri operator can actually open

An opportunity in this chain is a door with a guideline, a ceiling, and a stock of people who have already walked through. The landscape below is those doors. It is not a list of crops that should be processed, and it is not a market-size estimate.

## Aggregate, then capitalise the collective

The formation scheme has already registered 10,000 farmer producer organisations, so the open door is no longer a race to be registered. The open door is the support that, as on 31 December 2025, had not reached every registered body. The matching equity grant is up to Rs 2,000 per member and Rs 15 lakh per FPO. The credit guarantee covers up to Rs 2 crore of project loan. Equity grant of Rs 430.77 crore had reached 6,557 FPOs, and guarantee cover of Rs 662.71 crore had reached 2,671. Management support remains up to Rs 18 lakh over three years. An existing FPO with members and a crop, which has not drawn the grant or the guarantee, is the opportunity the stock itself identifies. For infrastructure loans under the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund, the agriculture ministry names NABSanrakshan alongside CGTMSE as the guarantee window for loans up to Rs 2 crore.

Women's collectives are a counted subset. As on 1 March 2026, 1,175 FPOs had only women members, and 23.55 lakh women farmers were on the scheme's register.

## Sell without treating the yard as the only gate

e-NAM is the market door. As on 28 February 2026 it held 1.80 crore farmers, 2.72 lakh traders, and 4,724 FPOs, across 1,656 mandis, with cumulative trade of Rs 4,82,350 crore and 13.22 crore metric tonnes since 2016. The April 2026 account describes services around that auction. The Platform of Platforms, launched on 14 July 2022, is the name for logistics, warehousing, quality, grading, packaging, inputs, and insurance reached through the e-NAM app. The electronic negotiable warehouse receipt, for stock in a warehouse accredited by the Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority, lets an FPO store, pledge, and sell on the platform. Both doors work where the warehouse is accredited and the state's licence lets the bid through.

The physical door at a mandi that still lacks kit is the integration grant of up to Rs 75 lakh, for assaying, weighing, grading, and packaging. A state mandi board is the applicant for that grant.

## Build the asset the loan and the grant will each accept

Since the Cabinet decisions of 28 August 2024, the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund funds farm-gate storage and logistics, community farming assets, and primary processing integrated with secondary processing in one unit. Standalone secondary processing stays outside. The subvention is 3 per cent for up to seven years, on a loan capped for that benefit at Rs 2 crore, under a 9 per cent interest ceiling. As on 15 July 2026 the fund had leveraged Rs 1,61,537.22 crore of capital investment, including Rs 50,332.66 crore in storage and logistics. The August 2025 note said new disbursement was to end with 2025-26. An operator in September 2026 confirms a fresh sanction on the portal, and treats subvention on an existing loan as the right that can still run for up to seven years.

The food-processing grant is the parallel door for an integrated cold chain, an agro-processing cluster, or an Operation Greens project. The 6 August 2026 rate card is 35 per cent in general areas and 50 per cent for difficult areas and for FPO, self-help group, and Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe promoters, generally capped at Rs 10 crore, and at Rs 15 crore for an integrated Operation Greens project. The August 2025 description excludes a standalone cold store from the integrated scheme. As of 30 June 2026, 311 projects were operational and 98 were ongoing. Approval is demand-driven.

The production-linked incentive is a later door. It pays after investment, for applicants whose whole manufacturing chain, including primary processing, is in India, and it supports branding abroad. The stated window runs to 2026-27. By 31 December 2025, 168 applicants were in, with about Rs 9,207 crore invested and Rs 2,714.79 crore of incentive disbursed.

## Cross the border under a named export scheme

APEDA's Bharat Krishi Niryat Yojana, described in the tender of 1 September 2026 for 2026-27 to 2030-31, is the export door for scheduled products. The three heads are export infrastructure (packhouses, pre-cooling, refrigerated transport, common facilities, and processing equipment that closes a safety gap), quality systems and testing, and market development, including fairs, trial shipments, brands, and geographical indications. The tender names exporters, FPOs, food manufacturers, traders, state governments, and food-testing laboratories. Behind that door, the 2024-25 annual report records agri exports of USD 51,913 million, and the summary table puts APEDA products at 55 per cent of that total. The tender does not print a fresh export target.
