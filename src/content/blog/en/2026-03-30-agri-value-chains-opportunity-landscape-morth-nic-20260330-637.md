# Three official doors into food processing

The Ministry of Food Processing Industries runs the processing link in this chain through three schemes. They were all reported as on 30 June 2026, and they fund different sizes of firm. Adding their rupees into one “opportunity” hides which door a founder can actually enter.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Sampada Yojana is the infrastructure door. The Ministry had approved 1,735 projects, with a project cost of ₹33,163.34 crore and approved grants or subsidy of ₹9,258.60 crore. Cold chain is one component inside that umbrella, not a fourth scheme. Its own stock that day was 409 projects and ₹3,089.08 crore sanctioned. Those cold-chain rupees sit inside the PMKSY family. Counting them again on top of ₹9,258.60 crore double-counts the grant.

PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises is the small-unit door. Credit-linked subsidy had been approved for 2,00,421 micro enterprises, with subsidy of ₹5,954.57 crore and private investment the Ministry put at about ₹14,480.24 crore. Seed capital of ₹1,438.29 crore had gone to 4,18,293 self-help group members. The unit rule is a 35 percent credit-linked capital subsidy, capped at ₹10 lakh. Eligible applicants include individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, FPOs, NGOs, cooperatives, self-help groups, and private limited companies. Common infrastructure for FPOs, self-help groups, cooperatives, and government agencies is also 35 percent, capped at ₹3 crore. This is a ₹10 lakh instrument. It is the wrong door for a plant that needs the PLI scale.

The Production Linked Incentive Scheme for Food Processing Industry is the scale door. The outlay is ₹10,900 crore from FY 2021-22 to FY 2026-27. As on 30 June 2026 the Ministry reported 163 applications from 127 companies at 212 locations in 22 states, including 69 micro, small, and medium applicants. Incentives disbursed up to June 2026 were ₹3,271.44 crore. Applicants had invested ₹9,207 crore against ₹7,722 crore committed, created about 34 lakh tonnes a year of capacity, and reported about 3.35 lakh direct and indirect jobs against a target of 2.50 lakh. Sales of PLI-supported products went from ₹58,758 crore in FY 2019-20 to ₹1,08,854 crore in FY 2025-26. A separate category table on the same date splits the 163 into 49 in ready-to-eat and ready-to-cook, fruit and vegetables, marine, and mozzarella; 15 in innovative and organic products; 70 in branding and marketing; and 29 in millet products, of which 21 are MSME millet applicants. The 69 and the 21 are answers from two replies. They overlap in meaning. Use the category table for mix and the 163 as the total.

## Which door matches the firm

A village unit with one product line is in PMFME, at a ₹10 lakh cap, and can also use the ₹3 crore common-infrastructure cap if it is an FPO or a cooperative building shared plant. A cold store or a processing line seeking a capital grant is in PMKSY, at 35 percent in general areas and 50 percent in difficult areas and for FPO, self-help group, and SC/ST promoters, generally capped at ₹10 crore. A firm that can show incremental sales of a qualifying product, and that will do the manufacturing chain in India, is the PLI applicant. The incentive follows eligible sales. It is not a civil-works subsidy.

APEDA’s export-promotion scheme for registered exporters, running from 2021-22 to 2025-26, is the overseas door for scheduled products. It does not replace an Export Inspection Council certificate. The Commerce briefing’s residue list still applies to anything that leaves the PMKSY shed in a container.
