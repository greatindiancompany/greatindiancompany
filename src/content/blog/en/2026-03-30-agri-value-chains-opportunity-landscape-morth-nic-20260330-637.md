An opportunity is a handoff an Indian buyer already pays to have done better. A gap with no buyer is a research topic. This page lists types. It estimates no market.

## Types you can inspect in a district

- **Aggregation that pays members.** A farmer producer organisation, or a tighter commission practice, that turns small lots into one invoice. The buyer is a mill, a retailer, or an exporter who already discounts mixed quality. Registration of the organisation is the start. Payment on delivery is the opportunity.
- **Assaying a mandi does not do.** e-NAM and out-of-district buyers need a grade. A yard that sells by sight leaves that grade unpaid. The opportunity is a test the buyer will accept, not a portal login.
- **Primary processing the buyer now does.** Cleaning, drying, dehusking, or splitting, where a rice mill, a dal mill, or a spice buyer currently rejects or reprices the raw lot. Confirm the repricing on recent bills before you buy the machine.
- **Dairy collection where the test is weak.** In districts where the cooperative or a private dairy already pays on fat and solids-not-fat, the opening is a society or chilling route that actually tests. Where no buyer pays on the test, there is no price for the skill.
- **Export preparation for a crop that already leaves.** Grapes, chilli, basmati, spices, or shrimp with an existing packhouse or plant standard. The opportunity is a lot that passes APEDA or MPEDA rules and the destination sheet. It is not a new brand.
- **Storage a bank can finance.** A Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority registration and an electronic negotiable warehouse receipt for a storable commodity such as potato, pulses, or grain. The buyer of the service is the stockist who already holds the crop and cannot borrow against the shed.

## Filters before you build

Name the buyer and the current cost of the bad handoff, in their bill or their reject note. Run the fix for one season without a new plant if you can. Check the lean weeks, not only kharif peak or the potato loading fortnight. A no on the first filter ends the idea. A no on the second means you are proposing capex, which belongs on the [capex watch](/blog/agri-value-chains-capex-cycle-watch-morth-nic-20260330-357).
