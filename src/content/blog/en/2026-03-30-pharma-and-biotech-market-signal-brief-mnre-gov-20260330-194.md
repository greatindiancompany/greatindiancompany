# Which Official Numbers Count as an India Pharma Market Signal

A market signal is a number with a publisher, a period, and a unit. Indian pharma has several of those in cabinet notes and parliamentary replies. It does not have, in the texts below, a monthly API price index or a live export dashboard. Using a 2020 expectation as if it were a 2026 result is how a brief becomes a brochure.

## Trade figures that carry a year

On 1 September 2022 the Press Information Bureau, announcing in-principle approval of three bulk-drug parks, stated two trade facts for the financial year 2021–22. India exported pharmaceuticals worth ₹1,75,040 crore, a total that includes bulk drugs and drug intermediates. Exports of bulk drugs and drug intermediates themselves were ₹33,320 crore. The same note said India is one of the major producers of active pharmaceutical ingredients, and that most bulk-drug imports were happening for economic reasons, alongside a policy aim of reducing that dependence.

Those rupee figures describe FY 2021–22. They are the right citation for a sentence about that year. They are the wrong citation for a sentence about the latest quarter. When a newer Directorate General of Commercial Intelligence and Statistics or Department release is in hand, replace them. Until then, keep the year in the sentence.

## A volume rank and an import gap, stated together

The 21 March 2020 cabinet note calls the Indian pharmaceutical industry the third largest in the world by volume. In the next breath it says India is significantly dependent on imports of bulk drugs used to make medicines, and that in some specific bulk drugs the import dependence is 80 to 100 percent. The September 2022 park note repeats the volume rank. The policy problem the schemes were built to answer sits in the second sentence, which is about specific molecules, not about every formulation India already makes at scale.

The cabinet note also records an expectation attached to the bulk-drug incentive: incremental sales of ₹46,400 crore and significant additional employment over eight years. The word on the page is expected. An expectation inside an approval note is a design target. Commissioned output, audited incremental sales, and incentive actually disbursed are later facts, and they live in Department disclosures and the approved-applicant lists, not in that sentence.

## Project counts are signals about the pipeline

The September 2022 note said 51 projects had been approved under the bulk-drug PLI and 14 had started manufacturing. The 6 February 2026 Lok Sabha reply says incentives cover 41 identified products and that 48 projects are approved for 33 bulk drugs. For the separate pharmaceuticals PLI, both the 2022 note and the 2026 reply state 55 approved applicants. A pipeline signal is the movement, or the lack of movement, between dated official counts. It becomes a market-share claim only after sales are published against the base year the scheme uses, which for the 2020 bulk-drug incentive is 2019–20 incremental sales.

Park construction is a different signal again. The February 2026 reply describes tenders awarded and works in progress at Jambusar, Haroli, and Nakkapalli. Common infrastructure under construction is a future cost base for manufacturers who take plots. It is not current API production from those parks.

## What to ignore when the label says pharma

Renewable-energy ministry pages do not report drug exports, CDSCO approvals, or PLI disbursement. A bank outlook and a trade-agency topic page are not a substitute for the Department of Pharmaceuticals schemes index, which holds the gazettes, the guidelines, and the applicant lists. Price control for scheduled medicines sits with the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority, a separate regime from a production incentive. Jan Aushadhi, listed on the same Department schemes page as the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana, is an access channel. An access channel and a manufacturing incentive can both be true and still describe different markets.
