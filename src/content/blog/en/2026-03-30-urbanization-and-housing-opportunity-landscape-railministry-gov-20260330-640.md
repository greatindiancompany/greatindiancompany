# Urban Housing Work That the Official Rules Still Leave Open

## Opportunity means a rule that still needs an operator

A landscape that lists “smart cities, affordable housing, proptech” without a rule is a keyword list. The official record leaves five kinds of work open because the document says who must still act.

## Origination inside the interest subsidy

PMAY-Urban 2.0’s Interest Subsidy Scheme is live on the terms PIB reported on 24 July 2025. A lender or housing finance company can originate a loan that fits income up to ₹9 lakh, a loan up to ₹25 lakh, a property up to ₹35 lakh, and carpet area up to 120 square metres, and can receive up to ₹1.80 lakh at 4 percent on the first ₹8 lakh, over up to 12 years, in five instalments. The open operational problem is keeping the loan inside the release test: active, and more than half the principal outstanding, at each instalment. The closed Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme is not an opportunity. It ended in 2021 for middle-income borrowers and in 2022 for the other eligible groups, after ₹58,885 crore had been released.

Partnership and beneficiary-led units carry ₹2.50 lakh of government assistance, with a central share that the December 2024 note sets by region. The open work is the state’s memorandum, the urban local body’s beneficiary list, and, for partnership projects, a partner who will build to that assistance. The note’s “more than 6 lakh” in-principle approvals were a December 2024 position from 29 States and Union Territories. A firm should read the current state sanction, not that sentence, before hiring a site team.

## Rental stock the scheme names and does not build itself

Affordable Rental Housing is a 2.0 vertical for working women, industrial workers, urban migrants, people who are homeless or destitute, students, and similar beneficiaries. The December 2024 note says public and private entities are expected to participate. That is an invitation written into a scheme document. It becomes a project only when a state brings a proposal and an entity is ready to own or operate rental housing under the scheme rules. It is distinct from selling an apartment into the Interest Subsidy Scheme.

## Water systems past the last printed year of funding

AMRUT 2.0’s guidelines still describe the assets: taps for 4,700 statutory towns, sewerage or septage for 500 cities, city water balance plans, and a technology sub-mission that encourages water-sector start-ups. They also describe the commercial conditions: 10 percent of million-plus city funds in partnership mode, and municipal bonds and credit ratings as reforms. The indicative outlay’s period runs through financial year 2025-26. The opportunity that survives that date is operation, non-revenue water, reuse of treated water, and any state plan still completing projects. It is a weak opportunity for a firm that needed a fresh central sanction in 2026-27 and does not have a later government decision.

Pey Jal Survekshan, in the same guidelines, is a published assessment of water quality and quantity. A company that measures leakage or tests supply has a named public use. The guideline does not promise a procurement quota.

## Systems already built, budgets already closed

All 100 Smart Cities have command centres, on both the MoHUA page and the August 2026 Lok Sabha reply. The page also describes SCADA on water networks in 31 cities, RFID waste vehicles in 48 cities, and smart roads. Advisory No. 27 of 10 June 2025 asks states to repurpose the special purpose vehicles. The open work is operations, maintenance, and integration for a city committee that decides the vehicle still has a role. The closed work is a centrally funded new city. MoHUA has said no new city will be taken up.

## Disclosure as a product

Section 4 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act requires seventy percent of allottee collections in a separate scheduled-bank account for land and construction. State authorities publish the register. Software, escrow operations, carpet-area measurement, and agent registration are work the statute creates. They are not created by a MoHUA mission circular, and they differ by state rule.

The first PMAY-Urban note’s estimate of 917 crore person-days is a dated construction estimate, not a vacancy list. Skills and that estimate are handled in the jobs brief.
