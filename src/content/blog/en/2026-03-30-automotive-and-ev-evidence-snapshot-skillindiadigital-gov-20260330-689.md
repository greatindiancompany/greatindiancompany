# Official Auto and EV Figures, Each with Its Date

This page collects figures that appear in a ministry gazette, a PIB reply, a scheme page, or a SIAM release. Each line carries the date of that document. A later notification can supersede an earlier line. Where that has already happened, both lines are kept and labelled.

## PM E-DRIVE, as amended on 10 August 2026

Source: gazette [S.O. 4424(E)](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/Gazette%20Notification%2010%2008%202026%20on%20enhancement%20of%20Outlay%2011900%20cr.pdf) and the allocation table on the [scheme portal](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/).

- Outlay ₹11,900 crore. Window 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2028. EMPS-2024 (1 April–30 September 2024) is subsumed.
- Fund-limited. Last date to submit any claim: 31 December 2027.
- Registered electric two-wheelers: 45,79,120 vehicles, ₹2,767 crore. From 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028, ₹2,500 per kWh, capped at ₹5,000 and at 15 percent of ex-factory price.
- E-rickshaws and e-carts: 39,034 vehicles, ₹50 crore.
- L5 three-wheelers: cap 2,88,809 and ₹857 crore in the table; segment closed 26 December 2025.
- E-ambulances: 3,811 and ₹500 crore. E-trucks: 5,643 and ₹500 crore. E-buses: 14,028 and ₹4,391 crore.
- Public charging: ₹2,000 crore. Testing agencies: ₹780 crore. Administration: ₹55 crore.

## Reimbursements and penetration, as on the July 2026 reply

Source: [PIB, 28 July 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2290696). These counts predate the August outlay change.

- As on 22 July 2026: 20,56,831 two-wheelers, ₹1,505.30 crore; 5,082 e-rickshaws and e-carts, ₹7.61 crore; 2,60,965 L5, ₹769.02 crore. Total 23,22,878 vehicles, ₹2,281.94 crore.
- No state-wise demand-incentive data is maintained.
- Charging, as on 1 July 2026: ₹689 crore approved for 6,562 chargers, to three oil-marketing companies and nine states.
- Buses: 14,000 allocated; quantity letters for 13,800; Surat concession 600; Hyderabad concession 915.
- Penetration: 0.7 percent in FY 2019-20 and 8.2 percent in FY 2025-26. The reply says no fuel or emissions assessment has been done.
- The reply's own two-wheeler terminal date that week was 31 July 2026. The August gazette replaces that date.

An earlier Vahan table, in a [reply of 11 March 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2110244), is a different series and, for FY 2024-25, an unfinished year at the time of that reply. Calendar 2024 on that page: 19,50,490 electric vehicles registered on Vahan out of 2,62,07,453 vehicles, a 7.44 percent share. FAME II charging allocation on the same page: ₹839 crore.

## PLI Auto, cells, and FAME II

Source: [PIB, 13 January 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2214039), unless another page is named.

- PLI Auto outlay ₹25,938 crore. Cabinet approval 15 September 2021. The [ministry page](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/en/pli-scheme-automobile-and-auto-component-industry) dates incentives to FY 2022-23–FY 2026-27 and disbursement to the following year.
- 82 approved applicants. Estimated investment ₹42,500 crore, incremental sales ₹2,31,500 crore, jobs estimate 1.48 lakh over five years.
- Till 30 September 2025: investment ₹35,657 crore, determined sales ₹32,879 crore, employment 48,974.
- Disbursed: ₹322 crore for FY 2023-24 performance; ₹1,999.94 crore for FY 2024-25 performance.
- Incentivised units till 31 December 2025: 13,61,488, including 10,42,172 two-wheelers, 2,38,385 three-wheelers, 79,540 four-wheelers, and 1,391 buses.
- Domestic value addition minimum 50 percent. As of 31 December 2025: 94 variants at eight Champion OEM applicants; 37 variants at ten Component Champion applicants.
- Cells: ₹18,100 crore, 50 GWh, approved May 2021. Awarded 40 GWh to firms; 10 GWh kept for grid-scale stationary storage. Ola Cell Technologies: 1 GWh installed, pilot since March 2024. Investment ₹2,878 crore and employment 1,118 till 30 October 2025.
- FAME II, [ministry page](https://www.heavyindustries.gov.in/en/fame-ii): ₹10,000 crore from 1 April 2019, about 86 percent for demand incentive. Original support envelope: 7,000 e-buses, 5 lakh e-three-wheelers, 55,000 e-four-wheelers including strong hybrids, 10 lakh e-two-wheelers. The [FAME II portal](https://fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in/) records an enhancement to ₹11,500 crore and a close for two-, three-, and four-wheeler subsidies on 31 March 2024 or when funds ended.

## SIAM, FY 2025-26

Source: [SIAM, 14 April 2026](https://www.siam.in/pressrelease-details.aspx?pid=605). Passenger-vehicle figures omit BMW, Mercedes, JLR, and Volvo. Commercial-vehicle figures omit Daimler.

- Domestic sales: passenger vehicles 46,43,439 (up 7.9 percent); commercial vehicles 10,79,871 (up 12.6 percent); three-wheelers 8,36,231 (up 12.8 percent); two-wheelers 2,17,05,974 (up 10.7 percent).
- Production of the covered categories: 3,47,08,984.
- Exports: passenger vehicles 9.05 lakh (up 17.5 percent); two-wheelers 51.8 lakh (up 23.4 percent); three-wheelers about 4.61 lakh (up 50.1 percent); commercial vehicles 0.95 lakh (up 17.4 percent).
- Electric passenger-vehicle registrations: up by more than 80 percent in FY 2025-26, in SIAM's words "compared to previous years." The release does not print the unit count behind that rate.
- Prior SIAM release, 15 April 2025: EV registrations 1.97 million in FY 2024-25, against 1.68 million in FY 2023-24, up 16.9 percent.
