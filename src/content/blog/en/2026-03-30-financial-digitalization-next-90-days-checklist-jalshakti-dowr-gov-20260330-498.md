# A 90-day credit and payments checklist for operators

This checklist is a work plan for the 90 days from 24 September 2026. It is not a new Reserve Bank circular. Most of the items are duties that are already in force. One date inside the window is specific: 1 October 2026, for card issuers.

## If you issue cards or authenticate domestic payments

Open the [authentication directions of 25 September 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898). Confirm that domestic digital payments are using at least two distinct factors, or that the transaction sits in a listed exemption. Confirm the issuer, not the app vendor, can compensate the customer in full if a loss follows a non-compliant authentication. If you issue cards, confirm the 1 October 2026 build: validation of non-recurring cross-border card-not-present transactions when an overseas merchant or acquirer asks for authentication, BIN registration with the networks, and a risk-based mechanism for cross-border card-not-present transactions. The directions themselves do not apply to cross-border digital payments as a whole. Do not assume a domestic UPI control covers that card work.

## If you touch a digital loan

Open the [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848) and test the live flow, not the policy PDF.

- The regulated entity's name is visible before the borrower commits. A multi-lender screen, required since 1 November 2025, shows every matching offer with amount, tenor, annual percentage rate, monthly repayment, and penal charges, plus a link to each key fact statement. Unmatched lenders are named. The ranking metric, if you rank, is disclosed in public.
- Disbursement lands in the borrower's account, or in an exception the directions actually name. Repayment does not pass through the service provider.
- The key fact statement and the privacy policy reach the borrower as digitally signed documents. The cooling-off period is at least one day and is printed up front, including any processing fee you keep if the borrower exits.
- The app does not hold ongoing access to contacts, call logs, files, or media. Any camera, microphone, or location access is one-time and for KYC.
- Data is stored in India. Anything processed abroad is deleted and brought back within 24 hours.
- Every loan, including short-tenor merchant credit, is reported to a credit information company.
- The digital lending app on the store is the same app filed on the Centralised Information Management System, and nobody describes that filing as Reserve Bank authorisation.
- If you offer a default loss guarantee, the cover is within five per cent of the amount disbursed from a frozen set of loans, in cash, a lien-marked fixed deposit, or a bank guarantee. The website disclosure for the past month goes up within seven working days. You do not offer that guarantee on revolving digital credit or on credit cards.

## If you move consented data or merchant funds

An account aggregator, under the [2016 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10598), should be tested for five failures: a consent artefact missing purpose or expiry, credentials collected from the customer, financial information retained after the fetch, a third party running the aggregation, and any attempt to execute a payment. The aggregator does none of those.

A payment aggregator should reopen the [15 September 2025 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896): authorisation if you are a non-bank, net worth at the ₹15 crore and ₹25 crore marks that apply to your year, escrow, merchant due diligence, and a named officer for merchant disputes. If the same firm also originates loans, the digital lending list above applies on top.

## If you quote UPI in a customer or investor update

Pull the month from NPCI's [product statistics](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics), not from a July 2026 figure that has gone stale. MeitY's [Digital India page](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/) is fine for a description of the rail. It is not the monthly table.
