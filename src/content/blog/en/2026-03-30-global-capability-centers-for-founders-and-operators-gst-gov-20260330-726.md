# Captive Hubs in India for Founders and Operators

Two readers land here. One is selling to a multinational's Indian centre. The other is standing that centre up. Both are offered the same oversized numbers. The useful official record is smaller, and it is enough to make the next decision.

## Confirm which GCC you mean

Industry shorthand uses GCC for a global capability centre: a captive technology, operations, or research unit of a foreign company. MeitY and STPI also use GCC for **Government Community Cloud**, a cloud programme for government workloads. If the PDF in front of you is a tender for racks, empanelment, or a community cloud, you are not looking at a captive-hub buyer. Put it in a different folder before you build a pipeline around it.

## Size only the line you sell or staff

The Reserve Bank's survey of 18 September 2026 estimates software services exports of **US$ 221.4 billion** in 2025-26, up **8.2 percent**, excluding overseas commercial presence. That total mixes captives with third-party firms. Use the activity line that matches the work:

- IT services: **US$ 147.0 billion**.
- Engineering services: **US$ 12.0 billion**, of which product design engineering was **US$ 3.0 billion**.
- Finance and accounting BPO: **US$ 8.0 billion**. Business consulting: **US$ 4.0 billion**.
- A residual "other BPO" line: **US$ 42.2 billion**. Do not describe that residual as your market.

Off-site delivery was **91.7 percent**. The United States took **54.1 percent**. A founder selling implementation help is selling into an off-site, dollar-invoiced, US-and-Europe export stock. A founder who quotes US$ 221.4 billion as "the GCC market" is quoting a wider object. The activity table is in the [market-signal brief](/blog/global-capability-centers-market-signal-brief-gst-gov-20260330-206).

The centre count of over **1,700** and the revenue of **$64.6 billion** appear in ministerial remarks PIB reported on 25 February 2025. The note names no survey. Leave them out of a pricing model. If a customer repeats them, ask which register produced them.

## Standing up the Indian entity

Make in India lists IT and BPM at **100 percent** on the automatic FDI route. The consolidated FDI policy on the DPIIT site is the text that governs, including any case that still needs government approval. The Finance Ministry told the Lok Sabha on 20 July 2026 that more than **90 percent** of FDI inflow uses the automatic route. DPIIT's annual report says the National Single Window System is also used to seek that approval where the government route still applies. Computer-software equity of **USD 13,946 million** in 2025-26 shows that capital is arriving in the sector. It does not show that your subsidiary's filing is complete.

Location is two lists. STPI-registered software exports, tentative for 2024-25, are concentrated in Karnataka (**₹4,58,669.50 crore**), Maharashtra, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh. STPI's own network is **67** centres, **59** of them in smaller cities, with incubation and a startup scheme of up to **₹25 lakh**. Pick the list that matches the job. A large engineering floor follows the export rupees. A pilot or a startup feeder can use the centre network. The [state brief](/blog/global-capability-centers-state-wise-patterns-gst-gov-20260330-326) is the worksheet.

## Data flows to the parent

Assume the Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies to digital personal data you process in India. The rules notified on 13 November 2025 allow transfer abroad until the government specifies otherwise. Rule 13(4), from **13 May 2027**, can keep specified personal data and related traffic data inside India for a notified Significant Data Fiduciary. Consent Manager registration is the nearer date: **13 November 2026**. Build the dataset list now. The category list is not in the rule yet. Operators who need the clause in full should use the [regulatory brief](/blog/global-capability-centers-regulatory-outlook-gst-gov-20260330-446).
