# What founders should do with Udyam, credit guarantees, and shop-floor schemes

Founders do not need another description of how large the MSME sector is. They need the order in which official tools change a firm's output and its cash. The order below follows the gates the Ministry, SIDBI, and the Reserve Bank actually set.

## Put the certificate ahead of the machine

Register on Udyam, or on the Udyam Assist Platform if the enterprise is an informal micro unit that needs a certificate banks can use. The Reserve Bank tells banks to classify priority-sector MSME loans from the Udyam Registration Certificate or the Udyam Assist Certificate. Scheme portals such as ZED ask for the Udyam number before a manufacturing unit can proceed.

Then read the ceilings that have applied since 1 April 2025. Micro stops at ₹2.5 crore of plant and machinery or equipment and ₹10 crore of turnover. Small stops at ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore. Medium stops at ₹125 crore and ₹500 crore. If the next machine crosses a ceiling, the firm changes category on that investment alone. Plan the loan, the scheme subsidy, and the three-year continuation of non-tax benefits of the old category as one decision. The three-year point is the Press Information Bureau's description in its 14 May 2026 note. Your tax adviser owns the tax side.

## Separate the loan from the invoice

If the firm is micro or small, ask the bank for a CGTMSE-covered facility. The trust, set up by the Ministry and SIDBI, guarantees collateral-free credit, and from 1 April 2025 that cover can go up to ₹10 crore for guarantees approved on or after that date. Turn up with accounts, not with a sector essay. If the firm is medium, do not spend a month chasing this guarantee.

If buyers pay late, the productivity problem is receivables. Section 15 of the MSMED Act is the micro and small supplier's clock: a written period no longer than 45 days, otherwise fifteen days from acceptance. Put eligible invoices on the Trade Receivables Discounting System where the buyer is live on it. Central public sector enterprises are under a budget mandate to use that system. For a debt that is already late, Samadhaan is the filing path to the state facilitation council, and only if Udyam registration pre-dates the invoice. As on June 2026 the Ministry had 2,56,892 applications on the portal and 58,148 disposals. File when the case is real. Do not treat the portal as a collection agency you control.

## Then touch the process

Manufacturing firms with Udyam can take the ZED pledge and, if the audit is worth it, a Bronze, Silver, or Gold certificate. The sticker prices before subsidy are ₹10,000, ₹40,000, and ₹90,000. Subsidy is 80 percent for micro, 60 percent for small, and 50 percent for medium, with extras for some owners and regions. Lean is the right door when the problem is waste on an existing line rather than a quality certificate. Look at the Ministry's May 2026 funnel before you hire a consultant on a promise of easy certification: most ZED registrations had not become certificates.

If a common facility centre in your cluster is among the 364 completed MSE-CDP projects reported as on June 2026, use it before you buy a duplicate machine. If your state has an approved RAMP project aimed at your trade, the entry is through that state project.

Ignore any pitch that offers a single "MSME productivity scheme." The official system is this sequence: certificate, ceiling, guarantee or receivable, then process.
