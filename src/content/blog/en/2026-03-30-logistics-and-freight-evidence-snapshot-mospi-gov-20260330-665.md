Evidence in Indian freight is a shelf, not a vibe. Each question has a document that can answer it, and several famous links that cannot. This snapshot is the shelf as it stood after the Lok Sabha replies of 11 August 2026. It does not add a number the shelf does not hold.

## Match the question to the paper

**What does the government say logistics costs, as a share of GDP?** The [reply to Starred Question 327](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf) cites a 2025 assessment by DPIIT with NCAER: about 7.9 percent of GDP, described as comparable to advanced economies. That reply is the citation. The assessment’s method note is the next document, and it is not reproduced in the answer. A speech is not this series. On [17 September 2022](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-launches-national-logistics-policy/) the Prime Minister used 13–14 percent as the figure to leave behind. File the speech under aims. File 7.9 percent under the 2026 citation of a 2025 assessment. Do not average them.

**Where does India sit on the global index?** The same reply: 38th on the latest World Bank Logistics Performance Index it is using, six places higher. Pillar scores are in the Bank’s edition, not in the reply. The 2030 top-25 aim is in the 21 September 2022 cabinet note, not in the rank line.

**What has been through the planning machine?** The [written reply of 11 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2297818): 396 projects evaluated, about ₹18.66 lakh crore, 256 sanctioned, 198 under implementation. The same note says Gati Shakti itself has no expenditure target. The annex rows that read cleanly are roads (171 evaluated, 108 sanctioned, 53 under implementation, ₹9,01,668 crore), railways (162, 102, 102, ₹5,43,315 crore), and housing and urban affairs (31, 17, 17, ₹2,88,614 crore). Other ministries are in the total. Do not repair a broken cell by arithmetic you cannot show.

**What was approved and built on the highway and rail networks in the reply’s three-year window?** Only the starred reply states it: 1,115 national highway projects, about 18,493 km, about ₹4.75 lakh crore approved; 32,370 km constructed. Rail: 237 projects, 9,703 km, about ₹1,90,333 crore, of which 40 new lines, 17 gauge conversions, 180 doublings. “Constructed” and “sanctioned” stay in different sentences.

**Which states have moved on paper?** Starred reply: 29 states and Union Territories have formulated logistics policies; 32 have granted industry status. The names are not in the answer. LEADS is named as the assessment framework. An edition’s ranks live in that edition.

**Which boxes can the state see?** The Logistics Data Bank, as described in the starred reply, tracks export-import containers across ports, container freight stations, and inland container depots. It is not a series for domestic tonnes.

## Ministry pages, and the link this brief drops

Road corridors: [Bharatmala](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala) on the roads ministry site. Use it when the question is the highway programme, not when the question is the GDP ratio.

Port programmes: [Sagarmala](https://shipmin.gov.in/en/division/sagarmala). Use it when the question is the maritime programme. When the question is tonnes in a given month, use that month’s ports-ministry cargo note and read whether the figure is an advance estimate. This snapshot does not borrow a monthly tonne from a search snippet.

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation homepage does not answer any question on this list. That ministry compiles national statistics, and some infrastructure series do sit in its system, but a front door is not a freight release. Earlier versions of this page pointed at that front door because a generator rotated registry links. The link is omitted. If a dated roads, rail, or ports indicator is later published there, cite the release and the date.

## Questions this shelf refuses

It will not give a pan-India freight rate per tonne-kilometre, a driver-wage index, a warehouse rent, or a rail share of domestic freight. Those may exist in other primary series. They are not in the August 2026 replies, and inventing them to fill a template is how this site used to sound. The credible path is to say the shelf is short, and to send the reader to the document that matches the question they actually have.

That is the standard on the [Great Indian Company](https://greatindiancompany.com/) desk: one question, one dated paper, no borrowed precision.
