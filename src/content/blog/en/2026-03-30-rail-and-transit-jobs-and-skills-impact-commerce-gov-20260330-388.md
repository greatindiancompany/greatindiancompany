# Railway Jobs, Safety Categories, and the Skills the Network Needs

Indian Railways hires against an operating railway: loco pilots, technicians, section controllers, and level-1 staff who keep trains moving. The 22 July 2026 Lok Sabha reply is the employment record. It is not a forecast of private-sector rail jobs.

## The ten-year comparison, and the open book

Recruitments from 2004-05 to 2013-14 were 4.11 lakh. From 2014-15 to 2025-26 they were 5.53 lakh. In FY 2026-27, up to 30 June 2026, the count was 3,396. Vacancies are filled by indents placed with the recruitment agencies as operations and technology change. The open book is larger than one quarter’s panels: recruitment against 1,61,889 non-gazetted vacancies has been taken up under the annual calendars for 2024, 2025, and 2026.

The annual calendar itself, published from 2024 for Group C posts, is the process change. The ministry lists what it is meant to give candidates: more than one chance as people become eligible each year, a known exam timetable, and a shorter path from notification to training and appointment.

## What the 2026 notices actually hire

Under the 2026 calendar, three centralised employment notifications covered 17,803 vacancies:

- Assistant loco pilots, 11,127 posts, notified in March 2026
- Technicians, 6,557 posts, notified in May 2026
- Section controllers, 119 posts, notified in June 2026

The 2025 calendar had already issued nine notifications for 51,970 vacancies, including 10,970 assistant loco pilots, 6,238 technicians, 2,585 junior engineers and related posts, and 22,195 level-1 posts. The 2024 calendar notified ten rounds covering 92,116 vacancies. First-stage or single-stage tests for those 2024 posts drew 3,58,59,270 candidates. Panels finalised in FY 2026-27 up to 30 June 2026 covered more than 3,300 candidates, a majority in safety categories, including technicians, junior engineers, and assistant loco pilots.

The skill the notices keep repeating is safety-category operations: driving, maintenance, and train control. Station redevelopment and metro construction employ other trades. Those headcounts are not in this reply.

## The fleet those skills have to run

Vande Bharat sleeper coaches, described in the 23 July 2026 release, carry Kavach, jerk-free couplers, higher acceleration, fire-safety systems, sealed gangways, and coach CCTV. As of that date the railway was running 162 chair-car Vande Bharat services and 2 sleeper services. Adding further sleeper services, the ministry said in March 2026, depends on section capacity, a path, rolling stock, matching maintenance infrastructure, and traffic. Hiring loco pilots and technicians is the staffing side of that constraint. It does not, by itself, create a path on a busy section.

Electrification at 99.6 percent by July 2026, stated in the 12 August 2026 backgrounder, means the residual skill shift is toward electric traction and train-protection systems, not a long diesel-to-electric conversion still ahead of the network.
