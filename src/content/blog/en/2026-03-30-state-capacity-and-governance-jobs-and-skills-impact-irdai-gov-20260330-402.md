# Civil Service Skills and the Demographic Pressure on State Finance

The skills that decide whether a scheme reaches a household are, first, the skills of the people who run the state. India's official answer is Mission Karmayogi. The fiscal answer, in the Reserve Bank's latest state-budget study, is that youthful and ageing states do not carry the same human-capital bill. A jobs brief that skips both will talk about placements and miss the administration that has to deliver them.

## The civil service learning system

DoPT's scheme note for the National Programme for Civil Services Capacity Building says iGOT-Karmayogi was built as part of the digital learning stack to train about two crore government employees, a reach DoPT describes as beyond traditional training. The Capacity Building Commission coordinates annual capacity-building plans. Karmayogi Bharat, fully government-owned and under DoPT, runs the platform and a content marketplace. Courses are meant to cover domain, functional and behavioural competencies, online, in person and blended, with a learning record that follows the official.

PIB's 11 March 2026 account of a Lok Sabha reply gives the stock as on 6 March 2026: more than 1.51 crore users, over 4,400 courses, more than 7.7 crore completions. MoUs with 29 states and Union Territories extend the mission below the state secretariat. The Cabinet Secretary chairs a coordination unit that watches implementation. A monitoring framework splits key performance indicators across DoPT, ministries and organisations, Karmayogi Bharat, and the Commission.

Two skill facts in that reply are easy to miss. Karmayogi Bharat is an NCVET awarding body, and the platform carries NCVET-approved qualifications in public policy, AI-led digital transformation, and public governance and administration. That pulls civil-service learning toward a credential, not only a completion click. And district-level monitoring of learning performance is not undertaken on iGOT. The state can see enrolment. The district, which is where scheme delivery actually happens, is not the unit of the learning dashboard.

Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh are the two states the reply singles out for sustained engagement: 7,46,135 and 9,36,951 registered users, and 43,96,651 and 42,78,599 course enrolments, as on 6 March 2026. More than 33 lakh employees nationally had enrolled in courses on the three new criminal laws, including more than 5.4 lakh in those two states. Those are workload skills for the justice and police systems, published because the ministry was asked, and they show what "role-specific" means when a law changes.

## Demography decides which skills the budget can afford

The RBI study released on 23 January 2026 takes demographic transition as the theme of state finance. Youthful states, it says, have a wider window: an expanding working-age population and stronger revenue mobilisation, which can be used for higher investment in human capital. Ageing states face a narrowing window, with pressure from a shrinking tax base and rising committed expenditure, and the Bank points them to revenue capacity and to reforms in healthcare, pensions and workforce policy. Intermediate states have to fund growth and prepare for ageing together.

This is a skills conclusion even though it sits in a budget study. A youthful state's capacity problem is building the human capital, in the administration and in the population, while the window is open. An ageing state's capacity problem is paying for healthcare and pensions without crowding out the staff who still have to run schemes. The press release does not assign each state to a group. The typology is the finding. The state chapter of the full study is where an operator should look before treating "Indian demography" as one market.

## District delivery still depends on the collector's office

NITI Aayog's Aspirational Districts Programme, launched in January 2018 for 112 of the most under-developed districts, is the published model of skills at the district. NITI anchors it. States drive it, through a committee under the chief secretary or a nodal officer. Ministries take pieces of the indicator set. The collaboration leg of the design explicitly includes the district collector. The ranking is a monthly delta on the Champions of Change portal. None of that is a substitute for iGOT. It is the management system around the officers whose learning iGOT does not yet report by district.

A founder hiring in a state, or selling a skilling product into a department, should read three dates: the DoPT design aim of about two crore users, the 6 March 2026 stock of 1.51 crore onboarded, and the January 2026 distinction between youthful and ageing state budgets. The gap between the design aim and the stock is a capacity fact. It is not a vacancy census, and this brief does not invent one.
