# A Founder’s Map of Urban Housing Missions

## Start from the transaction, not the theme

A company selling into urban India needs to know which official door the transaction uses. The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs runs several, and they do not share a customer, a tender, or a data room. Census 2011, still the ministry’s published urban baseline of 31.16 percent and 377.1 million people, is context. It is not a sales pipeline. Census 2027 will replace that baseline only when the Registrar General publishes tables. Phase I during 2026 records housing condition and amenities. It does not award a contract.

## If the product touches a home loan or a subsidised unit

Use the Interest Subsidy Scheme rules PIB set out on 24 July 2025. The portal named there is pmay-urban.gov.in. Fit the product inside income of ₹9 lakh, a loan of ₹25 lakh, a property of ₹35 lakh, and carpet area of 120 square metres, or do not book the subsidy. Build the servicing system so a yearly instalment is claimed only while the loan is active and more than half the principal is outstanding. The maximum subsidy in that reply is ₹1.80 lakh, at 4 percent on the first ₹8 lakh, for up to 12 years.

If the product is the house itself under Beneficiary Led Construction or Affordable Housing in Partnership, the December 2024 PIB note’s assistance is ₹2.50 lakh a unit, with the central share depending on the state or Union Territory. The buyer of that work is the state mission and the urban local body, after a memorandum. The December 2024 note is not a live tender list. Ask the state for the current sanction.

If the product is rental housing for workers, migrants, or students, read the Affordable Rental Housing vertical before pitching a for-sale apartment. The groups named for special attention, including street vendors under PM SVANidhi, construction workers, and safai karamcharis, are beneficiary categories. They are not a distribution partnership until a state says so.

## If the product touches a municipal system

AMRUT 2.0, in the 27 October 2021 guidelines, buys taps, sewerage, septage, and the plans and reforms around them. The buyer is the state and the city, through a State Water Action Plan, with funds in three instalments and a reform gate from the third year. The technology sub-mission encourages water start-ups. That is the sentence a water company can cite. The sentence it cannot cite is a guarantee of central sanctions after financial year 2025-26, which is the last year printed on that outlay.

Smart Cities command centres exist in all 100 cities. The central mission budget does not. MoHUA’s page says closure was 31 March 2025 and that no new city will be taken up. A founder selling software, cameras, or control-room operations is selling to a special purpose vehicle or a municipal body under state instruction, including Advisory No. 27 of 10 June 2025. Check the 31 March 2026 annex before assuming the city still has open mission projects. Many do not.

## If the product touches a private project for sale

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, is the operating system. Register where sections 3 to 19 require it. Keep seventy percent of allottee collections in the separate scheduled-bank account Section 4 requires, and use that money for land and construction. Measure in carpet area, which is also the subsidy’s measure. Read the state authority’s rules and, if a deadline is moving, MoHUA’s advisory of 31 July 2026 on force majeure extensions. A national “RERA compliance” badge that ignores the state register is not compliance.

## What not to build the company on

A 101st smart city. A revival of the Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme, which closed in 2021 and 2022 after ₹58,885 crore reached 25,04,220 beneficiaries. A 2026 official urbanisation rate. A job-creation claim that goes beyond the December 2024 estimate of 917 crore person-days on the first PMAY-Urban. Each of those has a document that either ended the programme or has not yet published the number.

The open doors are listed without a pitch in the [opportunity landscape](/blog/urbanization-and-housing-opportunity-landscape-railministry-gov-20260330-640). The statute is in the [regulatory outlook](/blog/urbanization-and-housing-regulatory-outlook-railministry-gov-20260330-440). The labour limit is in the [jobs note](/blog/urbanization-and-housing-jobs-and-skills-impact-railministry-gov-20260330-400). Great Indian Company is the index of those readings, not a second source of the figures.
