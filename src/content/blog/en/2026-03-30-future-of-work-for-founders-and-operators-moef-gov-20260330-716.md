# Future of Work in India: Founder and Operator Guide

## Start from the establishment

The codes that govern how a company employs people have been in force since 21 November 2025: the Code on Wages, 2019; the Industrial Relations Code, 2020; the Code on Social Security, 2020; and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020. The labour ministry's Lok Sabha reply of 20 July 2026 says Central Rules were notified on 8 May 2026. The first task is to name the appropriate government for each office, factory, or store. The ministry's handbook covers the central sphere. A state establishment follows that state's rules. Labour is concurrent, and the July reply said some states and Union Territories were still finalising rules.

## The letter

Issue an appointment letter. The ministry's 21 November 2025 note says letters are mandatory. Its media-sector note shows contents that make the letter usable: designation, wages, and social-security entitlement. On a fixed-term contract, the same implementation note says the employee receives permanent-worker benefits and qualifies for gratuity after one year rather than five. If a contractor supplies people, the ministry's contract-worker note puts health and social-security duties on the principal employer.

## The payroll date

In the central-sphere handbook, monthly wages are due before the seventh day of the next month. For an IT or ITES business, the ministry's sector note says to release salary by the 7th. Where the minimum-wage rule applies, overtime is at least twice the normal wage. Wages for the same or similar work are set without gender discrimination.

Night shifts for women are permitted, including in IT and ITES, with their consent and with the safety measures the ministry describes. Consent and safe transport belong in the roster before the shift is published.

## If the product is a platform

The Code on Social Security defines gig work, platform work, and aggregators. The ministry's note says an aggregator contributes 1 to 2 percent of annual turnover, capped at 5 percent of the amount paid or payable to gig and platform workers. Counsel should confirm whether the model is an aggregator's. If it is, the contribution needs a worker-level payment record. The July reply says the definitions support schemes for life and disability cover, accident insurance, health and maternity benefits, and old-age protection. Track the notified scheme. e-Shram, the unorganised-worker database the ministry launched on 26 August 2021, is the registration route the ministry has described. A national gig-worker total does not belong in the deck. NITI Aayog's 2022 report asked for gig workers to be identified in the Periodic Labour Force Survey so that the category has a survey home.

## Skills the company buys

Public short-term training under PMKVY 4.0 was the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship phase for FY 2022-2026, on National Skills Qualification Framework courses, with Skill India Digital as the record. A certificate is a training fact. The appointment letter is the employment fact.
