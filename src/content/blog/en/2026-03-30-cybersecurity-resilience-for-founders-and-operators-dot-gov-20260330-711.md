# What founders and operators owe under India's cyber rules

Start by asking which labels apply to the organisation. Most growing firms carry more than one. The duties do not collapse into the strictest single policy.

## If you are a body corporate, intermediary, data centre, or government organisation

The CERT-In directions of 28 April 2022 apply to service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates and government organisations. Two operator-level duties are unmistakable in the text.

Report the cyber incidents in the direction's annexure within six hours of noticing them or being told about them. The channels printed in the direction are incident@cert-in.org.in, phone 1800-11-4949 and fax 1800-11-6969. Six hours is from notice, not from the end of an investigation.

Enable logs of ICT systems and keep them securely for a rolling 180 days within Indian jurisdiction. Produce them with an incident report, or when CERT-In orders it.

Read the annexure for which incidents qualify, and read the 27 June 2022 extension if you are an MSME or if you run a data centre, VPS, cloud or VPN service. The extension's title, on CERT-In's directions page, covers enforcement timing for MSMEs and subscriber-validation arrangements. The file itself is the source for any date that differs from the April direction.

Name a person who can speak to CERT-In. The MSME baseline calls this a single point of contact for CERT-In and regulators.

## If you meet the MSME classification

The baseline of 1 September 2025 applies to enterprises covered by the Ministry of MSME notification S.O. 1702(E) of 1 June 2020. It is a minimum: fifteen controls, forty-five recommendations, a suggested yearly audit by an empanelled organisation, and a disclaimer that you still have to go further for your own risks.

The controls an operator can assign to an owner this month are concrete. Inventory hardware, software and information assets. Put a firewall at the perimeter and on the host. Use WPA2 or WPA3, and separate guest Wi-Fi. Turn on SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Use licensed endpoint protection and onboard with Cyber Swachhta Kendra. Restrict USB autorun. Remove default passwords. Patch from vendor and CERT-In advisories. Write an incident plan and test it. Keep the 180-day logs. Train people twice a year. Hold vendors to at least this baseline. Back up, test the restore, and keep a short continuity plan for critical applications. Review access at least quarterly and on every exit.

## If you process digital personal data

You are in Data Fiduciary territory under the Act enacted on 11 August 2023. As of 24 September 2026, check the commencement clause before you tell the team a duty is live. Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21 are in force. Rule 4, Consent Managers, arrives one year after 13 November 2025. The notice, safeguard, breach-intimation and significant-fiduciary rules arrive eighteen months after that publication.

Operators should still draft the artefacts, and they should label them with the commencement date. When Rule 6 is in force, the minimum safeguard list includes encryption, obfuscation, masking or virtual tokens; access control; logs and review; backups; a one-year log retention unless another law requires otherwise; a contract clause for any data processor; and technical and organisational measures that make the safeguards real. Rule 7 then requires a plain-language notice to affected people without delay, an immediate description to the Board, and a detailed report within seventy-two hours unless the Board allows longer.

PIB says people must be able to find a contact, and that access, correction, update, erasure and nomination requests are answered within a maximum of 90 days. Rule 14, which commences with the eighteen-month set, requires the grievance system itself to be published and to respond within a reasonable period not exceeding ninety days.

## If a regulator already supervises you

Banks, specified NBFCs, credit information companies and the named All India Financial Institutions follow the Reserve Bank directions in force since 1 April 2024, including a CISO who does not report to the Head of IT and a duty to notify CERT-In and the Reserve Bank. Housing finance companies report cyber incidents to the National Housing Bank.

SEBI-regulated entities follow the CSCRF, including the category they were placed in for this financial year and the SOC path that category requires. Insurers and IRDAI intermediaries follow the 24 April 2023 guidelines and the information-security risk committee.

Telecommunication entities follow the six-hour and twenty-four-hour reports, appoint a resident Indian as Chief Telecommunication Security Officer, and run the SOC and audit duties in the 2024 rules. Firms that are not licensees but use telecom identifiers to recognise customers should read the October 2025 amendment before they treat themselves as outside the telecom cyber rules.

The operator habit that survives all of these labels is a one-page matrix: event, who must be told, in how many hours, and which log proves it.
