# Climate Adaptation for Founders and Operators in India

Start from the site. A national climate slogan does not tell you which week the operation stops.

## Name the hazard that can stop the work

Use the National Disaster Management Authority's split. A plant on a river floodplain sits in the January 2008 flood guideline and in India Meteorological Department rainfall warnings. The authority's flood page ties most precipitation to June through September. A plant in a dense city also sits in the September 2010 urban-flood guideline, including in a week when the river is inside its banks. A shift roster on the plains from March to June sits in IMD's heat tests and in a heat action plan under the October 2019 guideline. A jetty, hatchery, or store on the mainland coast sits in the Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019, and in the hazard line a Press Information Bureau note described on 3 February 2022. A unit in the Himalaya sits in the October 2020 glacial-lake guideline, not in the cyclone guideline.

If the hazard column is wrong, the rest of the plan is about a different business.

## Then ask who pays

Selling into a state adaptation project means an action in a State Action Plan on Climate Change, a proposal prepared with NABARD, and approval by the State Steering Committee. That path is the one PIB described on 5 February 2018. The fund was made a non-scheme in November 2022. The last stock this brief can cite is 30 projects and ₹847.48 crore of sanctioned project cost, from the 5 August 2024 reply. Do not build a sales plan on a new round that PIB has not announced.

Selling cool roofs, warning tools, or farm water management can be a different sale: a city, a health system, or a farmer under an existing mission. That buyer is not a NABARD installment. The tasks are listed on the [opportunity page](/blog/climate-adaptation-opportunity-landscape-makeinindia-20260330-655).

## Price the friction the ministry already printed

In the Andhra Pradesh dairy file, the ministry's list was land, the choice of civil and technical agencies, and the design of an animal hostel. A founder who prices the technology and leaves out the land and the executing agency is repeating that file. It is one project. It is also the only implementation list the August 2024 reply spells out.

Keep mitigation metrics out of the operating review. The June 2025 non-fossil capacity milestone is a real energy result. It does not tell you whether the site survives a flood week.
