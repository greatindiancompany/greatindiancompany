# India Does Not Publish Insurance Penetration State by State

The 3.7 percent figure is a national ratio of premium to GDP. The [IRDAI Annual Report 2024-25](https://irdai.gov.in/en/document-detail?documentId=8375620) does not print a state-wise penetration league table, and this brief will not invent one. Anyone who needs state premium worksheets should start from IRDAI's [Handbook on Indian Insurance Statistics](https://irdai.gov.in/handbook-of-indian-insurance) and read the tables that are actually in the file. What the annual report does publish is where the offices are, and how rural obligations are counted.

## Offices cluster in large towns

On 31 March 2025, life insurers had 12,210 offices, 693 more than a year earlier. Public-sector life offices stayed at 5,004. Private life offices rose from 6,513 to 7,206. IRDAI says around 60 percent of life offices are in Tier I centres, defined as population of one lakh and above. About 0.75 percent are in Tier VI centres, population below 5,000. The tier table prints 7,373 Tier I life offices and 92 Tier VI life offices.

The public-sector life insurer had offices in 712 of 776 districts, which IRDAI calls 92 percent of districts. Private life insurers had offices in 637 districts, 82 percent of districts. Together they covered around 93 percent of districts. In 23 of 36 states and Union Territories, every district had a life insurance office. The report does not name those 23 in the paragraph that states the count.

General insurers, excluding the health-office story below, operated from 8,078 offices against 8,164 the year before. Public-sector general insurers closed 195 offices. Private general insurers added 110. Specialised insurers reduced by one. IRDAI says 5,831 general-insurance offices, 72.18 percent, are in Tier I, and 34 offices, 0.42 percent, are in Tier VI.

Stand-alone health insurers had 1,788 offices, up from 1,657. Of those, 1,433 (80 percent) are in Tier I. The report records one stand-alone health office in Tier V and one in Tier VI.

The [PIB note of 23 April 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2254950&lang=1&reg=3) gives the same national office count: 22,076 insurer offices in March 2025, against 21,338 in March 2024. Life 12,210 plus the general, health, and specialised total of 9,866 is 22,076.

## The rural unit is the Gram Panchayat, not the state

Under the rural, social-sector, and motor third-party regulations discussed in the same report, the rural unit is the Gram Panchayat. Life insurers are mandated to collectively insure 25,000 Gram Panchayats, with each insurer covering at least 10 percent of the population in the panchayats allocated to it. General insurers other than stand-alone health insurers, Agriculture Insurance Company, and ECGC have a collective minimum of 25,000 Gram Panchayats for fire and motor cover on dwellings, shops, and vehicles, at a minimum of 10 percent in allocated panchayats.

That is the official geography of "reach." It is a panchayat obligation and a district office map. It is not a state penetration ranking.
