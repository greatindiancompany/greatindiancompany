# What to read before the October monetary policy meeting

The next Monetary Policy Committee meeting is **5–7 October 2026**. September’s retail and wholesale indices come out after that meeting, on 12 and 14 October. The packet the committee can actually see is already public: the August CPI, the August WPI, and its own 5 August resolution. This is a reading order for that packet. It is not a forecast.

## 1. Start with the retail release, and mark what is provisional

Read the MoSPI press release of 14 September 2026, [CPI for August 2026](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1789381904344_6c792dcf-8a9f-4fca-93d3-99d833bdb358_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_August_2026.pdf), base 2024=100.

Write down four combined rates before reading any commentary:

- August CPI, provisional: **4.82%**. July, final: **4.45%**.
- August food (CFPI), provisional: **5.95%**. July: **5.52%**.
- August rural CPI: **5.23%**. August urban CPI: **4.31%**.
- The 2026 combined path in Annexure IV: 2.74%, 3.21%, 3.40%, 3.48%, 3.93%, 4.38%, 4.45%, 4.82%.

Then read the division table, not only the headline. In August, health was 1.34%, the housing-and-fuels division was 2.61%, restaurants and accommodation were 8.38%, and personal care and miscellaneous goods were 15.17%. Inside the groups, operation of personal transport equipment was 7.40% and other personal effects were 44.56%.

The release says August is provisional, the September CPI is due on 12 October 2026 or the next working day, and field staff collected prices from 1,407 urban markets and 1,465 villages with a 100% response rate in August.

## 2. Read wholesale fuel and manufactured goods next

Read the provisional WPI press note of the same day, [Wholesale Price Index for August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2309977), base 2022–23. The compiling office publishes the series at [eaindustry.nic.in](https://eaindustry.nic.in/download_data_2223.asp).

Write down:

- All commodities: **9.92%** in August, **9.78%** in July. Index 110.8, from 110.0.
- Fuel and power: **22.93%**. Mineral oils: **38.48%**. Electricity: **−1.73%**.
- Manufactured products: **8.37%**, weight 63.13.
- Food index: **7.05%**.
- Response rate on the August provisional: **84.4%**. June was revised from 9.87% provisional to **9.97%** final.

If a cost line is chemicals, textiles, basic metals, or food products, take the group rate from Annexure II rather than 9.92%. Those August rates were 14.30%, 12.63%, 10.88%, and 9.65%.

September WPI is scheduled for 14 October 2026.

## 3. Put the 5 August projection beside those prints

Read the [MPC resolution of 5 August 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63287). The meeting ended before the August indices existed. The resolution’s working retail figure is June at 4.4%. MoSPI’s June combined rate is 4.38%.

Record the projection as published, without updating it yourself:

- CPI for 2026–27: **5.0%**.
- July–September: **4.7%**. October–December: **5.9%**. January–March: **5.5%**. April–June 2027: **5.3%**.
- Core, CPI excluding food and fuel: **4.3%** for the year. The 3.9% core reading, and the 2.3–2.5% reading excluding precious metals, are for May–June.
- Repo rate: **5.25%**. Stance: neutral. Vote: unanimous. Next meeting: 5–7 October 2026.

July at 4.45% and August at 4.82% can be set next to the 4.7% Q2 projection. They do not complete the quarter. September is the missing month, and it will be published on 12 October, after the committee has met.

## 4. Separate three questions that the headline mixes together

Use the two releases to answer them on paper:

1. **Target variable.** Combined CPI is the policy index. August was 4.82%, above the 4% point target and below the 6% upper tolerance. The target dates and the band are explained in the note on [the August hold](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/inflation-trends-policy-explainer-20260330-054).
2. **Household basket.** Food, rural versus urban, vegetables that moved in opposite directions, rent, health, and jewellery. The household note walks through those weights and items.
3. **Firm costs.** WPI fuel and manufactured products. A 9.92% wholesale rate and a 4.82% retail rate can both be true. The gap is a margin question, not a reason to average the two indices.

For a state decision, use Annexure III rather than 4.82%. Telangana was 6.27% and Delhi was 2.61% in the same provisional month. State item indices on [eSankhyiki](https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/) carry MoSPI’s warning on thin samples.

## 5. After the meeting, read only the new pages

On 12 October, check whether September combined CPI, food, rural, and urban extended the monthly climb, and whether the personal-care division and the transport-operation group did the same. On 14 October, check fuel and power, mineral oils, manufactured products, and whether the August wholesale rate was revised the way June was. Then read the October resolution for a new projection. Until that resolution exists, the 5.0% year path and the 5.9% Q3 path are the committee’s latest published numbers.

The month that produced this packet is summarised in [retail inflation in August](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/inflation-trends-what-changed-20260330-004).
