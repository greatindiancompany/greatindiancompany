# What to re-read in India's payments rulebook this quarter

A founder checking "what changed" in Indian financial digitalization this quarter will not find a single new law that replaced UPI, account aggregators, or the Digital Rupee. The useful work is narrower. Several Reserve Bank directions are already binding, one card-issuer date sits just ahead of this note, and the latest official UPI snapshot is a July 2026 reading published in August, not a figure invented for a pitch.

This note is dated 24 September 2026. It does not forecast the next circular.

## Authentication is already a compliance date, not a proposal

The [Reserve Bank of India (Authentication mechanisms for digital payment transactions) Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898) were issued on 25 September 2025. Payment system providers and participants, including banks and non-banks, had to comply by 1 April 2026 unless a clause said otherwise.

The operating rule is plain. Domestic digital payment transactions need at least two distinct factors of authentication, unless an exemption in the annex applies. The same directions do not apply to cross-border digital payment transactions. They do set a later date for cards: by 1 October 2026, card issuers must validate non-recurring cross-border card-not-present transactions when an overseas merchant or acquirer asks for authentication, and they must register their Bank Identification Numbers with card networks. A risk-based check for cross-border card-not-present transactions is on the same date.

For a bank or a card issuer, that 1 October 2026 date is the concrete item inside the next few weeks. For a UPI app that only moves domestic payments, the two-factor rule is already behind the compliance date. If a loss arises from a transaction that did not follow these directions, the issuer compensates the customer in full.

## Digital credit and payment aggregation did not reset this quarter

Two consolidations from 2025 are the texts to reopen, not replace.

The [Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848), issued on 8 May 2025, repealed the 2 September 2022 digital lending guidelines and the 8 June 2023 default-loss-guarantee circular. Most of the directions took effect immediately. The multi-lender display rules took effect on 1 November 2025. Reporting of digital lending apps on the Reserve Bank's Centralised Information Management System was due by 15 June 2025. A reader in this quarter should treat those dates as past obligations, then check whether the app, the regulated entity, and the lending service provider still match the text.

The [Reserve Bank of India (Regulation of Payment Aggregators) Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896), issued on 15 September 2025, came into effect immediately unless a clause said otherwise. Non-bank aggregators need authorisation. Banks do not. An entity that carried on only physical point-of-sale aggregation had to apply by 31 December 2025 or wind up by 28 February 2026. That window is closed. A payment aggregator that also performs lending-service-provider work falls under the digital lending directions. An aggregator that only handles payments does not.

## The UPI number worth quoting, and the page that will replace it

The Press Information Bureau factsheet [UPI: Transforming India's Payment Landscape](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=150962&lang=1), posted on 24 August 2026, gives a July 2026 reading: 741 banks live on UPI, 2,365.8 crore transactions, and a value of ₹29.87 lakh crore. The same factsheet traces the public system from a pilot in April 2016 with 21 member banks, a public rollout in August 2016, and BHIM in December 2016. The National Payments Corporation of India still publishes the monthly series on its [UPI product statistics](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics) page. Use that page for any month after July 2026. Do not carry the July reading forward as if it were August or September.

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology describes the same rail on the [Digital India UPI page](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/). That page is the public-programme description. It is not a substitute for the monthly table or for a Reserve Bank direction.

## What this quarter is not

Nothing in the payments rulebook above is a drinking-water or sanitation programme, and a water-ministry link does not document UPI, account aggregators, or digital lending. Account-aggregator permissions still sit in the [NBFC-Account Aggregator Directions, 2016](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10598), updated as on 6 September 2024. The Digital Rupee pilots were announced for wholesale settlement on 1 November 2022 and retail use on 1 December 2022. Those design papers are still the right place to read what e₹ is. They are not a quarterly volume release.
