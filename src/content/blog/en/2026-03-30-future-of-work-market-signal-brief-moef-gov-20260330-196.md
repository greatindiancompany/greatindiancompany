# Future of Work in India: Market Signals from Labour Rules

## What counts as a signal

A future-of-work signal that can be checked is either a rule in force or a survey series. Hiring mood, and a brokerage note, are commentary. The Ministry of Labour and Employment, NITI Aayog, and the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) are the records used here.

## Platform work is a legal category

The Code on Social Security, 2020, in force from 21 November 2025, defines gig work, platform work, and aggregators. The ministry's implementation note sets the aggregator contribution at 1 to 2 percent of annual turnover, capped at 5 percent of the amount paid or payable to those workers, and describes an Aadhaar-linked Universal Account Number so that welfare benefits can move with a worker across states.

NITI Aayog's June 2022 report, *India's Booming Gig and Platform Economy*, recommended that official enumerations, including PLFS, identify gig workers. That recommendation is a signal about measurement. Estimates printed in the 2022 report stay in that report.

The ministry launched e-Shram on 26 August 2021 as a national database of unorganised workers, including gig and platform workers, and has described a platform-aggregator module on that portal. A registration is an administrative act. PLFS is the household survey. The two series answer different questions.

## IT and ITES: a calendar, and a contract form

The 21 November 2025 note includes a section for IT and ITES workers. Salary is to be released by the 7th of the month. Equal pay for equal work applies. Women may work night shifts with consent and safety measures. Social security is tied to fixed-term employment and to mandatory appointment letters.

That is a cash-timing signal and a contract-form signal for software and business-process employers. Demand for IT and BPM work lives in client orders and in company disclosures. It is not a series published by the labour ministry, and this brief does not translate announcements into a demand figure.

## The survey that measures work

PLFS, from the National Statistics Office, estimates labour force participation, the worker population ratio, and unemployment for rural and urban India. The press note for the annual report covering January to December 2025 is dated 27 March 2026. A reader who wants a level, or a change, in employment reads that release and any later bulletin.

## How to hold the three together

Use the social-security code for platform design, the IT and ITES section for payroll and shift rules, and PLFS for the employment picture. Mixing a registration drive, a services-export cycle, and a household survey into one "market is up" sentence drops the signal.
