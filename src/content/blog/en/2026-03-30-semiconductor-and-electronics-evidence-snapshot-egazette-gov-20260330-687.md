This page is a ledger. Each figure is tied to the government text that states it. Where a later text uses a different number, both dates are kept. Nothing here is a Great Indian Company estimate. The ledger is current to 24 September 2026. Parliament says the project-level file lives at [ism.gov.in](https://www.ism.gov.in/).

The 12 projects, in the 15 July note, are one silicon fab, one silicon-carbide fab, one integrated gallium-nitride micro-LED display fab, and nine packaging units. Commercial production had started at Micron, Kaynes, and CG Semi. One more start was expected in 2026 and was not named.

The 12 August 2025 four were approved together at around ₹4,600 crore and 2,034 skilled professionals, taking the count to 10 and the cumulative commitment to around ₹1.60 lakh crore. The 29 February 2024 three were expected, on that day, to create 20,000 direct jobs and about 60,000 indirect jobs. That jobs sentence is not the July 2026 figure of around 32,000 for all 12.

## Design, as of the July 2026 texts

The [Design Linked Incentive notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Notification-Design-Linked-Incentive-Scheme.pdf) caps product-design support at ₹15 crore per application and deployment support at ₹30 crore. The 15 July 2026 note and the 22 July reply agree on 24 projects with financial support and 105 startups or MSMEs with design-tool access. Parliament puts tool use above 87 lakh hours. The Cabinet note puts university design training at 315 universities and around 68,000 students. Chips to Startup, in the parliamentary reply, covers 320 organisations and more than 240 lakh hours of tool use.

The 7 February 2026 note, speaking as of January 2026, reports different counters: nearly ₹430 crore of venture capital into scheme startups, about 2.25 crore tool hours, 16 startup tape-outs with six chips fabricated at advanced nodes including 12 nm, and 56 academic chips at 180 nm at the Semiconductor Laboratory in Mohali. Those January figures stay in January. They are not silently updated to the July hours.
