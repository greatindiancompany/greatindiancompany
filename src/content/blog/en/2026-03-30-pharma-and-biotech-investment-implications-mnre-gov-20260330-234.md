# How to Read a Pharma PLI Award Before Underwriting the Plant

A production-linked incentive is a claim on eligible sales after a plant produces, measured the way the scheme guidelines measure them. It is not a cheque that arrives when the board approves capex. For Indian pharma, the underwriting file has to name which of two Department of Pharmaceuticals schemes is on the letter, because the cash-flow shape is different.

## Bulk-drug awards are greenfield and list-bound

The 21 March 2020 cabinet decision pays eligible manufacturers of identified bulk drugs for incremental sales over the 2019–20 base year, for six years. The rates in that decision are 20 percent for fermentation-based products and 10 percent for chemical-synthesis products. The envelope is ₹6,940 crore over eight years. The original identified set was 53 products. The 6 February 2026 Lok Sabha reply says the scheme provides incentives on the sale of 41 identified products manufactured through greenfield projects, with 48 projects approved for 33 bulk drugs.

For a credit committee, the operative questions are on the approval letter and the current guidelines, not in a sector essay. Is the molecule one of the products the firm was actually approved to make? Is the project greenfield, as the 2026 reply requires for this scheme? Which base-year sales will the incentive calculation use? Has the Department’s later corrigendum, including the one dated 4 May 2026 on the schemes index, changed a claim rule the model still assumes from the 2020 cabinet text? This brief does not restate that May corrigendum. The PDF does.

A 1 September 2022 official note said 14 of 51 then-approved projects had been commissioned. That is a commissioning fact as of that date. It is a reminder that approval and first commercial sale are different events. Incentive does not start because a project appears on a list.

## Pharmaceuticals awards can be brownfield, and the pool is grouped

The pharmaceuticals PLI, notified on 3 March 2021 with an outlay of ₹15,000 crore, groups applicants by FY 2019–20 global manufacturing revenue of pharmaceutical goods. The gazette allocates about ₹11,000 crore to Group A (revenue of ₹5,000 crore or more), ₹2,250 crore to Group B (₹500 crore to ₹5,000 crore), and ₹1,750 crore to Group C (below ₹500 crore), with an MSME sub-group inside Group C. The pools include administrative expenditure. SIDBI was named the project management agency when applications opened in 2021 through the portal pli-pharma.udyamimitra.in.

The February 2026 reply says 55 applicants are approved, for pharmaceuticals and in-vitro diagnostics, through greenfield and brownfield projects. Brownfield matters for underwriting: an incentive on incremental eligible sales from an existing site is not the same risk as a new fermentation block. The reply also says this scheme covers key starting materials, intermediates, and APIs other than those notified under the bulk-drug scheme, plus high-value categories such as biopharmaceuticals, complex generics, patented or near-expiry drugs, autoimmune drugs, and anti-cancer drugs. A model that stacks a bulk-drug PLI rate on top of a pharmaceuticals PLI for the same notified product has left the text.

The year-by-year rate for this second scheme is in the 1 June 2021 operational guidelines and later corrigenda. Use that table. The cabinet percentages of 20 and 10 belong to the bulk-drug scheme’s 2020 decision.

## Parks change the cost stack, on a public timetable

If the plant is proposed inside a bulk-drug park, the public capex is the common infrastructure, not the tenant’s reactor cost. The February 2026 reply gives total project cost and common-infrastructure cost for Jambusar, Haroli, and Nakkapalli, and a central grant capped at ₹1,000 crore per park. It also says the scheme runs through FY 2026–27, and it describes works still in progress. A tenant model that assumes steam, effluent treatment, and solvent recovery are already available is ahead of that reply. Plot price, allotment status, and the state implementing agency’s schedule are state documents. The central reply tells you the grant ceiling and the stage of the common works.

## What the award does not price

The cabinet’s expected incremental sales of ₹46,400 crore over eight years is the scheme’s stated expectation, not a revenue line for one company. Quality systems under the Drugs Rules, including the revised Schedule M notified on 28 December 2023, can delay commercial sale even when a PLI letter exists. CDSCO and the state licensing authority sit on a different file from the Department of Pharmaceuticals. An investor who has the PLI letter and lacks the manufacturing licence has half the permission set.
