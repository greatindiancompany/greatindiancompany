# How founders should operate on India's digital credit rails

If you run the app, assume you are the lending service provider until a lawyer shows you are the regulated entity. That single assumption fixes most product arguments in Indian digital credit. The [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848) leave the loan, the disbursement, the repayment account, the credit-information reporting, and the grievance outcome with the bank, NBFC, or other regulated lender. Your app can be excellent and still be an agent.

## Design the screen as a disclosure, not a funnel

Name the lender before the borrower accepts. If more than one lender can fund the request, show every matching offer with amount, tenor, annual percentage rate, monthly repayment, and penal charges, and link each key fact statement. Name the lenders that do not match. Do this the same way for similar borrowers. If you sort the list, publish the metric you sort on. The directions have required that view since 1 November 2025, and they incorporate the official meaning of dark patterns. A pre-checked box that hides a second lender is a product defect, not a growth tactic.

When the contract is signed, send the key fact statement, sanction letter, terms, statements, and privacy policies to the verified email or SMS as digitally signed documents on the lender's letterhead. Put the nodal grievance officer on the app, on your site, and in that statement. Link the lender's own page, the Complaint Management System, and the Sachet portal. If you are assigned to recover, the borrower hears from the lender by email or SMS before you call. You do not get the contact list. Camera, microphone, and location are one-time, for KYC, with consent.

Money is the test that matters more than the interface. The borrower receives the disbursement, except for the narrow cases the directions list. The borrower repays the lender, not your pool account. Your fee comes from the lender. A default loss guarantee, if you offer one, is capped at five per cent of the amount disbursed from a fixed set of loans, posted as cash, a lien-marked fixed deposit, or a bank guarantee, and disclosed on your site every month within seven working days. It does not cover revolving facilities offered digitally or credit cards. Invoking it does not erase the borrower's debt.

## If the product is data or a UPI credit line, stay inside that product

An account aggregator is a different company. The [2016 directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=10598) let you retrieve and present financial information under a consent artefact that states purpose, recipient, and expiry. You do not keep the information, you do not take the customer's bank password, you do not execute the transfer, and you do not bolt on another business. If your current product needs a warehouse of raw statements, you are not an aggregator. You may still be a lender or a service provider, and then the lending rules apply to whatever credit you touch.

Credit on UPI is a bank product. The [circular of 4 September 2023](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12532), updated on 12 February 2025, lets a scheduled commercial bank, including a small finance bank, link a pre-sanctioned line after the customer consents. You can build the payment experience on MeitY's [UPI rail](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/). You cannot invent the limit. Domestic payments still need two distinct authentication factors under the [directions that took effect for compliance on 1 April 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898), unless an exemption applies. The issuer carries the loss if it does not.

File every digital lending app on the Reserve Bank's Centralised Information Management System and say, in public, only what that filing is: a report by the regulated entity. It is not an authorisation of your brand. The Reserve Bank says it does not verify the data.
