# The Policy Shift from Course Lists to Civil Service Competencies

Two policy designs now define Indian state capacity. One rebuilds how civil servants learn. The other rebuilds how states are paid to invest. Both move the test from an announcement to a recorded competency or a recorded reform.

## What DoPT actually built

The Department of Personnel and Training describes the National Programme for Civil Services Capacity Building, Mission Karmayogi, as a shift onto a digital learning framework. The iGOT-Karmayogi platform is meant to give anytime, anywhere learning to about two crore government employees, a scale DoPT says traditional training could not reach. The Capacity Building Commission coordinates annual capacity-building plans and watches training institutions. Karmayogi Bharat, a not-for-profit company fully owned by the government and under DoPT, owns and runs the platform, including its content and intellectual property, and is responsible for a content marketplace.

That is an institutional split, and it matters. DoPT holds the programme. The Commission holds the plan and the supervision of training capacity. The company holds the software, the courses and the assessments. A ministry that still treats capacity as a nomination to a residential course is using the old object.

On 11 March 2026, PIB published the government's figures as on 6 March 2026: more than 1.51 crore users onboarded, over 4,400 courses across domain, functional and behavioural competencies, and more than 7.7 crore course completions. Memoranda of understanding were in place with 29 states and Union Territories for implementation below the state capital. A Cabinet Secretariat Coordination Unit, chaired by the Cabinet Secretary, monitors the mission. A monitoring and evaluation framework assigns key performance indicators to DoPT, ministries and departments, Karmayogi Bharat, and the Capacity Building Commission.

Karmayogi Bharat has also been recognised by the National Council for Vocational Education and Training as an awarding body. The same PIB note says the platform offers NCVET-approved qualifications in public policy, AI-led digital transformation, and public governance and administration. Courses on the three new criminal laws had more than 33 lakh enrolments. District-level monitoring of learning performance is not undertaken on iGOT. The competency system is real at the person and the state. It is not yet a district dashboard.

## The other shift: loans that buy reforms

SASCI began in 2020-21 as special assistance for state capital expenditure, in the form of 50-year interest-free loans. The 27 March 2026 guidelines keep that instrument and add a reform price list. Part III pays states that grow their own-resource capital expenditure by more than 10 percent. Part IV pays, in Rs 50 crore blocks, for named public-finance systems: at least six of seven IFMIS modules, a works-management system plus e-procurement, live integration of those systems, and full digitisation of drawing and disbursing officers, treasuries and works divisions. Part V pays for mining-administration reforms, including a chief-secretary coordination committee and a published auction calendar. The incentive, once released, may be spent on capital projects in any sector. The reform is the condition. The project is the use of proceeds.

The Fifteenth Finance Commission's report, summarised by PIB on 1 February 2021, already pointed this way. Horizontal devolution gave a 2.5 percent weight to tax and fiscal effort and a 12.5 percent weight to demographic performance, beside population, area, forest and ecology, and income distance. Vertical devolution was held at 41 percent of the divisible pool for 2021-26, about one percentage point below the Fourteenth Finance Commission's 42 percent, with the adjustment tied to the new Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh. The 2026 loan guidelines go further: untied SASCI funds for states are allocated in proportion to each state's share under the Sixteenth Finance Commission award.

## What the shift asks of a reader

A policy team that still files "training conducted" as the capacity metric is measuring the object DoPT has tried to retire. The live metrics are onboarded users, course completions, state MoUs, and whether a capacity-building plan exists for the role. A finance team that still files "allocation announced" is measuring the object the March 2026 guidelines retired. The live metrics are the finance secretary's certificate on PFMS, utilization, closure of scheme accounts, and the CAG's view of the IT reforms.
