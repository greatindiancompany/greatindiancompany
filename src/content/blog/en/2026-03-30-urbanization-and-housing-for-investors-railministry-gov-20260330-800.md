# How an Investor Should Read Urban Housing in India

## The position has to name its cash flow

Urban housing in India can mean a mortgage subsidy, a developer’s escrow, a municipal water contract, or a finished civic asset with no further central grant. These do not share a yield. The official record is good enough to say which cash flow exists. It is not a price index, and this note does not supply one.

## The mortgage book that is still open

The Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme is a closed book. PIB’s 24 July 2025 reply: subsidy up to ₹2.67 lakh a house, middle-income cover ended 31 March 2021, economically weaker and low-income cover ended 31 March 2022, ₹58,885 crore released to 25,04,220 beneficiaries. There is nothing left to originate under those windows.

The open subsidy is the Interest Subsidy Scheme inside PMAY-Urban 2.0, effective 1 September 2024. Maximum ₹1.80 lakh. Income up to ₹9 lakh. Loan up to ₹25 lakh. Property up to ₹35 lakh. Carpet area up to 120 square metres. Four percent on the first ₹8 lakh, up to 12 years, five yearly instalments, only while the loan is active and more than half the principal is outstanding. An investor in a housing finance company should underwrite the instalment condition, not the sanction letter alone. Prepayment can switch the subsidy off. A loan outside the caps was never in it.

The December 2024 PIB note’s ₹10 lakh crore investment figure and ₹2.30 lakh crore of government assistance describe the scheme’s published scale for 1 crore additional families. They are not a guidance range for a listed developer’s pre-sales. Unit assistance of ₹2.50 lakh under partnership and beneficiary-led construction is a transfer tied to an eligible house. In a general-category state the central piece of that assistance is ₹1.50 lakh. Models that book ₹2.50 lakh as a central inflow in those states are wrong on the ministry’s own split.

## The developer book is a statute, not a mission

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act requires, in Section 4, that seventy percent of amounts realised from allottees sit in a separate scheduled-bank account for land and construction. That limits the use of customer advances. It is a feature of project cash flow in every state where registration applies. It is also why a project that is not on the state register is a different risk from one that is. MoHUA lists a 31 July 2026 advisory on force majeure extensions of registration. An extension is a timing event. It is not a demand event. Carpet area is the legal measure and the subsidy measure. Super built-up area is a basis the Act was written to retire in registered sales.

Affordable Rental Housing is the vertical aimed at workers, migrants, and students. It is a hold-and-rent exposure if a private entity takes it up. It should not be valued as a for-sale launch.

## The municipal book is mostly spent at the Centre

Smart Cities central assistance is not a growth capex story. Outlay ₹48,000 crore, closure 31 March 2025, claims ₹47,458 crore, no new city. As reported to the Lok Sabha as on 31 March 2026, 7,790 of 8,064 projects were complete, and the partnership subset was 197 projects worth ₹9,277 crore, with 194 complete. Partnership was a thin slice of a ₹1,64,811 crore project book, and that slice is largely built. Residual value is operation of command centres and whatever role a state’s chief secretary assigns the special purpose vehicle under the 10 June 2025 advisory. Price that as a service contract with a municipal counterparty, after reading the city line in the annex. A city at 100 percent is not a tender pipeline.

AMRUT 2.0’s indicative ₹2,99,000 crore, including the central share of ₹86,760 crore, was published for 2021-22 through 2025-26. Ten percent of million-plus city allocations were to be in partnership mode, and credit ratings and municipal bonds are named reforms. A bond or a partnership bid still has to point at a state water action plan and at the property-tax gate that protects later instalments. This note does not roll the outlay into a new five-year period.

## The information the multiple is missing

The last official urban share MoHUA publishes is the 2011 table: 31.16 percent, 377.1 million people. Census 2027’s reference date is 1 March 2027. Until those tables exist, a terminal growth rate built on a rounder urbanisation percentage is an assumption, and it should be labelled as one. Great Indian Company will not replace the missing table with a global benchmark.
