# A 90-Day Reading Checklist for India DPI Disclosures

## What the next 90 days are for

From this brief's update date of 23 September 2026, ninety days is a reading window. It is not a predicted event calendar. The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 already points at the series worth reopening. This checklist names those series. It does not say they will move, and it does not report a reading taken after 6 March.

## Reopen these series, in this order

1. UPI monthly volume and value, and the count of banks live. The note's last stated month is January 2026: 21.70 billion transactions, over ₹28.33 lakh crore, and 691 banks. The footnote is NPCI's UPI statistics page.
2. Aadhaar generation and authentication. The note says more than 144 crore numbers as of March 2026, and over 2,707 crore authentications in 2024-25. The footnotes point to the UIDAI dashboard and a separate press release.
3. DigiLocker users and documents issued. The last stated point is 67.63 crore users on 5 March 2026, and over 950 crore documents by March 2026.
4. UMANG registrations, transactions, and services. The last stated point is 10.25 crore registrations, 723.36 crore transactions, and more than 2,400 services on 5 March 2026.
5. API Setu's catalogue. The last stated point, for March 2026, is 8,036 APIs, 6,592 consumers, 2,559 publishers, and 10,530 organisations. The footnote is the API Setu dashboard.
6. Direct Benefit Transfer. The last stated points are cumulative transfers crossing ₹49.09 lakh crore as of January 2026, and reported savings of more than ₹4.31 lakh crore from 2015 to March 2024.
7. DIKSHA sessions, users, enrolments, completions, and certificates, last stated as of 5 March 2026: 566 crore sessions and 2.11 crore registered users, among the other learning counts in the note.
8. Account-aggregator participation on the Department of Financial Services page, last stated as of 31 March 2026, with the progress figures credited on that page to Sahamati.

India Stack Global is the showcase page for the named platforms. It is a map of what exists. It is not the monthly series.

## How to log a real change

Write down the as-of date, the unit, and whether the new figure replaces a stock or a flow. Users, banks, and APIs are stocks. A month of payments or a year of authentications is a flow. A higher number with a later date is a change. A reprinted March number is not.

## What not to put on the list

Do not add a state ranking, a private-app market share, or a capital-spending line for UPI. The March note does not contain them. A 90-day checklist cannot assume a release will.
