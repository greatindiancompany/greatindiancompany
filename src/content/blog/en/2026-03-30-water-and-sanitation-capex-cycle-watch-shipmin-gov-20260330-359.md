# The capital cycle in rural taps, urban sewage, and the Ganga

These three programmes are not at the same point in the capital cycle. Rural drinking water has a newly enlarged ceiling and a still-small stream of fresh releases. Urban AMRUT projects are largely awarded, with a thinner book of physically completed works. Ganga-basin sewage treatment is a sanctioned capacity of which a stated portion is already built.

Reading them as one "water capex upcycle" hides which rupee is still a permission and which rupee is concrete.

## Rural drinking water: a ceiling, then a reimbursement

On 10 March 2026 the Cabinet set JJM 2.0's total outlay at ₹8.69 lakh crore and central assistance at ₹3.59 lakh crore, through December 2028. The central figure replaces a 2019-20 central approval of ₹2.08 lakh crore. The cycle this opens is service delivery: in-village maintenance, a digital identity for every village water system, and payments linked to individual schemes.

The first large cash movement the ministry reported inside FY 2026-27 does not look like a fresh construction boom. As on 6 August 2026, ₹5,289.09 crore of the ₹6,154.96 crore released was a reimbursement to ten states for approved schemes they had kept building with their own funds during the transition. The new rail, SNA SPARSH, had carried ₹865.87 crore across 13 states. In capital-cycle language, the Centre was catching up to work already in the ground, and was starting to pay scheme by scheme.

That pattern matters for anyone timing orders. A December 2028 outlay can still produce years of contracts. The August print shows that the near-term central cash was dominated by old approved schemes, not by a new menu of tenders announced that week.

## Urban projects: award runs ahead of physical completion

AMRUT 2.0's approved State Water Action Plans, as described to the Lok Sabha on 6 August 2026, contain 8,777 projects costing ₹1,97,191.85 crore, including operation and maintenance. The capital inside that book is uneven. Water supply is 3,572 projects and ₹1,18,968.38 crore. Sewerage and septage is 605 projects and ₹71,133.01 crore. Fewer contracts, larger contracts.

As on 9 July 2026, 7,687 projects with capex of ₹1,37,130.13 crore had been awarded, and works worth ₹66,472 crore had been physically completed. Awarded capex is the order book the states have placed. Completed works are the cycle's realised half. The gap between those two rupee figures is the live construction period, not a residual to be announced later.

Water-body rejuvenation (2,951 projects, ₹6,003.34 crore) and parks (1,649 projects, ₹1,087.12 crore) sit in the same approved total. They are not sewage plants. A capex screen that counts every AMRUT project as treatment capacity will overstate treatment.

## Ganga sewage: capacity added year by year

Namami Gange is implemented by the National Mission for Clean Ganga under the Department of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation. The department site is [jalshakti-dowr.gov.in](https://jalshakti-dowr.gov.in/).

Two PIB notes show the cycle moving. As of December 2025, the ministry said 3,977 million litres a day had been created, out of 6,610 million litres a day taken up across 218 projects costing ₹35,698 crore, with 138 projects operational. In FY 2025-26, a 21 April 2026 note said 538.03 million litres a day had been added through 18 projects in Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jharkhand, West Bengal, and Bihar, and that 28 sewage treatment plants were completed in the year, against 22 the year before, at an investment of nearly ₹4,700 crore. A later parliamentary statement, till June 2026, puts completed capacity at 4,263 million litres a day across 146 projects, inside a book of 219 projects and ₹35,970 crore.

The December, April, and June statements use slightly different project counts because they are different as-on dates. The direction they share is that capacity is being commissioned inside a book that is not yet fully built. Some of those projects, the April note says, are being implemented under the hybrid annuity model. The note does not say that the whole book is annuity-financed.

Watch the ceiling, the release, the award, and the commissioned plant as separate clocks.
