# India's defence record is a domestic series, not a world ranking

## What a fair comparison uses

A global benchmark needs two countries measured the same way. The Ministry of Defence pages a reader can open measure India against earlier Indian years. That is a real comparison. It is not a rank against another producer.

Three series are solid enough to repeat, each with its own notice.

Production, from the 9 August 2025 release: Rs 1,50,590 crore in FY 2024-25, 18 percent above the previous year's Rs 1.27 lakh crore, and 90 percent above Rs 79,071 crore in FY 2019-20. A PIB note of 20 November 2025 gives the FY 2023-24 figure more tightly, at Rs 1,27,434 crore, and recalls Rs 46,429 crore in 2014-15.

Exports, from the year-end export release: Rs 23,622 crore in FY 2024-25, against Rs 21,083 crore in FY 2023-24. The November 2025 note also records Rs 686 crore in FY 2013-14.

Budget, from the 1 February 2025 allocation release: Rs 6,81,210.27 crore for the Ministry in FY 2025-26, of which capital outlay on defence services is Rs 1,80,000 crore.

## Why these three cannot be stacked

Production of about Rs 1.51 lakh crore is the value of output. Exports of Rs 23,622 crore are a subset that crossed the border, on the Ministry's export definition, not a remainder left after subtracting from production. The Ministry budget of Rs 6.81 lakh crore includes pay, pensions, and civil organisations as well as buying equipment. Capital outlay of Rs 1.80 lakh crore is the investment slice of that budget, not the industry's sales.

A chart that places "budget", "production", and "exports" on one axis as if they were one growing object is a presentation error. Each series can be compared with its own past. None of them is India's share of world defence spending. These notices do not publish that share, and this brief will not borrow one from elsewhere.

## A second production figure for the same year

The 9 August 2025 release states Rs 1,50,590 crore for FY 2024-25 and headlines it as Rs 1.51 lakh crore. The 20 November 2025 note calls the same year's production Rs 1.54 lakh crore. Both pages are official. They are not interchangeable. When a document needs one rupee count, use Rs 1,50,590 crore and cite the August release. Treat Rs 1.54 lakh crore as the November note's rounded wording, and do not invent a revision story the note does not tell.

## What "ahead" can mean in this record

Inside India's own pages, a few comparisons are legitimate.

Exports in FY 2024-25 are higher than in FY 2023-24 by the Rs 2,539 crore the export release states. Production in FY 2024-25 is higher than in FY 2019-20 by the 90 percent that release states. The private share of exports is larger than the DPSU share of exports, while the November note says the private share of production is about 23 percent. Domestic procurement is a budget choice: Rs 1,11,544.83 crore of the FY 2025-26 modernisation budget was earmarked for Indian sources.

"Ahead of other countries" is a different sentence. It needs another government's number, the same definition, and the same year. Until that pair is on the table, the honest benchmark is India's series against itself.
