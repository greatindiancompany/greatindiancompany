# India's Insurance Book Is Life-Heavy Against a Non-Life World

The comparison worth making is the one IRDAI prints, not a league table assembled from secondary blogs. In the [Annual Report 2024-25](https://irdai.gov.in/en/document-detail?documentId=8375620), IRDAI cites the Swiss Re Sigma World Insurance Report for 2024 global figures: life penetration 3 percent and density USD 388; non-life penetration 4.3 percent and density USD 555; overall penetration 7.3 percent and density USD 943.

India's own 2024-25 print in the same report is overall penetration of 3.7 percent, life 2.7 percent, and non-life 1 percent. Density is USD 97 overall, USD 72 life, and USD 25 non-life. The [PIB note of 23 April 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2254950&lang=1&reg=3) restates the India side of that print.

Life penetration in India (2.7 percent) sits near the global life figure IRDAI quotes (3 percent). Non-life is the wide gap: 1 percent in India against 4.3 percent globally, and USD 25 of non-life density against USD 555.

## The mix is inverted

Swiss Re volumes reproduced by IRDAI for 2024 put world premium at USD 7,799.33 billion, 41 percent life and 59 percent non-life. India in that table is USD 141.16 billion, 74 percent life (USD 104.84 billion) and 26 percent non-life (USD 36.32 billion). PIB's independent statement that life is about 74 percent of Indian premium income matches that split.

Rank and depth diverge on purpose. IRDAI says India was the 10th largest market by nominal premium in 2024, with a 1.8 percent global share. The United States and China are first and second. IRDAI, again citing Swiss Re, says US premium was USD 3.5 trillion in 2024, almost 4.4 times the Chinese market, and that the US share of global premium rose to 45 percent.

## Read the year labels before comparing bars

IRDAI's narrative sentences give India's penetration and density for the financial year 2024-25. A note under the comparison table says the India data in that table cover April 2023 to March 2024, while the rest of the world is calendar 2023. The select-country appendix marks India with a hash and says those rows are financial years 2023-24 and 2024-25, showing India at 2.8 / 1.0 / 3.7 and then 2.7 / 1.0 / 3.7 for life, non-life, and total. Use the narrative sentences for the latest India year. Use the appendix when you need the two-year India pair next to named countries. Do not treat every column as the same twelve months.

Real growth in 2024, in the same Swiss Re tables, was 1.7 percent for India and 5.2 percent for the world. India's non-life real growth in that table is 0.2 percent, against 4.7 percent for the world. That is the operating gap behind the penetration gap.

The path from this gap toward 2047, without inventing a target ratio, is the [mid-term trajectory](/blog/insurance-penetration-mid-term-trajectory-ayush-gov-20260330-536).
