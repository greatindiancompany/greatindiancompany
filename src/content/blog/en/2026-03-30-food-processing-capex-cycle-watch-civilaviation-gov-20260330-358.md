# Food Processing In India: Where Processing Capex Actually Sits

## Watch the asset, not a spending cycle

This brief does not call a capex cycle, and it does not put a rupee figure on one. The assets Indian food-processing policy actually names are easier to list than a national spending wave.

## Cold chain and processing lines under PMKSY

The PMKSY page lists Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure and Creation or Expansion of Food Processing and Preservation Capacities as components under implementation. Cold-chain guidelines published by the ministry have described farm-level infrastructure in the crop catchment, a distribution hub with multi-temperature storage, and refrigerated or insulated transport, with irradiation able to stand alone. Eligible facilities in that older guideline include pack houses, pre-cooling, cold storage, controlled-atmosphere storage, freezers, and ripening chambers. Standalone pieces other than irradiation were not the shape the guideline said it would support.

Those guidelines have been revised before. The PMKSY page is the place to confirm which version applies before a project counts grant-eligible civil work. Grant rates and caps are printed there and in the guideline. They are not repeated here.

Operation Greens is capex aimed at perishables. The page ties the original vertical to tomato, onion, and potato, and says the set was widened to 22 perishables, with examples that include mango, banana, apple, pineapple, carrot, cauliflower, and beans.

## What is not the live new-build track

Mega Food Parks, and Creation of Backward and Forward Linkages, are on the discontinued list for the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with committed liability still provided for. Capex that assumes a fresh Mega Food Park approval is watching a scheme the ministry page says it stopped taking forward, apart from liabilities already committed.

## Group capex under PMFME

FPOs, self-help groups, cooperatives, government agencies, and private enterprises are named on the common-infrastructure page. The spend is a detailed project report for farm-gate assaying, sorting, grading, warehousing, and cold storage, or for a common processing facility for the district ODOP product. The page says a substantial part of that capacity should be open for others to hire. The path is the State Nodal Agency, the State Level Approval Committee, MoFPI where the page requires it, and then a bank. That sequence is the capex gate for a group facility. It is slower than buying a machine for an already licensed unit, and it is a different asset.

## Export-plant capex

An APEDA-recognized horticulture pack house is capex for sorting, grading, treatment, packing, pre-cooling, cold holding, and the quarantine and traceability steps the recognition procedure describes. It is not the same invoice as a domestic processing line. Meat plants sit under APEDA's inspection function for meat and meat products. A pack-house certificate does not substitute for that.

PLISFPI asked selected manufacturers to put mandated investment into plant and machinery in 2021-22 and 2022-23, counting 2020-21 spend. That clock, as printed, is not a 2026 greenfield round. The incentive years on the page still run through 2026-27 for beneficiaries already inside the scheme.
