# Watching the food processing capital cycle

Capital spending in this sector is not one cycle. PMKSY reimburses eligible civil works and machinery after a project is approved. PLI asks the firm to invest first and then claims incentive on sales. PMFME puts a capital subsidy on a bank loan for a micro unit. Watching the cycle means watching which of those clocks is turning, and whether the revised estimate still matches the budget estimate.

## What 2025-26 actually spent, by component

The ministry's statement of allocation and expenditure for 2025-26, in crore of rupees, is the cleanest view of the PMKSY capital mix. Budget estimate, revised estimate, and actual expenditure:

| Component | BE | RE | AE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creation and expansion of processing capacity | 347.28 | 295.58 | 252.80 |
| Cold chain | 171.63 | 129.29 | 125.71 |
| Operation Greens | 177.36 | 73.02 | 66.18 |
| Mega food parks | 80.40 | 50.05 | 41.81 |
| Agro-processing clusters | 55.90 | 51.70 | 41.55 |
| Food safety and quality assurance | 55.91 | 46.26 | 40.70 |
| Backward and forward linkages | 11.10 | 2.42 | 0.00 |
| Human resources and institutions | 3.60 | 1.48 | 1.07 |
| PMKSY total | 903.38 | 650.00 | 570.02 |

Processing-capacity grants were the largest PMKSY line and the one that absorbed the most actual spending. Operation Greens was cut hard at the revised-estimate stage, from Rs 177.36 crore to Rs 73.02 crore, and spent Rs 66.18 crore. Backward and forward linkages, already limited to old commitments, spent nothing. Mega food parks still drew Rs 41.81 crore, which fits a scheme closed to new projects and open for liabilities.

Beside PMKSY, the same statement shows the PLI at a budget, revised, and actual figure of about Rs 1,200 crore (actual Rs 1,199.96 crore). PMFME moved from a budget estimate of Rs 2,000 crore to a revised estimate of Rs 1,500 crore and actual expenditure of Rs 1,296.53 crore. The ministry total was Rs 4,364.22 crore at the budget-estimate stage, Rs 3,571.57 crore revised, and Rs 3,275.32 crore actual.

The lesson from that year is specific. PLI held its revised estimate. PMKSY and PMFME did not. A capital plan that assumes every opening estimate will be released is not how 2025-26 went.

## 2026-27 resets the opening bids

Demand No. 45 sets 2026-27 budget estimates at Rs 915 crore for PMKSY, Rs 1,200 crore again for PLI, and Rs 1,700 crore for PMFME. PMKSY's opening bid is above the 2025-26 revised estimate of Rs 650 crore and close to the previous opening bid of Rs 903.38 crore. PMFME's opening bid is above the previous revised estimate and below the previous opening bid of Rs 2,000 crore. PLI is flat.

These are the numbers to watch through the year: whether Operation Greens is cut again inside PMKSY, whether processing-capacity grants remain the main release, and whether PMFME's actuals land near Rs 1,700 crore or are revised down as they were in 2025-26.

## Private capital is already the majority of the cold-chain book

Project approvals are a stock, not a one-year flow. As on 31 December 2024, 394 cold-chain projects had a combined cost of Rs 11,466.38 crore, private investment of Rs 8,495.53 crore, and grant of Rs 2,970.85 crore. The grant cap of Rs 10 crore a project is why private money dominates the book. The cycle for a single project is then the 24- or 30-month build, the instalments, and the delay rule of up to 5 percent of an instalment per month.

PLI's reported investment of Rs 9,702 crore, against incentives disbursed of Rs 2,162.553 crore, is the parallel stock for larger manufacturers. That investment is reported by beneficiaries. It is not an audited national accounts line, and the review's wording should stay attached to the figure.

## What "capex cycle" should mean in a note

Track three series, each with its document. One, the component actuals inside PMKSY, because capacity creation, cold chain, and Operation Greens do not move together. Two, the gap between PMFME's budget estimate and its actuals. Three, the private share of sanctioned cold-chain cost, which tells you the ministry is a minority financier of the assets it approves. Headline outlays of Rs 6,520 crore or Rs 10,900 crore are the multi-year envelopes. They are the wrong number for this year's cash.
