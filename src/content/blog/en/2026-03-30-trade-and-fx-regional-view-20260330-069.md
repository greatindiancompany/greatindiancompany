# Where India's goods and software exports actually go

India's external map is three maps that share a coastline and little else. Customs merchandise has a destination table that moves every month. Trade-agreement partners are a policy list with their own annual export levels. Software exports, measured by the RBI, concentrate on the United States and Europe. A state ranking is a fourth map, maintained by DGCIS through the commerce ministry's trade portal, and it is not the table in the monthly PIB narrative.

## Merchandise in August: the United States leads, West Asia is uneven

DGCIS quick estimates for August 2026, provisional, rank selected export destinations in million US dollars. The largest April–August totals in that table:

| Destination | August 2026 | April–August 2026-27 | Change, April–August |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | 8,319.99 | 42,790.23 | +6.17% |
| UAE | 2,377.55 | 13,588.28 | −10.61% |
| China | 1,839.57 | 9,614.06 | +38.71% |
| Singapore | 1,877.75 | 9,497.67 | +96.56% |
| Netherlands | 877.95 | 6,945.08 | −21.26% |
| United Kingdom | 1,260.32 | 6,154.35 | +7.56% |

All-country merchandise exports for April–August were US$215,912.45 million. The United States line of US$42,790.23 million is the largest single destination in the selected-country table. The August United States print was up 21.83 per cent from US$6,829.03 million in August 2025, a faster month than the five-month pace.

The UAE moved the other way: August exports were US$2,377.55 million, down 26.10 per cent from US$3,217.34 million a year earlier. The Netherlands was also down in the month, from US$1,697.59 million to US$877.95 million. Singapore and China were up sharply on both the month and the five-month window. These are provisional quick estimates. A later DGCIS revision can change a cell. The ranks above are the ranks in this table, not a projection.

On the import side of the same DGCIS release, China supplied US$12,776.68 million of merchandise imports in August and US$65,486.70 million in April–August, up 27.01 per cent. Russia supplied US$41,442.21 million in April–August, up 56.69 per cent. The United States supplied US$28,090.72 million of imports in April–August, up 29.60 per cent. India sells to the United States and also buys from it. The export rank and the import rank are different lists.

## FTA partners are an annual merchandise list

The PIB note of 18 August 2026, sourced to the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, gives merchandise exports to FTA partners for 2025-26, in million US dollars. ASEAN as a group was 38,416.33. SAFTA was 25,774.68. Among countries, the UAE was 37,359.11, the United Kingdom 13,444.19, Singapore 11,863.66, Nepal 7,447.11, Australia 7,284.17, Malaysia 6,825.55, Japan 6,039.50, the Republic of Korea 6,012.03, Sri Lanka 5,516.83, Thailand 5,058.60, and Oman 4,021.48. New Zealand, at 566.51, is marked signed and not yet effective.

That UAE annual level and the April–August 2026 decline can both be true. One is a completed year in which the UAE was the largest individual FTA merchandise market. The other is the opening five months of the next year. The PIB note also says the India-UAE CEPA has been in force since 1 May 2022, and that bilateral trade crossed US$100.06 billion in 2024-25.

Australia is the other worked example in that note: merchandise exports of about US$7.28 billion in 2025-26, against a stated US$4 billion in 2020-21, with the agreement in force since 29 December 2022.

## Software has its own geography

The RBI software-services survey for 2025-26, released 18 September 2026, puts the United States at **54.1 per cent** of software exports, or US$119.7 billion. Europe is 31.8 per cent, or US$70.3 billion, of which the United Kingdom is 15.4 per cent, or US$34.0 billion. Asia is 6.3 per cent. Australia and New Zealand are 2.5 per cent.

The dollar invoiced 72.6 per cent of those exports. Off-site delivery was 91.7 per cent. Total software exports excluding overseas commercial presence were US$221.4 billion. The United States share of software is far above the United States share of April–August merchandise exports. A services firm and a goods firm do not have the same country risk, even when both write "United States" on the invoice.

Foreign affiliates add another map. Local software business by foreign affiliates of Indian companies was US$17.9 billion in 2025-26. The United States accounted for 42.2 per cent of software business by those affiliates.

## State rankings live on a different official screen

Monthly PIB trade notes rank countries, not states. DGCIS does compile state of origin. The commerce ministry's Trade Intelligence and Analytics portal is the public place that says it will show exports by state and district from DGCIS data. This brief does not quote a state share, because the monthly documents used above do not publish one. A founder comparing Gujarat with Tamil Nadu should open that portal, or the DGCIS quarterly report, and read the year selected on the screen. Mixing a state share from one financial year with the August country table would invent a regional story the releases do not tell.
