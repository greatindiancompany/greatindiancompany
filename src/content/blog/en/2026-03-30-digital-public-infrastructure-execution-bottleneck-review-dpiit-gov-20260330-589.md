# Where India's DPI Execution Is Counted and Where It Is Not

## A bottleneck in the record is a missing measure

The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 is uneven. Some digital public systems have users, transactions, and dates. Others are described without a volume. That gap is the execution fact available here. It is not a field audit. This page's 23 September 2026 update does not fill the gap with new counts.

## Counted at population scale

These systems have a number and an as-of date in the note.

- Aadhaar: more than 144 crore numbers as of March 2026, and over 2,707 crore authentications in 2024-25.
- UPI: 21.70 billion transactions in January 2026, 691 banks live, and 81 percent of domestic retail payment volume. The footnote is NPCI.
- DigiLocker: 67.63 crore users on 5 March 2026, and over 950 crore documents by March 2026.
- UMANG: 10.25 crore registrations and 723.36 crore transactions on 5 March 2026, across more than 2,400 services.
- eSanjeevani: 45.42 crore patients and 2.3 lakh healthcare providers as of 5 March 2026.
- DIKSHA: 566 crore learning sessions and 2.11 crore registered users as of 5 March 2026.
- API Setu: 8,036 APIs, 6,592 consumers, 2,559 publishers, and 10,530 organisations as of March 2026.
- ONDC: 1.16 lakh-plus retail sellers as of December 2025. Government eMarketplace: nearly 3.27 crore orders as of November 2025.

India Stack Global lists many of these on one page. The list confirms they are treated as a set. It does not add the missing volumes.

## Described without a comparable volume

e-Office is described as paperless file management on an open architecture for centre, state, and district use. The note gives no department count and no file count. The Skill India Digital Hub, launched in 2023, is described through the portals it joins — Udyam, e-Shram, the National Career Service, and ASEEM — without enrolments or placements. CoWIN's dose count, over 220 crore, belongs to the vaccination drive. The note does not give a current monthly use figure for that platform.

## Integration is asserted more often than it is measured

The note's main claim is that these systems work as one stack. The counts above are mostly separate. API Setu's publisher and consumer totals are the closest published measure of how many organisations exchange data through the government API catalogue. A state-by-state adoption table is absent, so uneven state execution cannot be located from this note.

## The practical bottleneck

Readers can see payments, identity, documents, telemedicine, and school learning in public numbers. They cannot see, from this note, how far e-Office or the skills hub has gone, or which state is behind on a shared rail. That is a disclosure bottleneck. It is visible before any operational bottleneck can be named.
