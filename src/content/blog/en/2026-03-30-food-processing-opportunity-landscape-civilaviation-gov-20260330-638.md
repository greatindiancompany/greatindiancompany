# Food Processing In India: Cold Chain, FPO Links, Export Plants, Clusters

## Four places the work actually is

The opportunity in Indian food processing, as the official pages draw it, is not a single market size. It is four kinds of asset: a cold chain that does not break, a link through an FPO or a common line, an export plant recognized for a scheduled product, and a cluster or district pattern you can point to on a list. This brief does not price any of the four.

## Cold chain

PMKSY still names Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure. The point of that component is continuity from the farm gate to the buyer, for horticulture and for other produce the guideline allows. Operation Greens is the perishable slice: tomato, onion, and potato at the start, then a ministry statement that the scope covers 22 perishables, with examples including mango, banana, apple, pineapple, carrot, cauliflower, and beans. The opportunity is the missing link for one of those crops, built as a chain. A chamber with no pre-cooling and no reefer is a different, weaker project.

## FPO linkages

PMFME is explicit that FPOs, self-help groups, and producer cooperatives are who the scheme means to support along the value chain, together with individual micro enterprises. The common-infrastructure page offers them farm-gate sorting, grading, warehousing, and cold storage, and a processing facility for the district's One District One Product. Others must be able to hire a substantial part of that capacity. The opportunity is a district where the ODOP product is real, the group can prepare a project report, and the state nodal path is the one you are willing to walk. It is not a national FPO count, which this page does not state.

## Export plants

APEDA's opportunity is inside its schedule: fruits and vegetables and their products, meat, poultry, dairy, bakery and confectionery, honey and jaggery, cocoa and chocolate, beverages, cereals, specified nuts, pickles, papads and chutneys, guar gum, floriculture, de-oiled rice bran, green pepper in brine, cashew, and basmati rice in the Second Schedule. For fresh horticulture, the plant opportunity is a recognized pack house with the right product scope. For meat, it is the inspection function in slaughterhouses and processing plants. For organic goods, it is production and packing under the National Programme for Organic Production. PLISFPI adds a manufacturing incentive in four segments, including processed fruits and vegetables and marine products, plus branding abroad. Marine products sit in that incentive. They do not sit on the APEDA schedule.

## State clusters

Agro-processing clusters remain a PMKSY component. Mega Food Parks do not, aside from committed liability in the 15th Finance Commission cycle. The cluster opportunity is a catchment plus shared infrastructure the ministry is still willing to consider, matched to a district product where PMFME is the better fit for a small group. Read the PMKSY date. The page says continuation through 31 March 2026, which has passed. An opportunity that depends on a new sanction under that umbrella waits on a later ministry decision. This landscape does not supply one.
