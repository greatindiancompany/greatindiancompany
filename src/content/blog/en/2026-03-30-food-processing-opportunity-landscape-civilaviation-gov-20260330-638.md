# Where the open doors are in food processing

An opportunity in this sector is a scheme component that still accepts a kind of project, plus a raw-material base the ministry has already measured. It is not a list of unnamed "high-growth categories." The doors below are the ones the latest ministry documents still describe as live. Whether a given month's application window is open has to be checked on the portal, because several of these schemes are drawn down by expression of interest rather than by a permanent counter.

## Infrastructure that can still be proposed

Under the restructured PMKSY, new projects continue in six places: integrated cold chain, creation or expansion of processing and preservation capacity, agro-processing clusters, food testing laboratories, research and development, and Operation Greens. Mega food parks and backward-and-forward linkage projects are not a door for a new promoter. They are a door only if the commitment already exists.

Cold chain is the most specified of the six. The annual report allows individuals, groups, cooperatives, self-help groups, farmer producer organisations, NGOs, and public enterprises to apply. The project needs farm-level infrastructure and at least one further link, unless it is irradiation, which may stand alone. The grant ceiling is Rs 10 crore. The Cabinet decision of 31 July 2025, reported in the January 2026 year-end review, points a Rs 1,000 crore slice at 50 multi-product irradiation units and 100 NABL-accredited testing labs. Fourteen irradiation proposals were approved in 2025-26. That is a narrow door with a published purpose, not a general invitation to build any warehouse.

Operation Greens is the surplus-management door inside the same umbrella. In 2025-26 the ministry's expenditure statement cut it from a budget estimate of Rs 177.36 crore to a revised estimate of Rs 73.02 crore. The component remains on the scheme list. Its cash is the volatile part.

Processing-capacity grants were the largest PMKSY release in that same year, at Rs 252.80 crore of actual expenditure against a budget estimate of Rs 347.28 crore. For a unit that wants its own line rather than a shared park, that component has been the busiest rupee flow.

## The micro-enterprise door

PMFME is open, on the September 2025 FAQ, to individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, startups, farmer producer organisations, NGOs, cooperatives, private companies, and self-help groups, for both new units and expansions. The economic boundary is the micro definition in that FAQ: plant and machinery or equipment not above Rs 2.5 crore, and turnover not above Rs 10 crore.

Three cheques exist, and they should not be collapsed into one:

- Credit-linked capital grant, 35 percent, up to Rs 10 lakh for a unit, on plant and machinery and technical civil work.
- The same rate for common infrastructure, up to Rs 3 crore, for groups and government agencies.
- Seed capital up to Rs 40,000 for a self-help group member, capped at Rs 4 lakh for the group.

Branding and marketing is a fourth door, and it prefers the district product. The ministry has approved a product for 726 districts. The FAQ says a non-ODOP unit can still take the capital subsidy and training. By January 2026, 27 marketing and branding proposals had been approved, including two national proposals for NAFED.

The coverage target is 2,00,000 units and 1,72,707 loans had been sanctioned. The remaining distance to the target is real. It is also a sanction target, so a new applicant is competing for bank appetite and for whatever appropriation Demand No. 45's Rs 1,700 crore in 2026-27 will support.

## The scale-up and brand door

PLISFPI, through 2026-27, is the door for firms that can meet a minimum sales threshold and a minimum investment. The segments named on the scheme page are ready-to-cook and ready-to-eat foods including millets, processed fruits and vegetables, marine products, and mozzarella. Smaller firms have a specific track for innovative or organic products in those segments, including free-range eggs, poultry, and egg products. A separate track pays for in-store branding, shelf space, and marketing abroad. A millet-based incentive was carved from savings inside the same Rs 10,900 crore outlay.

One hundred and seventy proposals are already approved. This is not an empty scheme waiting for a first applicant. The opportunity is the remaining incentive life, including quarterly claims, and any category the guidelines still list as open. The scheme portal is the check. A micro enterprise under the PMFME turnover cap will not meet a Category I sales floor written for large manufacturers.

## A raw-material backdrop, not a product tip

The ministry's 2021 production table, from Agriculture Statistics at a Glance, 2023, shows why so many of these doors are about milk, fruit, vegetables, onion, and pulses: India was first or second in the world in those lines. Marine products are a PLI segment even though meat, in that same table, was a smaller world share. Export doors that are already measured sit in APEDA's processed tables: miscellaneous preparations, cereal preparations, and processed vegetables were the largest processed value-added lines in 2024-25.

The practical map is short. Shared and unit infrastructure through the six live PMKSY components, with irradiation and labs called out by name. Micro capital, group infrastructure, seed capital, and ODOP branding through PMFME. Scale manufacturing and overseas brand support through PLI until 2026-27. Anything outside those doors needs a different budget head, and the documents do not provide one.
