# Official Figures on India's Captive Technology Hubs

Use this page as a shelf, not as a single score. A global capability centre is a foreign company's captive technology, operations, or research unit in India. None of the statistical series below is a census of those units. The ministerial count is identified separately so it is not mistaken for one.

Respondents to the 2025-26 RBI round were **2,363** of **7,569** companies contacted, about **89 percent** of estimated exports. DPIIT's services-sector equity, which includes outsourcing and R&D, was **USD 10,010 million** in 2025-26, separate from the computer-software line.

## A ministerial statement, labeled as one

PIB Delhi, 25 February 2025, reported remarks by the Secretary, Ministry of Labour and Employment: over **1,700** GCCs, **1.9 million** professionals, and **$64.6 billion** in revenue as of 2024, and a projection of about **$105 billion**, around **2,400** centres, and over **2.8 million** people by 2030. The note names no survey frame. It is the government text that states a centre count. It is not an RBI, DPIIT, or MoSPI series.

## An industry figure inside a government report

STPI's annual report reproduces, and marks as **Source: NASSCOM**, IT-BPM exports of **$224.4 billion** in 2024-25 and a direct employment base of **5.8 million**. NASSCOM is an industry body. Those figures are not the RBI export total and not the ministerial GCC headcount. For 2024-25 the RBI excluding-commercial-presence total is **US$ 204.7 billion**, and the wider total is **US$ 218.6 billion**. The gap is coverage and publisher. It is not a range to average.

## Empty cells worth keeping empty

- RBI, DPIIT, and MoSPI do not publish a count of global capability centres.
- MoSPI does not publish IT value added apart from the combined group above.
- DPIIT does not publish state-wise computer-software equity in the summary sheets used here. Its state table is all-sector.
- No document on this shelf publishes India's share of the world's captive centres.
