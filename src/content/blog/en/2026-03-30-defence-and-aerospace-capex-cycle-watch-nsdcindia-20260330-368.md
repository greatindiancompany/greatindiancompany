# Defence capital outlay in 2025-26 was raised, then fully used

## Start with the whole Ministry, then cut to capital

On 1 February 2025 the Ministry of Defence reported a provision of Rs 6,81,210.27 crore for FY 2025-26. That was 9.53 percent above the budget estimate of FY 2024-25, and the release describes it as 13.45 percent of the Union Budget.

The same release splits the provision four ways. Capital outlay on defence services is Rs 1,80,000 crore, or 26.43 percent. Revenue expenditure for the armed forces is Rs 3,11,732.30 crore, or 45.76 percent. Defence pensions are Rs 1,60,795 crore, or 23.60 percent. Civil organisations under the Ministry are Rs 28,682.97 crore, or 4.21 percent.

A capex watch that quotes only Rs 6.81 lakh crore is watching pay and pensions as well as equipment. The capital line is Rs 1,80,000 crore at the budget-estimate stage.

## Inside the capital line

Of that Rs 1,80,000 crore, the release plans Rs 1,48,722.80 crore for capital acquisition, which it calls the modernisation budget, and Rs 31,277.20 crore for research and development plus infrastructure.

For FY 2025-26, Rs 1,11,544.83 crore, or 75 percent of the modernisation budget, is earmarked for procurement from domestic sources. Of that domestic share, 25 percent, Rs 27,886.21 crore, is provisioned for domestic private industry.

Two neighbouring lines matter to industry and are not the services' capital outlay. The Defence Research and Development Organisation allocation is Rs 26,816.82 crore, against Rs 23,855.61 crore the year before. The iDEX line, including the ADITI sub-scheme, is Rs 449.62 crore for the year. The release also marks 2025-26 as a Year of Reforms aimed at simplifying the defence procurement procedure so the allocation is used.

The Union Budget demand for capital outlay on defence services shows the same Rs 1,80,000 crore budget estimate, against a revised estimate of Rs 1,59,500 crore for 2024-25 and actuals of Rs 1,54,256.28 crore for 2023-24. Those earlier years are the baseline for "was the money spent", not a second copy of the 2025-26 speech.

## The outturn, one year later

On 1 April 2026 the Ministry reported that the capital outlay for defence services had been fully used. At the revised-estimate stage the figure was Rs 1.86 lakh crore. The original appropriation was Rs 1.80 lakh crore. The Ministry of Finance augmented it during the year. The release says the higher requirement followed the pace of spending in the first two quarters and the increased requirement of the forces for modernisation and other purposes after Operation Sindoor. This note does not describe that operation. It records that the Ministry published the augmentation and the full use.

The same release says overall use of the defence budget, including civil and pension heads, was 99.62 percent of the final allocation. It also says full capital use in FY 2024-25 had already happened after many years. The cycle to watch is therefore budget estimate, revised estimate, then use. FY 2025-26 moved from Rs 1.80 lakh crore to Rs 1.86 lakh crore and was then reported as fully spent.

## What this does not forecast

Full use of a capital appropriation is not a forecast of the FY 2026-27 allocation, and it is not the value of domestic defence production. Production for FY 2024-25 was reported separately, at Rs 1,50,590 crore. Great Indian Company keeps the appropriation and the industry's output on different pages so a capex model does not treat a budget line as a sales line.
