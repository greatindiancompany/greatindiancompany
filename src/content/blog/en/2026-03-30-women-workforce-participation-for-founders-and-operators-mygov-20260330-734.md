# Headcount thresholds founders apply to women on the rolls

A founder hiring in India meets women’s work as a local labour pool and as a set of duties that switch on at a headcount. The national participation rate is context. The duties are concrete. The pool depends on whether the jobs are urban and salaried or rural and self-employed.

## Which labour pool the vacancy is actually in

If the workplace is urban and the role is a regular wage, the relevant PLFS cells for 2025 are these. Urban women aged 15 and above had a usual-status participation rate of 27.7 percent and a worker population ratio of 25.9 percent. Among urban women workers, 50.9 percent were already in regular wage or salaried employment. Urban female unemployment was 6.4 percent on usual status. For urban women aged 15–29 it was 18.9 percent. In the April–June 2026 week-status quarter, urban female unemployment was 8.7 percent and urban female participation was 25.0 percent.

If the workplace is rural, or the work is a household enterprise, the cells flip. Rural female participation was 45.9 percent on usual status, and 70.7 percent of rural women workers were self-employed. Only 9.3 percent were in regular wage jobs. A hiring plan that copies a city salary benchmark into that rural structure is benchmarking against the smaller status.

All-India, 18.2 percent of women workers were in regular wage employment and 64.2 percent were self-employed. The 40.0 percent participation rate includes both. EPFO’s 26.9 lakh net female subscribers in 2024–25 are the covered-establishment flow inside the wage status. They are the right comparison for a firm that will open a provident-fund account. They are the wrong comparison for a network of own-account workers.

## Three thresholds, in the order headcount hits them

At 10 employees, the sexual harassment law requires an Internal Committee. MoWCD, the nodal ministry, states that workplaces with 10 or more employees constitute that committee, and that a Local Committee at the district hears cases from smaller workplaces and complaints against the employer. SHe-Box, live since 29 August 2024, is where the complaint is routed and where committee details can be uploaded. Specified companies also state, in the Board’s report, that the committee has been constituted. A founder below 10 employees still has a path for a complainant: the Local Committee. A founder crossing 10 has the Internal Committee, a written policy, and the orientation MoWCD’s advisories ask for.

The maternity statute MoLE says it is implementing is the 1961 Act as amended in 2017. Paid leave is 26 weeks where the woman has fewer than two surviving children, with not more than eight weeks before the expected delivery, and 12 weeks where she has two or more surviving children. Adoptive mothers of a child below three months, and commissioning mothers, have 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over. Work from home after that leave is by mutual agreement where the work allows it. The woman is to be told, in writing and electronically at appointment, what the Act provides. Build the 26 weeks into the staffing plan for a covered employee. Do not describe it as a benefit that applies to a self-employed contractor the establishment does not employ.

At 50 employees, the same Act requires a creche, and four visits a day by the woman, including the rest interval. The Code on Social Security, 2020, as MoLE has described it, contains the 26-week provision, the creche at 50, and night shifts with adequate safety measures. Confirm what is notified for the establishment. The operating rule MoLE has pointed to in its replies remains the amended 1961 Act.

## Pay and hours, so the offer is not the wrong average

Women’s regular-wage earnings in the 2025 PLFS reading were ₹18,353. Self-employment earnings were ₹6,374. Casual earnings other than public works were ₹315. An offer should sit on the line that matches the status. Men’s regular-wage earnings in the same note were ₹24,217, so the official regular-wage gap is visible without a separate study. Hours differ inside the status too: men in regular wage work worked about 7.9 hours more per week than women.

A woman candidate is also living in the time-use results. Women participants in the 2024 Time Use Survey spent 289 minutes a day on unpaid domestic services, against 88 for men. Palna creches and Sakhi Niwas hostels exist because MoWCD treats that time as a barrier to paid work. An establishment at 50 employees has its own creche duty. An establishment below that can still know that the public creche approval count was 11,395 as of March 2025.

## A short operating list

Confirm whether the role is regular wage in an urban market or self-employment in a rural one, using the shares above. At 10 employees, constitute the Internal Committee and record it on SHe-Box. Apply the 26-week or 12-week maternity rule to covered employees, and say which rule applies to the individual. At 50 employees, provide the creche. Enrol covered employees with EPFO and read the female payroll print as a flow, not as the size of the Indian female workforce. The size, on usual status in 2025, is 20.0 crore employed women.
