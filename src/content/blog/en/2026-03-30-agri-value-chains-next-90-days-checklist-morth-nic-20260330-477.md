The next 90 days on an Indian agri value chain are a review of season, mandi or dairy dock, papers, and cash. This checklist is the review. It is not a set of findings, and it does not claim the coming quarter will differ from the last one.

Use one commodity and one buyer. Answer from your lots or write "not observed."

## Season and buyer

- Are these 90 days kharif arrival, rabi harvest, potato loading or release, or dairy flush or lean? The calendar decides when cash leaves.
- If the crop is paddy or wheat, is a public purchase centre open for your district, and are your lots being accepted?
- If the crop is sold in a mandi, what were arrivals and the modal price at that yard, and what did the commission agent settle to the farmer?
- If the crop is milk, what happened to fat, solids-not-fat, and litres versus the same weeks last year?
- If you export, did any lot fail a residue or health check, and did a foreign-trade notification change the commodity's status?

## Papers

- Which Agricultural Produce Market Committee licence or fee falls due?
- Does the food business licence under the Food Safety and Standards Act cover the step you will run in these 90 days?
- If you store against a receipt, is the warehouse still registered with the Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority?
- If you export, is the APEDA or MPEDA registration current for this product?
- Is any stock-limit notification under the Essential Commodities Act live for this commodity?

## Cash

- Days from delivery to cash in the bank, for the agent, the union, the procurement agency, or the importer. Use the bank date.
- Payables to farmers or members of a farmer producer organisation that are outside the agreed window.
- Power backup hours actually available for the cold store or the bulk milk cooler.

Sort answers into unchanged, worse for margin or time, and better for margin or time. Act on one worse item.
