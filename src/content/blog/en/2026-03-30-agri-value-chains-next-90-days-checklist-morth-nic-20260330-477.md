# What to check before the kharif procurement peak

The next 90 days from 24 September 2026 run into the kharif marketing season. The prices for that season are already law. The work is to match arrivals to the right instrument, not to wait for a new MSP notification.

Common paddy for Kharif Marketing Season 2026-27 is ₹2,441 a quintal. Grade A is ₹2,461. Both were approved on 13 May 2026 and both are ₹72 above 2025-26. Sunflower seed at ₹8,343, medium-staple cotton at ₹8,267, nigerseed at ₹10,052, and sesamum at ₹10,346 are the large-increase crops in the same decision. If the crop in the yard is one of those, the May table is the document. If the crop is wheat, it is the wrong document. Wheat for Rabi Marketing Season 2026-27 is ₹2,585, set on 1 October 2025, for the harvest that follows rabi sowing. Using ₹2,585 on a paddy heap, or ₹2,441 on a wheat contract for 2027, is a season error.

States open purchase centres. The Cabinet does not publish one national opening day. Before committing a mill or a transport contract, confirm the state’s centre notification, the moisture and quality cut, and whether the lot is being bought by the Food Corporation or a state agency. Coarse grains need a prior central approval of the state’s plan before they enter the central pool. Pulses and oilseeds are bought only when the state has asked under PM-AASHA and the market is below MSP. NAFED’s e-Samriddhi and NCCF’s e-Samyukti are the registration path for that scheme: Aadhaar, land record, bank account, crop, then a slotted visit and a bank credit. Cotton farmers use Kapas Kisan. Arriving at a paddy centre with a tur lot, or the reverse, wastes the slot.

## Four checks that are already specified

e-NAM payment is electronic, into the farmer’s bank account, after online bidding. The platform does not replace the mandi’s own rules. Confirm that the mandi is one of the 1,656 integrated as on 30 June 2026, and that assaying equipment is actually in use. The Centre has said 134 Rajasthan mandis were using AI-based assaying machines. A mandi outside that set may still be on e-NAM and may still be slow at quality testing. The integration grant is up to ₹75 lakh. Whether this mandi has drawn it is a state fact, not a national one.

An FPO that wants scheme money in this window should match the rule, not the slogan. Management support is up to ₹18 lakh over three years. The matching equity grant is up to ₹2,000 per member, capped at ₹15 lakh per FPO. The credit guarantee covers a project loan of up to ₹2 crore from an eligible lender. As on 31 December 2025 the equity grant had reached 6,557 of the 10,000 registered FPOs, and the guarantee had reached 2,671. If this FPO is outside those counts, the next 90 days are a file problem, not a harvest problem.

A cold-chain promoter waiting on a grant should use the June 2026 stock as context, not as a promise of release this quarter. Sanctioned grants were ₹3,089.08 crore and releases were ₹2,540.89 crore, with ₹125.71 crore released in the whole of 2025-26. A pending instalment follows the scheme’s own milestone. It does not follow the paddy calendar.

An exporter shipping new-crop rice, spices, or marine product in this window should read the Department of Commerce list from 10 June 2026 before the container moves: pesticide residues and aflatoxin in rice, residues in spices, antibiotic residues in marine products. MSP procurement paper is not an export health certificate. The Export Inspection Council’s risk-based inspection is the export gate.

Great Indian Company dates this list to 24 September 2026 so it expires when the season does.
