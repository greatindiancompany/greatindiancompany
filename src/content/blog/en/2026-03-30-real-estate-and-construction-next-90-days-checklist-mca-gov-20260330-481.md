# A Ninety-Day Checklist for RERA Projects and PMAY Files

The next ninety days from 24 September 2026 do not depend on a fresh circular. They depend on clocks already in the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act and in PMAY-U 2.0. A checklist that invents a filing date the statute does not contain is worse than no checklist. This one stays inside the July 2025 MoHUA FAQ and the mission FAQ.

## If you sell apartments or plots

The [RERA FAQ](https://www.mohua.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/542144159b61c980253a694f478d3a5d.pdf) is guidance, not a ruling on your project. Confirm the state rules before you treat a line below as your form.

- Do not advertise, book, or take expressions of interest until the project is registered in that state. Web portals that sell registered projects register as agents in each state they cover.
- If the financial year ended on 31 March 2026, the audit of the separate project account falls due within six months, which runs to the end of September 2026. The chartered accountant in practice has to certify that money collected for the project was used for the project and that withdrawals matched the percentage of completion. A firm with a different year-end should count six months from that date, not from 31 March.
- Withdrawals from the separate account still need the engineer, the architect, and the practising chartered accountant, in proportion to completion. Seventy percent of allottee receipts belong in that account. It is not an escrow that requires the authority to approve each debit.
- Section 11 requires the promoter to keep the authority's website current, including a quarterly update of specified project information. Treat the next quarterly upload as a standing duty. The FAQ does not add a special quarter-end form for September.
- Do not collect more than 10 percent before the agreement for sale is executed.
- If registration is close to expiry, extension is available for the Act's force majeure list, or, without fault, for not more than one year. A sales slowdown is not force majeure. The explanation lists war, flood, drought, fire, cyclone, earthquake, or another natural calamity.
- Structural complaints do not wait for a new policy. Liability runs five years from handover, and the FAQ says rectification is due within 30 days.
- If a plan change is major, stop and count consent. Two-thirds of allottees, promoter-held apartments out, one vote each, plus the competent authority.

Registration validity is the completion period the promoter declared at application. The ninety-day task is to compare that declared date with the site, not to assume the authority will extend it.

## If you are inside a PMAY file

The [mission FAQ](https://pmaymis.gov.in/PMAYMIS2_2024/faq.html) sets the household clocks.

- One vertical only. A beneficiary-led grant and the interest subsidy do not stack. A loan is allowed on a beneficiary-led house; the subsidy is not.
- Beneficiary-led central assistance moves by direct benefit transfer in three instalments, 40, 40, and 20, against physical progress. States may release their own first instalment before the central release so work can start. Do not book the Centre's share as received because the state has paid.
- Geo-tagging is five stages. If the beneficiary uploads the tag, the urban local body has 15 days to approve it, after which the system auto-approves. A file sitting on that clock is an execution issue, not a policy rumour.
- The interest subsidy, up to ₹1.80 lakh, is released in five yearly instalments. At each release the loan must still be active and more than half the principal must still be outstanding. A prepayment that drops the outstanding principal through that line changes the subsidy, on the terms the scheme page states.
- The assisted house is not to be sold or rented for five years from completion. A ninety-day sales plan that assumes otherwise conflicts with the FAQ.
- Applicants who took a government housing benefit in the last twenty years, or whose earlier PMAY-U sanction was curtailed after 31 December 2023, are not a pipeline for 2.0.

## If you lend or allocate

Re-read the 31 August 2026 [sectoral credit release](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63478) before the next committee. Housing was still in double-digit growth inside personal loans. Commercial real estate was named as a support for services growth. Neither sentence is a project sanction. The next release will land on the [data page](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/Data_Sectoral_Deployment.aspx). Until it does, do not update a model with a figure the July note did not print.

The operating version of this list for a promoter's desk is in the [founders' brief](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/real-estate-and-construction-for-founders-and-operators-mca-gov-20260330-721).
