# India's Energy Transition for Founders and Operators

Operators do not sell "the energy transition." They sell a system to a household, power to a company, a module to a developer, or hydrogen to a factory. Each of those has a government door, a compliance stack, and a way to get paid. The national targets explain why the doors exist. They do not replace the doors.

## If you install on roofs

PM Surya Ghar is the residential market with a published process. The household registers at pmsuryaghar.gov.in. You register as a vendor and list equipment and prices. The customer picks you. You install to the scheme's minimum technical specifications. The distribution company inspects, and the net-metering step has been pulled into the same portal. Central assistance is paid under a scheme whose household component is ₹65,700 crore, inside a ₹75,021 crore outlay that runs through 31 March 2027. MNRE publishes benchmark prices for modules, inverters, and other equipment so a quote can be compared with a reference.

The operating constraints are local and dated. As of 12 August 2026 the scheme had reached 51.58 lakh households and 14.8 GW, against one crore households. On 16 July 2026 Parliament's state annexure still showed a wide gap between applications and installations, for example a large application count in several states and a much smaller installation count. Your cycle time is feasibility, installation, inspection, meter, and subsidy release. A city programme was running in 41 cities in the Ministry's FY 2025-26 review, and banks had sanctioned 13.7 lakh loan applications and disbursed 11.3 lakh. If your customer needs a loan, that pipeline is part of the job.

KUSUM is the parallel door for pumps and agricultural solarisation, extended to 31 March 2027, with ₹5,000 crore in the 2026-27 Budget. It is a different customer and a different tender habit. Do not staff it with a rooftop sales script.

## If you sell power to a company

A consumer with contracted demand or sanctioned load of 100 kW or more can take green energy open access. A captive consumer has no minimum load. The nodal agency is supposed to decide in fifteen days. Grid-India runs the central window. State commissions still set the charges that decide whether the deal is cheaper than the distribution tariff: cross-subsidy surcharge, additional surcharge, and standby charges have been the historic deal-breakers, which is why the 2022 rules spent so much text on them.

From FY 2026-27 the renewable consumption obligation is 35.95 percent for open-access consumers and captive users. If your customer is a designated consumer, you are not only offering a cheaper kilowatt-hour. You are offering a compliance path. Wind and hydro count toward the specific components only if the project was commissioned after 31 March 2024. Distributed renewable energy is a separate component for distribution companies and covers projects up to 10 MW, which includes a lot of commercial rooftops. Know which bucket your plant fills before you write it into a contract.

FY 2025-26 is the demand proof. MNRE attributed about 15 GW of the year's solar addition to commercial, industrial, and captive projects. That lane is already a large business. It is also a credit business: payment comes from the buyer, not from a central subsidy.

## If you build a factory

The solar production-linked incentive pays selected manufacturers for five years after the plant is commissioned, on high-efficiency modules they make and sell. The outlay is ₹24,000 crore. Tranche II letters, issued by SECI in April 2023, covered 11 bidders and 39,600 MW of fully or partly integrated capacity. By April 2026, 8 of 12 PLI manufacturers had started production in some part of the chain, and national module nameplate was about 172 GW. The crowded product is the module. The dated regulatory frontier is ingot and wafer, with an Approved List pathway announced for June 2028, and cells, where capital goods for lithium-ion storage cells have a customs-duty exemption until 31 March 2028.

The quality-control order notified on 27 January 2025 applies Indian Standards to modules, storage batteries, and inverters. Domestic-content claims are checked through the National Institute of Solar Energy portal. Imports of listed equipment are monitored through the portal launched in October 2025. If your bill of materials depends on a duty exemption or on an exemption from the approved list, write the end date into the model. GST on renewable devices and parts is 5 percent from 22 September 2025.

The cell programme for advanced chemistry is a different ministry and a different auction. ₹18,100 crore, 50 GWh, 40 GWh already with four firms, 10 GWh reserved for grid-scale storage and put out to bid. If you are a module assembler, that scheme is not your PLI.

## If you are early: hydrogen, floating solar, biogas

Green hydrogen has a mission, a ₹17,490 crore SIGHT component inside a ₹19,744 crore outlay, and a thin production base of about 8,000 tonnes a year as of February 2026. An operator should not hire against the 5 million tonne aim. An operator can respond to a published pilot or an incentive tender, and can keep the offtake contract as the asset. Floating solar under the Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana is newer still: ₹5,070 crore, 5,000 MW, storage of at least two hours, sanctions from FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31. Compressed biogas has 217 commissioned plants out of 1,929 registered, and a new ₹23,731 crore scheme. Early means the tender is the opportunity. A registered intent is not revenue.

## A weekly operating rhythm

Read the MNRE monthly sheet for the technology you actually build. Read the obligation and the open-access charge that your customer pays. Read the list and the standard your equipment must meet. The 500 GW figure can stay in the strategy memo. It does not clear a meter, a list, or an invoice.
