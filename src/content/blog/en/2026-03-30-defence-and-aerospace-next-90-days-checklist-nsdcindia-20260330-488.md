# A 90-day defence watchlist that stays on public pages

## The window

This note is dated 24 September 2026. Ninety days runs to about 23 December 2026. The Union Budget for 2026-27 falls after that window. There is no public, unclassified calendar of acceptance trials in these pages. The checklist below is what a founder, a lender, or a ministry desk can open without asking for a restricted document.

## Open the sixth list at item level

On 18 August 2026 the Department of Defence Production notified 405 items, with an estimated business potential of Rs 3,070 crore. The release says each item has an indicative timeline, and that the list is on SRIJAN. In this 90-day window, the useful act is to filter the portal for timelines that fall before December 2026 or early 2027, then read whether development is recorded as successful. The press note does not repeat all 405 dates. Copying a date from memory will be wrong.

While there, keep the portal's other totals in their boxes. Up to June 2026 the release counts more than 33,000 items offered and more than 15,700 indigenised. A timeline on one line replaceable unit does not move those national totals by itself.

## Check the control list that is actually current

The DGFT SCOMET page shows an updated list as on 23 September 2025, and it keeps older files on the same page. Before a shipment, demo, repair-return, or exhibition, open the page and use the file it currently marks as the update. A year-old download in a shared drive is not the check.

If the item is on the munitions list, read the Department of Defence Production authorisation route as well. The Department's last published annual count is 1,762 authorisations in FY 2024-25. That count will not tell you whether your application this quarter is complete.

## File the capital outturn before the next speech

On 1 April 2026 the Ministry reported full use of a revised capital outlay of Rs 1.86 lakh crore for FY 2025-26, against an original Rs 1.80 lakh crore. Overall Ministry use, including pension and civil heads, was 99.62 percent. Before anyone models the next budget, write those two facts down. The next speech is outside this 90-day window. The outturn is already public.

## Ask whether the iDEX window you remember is still the scheme

The scheme page states support of Rs 498.78 crore from 2021-22 to 2025-26. That end year is over. The FY 2025-26 budget line was Rs 449.62 crore, including ADITI. In this quarter, confirm on the Department's page whether a new window, a closed window, or only ongoing projects are in force. Do not submit a grant plan against a five-year envelope that has expired.

## If the work is in space, use the space authorisation

Non-government space activity is authorised by IN-SPACe under the Indian Space Policy 2023. The policy ties authorisation to safety, national security, international obligations, and foreign policy. A defence industrial licence does not substitute for that authorisation. In this window, read the policy PDF and the authorisation norms on the IN-SPACe site, and note any guideline dated after the copy you already hold.

## What not to put on the list

Do not schedule a decision on a contract rumour, a weapons-performance claim, or a classified quantity. If it is not on pib.gov.in, mod.gov.in, srijandefence.gov.in, dgft.gov.in, isro.gov.in, or inspace.gov.in, it is outside this checklist. Great Indian Company would rather the 90 days be short than filled with items nobody can verify.
