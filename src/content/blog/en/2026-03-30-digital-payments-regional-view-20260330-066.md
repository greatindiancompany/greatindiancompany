Payment System Indicators - August 2026 is one national table. It has no state, district, rural or urban column. A sentence that ranks Maharashtra against Bihar, or metros against villages, is not a reading of this release. The figures below are the national August 2026 column, and RBI marks them provisional.

## Two AePS lines that do different work

AePS appears twice, and the sizes are not close.

AePS fund transfers, inside retail credit transfers: 0.23 lakh transactions, ₹13 crore.

AePS cash withdrawal at micro-ATMs, inside the channels section: 999.48 lakh transactions, ₹24,747 crore. The average withdrawal is about ₹2,476.

Anyone building a rural cash-out argument from this file is looking at the second line, the national micro-ATM total. The file still does not say which states those withdrawals happened in. Micro-ATM cash-out is also not the same object as UPI. UPI that month was 2,45,089.58 lakh payments.

## Credits that often reach a beneficiary account

APBS, in the retail credit-transfer block, was 2,884.71 lakh transactions and ₹50,205 crore in August 2026. NACH credit was 1,495.61 lakh and ₹2,18,769 crore. Both are national totals. A state government or a bank can be a large user of these pipes, and this table will not show that user's share.

## Acceptance stocks without a map

As on August 2026 the country had 8,130.31 lakh static UPI QR codes, 53.30 lakh Bharat QR codes, and 102.08 lakh PoS terminals, on the definitions in the infrastructure block. Static QR codes are static codes only. None of the three stocks is broken out by state, so a higher national QR stock is not evidence about a particular district.

The merchant brief treats those stocks as acceptance facts. It is here: [merchant acceptance](/blog/digital-payments-impact-on-smes-20260330-026).

## The companion file is organised by entity

RBI also releases Entity-wise UPI/IMPS/NETC/NFS/AePS/CTS/BBPS Statistics. On 24 September 2026 the newest file on that index was July 2026, a month behind the August system indicators. The rows are banks and other system participants. That is where an entity's reported volume lives. A state ranking is outside both files, and this brief does not open the entity workbook for a league table.

A reader who needs a geography has to use a release that prints geographies. Until then, the regional statement this source will support is the national one: cash-out at micro-ATMs was 999.48 lakh transactions in August 2026, APBS was 2,884.71 lakh credit transfers, and the QR and PoS stocks are national counts.

How those lines sit inside the wider taxonomy is in the [policy explainer](/blog/digital-payments-policy-explainer-20260330-056). The desk that holds both is the [Great Indian Company brief index](/blog).
