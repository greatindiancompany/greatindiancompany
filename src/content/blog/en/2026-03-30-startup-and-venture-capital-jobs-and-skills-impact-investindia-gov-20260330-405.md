Startup employment figures are large enough to enter a national jobs debate. They are also a different object from a labour-force survey. The official label, once you reach the annexure, is “self-reported”.

## The series, with the label attached

A Rajya Sabha reply published by PIB on 13 February 2026 says that as on 31 December 2025, 2,07,135 DPIIT-recognised startups had generated over 21.9 lakh direct jobs. Annexure I prints the count by the year of recognition and titles the jobs row “No. of direct jobs generated (self-reported)”:

| Year of recognition | Startups | Self-reported direct jobs |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2016 | 502 | 308 |
| 2017 | 5,473 | 52,055 |
| 2018 | 8,980 | 1,00,968 |
| 2019 | 11,885 | 1,63,694 |
| 2020 | 14,852 | 1,81,602 |
| 2021 | 20,282 | 2,11,316 |
| 2022 | 26,596 | 2,74,920 |
| 2023 | 34,842 | 3,92,181 |
| 2024 | 34,294 | 3,51,921 |
| 2025 | 49,429 | 4,67,549 |

The jobs column adds to 21,96,514, which is the “over 21.9 lakh” in the reply. The startup column adds to 2,07,135. Both checks use only the annexure. Jobs attributed to the 2024 recognition cohort (3,51,921) are lower than jobs attributed to the 2023 cohort (3,92,181), even though the two cohorts are similar in size. The 2025 cohort is larger on both counts. Nothing in the reply converts these rows into permanent payrolls, contract work, or a net addition to employment after closures.

PIB’s 17 April 2026 release moves the national total to more than 23.36 lakh direct jobs as on 31 March 2026, against a recognition stock that had crossed 2.23 lakh. For the financial year, direct jobs created were 4,99,400+ in 2025-26 and 3,66,870+ in 2024-25. PIB states that jobs rose 36.1 percent year on year while recognitions rose 51.6 percent. Recognition outran reported jobs in that comparison. The April release does not repeat the words “self-reported”; the February annexure does. Until DPIIT publishes a different method, the careful reading is that the later total continues the same self-reported series.

A Lok Sabha reply gives one state a June update: Karnataka’s 24,026 recognised startups had generated over 2.58 lakh direct jobs as on 30 June 2026. That is a state line, not a revision of the 23.36 lakh national figure.

## Scheme jobs are a subset

The 13 February reply also separates employment inside the three funding schemes, as on 31 December 2025. Startups backed by Fund of Funds portfolio investments had generated over 2 lakh jobs. Seed-fund startups had generated over 22,600. Startups with guaranteed loans had generated over 23,700. These are not additive to the 21.9 lakh in any way the reply demonstrates. A startup can sit in the registry and in a scheme. Quote them as programme claims.

## Skills programmes are a third object

The 15 January 2026 PIB note discusses the Atal Innovation Mission, run by NITI Aayog, with an outlay of ₹2,750 crore up to March 2028. It reports more than 10,000 Atal Tinkering Labs across 733 districts, engaging over 1.1 crore students and enabling 16 lakh+ innovation projects. Those are school and student figures. They do not add to DPIIT’s direct-job total. The same note describes AIM 2.0 pieces aimed at vernacular innovation centres, frontier districts, and a deep-tech reactor. They are capability programmes. They are not placement numbers.

Women’s participation is published as governance, not as a jobs split. As on 31 March 2026, more than 1.07 lakh recognised startups had at least one woman director or partner, about 48 percent. As on 30 June 2026 the Lok Sabha figure is more than 1.15 lakh. A director or partner is not an employee count. Karnataka’s reply adds a local cut: more than 11,400 of its 24,026 recognised startups had at least one woman director or partner.

## What a workforce plan can safely say

A company or a ministry can say that recognised startups had reported more than 23.36 lakh direct jobs by 31 March 2026, that the series is self-reported, and that a financial-year jobs increase of 36.1 percent sat beside a 51.6 percent increase in recognitions. A plan that needs unemployed-to-employed flows, wages, or durability after a startup is struck off needs a different statistical system. Closures are counted separately: the risk brief records the Ministry of Corporate Affairs figure.
