# Employment Reported Under PLI Auto and the Cell Programme

The jobs claim in this sector has an official before-and-after, and the after is the number to use. In September 2021 the PLI Auto notification estimated additional employment of over 7.5 lakh. In January 2026 the ministry's year-end review estimated 1.48 lakh jobs over five years and reported 48,974 jobs generated. Those are three different statements. Only one of them is a count of jobs the ministry says exist.

## Three figures, three jobs

The [23 September 2021 notification](https://pliauto.in/docs/press_release/2021-09-24%20PIB%20PR%20Govt%20notifies%20PLI%20for%20Auto_PressRelease.pdf) said that over five years the scheme would lead to fresh investment of over ₹42,500 crore, incremental production of over ₹2.3 lakh crore, and additional employment opportunities of over 7.5 lakh. A Cabinet note the week before had used "over 7.6 lakh" in a combined auto-and-drone announcement. The auto notification's figure is over 7.5 lakh.

The [13 January 2026 review](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2214039) keeps the investment estimate at ₹42,500 crore and puts incremental sales at ₹2,31,500 crore, with 1.48 lakh jobs over five years, for 82 approved applicants. It then states a generated figure: employment of 48,974, alongside cumulative investment of ₹35,657 crore and cumulative determined sales of ₹32,879 crore till 30 September 2025.

A reader who still quotes 7.5 lakh as the current official estimate is quoting the launch text. A reader who quotes 1.48 lakh is quoting the later estimate. A reader who quotes 48,974 is quoting the generated count the review pairs with a date. The review does not explain the gap between 7.5 lakh and 1.48 lakh. This brief does not invent a reason.

The cell programme's employment line is smaller and separate. Till 30 October 2025 the Advanced Chemistry Cell scheme had attracted investment of ₹2,878 crore and employment of 1,118. That count belongs to cell manufacturing under a ₹18,100 crore, 50 GWh programme. Adding it to 48,974 produces a sum the ministry does not publish as a combined auto-and-cell jobs total.

## The skill the schemes actually test

The documents name competencies by the certificates they require, not by a headcount of technicians.

Under PLI Auto, a product is incentivised only when domestic value addition is at least 50 percent. As of 31 December 2025, that certificate covered 94 variants at eight Champion OEM applicants and 37 variants at ten Component Champion applicants. The skill implied is the ability to localise a variant enough to pass a value-addition test administered through the scheme. The review does not publish a training curriculum for it.

Under PM E-DRIVE, the [28 July 2026 reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2290696) says OEMs have localised models and obtained phased-manufacturing-programme compliance certificates from the ministry's testing agencies. The FAME II portal had already made a related certification a condition: models were to be certified to AIS 156 under the Central Motor Vehicles Rules to claim that scheme's subsidy. The PM E-DRIVE allocation still includes ₹780 crore to upgrade those testing agencies. Certification capacity is the public skill infrastructure these texts fund. They do not report how many engineers that upgrade has trained.

The capital-goods scheme mentioned in the January review has its own training counts, including qualification packs and people trained at a welding centre. Those counts are for the capital-goods scheme. They are not an auto-sector jobs outcome, and this brief leaves them there.

## What a jobs model can honestly hold

A five-year model can carry 1.48 lakh as the January 2026 estimate and 48,974 as the generated count through the date the review gives. It can carry 1,118 for cells through 30 October 2025. It can note that determined sales, which pull component and vehicle employment with them, were ₹32,879 crore against a five-year incremental-sales estimate of ₹2,31,500 crore. It cannot turn the original 7.5 lakh into a forecast the ministry has reaffirmed. The ministry's later text uses 1.48 lakh.
