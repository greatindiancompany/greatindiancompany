# Work that India's cyber rules actually create

An opportunity, in this record, is a task an official text tells someone to perform. It is not a revenue pool. No document cited here prices the cybersecurity industry.

## Assurance work, with a public list of who may do it

CERT-In maintains a panel of information-security auditing organisations for vulnerability assessment and penetration testing of government and critical-sector systems. The 2023 annual report put the panel at 176. The Lok Sabha reply of 24 March 2026 put it at 237. The September 2025 MSME baseline invites those auditors to test smaller firms against the fifteen controls at least once a year, and it requires the auditor to say the test was against a minimum, not a full programme.

That is a defined piece of work: a baseline audit with a mandated disclosure about its scope. It is repeatable yearly. It is not a statement of how many MSMEs will buy it.

SEBI's framework adds a different assurance rhythm. After the glide path, regulated entities submit cyber-audit material on the framework's calendar. Market infrastructure institutions and qualified entities measure SOC efficacy every half year. Others obtain a yearly efficacy view from the SOC provider. Open VAPT observations go to the IT committee. The work is recurring review, not a single certificate.

IRDAI's guidelines make the information and cyber security policy an annual review by the CISO, approved by the information-security risk committee, and they attach a NIST framework annexure. Insurers and the intermediaries named in the April 2023 circular are the clients of that review.

## Monitoring work that smaller firms are not told to build alone

SEBI says an in-house SOC can be too costly and too specialised for smaller regulated entities, and it mandates BSE and NSE to run a Market SOC for that reason. Small-size and self-certification entities are mandated to onboard. The opportunity the circular creates is shared monitoring operated by the exchanges, plus a report back to SEBI on the Market SOC's functional efficacy.

Telecom entities establish their own SOC or one in collaboration with other telecom entities, keep the logs the rules list, and undergo periodic audits by a certified agency specified on the government portal. The portal, not this brief, sets the interval.

Cyber Swachhta Kendra is the public version of cleaning work. The 2023 report describes detection, ISP notification, and free removal tools built with Quick Heal, K7 and eScan, with 48.88 lakh cumulative downloads through December 2023, plus an Android mobile-security application. The MSME baseline tells firms to onboard with the Kendra for malware and botnet alerts. A private product can sit beside that service. The service itself is free and public.

## New offices and a new class of company

Consent Managers, which PIB says must be Indian companies, help people manage permissions. Registration under Rule 4 opens one year after 13 November 2025. The opportunity is a regulated intermediary role with a Board power to suspend or cancel. It is available only to applicants who meet the First Schedule. It is not a general software licence.

Every telecom entity needs a resident Indian as Chief Telecommunication Security Officer. Banks covered by the Reserve Bank directions need a CISO who is not the IT delivery head. Insurers need the fuller committee IRDAI lists. MSMEs are told to name one security in-charge. These are roles inside existing firms more often than they are new companies.

Training is also specified work. CERT-In's 26 programmes and 10,074 participants in 2023, CyTrain's more than 1,42,025 certificates for police and judicial officers up to 31 January 2026, and the MSME rule of awareness twice a year are three different classrooms. C-DAC's budget note lists cybersecurity among the domains of an R&D organisation with centres in twelve cities. That is public research capacity, not a startup census.

The landscape a founder can actually enter from these texts is narrow and real: empanelled audit, shared or certified monitoring, a Consent Manager application when the schedule fits, and training or tooling that helps a firm meet a control it has already been given. A landscape drawn as a single national market size is not in the record.
