An opportunity in India's cybersecurity resilience is a control an entity already owes and does not yet produce. It is work. It is not a market size. This page names types of work. It quotes no crore, no growth rate, and no share of spend.

## Work that exists because a duty exists

The duty has to be openable. For telecom networks, start with the Department of Telecommunications, [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/): licence security conditions, the Telecommunications (Telecom Cyber Security) Rules, 2024, security testing, and trusted-source rules. For other entities, start with the CERT-In direction or the sector regulator. Do not invent a duty from a slogan.

- **Security testing against a named requirement.** Labs and engineers who can take a product through Indian Telecom Security Assurance Requirements before connection. The opportunity is the product that must be certified and is not. A brochure about "cyber labs" is not the opportunity.
- **Trusted-source paperwork.** Vendors who sell into telecom networks and cannot assemble the product, component, and source detail the Trusted Telecom Portal asks for. The work is the file. The portal is not a consulting theme.
- **Audit operations.** Findings from a licence security audit that the entity cannot close with its own staff. The opportunity is closure with a date, not a retained slide deck.
- **The incident clock as a workflow.** Detection, a named filer, and a report that matches the direction. Many entities own a tool and lack the workflow. The [jobs page](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-jobs-and-skills-impact-dot-gov-20260330-391) is the person. This page is the unpaid control.
- **Retrieval, not storage.** Log platforms that cannot produce the record in the form the direction asks. The opportunity is the export that works during an incident.
- **A signer after the course.** Training that ends with a person who signs a real report or a real test on that entity's systems. A batch of certificates is a different product.

L.E.K. insights were paired with an earlier draft. An insights index is not a pipeline. It is omitted.

## How to tell work from a slogan

Name the entity, the instrument, the missing record, and the person who would pay for the missing record. If you cannot name the payer and the record, you have a theme. Themes are not a landscape.
