The global slice of Payment System Indicators - August 2026 is Part IV. Its heading is specific: international transactions done using cards, credit and debit, and prepaid instruments issued in India. July and August 2026 are the two months in the header. There is no UPI row in this part. A claim about UPI in another country needs a release that publishes that claim. This one publishes cards and prepaid instruments.

RBI marks the file provisional. Part IV is also not split by card scheme, so it is not a RuPay-abroad figure.

## August 2026, instruments issued in India

| Line | Volume (lakh) | Value (₹ crore) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Card payments | 140.80 | 10,537 |
| Credit cards | 93.31 | 8,146 |
| Credit cards at PoS | 34.15 | 2,365 |
| Credit cards online | 59.15 | 5,781 |
| Debit cards | 47.50 | 2,392 |
| Prepaid instruments | 26.56 | 1,139 |
| Cash withdrawal at ATMs abroad | 4.12 | 1,086 |
| Total international payments | 171.49 | 12,763 |

Credit cards are the largest piece of the card value: ₹8,146 crore of ₹10,537 crore. Online credit-card use was 59.15 lakh transactions, against 34.15 lakh at PoS. Cash taken from ATMs outside India was 4.12 lakh withdrawals and ₹1,086 crore, an average of about ₹26,360 per withdrawal.

July 2026, in the neighbouring column, was a little higher: total international payments of 181.67 lakh and ₹13,427 crore.

## Scale against the domestic August

Domestic UPI in the same release was ₹29,82,356 crore. International card payments of ₹10,537 crore are 0.35 percent of that UPI value. Domestic credit-card payments were ₹2,02,449 crore. International credit-card payments of ₹8,146 crore are 4.0 percent of that domestic credit-card value.

The comparison is a scale check. An international card purchase and a domestic UPI payment are different products. The check answers a narrower question: how large is the printed cross-border card flow next to the printed domestic flows. It is small next to UPI, and a modest slice of credit-card value.

## What a global reader still has to fetch elsewhere

UPI's links with payment systems outside India, visitor wallets, and merchant acceptance abroad are product and policy facts. They belong in an NPCI or RBI note that states them. Part IV will not supply them. NPCI's UPI product statistics remain the product page for the domestic UPI series that this file also reports, at 2,45,089.58 lakh transactions in August 2026.
