# Where Captive R&D and Tier-2 Delivery Can Still Grow

Opportunity, in the official record, is a line that is growing inside a published table, or a scheme that is funded and counted. It is not a slogan about India becoming a hub. Four openings meet that test. Each has a boundary printed in the same documents.

## In-house engineering is the activity line that moved

The Reserve Bank's 2025-26 survey puts engineering services at **US$ 12.0 billion**, up from **US$ 10.8 billion**. Inside that, product design engineering, covering mechanical and electronics work and excluding software, rose from **US$ 1.8 billion** to **US$ 3.0 billion**. Embedded solutions rose from **US$ 1.8 billion** to **US$ 2.0 billion**. IT services, the larger book, rose from **US$ 131.3 billion** to **US$ 147.0 billion**.

A captive R&D or product-engineering centre resembles the design and embedded lines more than it resembles the residual "other BPO" line of **US$ 42.2 billion**. The survey still does not label any row as captive. The opportunity for a parent is that this kind of export already has scale and grew in the latest year. The opportunity for a vendor is to sell into that activity, not into the entire **US$ 221.4 billion**.

Off-site delivery at **91.7 percent** means the commercial pattern is already "do the work in India". A new centre is joining a delivery model the export statistics have already recorded, rather than asking India to invent one.

## Entry for IT and BPM is on the automatic route

Make in India lists IT and BPM at **100 percent** foreign investment on the automatic route. The Ministry of Finance told the Lok Sabha on 20 July 2026 that more than **90 percent** of FDI inflow arrives on that route, and that strategically important sectors are the exception. Computer-software-and-hardware equity in 2025-26 was **USD 13,946 million** on the DPIIT sheet cited in that answer, the largest sector line in the top ten, after **USD 7,814 million** the year before.

The opening is speed of entry for a straightforward software or operations subsidiary. The boundary is the current consolidated policy: security conditions, and any investor the policy places on the government route, still have to be checked. Equity into the sector is also not a ring-fenced pool of captive deals. Hardware and share purchases sit in the same bucket. Read the boundary in the [investment brief](/blog/global-capability-centers-investment-implications-gst-gov-20260330-246).

## Smaller cities have a public platform

STPI, under MeitY, reports **59** of **67** centres in Tier II and Tier III cities. As on 31 March 2025 it had **24** Centres of Entrepreneurship with **805** startups onboarded, and **686** startups assisted under the Next Generation Incubation Scheme, which can provide up to **₹25 lakh**. Incubation covered **411** firms. New floor space came up at Koraput and Jajpur, and further centres were under implementation in cities including Agra, Gorakhpur, Amritsar, and Jamshedpur.

That is an opening for a feeder: a product startup, a specialised team, a pilot floor. It is a weak opening for a large export campus. The same annual report's tentative export table puts about **94 percent** of STPI-registered IT/ITeS exports in six states, with Karnataka alone near **43 percent**. A parent can use the scheme map to recruit and incubate outside the big six. It should not tell its board that the export stock has already moved there.

## Compliance work has a dated market

The data-protection rules create a narrow, dated demand from any captive that processes personal data in India. Consent Manager registration opens on **13 November 2026**. Notice, security, breach notice, and the cross-border rules commence on **13 May 2027**. A founder selling workflow around those duties is selling against a Gazette calendar. A founder selling "GCC transformation" without a duty, a dataset, or a date is selling outside the record. The clause that may later pin specified data inside India is Rule 13(4), and it names traffic data as well as personal data. Products that ignore logs will miss the sentence the rule actually wrote.
