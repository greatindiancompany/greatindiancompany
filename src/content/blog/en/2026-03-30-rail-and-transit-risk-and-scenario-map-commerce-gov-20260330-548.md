# Risks in India's Rail Build-Out That the Official Record Already Shows

These risks are gaps in figures the ministries have published. They are not probabilities, and they are not a downside case invented for a model.

## Most of the sanctioned railway is still uncommissioned

As on 1 April 2026, 514 projects covering 40,000 km and about ₹8.31 lakh crore were sanctioned. Length commissioned to March 2026 was 12,583 km. Expenditure was ₹3,05,253 crore. New lines had 3,361 km commissioned out of 16,634 km. Doubling had 6,177 km out of 19,379 km. Gauge conversion was closer to done, at 3,045 km out of 3,987 km. The execution risk is concentrated in new lines and doubling. Quoting the 8.32 km-a-day commissioning pace from 2014-26 without this stock overstates how much of the book is already a railway.

If commissioning continues near the recent pace, the open book still runs for years. If it slows, the ₹8.31 lakh crore sanction remains a sanction. Both readings use the same table. Neither is a ministry forecast.

## Terminal capacity and freight yield are already short of the headline

Commissioned Gati Shakti terminals have an estimated capacity of 224 million tonnes a year. They handled 146 million tonnes in 2025-26. Capacity on paper is not throughput. The risk for a terminal investor is the gap, not the count of 142 commissioned sites.

National freight loading rose 3.25 percent in 2025-26, to 1,670 million tonnes. Freight earnings rose 1.44 percent, to about ₹1,77,754 crore. A year in which tonnes outrun revenue is a yield risk for anyone modelling railway freight as a price-times-volume business. The ministry has not, in that 1 April release, assigned the gap to tariffs, lead, or commodity mix.

## The new passenger product is still a pilot fleet

Vande Bharat sleeper service stood at two services on 23 July 2026. The first, Howrah–Kamakhya, began on 22 January 2026. The 11 March 2026 reply listed what further services depend on: section capacity, a path, rolling stock, matching infrastructure, maintenance, and traffic. A scenario in which sleeper trains quickly become the long-distance product is not supported by the fleet count. Chair-car service is the scaled product, at 162 services.

Passenger fares carry a different risk. The railway disclosed a subsidy of ₹60,239 crore on passenger tickets in 2024-25, about 43 percent on average. Fare comparisons with China, Japan, and France describe the ticket. They do not describe an unsubsidised yield.

## One metro figure is internally inconsistent across PIB notes

The 9 August 2025 PIB note put the 2025-26 metro budget at ₹34,807 crore and the network at 1,013 km across 23 cities by May 2025. The 15 March 2026 note put the 2025-26 metro budget at ₹29,550 crore and the network at about 1,095 km across 26 cities. Use the later network length. Do not average the two budgets. The source risk is real: two official notes, one year, two budget figures.

The household findings in the March note — fewer missed home-loan payments in Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Delhi, as summarised from the January 2026 EAC-PM study — are a PIB summary of a study. They are not a credit model.
