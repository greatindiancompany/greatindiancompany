# Wholesale inflation is the cost print for smaller firms

A shop, a job-work unit, or a small manufacturer usually meets inflation first as an invoice, not as the consumer price index. In August 2026 the provisional Wholesale Price Index, base 2022–23, rose **9.92% year on year**. The provisional Consumer Price Index for the same month was **4.82%**. Both figures were released on 14 September 2026. They are different baskets, and the gap between them is the operating fact.

The wholesale estimates are compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser, an attached office of the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, and were issued as a press note carried by the Press Information Bureau. The office states that role on its [about page](https://eaindustry.nic.in/about_us.asp). Item-wise series from April 2023 sit at [eaindustry.nic.in](https://eaindustry.nic.in/download_data_2223.asp). The retail figures are MoSPI’s [August CPI release](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1789381904344_6c792dcf-8a9f-4fca-93d3-99d833bdb358_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_August_2026.pdf).

## Where the wholesale rate comes from

| WPI group | Weight | August 2026 inflation | July 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All commodities | 100 | 9.92% | 9.78% |
| Primary articles | 22.76 | 7.76% | 8.52% |
| Fuel and power | 14.11 | 22.93% | 20.05% |
| Manufactured products | 63.13 | 8.37% | 8.29% |
| Food index | 24.99 | 7.05% | 6.65% |

Manufactured products are 63.13% of the wholesale basket, so an 8.37% rate there moves the all-commodities index even when that group rate sits below the all-commodities rate. Fuel and power is a smaller weight, 14.11%, and a much faster rate. Primary articles cooled from 8.52% in July to 7.76% in August. The all-commodities index still edged from 9.78% to 9.92% because fuel and manufactured goods did not cool.

The press note names the main drivers in August as mineral oils (petroleum products), food articles, manufacture of food products, manufacture of basic metals, non-food articles, and manufacture of chemicals and chemical products.

## Lines that show up on a small firm’s bill

These are group inflation rates from the August annexure, all provisional:

- Mineral oils: **38.48%** (32.40% in July). Weight 8.19.
- Crude petroleum and natural gas: **34.41%**. Weight 0.72.
- Electricity: **−1.73%**. Weight 4.46. Power inside the fuel group did not follow mineral oils.
- Manufacture of chemicals and chemical products: **14.30%**.
- Textiles: **12.63%**.
- Rubber and plastic products: **11.18%**.
- Basic metals: **10.88%**.
- Electrical equipment: **11.11%**.
- Manufacture of food products: **9.65%**.
- Motor vehicles, trailers, and semi-trailers: **3.70%**.
- Computer, electronic, and optical products: **−0.47%**.

A unit that buys fuel, chemicals, cloth, or metal is in the fast groups. A unit whose main purchased input is electronics is in a group that was slightly down. “Wholesale inflation is 9.92%” is the average, not the invoice.

For April–August 2026–27, cumulative WPI inflation was 9.56% for all commodities, 25.20% for fuel and power, and 7.78% for manufactured products. A year earlier, April–August 2025–26, cumulative all-commodities inflation was −0.20%. The comparison is in Annexure IV of the same press note.

## What the retail index says about selling prices

CPI is what the household index records, which is closer to what many small firms can charge. In August, combined CPI was 4.82%. Food and beverages was 5.66%. Restaurants and accommodation services was 8.38%. Transport was 4.60%, and within it the operation of personal transport equipment was 7.40%. Health was 1.34%.

The August MPC resolution, written before these August indices, said the rise in inflation was mostly food and fuel, with limited pass-through of cost pressures and little sign yet of a broad-based increase. It also said the fuel spike had shown up in categories such as restaurant charges. Core inflation, CPI excluding food and fuel, was unchanged at 3.9% in May and June. That is an RBI figure for those two months. It is not an August core rate, and MoSPI did not print one in the August CPI release.

For a firm, the practical reading is narrower than the policy paragraph. Input groups in WPI, especially mineral oils, chemicals, textiles, and basic metals, were running well above the retail headline. Some selling-price groups in CPI were much closer to 4% or below it. Margin is the space between those two official prints, and it differs by line of business.

## Treat the first wholesale print as unfinished

August WPI was compiled with a weighted response rate of 84.4%. June’s provisional inflation of 9.87% was revised to 9.97% when the index was finalised, and the June index moved from 110.2 to 110.3. A cost budget that uses the first August print should leave room for that kind of revision. September’s wholesale release is scheduled for 14 October 2026.
