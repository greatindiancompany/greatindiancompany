# What Changed in Indian Rail and Metro This Quarter

The quarter that matters here is July–September 2026, the second quarter of FY 2026-27. Four Ministry of Railways statements, carried by the Press Information Bureau, changed the operating picture. They did not replace the last full metro snapshot.

## Both freight corridors are now described as commissioned

On 7 August 2026 the Union Minister for Railways told the Rajya Sabha that both dedicated freight corridors are commissioned: the Eastern corridor from Ludhiana to Sonnagar (1,337 km) and the Western corridor from Dadri to Jawaharlal Nehru Port (1,506 km). The same reply said about 443 trains a day run on the corridors, and that diverting freight onto them has freed paths on the conventional network.

The reply also recorded the next corridor, not as a construction award but as a study. Union Budget 2026 announced a Dedicated Freight Corridor from Dankuni in the east to Surat in the west. Preparation of the detailed project report has been taken up. A commissioned pair of corridors and a corridor still at the DPR stage are different facts. Treating them as one “DFC programme” hides that difference.

## The passenger fleet count moved to services, not a slogan

On 23 July 2026 the ministry reported 162 Vande Bharat chair-car services and 2 Vande Bharat sleeper services, plus 72 Amrit Bharat services. The first sleeper, Howrah–Kamakhya (27575/27576), started regular operation on 22 January 2026. An earlier reply, on 1 April 2026, had counted 81 chair-car pairs and one sleeper pair as of 25 March 2026. A pair is an up and a down service, so 81 pairs and 162 services can describe the same chair-car set. The later release is the one to quote for this quarter: 162 chair-car services and 2 sleeper services.

The same July reply put customer-amenities funding on a current-year footing. Plan Head 53 has an allocation of ₹11,972 crore for 2026-27, with expenditure of ₹4,633 crore up to June 2026.

## Electrification and Kavach, as of July 2026

A PIB backgrounder posted on 12 August 2026 stated that rail electrification had reached 99.6 percent by July 2026, from about 20 percent before 2014, and that Kavach version 4.0 had been commissioned on 2,633 route km as of July 2026. It also restated the railway budget at ₹2.78 lakh crore in FY 2026-27, against ₹32,000 crore in 2014-15. The more precise gross budgetary support figure remains the 1 April 2026 reply: ₹2,78,030 crore in Budget Estimates 2026-27.

## Hiring is a calendar, not a one-off drive

On 22 July 2026 the ministry said recruitment against 1,61,889 non-gazetted vacancies is underway under the annual calendars for 2024, 2025, and 2026. In FY 2026-27, up to 30 June 2026, panels for more than 3,300 candidates had been finalised, a majority of them in safety categories. The 2026 calendar had already notified three employment notices covering 17,803 vacancies, including 11,127 assistant loco pilot posts.

## What this quarter did not update

The metro figure used on this site is still the 15 March 2026 PIB note: about 1,095 km operational across 26 cities, including 55 km of the Delhi–Meerut RRTS. None of the July–August railway releases above replaced that metro total. A reader who needs the city network should stay with that March note, and with the [Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs metro policy](https://www.mohua.gov.in/upload/whatsnew/59a3f7f130eecMetro_Rail_Policy_2017.pdf), until MoHUA or PIB publishes a later network length.

The full-year freight result that these corridor statements sit on is still 1,670 million tonnes in 2025-26. That number, the zone split, and the commodity mix are in the [evidence snapshot](/blog/rail-and-transit-evidence-snapshot-commerce-gov-20260330-668).
