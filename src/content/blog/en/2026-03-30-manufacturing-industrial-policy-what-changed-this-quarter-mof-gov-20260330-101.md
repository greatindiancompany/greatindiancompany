# What changed in India's PLI manufacturing numbers through March 2026

As of 24 September 2026, the latest cumulative Production Linked Incentive (PLI) checkpoint in the official record used here is **31 March 2026**. It was placed in Parliament in July. There is no later cumulative update in these documents. A reader who needs an April-to-September 2026 number should stop and wait for the next Commerce and Industry reply, rather than extend the March figures.

## Two official checkpoints, not one blended story

On 27 March 2026 the Minister of State for Commerce and Industry, Jitin Prasada, told the Rajya Sabha the position **as on 31 December 2025**: cumulative investment over ₹2.16 lakh crore, production and sales over ₹20.41 lakh crore, exports over ₹8.3 lakh crore, and employment of more than 14.39 lakh people, direct and indirect. Electronics incentives disbursed were about ₹15,554 crore. Automobile and auto-component incentives disbursed were about ₹2,377.56 crore. That reply is on the Press Information Bureau as [PRID 2246089](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2246089).

On 28 July 2026 the same minister answered Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1423 with the position **as on 31 March 2026**: 892 applications approved across 14 sectors, actual investment over ₹2.40 lakh crore, production and sales over ₹22.66 lakh crore, exports over ₹15.2 lakh crore, employment over 14.15 lakh (direct and indirect), of which 8.4 lakh is described as direct, and cumulative incentive disbursement of ₹35,354 crore. The approved outlay stated in that reply is ₹1.91 lakh crore. The reply is [Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1423](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1423_Rd4xWf.pdf). A 21 July 2026 PIB note, [PRID 2287008](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287008), carries the same investment, export, and employment headlines and a sector table.

Between those two dates the reported investment, sales, and exports are higher. Reported employment is not. The December statement said more than 14.39 lakh. The March statement said over 14.15 lakh. These notes do not invent a reason for that difference. Anyone using the series for a jobs claim should keep both dates.

## What else the July replies actually added

The 28 July reply also records a trade-policy change that sits next to manufacturing. Press Note 3 of 2026, dated 23 July 2026, revises the FDI policy on e-commerce so that the restriction on the inventory-based model does not apply to exports of goods manufactured or produced in India. That is a rule for export sales of domestic goods. It is not a new PLI sector.

The same reply describes a 2026 change for investments from land-border countries in specified manufacturing activities, including electronic components, capital goods, polysilicon and wafers, advanced battery components, and rare-earth permanent magnets. Those proposals are to be decided within 60 days, and majority shareholding and control stay with resident Indian citizens or resident Indian entities. Read the annexure before treating that as a general FDI opening.

DPIIT's Annual Report 2024-25 still states a PLI incentive outlay of Rs. 1.97 lakh crore. The later parliamentary reply states ₹1.91 lakh crore. Use the dated reply you are quoting. Do not average them.

## What did not change in these papers

No document here says a fifteenth PLI sector was added. The count remains 14. DPIIT remains the nodal department for coordination and monitoring. The administrative ministry for each scheme still implements it. The 21 July note says the Empowered Group of Secretaries, chaired by the Cabinet Secretary, reviews implementation and that some schemes have been modified. It does not publish the text of those modifications.
