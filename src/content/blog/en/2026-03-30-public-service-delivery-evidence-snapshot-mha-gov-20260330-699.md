# Which official counts to cite for DigiLocker, UMANG, DBT, and CSCs

Public service delivery has plenty of official numbers. They come from different notes, with different cut-off dates, and they sometimes disagree by a small margin. The disciplined habit is to quote one sentence and its date. The undisciplined habit is to average them into a figure no document published.

## A baseline that is still the latest of its kind

On [3 August 2022](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1847837), PIB published a Lok Sabha reply from the Minister of State for Electronics and Information Technology. For service delivery it stated:

- Common Service Centres: over 400 digital services; 5.31 lakh functional centres, of which 4.20 lakh were at gram panchayat level.
- UMANG: more than 1,570 government services and over 22,000 bill-payment services.
- e-District: 4,671 e-services in 709 districts.
- DigiLocker: more than 11.7 crore users, more than 532 crore documents, 2,167 issuers.
- Direct Benefit Transfer: 315 schemes across 53 ministries, and Rs 24.3 lakh crore disbursed.

No 2026 note reviewed for this brief restates a national e-District service count. The 4,671 and 709 figures remain the dated national statement. They are not a current operations dashboard.

## Two August 2026 documents, read separately

The Lok Sabha reply submitted on 5 August 2026 and published by PIB on [6 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295635) states:

- DigiLocker: over 71.66 crore registered users, more than 936.03 crore documents, 2,822 issuers.
- UMANG: 2,575 services for individuals, including 17 from the Government of Tamil Nadu.
- DBT: 318 schemes of 56 ministries, over Rs 52 lakh crore.
- Common Service Centres: over 800 services; as of May 2026, 5.01 lakh functional, 3.91 lakh at gram panchayat level.

The PIB research note [Empowering Governance through Digital Infrastructure](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/aug/doc2026812952501.pdf), dated 12 August 2026, states:

- DigiLocker as of 31 July 2026: over 72 crore users and more than 934 crore documents.
- UMANG as of 23 July 2026: 2,585 services and over 798 crore transactions, from 166 services and 4.6 crore transactions in 2017.
- Common Service Centres as of June 2026: more than 5.16 lakh functional.
- DBT: 318 schemes across 56 ministries and more than Rs 52 lakh crore.

DigiLocker users, DigiLocker documents, UMANG's service count, and the CSC count each differ across these two August notes. The gaps are small next to the 2022 baseline and large enough to matter in a citation. Prefer the 6 August reply when you need a parliamentary sentence. Prefer the 12 August note when you need an as-of date or the only recent UMANG transaction total in this pair.

## A June note that should not be blended in

PIB's 27 June 2026 note, [The Digital India](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jun/doc2026627904001.pdf), says DigiLocker had over 70.69 crore users and more than 850 crore documents as of March 2026, and that UMANG grew from 166 services in 2017 to 2,572 services by June 2026. The transaction sentence in the text extraction of that note is garbled, so this brief does not cite a June transaction total from it. The same note's public-internet pillar says more than 6.5 lakh Common Service Centres. That centre count is not the May 2026 figure of 5.01 lakh in the August Lok Sabha reply. Cite one. Do not explain the gap with a story the notes do not tell.

On cash, the June note says that as of June 2026 direct benefits worth over Rs 51 lakh crore had been transferred to 176 crore citizens. The note prints that beneficiary count and does not say whether it counts unique people or repeated payments. The August parliamentary reply says over Rs 52 lakh crore and does not repeat the 176 crore figure. Keep each sentence with its own date.

A separate PIB note on [15 June 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=158902&id=158902&lang=1&reg=1) says approximately 6 lakh CPGRAMS grievances were resolved between January 2025 and February 2026, with 69.8 percent rated satisfactory. That is a grievance statistic. It is not a DigiLocker or DBT stock figure.
