# State and district patterns in India’s export trade

State export league tables travel faster than the footnotes. The official pattern in 2026 is two layers that answer different questions: where a district has an export plan, and where the customs value was actually booked.

## The plan layer is Districts as Export Hubs

On 24 March 2026 the Minister of State for Commerce and Industry told the Lok Sabha that State Export Promotion Committees and District Export Promotion Committees had been constituted in all 36 states and Union territories. Draft District Export Action Plans had been prepared for 590 districts, and 249 of those had been formally notified by the district committees. The [reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2244401) is an institutional count. It is not a ranking of export value.

The products named in that reply illustrate the point. Sabarkantha was associated with ceramic and tiles, and potato. Jalgaon was associated with banana and Bharit brinjal. The reply also said products had been identified across all 52 districts of Madhya Pradesh, with onions and pharma cited for Indore and oranges for Agar Malwa, and it gave examples for Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand. Those are identified products. They are not district export totals.

The same reply said export data are published at [niryat.gov.in](https://niryat.gov.in) and [dgciskol.gov.in](https://www.dgciskol.gov.in). The Department of Commerce also maintains region-wise merchandise series on [TRADESTAT](https://tradestat.commerce.gov.in/eidb/region_wise_export). A state share taken from a slide, without the year and the DGCIS extract, is not something this brief will invent.

## Why the value layer will not match the plan layer

Customs value is booked against the declaration: the port, the IEC holder’s registration, and the state code DGCIS maps. A district action plan is a list of products and bottlenecks. A cluster can have a notified plan and still show a small customs value if the exporter of record sits in another state, or if the goods move through a port that the local narrative does not mention. Comparing “notified plans” with “top exporting states” as if they were one series produces a false concentration story.

Foreign Trade Policy 2023 had already put districts into the rulebook: state and district export promotion committees, and a district-specific action plan for identified products and services. The March 2026 reply is the later count of how far that paperwork had gone: 590 drafts, 249 notified. The gap between those two numbers is the execution fact. A draft plan is not a notified plan, and a notified plan is not a shipment.

## How an operator should use the map

If you are choosing a factory district, start with the notified action plan and the product list, then pull the state and district value from the DGCIS portal for the year you are financing. If you are briefing a government, report coverage (36 states and Union territories, 590 drafts, 249 notified) separately from value.
