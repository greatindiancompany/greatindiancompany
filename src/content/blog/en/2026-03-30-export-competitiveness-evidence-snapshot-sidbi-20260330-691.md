# A snapshot of the trade figures that can be checked

Quote the series with the number. The figures below are only those read from the named release. Where a balance is the difference of two printed totals, the arithmetic is shown.

| What | Figure | Where it is printed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| July 2026 merchandise exports | US$ 44.24 billion (US$ 36.98 billion in July 2025) | Commerce, [13 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2298878) |
| July 2026 merchandise imports | US$ 76.22 billion (US$ 64.86 billion) | Same release |

| August 2026 merchandise imports | US$ 70,670 million, up 14.1 percent from 61,956 | DGCIS quick estimate, [import annex](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/sep/doc_9878_20260915_19565401.pdf) |

| April–August 2026–27 merchandise exports | US$ 215,912 million, up 17.8 percent from 183,212 | Country annex |

July in the August export annex is 44,243 million dollars, and July in the import annex is 76,223 million. Both sit on the July narrative after ordinary rounding into billions. That overlap is why the annex is usable. Adding the 13 August April–July total to the August month will not reproduce the annex cumulative. Do not do it.

## Services, two different prints for July

The 13 August Commerce release estimated July 2026 services exports at US$ 35.89 billion and imports at US$ 18.94 billion, and said RBI data then stopped at June. On 31 August, RBI’s provisional July print was exports of US$ 38,253 million, up 13.4 percent, and imports of US$ 20,606 million, up 19.1 percent. April–July services exports in the four provisional RBI months are 37,021 + 33,355 + 36,371 + 38,253 = 145,000 million dollars. The Commerce April–July services export estimate was US$ 142.64 billion. The gap is the July estimation.

For April–July, Commerce estimated a services trade surplus of US$ 69.17 billion, against US$ 64.35 billion a year earlier, and said services exports were estimated to have grown 6.38 percent. Mark that surplus as an estimate.

## Balance of payments, which is not the quick estimate

April–June 2026–27, RBI preliminary, 1 September 2026: current account deficit US$ 4.2 billion (0.5 percent of GDP); goods deficit US$ 86.1 billion; net services US$ 51.6 billion on services exports of US$ 106.2 billion; personal transfers US$ 42.9 billion.

Full year 2025–26, RBI preliminary, 8 June 2026: current account deficit US$ 25.2 billion (0.6 percent of GDP); goods exports US$ 446.1 billion; goods imports US$ 783.4 billion; services exports US$ 421.3 billion; net services US$ 216.6 billion.

National-accounts real export growth for April–June 2026–27 was 12.0 percent in the MoSPI estimates reported on 1 September 2026. That is not a dollar customs rate.
