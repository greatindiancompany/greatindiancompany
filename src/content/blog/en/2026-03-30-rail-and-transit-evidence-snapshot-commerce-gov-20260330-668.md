# The Indian Rail and Metro Figures That Are on the Official Record

Each figure below is tied to a Ministry of Railways or PIB release. Where two releases disagree, both are shown. Nothing here is estimated by Great Indian Company.

## Freight and passengers, FY 2025-26

From the 1 April 2026 release:

- Freight loading: 1,670 million tonnes, up 3.25 percent
- Wagons handled: 2,91,86,475, up 4.56 percent from 2,79,12,271
- Freight earnings: about ₹1,77,754 crore, up 1.44 percent from about ₹1,75,302 crore
- Fertilisers up 13.49 percent; pig iron and steel up 13.11 percent
- Iron ore up 6.74 percent, to 190.12 million tonnes; cement up 4.74 percent, to 157.17 million tonnes
- Passengers: 741 crore, up 3.54 percent from 716 crore
- Passenger revenue: about ₹80,000 crore, up 5.96 percent from about ₹75,500 crore

The 7 August 2026 reply repeats 1,670 million tonnes for 2025-26 and calls Indian Railways the second-largest freight-carrying railway in the world. Its loading series runs from 1,098 million tonnes in 2014-15 to that 1,670.

## Corridors, terminals, and track

From the 7 August 2026 reply, unless noted:

- Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor: Ludhiana to Sonnagar, 1,337 km, commissioned
- Western Dedicated Freight Corridor: Dadri to JNPT, 1,506 km, commissioned
- About 443 trains a day on the dedicated freight corridors
- Dankuni–Surat corridor: announced in Union Budget 2026; detailed project report taken up
- Gati Shakti terminals commissioned: 142; estimated capacity 224 million tonnes a year; about ₹10,000 crore private investment; 146 million tonnes handled in 2025-26
- Private wagon rakes: about 275 special-purpose, 397 general-purpose, and 54 automobile rakes
- New track commissioned, 2014-26: 36,429 km (8.32 km a day), against 7,599 km in 2009-14 (4.2 km a day)
- Sanctioned projects as on 1 April 2026: 514 projects, 40,000 km, about ₹8.31 lakh crore; 12,583 km commissioned and ₹3,05,253 crore spent to March 2026

An earlier stock, in the 1 April 2026 release, was 128 commissioned terminals and about ₹9,183 crore of private investment, with in-principle approval for 292 more. The August stock is the later one.

## Passenger fleet, budget, and safety systems

- 23 July 2026: 162 Vande Bharat chair-car services, 2 Vande Bharat sleeper services, 72 Amrit Bharat services
- First sleeper service, Howrah–Kamakhya, from 22 January 2026
- 11 March 2026: Vande Bharat Sleeper fare ₹2.40 to ₹3.80 per passenger-kilometre; passenger subsidy ₹60,239 crore in 2024-25
- 1 April 2026: gross budgetary support ₹2,78,030 crore in Budget Estimates 2026-27, of which ₹79,072 crore is for new tracks; 120 Vande Bharat trainsets taken up at the Marathwada Rail Coach Factory, Latur, set up at ₹685.79 crore
- 12 August 2026 backgrounder: electrification 99.6 percent by July 2026; Kavach version 4.0 on 2,633 route km as of July 2026
- 22 July 2026: recruitment underway against 1,61,889 non-gazetted vacancies; 5.53 lakh recruitments from 2014-15 to 2025-26

## Metro

From the 15 March 2026 PIB note: about 1,095 km operational across 26 cities, including 55 km of Delhi–Meerut RRTS, up from 248 km and 5 cities in 2014. Since 2014, 38 projects covering 1,051 km sanctioned at about ₹3.44 lakh crore. Metro budget in that note: ₹29,550 crore in 2025-26, against ₹5,798 crore in 2013-14. Namo Bharat on Delhi–Meerut: 160 km/h operational, 180 km/h design, in service since October 2023.

A 9 August 2025 PIB note had reported 1,013 km across 23 cities by May 2025, daily ridership above 1.12 crore, and a 2025-26 metro budget of ₹34,807 crore. The March 2026 length and budget are the later pair. The ridership figure was not restated in March.
