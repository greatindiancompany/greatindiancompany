# India's DPI Path from Jan Dhan to Courts and Commerce

## A sequence, not a target year

The Press Information Bureau research note of 6 March 2026 tells the mid-term story of India's digital public infrastructure as a sequence of systems. It does not set a 2030 target, a spending path, or a coverage goal beyond the figures already reached. This brief keeps that sequence and stops there. The 23 September 2026 edit does not extend it.

## 2014 to 2016: accounts, identity, documents, payments

Jan Dhan launched in August 2014 as the account layer. The note says accounts later stood at 57.71 crore in March 2026, up from 14.72 crore in 2015. DigiLocker launched in 2015. Digital India launched on 1 July 2015, with the three vision areas still stated on the programme about page: infrastructure as a utility, services on demand, and digital empowerment.

The note places Aadhaar authentication on that base. More than 144 crore Aadhaar numbers had been generated as of March 2026. UPI is the retail real-time system the note then measures through January 2026. On a separate Department of Financial Services page, the account-aggregator master direction is dated 2 September 2016.

## 2017 to 2021: service windows, learning, health, and interfaces

UMANG launched in 2017 as the mobile window for government services. DIKSHA launched in 2017 as the school-learning platform. eSanjeevani launched in November 2019 for telemedicine. API Setu was initiated by MeitY in March 2020. Aarogya Setu launched on 2 April 2020 and, the note says, later became a national health app on the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. CoWIN launched on 16 January 2021 and managed over 220 crore vaccine doses. POSHAN Tracker rolled out on 1 March 2021. PM GatiShakti launched on 13 October 2021 as a GIS planning layer for infrastructure projects.

## 2022 to 2027: commerce, skills, and a stated court outlay

ONDC launched in 2022 as an open commerce network. The Skill India Digital Hub launched in 2023. e-Courts Phase III, approved in September 2023, runs from 2023 to 2027 with an outlay of ₹7,210 crore. Those are the furthest dated programme points in the note. Nothing in the note extends the sequence past 2027 with a new milestone.

India Stack Global now lists 15 of these platforms in one showcase, including U-WIN alongside Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, and the health and learning systems. The list is a map of what was built. It is not a roadmap of what comes next.

## How to read the middle distance

The path on record runs from a JAM base to shared rails for payments, documents, health, learning, commerce, and courts. The March 2026 counts are the latest points the note gives. They are not a projection.
