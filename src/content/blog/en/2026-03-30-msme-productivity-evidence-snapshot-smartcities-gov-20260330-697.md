# The official numbers behind India's MSME productivity story

This page is a sheet. Every figure is tied to the release that printed it. Where two official notes disagree, both are shown. Nothing here is a forecast, and nothing here multiplies one series by another.

## Classification in force since 1 April 2025

Source: Ministry of MSME notification S.O. 1364(E) dated 21 March 2025, and the Ministry's "What's MSME" page.

| Category | Investment ceiling | Turnover ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Micro | ₹2.5 crore | ₹10 crore |
| Small | ₹25 crore | ₹100 crore |
| Medium | ₹125 crore | ₹500 crore |

Either ceiling moves an enterprise up. Both must fall for it to move down. The Press Information Bureau's Budget 2025-26 note described the change as 2.5 times on investment and 2 times on turnover.

## Unincorporated activity, ASUSE 2025

Source: MoSPI via PIB, 24 March 2026. Reference period January–December 2025. Compared with ASUSE 2023-24 (October 2023–September 2024). Universe: unincorporated non-agricultural manufacturing, trade, and other services, excluding construction. Value figures are current prices. Per-worker and per-establishment gross value added refer to market establishments.

| Indicator | ASUSE 2023-24 | ASUSE 2025 | Published change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Establishments | 7.34 crore | 7.92 crore | about 7.97 percent |
| Workers (printed in hundreds) | 12,05,998 | 12,80,518 | 6.18 percent; PIB also states about 12.81 crore workers and more than 74.52 lakh jobs added |
| Gross value added, market establishments | ₹17,97,278 crore | ₹19,92,577 crore | 10.87 percent |
| GVA per worker | ₹1,49,742 | ₹1,56,539 | 4.54 percent |
| GVA per establishment | ₹2,45,687 | ₹2,52,699 | levels published; a percent is not stated in the highlight |
| Emolument per hired worker | ₹1,41,071 | ₹1,46,550 | 3.88 percent |
| Establishments using internet | 26.7 percent | 39.4 percent | published as a rise, without a single percent in the highlight |
| Women-owned proprietary establishments | 26.2 percent | 27.0 percent | published as a rise to 27 percent |

Sector shares of gross value added in the 2025 round: other services 42 percent, trade 37 percent, manufacturing 21 percent. Gross-value-added growth inside the 10.87 percent: trade 16.77 percent, manufacturing 8.52 percent, other services 7.36 percent. The sample for ASUSE 2025 was 6,70,289 establishments. MoSPI said the full report and unit-level data would be released in April 2026. Use mospi.gov.in for anything beyond this press note.

## Contribution shares, a third population

Both the 14 May 2026 and 26 June 2026 notes say about 31.1 percent of GDP, 35.4 percent of manufacturing output, and 48.58 percent of exports. The June note calls these January 2026 data. They are not ASUSE ratios.

Employment language differs: about 32.8 crore livelihoods in the May note, and more than 38.9 crore people employed in the June note. Those are successive Ministry statements. They are not a revision of the 12.81 crore ASUSE worker estimate.

## Credit and process funnels, for context

CGTMSE ceiling for micro and small enterprises: credit support up to ₹10 crore on guarantees approved on or after 1 April 2025. Approvals from 1 January to 30 November 2025: 29.03 lakh guarantees, ₹3.77 lakh crore (Ministry note, 26 June 2026). ZED as on May 2026: more than 93.61 lakh registered, more than 6.68 lakh certified. Lean as on May 2026: more than 65,647 registered, nearly 18,961 certified.
