# Conditions That Can Close an EV Incentive Before the Printed End Date

The risk that has already occurred is a segment closing early because its target was met. The risk that is written into the scheme, and has not been triggered in the texts below, is a sub-component running out of money before 31 March 2028. Around those two rules sit slower risks: sales that never become "determined," cells that stay in pilot, buses that have a letter and not a prototype clearance, and a demand year SIAM has already flagged.

## The scheme can end by the rupee, not only by the date

When the ministry extended PM E-DRIVE on 7 August 2025, [PIB recorded](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2154408) that the total payout was limited to the sanctioned outlay, then ₹10,900 crore. If funds for the scheme or a sub-component were exhausted before 31 March 2028, that piece would close and further claims would not be entertained. [S.O. 4424(E)](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/docs/policy_document/Gazette%20Notification%2010%2008%202026%20on%20enhancement%20of%20Outlay%2011900%20cr.pdf) keeps the sentence and changes the ceiling to ₹11,900 crore.

L5 is the worked example of an early close for a different reason. The target was achieved, and the segment was closed on 26 December 2025. The portal then set 25 April 2026 as the last claim date for L5 vehicles sold or registered on the closure day. A scenario in which "the scheme runs to 2028" is true for segments the gazette leaves open. It is false for L5, and it is conditional for every open segment on money remaining.

The two-wheeler rate is already the lower step: ₹2,500 per kWh and ₹5,000 per vehicle from 1 April 2025 through 31 March 2028, also capped at 15 percent of ex-factory price. The gazette says that per-kWh amount can be reviewed if vehicle costs fall, and would then be notified. A further cut is a power the notification reserves. It is not a cut the notification has already made.

As on 22 July 2026, before the outlay increase, reimbursements were ₹2,281.94 crore. That snapshot cannot be read as headroom against ₹11,900 crore, because the ceiling changed afterward and because a large share of the outlay is buses (₹4,391 crore), chargers (₹2,000 crore), and testing agencies (₹780 crore), not two-wheeler cheques.

## Manufacturing risk is a certificate that has not been issued

PLI Auto pays only after domestic value addition of at least 50 percent. The [January 2026 review](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2214039) shows the filter working: 82 approved applicants, certificates for 94 OEM variants at eight applicants and 37 component variants at ten applicants, determined sales of ₹32,879 crore till 30 September 2025 against a five-year incremental-sales estimate of ₹2,31,500 crore. The risk for an investor is treating approval, or even reported investment of ₹35,657 crore, as a right to the incentive. The disbursed claims — ₹322 crore for FY 2023-24 and ₹1,999.94 crore for FY 2024-25 — are the amounts that cleared.

The cell programme concentrates the same risk in capacity. Forty gigawatt-hours have been awarded against a 50 GWh target. The installed fact the review states is 1 GWh at Ola Cell Technologies, in pilot production since March 2024. Investment of ₹2,878 crore till 30 October 2025 is real capital. It is not 50 GWh of cells.

Buses carry execution risk in public. The July 2026 reply has quantity letters for 13,800 buses and concession agreements in Surat (600) and Hyderabad (915). Deployment still depends on prototype approval. The payment-security mechanism, ₹3,435.33 crore, exists because a public-transport default is a recognised risk; authorities must repay within 90 days. Fifteen states and Union territories had filed the debit mandate by 22 December 2025. A city without that mandate is outside that list.

The July reply also says the government has not assessed the scheme's effect on fuel use or emissions. A scenario that needs a measured emissions outcome cannot take it from that reply. The reply does give penetration: 0.7 percent in FY 2019-20 and 8.2 percent in FY 2025-26.

## Demand risk SIAM is willing to name

SIAM's [FY 2025-26 release](https://www.siam.in/pressrelease-details.aspx?pid=605) expects growth in every category in FY 2026-27 and names the conditions that could interrupt it: the West Asia conflict, crude and commodity prices, higher exchange rates, and shipping-route disruption. Those are SIAM's risks, attached to a year in which second-half domestic sales did the work. They sit outside the gazette. A founder who models only the subsidy and ignores the wholesale year will miss the demand line SIAM actually publishes.
