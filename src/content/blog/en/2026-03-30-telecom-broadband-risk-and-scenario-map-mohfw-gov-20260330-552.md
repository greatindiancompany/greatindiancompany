# Four Ways India's Broadband Figures Get Misread

These are reading risks, not macro scenarios with invented probabilities. Each one is a way a true official figure produces a false decision when the definition next to it is dropped. The remedy in each case is a second figure from the same family of documents.

## A billion broadband lines described as a fibre market

At end-July 2026 TRAI reported 1,094.48 million broadband subscribers. Mobile wireless access was 1,027.50 million. Fixed wired access was 48.14 million. Fixed wireless, in the segment table, was 18.84 million. A strategy that hears "over a billion broadband users" and staffs a fibre-to-the-home business for that base is staffed for a market about twenty times larger than the wired book. The same mistake runs in reverse for a handset business that quotes only the wired line and calls India under-connected. Rural wireline tele-density of 0.57 percent and rural tele-density of 61.05 percent can both be true. They describe different networks.

Machine-to-machine SIMs add a further cut. Headline tele-density of 94.68 percent includes them. TRAI also publishes a broadband total excluding machine-to-machine cellular connections, 1,005.72 million at end-July, beside 1,094.48 million with them. Pick the row that matches the product.

## A service-ready gram panchayat described as a home that is online

PIB's 12 February 2026 reply says nearly 2.14 lakh of 2.56 lakh gram panchayats were online under BharatNet Phases I and II. The same reply says optical fibre with 95 percent uptime had reached 42,000 villages by December 2025, toward a 2030 target of 2.7 lakh. In Tamil Nadu, 10,869 gram panchayats were service-ready and 808 fibre-to-the-home connections had been provided through BharatNet, against a plan of 5,27,506 household connections under the amended programme. A lender, a school programme, or a telemedicine pilot that treats "service-ready" as "the household can hold a video call" is using the first number as if it were the third.

## A TRAI recommendation described as spectrum cash already committed

TRAI's 24 February 2026 press note recommends an auction of available spectrum in nine bands, including 600 MHz, 3300 MHz, and 26 GHz, and it prints recommended reserve prices. DoT asked for that advice. DoT had not, in the documents cited here, issued the notice that would turn the advice into bids. The upper 6 GHz ranges are recommended to stay out of the forthcoming auction. A financing model that books the reserve-price table as a payable in the next quarter is early. A model that ignores the table entirely will be late on the day a notice appears. The coverage-hole idea, a reduction of up to 10 percent of the auction price in exchange for new 4G or 5G sites, is in the same category: designed, and not yet a licence condition.

## A national right-of-way average described as every state's clock

Disposal time fell from 455 days to 30.4 days, and 33 of 36 states and union territories had implemented the Telecommunications (Right of Way) Rules, 2024. Tamil Nadu's average in the February 2026 reply was about 85 days, and the state was named among those still to comply, with West Bengal. A fibre build that prices permission at the national average will miss its calendar in the states the reply itself flags. Anchor-institution connectivity shows the same split from the other direction: Tamil Nadu at 84.74 percent, the national figure at 68.8 percent, both aiming at 90 percent by 2030.

The month-to-month subscriber series that keeps the first misread in check is the [quarterly change note](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/telecom-broadband-what-changed-this-quarter-mohfw-gov-20260330-112).
