The same traveler is a different business across a state border because the runway, the season, the permit, and the buyer change. This page does not rank states and does not quote a survey. It names patterns worth checking before anyone treats India as one route and one hotel.

## Patterns tied to a place

These are institutional patterns, not a league table.

- **Delhi and Mumbai.** Constrained metro airports, international gateways, and slot allocation. Leisure travel and airline banking share the same scarce hour. A hotel plan that needs another evening wave of flights has assumed a slot.
- **Bengaluru and Hyderabad.** Large concession airports whose weekday demand is not only leisure. A weekend-holiday story does not describe a Monday morning bank.
- **Goa.** Beach travel with a domestic season and, in winter, charter and other international traffic when those programmes run. Dabolim and Manohar International at Mopa are different airports. A room in one catchment is not a room in the other.
- **Kerala.** More than one airport has handled international traffic. The product is often a circuit of backwaters, hills, and stays, not a single gateway hotel. A houseboat is a state and local operating problem. It is not a Directorate General of Civil Aviation certificate.
- **Rajasthan.** Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur, and the roads between them. Winter carries the heritage season. Rail still moves part of the circuit. An airport improvement that ignores the road day is a partial product.
- **Himachal, Uttarakhand, and Ladakh.** Short runways, weather, and roads that close. Pilgrimage helicopters are civil-aviation operations with a short window. A summer hill station and a high-altitude airfield do not share a playbook.
- **The Northeast.** Guwahati is the usual hub, many city pairs are thin, and some districts add a permit a metro check-in never asks for. A UDAN award is not the same fact as a flight that continues after the support.
- **The islands.** Andaman and Lakshadweep trips are capped by aircraft seats and ships. The permit and the berth are the product. A mainland occupancy figure does not describe them.
- **Tamil Nadu's temple and medical trips.** Chennai and other gateways serve temple circuits and hospital stays. Those guests do not behave like a beach week. Count them on their own row.

## What the paper changes

Safety paper is national: the Directorate General of Civil Aviation, the Bureau of Civil Aviation Security, and, for major airports, the Airports Economic Regulatory Authority. Tourism paper is not. A state tourism department, a monument authority, a forest department, or the visa rule can stop a guest the airline has already carried. The [policy-shift page](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-policy-shift-explainer-niti-gov-20260330-147) is how to tell an amendment from a speech. The [regulatory stack](/blog/aviation-and-tourism-regulatory-outlook-niti-gov-20260330-427) is the paper after the passenger leaves the terminal.
