# How Founders Should Read Dedicated Freight Corridors, Cargo Terminals, and Metro Cities

A founder shipping cement is not in the same system as a founder selling rides to a metro station. The ministries have already split the map. Use their split.

## If you move bulk, start with the two open corridors

The Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor, Ludhiana to Sonnagar, 1,337 km, and the Western corridor, Dadri to Jawaharlal Nehru Port, 1,506 km, are commissioned. About 443 trains a day were running on them when the railways minister answered the Rajya Sabha on 7 August 2026. The effects he stated are the ones an operator can plan around: double-stack container trains, higher axle-load trains, faster reach from western ports into the northern hinterland, and room for terminals along the corridor. Freight diverted onto the corridors also frees paths on the mixed passenger-and-goods network.

Dankuni to Surat is not that map yet. It was announced in Union Budget 2026, and the detailed project report has been taken up. A plant location that assumes the east-west corridor is open is ahead of the reply.

National loading in 2025-26 was 1,670 million tonnes. The commodities that grew fastest in the 1 April release were fertilisers and pig iron and steel, both above 13 percent, with iron ore and cement also up. If your product is one of those, the railway’s own year says the tonnes are there. Earnings grew more slowly than tonnes, at 1.44 percent against 3.25 percent loading growth. A logistics price built only on volume growth will not match the freight bill the railway reported.

## If you build a terminal, the siting test is published

Gati Shakti terminals are located on industry demand, cargo potential, railway infrastructure, and the logistics potential of the area. On 23 July 2026 the ministry reported 142 commissioned, 310 further locations with in-principle approval, estimated capacity of 224 million tonnes a year, about ₹10,000 crore of private investment, and 146 million tonnes handled in 2025-26. Handled tonnes below estimated capacity is the operating question: a terminal that exists on paper still has to turn wagons. The policy gives 24 months to build after approval. Wagon schemes let industry bring specialised rakes — about 275 special-purpose and 397 general-purpose rakes were already operational in the August reply, plus about 54 automobile rakes.

Parcel founders have a narrower door. The rapid cargo scheme allows aggregators, alongside India Post, to book space. CONCOR is on Delhi–Kolkata and Mumbai–Kolkata and is piloting door-to-door work at Sonik. That is a pilot geography, not a national parcel concession.

## If you serve a city, use the metro map

About 1,095 km of metro and regional rapid transit was operational across 26 cities in the 15 March 2026 PIB note, including 55 km of Delhi–Meerut Namo Bharat. Namo Bharat runs at 160 km/h, with a design speed of 180 km/h, and it is not a Vande Bharat train. Vande Bharat is the mainline product: 162 chair-car services and 2 sleeper services as of 23 July 2026. A consumer product attached to “semi-high-speed rail” has to say which of those it means.

Central metro assistance runs through the Metro Rail Policy: a city transport authority, a mobility plan, and private participation in some form. A founder hoping to operate a metro is inside a special-purpose vehicle with the Centre and the state, not inside Indian Railways.
