# What India's citizen-service notes added by September 2026

The quarter ending in September 2026 did not create a new delivery ministry. It added dated counts for platforms that already carry certificates, mobile services, cash transfers, and assisted counters, and it left one older national figure untouched.

## The 5 August 2026 Lok Sabha reply

On 5 August 2026 the Minister of State for Electronics and Information Technology, Shri Jitin Prasada, submitted a Lok Sabha reply that the Press Information Bureau published the next day ([PIB, 6 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295635)). For a reader who wants one parliamentary snapshot, that reply is the cleanest document in this quarter.

It states that DigiLocker gives citizens access to authentic digital documents issued by original issuers, with over 71.66 crore registered users, more than 936.03 crore documents, and 2,822 onboarded issuers. UMANG, the Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance, is described as a single mobile platform offering 2,575 services for individuals. Direct Benefit Transfer is described as Aadhaar-linked cash benefits from 318 schemes of 56 ministries, with over Rs 52 lakh crore transferred nationwide.

Common Service Centres are described as the assisted channel. The reply says they deliver over 800 services. As of May 2026, 5.01 lakh centres were functional, of which 3.91 lakh were at gram panchayat level. The same reply names a state front door: the Tamil Nadu e-Governance Agency's e-Sevai portal at [tnesevai.tn.gov.in](https://www.tnesevai.tn.gov.in/), for end-to-end online delivery of citizen services without manual intervention.

## A second August note, with a different cut-off

A PIB research note dated 12 August 2026, [Empowering Governance through Digital Infrastructure](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/aug/doc2026812952501.pdf), publishes nearby figures with explicit dates. As of 31 July 2026 it records over 72 crore DigiLocker users and more than 934 crore issued documents. As of 23 July 2026 it records 2,585 UMANG services and over 798 crore UMANG transactions, against 166 services and 4.6 crore transactions in 2017. As of June 2026 it records more than 5.16 lakh functional Common Service Centres. It repeats the DBT framing of 318 schemes across 56 ministries and more than Rs 52 lakh crore.

Those two August documents sit a few days apart and do not match to the last digit. Cite the sentence you opened. Do not blend 71.66 crore with "over 72 crore," or 5.01 lakh centres as of May with more than 5.16 lakh as of June, into a single unofficial total.

## What the quarter did not refresh

The national e-District count in wide circulation is older. A Lok Sabha reply published by PIB on [3 August 2022](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1847837) said the e-District Mission Mode Project had launched 4,671 e-services in 709 districts, covering certificates such as birth, caste, death, income, and local resident, plus pensions and selected departmental services. The August 2026 reply talks about certificates, pensions, and welfare schemes on state portals and mobile applications. It does not replace the 2022 district count.

The National e-Governance Service Delivery Assessment portal for the 2025 cycle was launched on 25 May 2026, which falls in the previous quarter. The [28 May 2026 PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2266244) says the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances will hold consultations, capacity-building sessions, and review meetings during that cycle, and that nodal officers were asked to submit data on the portal. A reader in late September is inside that cycle. The release says the walkthrough covered timelines. It does not print the submission dates.

## How to use this quarter's notes

Treat 5 August 2026 as the parliamentary baseline for DigiLocker, UMANG's service count, DBT scheme coverage, and Common Service Centres. Treat 12 August 2026 as a research-note cut with its own as-of dates, including the only recent UMANG transaction total in this set. Keep 4,671 e-services and 709 districts labelled as 3 August 2022. Keep NeSDA 2025 labelled as an assessment cycle that opened in May, not as a new service catalogue published this quarter.
