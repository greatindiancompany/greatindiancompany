# What procurement, not the MSP list, is signalling

The MSP list covers 22 mandated crops. The procurement book does not. The market signal is in the tonnes actually bought, and those tonnes are still dominated by rice and wheat.

In Rabi Marketing Season 2024-25 the Food Corporation of India procured 266 lakh tonnes of wheat. That was above 262 lakh tonnes the previous season and well above 188 lakh tonnes in RMS 2022-23. More than 22 lakh farmers were paid, and about ₹0.61 lakh crore was credited to their accounts. For the following season, RMS 2026-27, the government has only an estimate so far: about 297 lakh tonnes, with farmers expected to receive about ₹84,263 crore at the announced MSP. An estimate at the start of a season is not a completed procurement print. Do not put 266 and 297 in the same column and call both actuals.

Foodgrains as a whole, measured July to June, rose from 761.40 lakh tonnes in 2014-15 to 1,175 lakh tonnes in 2024-25. The MSP value paid on that book rose from ₹1.06 lakh crore to ₹3.33 lakh crore, and the number of farmers reached in 2024-25 was 1.84 crore, up from 1.63 crore in 2021-22. Over the same July-to-June comparison, the value paid moved from ₹2.25 lakh crore in 2021-22 to ₹3.33 lakh crore in 2024-25.

Paddy, counted on a different multi-year window, was 7,608 lakh tonnes during 2014-15 to 2024-25, against 4,590 lakh tonnes during 2004-05 to 2013-14. Fourteen kharif crops together were 7,871 lakh tonnes against 4,679 lakh tonnes. MSP paid to paddy growers over those later eleven years was ₹14.16 lakh crore, against ₹4.44 lakh crore in the earlier decade. The kharif-crop total was ₹16.35 lakh crore against ₹4.75 lakh crore. Paddy is almost the entire kharif procurement story. The other thirteen crops are the residual.

## Pulses are a policy promise with a thin delivered book

Budget 2025 said 100 percent of state production of tur, urad, and masoor would be procured for four years, through 2028-29. The PM-AASHA guarantee for pulse procurement at MSP was raised from ₹45,000 crore to ₹60,000 crore. By 25 March 2025, tur actually procured was 2.46 lakh tonnes, from five states (Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Telangana), reaching 1,71,569 farmers.

Over a longer window the Ministry reports pulse procurement of 1.52 lakh tonnes during 2009-14 and 82.98 lakh tonnes during 2020-25. That is a real increase, and it is still a small book next to a single wheat season of 266 lakh tonnes. NAFED and NCCF buy pulses, oilseeds, and copra under the Price Support Scheme when a state requests it and the market price is below MSP. They do not run an open-ended cereal-style operation.

The useful signal for a trader or a founder is therefore crop-specific. A wheat or paddy plan can assume a large public buyer in the surplus states, with volumes that have been printed. A tur, mustard, or nigerseed plan should assume a request-based buyer, a guarantee ceiling, and a delivered quantity that the latest state-wise note still measures in low single-digit lakh tonnes for tur. The MSP on the crop can be high, as wheat’s 109 percent margin shows, while the tonnes bought stay a function of arrival, state participation, and the agency’s instruction.
