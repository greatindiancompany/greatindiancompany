A mid-term trajectory for India's cybersecurity resilience is a set of questions you can answer later with a document. It is not a path this page has chosen. Nothing below is a forecast.

## Questions that have a record

Ask these over several years. Write the record that would settle each one, and the date you will look.

- **Connection gate.** Will new telecom equipment still have to clear a trusted-source check and a security test before it is connected? The record is the licence condition or the rule on the Department of Telecommunications site, [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/), plus the certificate or determination for the product you care about.
- **Incident clock.** Will the entities you operate still have a timed reporting duty, and will that duty still sit with CERT-In rather than inside a telecom licence? The record is the direction that names the entity, dated.
- **Critical systems.** Will protection of critical information infrastructure remain a separate office from the telecom licence file? The record is the notification that designates a protected system, not a speech about infrastructure.
- **Sector split.** Will banks, market infrastructure, and insurers still follow their own regulators' cyber directions? The record is the circular that applies to that licence. A telecom rule does not answer it.
- **Data protection beside security.** Will personal-data duties under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, stay a different compliance file from telecom cyber security rules? The record is the provision you are relying on. This page does not decide the status of rules under that Act.
- **Use, not purchase.** Will detection platforms bought in earlier years show a log retrieved during an incident? The record is the incident file, not the invoice.

L.E.K. insights were paired with an earlier draft. An insights index cannot settle these questions. It is omitted.

## How to keep a trajectory

One question, one record, one review date. If the record is unchanged, the trajectory for that question is "no new instrument seen." That is an answer. Do not replace it with a scenario you prefer.

The shelves to put on the review calendar are the [regulatory outlook](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-regulatory-outlook-dot-gov-20260330-431). Forks you can define without a probability are on the [scenario map](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-risk-and-scenario-map-dot-gov-20260330-551).
