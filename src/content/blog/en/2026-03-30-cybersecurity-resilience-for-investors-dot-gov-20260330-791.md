Investors looking at cybersecurity resilience in India should ask which asset the company cannot connect, report, or audit. A sector story is not diligence. This page is a question list. It has no price target, no multiple, and no recommended stock.

## Questions that have a document

Ask for the document before you accept the answer.

- **Connection.** Which elements in the build plan still lack a trusted-product determination or a security certificate? The rules live on the Department of Telecommunications shelf, [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/). The company's answer is a list of products, not a paragraph about national policy.
- **Vendor concentration.** What share of the planned network cannot be connected if one vendor loses trusted-source status? Ask for the second source. This page does not assert that any vendor has lost status.
- **The clock.** Who files incident reports, which direction sets the clock, and how many of the last reports were late? Accept a log. Do not accept "we comply."
- **Open findings.** What did the last licence security audit leave open, who owns each finding, and what is the next date? A certificate of completion for the audit engagement is not the finding list.
- **Licence dependence.** Which revenue sits on a telecom licence, a banking licence, or another licence that carries a cyber direction? A product company with no such licence is a different file from a licensee.
- **Spend versus use.** What did the security operations centre or the log platform do on the last incident? An invoice shows spend. The incident file shows resilience. The distinction is the [capex page](/blog/cybersecurity-resilience-capex-cycle-watch-dot-gov-20260330-351).

McKinsey's India insights index was paired with an earlier draft. It is not a data room. The link is omitted.

## What to do with a blank answer

Write "not shown" and the date you asked. A blank on connection status or on the incident log is a diligence result. It is not a reason to average in a country index.
