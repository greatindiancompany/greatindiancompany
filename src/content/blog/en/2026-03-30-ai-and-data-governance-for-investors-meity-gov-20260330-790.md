An investor reading India AI and data governance is underwriting instruments, not a theme. Four of them are solid enough to put in a memo. Several numbers that circulate with them are not.

## What will stand up in a memo

The IndiaAI Mission was approved on 7 March 2024 with an outlay of Rs. 10,371.92 crore over five years. That is the Lok Sabha reply of 5 August 2026. It is a government spending envelope. It is not the revenue of the companies that use the compute, and it is not a fund an outside investor can subscribe. Other notes round it to ₹10,371 crore or "about ₹10,372 crore." Use the paisa figure, and treat the others as rounding. On 6 March 2025 the MeitY Secretary said nearly 45 percent of the funding sat in compute. That is a statement of that day. The August 2026 reply does not republish a pillar split, so do not invent one.

The DPDP Act, No. 22 of 2023, received assent on 11 August 2023. G.S.R. 843(E) of 13 November 2025 is the clock. Consent Manager registration is 13 November 2026. The general duties of a Data Fiduciary, the cross-border section, the Significant Data Fiduciary power, and the penalty chapters are 13 May 2027. Section 43A of the IT Act is omitted on that same May date, not before. A deal model that books DPDP fines, or that drops the older IT Act compensation section, in calendar 2026 is early.

Payment-system data is already a condition of operating. The Reserve Bank's circular of 6 April 2018 requires storage in India, with a narrow foreign-leg exception and a FAQ rule that data processed abroad comes back within one business day or 24 hours, whichever is earlier. If the company is a payment system provider, this is diligence for today. It is not a general localisation statute for every portfolio company. Section 16(2) of the DPDP Act, once section 16 is in force, keeps that stricter payments rule alive.

CERT-In's directions of 28 April 2022 are also current diligence: six hours to report a listed incident, including incidents that affect AI and machine-learning systems, and 180 days of logs inside India, for the entities the directions name.

## What will not stand up

Empanelled GPUs are access, not a line that says the state owns the chips. The prints move: 10,000 at the March 2025 launch, with 8,693 more then to be added; 38,231 from 14 providers as on 25 March 2026; 45,000+ as of June 2026. Do not add them. A 3,000-GPU sovereign cluster is described in the November 2025 guidelines as being constructed. It is not part of the shared pool. Hours consumed, 93.18 lakh by 237 projects as of August 2026, are usage of a subsidy. The [capex note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-capex-cycle-watch-meity-gov-20260330-350) keeps the sequence.

PIB's note of 13 March 2026 says the India AI Impact Summit saw investment commitments of around $250 billion. The note says commitments. It does not list contracted closings. Adding that sentence to Rs. 10,371.92 crore mixes a press figure with a Cabinet outlay.

The Schedule ceiling of ₹250 crore for a security failure is a maximum, and the sections that let the Board impose it start on 13 May 2027. No company in this set of notes has been notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary, because section 10 is on that same date. The [risk note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-risk-and-scenario-map-meity-gov-20260330-550) is the one-page version.

The India AI Governance Guidelines of 5 November 2025, and the Reserve Bank's FREE-AI report of 13 August 2025, are not licence conditions. SEBI's 2019 circulars already require certain regulated persons to report AI and machine-learning systems. A November 2024 proposal to make those persons solely responsible for third-party AI output was, in SEBI's December 2024 memorandum, still to be notified. Do not underwrite it as a regulation, and do not ignore a notification that appears after this reading. The [regulatory note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-regulatory-outlook-meity-gov-20260330-430) has the stack.

A Consent Manager is a narrow future business: an Indian company, net worth not less than ₹2 crore, registration from 13 November 2026, and only once a Board exists that can register it. This series has not cited the order that names the Chairperson.

## How to read a pitch against this

If the pitch says "India has mandated data localisation," ask which instrument: payments, logs, or a future and conditional personal-data power. If it says "the AI market is the mission outlay," it has confused a budget with a customer. If it says "DPDP is in force," ask which section, and open G.S.R. 843(E). The [ledger](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-evidence-snapshot-meity-gov-20260330-670) is the table. The [investment note](/blog/ai-and-data-governance-investment-implications-meity-gov-20260330-230) is the longer version of the money distinction.
