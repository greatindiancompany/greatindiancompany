A policy shift in India's cybersecurity resilience is a change in the duty, the office, or the instrument people are expected to follow. A speech can point toward one. It becomes a shift when a public document says the rule or the process has changed. This page does not declare that a named instrument changed on a named date.

## Sort the page before you use the word

The Department of Telecommunications site, [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/), is the shelf for the telecom slice. Read each page into one bin:

- **Instrument.** It sets or amends a requirement, a standard, a licence condition, or a procedure. On this shelf that includes telecom cyber security rules made under the Telecommunications Act, 2023, security conditions in a telecom licence, Indian Telecom Security Assurance Requirements, mandatory testing and certification of telecommunication equipment, and trusted-source determinations under the National Security Directive on the Telecommunication Sector.
- **Procedure note.** It explains how to file on a portal, including the Trusted Telecom Portal or a security-certification portal. The note is not the duty unless it cites the instrument that creates the duty.
- **Program description.** It describes a centre, a lab, or a capacity effort, such as the National Centre for Communication Security.
- **Announcement.** It reports an event, a speech, or a meeting.

Call it a shift only from the instrument bin, and only when you can name the earlier text it changes. A first-time rule is a new duty. Label it as new. Do not label it a shift.

## Four lines, or it is not a shift

1. Title and the date printed on the new page.
2. Title of the page or licence text it updates, if it cites one.
3. The duty that is different, in a sentence you can trace to the page.
4. Who the page says must act.

L.E.K. insights were paired with an earlier draft. An insights index is commentary. It is omitted here. It cannot fill the four lines.

CERT-In directions, critical-information-infrastructure duties, and the cyber directions of the Reserve Bank, SEBI, or IRDAI sit on other shelves. A Department of Telecommunications page does not move those duties.
