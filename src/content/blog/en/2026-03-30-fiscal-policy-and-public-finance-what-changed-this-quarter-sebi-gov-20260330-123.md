The first quarter of 2026-27 is on the Controller General of Accounts (CGA) page as unaudited provisional figures through June 2026: [Union Government Accounts at a Glance](https://cga.nic.in/writereaddata/MonthAccount/62026/DATA2627.htm). That print is the right place to see whether the Union’s cash position is tracking the Budget estimates. It is the wrong place to declare that the year is already won or lost.

CGA states the reason on the same page. The fiscal deficit shown during a financial year is not necessarily the deficit for the year. Receipts and spending do not arrive on the same calendar. A quiet revenue deficit in June can widen later when subsidies, transfers, or capital bills bunch. A heavy early deficit can shrink if tax inflows catch up.

## What June actually printed

Figures below are rupees crore, Budget estimates for 2026-27 against actuals up to June 2026. The bracket is the same ratio in the corresponding period of the previous year.

| Head | Budget estimate | Actuals to June | Share of estimate (previous year) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Revenue receipts | 35,33,150 | 10,14,240 | 28.7% (26.7%) |
| Tax revenue, net to Centre | 28,66,922 | 6,36,576 | 22.2% (19.0%) |
| Non-tax revenue | 6,66,228 | 3,77,664 | 56.7% (64.0%) |
| Non-debt capital receipts | 1,18,397 | 35,003 | 29.6% (36.9%) |
| Revenue expenditure | 41,25,494 | 10,16,818 | 24.6% (24.0%) |
| Interest payments | 14,03,972 | 3,46,414 | 24.7% (30.2%) |
| Capital expenditure | 12,21,821 | 3,40,258 | 27.8% (24.5%) |
| Total expenditure | 53,47,315 | 13,57,076 | 25.4% (24.1%) |
| Fiscal deficit | 16,95,768 | 3,07,833 | 18.2% (17.9%) |
| Revenue deficit | 5,92,344 | 2,578 | 0.4% (6.4%) |
| Primary deficit | 2,91,796 | −38,581 | −13.2% (−36.0%) |

A quarter of the year had passed. Total spending was 25.4 percent of the estimate, a little ahead of the previous year’s pace. Capital expenditure was further ahead, at 27.8 percent against 24.5 percent a year earlier. Net tax revenue was also ahead of last year’s pace, at 22.2 percent of the estimate against 19.0 percent. Non-tax revenue was large in rupees but slower than last year as a share of the estimate.

The revenue deficit was only ₹2,578 crore, 0.4 percent of the full-year estimate, against 6.4 percent at the same point a year earlier. The primary balance was a surplus of ₹38,581 crore at this date. Neither number is a forecast. Both are timing statements.

## How this sits next to the Budget

The Medium-term Fiscal Policy Statement laid with the Union Budget 2026-27 puts the same year’s fiscal deficit at ₹16.96 lakh crore, or 4.3 percent of GDP, and the revenue deficit at 1.5 percent of GDP. The CGA crore figures for those estimates match that statement (₹16,95,768 crore and ₹5,92,344 crore). June has used 18.2 percent of the fiscal-deficit estimate, close to last year’s 17.9 percent.

Interest has used less of its estimate than it had by June a year earlier (24.7 percent against 30.2 percent). That is a cash fact about the first quarter. The Budget still estimates interest payments for the full year at ₹14.04 lakh crore. Do not treat the June interest ratio as a cut in the annual coupon bill.

Major subsidies through June were ₹1,14,812 crore, 28 percent of the ₹4,10,512.51 crore estimate, against 22 percent a year earlier. Urea was the fast item: ₹53,034 crore, 45 percent of its estimate. Food and nutrient-based fertiliser were each near 22 percent. Petroleum subsidy was only ₹282 crore. Subsidy timing often moves with procurement and import prices, so this row will keep moving.

## What a reader should not conclude

Net tax at 22.2 percent of the estimate after three months is not “behind.” Direct taxes are seasonal, and the Budget’s own gross tax estimate is a full-year number. Non-debt capital receipts at ₹35,003 crore include ₹24,941 crore of “other receipts.” The fiscal statement says those miscellaneous capital receipts depend on market conditions. A strong June does not lock in the ₹80,000 crore estimate.

Financing of the ₹3,07,833 crore deficit was mostly domestic. Market borrowings were ₹2,39,058 crore. Securities against small savings were a net negative in the quarter. That mix can change when the small-savings and cash-balance lines reverse.

Later monthly accounts, when CGA publishes them, replace this quarter. The June page is the baseline, not the last word.
