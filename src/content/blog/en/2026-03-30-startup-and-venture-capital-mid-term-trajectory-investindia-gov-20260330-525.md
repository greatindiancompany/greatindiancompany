The medium-term story is a decade of administrative scale, then a rewrite of the definition, then a second pool of fund commitments. It is not a smooth line of venture dollars. The official points are dated, and the gaps between them are part of the record.

## How the registry was built

PIB states that Startup India was launched on 16 January 2016 with an action plan of schemes and incentives. The Rajya Sabha annexure of 13 February 2026 counts recognitions by calendar year through 31 December 2025:

| Year | Entities recognised |
| --- | ---: |
| 2016 | 502 |
| 2017 | 5,473 |
| 2018 | 8,980 |
| 2019 | 11,885 |
| 2020 | 14,852 |
| 2021 | 20,282 |
| 2022 | 26,596 |
| 2023 | 34,842 |
| 2024 | 34,294 |
| 2025 | 49,429 |

The column sums to 2,07,135, matching the reply’s stock as on 31 December 2025. The only year in this table in which recognitions fell is 2024, down from 34,842 to 34,294. The rebound in 2025, to 49,429, is the largest single calendar year in the annexure. Financial year 2025-26, which overlaps that calendar year and the first quarter of 2026, is larger still: PIB reports more than 55,200 recognitions, a 51.6 percent rise on 36,400+ in 2024-25, and calls it the highest financial year since the launch.

After the year-end, the stock kept moving: crossed 2.23 lakh as on 31 March 2026, then 2,40,092 as on 30 June 2026, of which 16,334 were recognised in April–June. Part of the 2026 flow sits under a wider definition. G.S.R. 108(E) of 4 February 2026 raised the turnover ceiling from ₹100 crore to ₹200 crore, added a 20-year, ₹300 crore deep-tech category, and admitted cooperatives. DPIIT has not split post-February recognitions into “new firms” and “newly eligible older firms”. A ten-year chart that ignores the break in the rule will over-read the slope.

## Capital followed a different slope

The first Fund of Funds, ₹10,000 crore in corpus and operated by SIDBI, spent the decade committing to SEBI-registered funds rather than building the registry one cheque at a time. By 31 December 2025 those funds had invested ₹25,547.98 crore in 1,371 startups. By the end of March 2026 PIB described disbursements above ₹7,000 crore to over 135 funds, and portfolio investment above ₹26,900 crore in over 1,420 startups. The registry added tens of thousands of names a year. The government-backed venture portfolio, after the same decade, is a stock of a little over 1,400 companies in the March 2026 print. These documents do not give a year-by-year count of new portfolio companies, so that stock should not be turned into an annual rate.

Seed and guarantee instruments are younger than the 2016 launch. The seed fund runs from 1 April 2021, with a ₹945 crore corpus that was fully committed by the end of 2025-26 and then extended for completion of approved funding. The credit guarantee runs from 1 April 2023. Its covered stock at the end of 2025-26 was more than 410 loans and over ₹1,250 crore, after the per-borrower cover rose to ₹20 crore. They widen the kind of capital. They do not close the gap between 1,420 fund-backed companies and 2.4 lakh recognised ones.

## The next official horizon is a cycle, not a year

Fund of Funds 2.0 places another ₹10,000 crore into commitments “spread over the 16th and 17th Finance Commission cycles”. The guidelines do not convert those cycles into a quarterly drawdown. They do fix a review rhythm: at least half-yearly by the department, annually in a utilisation report, and every five years by an independent evaluator from the date of operationalisation. A second implementation agency is to be selected beside SIDBI. Until that selection and the first letters of intent are published, the medium-term capital path is a rulebook plus an unfilled agency seat.

A nearer dated outlay sits outside DPIIT. The 15 January 2026 PIB note puts the Atal Innovation Mission outlay at ₹2,750 crore up to March 2028. That money is for innovation infrastructure, including tinkering labs, not for the fund-of-funds corpus.

The clean way to talk about the next several years is therefore three sentences. The recognition stock is already above 2.4 lakh and is measured under a definition that changed in February 2026. The first fund of funds has a disbursement history and a portfolio of a little over 1,400 companies. The second fund of funds is a ₹10,000 crore commitment authority paced to two Finance Commission cycles, with segment multipliers written down and drawdowns not yet the public series.
