# A quarter-ahead watchlist for India's industrial policy

This checklist covers 24 September 2026 through 23 December 2026. Nothing in the official papers used here announces a PLI event on a date inside that window. The work is to notice a new primary document when it appears, and to avoid updating a model with a number that document has not printed.

The baseline, until a later reply replaces it, is the 28 July 2026 answer to [Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 1423](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU1423_Rd4xWf.pdf): position as on 31 March 2026.

## Check these before you change a model

1. **Look for a post-March cumulative.** Search PIB and the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha sites for a Commerce and Industry reply that states PLI investment, sales, exports, employment, and disbursement after 31 March 2026. If you cannot find one, keep the March stock and write the date on the slide. Do not inflate ₹2.40 lakh crore of investment or ₹35,354 crore of disbursement by a quarter of growth you were not given.

2. **Open the ministry that pays your sector.** DPIIT coordinates. The annexure in [PIB PRID 2287008](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287008) names the implementing department: MeitY for large-scale electronics and IT hardware, Pharmaceuticals for drugs, bulk drugs, and medical devices, Telecom, Food Processing, Civil Aviation for drones, DPIIT for white goods, Heavy Industries for automobiles and ACC batteries, Textiles, Steel, and MNRE for solar modules. A guideline change will come from that ministry. A DPIIT homepage update may not mention it.

3. **Treat corridor approval as a start, not a plot.** The 30 August 2024 note, [PIB PRID 2050136](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2050136), approves nodes and estimates ₹28,602 crore. Before a site visit is called a decision, check the India Industrial Land Bank at [indiaindustriallandbank.gov.in](https://indiaindustriallandbank.gov.in/) and the node SPV. The press note's named list is shorter than its "twelve areas" headline. Do not invent the missing project.

4. **If you sell to the Centre, re-read local content.** The Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order, 2017, as described in the [DPIIT Annual Report 2024-25](https://www.dpiit.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/06/3d9c9c2daeefb97bb9ce964370938b71.pdf), was last amended on 19 July 2024. Class-I is at least 50 percent local content. Class-II is at least 20 percent and under 50 percent. Global tenders under Rs. 200 crore need approval. Confirm the line item you bid, including whether a PLI deemed Class-II status applies.

5. **If you import or make a DPIIT-notified product, find the order, not the count.** The annual report's 83 quality control orders are a stock at the time of that report. A product is covered only if its own order says so.

6. **If the investor is from a land-border country, read the 2026 paragraph before the term sheet.** The 28 July reply describes a 60-day process for specified manufacturing activities and a rule that majority control stays with resident Indians. Press Note 3 of 2026, dated 23 July 2026, is a separate point: inventory-based e-commerce restrictions do not apply to exports of goods made in India.

7. **Keep three numbers in three cells.** PLI actual investment, over ₹2.40 lakh crore as on 31 March 2026. PLI incentives disbursed, ₹35,354 crore as on that date. Manufacturing FDI, USD 19.04 billion in FY 2024–25. They do not add.

8. **Do not brief a hiring plan off 14.15 lakh.** The July reply splits direct employment at 8.4 lakh. The annexure column sums to 8,49,069. A December 2025 reply said more than 14.39 lakh. The [jobs note](/blog/manufacturing-industrial-policy-jobs-and-skills-impact-mof-gov-20260330-381) sets out why those sentences differ.

9. **Write down the document you used.** A figure without the reply date will be wrong the next time Parliament updates the stock. The [evidence snapshot](/blog/manufacturing-industrial-policy-evidence-snapshot-mof-gov-20260330-661) is the table to attach until that update exists.
