# The payments evidence to open before you write a board note

A board note on Indian financial digitalization should carry dates on every figure and a link that a director can open. The figures below are the ones this note is willing to repeat, because they appear in a Press Information Bureau document with a posting date. They will go stale. The monthly table will not.

## The two readings that can be cited together

From the factsheet [UPI: Transforming India's Payment Landscape](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=150962&lang=1), posted on 24 August 2026:

- July 2026: 741 banks live on UPI, 2,365.8 crore transactions, value ₹29.87 lakh crore.
- Annual volume from 2 crore transactions in FY 2016-17 to over 24,162 crore in FY 2025-26.
- Annual value from ₹0.07 lakh crore to about ₹314 lakh crore across the same years.
- About 70 per cent of India's digital payment transactions in FY 2023-24, as that factsheet states it.
- About 49 per cent of global real-time payment volume as of 2024, which the factsheet attributes to an IMF paper published in June 2025. That is a 2024 real-time share, not a 2026 share of every payment instrument.
- A pilot in April 2016 with 21 member banks, a public rollout in August 2016, and BHIM in December 2016.
- UPI live in 11 foreign countries for acceptance or cross-border remittances, with the factsheet naming 2026 steps for Cambodia, Greece, and the Maldives.

From the PIB note [Faster, simpler and more inclusive transactions for every Indian](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?NoteId=158149&ModuleId=2&lang=1), posted on 11 April 2026:

- January 2026: 21.70 billion transactions and ₹28.33 lakh crore.
- 81 per cent of retail digital transactions in India, as that note states it.
- Banks on the network from 216 in 2021 to 691 by January 2026.

July's 741 banks and January's 691 banks are successive readings, not a contradiction. For any later month, use NPCI's [UPI product statistics](https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics). Read the footnote on same-account transfers before comparing a long history. This note does not reprint an August or September 2026 row, because that table is rendered by NPCI and should be quoted from the live page.

## What those numbers are not evidence of

They are not a credit book. Digital lending quality, default loss guarantees, and app inventories are in the [Digital Lending Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848), which do not publish a national non-performing-asset rate for "fintech credit" in the sections that define the controls. They are not a state ranking. They are not Digital Rupee circulation. The [concept note](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationReportDetails.aspx?ID=1218) and the 2022 pilot releases define e₹. They are the wrong documents for a volume horse-race with UPI.

They are also not evidence that a particular company processed the transactions. The system total includes every participating bank and app. MeitY's [Digital India page](https://www.digitalindia.gov.in/initiative/unified-payment-interface-upi/) explains the shared design. A firm-level claim needs the firm's own disclosure.

## A minimum pack for the appendix

A serious appendix has four links and no private blog. NPCI for the latest month. The August 2026 PIB factsheet for the long arc and the July reading. The April 2026 PIB note if the January reading or the 81 per cent retail share is in the text. The Reserve Bank direction that matches the activity being discussed, so a payments total is not asked to prove a lending conclusion.

Great Indian Company exists so that pack has a stable home. The index is [greatindiancompany.com/blog](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog). How not to stretch the same figures is the [market-signal brief](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/financial-digitalization-market-signal-brief-jalshakti-dowr-gov-20260330-218).
