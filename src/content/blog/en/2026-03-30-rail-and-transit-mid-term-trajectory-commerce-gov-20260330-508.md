# The Path from Bulk Freight to Dedicated Corridors and Metro Cities

A mid-term path is the series a ministry has already published. It is not a forecast pasted onto that series.

## Freight loading climbed, with one soft year

The 7 August 2026 reply gives Indian Railways freight loading, in million tonnes:

- 2014-15: 1,098
- 2015-16: 1,104
- 2016-17: 1,109
- 2017-18: 1,162
- 2018-19: 1,223
- 2019-20: 1,210
- 2020-21: 1,233
- 2021-22: 1,418
- 2022-23: 1,512
- 2023-24: 1,591
- 2024-25: 1,617
- 2025-26: 1,670

The step-up after 2020-21 is the steep part of the series. 2019-20 is the year the series dipped. The ministry describes the 2025-26 result as making Indian Railways the second-largest freight-carrying railway in the world. The commodity content of the latest year — faster growth in fertiliser, steel, iron ore, and cement — is in the [market-signal brief](/blog/rail-and-transit-market-signal-brief-commerce-gov-20260330-188).

## Corridors changed the network the series runs on

The same reply says the Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor, Ludhiana to Sonnagar, 1,337 km, and the Western corridor, Dadri to Jawaharlal Nehru Port, 1,506 km, are both commissioned. About 443 trains a day operate on them. The ministry’s stated effects are double-stack container movement, higher axle-load trains, faster access from western ports to the northern hinterland, and new terminals along the corridors. Diverting freight off the mixed network has also freed paths for other goods and coaching trains.

The next named step is not commissioned. Union Budget 2026 announced a corridor from Dankuni to Surat. The detailed project report is being prepared. The trajectory on the record is two open corridors plus one study. Extending the tonne series in a straight line through an unbuilt east-west corridor would be a guess.

## Metro and electrification are separate paths

Metro length, in the 15 March 2026 PIB note, went from 248 km in 2014 to about 1,095 km by 2025, and from 5 cities to 26, including 55 km of Delhi–Meerut RRTS. Since 2014, 38 projects covering 1,051 km have been sanctioned at about ₹3.44 lakh crore. That is an urban path under the housing and urban affairs ministry. It is not the freight series.

Electrification, in the 12 August 2026 backgrounder, reached 99.6 percent by July 2026. Kavach version 4.0 was commissioned on 2,633 route km as of July 2026. The passenger product that sits on the electrified network is still small in its sleeper form: 2 Vande Bharat sleeper services, beside 162 chair-car services, as of 23 July 2026.

## What the path does not yet show

These releases do not publish a delivered modal-share target, a commissioned Dankuni–Surat corridor, or a large sleeper fleet. They do show a bulk railway that kept adding tonnes, a freight-only pair of corridors now open, and a metro map that grew from a handful of cities to 26. Risks in the unfinished part of that path are mapped in the [scenario note](/blog/rail-and-transit-risk-and-scenario-map-commerce-gov-20260330-548). The fare and rank comparisons are in the [benchmark](/blog/rail-and-transit-india-vs-global-benchmark-commerce-gov-20260330-268).

Great Indian Company stops the trajectory where the latest ministry number stops.
