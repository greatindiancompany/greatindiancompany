A policy shift in an Indian agri value chain is a change in a rule that changes the mandi, the purchase centre, the stock a trader may hold, or the export document. Until one of those moves, the chain is on the old rule. This page does not declare that a shift has occurred.

## The instruments that actually reach a lot

People use "policy" for several different Indian instruments.

- **State marketing law.** Agricultural marketing is administered under state Agricultural Produce Market Committee Acts. The yard, the commission agent, the market fee, and the freedom to sell outside the notified yard are state facts. A central speech does not amend that Act.
- **Procurement and minimum support price.** The Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices recommends support prices. The decision that reaches a farmer is whether a centre buys paddy or wheat at that price, and when it pays. Pulses and oilseeds can have a support price with thin procurement. Most fruit and vegetables are outside that system.
- **The Essential Commodities Act, 1955.** When a notification imposes a stock limit on a foodstuff, traders and processors must hold less. That is an inventory rule, not a production target.
- **Foreign trade notifications.** The Directorate General of Foreign Trade can move onion, rice, wheat, sugar, or another commodity between free, restricted, and prohibited. Exporters feel it on the next shipment. Domestic mandi sellers feel it when the export outlet disappears.
- **Food and warehouse law.** A Food Safety and Standards Act licence binds a food business. A warehouse registered with the Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority can issue a negotiable warehouse receipt. Those are operating permissions.

The three farm laws passed in 2020, including the attempt to open a trade area outside APMC yards, were repealed in 2021. Trade outside mandis and contract farming follow the state statute now in force. Calling a repealed Act the current rule is a mistake.

## How to test the word "shift"

Name the instrument, the state or the notification number, the commodity, and the counterparty who now refuses the lot or charges a new fee. If you cannot name that counterparty, you are still reading an announcement. The [regulatory stack](/blog/agri-value-chains-regulatory-outlook-morth-nic-20260330-437) lists the layers.
