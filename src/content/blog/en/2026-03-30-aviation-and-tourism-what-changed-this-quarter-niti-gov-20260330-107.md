A quarter changes Indian aviation or tourism when a flight, a charge, a traffic right, or a guest's permission differs from the previous comparable season in a way an operator can show. A speech about connectivity does not do that. This page has not read a Directorate General of Civil Aviation direction, an Airports Economic Regulatory Authority order, a regional-route list, or a visa notification for the quarter, and it reports no shift.

## Where a real change shows up

Hold the comparison to one airport or one circuit, one operator, and the same season a year earlier. A winter week and a monsoon week are not a pair.

- **The schedule.** Departures that operated, with the aircraft and the days, against the same weeks last year. A Directorate General of Civil Aviation filing that was not flown is not capacity. A regional route awarded under UDAN, the Regional Connectivity Scheme, that stops operating is a change. A fresh award that has not boarded a passenger is not.
- **Airport charges.** An Airports Economic Regulatory Authority tariff order for a named major airport changes what an airline pays on the aero side. A concessionaire's note is not that order. Airports the Airports Authority of India still runs do not all sit in the same tariff file.
- **Rights to fly.** A change in an air services arrangement, in who is designated, or in how much international capacity may be sold. A slot at a constrained airport is a separate allocation. One does not imply the other.
- **The guest's permission.** Visas are a home-affairs and immigration matter, not a Ministry of Tourism campaign. A category, a suspension, or a condition this circuit's guests actually meet is a change. The slogan on the poster is not.
- **The rule at the destination.** A permit for a hill, border, island, or wildlife area, or a change in what a monument authority or a state tourism body charges to enter. Last winter's brochure is not the rule.

## What to leave out

A peak that repeats every year is continuity. A terminal inauguration without flights using the new stands has not added seats. A hotel opening in a city whose busy hour is already allocated has not created demand. NITI Aayog and consulting homepages were paired with earlier drafts of this topic by rotation. They are not records of a flight or a guest, so they are not used here.

## How to close the quarter

Write seats actually flown, the charge that changed, the right or the slot, the visa or permit the guest used, and rooms sold in the same weeks last year. If the cell is empty, leave it empty.
