India's monthly payment picture has a home: Statistics, Data Releases, Payment System Indicators, on the Reserve Bank of India website. The edition used here is Payment System Indicators - August 2026. The publisher of that table is the Reserve Bank. NPCI publishes separate product statistics for UPI, the BHIM app and RuPay, and the cheque line in this file is labelled NPCI-managed. A MeitY or DPIIT homepage does not carry these monthly rows.

## Five parts, one unit system

Part I is the system statistics. Volume is in lakh. Value is in ₹ crore. The payment block opens with RTGS, then retail.

Retail credit transfers, in the order printed, are AePS fund transfers, APBS, IMPS, NACH credit, NEFT and UPI. UPI has a sub-line, "of which USSD". USSD transactions are already in the UPI number.

Debit transfers and direct debits follow: BHIM Aadhaar Pay, NACH debit, and NETC linked to a bank account. BHIM Aadhaar Pay is this debit line. It is not the volume of the BHIM app. App volume is NPCI's BHIM product statistics.

Card payments come next, split into credit cards and debit cards, and each of those into PoS and a residual "others" line. Prepaid payment instruments follow, split into wallets and cards. Paper is the CTS cheque line managed by NPCI.

The digital total is labelled (1+2+3+4+5). It adds RTGS, retail credit transfers, debit transfers, cards and prepaid instruments. It leaves paper out. The grand total adds paper back. UPI is already inside retail credit transfers, and so already inside both totals.

## What the card lines are, and what RuPay is

A credit-card figure in this release is all credit-card payments in the domestic table, across schemes. The file does not print a RuPay row. RuPay card usage is an NPCI product series. Treating the RBI credit-card line as a RuPay line overstates one scheme by the amount of every other scheme in the same cell.

The same boundary applies to BHIM. August 2026 BHIM Aadhaar Pay was 26.75 lakh transactions and ₹720 crore. That sentence is true of the debit line. It is silent on the BHIM app.

## The notes that govern comparison

The August 2026 file states six notes that change how a policy reader should use it.

The data are provisional. ECS debit and credit merged into NACH from 31 January 2020. From November 2019, card and prepaid figures may not be comparable with earlier periods, because RBI published finer categories and revised definitions. Only domestic financial transactions are in the domestic blocks; the format includes e-commerce, FASTag, digital bill payments and card-to-card transfers at ATMs, and it excludes failed transactions, chargebacks, reversals and expired cards or wallets. Fraud statistics, in their own part, are those reported in the Central Payments Fraud Information Registry by scheduled commercial banks excluding regional rural banks, non-bank prepaid issuers, and non-bank credit-card issuers. Attempts are excluded. The UPI QR infrastructure line, included from September 2020, counts static codes only.

Part II is modes and channels: mobile-app payments, internet or netbanking, ATM cash, and AePS at micro-ATMs. Those rows describe a channel. They are not an extra system to add to Part I. Part IV is international use of cards and prepaid instruments issued in India. Part V is the fraud series.

## How to cite it

Cite the release name and the column. "UPI in August 2026" means the August 2026 column: 2,45,089.58 lakh transactions and ₹29,82,356 crore. "UPI in FY 2025-26" means the financial-year column: 24,16,168.86 lakh and ₹3,14,23,250 crore. Those are different objects in the same row.
