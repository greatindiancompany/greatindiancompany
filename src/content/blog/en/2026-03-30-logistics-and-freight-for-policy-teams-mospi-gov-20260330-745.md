A state logistics cell is not a miniature Union ministry, and it is not a helpdesk for every delayed truck. The national papers draw a narrower job. Most cells are busy with the parts that look like strategy and late on the parts a shipper can feel.

## The job the 2022 design assigned

The [cabinet note of 21 September 2022](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/policy-introduces-unified-logistics-interface-platform-standardization-monitoring-framework-and-skill-development-for-greater-efficiency-in-logistics-services/) said all states and Union Territories were onboarded onto the national framework, fourteen states had logistics policies, and thirteen had drafts. It also said the Gati Shakti institutions at the Centre and in the states would monitor the logistics policy. The split at the top is the split a cell should copy. The Network Planning Group looks at infrastructure projects for multimodal fit and last-mile gaps. The note said the Empowered Group of Secretaries would set up a Services Improvement Group, on the pattern of the planning group, to watch process, regulation, and digital gaps. The August 2026 reply says a Service Improvement Group has since been institutionalized.

A cell that only chases ribbon-cuttings is doing half the design. A cell that only writes a policy PDF is doing the other half. The shipper meets both: the approach road, and the permission or the document that holds the vehicle after the road is built.

## The scoreboard the Centre now uses in Parliament

On 11 August 2026 the ministry told the Lok Sabha, in the [reply to Starred Question 327](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf), that 29 states and Union Territories have formulated logistics policies and 32 have granted the sector industry status. A cell should know which sentence is true of its own jurisdiction, and should be able to produce the notification. Being inside a national count is not the same as having a text a warehouse developer can read.

LEADS — Logistics Ease Across Different States — is the assessment framework the reply says has been institutionalized. Policy teams should treat the latest named edition as the external mirror, and should read the pillar that matches the complaint in front of them. A celebration of a composite rank that is two editions old is how cells lose the room. This brief does not supply ranks. The edition does.

The [written reply the same day](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2297818) adds the constraint cells forget in budget season. Gati Shakti has no quantified targets and no expenditure requirement of its own. All 36 states and Union Territories are onboarded. States are encouraged to use the platform for district planning. About 1,800 data layers are integrated, and a public query platform opened in September 2025. The cell’s Gati Shakti job is to put truthful layers in, and to use them when a road, a siding, or a warehouse is proposed. It is not to announce a state “Gati Shakti outlay.” The rupees still sit in the state scheme or in the central ministry that owns the asset. National highways remain a [roads-ministry](https://morth.nic.in/en/bharatmala) programme even when they are the most important logistics asset in the district.

## A work programme that fits the papers

Five tasks follow from the text, without a new scheme.

Keep the state logistics policy and the industry-status notification current, and publish the text. If you are outside the 29 or the 32, say so. A draft is not a formulated policy, which is why the 2022 note kept those words apart.

Staff the services side. Grievances the Service Improvement Group can hear will often be state interface problems: a local permit, a city restriction, a warehouse approval. The model guidelines for city logistics plans, which the August reply says have been launched, are the urban brief. National highway kilometres will not solve the last street.

Use district planning on the master plan before a park or a spur is announced. The test is whether the connecting project is sanctioned and under implementation, not whether it has been drawn. On 11 August 2026 the national book showed 256 sanctioned and 198 under implementation. Ask where the district’s link sits.

Point firms to the instruments the Centre already runs: the GST movement document, FASTag, ULIP for data uses, the Logistics Data Bank for export-import containers. A state portal that duplicates them badly is not reform.

Ask for the sectoral plan if the state’s cargo is coal, steel, fertilizers, cement, pharmaceuticals, or food. Those are the chains the August reply says have plans initiated. A cell in a pharma cluster and a cell in a coal belt are not implementing the same logistics policy, even if both PDFs share a cover.

The Centre’s 2030 lines — a globally comparable cost, a top-25 index rank — are not a state KPI you can hit alone. The state KPI the papers support is dull and checkable: a real policy text, an honest industry-status notification, layers that match the ground, and a service desk that can move a grievance. That is a policy job. [Great Indian Company](https://greatindiancompany.com/) writes it up so a cell can tell a strategy off a circular.
