# How RERA and PMAY-U 2.0 Divide India's Housing Rules

India did not replace the real-estate sale law with a housing subsidy, or the subsidy with the sale law. The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, tells a promoter what must be true before a plot or apartment is marketed. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban 2.0 tells an eligible urban family, and the state that sponsors the file, what central assistance can be paid. A policy reader who treats them as one "real estate scheme" will mis-state both.

MoHUA's July 2025 [FAQ on the Act](https://www.mohua.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/542144159b61c980253a694f478d3a5d.pdf) says the document is general guidance, not a final view on an individual case. The Act itself is on [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2158?locale=en).

## What the sale law actually shifted

Parliament passed the Bill in March 2016. The President assented on 25 March 2016. The institutional chapters were notified from 1 May 2016. Sections 3 to 19, which cover registration and the duties of promoters and allottees, commenced on 1 May 2017.

The operating shift is preventive, which is the reason the ministry gives for the law. Consumer-protection suits arrive after a loss. Registration is meant to arrive before the advertisement. Section 3 requires registration with the state Real Estate Regulatory Authority before advertising, marketing, booking, or inviting people to purchase. The authority has 30 days to decide a complete application. If it does not, Section 5 deems the project registered.

The money rule is in Section 4(2)(l)(D). The promoter keeps a separate bank account for every project and deposits seventy percent of the money received from allottees, for construction and land cost. The July 2025 FAQ is explicit that this is a self-maintained account, not an escrow that needs the authority's approval for each withdrawal. Withdrawals follow the percentage of completion and need certificates from an engineer, an architect, and a chartered accountant in practice. Accounts are audited within six months after the financial year, and the audit has to verify that collections for that project were used on that project.

Public development authorities and housing boards are promoters when they sell to the public. A web portal that sells registered projects is a real estate agent and, if it operates in more than one state, must register with each state authority.

## What the mission pays for

[PMAY-U 2.0](https://pmaymis.gov.in/PMAYMIS2_2024/faq.html) is a five-year mission, 2024 to 2029, implemented from 1 September 2024. States participate after signing a memorandum of agreement with MoHUA. A family of husband, wife, and unmarried children, in the EWS, LIG, or MIG band, living in an urban area and owning no pucca house anywhere in India, may seek assistance to construct, purchase, or rent. The official FAQ sets EWS income up to ₹3 lakh, LIG from ₹3 lakh up to ₹6 lakh, and MIG from ₹6 lakh up to ₹9 lakh.

Assistance is one vertical, not a stack. Beneficiary-led construction and affordable housing in partnership carry central assistance of ₹1.50 lakh per unit in plain states, ₹2.25 lakh in the North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and the Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir, Puducherry, and Delhi, and ₹2.50 lakh in other Union Territories. Plain states must add at least ₹1 lakh; the special-category list must add at least ₹0.25 lakh. The interest subsidy is a separate central-sector benefit of up to ₹1.80 lakh on eligible home loans. Someone taking the beneficiary-led grant may still borrow, but the FAQ says that borrower is not eligible for the interest subsidy as well.

Ownership is part of the design. The house is to be in the name of the female head, or in joint name, unless there is no adult woman in the family. A completed house is not to be sold, transferred, or rented out for five years from completion.

## Where the two regimes meet

A PMAY partnership project that is marketed as a sale is still a real estate project if it crosses the Act's threshold. Section 3(2) leaves out projects where the land does not exceed five hundred square metres, or the number of apartments does not exceed eight, counting all phases, plus completed projects that already had a completion certificate, and pure renovation with no new allotment. The central FAQ repeats that "or". State rules and the state authority's practice are what a promoter must follow; the FAQ tells the reader not to treat it as a case ruling.

Rental policy is a third track. The Union Cabinet approved the Model Tenancy Act on 2 June 2021 and MoHUA circulated it on 7 June 2021 for states to enact or adapt. A [Rajya Sabha reply on 25 July 2022](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1844644) named Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, and Assam as having revised tenancy laws on those lines. That reply is not a 2026 adoption map.

The standing rulebook is set out in the [regulatory outlook](https://greatindiancompany.com/blog/real-estate-and-construction-regulatory-outlook-mca-gov-20260330-441). Great Indian Company publishes this split so a policy reader does not brief a subsidy question with a sale-law answer.
