# India's Broadband Market Signal in the July 2026 Book

A market signal is a figure that changes a decision. In India's broadband book the decision-changing figures are the segment growth rates, the concentration of the mobile book, and the revenue per gigabyte sitting under a very large data volume. All three are in TRAI publications. None of them is a forecast.

## Growth is uneven inside a huge base

At end-July 2026 India had 1,094.48 million broadband subscribers. In that month the fixed-wireless segment grew 2.60 percent, fixed wired access grew 0.90 percent, and mobile wireless access grew 0.59 percent. The mobile segment is 1,027.50 million of the total. A slower rate on that base still adds more lines than a faster rate on fibre. Fixed wired access is 48.14 million. Fixed wireless, as TRAI groups it in the segment table, is 18.84 million.

Inside fixed wireless, 5G fixed wireless access is the line to watch. It reached 13.21 million at end-July, and the rural share was 50.31 percent (6.64 million rural, 6.56 million urban). Unlicensed-band radio fixed wireless was 5.14 million and mostly urban (71.07 percent). The product that is spreading outside cities is 5G home broadband. The older unlicensed radio product is still a city product.

## Concentration depends on which market you name

The top five providers of broadband, wired and wireless together, held 98.59 percent at end-July: Jio 535.12 million, Airtel 384.23 million, Vodafone Idea 129.96 million, BSNL 27.28 million, and Atria Convergence Technologies 2.47 million. On fixed wired access alone, the top five held 71.71 percent: Jio 14.69 million, Airtel 11.28 million, BSNL 4.53 million, Atria 2.47 million, and Kerala Vision Broadband 1.55 million. Wireless broadband, fixed plus mobile, is a three-player market plus BSNL. Fibre-to-the-building still has a fifth name a reader has to look up.

Private access providers held 92.75 percent of wireless mobile subscribers. BSNL and MTNL together held 7.25 percent. On the wireline telephone book the public-sector share, BSNL, MTNL, and APSFL, was 16.28 percent.

## Users move, and they consume a lot of data

In July 2026, 15.98 million subscribers submitted mobile-number-portability requests. Zone I (north and west) accounted for 8.89 million and Zone II (south and east) for 7.09 million. The largest counts were Uttar Pradesh (East) at 2.29 million, Uttar Pradesh (West) at 1.55 million, Madhya Pradesh at 1.52 million, and Bihar at 1.47 million. Portability is a competitive signal in the circles where the absolute base is large, which is a different list from the circles where tele-density is high.

For the quarter ended 31 March 2026, TRAI reported monthly wireless ARPU of Rs 196.04, average wireless data use of 26.70 GB per wireless data subscriber per month, and average revenue realisation of Rs 7.51 per GB. Total wireless data usage in the quarter was 77,953 petabytes. Public Wi-Fi hotspots numbered 56,222 and consumed 8,576 terabytes. The hotspot system is real and small next to the mobile pipe.

Machine-to-machine cellular connections were 137.70 million at end-July, with Airtel at 61.33 percent of that book. That is a second market inside the same licence, with a different leader from the consumer broadband ranking.
