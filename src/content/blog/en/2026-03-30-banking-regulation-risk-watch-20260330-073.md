# When the Reserve Bank places a scheduled commercial bank under PCA

Prompt corrective action is the Reserve Bank's published trigger for stepping into a weak scheduled commercial bank before the weakness is only a resolution case. The text that governs those banks is the [circular of 2 November 2021](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12186&Mode=0), effective 1 January 2022. It revised the framework of 13 April 2017.

The circular is addressed to scheduled commercial banks excluding small finance banks, payment banks, and regional rural banks. A breach at one of those excluded banks is not read off this matrix.

## What is watched

The revised framework monitors capital, asset quality, and leverage. The indicators are the capital-to-risk-weighted-assets ratio or the Common Equity Tier 1 ratio, the net non-performing advances ratio, and the Tier 1 leverage ratio. Breach of any risk threshold may result in invocation. Profitability is no longer an entry indicator. It returns later, as one factor in whether the Reserve Bank is comfortable letting a bank out.

A breach of either the capital-to-risk-weighted-assets ratio or Common Equity Tier 1 is enough on the capital line. The indicator is the regulatory minimum plus the applicable capital conservation buffer, and the common-equity pre-specified trigger plus the buffer. From that level:

- Risk threshold 1 is up to 250 basis points below the capital indicator, or up to 162.50 basis points below the common-equity indicator.
- Risk threshold 2 is more than 250 and up to 400 basis points below on capital, or more than 162.50 and up to 312.50 basis points below on common equity.
- Risk threshold 3 is anything further below.

Net non-performing advances are stated as levels, not as gaps under a minimum. Threshold 1 is 6 percent and under 9 percent. Threshold 2 is 9 percent and under 12 percent. Threshold 3 is 12 percent or more. The ratio is net non-performing advances to net advances.

Leverage uses the regulatory minimum Tier 1 leverage ratio. Threshold 1 is up to 50 basis points below that minimum. Threshold 2 is more than 50 and up to 100 basis points below. Threshold 3 is more than 100 basis points below.

The framework applies to banks operating in India, including foreign banks operating through branches or subsidiaries, on these indicators. Placement is generally based on audited annual results and the Reserve Bank's ongoing supervisory assessment. The Reserve Bank may also impose the framework during a year, or move a bank between thresholds, if circumstances warrant. The circular says the framework does not stop the Reserve Bank from any other action it considers fit.

## What the thresholds require

Some actions are mandatory. At threshold 1 the bank is restricted from distributing dividends or remitting profits, and promoters, owners, or the parent of a foreign bank are to bring in capital. Threshold 2 adds a restriction on domestic and/or overseas branch expansion. Threshold 3 adds restrictions on capital expenditure, other than technological upgradation within board-approved limits.

A longer discretionary menu can be used at any threshold: special supervisory monitoring, a board-level review of the business model, changes in management or supersession of the board under the Banking Regulation Act where those sections apply, plans to raise capital, a time-bound plan to reduce the stock of non-performing advances, and restrictions on wholesale funding, new business lines, or staff expansion. The menu includes resolution by amalgamation or reconstruction under section 45 of the Banking Regulation Act. Discretionary does not mean informal. It means the Reserve Bank selects from a published list.

## How a bank leaves

Exit, and withdrawal of restrictions, is considered if no risk threshold is breached in four continuous quarterly financial statements, one of which is the audited annual statement, subject to the Reserve Bank's assessment, and if the Reserve Bank is comfortable, including on whether profitability is sustainable. Four clean quarters are a condition the circular states. They are not an automatic release.

This brief does not list banks currently under the framework. That list changes with supervisory action and is not a ratio a reader can compute from the thresholds alone, because invocation also rests on the Reserve Bank's assessment.
