# The Multi-Year Path of India's Software Exports

A mid-term view of captive hubs is usually drawn as a smooth climb to 2030. The official series do not draw that line. They show three positive latest-year paths, with different shapes, and they stop at the last published year. This brief does not extend them.

## Exports: a high single-digit path, with a changing mix

The Reserve Bank's annual survey, on a basis that excludes overseas commercial presence:

| Year | Software services exports | Growth the Bank reported |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2024-25 | US$ 204.7 billion | 7.3 percent |
| 2025-26 | US$ 221.4 billion | 8.2 percent |

Including services delivered by foreign affiliates of Indian companies, the totals were **US$ 218.6 billion** and then **US$ 239.3 billion**. The Bank put growth of that wider total at **9.5 percent** in 2025-26, after **6.5 percent** in 2024-25.

The mix moved while the total rose. Computer services went from a **67.4 percent** share to **69.3 percent**. IT services went from **US$ 131.3 billion** to **US$ 147.0 billion**. Engineering services went from **US$ 10.8 billion** to **US$ 12.0 billion**. BPO went from **US$ 55.8 billion** to **US$ 56.0 billion**, so its share of the total fell. Software product development went from **US$ 6.8 billion** to **US$ 6.4 billion**. Off-site delivery went from **90.7 percent** to **91.7 percent**. The US share went from **52.9 percent** to **54.1 percent**.

The path that is in the data is steady export growth, a tilt toward IT and engineering services, and a BPO and product-development book that did not share the same pace. It is not a path labeled "captive centres".

## Equity: two soft years, then a jump

DPIIT equity into computer software and hardware, in USD million:

| Year | Equity inflow |
| --- | ---: |
| 2022-23 | 9,394 |
| 2023-24 | 7,973 |
| 2024-25 | 7,814 |
| 2025-26 | 13,946 |

The first three years are from the factsheet updated to March 2025. The 2025-26 figure is from the March 2026 factsheet as placed in the Lok Sabha on 20 July 2026, which also prints 2023-24 and 2024-25 consistently with the older sheet. Cumulative equity from April 2000 to March 2026 was **USD 124,644 million**.

Export growth and equity growth did not move together. Exports rose in both of the last two years. Equity in this sector fell from 2022-23 to 2024-25 and then rose by about **78 percent** in a single year. A narrative that says "the GCC investment cycle and the export cycle are the same cycle" is not what these two tables show. They can be related. They are not the same series.

Gross FDI, a wider Ministry of Finance concept in that Lok Sabha answer, was **USD 94.84 billion** in 2025-26 against **USD 80.61 billion** the year before. Net FDI was **USD 6.95 billion** against **USD 0.96 billion**. The multi-year equity story and the one-year gross-versus-net story should stay in separate sentences.

## Two other paths, both partial

STPI-registered IT/ITeS exports rose from **₹9,43,312.66 crore** in 2023-24 to **₹10,69,270.59 crore** in 2024-25, growth of **13.35 percent**, and STPI marks the latest year tentative. That is a registered-unit path. It is steeper than the RBI's dollar growth for 2024-25, and it covers a different set of firms. It is not a reason to replace the RBI total.

MoSPI's real GVA group "Financial, Real Estate, IT, Professional Services & Ownership of Dwelling" grew **5.5 percent**, then **10.0 percent**, then **10.4 percent** in the 2025-26 provisional estimates, reaching **₹77,09,678 crore** at 2022-23 prices. Finance and dwellings are inside the cell. The acceleration is real for that group. It is not an IT-only CAGR.

## The 2030 sentence that is only a statement

PIB Delhi, on 25 February 2025, reported a projection of about **$105 billion**, around **2,400** centres, and over **2.8 million** people by 2030, in remarks by the Labour Secretary. The note does not publish the method. This brief does not adopt that projection as a base case, and it does not replace it with a different one. A reader who needs 2030 in a model has to name a publisher and a method the publisher has actually disclosed.
