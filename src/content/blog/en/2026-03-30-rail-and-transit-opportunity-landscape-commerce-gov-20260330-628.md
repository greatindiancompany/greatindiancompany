# Where Private Capital Can Still Enter Indian Rail Freight and Metro Supply

The dedicated freight corridors are public works, and they are already commissioned. The openings that remain for a private balance sheet are the ones the Ministry of Railways and the urban affairs ministry have written down.

## Terminals and wagons, on railway rules

The Gati Shakti Multi-Modal Cargo Terminal policy is the clearest opening. By 7 August 2026 it had commissioned 142 terminals, drawn about ₹10,000 crore of private investment, and handled 146 million tonnes in 2025-26, against estimated capacity of 224 million tonnes a year. Siting follows industry demand, cargo potential, railway infrastructure, and the logistics potential of the area. The commercial terms — no departmental charges, no land licence fee for connectivity land, railway maintenance outside the yard, and a freight rebate for terminals above one million tonnes of outward traffic — are the offer. The limit is the 24-month construction window and the fact that in-principle approval is not commissioning. Hundreds of locations were still at approval in the July 2026 account.

Wagon schemes are the second opening. About 275 rakes of special-purpose wagons and 397 rakes of general-purpose wagons were operational under private investment, the August reply said, covering commodities such as cement, oil, steel, fly ash, iron ore, and coal. A separate automobile scheme had about 54 industry-owned rakes. These rakes use the railway. They do not own it.

## Parcels, not a new railway

The Joint Parcel Product–Rapid Cargo Service is aimed at door-to-door parcels, with online booking of space through aggregators in addition to India Post, on a virtual aggregation platform. CONCOR is running that product on Delhi–Kolkata and Mumbai–Kolkata and piloting door-to-door logistics at Sonik goods shed. For a logistics founder, that is a published channel. It is a small one next to 1,670 million tonnes of bulk freight.

## Metro supply, under a domestic-content rule

The 9 August 2025 PIB note on the Metro Rail Policy records domestic procurement of at least 75 percent of metro cars and 25 percent of key equipment and subsystems for the Make in India provisions it describes. Bharat Earth Movers Limited had supplied over 2,000 metro coaches, as of May 2024, to cities including Delhi, Jaipur, Kolkata, Bengaluru, and Mumbai. The Metro Rail Policy also requires some form of private participation before central assistance. That participation can be construction, operations, fare collection, or another unbundled activity. It is a project role inside a publicly owned special-purpose vehicle, not a private metro concession by default.

On the mainline side, manufacture of 120 Vande Bharat trainsets has been taken up at the Marathwada Rail Coach Factory at Latur, set up at ₹685.79 crore, through a technology partner. That is a factory the railway has established. A supplier’s opening is the partner and component work around a defined trainset order, not an export story this release does not tell.

## What is not an opening

The Eastern and Western dedicated freight corridors are commissioned ministry assets. Dankuni–Surat is a detailed project report. Passenger fares are set by the railway, with a disclosed subsidy of ₹60,239 crore in 2024-25. None of those is a bid a founder should write this quarter.

How an operator uses the terminal rules day to day is in the [founder brief](/blog/rail-and-transit-for-founders-and-operators-commerce-gov-20260330-708). The legal tests are in the [regulatory outlook](/blog/rail-and-transit-regulatory-outlook-commerce-gov-20260330-428).

Great Indian Company points at the gate that is open — terminals, wagons, parcels, and metro supply — and names the trunk line that is not for sale.
