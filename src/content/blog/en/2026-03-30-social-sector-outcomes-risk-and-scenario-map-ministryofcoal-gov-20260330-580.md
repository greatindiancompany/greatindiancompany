# A Scenario Map for India’s Social Outcome Risks

These are not probabilities. They are paths already visible in published Indian series, and the consequence if the next release looks like the last one. A risk register that invents a percentage chance is less useful than a register that names the indicator which would confirm or retire the risk.

## If stunting keeps falling and weight does not

NFHS-6 moved stunting in children under five from 35.5% to 29.3%, severe wasting from 7.7% to 5.2%, wasting from 19.3% to 19.0%, and underweight from 32.1% to 31.8%. If the next survey repeats that pattern, a review built only on stunting will report success while about one in five children is still wasted and about one in three is still underweight. The operational risk is a supplementary-nutrition and growth-monitoring system that is scored on the indicator which already moved.

## If infant feeding keeps splitting

Early initiation rose from 41.8% to 50.1%. An adequate diet at 6-23 months rose from 11.0% to 15.3%. Exclusive breastfeeding under six months fell from 63.7% to 55.8%. Children under six months currently breastfeeding stayed at 95.6%. The risk is a communications campaign, including Poshan Maah, that quotes “breastfed” and never checks the exclusive-breastfeeding line. Those are different behaviours, and only one of them deteriorated between the two surveys.

## If large states keep diverging

Bihar’s stunting fell from 42.9% to 35.6% and its wasting from 22.9% to 19.0%. Madhya Pradesh’s stunting fell from 35.7% to 31.4%, while wasting rose from 18.9% to 23.8% and underweight rose from 33.0% to 39.7%. Uttar Pradesh’s stunting fell from 39.7% to 31.5%, while wasting rose from 17.3% to 19.2% and underweight rose from 32.1% to 34.5%. A single national diagnosis of “nutrition is improving” will send the same response to a state where weight worsened. The confirming document is the next state fact sheet, not a new national average alone.

## If anaemia stays unpublished in this series

The 29 May 2026 press note does not give a national anaemia prevalence. The nutrition lines in the May 2026 fact sheets used for the comparisons above do not either. The risk is a strategy or a product claim that treats NFHS-5 anaemia as if NFHS-6 had refreshed it. Until the ministry publishes a new figure in a named survey, the honest line is that this fact-sheet round does not update anaemia.

## If the rural wage transition frays

From 1 July 2026 the guarantee is 125 days under a new Act, the 2005 Act is repealed, and the notified wage is nowhere below ₹300 (national average ₹327.4, from ₹298.8). States may notify up to sixty days of agricultural pause. Unemployment allowance and a 0.05 percent daily delay compensation are written down. The ministry told the Lok Sabha on 11 August 2026 that more than 9.69 crore person-days had been generated in roughly the first month. That early output does not retire the risk. The risk shows up if pauses are notified in a way that crowds the remaining year, if job-card conversion blocks demand, or if wages slip past fifteen days without the compensation the Act states. A second risk is analytical: splicing FY 2025-26 MGNREGS totals onto the new series as if the statute had not changed.

## If schooling gains stay at the facility layer

UDISE+ 2025-26 shows preparatory and secondary dropout down, secondary retention up from 47.2% to 51.9%, and pupil-teacher ratios inside the National Education Policy’s 30:1 reference. The middle dropout series in the same table moves from 3.5% to 3.6%. Single-teacher schools still number 1,00,843. The risk is declaring a learning or completion outcome from a facility census. Secondary retention near half is the level that makes that risk concrete.

## If the central pension rate stays where it was set in 2012

On 21 July 2026 the Ministry of Rural Development said the central NSAP pension assistance was last revised in 2012, that no revision is under consideration, and that state top-ups run from ₹50 to ₹5,700. In several states the amount received averages about ₹1,100. The risk for household welfare, and for any product that assumes pension income, is using one national figure. The ministry has also said evaluations recommended more adequate assistance. The recommendation is not a revised rate.
