# Official releases to read before December 2026

This checklist covers 24 September 2026 through the scheduled second-quarter GDP release. It lists documents already dated by the agencies. It does not forecast what those documents will say.

## 30 September 2026: marketplace audit report

Under paragraph 5.2.15.2.4 of the Consolidated FDI Policy Circular of 2020, an e-commerce marketplace entity with FDI has to obtain and maintain a statutory auditor’s report by 30 September each year, confirming compliance for the preceding financial year. For the year ending 31 March 2026, that date falls inside this window. The report is a compliance artefact, not a demand statistic.

## 5–7 October 2026: the Monetary Policy Committee

The resolution of 5 August 2026 scheduled the next meeting for 5–7 October. That meeting is the first after MoSPI’s 31 August estimate that real GDP grew 7.8% in April–June, against the MPC’s August projection of 7.0% for that quarter and 6.7% for 2026-27. The August resolution also projected CPI inflation at 5.0% for the year, with Q2 at 4.7% and Q3 at 5.9%, and kept the repo rate at 5.25% with a neutral stance.

What to read in the October statement: whether the growth projection moves after the stronger first-quarter print, and whether the inflation path still treats food and fuel as the near-term drivers. What it will not be: a retail same-store series.

## 12 October 2026: September CPI

The August 2026 CPI release, which put provisional combined inflation at 4.82%, says the September index will be released on 12 October 2026 or the next working day. August is still marked provisional. The useful comparison is rural against urban, and food (CFPI) against the all-items index, on the 2024=100 base. MoSPI’s release calendar, not this brief, fixes the date of the October index.

## 30 November 2026: July–September GDP

The Q1 press note schedules quarterly GDP for July–September 2026-27 on 30 November 2026. The line to open first is real PFCE, then its nominal share of GDP, then real GFCF. In April–June those three were 7.1% growth, a 55.6% nominal share, and 11.9% real GFCF growth. The second quarter will show whether that mix held. MoSPI has already warned that indicator-based quarters are revised when source data improve.

Between these dates, the standing references remain the [August MPC resolution](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63287), the [August CPI release](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1789381904344_6c792dcf-8a9f-4fca-93d3-99d833bdb358_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_August_2026.pdf), and the [Q1 GDP press note](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1788172583113_d65a77cf-240e-4491-82ee-59f78618fa41_Press_Note_on_GDP_Estimates_for_Q1_2026-27.pdf).
