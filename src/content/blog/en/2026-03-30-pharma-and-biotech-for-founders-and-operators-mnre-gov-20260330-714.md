# What a Founder Separates Before an API Plant or a Biotech Park

Founders lose months by treating “pharma and biotech policy” as a single application. The operating choice is a sequence of separations. Each one points at a different office, and each office has already published the boundary.

## Separate the product from the campus

If you will manufacture a critical key starting material, drug intermediate, or API on a new site, the relevant production incentive is the bulk-drug PLI. The 6 February 2026 Lok Sabha reply describes it as greenfield and limited to identified products: 41 products in that reply, 48 approved projects, 33 bulk drugs, ₹6,940 crore. Your first artefact is not a pitch deck. It is a check of whether your molecule is on the current eligible list in the Department of Pharmaceuticals guidelines, and whether an invitation on the schemes page is open on the day you look. The March 2020 cabinet list of 53, with 26 fermentation products at a 20 percent rate and 27 chemical-synthesis products at 10 percent, explains the original design. The approval letter and the latest corrigendum, including any dated 2026, decide your case.

If you will make a biopharmaceutical, a complex generic, or an API that is outside that notified list, you are looking at the other PLI, notified on 3 March 2021, outlay ₹15,000 crore, 55 approved applicants in the 2026 reply, greenfield or brownfield. Brownfield means you may be adding a line to a site you already run. The group you fall into follows global manufacturing revenue of pharmaceutical goods in FY 2019–20. MSMEs have a sub-group in the gazette. The 2021 filing window is closed. Ask the schemes page what, if anything, replaced it.

If you do not yet have a product to manufacture, a bulk-drug park plot is probably the wrong first contract. The Department of Biotechnology’s park note and BIRAC’s incubator map are built for incubation, demonstration, and pilot work. BioE3, approved on 24 August 2024, names precision biotherapeutics and bio-based chemicals as policy themes. Those themes help you describe the company. They do not allot a plot in Bharuch.

## Separate the shared utility from your own reactor

A park reduces the set of utilities you build yourself, after the utilities exist. The only central bulk-drug parks in the February 2026 reply are Jambusar in Gujarat, Haroli in Himachal Pradesh, and Nakkapalli in Andhra Pradesh. Write to the named state agency and ask which of effluent treatment, solvent recovery, steam, and power is under contract, and what a tenant may assume. If the reply’s last public word on Himachal steam is that the tender has been floated, your plan needs a temporary utility or a later start. Building outside the parks means you permit and pay for those utilities yourself, and you look at the Department’s common-facility assistance only if its current guidelines cover your cluster.

## Separate the incentive file from the licence file

The Department of Pharmaceuticals does not grant a drug manufacturing licence. CDSCO, under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, states that it handles new drugs, clinical trials, standards, and import quality, and that state drug controllers license manufacturing activity the Act leaves with them. SUGAM is where you file central permissions and where state licence details can be recorded. The New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, govern trials and bioavailability work, including a six-monthly status report. Revised Schedule M, notified on 28 December 2023, is the premises and quality-system standard your licence will be held to.

Put four documents in one folder before you spend on land: the scheme guideline version you are relying on, the product’s approval or eligibility proof, the state or central licence path with the form name, and the Schedule M gap list for the proposed block. If one of the four is missing, the project is still a reading project. The [90-day list](/blog/pharma-and-biotech-next-90-days-checklist-mnre-gov-20260330-474) is that reading project written as a calendar.

Great Indian Company writes these separations so an operator can see which office answers which question: [greatindiancompany.com](https://greatindiancompany.com).
