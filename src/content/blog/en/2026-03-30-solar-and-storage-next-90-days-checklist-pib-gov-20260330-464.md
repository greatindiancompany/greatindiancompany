# What to Verify in Indian Solar and Storage Before Year-End

From 24 September 2026, the next ninety days run to late December. This checklist does not predict a circular that has not been issued. It lists checks against documents that already exist, where a stale copy would change a purchase, a bid, or a grant claim.

## Confirm the module and the cell

MNRE's ALMM page was updated on 16 September 2026. It says the first cell list was issued on 31 July 2025 and is updated regularly, and that only List-I modules may be used in government projects, schemes, open access, and net-metering. A separate MNRE notice carries an updated List-I dated 3 August 2026.

Before ordering modules for a covered project, match the model number to the list file dated most recently, not to a capacity headline. Then apply the cell rule in PIB release 2082901 of 10 December 2024. From 1 June 2026, covered projects must use List-II cells, except where the last bid-submission date fell before that order. In this ninety-day window the effective date is already behind you. The check is documentary: bid date, model, cell, and whether a thin-film integrated module is the compliance path the note allows.

## Do not treat battery grant rates from 2023 as current

PIB release 2118325 of 3 April 2025 says viability-gap support for batteries fell from Rs 96 lakh per MWh to Rs 46 lakh per MWh or 30 percent of capital cost, whichever is lower. Scheme capacity rose from 4,000 MWh to 13,200 MWh inside an allocation of Rs 3,760 crore. No project had reached financial closure in 2024-25, so the first 10 percent was not paid and expenditure that year was nil.

If a term sheet still uses the Rs 96 lakh rate, correct it before a lender or a bid committee sees it. If a project is aiming at the grant, the milestone to document is financial closure, then commercial operation for the 45 percent slice. This checklist cannot say which projects will close by December. The April 2025 reply is the latest status these pages give, and it is a status of non-closure for that financial year.

## Read SECI's book as awarded, commissioned, or still to be built

SECI's what-we-do page, with figures as of 1 May 2025, says more than 73.8 GW had been awarded, including 45.9 GW of solar, and that products include round-the-clock power, firm and dispatchable renewable energy, and utility-scale batteries. Before treating a pipeline slide as revenue, mark each project as awarded or commissioned and name the product. A plain solar award and a dispatchable award are different contracts.

Also separate manufacturing. MNRE's PLI page and SECI's solar page both describe tranche-II factory awards of about 39,600 MW in April 2023. Incentive cash follows commissioning and then five years of manufacture and sale. A ninety-day plan that books PLI as quarterly revenue, without a commissioning notice, is ahead of the scheme text.

## Household rooftop: use the August operating note

PIB release 2294208 of 4 August 2026 is the progress print to keep on the desk through this quarter. It reports subsidy release, vendor ratings, a fifteen-day subsidy path, deemed approval up to 10 kW, and distribution-company charges removed in 32 states and Union Territories. A sales script written from the February 2024 cabinet note alone will miss the loan rate and the vendor-rating system described later. Re-read the August note before printing a customer offer, and confirm the live rate on the national portal. The press note is not the sanction letter.

## What you do not need to invent

You do not need a new national target, a state ranking, or a global share to make a decision this quarter. You need the current ALMM file, the bid date against 10 December 2024, the battery grant rate in the April 2025 reply, and a SECI figure labelled awarded or commissioned.
