## Counts that must stay in their own rows

The February 2025 note’s “400+” new courses, the August 2026 note’s “over 600” PMKVY courses, and the 3 August list of NCVET qualifications (116 in artificial intelligence and machine learning, 54 in automation and robotics, 80 in data analytics, 71 in cybersecurity, 17 in drones, 99 in green skills, plus 50 SOAR artificial-intelligence courses) are different catalogues. They are not added in this table.

No row here is a placement rate, a state rank, or a 2026 education-to-GDP ratio. Those were not in the texts above.
