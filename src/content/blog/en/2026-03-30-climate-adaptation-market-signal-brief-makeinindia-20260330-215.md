# Climate Adaptation Market Signals in India

A market signal, in this brief, is a public rule or programme that creates a buyer, a standard, or a recurring operating need. No page cited here from the environment ministry, the disaster authority, the Press Information Bureau, or the India Meteorological Department publishes a rupee size for an Indian "adaptation market." A slide that leads with one is not using these sources.

## Heat is a seasonal operating standard

IMD declares a heat wave only after a published test. The criteria printed with its heat bulletin, and restated in a 6 April 2021 IMD annexure carried on PIB, require maximum temperature of at least 40°C in the plains, 37°C at coastal stations, or 30°C in the hills. A departure of 4.5°C to 6.4°C is a heat wave, and above 6.4°C is severe. In the plains, 45°C and 47°C are absolute tests. The test must hold at two stations in a meteorological subdivision for two consecutive days, and the declaration comes on the second day.

The National Disaster Management Authority's October 2019 guidelines tell governments to prepare heat action plans. Its heat resources include cool-roof guidance for house owners. The signal is a district that must act on a defined hot day, and a building measure NDMA has bothered to explain. It is not a national procurement calendar.

## Water, farms, and a stated goal

The National Water Mission, in the environment ministry's FAQ of 1 December 2021, is built around a public water database, conservation, attention to over-exploited areas, basin-level management, and a goal of raising water-use efficiency by 20 percent. That 20 percent is the mission's stated goal in the FAQ. It is not a result measured on this page. The National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture is described there as operational from 2014-15, aimed at location-specific farming systems, soil and moisture conservation, soil health, and water management. A PIB note of 5 February 2018 adds the adaptation fund's eligible sectors: agriculture, animal husbandry, water, forestry, and tourism.

## The coast is a named set of tasks

On 3 February 2022, PIB said the ministry had demarcated a hazard line for the entire mainland coast and pointed to the Coastal Regulation Zone Notification, 2019. The same note names three coastal projects under the adaptation fund: integrated farming in the Kaipad wetlands of north Kerala, habitat and livelihood work in the Gulf of Mannar in Tamil Nadu, and dairy resilience in coastal and arid parts of Andhra Pradesh. Those are project titles, not a market forecast.

## What does not belong on this page

The July 2025 figures for solar capacity and the non-fossil share of installed power are mitigation milestones. Quoting them as adaptation demand counts the energy transition twice.
