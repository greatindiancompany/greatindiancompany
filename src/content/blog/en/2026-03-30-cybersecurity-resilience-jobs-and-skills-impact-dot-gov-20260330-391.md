A job in India's cybersecurity resilience is a person who can produce a record a direction already requires. A course certificate on a wall is not that job. This page names the roles by the record they sign. It has no vacancy count, no salary, and no headcount for the country.

## Roles tied to a record

- **The incident filer.** The person who can file the report inside the clock the direction sets. CERT-In's direction and a telecom incident process may both apply. They are two duties. The night-shift name is the resilience fact. The homepage of the Department of Telecommunications, [dot.gov.in](https://www.dot.gov.in/), will not list your night shift.
- **The audit signer.** The person who signs the security audit a telecom licence or a sector regulator asks for, and who owns each open finding. An empanelled firm that delivers a PDF and leaves is a vendor. The owner of the finding is the job inside the entity.
- **The security-test engineer.** The person who can run or interpret an Indian Telecom Security Assurance Requirements test for a product that must clear the security gate before connection. The National Centre for Communication Security is the public side of that gate. A general network engineer is not automatically this role.
- **The trusted-source operator.** The person who files product and vendor detail on the Trusted Telecom Portal and can say whether a named element is allowed on the network. Procurement that cannot answer that question will buy equipment the network cannot connect.
- **The sector-licence owner.** In a bank, a market institution, or an insurer, the person who maps the regulator's cyber direction to systems. That direction is not a Department of Telecommunications page.

L.E.K. insights were on an earlier draft. They are not a skills register. The link is omitted.

## What a skill is

A skill is the ability to do one of those signatures on this entity's systems. Training counts when the same person then signs a real report, a real finding, or a real test. A batch size, a portal of courses, and a national mission statement do not substitute for the name on the roster.
