# The cyber roles and training counts official texts name

Official documents name offices and training programmes. They do not publish a national count of cybersecurity jobs, a vacancy rate for the private sector, or a salary band. The useful reading is which roles the rules make mandatory, and which public training numbers have actually been reported.

## Offices a regulated firm has to staff

**A point of contact for CERT-In.** The MSME baseline of 1 September 2025 tells firms to assign a security in-charge as the single point of contact for CERT-In and regulators. The April 2022 directions, which that baseline tells firms to follow, are the reporting regime behind that contact.

**A bank CISO with a structural separation.** Under the Reserve Bank's IT directions effective 1 April 2024, a senior executive, preferably of general-manager rank or equivalent, is designated Chief Information Security Officer. The person needs a technical background. The office is staffed in line with business volume and technology complexity. The CISO does not report directly to the Head of IT, is not given business targets, and reports to the executive director or equivalent who oversees risk. The role includes driving the cybersecurity strategy, enforcing information-asset policies, coordinating with external agencies, managing the security operations centre, and placing a quarterly review before the board or its risk and IT committees.

**An insurer's committee, not a single badge.** IRDAI's 2023 guidelines seat the Chief Risk Officer, CISO, Chief IT Security Officer, Chief Security Officer, Chief Human Resource Officer, Chief Technology Officer, and the heads of operations, finance, legal and compliance on the Information Security Risk Management Committee. A meeting needs the CISO and at least two members; all members meet at least twice a year. The CISO owns the information and cyber security policy, reports toward the executive overseeing risk or, in that person's absence, the CEO, and is responsible for notifying relevant regulators about incidents. If the CISO is absent, the Chief Risk Officer assumes the CISO's decision role. The guidelines also say the CISO should have a reasonable minimum term. They do not define that term in a number of years.

**A telecom security officer who must be resident.** Every telecommunication entity appoints a Chief Telecommunication Security Officer. The rules require that officer to be a citizen and resident of India, responsible to the board or similar governing body, and to be the coordinator with the Central Government for implementation and for incident reporting. A change of officer is intimated promptly.

**A person the public can actually reach.** PIB's account of the DPDP Rules says every data fiduciary must display contact information of a designated officer or Data Protection Officer so people can raise questions about processing. PIB also says requests to access, correct, update, erase or nominate are answered within a maximum of 90 days. Rule 14, in the eighteen-month commencement group, requires a published grievance system that responds within a reasonable period not exceeding ninety days. Significant data fiduciaries carry additional audit, impact-assessment and technology due-diligence duties once those rules have commenced.

## Training the public record counts

CERT-In's Annual Report 2023 says it organised 26 training programmes that year, for 10,074 participants. The audience it lists includes system and network administrators, database administrators, application developers, IT managers, CISOs, CIOs and IT security professionals, drawn from government, public and critical-sector organisations. The activity table in the same report lists 26 trainings and 22 security drills. The narrative separately describes 14 domestic cyber-crisis exercises. Those are the report's own counters; this brief does not force them into one total.

The MSME controls ask for basic cybersecurity awareness at least twice a year for employees and contractors, covering phishing, passwords, social engineering, device use, and handling of sensitive information, plus participation in CERT-In workshops and drills.

On the law-enforcement side, the 24 March 2026 Lok Sabha reply says the CyTrain portal had, up to 31 January 2026, more than 1,51,081 police and judicial officers registered and more than 1,42,025 certificates issued. Under the CCPWC scheme, more than 24,600 police personnel, judicial officers and prosecutors had been trained. Those figures describe public investigative capacity. They are not a private-sector workforce survey.

A hiring plan can follow the named offices: a reachable contact, a CISO who is not the IT delivery head, a resident telecom security officer where the telecom rules apply, and a training rhythm the MSME baseline already sets at twice a year. A hiring plan that quotes a national talent shortage in a specific number is quoting something these documents do not give.
