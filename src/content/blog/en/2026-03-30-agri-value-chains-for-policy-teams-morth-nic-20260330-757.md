# Which ministry holds which lever

A policy team that treats "agri value chain" as one programme will brief the wrong minister. The crop estimate, the support price, the cash transfer, the farmer company, the mandi platform, and the infrastructure loan sit with the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. The processing grant and the production-linked incentive sit with the Ministry of Food Processing Industries. Scheduled-product export promotion sits with APEDA, a statutory body under the Ministry of Commerce. Credit guarantee for part of the loan book is named as NABSanrakshan, alongside CGTMSE. The mandi underneath the national screen is still a state market committee.

## What Agriculture holds

The Directorate's production print is released by the agriculture minister. The Third Advance Estimates of 27 May 2026 put 2025-26 foodgrain at 376.563 million tonnes. The sowing print is also Agriculture's: kharif area of 1,086.31 lakh hectares as on 4 September 2026. The support price is a Cabinet decision on the ministry's recommendation. Common paddy for marketing season 2026-27 is Rs 2,441 a quintal, set on 13 May 2026, on the rule of at least one and a half times cost. Procurement history in that release, 8,418 lakh metric tonnes of paddy over 2014-15 to 2025-26, is the buying record, which also involves the food ministry's agencies. The price lever and the purchase lever are related and are not the same file.

PM-KISAN is Agriculture's transfer. The Cabinet on 31 July 2026 continued it to 2030-31 with an outlay of Rs 3.15 lakh crore. The stock then was more than Rs 4.47 lakh crore through 23 instalments. A Lok Sabha reply on 10 March 2026 added the identity rule a policy team will be asked about: Farmer ID is mandatory for new registrations in the 19 states where the farmer registry has started, and is not required where that work has not begun. Farmers without a phone can use a common service centre or a block office.

The 10,000-FPO scheme is Agriculture's, outlay Rs 6,865 crore. Registration was complete as on 1 March 2026. Equity grant and guarantee, as on 31 December 2025, had reached 6,557 and 2,671 organisations. e-NAM is Agriculture's platform on top of state mandis: 1,656 mandis, cumulative trade of Rs 4,82,350 crore through February 2026, integration grant up to Rs 75 lakh a mandi. The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund is Agriculture's loan window: 3 per cent subvention, 9 per cent cap, guarantee up to Rs 2 crore through CGTMSE and NABSanrakshan, scheme described as running to 2032-33, with new disbursement described in August 2025 as ending in 2025-26.

## What Food Processing holds

The processing ministry's umbrella, Pradhan Mantri Kisan Sampada Yojana, had an outlay of Rs 6,520 crore up to 2025-26 and 1,607 approved projects by 31 December 2025. Its cold-chain component, as of 30 June 2026, had 409 projects approved, 311 complete, Rs 3,089.08 crore of grant sanctioned and Rs 2,540.89 crore released, and 9.236 lakh metric tonnes of cold storage created. The rate card restated on 6 August 2026 is 35 per cent or 50 per cent, with caps of Rs 10 crore or Rs 15 crore.

The production-linked incentive is the same ministry and a different instrument. Outlay Rs 10,900 crore for 2021-22 to 2026-27. As on 31 December 2025: 168 applicants, about Rs 9,207 crore invested by them, around 35 lakh metric tonnes a year of capacity, Rs 2,714.79 crore of incentive disbursed. The incentive requires the full manufacturing chain, including primary processing, to be in India.

## The three cold-chain numbers a brief must not add

A coordination failure looks like a single "cold storage created" sentence. Three official numbers are in circulation, and they measure different things.

The Department of Agriculture's stock of cold storage, cited in a food-processing parliamentary answer, was 402.87 lakh metric tonnes as on 31 January 2026. That is the national installed base.

The food-processing ministry's own completed projects had created 9.236 lakh metric tonnes by 30 June 2026. That is a flow of grant-aided capacity, a small slice of the stock.

The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund, as of 30 June 2025, had sanctioned 2,454 cold-store projects for Rs 8,258 crore, inside a book that also contained 30,202 custom hiring centres. That is a loan count, not a tonne count, and it is a year older than the grant completion figure.

Agricultural Marketing Infrastructure, a storage subsidy under Agriculture, had 982.94 lakh metric tonnes of sanctioned capacity as of 30 June 2025. Those are godowns. Adding them to cold-store tonnes produces a storage number nobody published.

## What Commerce holds, through APEDA

APEDA reports agri exports of USD 51,913 million in 2024-25, and its summary table puts scheduled products at 55 per cent of that. Bharat Krishi Niryat Yojana, described in APEDA's tender of 1 September 2026, is the 2026-27 to 2030-31 instrument, with heads for export infrastructure, quality, and market development. A state government and an FPO are named stakeholders. The export lever is not the MSP lever, even when both touch the same crop.
