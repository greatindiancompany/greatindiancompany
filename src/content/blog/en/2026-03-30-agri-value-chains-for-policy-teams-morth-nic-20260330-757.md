Policy teams meet Indian agri value chains through state marketing law, procurement, food-processing assets, and trade notifications. The chain answers a narrower question than a scheme document: did the rule or the asset show up at this mandi, this centre, or this plant? This page is not an evaluation of any programme.

## Checks that stay at the lot

Pick a commodity, a market or a plant, and a recent season.

- **The yard.** What fee was charged, who was licensed, and was a sale outside the notified area lawful under that state's APMC Act? A central reform narrative that the yard fee contradicts is a narrative. The [state page](/blog/agri-value-chains-state-wise-patterns-morth-nic-20260330-317) is why that gap is ordinary. The 2020 farm laws were repealed in 2021, so there is no national trade-area statute to point to instead of the state Act.
- **e-NAM.** Is this mandi on the platform, and does it assay? A login without a grade is not inter-mandi price discovery.
- **Procurement.** For paddy or wheat, was the purchase centre open, were lots accepted at the support price, and were sellers paid? Pulses and oilseeds need their own column. Horticulture usually has no column.
- **The assisted asset.** A warehouse, packhouse, or food-processing unit financed under a Ministry of Food Processing Industries scheme or the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund counts if it took this commodity on recorded days. An inauguration without an intake record is not delivery.
- **The paper that stops trucks.** Yard fee, FSSAI step, warehouse receipt, GST document, or a Directorate General of Foreign Trade status. The layers are the [regulatory stack](/blog/agri-value-chains-regulatory-outlook-morth-nic-20260330-437).
- **The buyer after the public step.** If a mill or an exporter rejects what a centre or a yard released, the grade is not shared. That is an operating fact.

## Inferences the chain will not support

A busy mandi does not prove a scheme caused the traffic. The gap between a farm-gate anecdote and a city retail price includes fees, freight, loss, and margin. One monsoon can dominate a before-and-after story. Read [how to tell a policy shift](/blog/agri-value-chains-policy-shift-explainer-morth-nic-20260330-157) before describing a notification as implementation in every state.

Publish the missing cells. A team that needs a number will otherwise insert one.
