## Build on assets that are already commissioned

A founder moving goods in India can treat two rail corridors as operating infrastructure. The Railways Minister told the Rajya Sabha on 7 August 2026 that the Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor, Ludhiana to Sonnagar, 1,337 km, and the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor, Dadri to Jawaharlal Nehru Port, 1,506 km, have been commissioned. About 443 trains a day were running on them. The ministry's design point for a western-port shipper is faster access to the northern hinterland, and for eastern mineral traffic a dedicated path that is no longer sharing every block with passenger trains. Double-stack container trains and higher axle loads are what the replies say the corridors enable.

The practical companion is a terminal, not a press conference about a future line. Under the Gati Shakti Cargo Terminal policy, 142 terminals were commissioned by that August reply, with about ₹10,000 crore of private money in them, an estimated 224 million tonnes a year of capacity, and 146 million tonnes handled in 2025-26. If your cargo is cement, oil, steel, fly ash, or automobiles, the same reply records industry-owned wagon rakes already running: about 275 special-purpose rakes, 397 general-purpose rakes, and about 54 automobile rakes.

## Use the data layer before you invent one

DPIIT's Unified Logistics Interface Platform had connected more than 30 systems and passed 160 crore transactions as of August 2025. The Logistics Data Bank had tracked more than 75 million export-import containers across 101 inland container depots. A logistics product that duplicates container visibility, without a reason the Data Bank cannot see the move, is competing with a public system. A product that sits on those interfaces, or that serves the smaller operators DPIIT says still lack digital literacy, is aimed at a gap the ministry has named.

State rules change the unit economics of a warehouse or a park. By 16 September 2025, 19 states had granted industry status to logistics. Twenty-seven states and Union Territories had a logistics policy, and nine policies were still in draft. The first site screen is that pair of facts for the state you want, plus LEADS 2024 if you want the ministry's grouping. Coastal achievers in the 3 January 2025 release were Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Odisha, and Tamil Nadu. Kerala and West Bengal were coastal aspirers. The label is a logistics-ease grouping. It is not a veto on a port that sits in an aspirer state.

## Coastal and park choices

If the leg is coastal, the published incentives include a 40 percent discount on vessel and cargo charges at major ports, priority berthing, bunker-fuel GST at 5 percent rather than 18 percent, and green channel clearance. Fifteen Sagarmala projects worth ₹849 crore had been completed for coastal berths and Ro-Ro or Ro-Pax jetties. Sagarmala's startup channel, S2I2, opened on 19 March 2025 for green shipping, smart ports, maritime logistics, and shipbuilding technology.

A multimodal park is a partnership, not a solo greenfield. The pattern on record since 15 February 2022 is state or port equity: Assam at Jogighopa, JNPT at Nagpur, Chennai Port and SIPCOT at Mappedu, KIADB at Bengaluru. Confirm a later status before you treat a 2022 bid as a live tender. DPIIT said in September 2025 that agencies were still harmonising how parks are planned.

## What not to put in the operating plan

Dankuni to Surat is a Union Budget 2026 proposal. On 7 August 2026 its detailed project report was being prepared and updated. The February briefing's 2,052 km and nearly ₹1.20 lakh crore describe the proposal. They are not a date when your container can ride that line. The India-Middle East-Europe corridor is a September 2023 memorandum. The commerce minister said on 16 April 2025 that it would cut logistics costs by up to 30 percent and time by 40 percent. File that as his expectation for a corridor still being arranged with the Gulf and Europe. The domestic cost percentage is waiting on the DPIIT-NCAER study, which in September 2025 was in its final stages.

A founder memo that survives contact with these releases has three lines: which commissioned corridor or port the goods touch, which terminal or wagon scheme the firm can actually contract, and which state notification covers the site. Everything else is a watch item.
