Capital in Indian education and skilling does not arrive as a smooth ramp. It is announced in a budget estimate, often reduced in the revised estimate, and then rewritten in the next budget. Watching that cycle is more useful than quoting the largest number in a speech. The figures here are from the Union Budget 2026-27 documents.

## School schemes: announced, revised, restored

[Statement 12](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/eb/stat12.pdf) of the Expenditure Profile is the cleanest place to see the school lines that are wholly for children.

Samagra Shiksha, the integrated school scheme from pre-school to senior secondary, spent ₹35,001.58 crore in 2024-25. The 2025-26 budget estimate was ₹41,250 crore. The revised estimate was ₹38,000 crore. The 2026-27 budget estimate is ₹42,100 crore.

PM Schools for Rising India (PM SHRI) is the sharper capital swing. Actual expenditure in 2024-25 was ₹3,504.30 crore. The 2025-26 budget estimate of ₹7,500 crore was revised to ₹4,500 crore. The 2026-27 budget estimate returns to ₹7,500 crore. PM SHRI is an exemplar-school programme, not a grant to every school. A restored budget estimate means the Centre has put the money back on the page. It does not mean last year's cut has been filled in arrears.

PM POSHAN moved from a 2025-26 budget estimate of ₹12,500 crore to a revised estimate of ₹10,600 crore, and to ₹12,749.99 crore for 2026-27. Meals are recurrent, but the same revision habit shows up.

One line is new rather than restored. The Atal Tinkering Labs scheme shows no 2024-25 actual and no 2025-26 budget estimate in Statement 12, then ₹500 crore in the 2025-26 revised estimate and ₹3,200 crore in the 2026-27 budget estimate. That is a capital intention at the start of its budget life. Until expenditure appears in a later actuals column, it is a plan.

The Department of School Education's own totals, in the [Expenditure Profile](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/eb/vol1.pdf), follow the scheme pattern. Centrally sponsored schemes were ₹62,660 crore in the 2025-26 budget estimate and ₹53,730.02 crore in the revised estimate, then ₹63,010.02 crore for 2026-27. Central sector schemes moved the other way, from ₹429 crore budgeted for 2025-26 to ₹879 crore revised, and to ₹3,631 crore in 2026-27. Do not describe "the school budget" as one cycle. The sponsored schemes and the central schemes did not move together.

## ITI upgrades: a large estimate, a small revised year

The skill ministry's capital story is PM-SETU, the Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs. The [Demand for Grants](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/eb/sbe92.pdf) puts the ministry's net provision at ₹9,885.80 crore for 2026-27, against a revised ₹2,703.54 crore in 2025-26 and a budget estimate that year of ₹6,100.10 crore.

Inside that, centrally sponsored schemes were budgeted at ₹3,049.90 crore for 2025-26 and revised to ₹367 crore. The 2026-27 estimate is ₹6,140.50 crore, of which the demand shows ₹58.70 crore as capital and the rest as revenue. A [Rajya Sabha reply on 1 April 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2247753) identifies PM-SETU's budget estimate as ₹3,000 crore in 2025-26 and ₹6,140.50 crore in 2026-27. The reply says the scheme was launched on 4 October 2025, that 2025-26 was a preparatory phase, and that funds for ITI upgradation are released only after a special purpose vehicle is formed. As of that reply, proposals from state steering committees had not yet been received for the national committee. The revised estimate of ₹367 crore for the whole sponsored-scheme bucket is what a preparatory year looks like when the release condition has not been met.

The works, when they proceed, are specified. Component I upgrades 1,000 government ITIs, 200 hubs and 800 spokes, with smart classrooms, laboratories, digital content, and new courses. Component II augments five National Skill Training Institutes, at Bhubaneswar, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kanpur, and Ludhiana, including centres of excellence for training of trainers. The reply's progress markers were administrative, not buildings: 35 states and Union Territories had identified clusters, 32 had formed state steering committees, 25 had created budget heads, 19 had floated expressions of interest, and 12 had floated requests for proposals. That is the capital cycle before concrete.

## What to watch next

For the rest of 2026-27, the test is whether PM SHRI and Samagra Shiksha move from the new budget estimate toward expenditure, or whether another revised estimate repeats the 2025-26 cut. For PM-SETU, the test is whether state proposals reach the National Steering Committee and whether special purpose vehicles are formed, because the demand document ties release to that step. A budget estimate of ₹6,140.50 crore that is not released is not capital formation.

Kendriya Vidyalaya and Navodaya lines, ₹10,129.41 crore and ₹6,025 crore in the 2026-27 budget estimate, are steadier institutional capital and operating support. They do not swing like a new scheme. A capex watch that only tracks the new name will miss the systems that already run schools every day.
