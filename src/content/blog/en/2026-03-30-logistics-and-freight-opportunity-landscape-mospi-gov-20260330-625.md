The opportunity in Indian logistics is not a blank map. The government has already named the shelves where private work fits. What it has not done, in the August 2026 replies, is size the revenue. Anyone who puts a market-crore figure under these shelves is no longer reading the record.

## Data, for firms that can describe a use

The Unified Logistics Interface Platform is operational, according to the [11 August 2026 reply](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AS327_tYLfn2.pdf), and it integrates ministry logistics systems through APIs. The door is [goulip.in](https://goulip.in/). The opportunity is a product that does something a shipper or a transporter will pay for once a specific field is reliable: slotting, exception alerts, proof that a document and a vehicle match. The opportunity is not “access to all government logistics data.” Access follows a use. A pitch that cannot name the field and the user is early.

Beside it, the Logistics Data Bank tracks export-import containers across ports, container freight stations, and inland container depots. That is a defined trail. Tools that sit on that trail — dwell alerts, handoff between port and inland depot, a customer view that does not require a phone call — have a public object to attach to. Tools that promise the same for every domestic truck are promising a trail this reply does not describe.

A public Gati Shakti query platform, opened in September 2025 according to the written reply of the same day, lets outside users ask for analytic reports. Site selection, spur planning, and clash checks are the obvious uses. About 1,800 layers is the stock the reply claims. A layer that does not include your commodity’s siding is a product gap and a planning gap at once.

## Parks, hinterland, and the chains already named

Multi-modal logistics parks under Bharatmala are far enough along that the roads ministry hosts the [model concession documents](https://morth.nic.in/en/standard-document/bharatmala). The opportunity around a park is not only the concessionaire. It is the operator inside it, the fleet that feeds it, and the shipper who will not move until the highway and the rail link are both in the “under implementation” column rather than merely evaluated. The planning group had 198 projects under implementation out of 256 sanctioned. A park business plan that ignores which column its link is in is a real-estate plan.

Port hinterland is the maritime version. [Sagarmala](https://shipmin.gov.in/en/division/sagarmala) is the programme. The August replies place it with highways and dedicated freight corridors as connectivity that is supposed to cut friction for trade. Opportunities that match the reply’s own language are the ones tied to a port, a freight station, or an inland depot the Logistics Data Bank can see. A coastal or port-linked warehouse for a named commodity is closer to the agenda than a generic “logistics hub” with no gate.

The Sectoral Plan for Efficient Logistics has been initiated for coal, steel, fertilizers, cement, pharmaceuticals, and food. Those six are the Centre’s own shortlist of chains worth a plan. A builder who picks one of them can ask for the plan and design to it. A builder who picks a seventh should not pretend the shortlist covered them. Pharmaceuticals and food pull standards, temperature, and waste reduction, which the 2022 cabinet note already flagged for agriculture and for smaller firms. Coal, steel, cement, and fertilizers pull bulk handling and the rail or coastal leg.

## Standards and skills, which are quieter markets

The same reply says an e-handbook on warehousing standards, model guidelines for city logistics plans, and NLP-Marine have been undertaken, and that processes at ports, land ports, and airports have been standardised. Standards create work for firms that can audit, fit out, and operate to them. They also raise the cost of a non-standard shed. City logistics is the urban end of the network, where the national highway count stops. The 2022 note’s emphasis on warehouses with spatial planning, digitisation, and track-and-trace is the policy language for that fit-out market. It is [here](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/policy-introduces-unified-logistics-interface-platform-standardization-monitoring-framework-and-skill-development-for-greater-efficiency-in-logistics-services/), and it contains no order book.

Skills are a service opportunity with a boundary. Sixty-eight qualification packs are operational, and courses sit in more than 100 universities and institutes. Training that maps to a pack can be sold. Training that promises a national hiring wave cannot be tied to this reply, because the reply has no job count.

Industry status in 32 states and Union Territories, and formulated policies in 29, change the state conversation about land and incentives. They are an opportunity to read a notification, not an opportunity to assume the incentive is uniform.

The landscape, stripped of market-size theatre, is four jobs the papers already describe: make a named data field useful, operate at a park or a port trail that is actually being built, specialise in a commodity that has a sectoral plan, and meet a standard the Centre says it has written. [Great Indian Company](https://greatindiancompany.com/) is the desk for founders who want that list without a fabricated TAM.
