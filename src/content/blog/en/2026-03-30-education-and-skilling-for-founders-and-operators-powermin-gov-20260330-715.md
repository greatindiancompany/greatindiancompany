A founder in Indian skilling is usually selling one of four things: a short course the government will reimburse, a course an employer will host as an apprenticeship, a service to a school or ITI, or a direct fee to a learner. The rules are different. The fastest way to waste a year is to build the product for one buyer and the paperwork for another.

## If the payer is PMKVY

PMKVY 4.0 runs through project implementing agencies. The year-end review describes more than 15,500 institutions, including skill hubs inside schools, colleges, and ITIs. Your centre needs to be affiliable by an awarding body that the National Council for Vocational Education and Training recognises. NCVET does not, in its own description, license every classroom. It recognises the awarding body, and the awarding body affiliates training partners and assessment agencies. A certificate that does not come from an approved NSQF qualification is a private document. Learners may still want it. The scheme will not treat it as its own.

New-age roles are where the Ministry has said it will pay the top common-norms rate, ₹49 per hour, so that tools and trainers exist. That figure is as on 30 June 2026, alongside more than 5.44 lakh trainees in those roles and ₹414.86 crore used. Build the unit cost under that ceiling if the public scheme is the customer. On-the-job training is part of the course design the Ministry describes, not an optional extra you can drop to save money.

Do not promise a placement rate "as per PMKVY" for the current version. The Ministry has said placements were tracked in the short-term component of the first three versions, through 2021-22, and that version 4.0 does not track them that way. If you track your own alumni, say it is your tracker, with the cohort and the date. The national number you cannot cite does not become citable because a pitch deck needs it.

Trainers are a separate compliance object. ₹200 crore is earmarked for a pool of trainers and assessors, with NCVET procedures on the [Skill India Digital Hub](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in/). A centre whose trainers are not in that framework will struggle to deliver the courses the hub is built to record. Check that your batch on the hub matches the batch in your classroom. Scheme counts are how the Ministry sees you.

## If the payer is an employer

Apprenticeship is a contract, not a batch you fill and invoice as training. Designated trades and optional trades are both real. Optional trades are how a firm specifies a role the designated list does not name. The Ministry's count of IT and ITeS apprentices, 3,44,003 under NAPS-2 from 2022-23 to 31 December 2025, shows where volume has actually sat. Artificial-intelligence-related trades in that same window were 1,991. If your company story is "AI apprentices at national scale," the official count is the objection you should answer, not a detail to omit.

Engagement is easier to celebrate than completion. Final assessments were 2.33 lakh in 2024-25. An operator who is paid on engagement and who never sees assessment will be misread by any buyer who has opened the year-end review. Build the operating rhythm around on-the-job training logs and the final assessment, because that is the part the public numbers show as scarce.

## If the payer is a state building an ITI

PM-SETU is not a short-term batch contract. It upgrades 1,000 government ITIs, 200 hubs and 800 spokes, and five National Skill Training Institutes. Release of upgradation funds depends on a special purpose vehicle. As of 1 April 2026, state proposals had not yet reached the national steering committee, 19 states and Union Territories had floated expressions of interest, and 12 had floated requests for proposals. The founder move is to read the request in a specific state, not to hire a national sales team against the ₹6,140.50 crore budget estimate. That estimate was paired, the year before, with a revised sponsored-scheme provision of ₹367 crore. Budget estimates move.

The Craftsmen Training Scheme courses for semiconductor technicians and green-hydrogen technicians tell you which labs a modernised ITI is being asked to run. A vendor who can equip NSQF 4.5 and 3.5 courses is closer to the notified curriculum than a vendor with a generic "smart classroom" catalogue.

## If the payer is the learner or the school

Fee-paid courses and school vocational labs sit beside this public machinery, not inside every part of it. The policy wants vocational exposure inside ordinary schools, and PM SHRI plus the announced content-creator labs are school-side capital. A school still needs its board and its state recognition. Your content does not confer that. Selling foundational-learning material in 2026-27 is timely because NIPUN Bharat's Grade 3 horizon is that year. It is timely only if a state mission or a school is the buyer. The policy paragraph is not a purchase order.

The operator's checklist is short. Name the payer. Match the recognition body to that payer. Put the batch on the hub if the scheme requires it. Price public new-age training inside the notified hourly ceiling. Treat apprenticeship completion as the product. Treat ITI capital as a state tender with a vehicle condition. Leave unrelated ministry links out of the data room. They signal that the file was assembled by a template, and a scheme appraisal will notice.
