# The ASUSE signal inside MSME productivity

The market signal in India's small-enterprise economy is not a single index. The cleanest official activity print is the Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises. ASUSE 2025, released by MoSPI on 24 March 2026, covers unincorporated non-agricultural establishments in manufacturing, trade, and other services for January–December 2025. Construction is outside the survey. Limited liability partnerships are outside it. Udyam registrations are a different count.

## What moved

Against ASUSE 2023-24 (October 2023–September 2024), the Press Information Bureau note reports:

- Establishments: 7.34 crore to 7.92 crore, about 7.97 percent.
- Employment: up 6.18 percent, more than 74.52 lakh jobs added, about 12.81 crore workers in the 2025 round.
- Gross value added at current prices: up 10.87 percent. Trade rose 16.77 percent, manufacturing 8.52 percent, other services 7.36 percent.
- Share of gross value added: other services 42 percent, trade 37 percent, manufacturing 21 percent.
- Establishments using the internet: 26.7 percent to 39.4 percent.
- Women-owned proprietary establishments: 26.2 percent to 27.0 percent.

Gross value added per worker, for market establishments and in current prices, moved from ₹1,49,742 to ₹1,56,539. MoSPI calls that 4.54 percent. Gross value added per establishment moved from ₹2,45,687 to ₹2,52,699. Emoluments per hired worker rose 3.88 percent, from ₹1,41,071 to ₹1,46,550.

## How to read the gap

Headcount and establishment counts grew faster than output per worker and output per establishment. Value added grew faster than either productivity ratio, and the trade sector carried the largest value-added gain. A lender or a buyer who treats "the MSME sector is expanding" as "each workshop is producing more" is reading the wrong line.

The internet figure is an operating signal with a narrow meaning. A larger share of establishments uses the internet. The survey does not say that those connections caused the 4.54 percent rise in current-price value added per worker. Digital use and productivity are published side by side. They are not a causal model.

Prices are current prices. A 10.87 percent rise in gross value added is not a volume measure. Anyone who needs a real productivity claim has to deflate it with an official price series and show that work. This brief stops at the nominal figures MoSPI published.

## What the signal is for

For a founder selling into this economy, the demand-side clue is the composition: services and trade hold most of the measured value added, and trade's value added grew fastest. For an operator inside manufacturing, the relevant clue is slower value-added growth than trade, plus a still-modest rise in value added per worker. For a credit officer, registration growth on Udyam is a formalisation signal. ASUSE is the activity signal. SIDBI and the Ministry's schemes respond to the registered enterprise. They do not rewrite the survey.

The reference calendars differ, and MoSPI has said the 2025 sample design supports quarterly estimates and, for participating states, district estimates. National headlines are not a district order book.
