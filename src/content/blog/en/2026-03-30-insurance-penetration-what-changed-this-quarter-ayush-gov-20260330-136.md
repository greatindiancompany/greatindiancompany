# India's Insurance Penetration Stayed at 3.7 Percent in 2024-25

Insurance penetration in India is an annual ratio, not a quarterly release. The latest full-year print is the [IRDAI Annual Report 2024-25](https://irdai.gov.in/en/document-detail?documentId=8375620). In 2024-25 the ratio remained 3.7 percent of GDP, the same as 2023-24. Life insurance penetration declined from 2.8 percent to 2.7 percent. Non-life penetration stayed at 1 percent.

That is the change. A flat headline hides a split inside the ratio.

## What the ratio measures

IRDAI defines insurance penetration as insurance premium as a percentage of GDP, and insurance density as premium per person. The Press Information Bureau restated the same definitions in its [23 April 2026 note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2254950&lang=1&reg=3): gross premiums for direct life and non-life business, divided by GDP.

Because the denominator is GDP, rupee premium can rise while the ratio does not. IRDAI, citing provisional National Statistical Office estimates, puts GDP at current prices at ₹330.68 lakh crore in 2024-25, against ₹301.23 lakh crore in 2023-24, a growth of 9.8 percent. PIB reports that the sector collected premiums of ₹11.93 lakh crore in FY 2024-25. The published penetration ratio is the one to use. It is 3.7 percent. Do not back-solve a new ratio from those two totals. The official series already does that job, on IRDAI's premium definition.

## Density moved the other way

Insurance density rose from USD 95 in 2023-24 to USD 97 in 2024-25. Life density rose from USD 70 to USD 72. Non-life density stayed at USD 25. IRDAI says this upward trend in density has held since 2016-17.

A reader who watches only penetration will miss that per-person premium in dollars edged up, while the life share of GDP edged down. Both statements are in the same annual report.

## What did not arrive this quarter

There is no official quarterly penetration number to update a dashboard with. The 2024-25 report is the current annual account. PIB's April 2026 note repeats that year's scale: 3.7 percent penetration, life 2.7 percent, non-life 1 percent, density USD 97.

Other movements in the same year are real, and they are not the penetration ratio:

- Life insurers' offices stood at 12,210 on 31 March 2025, 693 more than the year before.
- Individual life agents stood at 31.23 lakh, against 28.95 lakh a year earlier.
- Stand-alone health insurers' offices rose from 1,657 to 1,788.
- General insurers' offices fell from 8,164 to 8,078.

Those are distribution facts. They do not rewrite the 3.7 percent.

## How to read the next print

The next time the ratio can change in an official series is the following IRDAI annual report. Until that volume is out, a "quarterly" move in penetration is not something IRDAI has published.
