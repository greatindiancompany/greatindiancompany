## The reply that closes a common question

Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 2523, answered on 3 August 2026, asked for PMKVY 4.0 short-term training targets for 2025-26 and 2026-27, particularly in Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra. The Minister of State (Independent Charge) for Skill Development and Entrepreneurship answered that the scheme, implemented from 2022-23 through short-term training and recognition of prior learning, is demand-driven and industry-oriented. Therefore no state-wise training targets, including for those two states, have been set.

A state pattern for PMKVY 4.0 cannot be read off a target sheet. Coverage follows proposals that project implementing agencies submit, after a skill-gap review, as the Rajya Sabha was told on 12 August 2026.

## Where states still appear

Absence of a training quota is not absence of the states.

School education is shared. NEP 2020 tells states to expand open schooling in regional languages through State Institutes of Open Schooling, and it gives state governments a role in teacher-education standards and in gender-inclusion priorities. Samagra Shiksha, named in the policy as the successor to Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, is the school-education scheme through which centre and states already work. This brief does not invent a state-by-state Samagra allocation.

On the skilling side, the 12 August 2026 reply says Kaushal Samiksha Kendra inspections are carried out by the ministry, the Regional Directorates of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, State Skill Development Missions, and National Skill Development Corporation monitoring teams. State missions are in the inspection chain even though they do not hold a PMKVY trainee quota.

The 3 August reply also describes a specific school project: skill labs and trainer training in 400 Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas and 200 Eklavya Model Residential Schools, funded through the National Skill Development Fund. The Eklavya schools, established by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, serve Scheduled Tribe students, including those in remote areas. The project’s stated scale is 1,200 vocational skill labs aligned with the CBSE curriculum, two labs in each selected school, and 1,200 skill teachers, two per school. That is a listed-institution pattern, not a ranking of all states.

## What this page will not do

It will not name a leading or lagging state for learning outcomes or for PMKVY batches. The August replies used here do not publish that ranking. AISHE and school statistics can show state enrolment when a table is released. Those tables are not reproduced here.
