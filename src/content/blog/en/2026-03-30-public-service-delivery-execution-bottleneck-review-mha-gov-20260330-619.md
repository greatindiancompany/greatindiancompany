# The last-mile bottlenecks NeSDA and DBT already name

India's service stack is large enough that the bottleneck is rarely "no portal." DARPG and the DBT Mission already name the points where a portal stops being a service. Those names are a better review agenda than a generic digital-transformation list.

## The portal that informs and does not deliver

The [28 May 2026 PIB release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2266244) on NeSDA 2025 lists assessment parameters that are bottleneck labels: accessibility, content availability, ease of use, information security and privacy, end service delivery, integrated service delivery, status and request tracking, open government data, e-participation, and emerging technologies. End service delivery and status tracking are the execution tests. A page that explains eligibility can score on content and still leave the citizen in a queue.

The release also splits what is assessed. State, UT, city, and central ministry portals are one class. Their service portals are another. A government homepage and the system that signs a certificate can both be "live" while only one of them finishes the request. DARPG's [way-forward page](https://darpg.gov.in/relatedlinks/nesda-way-forward-status-implementation) after NeSDA 2021 says the monitoring aim includes saturation of e-services, faceless and entitlement-based delivery, identification of bottlenecks, and sharing of practices. Bottleneck identification is an official purpose of the follow-up, not an outside criticism.

The 2025 cycle proposes 59 mandatory services for every state and Union Territory. A state that has digitised a subset, and left the rest as a counter visit, will show that gap against the list. This brief does not know each state's gap. The assessment portal is how DARPG is collecting it.

## The district workflow behind the form

e-District was designed as backend computerisation so that high-volume services could come out through common service centres, not only as a new URL. Tripura's [IT department page](https://dit.tripura.gov.in/e-district) shows what "finished" looks like when a state describes it: online application and online output, a digitally signed certificate, verification on the same platform, email, SMS, status enquiry, and an integrated payment (eGRAS), with a District e-Governance Society in each district. Where any of those is missing, the bottleneck is the step that still needs a visit, a wet signature, or a payment that does not reconcile.

The [6 August 2026 Lok Sabha reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295635) keeps state portals as the place certificates and pensions are delivered, and treats UMANG as a single mobile platform that includes some state services. The integration bottleneck is the distance between those two lists. Tamil Nadu's 17 services on UMANG are a published example of a partial join, next to an e-Sevai portal the reply describes as end-to-end.

## The cash bottleneck is seeding and cash-out

DBT's last mile is not the scheme count. The [Aadhaar Payments Bridge procedure](https://dbtbharat.gov.in/data/dbt_payments/Standard-Operating-Procedure-of-Aadhaar-Payments-Bridge-(APB).pdf) puts the friction at the bank: map Aadhaar to the right account, refuse seeding when the account cannot receive the credit, and repair wrong credits because the bank is liable to the paying department. UIDAI's FAQ adds the citizen constraint of one mapped account, and the physical constraint of withdrawal through a branch, a correspondent, a post office, or a micro-ATM. A transfer that succeeds in the core banking system and stalls at the village cash point is the bottleneck the cumulative rupee figure hides.

## The assisted counter is the designed workaround

Common Service Centres, 5.01 lakh functional as of May 2026 in the August reply, exist because self-serve is not the whole population. They are a bottleneck mitigator and, when the entrepreneur's machine or the state portal is down, a bottleneck of their own. CPGRAMS on UMANG gives the resident a record when either channel fails. A service owner who measures only completed app submissions will not see the counter queue or the grievance that started there.
