# Food Processing In India: The Path Written Into The Schemes

## Trajectory means the text, not a forecast

A mid-term path is whatever the scheme pages still commit to. This brief does not extend those pages with a growth rate. It also does not treat a target year printed beside a rupee projection as a result that has arrived.

## The infrastructure path MoFPI still lists

Under PMKSY, the components the ministry page says will be implemented are cold chain and value addition, processing and preservation capacity, agro-processing clusters, food safety and quality assurance infrastructure, human resources and institutions for research and development, and Operation Greens. The direction in that list is physical: fewer breaks between farm and buyer, more processing capacity, and clusters rather than a scatter of single machines.

The same page narrows the older map. Mega Food Parks and backward-and-forward linkage schemes are described as discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with committed liability retained. The mid-term path is not "more mega parks." It is the six components still named.

The page dates the continuation through 31 March 2026. That date is past as of this update. Until MoFPI publishes a later window, the honest mid-term statement is that the component list is known and the authority to treat it as still open is not something this brief can add.

Operation Greens shows the perishable path in crop terms. It began with tomato, onion, and potato. The page says it was widened to 22 perishables and names examples including mango, banana, apple, pineapple, carrot, cauliflower, and beans. The trajectory for those crops is value addition along a chain, not a new commodity board.

## The micro path

PMFME is aimed at micro enterprises that are still informal, and at FPOs, self-help groups, and producer cooperatives. Common infrastructure on the ministry page is farm-gate grading and cold storage, and a shared processing line for the district product, open to hire. Over a few years, the path written there is formal units tied to a district product, not a single national plant.

## The manufacturing path with an end year still on the page

PLISFPI runs, on its own page, from 2021-22 to 2026-27. The segments are ready-to-cook and ready-to-eat foods including millets, processed fruits and vegetables, marine products, and mozzarella cheese, plus branding abroad. Investment conditions for selected applicants were loaded into the early years of that window. The remaining published path is incentive years for those who were selected, including payment in the year after the year of performance, through the end of the stated period. It is not an open-ended capacity race, and the page calls the scheme fund-limited.
