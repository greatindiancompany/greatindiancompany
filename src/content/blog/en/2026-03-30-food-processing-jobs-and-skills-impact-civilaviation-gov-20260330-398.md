# Food Processing In India: Supervisors, Handlers, And Plant Skills

## Skills this brief will name, and counts it will not

This page does not estimate how many jobs food processing adds. Scheme pages publish employment projections. Copying them forward would pretend a target is a headcount. The roles that are defined in public rules are enough for an operator to staff against.

## The defined role is the food safety supervisor

FSSAI's FoSTaC page defines a food safety supervisor as a person appointed by the food business operator who holds a valid FoSTaC certificate issued by FSSAI. The work is the hygiene and sanitary practice in Schedule 4 of the Food Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Regulations, 2011.

The same page assigns the supervisor a practical list: recognize and prevent handling risks, train and supervise other food handlers, implement Schedule 4, and keep the business aligned with FSSAI requirements. It also says the supervisor should carry out periodic onsite training of all food handlers at least quarterly and maintain a record. A handler, in that text, is a person who directly handles packaged or unpackaged food, preparation equipment and utensils, or food-contact surfaces.

That is a plant role with a certificate and a training log. It is not a generic "skilling initiative."

## What the floor still needs beside that certificate

A cold chain and a pack house need people who can run pre-cooling, temperature logs, and grading lines. APEDA's pack-house recognition is about those steps for horticulture exports: sorting, grading, treatment, packing, pre-cooling, cold storage, quarantine, and traceability. The skill is product-specific. A grape line and a mango line do not train the same defect list, which is why APEDA's recognized-packhouse record carries a product scope.

An FPO common facility adds a different skill set. PMFME's common infrastructure includes assaying, sorting, and grading at the farm gate, plus a shared processing line for the district product. The ministry page also says training and capacity building for group members can sit inside the project report, with training cost norms taken from the skill ministry. The supervisor certificate does not, by itself, teach assaying or the district process.

## What MoFPI says it stopped funding as a skill scheme

PMKSY still lists Human Resources and Institutions for research and development among components under implementation. The same page says human-resource promotional activities, skill development, and HACCP were discontinued in the 15th Finance Commission cycle, with committed liability provided for. A training budget that assumes the discontinued skill and HACCP track is still open for new support should be checked against that sentence before it is spent.

## How a plant uses this

Staff the Schedule 4 role first, keep the quarterly handler record, and then match process skills to the asset you actually run: a cold chain, an ODOP common line, or an export pack house. Headcount targets belong in a filing, not in this brief.
