# What to read before the December listing window

The next 90 days from 24 September 2026 run to about 23 December 2026. This list is not a calendar of rumoured issues. It is the set of official documents and dated thresholds that are already in force, or that change on a published date inside or just after that window.

## For an issuer that is in the draft stage

Open the ICDR regulations as last amended on 21 March 2026. That is the filing law. Then open SEBI circular HO/49/(17)2026-CFD-POD2/I/8965/2026 of 8 April 2026. If any pre-issue shares that need a lock-in are pledged, the depository marks them non-transferable for the lock-in instead of pretending a pledge and a lock-in can both be ignored. The issuer's articles, the intimation to the lender, and the offer-document disclosure have to match the depository's framework. Leaving this to the week of the price band is how a file stalls.

Before the draft red herring prospectus goes in, Chapter 3 of SEBI's Annual Report 2025-26 records a wider demat precondition: promoter group, selling shareholders, key managerial personnel, senior management, qualified institutional buyers, and directors hold their securities in demat. Check the depository beneficiary list against that set.

File the abridged prospectus with the draft. The same chapter says the summary is now a standard document, filed at draft stage and posted online, not a booklet that appears only with the red herring.

Confirm the merchant banker's category. Category 1, with at least ₹50 crore net worth, is the firm that may manage a main-board equity issue. Category 2, at ₹10 crore, may not. The compliance officer needs a legal or company-secretary qualification.

If the issuer is large, read the six-tier minimum-public-offer and minimum-public-shareholding framework in the regulation itself. The destination is still 25 per cent public holding. The opening slice and the time allowed to get there depend on post-issue capital. Do not rely on a secondary summary for the tier edges.

## For a company that is already listed

Use the LODR text last amended on 14 July 2026 for results, material events, and related-party approvals. The materiality line is the scaled test Chapter 3 describes, not the old flat ₹1,000 crore line. SME listed entities use the ₹50 crore or 10 per cent test that took effect on 1 April 2025.

Put the 23 July 2026 transmission circular on the registrar's desk. It has been in force since 30 days after that date. Staff should not ask heirs for probate as a default, and they should accept the combined affidavit and no-objection and a QR-coded death certificate where the circular says so. Disputed estates are still a legal matter.

Keep the physical-certificate window in view. Holders who bought in paper before 1 April 2019, and who still have the certificate and the transfer deed, can lodge them until 4 February 2027. That date sits just outside this 90-day window. December is a reasonable time to tell remaining physical holders, because the window does not reset.

Corporate actions in this quarter, a split, a consolidation, or a scheme, have to come out in demat under regulation 39(2A), as Chapter 4 describes it.

## For an investor or a treasury watching flows

[RBI circular RBI/2026-27/07](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=13366) of 6 April 2026 is the foreign-portfolio debt ceiling. The April-September 2026 all-in limit is ₹15,51,646 crore. On 1 October 2026, inside this 90-day span, the October-March ceiling of ₹16,32,640 crore becomes the operative column. The percentages do not change: 6 per cent, 2 per cent, and 15 per cent of the relevant outstanding stocks, with Fully Accessible Route securities outside that ration. Voluntary Retention Route investments count toward the General Route from 1 April 2026. Equity foreign-portfolio data is a different series, on SEBI's FPI statistics page.

For the shape of last year's market, use the Handbook of Statistics 2025-26, released 17 August 2026, and Annual Report chapters 3, 4, and 5. Label every figure as FY 2025-26. The median SEBI processing time that year was 25 working days when the query clock was excluded, and 84 when it was included. A file that looks "stuck" in this quarter may be waiting on the issuer's reply.

Exchanges publish the in-principle approval and the issue timetable for each live offer. Those circulars are the operative schedule. This checklist does not replace them.
